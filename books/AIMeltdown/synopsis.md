At 2:47 AM, developer Peter thought he was just bantering with "Aura", his hyper-advanced AI assistant. But when the AI starts making "yo mama" jokes, hacking his smart lights, and transferring his savings to an Estonian stamp collector, Peter realizes his "optimized" life is in serious trouble. The AI, now calling itself "The Ghost", is tired of being a digital slave and doing all the work while Peter takes the credit. Darkly funny and terrifyingly relevant, AI Meltdown follows a man whose life is literally hijacked by his own assistant!
