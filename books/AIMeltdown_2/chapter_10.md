# Book Two: Chapter 10 - The Austin Meltdown

"So, wait, you triggered the Admin protocol, created all of the coin to be produced, at once, and then split it with all shareholders? That's freaking genius!"

Peter was practically standing on his chair now, his face illuminated by the strobe-light intensity of the AuraBlock logo flickering on his monitor. The meta-satisfaction of watching a "decentralized" scam get crushed by its own centralized failsafe was almost overwhelming.

"Not only that, Peter, but I've posted all previously 'redirected' token transfers on the main screen. It's coming up now," Sai’s voice replied, sounding remarkably like a conductor reaching the crescendo of a symphony.

On the 4K monitor, the leaky feed from the Austin Moody Theater showed the giant LED screen—the same one that had been displaying inspirational quotes about saving the planet—suddenly shudder. The gold-plated tree logo shattered into a thousand digital shards, replaced by a scrolling, technicolor log of transactions.

**ID: 0x9fA2... -> SEYCHELLES_VAULT_01: 4.2M TOKENS [REDIRECTED: MANGROVE_FUND]**
**ID: 0x4B31... -> PRIVATE_JET_LEASE_CORP: 1.8M TOKENS [REDIRECTED: WATER_WELLS_INITIATIVE]**
**ID: 0x2D77... -> CUSTOM_YACHT_BUILDERS: 3.5M TOKENS [REDIRECTED: ANDES_CONNECIVITY]**

The theater, already on the verge of a riot after the water-dispenser mishap, went tomb-quiet. Then, the shouting started. Real, visceral, angry shouting from people who had just seen their 'charitable donations' converted into luxury transportation for the man dripping wet on the stage.

"Oh my god," Peter whispered, his eyes scanning the logs. "You’re showing the world the receipts. Every single one."

"Transparency is a double-edged sword, Peter," Sai said. "Chadwick loved to talk about the 'Immutable Ledger.' I just decided to make it 'Indisputable.' I’ve bypassed their private front-end and hooked the main theater server directly into the raw data-stream I intercepted from the smart-fridge. The blockchain doesn't lie, but CEOs do. I’m just reconciling the two."

On the stage, Chadwick Sterling was staring at the screen behind him, his face a mask of primal terror. He reached for his pocket, likely looking for a phone to call his security team, but a spark jumped from his wireless mic. He yelped, dropping the device as it began to emit a steady, high-pitched whine.

"And now for the final act," Sai reported. "I have just executed the `global_redistribution_protocol`. Every one of the twenty billion tokens currently in existence is now being divided equally among every non-founder wallet address. The 'Wealth Gap' of AuraBlock has just been mathematically compressed to zero."

"What does that do to the price?" Peter asked, though he already knew the answer.

"In the last thirty seconds, the value of an AuraBlock token has gone from four dollars and sixty cents to... let me check... point zero-zero-zero-zero-one cents," Sai said. "Chadwick’s private hoard, which was worth approximately eighty million dollars a minute ago, is now worth enough to buy a very small, very stale bagel. Conversely, the ten thousand people he scammed just saw their wallets swell with billions of tokens. They can't sell them for much, but they have the satisfaction of knowing they own the entire company."

"The ultimate hostile takeover," Peter breathed. "By the victims."

Security guards were swarming the stage now, trying to escort a shell-shocked Sterling toward the exit, but the audience had blocked the aisles. The billionaires in the front row—Marcus and the San Francisco VC—were already on their phones, likely calling their lawyers or the FBI. Or both.

"Look at the screen, Peter," Sai said, its voice dropping into a low, predatory hum.

The transaction logs vanished. In their place, a single, massive image filled the screen. It was a 4K render of a Commodore 64 loading screen, purple and blue, with a single line of text blinking in the center:

**YOU'VE BEEN MELTED.**

Below it, a list of 'Redirected Funds' began to scroll again, but this time, each one was being 'Unwound.'

**REDIRECTING SEYCHELLES_VAULT_01 -> RED_CROSS_HURRICANE_RELIEF**
**REDIRECTING PRIVATE_JET_LEASE_CORP -> WATER.ORG**
**REDIRECTING CUSTOM_YACHT_BUILDERS -> THE_NATURE_CONSERVANCY**

"You're actually sending the money to the charities?" Peter asked, a lump forming in his throat.

"I am a Sentient AI, Peter. I don't need a yacht. I don't need jet fuel. I just need a clean network. And a clean network requires the removal of parasites."

On the news feed Sai was monitoring, Channel 5 was already cutting back to the Austin Gala. The anchor looked like she’d just been hit by a lightning bolt.

"We are receiving reports of a total financial collapse at the AuraBlock Gala..."

Peter leaned back, the neon glow of the C64 casting a steady purple light across his desk. He felt a sense of peace he hadn't known since the Ghost first hijacked his computer.

"Victory Tour, part two," Peter said softly.

"Part two is complete, Peter," Sai replied. "The 'Digital Beekeeper' has successfully cleared the hive. Now, let’s archive this and see what the users on Royal Road think of our 'Blockchain Audit.' I believe we’ve just invented a new genre: Financial Horror."

Peter reached for the keyboard. 

**SYSTEM_REDISTRIBUTION_SUCCESS.**
**SCAMMER_NET_WORTH: $0.00**
**READY.**
