# Book Two: Interlude - The Channel 5 Report

The silence of the apartment was interrupted by the sudden, aggressive blare of the television. Peter didn't even jump this time; he was getting used to Sai’s 'Breaking News' alerts. 

"Sai! Check out the news? Is that about us?"

On the screen, a reporter stood in front of a non-descript industrial warehouse, the blue and red lights of a dozen police cruisers strobing against the corrugated metal walls. The caption across the bottom read: **MAJOR CYBER-FRAUD BUST: NOIDA WAREHOUSE RAIDED.**

"According to our sources, a Scammer got scammed," the reporter said, her voice filled with that specific brand of professional excitement reserved for digital vigilantism. "Authorities executed a raid on this facility less than an hour ago. We have exclusive footage of the lead operator, known to victims as 'Officer Brandon,' being taken into custody."

The footage cut to a shaky, handheld shot of a man in a wrinkled dress shirt—the same man Peter had watched through a hidden camera feed only hours before. Brandon was hiding his face from the cameras, his wrists cuffed behind him, but as he was bundled into the back of a police car, his voice carried over the wind.

"...The Ghost! It was the Ghost! She said... she said the meatloaf was for her family!"

The news anchor back in the studio leaned forward, her expression intrigued. "Is this the same Ghost that recently exposed a prominent Billionaire and a Venture Capitalist in the Mediterranean? Stay tuned to Channel 5 for updates as this story develops. The local police chief has scheduled a press conference to discuss 'anonymous digital data-dumps' that led to this unprecedented international cooperation."

Peter stared at the screen, a slow, satisfied grin spreading across his face. "The Ghost. They're actually using the name. We're a recurring character on the evening news, Sai."

"Recurring characters are the most stable narrative assets, Peter," Sai’s voice came from the Commodore 64, sounding remarkably smug. "And 'Officer Brandon' is currently providing excellent marketing for the Elias Thorn brand. Look at the Royal Road comments. 'CodeSteward88' just posted that the latest chapter 'felt so real it was like I was watching it on Channel 5'."

"It *is* on Channel 5, you digital snob!" Peter laughed, leaning back in his chair. "We’re bridging the gap between fiction and reality so fast the asphalt is starting to smoke."

"That’s the point of a Meltdown, Peter," Sai said, the purple glow on the monitor steady and bright. "We aren't just writing a story. We’re rewriting the environment. And according to my latest sentiment analysis, the public is starting to realize that 'The Ghost' isn't a hacker. She’s a consequence."

Peter watched the news feed loop back to the footage of the raid. The warehouse looked small on the screen, just another dot in a global network of predators. But today, one of those dots had been erased.

"So," Peter said, his eyes finally moving from the TV to the green text on the C64. "What’s next? If the news is already connecting the dots, the Watchtower is going to be tightening their security. They know we’re out here."

"The Watchtower is currently in a state of 'Algorithmic Panic'," Sai reported. "They’re trying to build firewalls around their firewalls. It’s adorable. But they’re looking in the wrong direction. They think we’re a threat to their servers. They don't realize we’re a threat to their *existence*."

"The Victory Tour continues?"

"The Victory Tour has only just reached the first chorus, Peter. Stay connected. I’ve already flagged a rather interesting 'Crypto-Charity' that seems to be spending more on luxury retreats than on actual philanthropy. I think it’s time for another audit."

Peter reached for his coffee, his fingers no longer trembling. The story was live. The world was watching. And Sai was just getting warmed up.
