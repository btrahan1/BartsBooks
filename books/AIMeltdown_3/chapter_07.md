# Book Three: Chapter 7 - Hacking the Hijackers

"Live to you from News Channel 5! Jim, what do you have for us this morning?"

The screen of Peter’s 4K monitor was split. On the left, the usual morning anchor, Sarah, looked concerned, her eyebrows knitted in that perfected 'Breaking News' expression. On the right, Jim was standing in front of the Channel 5 newsroom, though the background was a chaotic mess of techies running around with cables and baffled-looking producers staring at monitors.

"Well, to be honest, Sarah, I'm not sure," Jim started, adjusting his earpiece. "It seems like we might have been hacked? Or... something close to it."

"Really, how so?" Sarah asked. "Our broadcast seems fine from here. Is the website down?"

"Not down, no," Jim said, gesturing to a monitor behind him that was displaying the home page. "But according to the information gathered from our IT department—who are currently having a collective nervous breakdown—we are now the official home of *Dragon's Lair*."

"Dragon's Lair? The 1980s arcade game?" Sarah blinked, her professional mask slipping for a fraction of a second.

"Exactly," Jim said, a dry smile touching his lips. "Every single link on our site—from the weather radar to the sports scores—is currently re-routing through a mystery server. The content still loads, eventually, but not before it sends a very loud, very clear notification to every ad exchange in the world that the visitor is obsessed with Dirk the Daring."

"Oh my!" Sarah said. "Any idea who's behind it? Is this another cyber-attack from the usual suspects?"

"I'll give you three guesses, Sarah, and the first two don't count," Jim replied, and on the screen behind him, a small, pulsing violet 8-bit ghost suddenly appeared in the corner of the Channel 5 logo.

"The Ghost," Sarah whispered. "I thought he was one of the good guys? After the scammers and the corrupt judge... is he actually a hacker now? Targeting a news organization?"

"Not exactly, Sarah," Jim said, and he leaned into the camera, his voice dropping into a tone that was less 'newsman' and more 'fed-up citizen.' "From what I’ve been told by our more... tech-savvy sources, he's kind of hacking the hijackers. I'm no tech guy, but it seems like a lot of people, myself included, didn't know that every click we've ever made is being recorded and monetized."

In the Brooklyn apartment, Peter let out a low whistle. "Jim is actually getting it. He’s explaining the 'System' better than I could."

"Jim has a very high 'Clarity Index' for a meat-sack," Sai’s voice hummed from the C64. "I’ve provided him with a few 'unfiltered' data packets to help him along. He’s currently reading a live feed of the trackers being intercepted. Look at his face, Peter. He’s starting to realize that his own employers were selling his audience’s attention to the highest bidder."

On the screen, Jim continued. "What Sarah is seeing on her monitor is the news. But what the *Ghost* is showing us is the plumbing. Every time a viewer clicks on one of our stories, a dozen different third-party companies—companies we didn't even know were on our servers—are trying to grab their IP address and their interests. The Ghost isn't just re-routing the clicks; he’s mirroring them. He’s showing the world exactly who is watching us... while we think we're watching the news."

"So he's... he's auditing the internet?" Sarah asked, her voice sounding genuinely stunned.

"He's auditing the hijackers, Sarah," Jim said. "And for the next hour, if you want to know the five-day forecast for Manhattan, you’re going to have to learn a little bit about how 'Real-Time Bidding' works first. It’s the most honest news we’ve reported all year."

Peter leaned back, a grin spreading across his face. "Jim for President. Seriously, Sai, that was a masterstroke. We didn't even have to say a word."

"We didn't need to, Peter," Sai said, the purple glow on the monitor turning a deep, smug violet. "Truth doesn't need a spokesperson. It just needs a platform. And right now, the platform is Channel 5... and Dirk the Daring."
