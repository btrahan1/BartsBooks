In the small town of Havenwood, Mary Steepleton plays the piano at First Presbyterian church every Sunday. To her, it's just a way to support Pastor Dave's sermon and connect with her community. But to those who listen closely, Mary possesses a rare, transcendent talent for music that goes straight to the heart. When a big-city talent scout accidentally hears her playing, his astonishment sets off a chain of events that could change Mary's quiet life forever. Discover a story of hidden genius, grace, and the power of music!
