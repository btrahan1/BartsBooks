## Chapter 2: The Kindergarten of War

The newly formed "Paladin Fist"—ten of the strongest, most confused men in the Order—stood in a line.

Prince William paced back and forth in front of them, his baton tapping a rhythm on his armored leg.

"You are the Fists!" William declared. "Ten men. One goal!"

He stopped and glared at a man whose stance was slightly off.

"But you lack balance!" William shouted. "You do not sense the grain! You do not understand flow! You are fighting the air, and the air is winning!"

The whispers started in the ranks. *"What is he talking about?"* *"Grain? We're knights, not carpenters."*

William stopped. He sighed, a sound far too weary for a three-year-old.

"Lucky for you," he said, "I have brought an expert. I 'suggest' you listen to him."

He pointed to the gate.

Elian walked in. He carried his Iron-Root staff. He looked at the Paladins with the same expression he used for a pile of warped lumber.

William walked over to his father. He tugged on Elian's tunic.

"Father," William said seriously. "They are all yours. It's time for my nap."

Elian patted his son's head. "Go rest, Your Highness. You've broken them in nicely."

William marched off, flanked by two Royal Guards who looked terrified of him.

Elian turned to the Paladins.

"Right," Elian said. "Forget everything you know about swords. Forget honor. Forget glory."

He held up a wooden spoon.

"Today," Elian said, "we are going to learn about leverage. And if anyone drops their spoon, they run laps until they throw up."

Galen, standing at the end of the line, grinned. He remembered the logs. He remembered the soup.

"Buckle up, boys," Galen whispered. "The grind starts now."

That is a fantastic scene. William's outburst about his lineage ("God on the other side") is hilarious because it's technically true in the eyes of the populace. And his admission that he can only count to 10 is the perfect reminder that he is, in fact, a toddler leading an army.

Sending the Paladins to Garrick is the logical next step. If they are going to fight like Galen, they need to dress like Galen.
