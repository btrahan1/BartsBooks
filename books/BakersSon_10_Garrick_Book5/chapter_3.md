## Chapter 3: The Mythril Makeover

Two weeks of spoon-drills and leverage training had transformed the Paladins. They moved better. They stood straighter. But they were still encased in heavy, clanking iron.

William strode into the middle of the training ground. "Line up! Do it! Do it now!"

The men scrambled.

"He's just so darn cute," a new recruit whispered.

William froze. The air temperature dropped. He turned slowly.

"Cute?" William asked, his voice low and dangerous. "Did you just call me 'cute'?"

He drew himself up to his full three feet of height.

"I have the bloodline of the greatest Queen this country has ever known on one side, and that of a *God* on the other! And you call me cute!"

Finn, watching from the sidelines, whispered to Elian, "So you're a God now?"

"According to the street merchants, yes," Elian sighed. "That makes him... Hercules, apparently. According to his mother."

William turned back to the troops. "Now... as I was saying... adequate. And adequate isn't going to cut it! Do you know how many undead you will be facing?"

He paused for dramatic effect.

"Well, me neither," William admitted. "I can only count to ten! And I know it's a lot more than that!"

"Pfft... he is doing Algebra!" Elian whispered to Finn.

William glared at his father, putting a finger to his lips. *Shush.*

He turned back to the men. "The problem is, you are all thinking like Paladins! You hide in your turtle shells and pray to your God to protect you!"

He tapped his own chest.

"That changes today. Report to Garrick the Tailor at once! You will be wearing our new uniforms. Like mine."

He gestured to his clothing. It was a miniature, perfect replica of Galen's Mythril-Weave suit. Light. Flexible. Shimmering.

"Do it! Do it now!"

The Paladins looked at each other. They looked at their heavy plate. They looked at Galen, who was doing a one-handed handstand in the corner.

They ran for the gate.

**[The Gilded Hem]**

Garrick looked at the ten sweaty, hulking men crowding his shop.

"Lord William sent us," the leader said. "For the... uniforms."

Garrick sighed. "Of course he did. Martha! Get the Mythril! And lock the door. We're going to need a lot of tea."

He looked at the men.

"First rule," Garrick said, snapping his tape measure. "If you rip the seams, you pay for them. Second rule... if you call me a 'seamstress', I will sew your mouth shut. Clear?"

"Clear," the Paladins chorused.

"Good. Now strip. Let's make you shiny."

This is a great transition. William has set the stage, Garrick has provided the gear, and now it's time for the real work. Valerius is the perfect instructor for this phase—he understands the transition from "heavy/rigid" to "fluid/deadly" better than anyone.

Galen acknowledging Valerius shows the mutual respect in the "Team."
