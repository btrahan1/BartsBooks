## Chapter 13: The Inspection

A week passed. A week in which Prince William established a new, unshakable routine in the Iron Citadel. Mornings were for "lessons" (in which William would lecture the King's ministers on topics ranging from crop rotation to the proper seasoning of wood for catapults). Afternoons were for "naps." And evenings were for "strategic planning."

King Theron found himself in a state of constant, low-grade bewilderment. His castle was being run more efficiently. His cooks were experimenting with spices (on William's orders). And his days were filled with unsolicited, but surprisingly sound, advice.

One morning, William marched into the throne room, a scroll of parchment tucked under his arm.

"Theron," he announced. "I need to inspect my city." He paused, tapping his chin. "I mean... *your* city." He gave a small, disarming smile. "Freudian slip, pardon me."

The King just stared. The boy had been reading his psychology texts.

"This castle is a fortress, not a home," William continued, unrolling the scroll. It was a crude but surprisingly accurate map of the capital he had drawn from memory. "It is structurally sound but spiritually bankrupt. I need to see the rest of the structure. The markets. The forges. The... ugh... slums."

"You want to go for a walk?" Theron asked, a dangerous edge to his voice. "You are a prisoner, Prince William. Not a tourist."

"I am a high-value asset," William corrected calmly. "An investment. And it would be a very poor return on your investment if your asset were to, say, be harmed while under your protection. Unhappy investors are so... volatile."

He rolled the map back up. "Now, I will need a guard. An escort, for my 'protection'. I believe the captain who escorted me here will do nicely. He's trainable."

He started for the door, then paused, turning back to the King with a look of grave seriousness.

"After all," Prince William said, his voice dropping, "we can't have your golden goose get kidnapped... again... now can we?"

The logic was inescapable. The threat was veiled but clear. If anything happened to William while he was out "inspecting," it would be the King's failure.

Theron rubbed the bridge of his nose, a gesture he had picked up from Elian without realizing it.

"Take a full squad," the King growled. "And be back by dinner. We are having mutton. It is... adequate."

William beamed. "Excellent! Forward progress!"

He marched out of the throne room, leaving King Theron to wonder how, exactly, he had lost control of his own capital city to a six-year-old who was now using his own men to conduct urban planning surveys.

The crossover is here! This is the perfect moment for Ren and the Grey Service to appear. Just when William is feeling overwhelmed by the sheer scale of the "project," his own personal intelligence agency shows up.

Ren's grin is a fantastic detail. She's not just a spy; she's a loyal operative who is absolutely delighted to see her tiny, terrifying commander.
