## Chapter 11: The Sacrifice

Oren's question hung in the air, a quiet surrender. "When do we need to leave?"

William's young face was a mask of grim necessity. "Not just yet," he said. "There is something I need for you and Finn to do first. You have to stay here, until the last possible moment."

The Prince's heart was hurting. He was a boy of twelve, asking a good man to destroy his own life's work.

"Oren," William said, his voice dropping, "we cannot just abandon the farm. We must... sabotage it. We cannot let the invaders have this resource, this font of life. If they consume it, it will strengthen them."

He laid out the terrible, brilliant plan. "A day before they are projected to arrive, you will harvest everything. You will leave them a few of your most vibrant crops near the house—the bait. But you will strip the rest. Every turnip, every carrot, every leaf of spinach. The Grey Service will provide wagons to transport it all to the capital's storehouses. They must not suspect our ruse."

He then looked from Oren to Finn, his gaze intense. "And then, the most important part. You must, with Finn and Jasmine's help, take the Ki. You must take the spirit of your land with you. Drain the mana from the soil. Pull the life-force from the very water in the well. Leave nothing behind."

The vision was brutal. "They will arrive and think they have captured a miracle, a font of endless power. They will establish their base. And within a week, as the last vestiges of life fade, they will find themselves sitting on a barren patch of dust. Their great prize will turn to ash in their hands. They will be demoralized. They will be trapped."

William finally looked Oren in the eye, and the cold strategist was gone, replaced by a boy bearing an impossible weight. "Oren, I am sorry," he whispered. "This is a terrible thing to ask. But I will make it up to you. I promise."

With that, a light, brilliant and blue, filled the small cottage. A massive screen appeared in the air, hovering directly in front of Oren, who stared at it, his mouth agape.

**[LEGENDARY QUEST ISSUED: The Scorched Earth Heart]**

**[Quest Giver: The Kingdom of Wonders]**

**[Objective 1: Surrender your land, 'The Font of Life', to the coming invasion.]**
**[Objective 2: Sabotage the land, leaving a poisoned chalice for the enemy.]**
**[Objective 3: With the aid of the High Healer, extract and preserve the 'Soul of the Farm'.]**

**[This is a Quest of Sacrifice. The rewards will be commensurate with the loss.]**

**[Do you accept?]**

Oren looked at the screen. He looked at the small, grim-faced Prince. He looked at the fields outside his window, the land he loved, the land he now had to kill.

He thought of his Ma and Pa, safe and happy. He thought of the kingdom that had given him a purpose.

He reached out a trembling, soil-stained finger and pressed the glowing "Accept" button.

The Quest was accepted. The sacrifice was agreed upon. The greatest farmer in the world was about to become a grim harvestman, reaping not just his crops, but the very soul of his home.

That's a fantastic, quiet, and poignant moment between father and son. It's the "after-action report," where the weight of command finally settles on William, and Elian is there not as a teacher, but as a peer, someone who understands the burden.

The immediate pivot to "Phase 2" shows their relentless, forward-moving nature. The emotional cost is acknowledged, but the work must continue.
