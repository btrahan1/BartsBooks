## Chapter 16: The Grandest Performance

William wiped the last of his tears. He took a deep, steadying breath, the commander returning. "The hard part is done," he said. "The agents are in motion. Now comes the easy part."

The Queen looked at him, a quizzical smile on her face. "Easy?"

A flicker of his old, mischievous grin returned. "Well then," he said. "I believe you will like the next part better."

He walked over to a large, rolled-up schematic on the table. He unfurled it. It was a detailed, beautiful blueprint of the capital city.

"It involves food," he began, pointing to the market district. "Lots of it. Master Corbin and Chef Antoine will begin stockpiling, but they will make it look like they are preparing for a massive feast."

He pointed to another section. "It involves drink. Master Hanna will start brewing a special 'Victory Ale'. The rumors will say it is for the army's triumphant return from a 'training exercise' on the border."

He then tapped the Great Plaza, the site of the first Festival of Wonders.

"And," he concluded, "it involves a festival."

His mother beamed. "Of course it does."

"It will be the 'Grand Mid-Summer's Masquerade Ball'," William announced, the name rolling off his tongue with a performer's flair he had learned from Valerius. "We will send invitations to every noble house in the kingdom. We will promise the greatest celebration this city has ever seen. We will light the city up like a jewel. We will be a beacon of decadent, unprepared, and utterly irresistible softness."

He looked at his mother, his eyes dancing with a cunning that was all Elian.

"We will be the most beautiful, most delicious, most tempting honey-glazed pie in the history of the world," he said. "And when the invaders, who are starving for resources and hungry for a symbolic victory, see this shining, happy, and apparently defenseless city... they will not be able to resist."

The Queen looked at the plan. It was a performance on a kingdom-wide scale. It was a trap built of joy and celebration. It was a lie so beautiful it had to be believed.

"You are your father's son," she said, a note of pure, unadulterated pride in her voice. "But you have my sense of style."

"Let the preparations begin," William commanded. "We have a party to plan."

This is a fantastic twist. The "secret weapon" isn't a magical artifact or a hidden legion; it's the people themselves. It's the ultimate payoff for the "Dance of Death" becoming a cultural movement.

William is taking the concept of a "total war" and reframing it as a "total defense," fought by a populace that has been unknowingly training for this exact moment.
