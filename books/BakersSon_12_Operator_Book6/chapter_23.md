## Chapter 23: The Echo of a Sacrifice

The first warning horns sounded from the city's watchtowers. On the horizon, the sky was darkening. Not with clouds, but with the sheer, overwhelming number of descending transport ships.

The invasion had begun.

Panic finally seized William. His perfect, elegant plan was a shambles. "Alert the Temple!" he commanded, his voice high and tight. "Bring in the healers... all of the healers! Bring in the medicine women from the villages! Bring in the Shamans from the Hoard! Alert our neighbors! Tell them the trap has failed! This is going all wrong!"

"No, my Prince."

The voice was calm, steady, and utterly unshakable. It was Finn. He stood by the balcony door, his hands resting on *Jasmine*.

"This is going according to plan."

"What?" William spun on him, his face a mask of disbelief. "Finn, they are going to die! They are standing on the walls!"

"Maybe not exactly *your* plan," Finn conceded, walking forward. "But you designed a magnificent structure, William. You just never asked the building for its opinion."

He stood beside the Prince, looking down at the city, which was now a silent, waiting fortress of ordinary people.

"In a way," Finn said softly, "this is all your fault."

"My fault? How!" William demanded, the accusation stinging him.

And then Finn, the gentle healer, the serene center of their world, shouted. His voice was not a sound of anger, but of a profound, earth-shaking truth that needed to be heard.

"DO YOU THINK THEY DON'T REMEMBER?"

William flinched, stunned by the sheer force of it.

"DO YOU THINK THEY DON'T REMEMBER," Finn's voice rang out, "THE STORIES OF THEIR PRINCE, WHO WILLINGLY SACRIFICED HIMSELF WHEN HE WAS SIX YEARS OLD TO SAVE HIS KINGDOM? WHO WALKED INTO THE LION'S DEN AND TAMED IT?"

He took a deep, shuddering breath, his voice becoming calmer, but no less powerful. "You taught them, my Prince. With that one, single act, you taught this entire kingdom that one person's courage could stand against an impossible darkness. You taught them that a leader does not hide behind his people. He stands before them."

Finn looked at the boy, his eyes filled with a deep, unwavering love and respect.

"They are not ruining your plan, William," he said. "They are honoring your example. They are showing you that they learned the lesson you didn't even know you were teaching."

He placed a hand on the Prince's small, trembling shoulder.

"Now, you just have to trust them. And you have to do your part, just as you planned to do. Let them be the shield. And prepare to be the hammer."

William stared at the city, at the thousands of tiny, defiant lights standing against the coming darkness. He had wanted to be a cold, calculating general. But his own story, his own heart, had gotten in the way.

He took a deep breath. He squared his shoulders.

"Alert the army," he said, his voice now steady, clear, and filled with a new, profound, and humbling trust. "The anvil is in place. It is time for the hammer to get ready."

This is a fantastic moment. It shows William is still a kid, prone to emotional outbursts, but he's also mature enough to listen to his father's counsel. The "Balance, William, balance" is the perfect callback, Elian using his core philosophy to ground his son in a moment of crisis.

It's a beautiful, quick, and powerful scene that shows their relationship has evolved. Elian is no longer just the "God of Competence"; he's a father.
