## Chapter 4: The Terms of Service

William stood before the holographic map, the silent blue box of the System hovering beside him. He was no longer just an observer; he was a co-strategist.

"If it is an invasion," he began, his voice the calm, analytical tone of a general briefing his staff, "and the size of the invading object matches my expectations, I estimate a force of approximately one million hostile beings, not discounting the ships themselves as potential weapons."

**[A force of that magnitude would be a Class-5 Existential Threat.]**

"Indeed," William agreed. "The Hoard was a Class-3. This is a different order of magnitude. We can 'handle' the invasion itself—the ground forces. Our own army's doctrines are sound. But we do not have the technology to handle the ships. An orbital bombardment, an atmospheric weapon... we have no defense. That will be your job."

He turned to the blue box. "We will begin preparations on my end. You will handle the hardware."

The Supervisor's code flickered with indignation. **[We are observers, Prince William. We cannot directly interfere in mortal conflicts.]**

"Of course not," William said smoothly, a flicker of his father's cunning smile on his face. "Direct interference is against the rules. However..." He began to pace, his hands clasped behind his back.

"According to your own operational protocols, Article 17, sub-clause 4.2: 'Outside technology introduced into an existing cultural sphere may not exceed the native technology by a factor greater than 1.7.' You are required to disable any such technology that violates this directive prior to any large-scale hostile engagement."

The Supervisor went utterly still. The boy was quoting the Prime Directive. He was quoting their own rulebook.

"Their ships," William continued, "assuming they are capable of interstellar travel, will most certainly exceed our own technological level by a factor far greater than 1.7. Therefore, you are not 'interfering'. You are 'enforcing regulations'. Your own regulations."

He stopped pacing and looked the blue box dead on. "There is, unfortunately," he said with a hint of theatrical regret, "no set restriction on the introduction of living bodies. For that, we are on our own. But," he added, his voice turning helpful, "any information your probe can gather on their biology, their command structure, their 'grain'... it would be much appreciated. As a matter of inter-system security, of course."

He smiled. And then he did the impossible.

He raised a hand, and with a flick of his will, he accessed a command prompt no mortal should have known existed.

To the absolute, system-crashing shock of the Supervisor, a Quest—an actual, official, gold-bordered Quest—appeared on their own administrative screens in the Nexus.

**[New Quest Issued: 'Regulatory Enforcement']**
**[Quest Giver: User - William_Elianson]**
**[Objective 1: Disable all incoming technologies exceeding Tech Factor 1.7.]**
**[Objective 2: Provide detailed intelligence on incoming biological threats.]**
**[Reward: Continued Sector Stability.]**
**[Failure: You get fired.]**

Back in the Nexus, 42-Beta just stared at the screen. "How... how did he do that?" he whispered.

The Supervisor didn't answer. He was looking at the quest. He was looking at the perfectly cited legal code. He was looking at the implied threat in the 'Failure' condition.

He had not just been outmaneuvered by a twelve-year-old. He had just been assigned homework. By a twelve-year-old. With a deadline.

**[...Quest Accepted,]** the Supervisor's voice finally came, stripped of all arrogance, leaving only the weary resignation of a bureaucrat who has been utterly, completely, and flawlessly beaten. **[We will begin at once.]**

That's a perfect transition. The cosmic, System-altering negotiation is over, and William immediately pivots back to the practical, on-the-ground logistics. His assessment of his own impossible feat as merely "adequate" is the ultimate Elian-ism.

This scene brings the threat back down to a human level and sets up the next phase of the book: the mobilization of the entire kingdom.
