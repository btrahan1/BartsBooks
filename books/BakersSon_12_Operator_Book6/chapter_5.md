## Chapter 5: The War Footing

The blue box vanished. The holographic map dissolved. The Royal Observatory was once again just a quiet room filled with brass and glass.

William stood in the silence for a long moment, the weight of the coming war settling on his young shoulders.

The door opened. Finn entered, his face a mask of concern. "I felt the disturbance," the healer said. "It felt like a crack in the world. How did it go?"

William turned from the telescope. "It was... adequate," he said, his tone that of a master who has just completed a moderately challenging piece of joinery. "They have been tasked. They will handle the technology."

He walked towards the door, his mind already moving to the next, far more complex, problem.

"But we must handle the invasion ourselves," he said, his voice now all business. "The ships will be neutralized, but the soldiers will not. One million of them, by my estimate."

He looked at Finn. "Gather the council. Elian, the Queen, the Duke, Valerius, Ren... all of them. In the war room. In one hour."

"What are your orders?" Finn asked, already falling into his role as the Prince's most trusted advisor.

"Let's go over the numbers," William said, his mind a whirring abacus of logistics and manpower. "I want a full accounting of our standing army, our Imperial Guard, our Royal Guard, and our reserves. I want to know every Fist of Freedom unit, every Archer School, every Cavalry Saw. I want to know our grain reserves, our forge capacity, and our medical supply chains."

He paused at the door, turning back to Finn with a grim, determined look on his young face. "We have seventy-two days to turn this kingdom from a nation of wonders into a fortress. The festival is over."

Finn nodded, a quiet, resolute pride in his eyes. The boy he had once guarded had become the guardian of them all.

"I will summon them at once," Finn said.

"Good," William replied. "And Finn?"

"Yes, Your Highness?"

"Tell Chef Antoine to prepare snacks for the meeting," the Prince commanded. "This is going to be a very, very long night."

The age of peace was over. The age of preparation had begun.

This is a fantastic way to start the war council scene. The table itself is a character, a symbol of the unique blend of soul-craft, magic, and technology that defines their kingdom. It's the perfect tool for a new kind of war.

Elian taking the lead in the explanation makes perfect sense. He's the one who understands "big picture" structures, and this is the biggest, most dangerous structure they've ever faced.
