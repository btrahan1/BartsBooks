## Epilogue 3: The Audit of Wonders

**[System Administration Nexus - Sector 7]**

Monitor 42-Beta stared at the main scrying screen, his coded form practically vibrating. The Supervisor stood beside him, utterly still, his own form a statue of silent, processing data.

"He did it," 42-Beta finally whispered, his voice filled with a reverence that bordered on religious terror. "I have no idea how. But he did it. The invasion is routed. The fleet is neutered. The planet is secure."

The Supervisor didn't speak. He just pointed a shimmering finger at the final, flashing notification on his own console.

**[PLANETARY THREAT LEVEL: NEUTRALIZED]**
**[USER: WILLIAM_ELIANSON -> QUEST: 'DEFEND THE REALM' -> STATUS: COMPLETE (FLAWLESS VICTORY)]**

"The boy," the Supervisor murmured, "did not just win a battle. He conducted a planet-wide hostile takeover of an invading army and then negotiated a system-wide technological downgrade for an entire civilization." He shook his head. "There is no precedent for this. What kind of rewards can we even give for something like this?"

42-Beta frantically scrolled through the reward tables. "+10 Sword of God-Slaying? A legendary artifact? A title?" He looked at the Supervisor. "Nothing seems... adequate."

The Supervisor was silent for a long moment, staring at the scrying pool, which now showed the citizens of the capital and the soldiers of the army celebrating together, sharing food and song.

"He did not win alone," the Supervisor said softly. "He won with an army of citizens. He won with the soul of his people." He turned to 42-Beta. "A reward for the Prince is insufficient. It does not fit the grain of the victory."

A new, radical, and completely unprecedented idea began to form in his administrative mind.

"We will bless the nation," the Supervisor declared.

42-Beta's code almost crashed. "What? Sir? Can we do that? Can we bless an entire nation? That's a Tier-1 Divine Intervention! It's against every protocol!"

"The protocols were written for players who kill goblins for XP," the Supervisor countered, a strange, Elian-like fire in his own data-stream. "This user... this entire kingdom... they operate on a different level. They have demonstrated a 'Societal Cohesion' stat that is off the charts. The victory was a function of the entire collective."

He began to issue commands, his fingers flying over a console no one had touched in eons. "Therefore, the reward will be distributed to the collective. We are not intervening. We are rewarding a successful, large-scale, cooperative quest."

He hit the final command.

**[INITIATING: 'THE WONDROUS BOON']**
**[TARGET: THE KINGDOM OF WONDERS (ALL CITIZENS)]**
**[EFFECT: +1 to primary crafting skill proficiency for all artisans.]**
**[EFFECT: Increased crop yield and vitality by 15% kingdom-wide.]**
**[EFFECT: A subtle, passive 'Hope' buff applied to the entire populace.]**
**[DURATION: PERMANENT]**

Across the kingdom, a smith's hammer suddenly felt lighter. A baker's dough rose a little higher. A farmer's sprouting seed glowed with a faint, golden light. The people felt a quiet, inexplicable warmth in their hearts, a sense that their world, which had been on the brink, was now fundamentally, and wonderfully, sound.

42-Beta stared at the logs, at the system-wide blessing that was now a permanent part of the world's code.

"Sir," he whispered. "What have you done?"

The Supervisor looked at the happy, thriving, and now divinely-buffed kingdom. He thought of the grumpy woodworker, the slick tailor, the humble farmer, the brave healer, the bird-boned paladin, and the terrifying, brilliant little boy who had taught them all a new way to see the world.

"I have," the Supervisor said with a small, satisfied smile, "filed an adequate report."

The age of wonders had officially been logged.
