## Chapter 10: The Talk

That night, Bran sat alone in his small room.

He stared at the air in front of him.

"Alright," Bran said. "Show yourself."

Nothing happened.

"I know you're there," Bran said, crossing his arms. "The blue boxes. The weird text. You think I can't read? I went to school."

He waited. Still nothing.

Bran sighed. He stood up and grabbed his rolling pin. "Okay. I guess I'll just break this table then. And maybe the wall. And maybe the support beam for the roof. Accidents happen when I get frustrated."

A blue box flickered into existence instantly.

**[USER ALERT]**
**[Please do not damage the structural integrity of the Dormitory.]**
**[System is listening.]**

Bran sat back down, satisfied. "Good. We need to talk."

**[Query: User wishes to report a bug?]**

"I want to know why you're lying to me," Bran said. He pointed at the box. "You keep trying to give me combat classes. 'Brawler'. 'Berserker'. 'Tank'. I don't want them."

**[Correction: User possesses physical attributes exceeding the parameters of the [Baker] class. Reassignment is necessary for System stability.]**

"My attributes are fine," Bran argued. "I'm strong because I work. I'm fast because I don't like burning the cookies. Why can't I just be a really, really good Baker?"

**[Analysis: A [Baker] cannot survive a Level 40 Dungeon.]**

"I don't *want* to go to a Level 40 Dungeon! I want to stay in the kitchen!"

The blue box seemed to pause. It flickered, processing this strange lack of ambition.

**[Proposal: System detects High Compatibility with [Crafting] path. However, external threats (The Tournament) are imminent. Survival probability: 12%.]**

Bran leaned forward. "So help me. But help me *my* way. Stop trying to make me a warrior. Give me tools I can use."

The System was silent for a long time. The Monitor in Sector 7 was likely sweating. Finally, the text changed.

**[Compromise Accepted.]**
**[System will cease Combat Class prompts.]**
**[System will optimize Culinary Skills for "High-Stress Environments".]**
**[New Skill Tree Unlocked: The Combat Chef.]**

"No," Bran corrected. "Not Combat Chef. Just... *Battle Baker*."

**[...Processing...]**
**[Class Updated: Battle Baker (Unique).]**
**[Passive Skill Added: Misunderstanding.]**
**[Description: Enemies will perceive your cooking tools as legendary weapons. Your cooking actions will be interpreted as martial arts. Charisma +20 when holding bread.]**

Bran grinned. "Now we're cooking."
