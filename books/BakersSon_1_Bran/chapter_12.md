## Chapter 11: The Amulet

The next morning, Bran found a small package outside his door. There was no note, but the paper smelled of Elder Feng’s sandalwood incense.

Inside was a simple bronze amulet on a leather cord.

Bran picked it up. A new System box appeared.

**[Item: Amulet of the Common Man.]**
**[Rank: Rare.]**
**[Function: Masks the wearer's true stats. Projects a "False Status" to observers based on their expectations.]**

Bran put it on. He felt a cool wave wash over him.

He walked out into the courtyard. Jin and Mei were waiting for him.

Jin squinted at him. He pulled out a small crystal—a scouter device used by disciples to gauge power levels.

"Whoa," Jin said. "Bran, look."

He showed Bran the crystal.

**Name: Bran**
**Rank: Body Tempering (Stage 2)**
**Class: Heavy Laborer**
**Strength: 15 (High for a mortal)**
**Threat Level: Low**

"It worked," Bran said.

"What worked?" Mei asked.

"The camouflage," Bran said, tucking the amulet under his shirt. "Before, people looked at me and saw a question mark. Or they saw nothing and got scared. Now... they see what they expect to see. A strong guy who carries boxes."

"Why would you want to look weaker?" Jin asked, confused. "Everyone here tries to look stronger."

"Because," Bran said, picking up a crate of vegetables with one hand—the amulet glowing faintly as it masked the impossible ease of the lift. "*'The loaf that rises too fast creates a hollow crust.'* If they think I'm just a strong laborer, they won't try to fight me. They'll underestimate me."

"And when the Tournament starts?" Mei asked worriedly.

Bran looked at the mountain peak where the arena was being built.

"When the Tournament starts," Bran said, patting his rolling pin, "I'll take the amulet off."

He smiled, but it wasn't his usual sunny smile. It was the smile of a man who was about to put a very difficult loaf into a very hot oven.

"And then we'll see who gets burned."

This is the natural progression. Bran is accepting the reality of the situation (he has to fight), but he is maintaining his core identity: **Business**. He isn't fighting for honor; he's fighting for a paycheck and the right equipment.

The dialogue between him and Feng during the exercises is a great vehicle for exposition that doesn't feel like an info-dump because they are physically active while talking.
