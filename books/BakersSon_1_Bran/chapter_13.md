## Chapter 12: The Uniform

The sun was barely up, painting the clouds in hues of bruised purple and gold.

*Sweep. Block. Strike.*

Bran moved in sync with Elder Feng. His form had improved. He was no longer just mimicking; he was feeling the flow. He still visualized it as spreading flour, but the result was the same: fluid, efficient power.

"About this tournament..." Bran said, not breaking the rhythm of the breathing exercise. "Tell me about it, if you would, Sect Leader."

Feng transitioned into *'The Willow Bends'*. "It is the Tri-Annual Sect Gathering. The Outer Disciples compete for spots in the Inner Court. The Inner Disciples compete for resources and rank. It is violent. It is necessary."

"And me?" Bran asked, mirroring the bend. "I'm not a disciple. I'm kitchen staff."

"You would be entered as a 'Sponsored Independent'," Feng explained. "It is rare, but allowed. Usually, it is for visiting mercenaries or children of nobility. You will represent my personal interest."

"Your personal interest," Bran repeated. "Meaning, if I win, you look good. If I lose, I'm just a foolish baker who got crushed."

"Precisely." Feng didn't sugarcoat it. "However, the rewards for the Independent are theirs to keep. Spirit Stones. Rare artifacts. And, as I promised, access to the Archives."

Bran stopped moving. He stood up straight, wiping his hands on a towel.

"We need to talk about my pay," Bran said.

Feng stopped mid-pose. He lowered his arms slowly. "We have a contract, Bran."

"The contract is for baking," Bran said firmly. "Fighting in a tournament? That's 'Hazard Pay'. That's 'Overtime'. My Dad needs a retirement fund. The pill you gave him bought him time, but it didn't buy him comfort. I want him to have enough gold to hire two assistants so he never has to lift a flour sack again."

Feng turned to face him. "You are mercenary for a man who claims to have no ambition."

"I have ambition," Bran corrected. "I have the ambition to be a good son. *'A full purse makes for a light heart,'* as Dad says."

"Very well," Feng nodded. "If you reach the semi-finals, I will send five hundred gold coins to Riverwood. If you win... I will grant your father protection under the Sect's banner. No tax collector or bandit will ever touch the *Warm Hearth Bakery* again."

Bran nodded. That was security. That was worth bleeding for.

"One more thing," Bran added. "Expenses."

"Expenses?"

"Culinary ones. If I'm going to fight, I'm doing it my way. I need ingredients. High-grade ones. Star-Dust Sugar, Dragon-Root Ginger, those weird glowing mushrooms from the dank caves."

"Approved," Feng waved a hand. "Take what you need from the stores."

"And," Bran continued, looking down at his stained, oversized kitchen rags. "I need to dress the part. If I am to compete, I'm not going out there looking like a sack of laundry. Professionalism is key. *'A clean apron is a clean mind.'*"

Feng looked Bran up and down. The boy was massive. His current clothes were practically bursting at the seams.

"There is an Armorer in the crafting district," Feng said. "Tell him I sent you. Tell him... tell him to make you something that breathes."
