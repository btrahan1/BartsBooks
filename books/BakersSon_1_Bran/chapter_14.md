## Chapter 13: The Armorer

The Armorer was a dwarf named Korg. He didn't look like the dwarves in stories; he was clean-shaven and wore spectacles. He was hammering a piece of breastplate that glowed red hot.

Bran walked into the shop. The heat was intense. Bran smiled. It felt like home.

"Shop's closed," Korg grunted, not looking up. "Backlog is three months."

"Elder Feng sent me," Bran said.

Korg stopped hammering. He looked up. He adjusted his spectacles.

"The Baker," Korg said. "I've heard the whispers. You're the one who fed the cat."

"He likes honey," Bran shrugged. "I need a uniform."

"Armor?" Korg asked, eyeing Bran's frame. "I have some surplus plate mail that might fit. Heavy stuff. Adamantite alloy."

"No," Bran shook his head. "No plate. It restricts the shoulders. I can't knead in plate. And no chainmail, the flour gets stuck in the links. It's a nightmare to clean."

Korg frowned. "Leather?"

"Too stiff. I need mobility. I need heat resistance. And I need pockets. Big pockets."

Korg put down his hammer. He looked intrigued. "So, you want battle-armor that feels like chef's whites?"

"Exactly. But tough. Like... *really* tough."

Korg rubbed his chin. "I have a bolt of Spider-Silk woven with Salamander Hide. It's white. Fireproof up to magma temperatures. Cuts like steel, but moves like linen."

"Perfect," Bran said.

"And for defense?"

Bran placed his rolling pin on the anvil. *THUD.*

"I have this," Bran said. "But it's wood. Iron-wood, but still wood. It's getting charred."

Korg picked up the rolling pin. He whistled. "This thing has density. You've been channeling unconscious Qi into this for years, boy. It's practically a Spirit Weapon already."

"Can you reinforce it?"

Korg grinned. A manic, craftsman grin.

"Reinforce it? Boy, I'm going to band it with Star-Metal and core it with a Gravity Stone. It'll weigh a ton, but it'll flatten a tank."

Two days later, Bran stood before the mirror in the armory.

He looked transformed.

He wore a double-breasted jacket made of the white Salamander-Silk. It was pristine, gleaming softly in the light. It buttoned up the side with brass clasps. The sleeves were rolled up, revealing his massive forearms.

He wore black trousers made of a flexible, cut-resistant weave, tucked into heavy boots with steel toes (and arch support).

Around his waist was a utility belt. It didn't hold daggers or potions. It held:
*   A pouch of high-grade flour.
*   A shaker of salt.
*   A bundle of dried herbs.
*   A small jar of starter culture.
*   A jagged obsidian knife.

And strapped to his back, in a custom leather sheath, was **The Roller**.

It was beautiful. Dark, polished wood banded with gleaming silver metal. It pulsed with a heavy, gravitational aura.

Bran adjusted his collar. He put on a tall, white hat—not a floppy toque, but a structured, military-style cap.

He looked like a General. A General of the Oven.

Korg nodded in approval. "You look sharp, kid. Sharp enough to cut glass."

Bran flexed his shoulders. The fabric moved with him, silent and smooth.

"It feels good," Bran said. He looked at Korg. "How much?"

"Put it on Feng's tab," Korg laughed. "I just want to see the look on the High Priest's face when a Chef walks into the arena."

Bran turned to the door. He touched the Amulet of the Common Man under his jacket. It was still humming, hiding his power.

"Time to go to work," Bran said. "The oven is preheated."

This is the perfect calm before the storm. Bran's influence is spreading. He hasn't just improved the food; he has improved the *morale* and the *methodology* of the entire Outer Sect. His "Dao of Baking"—patience, preparation, rhythm—is infecting everyone else.

Let’s showcase this transformation in **Chapter 14: The Well-Oiled Kitchen**.

It serves two purposes:
1.  It solidifies Bran’s leadership. He isn't just a brute; he's a Manager.
2.  It sets the stage for the Outer Disciples (specifically Mei and Jin) to surprise everyone at the tournament, thanks to Bran’s influence.
