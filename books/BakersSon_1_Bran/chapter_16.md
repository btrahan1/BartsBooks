## Chapter 15: The Opening Ceremony

The Arena of the Clouds was a massive coliseum carved into the flat summit of the mountain. Banners of every color snapped in the wind, representing the different Halls of the Sect.

Thousands of disciples filled the stone bleachers. The noise was deafening—drums, horns, and the roar of the crowd.

In the center of the arena, the Sect Patriarch—an ancient man floating cross-legged in mid-air—was giving a speech about "Honor" and "Destiny."

Nobody was listening to him.

They were all looking at the north entrance tunnel.

"Get that cart moving! Pivot! Pivot!"

Bran came storming out of the tunnel, but not with a weapon. He was pushing a customized, reinforced food cart with steam whistling out of its brass vents. He was flanked by Mei, Jin, and a squad of kitchen disciples carrying trays.

Bran was wearing his battle uniform—the gleaming white Salamander-Silk jacket, the black trousers, the military cap. The massive, iron-wood rolling pin was strapped to his back.

He looked impressive. Until he started yelling about condiments.

"Sauce station on the left! Hydration station on the right!" Bran barked, parking the cart near the competitor’s waiting area. "And keep the Spicy-Buns away from the Water-Mages, it messes with their chi flow!"

The crowd murmured.

*"Who is that?"*
*"Is that the Chef?"*
*"Why is he on the field? Is he lost?"*
*"Wait, look at his arm band. He's a registered combatant."*

A group of Inner Disciples from the Hall of Swords pointed and laughed.

"Look at the clown," one sneered. "He brought snacks to a death match."

Bran ignored them. He was busy setting up a portable grill. *Sssssss.* The smell of seared meat and caramelized onions began to waft over the arena, overpowering the smell of ozone and fear.

"Get your energy here!" Bran announced, his voice projecting clearly. "Don't fight on an empty stomach! *'A hungry warrior makes sloppy mistakes!'*"

Curiosity got the better of them. A few Outer Disciples drifted over. Then a few more. Bran handed out the "Energy-Bars" and savory pies with efficient speed.

"Thanks, Bran," a nervous fighter said, taking a pie. "I was shaking. I think my blood sugar was low."

"Eat up," Bran said, slapping him on the back. "Chew slowly. Breathe."

***

Up in the VIP box, Elder Feng sat next to the other Elders.

"Feng," the Elder of the Sword Hall grumbled. "Your... *pet* is making a mockery of the proceedings. He is selling pastries."

"He is ensuring the athletes are at peak performance," Feng said calmly, sipping his tea. "It is a service."

"He is a distraction," the Sword Elder scoffed. He looked down at Bran through a viewing crystal. "Let us see what this 'prodigy' of yours is made of."

He scanned Bran with the crystal.

**[Scan Result]**
**Name: Bran**
**Rank: Body Tempering (Stage 3)**
**Class: Combat Cook**
**Strength: 18**
**Agility: 12**
**Note: Carrying Heavy Load.**

"Stage 3?" The Sword Elder laughed. "He is barely above a novice! My disciples are in the Foundation Establishment realm! One strike and your baker will be paste."

Feng didn't correct him. He knew the bracelet was doing its job. It showed Bran as "strong for a peasant," but laughably weak for a cultivator.

"We shall see," Feng said. "The brackets have been posted."

***

Down on the field, a gong sounded. The Patriarch finished his speech. The crowd roared.

A massive illusion board lit up in the sky, showing the matchups.

Bran looked up, shielding his eyes from the sun. He scanned the names.

**Match 12:**
**Li (Hall of Swords - Foundation Establishment) vs. Bran (Kitchen Staff - Independent)**

Bran sighed. "Of course. It had to be him."

"Bran!"

He turned. It was Disciple Li.

Li looked different. He was wearing gleaming blue armor. He held a new sword—one that pulsed with angry, jagged energy. His eyes were bloodshot. He looked like he hadn't slept; he looked like a man obsessed with revenge.

"I saw the board," Li hissed, walking up to the food cart. He kicked a wheel, but the reinforced cart didn't budge. "Fate has delivered you to me, Baker."

Bran flipped a burger on the grill. "Hello, Li. You look tired. Bags under your eyes. *'Rest is the yeast of the body,'* you know."

"Shut up!" Li drew his sword. The air around him crackled. "No more tricks. No more lucky shots. Today, I will peel you like one of your turnips. I will expose you for the fraud you are."

Bran looked at the sword. He looked at Li’s stance.

"Your stance is still too heavy on the heels," Bran critiqued. "And you're holding your breath again."

"I will kill you!" Li screamed.

"Save it for the ring," Bran said, pointing a spatula at him. "I'm working right now."

Li growled and stormed off toward the preparation benches.

Bran watched him go. He felt a tug on his sleeve.

It was Mei. She looked terrified.

"Bran... Li is Foundation Establishment now. He broke through last week. That means he can project Qi. He can hit you from a distance."

"Distance, huh?" Bran mused. He reached back and patted the handle of his rolling pin.

"Well," Bran said, sliding a perfectly cooked patty onto a bun. "My Daddy always said, *'If the oven is too hot, use a longer peel.'*"

He handed the burger to Mei.

"Hold the fort, Mei. I have a feeling my lunch break is going to be... vigorous."

This is a sophisticated approach to the fight scene. Instead of a simple "OP stomp," we get a character study. Bran is teaching Li *during the fight*. He is treating the duel like he treats a struggling apprentice in the kitchen: with patience, correction, and firmness.

This changes the dynamic from "Good vs. Evil" to "Master vs. Student," which is infinitely more interesting.

Here is **Chapter 16: The Lesson of the Loaf.**
