## Chapter 17: The Proof is in the Pudding

While Bran was busy serving post-match snacks to a very confused Disciple Li, the Junior Brackets were underway in the side arenas.

**Arena B: Jin vs. The Iron-Skin Monk**

Jin adjusted his glasses. They were fogging up.

Opposite him stood a disciple from the Hall of Endurance. The boy wasn't wearing a shirt, and his skin had a metallic, gray sheen. He looked like a statue come to life.

"Surrender, beanpole," the Monk grunted. "I cannot be hurt. I have trained my skin against waterfalls and rockslides."

Jin swallowed. He was terrified. But then, he felt a warmth in his stomach.

It was the **"Dense-Nut Energy Bar"** Bran had given him an hour ago.

*Effect: +20% Stamina Regeneration. +10% Focus. Trait: "Stubbornness of the Oat."*

Jin took a deep breath. He remembered Bran's advice. *'Keep stirring him until he gets tired.'*

The Monk charged. He swung a fist like a sledgehammer.

Jin dodged. He didn't block—he couldn't block that—he slipped past it like a ladle through broth.

The Monk swung again. And again.

Jin kept moving. He ran circles around the slow, heavy opponent.

Ten minutes passed.
Twenty minutes passed.

The crowd was getting bored. "Hit him!" someone shouted.

The Monk was panting. His metallic skin was starting to fade, his Qi draining from maintaining the armor spell.

"Stop... moving..." the Monk wheezed.

Jin wasn't tired. The Energy Bar was burning in his gut like a coal fire, feeding him steady, relentless energy. He felt like he could run for days.

"Now," Jin whispered.

The Monk threw a lazy, exhausted punch. Jin stepped in. He didn't use a sword. He used a wooden practice staff.

He visualized the giant cauldron in the kitchen. The thick, resistant stew.

*Stir. Stir. CLACK.*

Jin swept the Monk's legs. The Monk, too tired to brace himself, fell over with a heavy *THUD*.

Jin placed the tip of his staff on the Monk's throat.

"Yield," Jin said, his voice steady.

The Monk looked up at the ceiling, gasping for air. "I... yield. How... do you have... so much energy?"

Jin smiled, pushing his glasses up. "Oats. Lots of oats."

***

**Arena C: Mei vs. The Illusionist**

Mei was in trouble.

Her opponent, a girl from the Hall of Mirrors, had cast a spell that created five copies of herself. They were circling Mei, giggling.

"Which one is real?" the illusions taunted. "Guess wrong and you lose!"

Mei spun around, clutching her twin daggers. She was dizzy. Her eyes darted back and forth. Panic was setting in.

Then, she burped.

It tasted like peppermint.

She had eaten one of Bran's **"Clear-Mind Mints"** before the match.

*Effect: +15% Perception. Clears minor mental debuffs. Trait: "Cool Head."*

The minty freshness hit her sinuses. It was sharp. Cold. It cut through the panic like a knife through hot butter.

Mei closed her eyes.

She remembered the kitchen. The chaos. The noise. Bran teaching her to ignore the yelling and focus on the whisk.

*'Shoulders down. Wrist loose.'*

She took a deep breath through her nose.

She couldn't *see* the real girl, but the mint heightened her other senses. She could smell.

Four of the illusions smelled like ozone and magic.
One of them... smelled like lavender perfume and sweat.

"There," Mei whispered.

She didn't open her eyes. She lunged to her left, driving her blunt practice dagger forward.

The illusion gasped—a real gasp.

The spell shattered. The four copies vanished. The real girl stood there, Mei’s dagger pressed against her ribs.

"How?" the Illusionist stammered. "My visual distortion is perfect!"

"You smell nice," Mei said, opening her eyes and grinning. "But you can't hide from a sous-chef."

***

**The Aftermath**

Bran was packing up the grill when Mei and Jin came running up to the cart. They were sweaty, bruised, and beaming.

"I won!" Jin shouted, waving his staff. "He couldn't hit me! I outlasted an Iron-Skin!"

"I found her!" Mei cheered. "I closed my eyes and I just *knew*!"

Bran looked at them. He saw the pride in their faces. He felt a lump in his throat that had nothing to do with bread.

"Good job," Bran said, handing them fresh towels. "I knew you could do it. *'The ingredients were always good; they just needed the right heat.'*

"We're in the semi-finals," Jin said, breathless. "Bran, we're actually going to place!"

"Don't get cocky," Bran warned, though he was smiling. "Semi-finals means harder opponents. Harder crusts to crack."

He looked up at the scoreboard.

**Quarter-Finals Bracket:**
**Bran vs. The Golem of the Earth Hall.**

Bran groaned. "A Golem? Really? Why is everything I fight made of rock or metal?"

"Maybe the System thinks you need to tenderize more meat?" Mei suggested cheekily.

Bran laughed. He grabbed his rolling pin.

"Well," Bran said. "If it's rock, I guess I'm making crackers."

**[System Notification - Private Channel]**

**Monitor 42-Beta:** "Sir. The Baker's buffs are affecting the other brackets. The 'Zone of Happiness' is spreading. The underdog win rate has increased by 300%."

**Supervisor:** "Send the Golem. A Level 40 Earth Elemental. Immune to 'Misunderstanding'. Immune to 'Charisma'. It has no stomach, so he can't feed it. Let's see him bake his way out of this."

**Monitor 42-Beta:** "Sending the Golem. Godspeed, Bread Boy."

This is powerful. By calling out the System *during* the event, Bran strips away the "game" aspect and makes it personal. He knows he's being targeted, and his reaction isn't fear—it's the annoyance of a contractor dealing with a client who keeps changing the specs mid-job.

It also sets up a fantastic mechanic: **Malicious Compliance.** If the System is going to cheat by sending a monster he can't "feed" or "charm," Bran is going to use his "Battle Baker" class in the most literal, destructive way possible to prove a point.

Here is **Chapter 18: The Cracker**.
