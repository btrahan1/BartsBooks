## Chapter 18: The Cracker

The Golem was not a disciple in a suit. It was ten feet of animated granite, runic carvings glowing magma-orange on its chest. It had no face, just a jagged slit where a mouth should be.

It didn't breathe. It didn't have a stance. It just radiated heavy, crushing gravity.

The crowd was silent. This was a summon from the High Elders—a test meant for Elite Disciples, not a cook.

Bran stood across from the monstrosity. He adjusted his white cap. He looked up at the empty air above the arena.

"System," Bran said, his voice low but cutting. "You are cheating."

The air shimmered. Only Bran could see the text box that flickered into existence.

**[System Alert: Anomaly Detected. Threat Level: High. Proceed with Elimination.]**

"We had a deal," Bran continued, ignoring the threat. "I stay in the kitchen, I play your game, and you leave me alone. You have broken it."

The Golem took a step forward. The ground shook. *THOOM.*

Bran unslung **The Roller**. The heavy iron-wood and star-metal weapon hit his palm with a reassuring slap.

"We will talk later," Bran promised the empty air. "And I'm bringing the receipt."

The Golem roared—a sound like grinding tectonic plates—and charged.

It didn't use martial arts. It used mass. It swung a fist the size of a beer keg aimed directly at Bran’s head.

Bran didn't deflect this time. You don't deflect a landslide.

He rolled.

Bran dove under the swing, his white coat blurring. He came up behind the Golem.

"No stomach," Bran analyzed instantly. "No hunger. No negotiation."

He tapped the Golem’s leg with his rolling pin. *Clink.* Solid stone.

"Too hard to knead," Bran muttered. "So we have to break the structure."

The Golem spun around with surprising speed, bringing both fists down in a hammer smash.

Bran leaped back. The shockwave knocked him off balance, sending dust flying into his eyes.

"Dirty kitchen," Bran coughed.

He needed a strategy. He couldn't charm it. He couldn't feed it. He had to *cook* it.

He looked at the Golem's glowing chest runes. Heat. It was powered by a core of magical heat.

"Heat expands," Bran whispered. "Stone is rigid. If you heat a stone too fast..."

Bran reached into his utility belt. He didn't pull out a weapon. He pulled out a flask.

**[Item: Concentrated Moon-Water.]**
**[Properties: Extremely Cold. Used for chilling dough instantly.]**

The Golem charged again.

Bran stood his ground. He waited.

"Come on, you big crouton," Bran taunted.

The Golem swung.

Bran didn't dodge. He activated **[Pan Block]**.

He held up his rolling pin horizontally. The stone fist connected.

*CRUNCH.*

Bran’s boots plowed furrows into the arena floor as he was driven backward ten feet. His arms screamed. The impact was like stopping a runaway wagon. But the pin held.

The Golem was momentarily stunned by the resistance. It leaned in, its chest runes glowing brighter as it prepared a magma blast.

"Now," Bran grunted.

He uncorked the flask with his thumb and splashed the Moon-Water directly onto the Golem's glowing, super-heated chest.

*HISSSSSSSSSSSS!*

A cloud of steam exploded outward.

Thermal shock is a law of physics, and physics applies even to magic rocks.

The super-heated stone chest met the freezing water. The rapid contraction was violent.

*CRAAAACK.*

A hairline fracture appeared on the Golem’s chest.

The Golem staggered back, letting out a sound of confused distress.

"Structure compromised," Bran said, spinning his rolling pin. "Time to flatten."

Bran charged.

He didn't swing wildly. He used the **[Battle Baker]** skill: **[Perussive Maintenance]**.

He aimed for the crack.

*WHAM.*

The rolling pin connected with the sound of a thunderclap. The crack widened.

*WHAM.*

"You!" *WHAM.* "Are!" *WHAM.* "Lumpy!"

The Golem tried to swipe at him, but its movements were jerky now, its core unstable. Bran danced around the stone limbs, a white whirlwind of destruction.

"My Daddy always said!" Bran shouted, winding up for a final strike. "*'If the cracker is too hard, smash it into crumbs!'*"

He jumped. He brought **The Roller** down with both hands, putting every ounce of his STR: 18 (which was definitely a lie) into the blow.

He hit the fracture dead center.

*KA-BOOM.*

The Golem didn't just break; it exploded.

Chunks of granite rained down on the arena. The glowing core flickered and died, rolling across the floor like a spent coal.

Bran landed in a crouch, surrounded by rubble. He stood up slowly, dusting powdered stone off his shoulder.

The arena was dead silent. The Moderator dropped his microphone.

Bran looked up at the sky. He pointed his rolling pin at the invisible System.

"Order up," Bran said cold. "Send the manager."
