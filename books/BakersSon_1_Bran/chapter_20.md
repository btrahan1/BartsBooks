## Chapter 19: The Ladle of Law

The celebration in the Outer Kitchens was legendary. There was roast Thunder-Beef, endless casks of ale, and singing that shook the rafters. Elder Feng had publicly awarded Bran a pouch of high-grade Spirit Stones (worth a small kingdom) and, true to his word, had dispatched a squad of Elite Guards to Riverwood to protect the bakery.

Bran smiled, drank a little ale, and accepted the back-slaps. But his eyes remained sharp.

When the party wound down and the last disciple had stumbled off to bed, Bran went to the pantry. It was his office. His sanctuary.

He closed the door. He locked it.

He sat on a sack of flour and placed his rolling pin on his knees.

"System," Bran said. "Get out here."

The blue box appeared instantly. It seemed... hesitant. Smaller than usual.

**[User Query Acknowledged.]**

"You tried to kill me," Bran stated.

**[Correction: System attempted to scale difficulty to User's potential.]**

"You sent a rock monster to a cooking contest," Bran snapped. "That's not scaling. That's trying to get me fired. Or dead."

Bran stood up. He paced the small room.

"I kept my end of the deal. I fought. I won. I wore the silly uniform. But you broke the contract. You tried to force me into a Combat Class by making me fight something I couldn't cook."

**[Analysis: User successfully cooked the opponent via Thermal Shock. Combat Class [Geomancer] compatibility increased to 15%.]**

"Stop!" Bran shouted, slamming his hand against a shelf. A jar of spices rattled. "I don't want to be a Geomancer! I don't want to be a Warrior! I am a Baker!"

He took a deep breath.

"Here is the new deal. The *Final* deal. If you break this one, I won't just break the monsters. I'll break the narrative."

The System paused.

**[Query: Define "Break the Narrative"?]**

"I'll quit," Bran said simply. "I'll lose the next match on purpose. I'll take my dad, move to a desert island, and bake coconuts for the rest of my life. Zero conflict. Zero XP. Zero entertainment for whoever is watching."

The cursor blinked rapidly. This was the ultimate threat to a System designed to generate content. A boring protagonist was a dead protagonist.

**[...Calculatng...]**
**[Threat Assessment: Critical.]**
**[User leverage accepted.]**
**[State your terms.]**

Bran sat back down. He counted on his fingers.

"One: No more 'Impossible' monsters. If I fight, it has to be something biological. Something with muscles, nerves, or a stomach. If you send a ghost or a robot, I walk."

**[Term 1: Accepted. Biological entities only.]**

"Two: I want an Inventory. A real one. Not this 'pockets' nonsense. I need to carry fifty sacks of flour and a portable oven without looking like a pack mule. Unlimited weight for 'Culinary Supplies'."

**[Term 2: Accepted. [Dimensional Pantry] skill unlocked.]**

"Three," Bran leaned in, his eyes hard. "I want a Skill that lets me end a fight *without* hurting people. I'm tired of cracking bones. I want to end fights my way. The Baker's way."

The System processed this. It scanned the archives of millions of combat skills. Stun? Sleep? Paralyze?

**[Proposal: Skill [Comfort Food].]**
**[Effect: User can manifest "Edible Construct". Upon consumption, target enters a state of "Food Coma" (Stun) for 1 hour. Target becomes non-hostile and highly agreeable.]**

Bran frowned. "Close. But I have to force-feed them? That's rude."

**[Modification: Skill [Aroma of Peace].]**
**[Effect: AOE (Area of Effect). User releases the scent of "Freshly Baked Bread" infused with Calming Qi. Targets within 50 feet lose the "Will to Fight" and gain the debuff "Suddenly Hungry". Combat ends as participants seek snacks.]**

Bran smiled. A real, genuine smile.

"Now that," Bran said, "is a skill."

**[Term 3: Accepted. Skill [Aroma of Peace] added to Skill Tree.]**

"Good," Bran said. "Now, go away. I have to prep the sourdough for the semi-finals."

The blue box lingered for a moment.

**[Note: The Semi-Final opponent is... difficult. Biological. But difficult.]**

"I'll handle it," Bran said, waving his hand. "Just keep the rocks out of my kitchen."

**[System Standby.]**

The box vanished.

Bran let out a long sigh. He felt lighter. He opened his new **[Dimensional Pantry]**. A swirling vortex appeared in the air. He tossed a sack of flour into it. It vanished seamlessly.

"Perfect," he whispered.

He reached into his pocket and pulled out the Amulet of the Common Man. He looked at it.

"Biological but difficult," he muttered, recalling the System's warning.

He put the Amulet back on.

"Well," Bran said to the empty room. "As long as it eats, I can beat it."

"Sir," 42-Beta said. "I think you're forgetting one thing."

*"What?"*

"Yeast loves sugar," 42-Beta said. "But it *thrives* in a toxic environment."
