## Chapter 20: The Sourdough Solution

The Semi-Finals.

The arena had been modified. The stone floor was gone, replaced by a swampy, mist-filled terrain. The air smelled sweet and sickly—like rotting flowers.

The crowd was already holding their breath. Some were wearing masks.

Bran stood on a dry patch of land, adjusting his white cap. He sniffed the air.

"Almonds," he muttered. "And... trace arsenic. A little bit of nightshade."

Opposite him stood **Viper-Mistress Yana**. She wore robes of shimmering purple and green scales. She didn't hold a weapon. Her weapon was her breath, her skin, and the air around her.

She smiled, and her lips were painted a toxic black.

"A Baker," Yana purred. Her voice sounded like silk sliding over a knife. "How quaint. I heard you broke the Golem. Impressive physics. But you can't hit what you can't touch."

She waved a hand. A cloud of green gas rolled toward Bran.

"My poison melts steel," Yana warned. "It turns lungs to liquid. Surrender, little baker, and I will give you the antidote."

Bran didn't surrender. He didn't even cover his mouth.

He reached into his new **[Dimensional Pantry]**.

He pulled out a large, sealed ceramic jar. It was his Mother Starter—the one he had been feeding since he was five. But this wasn't the polite, sleepy starter he used for brioche.

This was the "War Starter." He had been feeding it with the high-energy Spirit Flour and letting it ferment in the ambient mana of the mountain. It was bubbling aggressively against the lid.

"Poison," Bran said, looking at the green cloud. "It's just biological waste, right? Bacteria? Toxins?"

"It is the essence of death!" Yana hissed.

"It's food," Bran corrected.

He popped the lid.

*POP.*

A smell hit the arena. It wasn't the smell of fresh bread. It was the smell of intense, sour, alcoholic fermentation. It smelled like a brewery exploded inside a vinegar factory.

Bran held the jar out toward the green cloud.

"Eat up, boys," Bran whispered to the jar.

He activated a new skill he had discovered while organizing his pantry: **[Fermentation Aura]**.

The starter culture in the jar surged. It wasn't just yeast; it was a magical, hyper-active bacterial colony that Bran had accidentally cultivated to be the apex predator of the microbial world.

The green poison cloud touched the invisible aura of the yeast.

And the yeast *ate* it.

The green mist didn't melt Bran. It swirled into the jar, sucked in by the voracious appetite of the Super-Yeast. The yeast fed on the toxins, breaking down the complex chemical bonds and converting them into... gas. Harmless, carbon dioxide gas.

The jar bubbled violently. It grew warm.

Yana stared. Her lethal mist was disappearing.

"What... what is that?" she screamed.

"Sourdough," Bran explained calmly. "It's very aggressive. It loves complex sugars and proteins. And apparently, it thinks your poison is delicious."

Yana shrieked in rage. She unleashed a torrent of liquid venom from her sleeves.

Bran didn't dodge. He tossed a handful of flour into the air. **[Flour Screen]**.

The flour mixed with the venom, clumping instantly into toxic dough balls that fell harmlessly to the ground.

"You're just giving me ingredients!" Bran called out.

Yana was panting. She was running out of venom. Every attack she threw was neutralized, eaten, or turned into dough.

"I will kill you!" she screamed, charging him with a poisoned dagger.

Bran waited. He sighed.

"Time for the new recipe," he muttered.

He clasped his hands together. He channeled his intent. Not violence. Not destruction.

He thought about Sunday mornings. He thought about warm blankets. He thought about the smell of cinnamon rolls coming out of the oven.

**[Skill Activation: Aroma of Peace]**

A golden ripple expanded from Bran. It wasn't Qi. It was... comfort.

The smell of freshly baked, warm, buttery bread—with a hint of vanilla—washed over the swamp.

The crowd stopped cheering. They closed their eyes and inhaled. Even the grumpy Sword Elder in the VIP box felt his shoulders relax.

Yana froze mid-charge.

Her dagger lowered. The rage in her eyes flickered and died, replaced by a sudden, overwhelming confusion. Her stomach gave a loud, traitorous growl.

"I..." Yana blinked. "Why am I... so hungry?"

She looked at Bran. He didn't look like an enemy anymore. He looked like the guy who had the snacks.

"I haven't eaten since yesterday," Yana murmured, dropping her dagger. "I was fasting for potency."

Bran walked over. He reached into his pocket (actually his Dimensional Pantry) and pulled out a simple, warm roll. He had baked it that morning.

"Here," Bran said gently. "Eat. *'Anger is just hunger with a bad attitude.'*"

Yana took the roll. She took a bite. She slumped to her knees, chewing slowly, tears pricking her eyes.

"It's warm," she whispered.

"It's over," Bran said.

The referee wiped drool from his mouth. "Winner... by... uh... Lunch Break?"

Bran looked up into the nothingness of the sky. He winked.

"Acceptable," he said.

***

Up in the stands, Elder Feng was leaning over the railing, grinning like a madman.

"He fed the poison to the yeast," Feng cackled. "He neutralized the venom with flour. And then he defeated her with a dinner roll."

The other Elders were silent. They were terrified.

"Feng," the Alchemy Elder whispered. "That boy... his yeast consumed a Rank 4 Neurotoxin. Do you realize what that means?"

"It means," Feng said, sitting back down, "that we are going to have very interesting bread at the victory feast."

Feng looked at Bran, who was helping Yana stand up.

"I haven't felt this alive in decades," Feng murmured. "Go on, Baker. Show us the finals."

This is the maturation point. Bran is finally connecting the dots. He's realized that Feng hasn't just been letting him "exercise"; Feng has been secretly training him in the Sect’s highest martial arts, disguised as morning stretches.

It recontextualizes everything. The "Spread the Flour" move is actually *'The Great Wind disperses the Clouds'*. The "Punch the Dough" move is *'The Mountain-Shattering Palm'*.

Bran isn't just a Baker with high stats anymore. He is a Martial Artist who speaks a different language.

This leads us to the Finals. The opponent needs to be the antithesis of Bran. Someone who is pure, disciplined, lethal martial perfection.

Let's introduce **Chapter 21: The Final Proof**.
