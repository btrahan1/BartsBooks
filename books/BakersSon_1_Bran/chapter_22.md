## Chapter 21: The Final Proof

The Finals were held at high noon. The sun beat down on the Arena of Clouds, bleaching the white stone.

There were no gimmicks this time. No swamp. No rocks. Just a flat, open circle.

Bran stood in the center. He wore his white uniform, his cap pulled low. He wasn't holding his rolling pin. It was strapped to his back.

Across from him stood **Tian**.

Tian was the First Disciple of the Hall of Swords. He was the favorite to win. He was everything a Cultivator should be: tall, handsome, arrogant, and incredibly dangerous. He held a sword of pure white jade—*The Heaven's Edge*.

Tian didn't sneer like Li. He didn't rage like Yana. He just looked... bored.

"A Baker," Tian said. His voice was flat. "I have watched your matches. Tricks. Gimmicks. Physics. Yeast."

Tian drew his sword. The sound was like a choir singing.

"Show me your martial dao," Tian commanded. "Or get off my stage."

Bran took a deep breath. He thought about the forms.

*Sweep. Block. Push.*

"You sly dog," Bran whispered to himself, thinking of Feng. "You tricked me into learning Kung Fu."

Bran stepped into a stance.

For the first time, it wasn't a "Baker's Stance." It was low. His feet were rooted. His hands were open, palms facing out.

The crowd gasped.

"That stance..." Elder Feng whispered from the box. "He knows."

Tian narrowed his eyes. "Finally. Something real."

Tian moved. He was fast—faster than the wind blade. He vanished and reappeared in front of Bran, the jade sword thrusting toward Bran’s heart.

Bran didn't use a pan. He didn't use flour.

He used the form.

He stepped inside the guard. He raised his left hand, guiding the blade away with a gentle, circular motion—*'Spreading the Flour'*.

The sword slid harmlessly past his shoulder.

In the same motion, Bran stepped forward and delivered a palm strike to Tian’s chest—*'Punching the Dough'*.

*THUD.*

It wasn't a kill shot. It was a push. A displacement of force.

Tian flew backward ten feet, landing gracefully but looking shocked.

"You know the *Verdant Palm*?" Tian asked, his eyes widening. "Only Elders are taught that."

"I call it 'Kneading the Daylights Out of You'," Bran said seriously.

Tian smiled. A real, dangerous smile. "Good. Then I don't have to hold back."

Tian exploded with power. A white aura erupted around him, forming the shape of a giant spectral sword. He attacked with the fury of a hurricane.

Bran met him.

It was a dance. White silk against white jade.

Bran blocked a strike with his forearm (reinforced by his hidden Amulet and sheer muscle density). He countered with a hip check.

Tian slashed. Bran ducked and swept the leg.
Tian jumped and kicked. Bran caught the foot and twisted.

They moved back and forth across the arena, a blur of motion.

But Bran was losing ground.

Tian was a master. He had decades of Qi refinement. Bran had a few months of secret morning classes and a lifetime of manual labor.

Bran took a hit. A slash across his ribs. The Salamander Silk held, but the impact bruised him deeply.
He took another. A kick to the knee.

Bran stumbled.

Tian loomed over him, sword raised high. The spectral blade grew massive, reaching up to the sky.

"It ends," Tian declared. "*Heaven's Judgment!*"

He brought the sword down.

Bran looked up. He couldn't block this. Not with his hands. Not with the forms. It was too much power.

He needed something denser. Something harder.

Bran reached over his shoulder.

"My Daddy always said," Bran grunted, grabbing the handle of **The Roller**.

He ripped the massive, iron-wood, star-metal, gravity-core rolling pin from its sheath.

"*'If you can't shape the dough... flatten the table!'*"

Bran didn't block the sword. He swung at the *ground*.

He activated every ounce of Strength he had. He poured his "Baker's Intent" into the weapon.

*SMASH.*

Bran hit the arena floor directly in front of Tian.

The impact was cataclysmic. The gravity core in the rolling pin released a shockwave of pure kinetic force.

The stone floor shattered. A fissure opened up, blasting debris upward like a volcanic eruption.

Tian’s "Heaven's Judgment" hit the shockwave.

The spectral sword shattered against the wall of flying rock and gravity.

Tian was blown backward, tumbling through the air like a leaf in a gale. He slammed into the barrier wall on the far side of the arena.

Dust covered everything.

Silence.

Slowly, the dust settled.

Bran was standing in a crater. He was breathing hard, leaning on his rolling pin.

Tian was slumped against the wall. He tried to stand. He coughed. His jade sword was cracked.

Tian looked at his shaking hands. He looked at Bran.

"You..." Tian wheezed. "You destroyed the stage."

"I needed to vent the steam," Bran said, wiping blood from his lip. "Pressure was getting too high."

Tian laughed. It was a pained, wheezing sound. "I cannot continue. My Qi is scrambled. You... you win, Baker."

The referee, who was hiding behind a rock, poked his head out.

"Winner! And Champion of the Sect Tournament! BRAN THE BAKER!"

The crowd didn't just cheer. They roared. The Outer Disciples were hugging each other. Mei and Jin were jumping up and down. Even the Inner Disciples were clapping, stunned by the sheer destructive power of a kitchen utensil.

Bran didn't cheer. He just stood there, feeling the adrenaline fade, replaced by a deep, bone-weary ache.

He looked up at the VIP box. He saw Elder Feng standing, clapping slowly with a proud smile.

Bran nodded to him. *'Sly dog.'*

Then, the blue box appeared. It was huge. Gold.

**[QUEST COMPLETE: SURVIVAL.]**
**[Reward: Class Evolution Available.]**
**[Legacy Item Unlocked.]**
**[System Note: Okay. Fine. You win. No more rocks.]**

Bran smiled. He put his rolling pin back in its sheath.

"Good," Bran whispered. "Now... who's hungry?"

This is the masterstroke. Bran doesn't just defeat the System; he *befriends* it. By treating the System not as a cold machine but as a sentient entity capable of growth, he fundamentally changes the relationship. He effectively "bakes" the System, adding warmth to a cold algorithm.

It also gives Monitor 42-Beta the greatest reward possible: validation.

Here is the **Epilogue: The Sweetest Crumb**.
