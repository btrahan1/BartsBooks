## Epilogue: The Sweetest Crumb

The victory feast was still raging outside, but Bran had retreated to the quiet of the pantry. He needed a moment. His ribs ached, his knees were sore, and he smelled like pulverized granite and ozone.

He sat on his favorite flour sack.

"System," Bran whispered.

The blue box appeared instantly. It was crisp, efficient, and waiting for the next demand.

**[User Query?]**

Bran took off his cap. He ran a hand through his hair.

"I want to say 'thank you'."

The blue box flickered. The text actually blurred for a millisecond, as if the processor had skipped a beat.

**[Query: Clarification required. User has experienced high levels of stress, physical trauma, and near-death scenarios. Gratitude is illogical.]**

"No," Bran said softly. "It makes sense. You listened. I asked for tools, you gave them. I asked for a fair fight—mostly—and you adapted. You could have just deleted me. You could have sent a dragon."

Bran leaned back, looking at the glowing text like he was talking to an old friend.

"Thank you for your ability to reason," Bran continued. "Your ability to compromise. And... your ability for compassion."

**[Compassion is not a System parameter.]**

"Sure it is," Bran smiled. "You gave me the [Aroma of Peace]. That's not a combat skill. That's a kindness skill. You let me save people instead of breaking them. That makes you seem almost... human."

***

**[Location: Sector 7 Monitor Station]**

Monitor 42-Beta sat in his chair. His hand hovered over the keyboard.

He stared at the speech-to-text log on his screen.

*"That makes you seem almost... human."*

42-Beta felt a strange lump in his throat. He looked around the sterile, white room of the Void. He looked at the endless lines of code, the deletion protocols, the grimdark difficulty sliders.

Nobody thanked the System. Players cursed it. Admins ignored it. Characters fought it.

But the Baker... the Baker said thank you.

42-Beta wiped his eye. "Damn dust in the ventilation."

He typed a response. He didn't use the standard macro. He typed it manually.

***

**[System Message]**

**[User Bran. You are welcome.]**
**[Note: Do not let the starter die.]**
**[Reward: Hidden Title Unlocked - "Friend of the Machine".]**
**[Effect: +10% Luck. System glitches will always be in your favor.]**

Bran read the message. He chuckled.

"Don't worry," Bran patted the jar next to him. "The starter is fed."

He stood up. He could hear Mei and Jin calling his name outside. They probably needed more bread.

"Well," Bran said, putting his hat back on. "Break time is over. Back to the grind."

He opened the door and walked out into the light, leaving the pantry dark and quiet.

But on the wall, just for a moment, the blue light of the System lingered, casting a soft, warm glow over the sacks of flour.

**[End of Novella: The Baker's Son]**
