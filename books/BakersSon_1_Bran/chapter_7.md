## Chapter 7: The Yeast Heist

The sun had set hours ago. The Outer Court was asleep, save for the glowing patrols of the Discipline Hall enforcers.

Bran, Mei, and Jin were crouching behind a row of giant jade trash bins behind the Inner Kitchens.

The smell coming from the building was intoxicating. While the Outer Kitchens smelled of sweat and burnt garlic, the Inner Kitchens smelled of honey, ambrosia, and roasted phoenix.

"Okay," Jin whispered, pushing his glasses up his nose. He had unfolded a crumpled piece of parchment. "The storage pantry is on the east side. The Golden Sun Yeast is kept in a clay jar on the top shelf. But..."

"But?" Bran asked.

"The Guardian," Mei shivered. "They call him 'Whiskers of Doom.'"

Bran raised an eyebrow. "Scary name."

"He's a Third-Stage Spirit Beast," Jin hissed. "A Scarlet Tiger-Cat. He eats intruders."

"Does he eat bread?" Bran asked.

"What?"

"Never mind," Bran said. He reached into his oversized pocket and patted the fresh, warm buns he had baked earlier that evening using his own starter and some "borrowed" flour. They were glazed with honey.

"Stay here," Bran commanded. "If I'm not back in ten minutes, go back to the dorms and deny everything."

"Bran, wait—" Mei started, but Bran was already moving.

He didn't use stealth skills. He didn't merge with the shadows. He just moved quietly because he was wearing soft-soled flour-sacking shoes, and he knew how not to rattle the floorboards. *'Walk like the dough is rising,'* he thought.

He reached the pantry door. It was locked with a magical seal.

Bran looked at the glowing rune. He didn't know magic. But he knew hinges.

He pulled a small metal spatula from his belt. He wedged it into the hinge pin of the door. With a gentle *twist-pop*, the pin slid out. He caught it before it hit the ground. He did the same for the bottom hinge.

He lifted the door out of the frame and set it aside.

"Lock's still locked," he whispered to himself.

He slipped inside.

The pantry was a treasure trove. Sacks of Star-Dust Sugar. Barrels of Moon-Water. And there, on the top shelf, a golden jar that pulsed with warm light. The Golden Sun Yeast.

Bran stepped forward.

*Grrrrroooowwwwwwl.*

The sound vibrated in Bran’s chest. It sounded like a tectonic plate shifting.

Bran froze. He looked toward the corner of the room, where a pile of empty grain sacks lay.

Two eyes opened. They were the size of dinner plates, glowing a luminous, predatory yellow.

The creature stood up. It was a cat. But it was the size of a carriage horse. Its fur was a burning crimson, rippling with heat. Its claws, which extended slowly into the wooden floor, were like steak knives.

The Scarlet Tiger-Cat yawned, revealing a mouth full of fangs that could snap a man in half. It lowered its head, ears flattening, and let out a hiss that smelled like brimstone.

Bran looked up at the beast. He looked at the claws. He looked at the muscles rippling under the fur.

"That's a big cat," Bran said.

The cat pounced.

It was a blur of red death. A normal disciple would have frozen. A warrior would have drawn a sword.

Bran did neither. He dropped his center of gravity and held out his hands.

"Down!" Bran said firmly.

The cat, mid-air, was confused. Most prey ran. This prey was... scolding it?

The cat landed on Bran. Or rather, it tried to. Bran caught the beast by its front paws.

It was like catching a falling anvil. The impact drove Bran’s boots an inch into the floorboards. His muscles screamed as the weight of the Spirit Beast bore down on him. But Bran didn't buckle. He had held up collapsing roofs before. He had carried the weight of the bakery on his shoulders for years.

"Bad kitty!" Bran grunted, staring into the glowing yellow eyes. "No jumping in the pantry! *'Paws on the floor, or out the door!'*"

The cat roared, blasting hot air into Bran’s face. It tried to swipe at him, but Bran’s grip was like iron. He had the "Baker’s Grip"—the strength needed to knead dough that fought back. He held the paws wide, neutralizing the claws.

"You're just hungry, aren't you?" Bran strained, his biceps bulging like cantaloupes. "Is that it? The chefs don't feed you the good stuff?"

The cat blinked. It stopped roaring. It chuffed, confused by the lack of fear.

Bran released one hand—risking death—and lightning-fast, pulled a honey-glazed bun from his pocket.

He shoved it into the cat's mouth.

The cat bit down.

The room went silent.

The cat chewed. Its ears perked up. The sweetness of the honey, the fluffiness of the yeast, the texture... it was unlike the raw meat it was usually tossed.

The giant red beast swallowed. It looked at Bran. It looked at Bran’s pocket. It made a sound that was half-purr, half-engine-rumble.

"That's better," Bran said, wiping cat slobber off his tunic. He pulled out another bun. "Sit."

The Third-Stage Spirit Beast, a monster capable of slaughtering a squad of soldiers, sat down on its haunches. The floorboards creaked.

Bran tossed the bun. The cat caught it out of the air.

While the beast was distracted, Bran reached up and grabbed a handful of the Golden Sun Yeast from the jar, stuffing it into a small pouch.

"Pleasure doing business with you," Bran whispered.

He backed away slowly. The cat watched him go, licking honey from its whiskers. It didn't attack. It just let out a soft *mrrrp*.

Bran slipped out, replaced the door hinges, and vanished into the night.

***

Back behind the jade bins, Mei and Jin were chewing their fingernails.

"He's dead," Jin moaned. "We heard a roar. He's been eaten."

"I'm not dead," Bran said, dropping down beside them. He smelled like singed hair and sugar.

"Bran!" Mei squeaked, hugging him before she could stop herself. She pulled back, blushing. "We... we heard the Guardian."

"Yeah," Bran said, dusting off his sleeves. "He was a bit grumpy. Low blood sugar. I fixed it."

He held up the pouch. The yeast inside glowed softly.

"Got it."

Jin stared at him with wide eyes behind his glasses. "You... you walked past Whiskers of Doom? How? Did you use an Invisibility Talisman?"

"Nope," Bran said, standing up. "Just a honey bun. *'The way to a beast's heart is through the stomach,'* my Daddy always says. Even if the stomach is the size of a wine barrel."

As they walked back to the dorms, the "whispers" in the wind seemed different to Bran. They weren't voices of disciples this time.

Somewhere in the distance, a blue box flickered in the air above the Inner Kitchens.

**[System Notification]**
**[Skill Unlocked: Beast Taming (Culinary).]**
**[Proficiency: Novice.]**
**[Note: You have successfully bribed a predator with carbs. This is not a standard taming method, but the System accepts it.]**

Bran smiled. Tomorrow morning, the Outer Kitchen was going to have some *really* good bread.

This is the payoff. Bran has the legendary yeast, he has his friends, and he has his skill. Now he's going to disrupt the status quo by doing the most revolutionary thing possible in a grimdark cultivation sect: making breakfast enjoyable.
