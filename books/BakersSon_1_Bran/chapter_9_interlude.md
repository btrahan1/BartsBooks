## Interlude: The Ticket

**[LOCATION: THE VOID BEYOND]**
**[SYSTEM TERMINAL: SECTOR 7 (MORTAL REALM)]**

The monitor was a wall of cascading green code, flowing like a digital waterfall. Usually, the stream was smooth. Predictable.

*Hero born. Monster slain. Cultivator ascends. Peasant dies.*

It was a boring sector.

Monitor Unit 42-Beta sat in his chair, sipping a cup of virtual coffee. He leaned back, putting his feet up on the console.

"Another quiet millennium," 42-Beta sighed. "Maybe I'll request a transfer to a Dungeon World. At least there's explosions."

Suddenly, a red pixel flashed on the screen.

Then another.
Then a whole block of code turned angry, pulsating crimson.

**[ALERT]**
**[LOGIC ERROR DETECTED]**
**[COORDINATES: VERDANT GRAIN SECT]**

42-Beta sat up, spilling his coffee. "What the...?"

He tapped the keyboard, zooming in on the anomaly.

**[EVENT LOG]**
**[Subject: Bran (Level 0 Civilian)]**
**[Action: Destroyed Spirit Weapon (Rank 2).]**
**[Method: Grip Strength.]**
**[Mana Used: 0.]**
**[Qi Used: 0.]**
**[Skill Used: ...Knead?]**

"That's impossible," 42-Beta muttered. "You can't break Spirit Steel without Qi. The physics engine forbids it."

He ran a diagnostic. The System checked the math.

**[CALCULATION RESULT: PHYSICS OVERRIDDEN BY "INTENT".]**
**[SUB-NOTE: USER BELIEVED THE SWORD WAS "STALE BREAD."]**

42-Beta stared at the screen. The user didn't overpower the system; he *misunderstood* it so hard that the reality engine just gave up and let him win.

"This is bad," 42-Beta said. "If he keeps leveling up non-combat skills and using them for combat, he's going to corrupt the Class Tree. We could have a Level 50 Baker one-shotting a Dragon because he thinks it's a giant turkey that needs roasting."

Another alert popped up.

**[ALERT: USER HAS TAMED APEX PREDATOR WITH "SUGAR."]**
**[ALERT: USER IS GENERATING "ZONE OF HAPPINESS" IN A GRIMDARK SETTING.]**

42-Beta swallowed. The Happiness metric was spiking in Sector 7. That wasn't allowed. This was a Cultivation World. It was supposed to be suffering and struggle.

He reached for the red phone on his desk. The one that connected directly to Management.

"Supervisor?" 42-Beta said into the receiver, his voice trembling. "We have a problem in Sector 7."

*"What kind of problem?"* a bored voice replied. *"Is it another Reincarnated Demon Lord?"*

"No, sir. Worse."

42-Beta watched the screen as Bran handed a bun to a crying girl.

"It's a Baker, sir. And he's... nice."

There was a long silence on the other end of the line.

*"Delete him,"* the Supervisor said.

"I... I can't, sir. He's protected by a plot armor coefficient I've never seen before. It's listed as 'Wholesome Chad Energy'."

*"Then escalate the difficulty,"* the Supervisor commanded. *"Throw him into the Tournament. Send the Elites. Break him. Make him pick up a sword like a normal protagonist."*

"Understood, sir."

42-Beta hung up. He typed a command into the console.

**[QUEST GENERATED]**
**[EVENT: SECT TOURNAMENT]**
**[PARTICIPANT LOCKED: BRAN]**
**[REWARD: SURVIVAL.]**
**[FAILURE PENALTY: DELETION.]**

42-Beta watched the code execute. He felt a pang of guilt.

"Sorry, kid," he whispered to the screen. "System's gotta have balance. You can't just bake your way to godhood."

On the screen, unaware of the cosmic forces aligning against him, Bran sneezed.

"Excuse me," he said, wiping his nose with a floury hand. "Must be pepper in the air."
