# The Baker's Son: Bran's Story

Bran, a humble baker's son with incredible strength from years of kneading dough, is recruited by a powerful cultivation sect. Instead of becoming a typical warrior, he uses his "Dao of Baking" to solve problems, tame beasts, and disrupt the status quo of the sect.
