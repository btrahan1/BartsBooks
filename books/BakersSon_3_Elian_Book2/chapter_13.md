## Chapter 13: A Wondrous Proclamation

The days that followed were a blur of organized, creative chaos. The Artisan's Alliance was no longer a theory; it was a living, breathing entity. The guild masters, once rivals, now met weekly in Elian's workshop, sharing techniques, solving problems, and driven by a shared, impossible deadline.

Piper was in her element, a general commanding a multi-front war of logistics and marketing. Lyra was the quiet, indispensable diplomat, smoothing over the ruffled feathers of proud masters and coordinating their efforts with the palace. And Elian, the reluctant catalyst, had become their spiritual center, the quiet master they all looked to, not for orders, but for the simple, powerful question: "Does it have a soul?"

The Queen and the Duke, true to their word, threw the full weight of their influence behind the festival. The city itself began to transform. The Great Plaza was cleared, and under Joric's masterful direction, elegant wooden booths began to rise, each one a unique work of art in its own right. Banners of every color were hung from the rooftops. The air, once thick with the mundane smells of city life, now carried the exciting scents of fresh-sawn lumber, hot metal, and simmering dyes.

And then, one week before the event, the proclamations went out.

Royal criers, their voices magically amplified to carry across every city square and village green in the kingdom, unfurled their scrolls.

**"Hear ye, hear ye!"**

The proclamation, penned by Lyra and embellished by a poet from the royal court, was not a simple announcement. It was an invitation to a dream. It spoke of a renaissance, of a celebration of the kingdom's very soul. It promised sights, sounds, and crafts the likes of which the world had never seen.

**"By decree of Her Majesty, Queen Isolde, and in partnership with the Artisan's Alliance, you are hereby invited to a grand celebration of our kingdom's heart and hands!"**

The criers' voices rang out, from the northernmost mountain holds to the southernmost fishing villages.

**"On the first day of April, in the capital city of the Kingdom! Come one, come all... to the Festival of Wonders!"**

The kingdom, weary from a long winter and the distant rumors of trouble in the north, heard the call. A "Festival of Wonders." The name itself was a promise of magic, of hope.

In a dark, cold workshop in the northern territories, a foreman of the Iron Assembly heard the proclamation and scoffed. "Wonders," he sneered to his workers. "They are selling sentiment. We sell perfection. Let them have their little party. It will be a footnote in the face of our progress."

He did not understand. He could not understand.

He thought they were selling objects.

But as the entire kingdom began to buzz with excitement, as travelers began to pack the roads, all heading towards the capital, they were coming not just to buy a pot or a blanket or a sword.

They were coming to buy a story. They were coming to witness a miracle. They were coming to be a part of the wonder. The battle was already half-won.

This is a wonderful, logical, and character-driven next step. A festival of craft must also be a festival of taste. By applying his "find the soul" philosophy to food, Elian is expanding his revolution and creating a new, delicious challenge. This is the perfect way to introduce another master artisan.
