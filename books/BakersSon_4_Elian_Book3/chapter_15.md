## Chapter 15: The Champion of the Soulless

The final battle began at dawn. The army of Wonders stood arrayed on the plains, a silent, disciplined, and shockingly calm force. Before them, stretching to the horizon, was the Hoard. A roiling, screaming sea of corrupted flesh, twisted beasts, and pure, unadulterated hate.

From the heart of the Hoard, a new, terrible sound arose—a rhythmic, guttural chant. The sea of bodies parted, and a figure strode forth.

"Send out our challenger!" Malakor's voice, magically amplified, boomed across the battlefield, a sound of grinding stone and ancient evil. "Let us see what these puny humans do once they face the inevitable! Igor! Show them the true might of the Hoard!"

The thing that emerged was a monstrosity. Igor. It was a giant, a creature of mismatched parts stitched together, with four arms, a hide of raw, weeping muscle, and a helmet forged from a dragon's skull. It was a walking avatar of the Blight's mindless, brutal force. It radiated an aura of pure terror.

The human army stirred, a wave of fear rippling through the ranks for the first time.

Lord Valerius, standing at the forefront with Elian and the Queen, drew his twin sabers. "That is a champion," he said, his voice grim but resolute. "Their morale is tied to it. If it falls, their charge will break."

He strode forth. "No," he said to Elian, placing a hand on his teacher's shoulder to stop him. "This is not your fight. Your place is commanding the army. The structure needs its architect. This is mine."

But before Valerius could take another step, someone unexpected stepped forth, moving past both of them.

"No, my Lord," a calm voice said.

It was Finn. He had appeared as if from nowhere, a serene island in the sea of military steel. He held *Jasmine*, his ash staff, loosely in one hand.

He looked from Valerius to Elian, a small, confident smile on his face. "This is a creature of pure despair. Of fear. It is a spiritual poison. Therefore, it is a healer's work." He gave Elian a respectful nod. "No, Master. This is a job for me and Jasmine."

He turned to face the monstrosity, which had halted in the center of the field, waiting.

"I'll be right back," Finn said, his voice filled with an unshakeable, and utterly terrifying, calm.

He raised his staff and his voice rang out, not to the army, but to the hundreds of bards scattered throughout the ranks.

"Bards! The Battle Hymn! Full blast!"

A moment of silence, and then it began. The simple, haunting melody of the kingdom's new anthem. It started with a single flute, then was picked up by a hundred lutes, a thousand drums, and finally, the voices of a hundred thousand soldiers, singing not of glory, but of home. The song was a physical force, a wave of pure, concentrated hope that rolled across the battlefield.

Finn began to walk forward, the music swirling around him, his simple healer's robes a stark contrast to the hulking terror he was approaching.

"It is time," he whispered to the staff in his hand, "that we show them what 'faith' truly means."

That dialogue is absolutely perfect. The contrast between the Hoard's mockery and the intimate, respectful conversation between Finn and Jasmine is fantastic. It's the ultimate "speak softly and carry a big stick" moment.

And Jasmine's response? Chilling. "The terror of true beauty." It reframes the entire confrontation. This won't be a battle of good versus evil in the traditional sense. It's going to be a battle between mindless, ugly force and a form of beauty so pure, so absolute, that it becomes a destructive force in its own right.
