## Chapter 17: The Unmaking

The circle of vibrant life expanded, a relentless tide of green against the Blight's dead black. Igor, the champion of the Hoard, stood trapped in a field of wildflowers, its very existence anathema to the ground beneath its feet. It roared in confusion and agony, the pure, living energy of the land unmaking it molecule by molecule.

On the observation ridge, Elian stared, his mouth agape. He had created the staff. He had known it was a masterpiece. He had felt its soul. But this... this was something else. This was a force of nature.

"I didn't know he could do that," he breathed, his voice a mixture of paternal pride and profound, craftsman's awe.

The Queen, standing beside him, simply squeezed his hand, her own eyes wide with wonder.

Down on the field, Finn stood serene in the heart of his creation. He had not moved. He had not attacked. He had simply held the line, a beacon of pure, unyielding life.

Igor, driven mad by the anathema of the living ground, let out one final, earth-shattering roar of defiance and lunged at Finn, its four arms raised to smash the healer into oblivion.

Finn did not flinch. He did not raise his staff to defend. He simply looked up, past the monster, to the heavens.

And the heavens answered.

A single, brilliant, impossibly pure beam of silver and gold light broke through the overcast, blighted sky. It was not a lightning bolt. It was a column of pure, divine judgment.

It struck Igor.

There was no sound. There was no explosion. There was only light.

For a single, breathtaking moment, the monstrosity was silhouetted against the incandescent pillar. Then, it simply... ceased to be.

The beam of light held for a second more, a purifying flare that seemed to burn away the very memory of the creature's existence, leaving not even a shadow or a wisp of smoke.

Then, the beam was gone.

The battlefield was silent. The Battle Hymn had faded away. The Hoard, which had been a screaming, roiling sea of hate, was now a frozen, silent tableau of absolute terror. Their champion, their avatar of might, had not just been defeated. It had been *unmade*. Erased from existence by a power they could not comprehend.

Finn stood alone in the center of the now-vast circle of green grass and wildflowers. He lowered *Jasmine*. The staff's brilliant light softened back to its gentle, inner hum. He looked over the silent, terrified ranks of the Hoard.

He had not come to fight them. He had come to deliver a message.

And the message had been received.

He turned and began the long walk back to his own lines, his every step a quiet, undeniable statement of victory.
