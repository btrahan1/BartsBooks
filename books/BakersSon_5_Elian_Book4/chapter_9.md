## Chapter 9: The Race to the Surface

While the Golems crumbled into heaps of vibrating dust, Finn moved. He was a streak of gray robes and white light, weaving through the falling debris.

He reached the dais. The Egg was massive, the size of a beer barrel, its surface sleek and black as obsidian. He placed his hands on it. It was cold, terribly cold, but deep inside, he felt a faint, sluggish pulse. A heartbeat.

"Strong stuff, dragon's eggs," he whispered, a healer's assessment. It was alive, but barely.

He didn't try to lift it with muscle. He tapped *Jasmine* against it. *"Help me,"* he asked the wood. Vines of pure light erupted from the staff, wrapping around the egg, forming a net, a cradle of energy that made the heavy stone light as a feather.

He slung it over his shoulder in a velvet pouch he had prepared. He turned and dashed back towards the team.

"Got it!" Finn shouted.

Elian looked at the crumbling army, at the Shadow Lords now shrieking in impotent rage from the shadows. The job was done. The structure was broken.

"To the Dragon!" Elian roared, raising his staff.

"To the Dragon!" came back the roar from ten thousand throats.

The retreat was not a flight; it was a parade. The Hoard marched out, their shields still beating the rhythm of victory, carrying the hope of the world back to the surface.
