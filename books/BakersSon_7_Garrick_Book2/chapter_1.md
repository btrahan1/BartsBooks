### Chapter 1: The Healer’s Habit

The bell of *The Gilded Hem* chimed. It was a soft, polite sound, not the demanding ring of a noble or the sharp rap of the Grey Service.

Garrick looked up from a pile of receipts (business was booming, and the taxes were delightfully low thanks to a certain Royal Decree).

Standing in the doorway was a man who radiated a calm so profound it felt like a physical drop in temperature. He wore simple, slate-gray robes that were impeccably clean but tragically cut. In his hand, he held a staff of pale ash that seemed to glow with an inner pearlescent light.

Garrick adjusted his monocle. He recognized the man instantly. Everyone did. It was **Finn**, the High Healer of the Silent Leaf, the man who had turned a battlefield into a garden.

"High Healer," Garrick said, stepping out from behind the counter and offering a bow that was respectful but not fawning. "You bring a great deal of tranquility into my shop. It’s making the velvet nervous."

Finn smiled. It was the smile of a man who had stared down a dragon and offered it a tissue. "Master Garrick. I am told you are the man to see when one needs... practical miracles."

"I specialize in the impossible," Garrick agreed smoothly. "Usually involving corsets and egos. But for a man of the cloth? I assume you don't need to look thinner."
