## Chapter 8: The Shadow Suit

Ren stood on the dais in *The Gilded Hem*, her arms raised. Garrick was pacing around her, a measuring tape draped around his neck like a priest's stole.

"It's a rush job," he complained, tsking as he measured her inseam. "And the materials... highly unstable. Shadow Silk is meant to drape, not to cling. If you stretch it too far, you'll become visible, and then you'll just be a girl in a very expensive bodysuit getting arrested."

"Can you do it or not?" Ren asked, patience wearing thin.

Garrick hemmed and hawed. "A year from now? Impossible. The mana weave would degrade. But..." He squinted at her. "You're still growing. That complicates the enchantments."

He walked to his shelf of exotic materials.

"But," he mused, "I could make something that would fit you, and function... for the next few months at least. After that, it will simply dissolve into smoke."

He started pulling items down, mumbling to himself, his mind already constructing the pattern.

"Shadow Silk for the base... obviously. It absorbs light. But for the ward-breaking..." He pulled out a swatch of fur that looked like a piece of the night sky. "Black Leopard's skin. From the Void Jungles. It moves silently through magical fields."

He grabbed a jar. "And Ostrich feathers. Ground into dust. For the *weight*. You need to be light enough to walk on a rafter without creaking."

He turned back to her, his eyes gleaming with the challenge.

"It will be tight," he warned. "It will be hot. And it will cost you a favor to be named later."

"Put it on my tab," Ren said. "I'm good for it."

"Oh, I know," Garrick smirked. "The Grey Service always pays its debts."

He snapped his fingers. "Martha! Clear the table! We are making a ghost."

***

Two hours later, Ren looked in the mirror.

She wasn't there.

Or rather, she was, but her outline was blurred, hard to focus on. If she stood perfectly still, she looked like a shadow cast by a piece of furniture. If she moved, she looked like a trick of the light.

The suit was black, seamless, and covered in intricate, almost invisible runes stitched in gray thread. It covered her from neck to toe.

"The hood completes the circuit," Garrick said, handing her a cowl made of the same material. "Put it on, and you disappear from magical detection. Take it off, and you're just... mostly invisible."

Ren pulled the hood up. She vanished completely.

"Perfect," her disembodied voice said.

"Don't get it dirty," Garrick warned the empty air. "Dragon's blood is impossible to get out of silk."

"I'll try not to bleed on it," Ren promised.

The bell chimed as the invisible girl left the shop.

Garrick sighed and sat down. He looked at Martha.

"She's going to start a war, isn't she?"

"Probably," Martha said, knitting calmly. "But at least she'll be the best-dressed soldier on the field."

This is a fantastic callback. Finn (and Jasmine) intervening adds that crucial layer of spiritual/healing protection to the stealth mission. It also reinforces the idea that even the best stealth tech (Garrick's suit) has a weakness against true, soulful perception (Jasmine).

And "Please stand absolutely still" is a wonderful echo of Garrick's "Don't move" commands, showing that the Healer and the Tailor are two sides of the same meticulous coin.
