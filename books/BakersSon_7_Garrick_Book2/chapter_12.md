## Chapter 10: The Vent of Bad Intentions

The Artificer's Guild was a fortress. High walls, magical sensors, and guards who looked like they were carved from granite. But every fortress has lungs.

Ren found the ventilation shaft on the roof. It was small. Impossibly small.

"Garrick said ostrich feathers," she muttered to herself, peering into the dark, dusty throat of the building. "He didn't say anything about being made of rubber."

She exhaled fully, compressing her ribcage, and shimmied into the vent.

It was tight. The metal walls pressed against her shoulders. The Shadow Suit lived up to its price tag; it was slick, allowing her to slide over rivets that would have snagged normal cloth. But the darkness was absolute, and the air was stale and hot.

"Good thing I am not claustrophobic," she whispered, her voice sounding loud in the confined space.

She pulled herself forward by her fingertips. *Scrape. Slide. Scrape.*

"This is sooo narrow. Nope, not claustrophobic one bit."

She reached a bend. It was a ninety-degree turn. She had to contort her body, twisting like a corkscrew. Her knee jammed. Panic flared in her chest—a cold, trapped feeling.

"Suck it up, buttercup!" she hissed at herself. "You've squeezed through smaller holes to steal apples. This is for the kingdom. This is for the baby. This is for the stupid duck."

She forced herself to relax. She visualized the "flow" Elian talked about. She became water. She slipped through the bend.

Ahead, she saw light. A grate.

And she heard voices.

She crawled to the grate and peered down.

She was directly above the main assembly floor. But it wasn't making toys or clocks.

It was a factory of nightmares.

Tables were lined with the dead wood Elian had identified. Men in heavy aprons were carving it, not with care, but with brutal, mechanical strokes. They were embedding jagged shards of black crystal into the wood.

And in the center of the room, pulsating with a sick, violet light, was a massive machine. It looked like a heart made of brass and bone. It was thumping. *Thump-thump. Thump-thump.*

Ren swallowed. It sounded like the heartbeat of the festival, but wrong. Inverted.

"The resonance is stabilizing," a man in a red robe said. The Guildmaster. "The Blight-Core is accepting the graft."

Ren's eyes widened. They weren't just making weapons. They were building a bomb. A bomb made of hate.

She reached for her belt pouch. She needed proof. She pulled out a small, mirrored disc—a scrying tool she had "borrowed" from a mage.

She angled it through the grate.

*Snap.*

A loose screw from the grate, vibrated loose by the machine below, fell.

It hit the floor with a tiny, metallic *ping*.

The Guildmaster looked up. He looked right at the vent.

"We have a rat," he said calmly.

He raised his hand. A bolt of violet lightning shot from his fingers, blasting the grate off its hinges.

Ren scrambled back, but the shockwave hit her. She tumbled backward into the dark shaft, sliding down, down, down into the belly of the beast.

"Well," she thought as she fell. "That's not good."

Hahaha! "Mr. Miyagi." That pop-culture reference (even if she doesn't know it's one, it fits her snarky worldview perfectly) grounds the scene instantly. It's the perfect reaction to unwanted spectral advice while plummeting to your doom.

It also shows she *did* listen to Elian, even if she mocks him.
