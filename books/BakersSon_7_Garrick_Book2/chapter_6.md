## Chapter 4: The Grain of Intrigue

The new workshop of William's Wonders was a cathedral of industry. The air hummed with the sound of a hundred artisans working in concert.

Elian stood in the center of the main floor, inspecting a massive support beam destined for the Royal Library. He was frowning at a knot in the wood, arguing silently with it about load-bearing capacity.

"Elian."

He turned. Finn stood there, looking serene in his simple robes, *Jasmine* leaning against his shoulder.

"Finn," Elian grunted, a flicker of warmth in his eyes. "You look well. The staff seems happy. Did you come to tell me you've healed another dragon, or are you just here to distract me from this stubborn oak?"

"Actually," Finn said, his expression serious, "I need advice. About structure. About... how things fit together."

Elian put down his caliper. "Go on."

Finn explained. He told him about the Grey Service. About Ren. About the deal with the Tailor, the "protection" of the city's underbelly, and the strange, symbiotic relationship the Temple now had with a guild of child spies.

Elian stared at the Master Healer. He blinked once. Then twice.

"Run that by me again?" Elian asked, his voice flat. "You're involved in what now?"

"A... mutual aid network," Finn tried to clarify. "With the local organized crime element. Which is actually a logistics company run by a teenager in a very nice coat."

Elian rubbed his temples. "So, let me get the grain of this straight. You, the High Healer of the Silent Leaf, a man who communes with gods, are now the on-call physician for a street gang that is essentially running a shadow government?"

"When you put it that way..." Finn winced.

"And this Tailor," Elian continued, latching onto the detail that offended his sensibilities the most. "He charges nobles a thousand gold for a dress, uses the money to buy boots for thieves, and calls it 'redistribution'?"

"Essentially," Finn nodded.

Elian was silent for a long moment. He looked at the oak beam. He looked at the orderly chaos of his own workshop. He thought about the structural integrity of the kingdom.

"It's messy," Elian finally pronounced. "The joinery is all wrong. It relies on too many moving parts. It's unstable."

He picked up a chisel.

"But," he added, a grudging respect creeping into his voice, "it is also... highly functional. It addresses a flaw in the social foundation that the Crown cannot reach."

He looked at Finn. "Why are you telling me this?"

"Because," Finn said, his voice dropping. "Ren found something. A rot. Not in the slums, but high up. In the Guilds. Someone is trying to undermine the Alliance. Someone is importing 'Iron Assembly' goods again. But they're hiding them inside... other things."

Elian's eyes snapped up. The boredom vanished. The craftsman was gone; the Warden was back.

"Hiding them?" Elian asked, his voice dangerous.

"Smuggling them," Finn corrected. "Inside hollowed-out logs. Inside crates marked as 'Grain'. The Grey Service intercepted a shipment. They brought it to me because they sensed... sickness."

Finn leaned in. "The wood wasn't just hollowed out, Elian. It was *dead*. Blighted dead."

Elian's hand tightened on the chisel until his knuckles turned white. The Blight. Here.

"Show me," Elian commanded. "Now."

This is a fantastic development. Elian bringing his toddler son to a meeting with a gang leader about Blighted smuggling is peak Elian. It shows he wants to expose William to the "real" world early, and it adds a wonderful layer of absurdity to a serious situation.

Also, Elian meeting Ren is going to be electric. The grumpiest man in the kingdom meets the sharpest girl in the city.
