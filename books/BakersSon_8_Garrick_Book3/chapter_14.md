## Chapter 14: The Adequate Ruler

The Royal Audience Chamber was usually a place of solemnity. Today, it was a place of suppressed giggles.

The Queen sat on her throne. Prince William sat on a smaller, identical throne next to her (carved by Elian, naturally).

A scout from the Royal Guard knelt before them, dusty from the road.

"Report!" the Queen demanded, her voice crisp.

"Yeah! Report!" William echoed, banging his baton on the armrest. "Do it. Do it now!"

The scout looked at the Queen, then down at the toddler prince. He swallowed hard. He had heard the stories. The Smuggler's Warehouse Incident was already legend in the barracks.

"Ahem. Yes, Your Highnesses," the scout said. "They ran. And they kept running. Like the devil himself was chasing them."

He glanced down at William again, as if checking for horns beneath the golden curls.

"We followed them to the border, and beyond," the scout continued. "They crossed into Ostengard without stopping. I don't think they'll be back anytime soon."

"Good!" stated Prince William, nodding with grave satisfaction.

He pointed his baton at the scout. "Take your scouts to the Kitchens. Ask the cooks, *nicely*, for some food. Ask them for 'William's Favorite'. They'll know what you mean."

(William's Favorite was a specific type of honey-glazed bear claw pastry that Chef Antoine made exclusively for the royal nursery).

The scout blinked. "Thank you, my Prince."

William looked up at his mother for approval. He had been firm. He had been decisive. And he had ensured the troops were fed.

"Well done, Son," the Queen said, smoothing his hair. "You handled that perfectly. You'll make a fine ruler someday."

William considered this. He looked at his baton. He looked at the throne room.

"That," he said, adopting his father's most critical, discerning tone. "That would be... adequate, I suppose."

He then dropped the baton, slid off the throne, and grinned, becoming a toddler again in an instant.

"Now," he shouted, running behind the heavy velvet drapes. "Want to play hide and seek?"

The Queen laughed, the sound ringing through the hall.

"One... two... three..." she began to count.

The kingdom was safe. The smugglers were gone. And the future King was currently hiding behind a curtain, giggling.

It was, she decided, more than adequate. It was perfect.

This brings us back to the core theme: legitimacy. Ren started as a street rat, then a gang leader, then a "service provider." Now, she's being publicly recognized by the Crown. It's the ultimate validation of her journey and Garrick's "mending."

The fact that they look fabulous is the icing on the cake. They aren't just receiving a medal; they fit the part.
