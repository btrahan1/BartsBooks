## Chapter 2: The Silent Market

The village market of Greenhollow was a modest affair. A few farmers selling grain, a blacksmith selling nails, and a lot of mud.

Oren stood behind his small cart. He had arranged his vegetables neatly: the glowing purple turnips, the golden carrots, and the potatoes that seemed to vibrate slightly.

He stared at his boots. He hated this part.

"How much for the carrots?" a housewife asked.

Oren opened his mouth. His throat closed up. His heart hammered. He held up two fingers.

"Two coppers?" she asked.

He nodded.

She tossed the coins on the cart and grabbed a bunch. As she walked away, she took a bite of a raw carrot.

"Oh my," she gasped. "I can... I can see the dust motes in the air. I can see the weave of my shawl." **[Effect: Eagle Eye +2]**. She hurried off to tell her sister.

Oren sighed. He just wanted to go home.

A shadow fell over his cart.

He looked up. Standing there was a boy, maybe ten years old, wearing a very strange outfit. He wore a slate-blue jacket that was too nice for a farm boy, sturdy boots, and a truly magnificent hat with earflaps.

It was Pip. He was on a "scouting mission" for Ren (meaning he was bored and wanted to see the countryside).

Pip looked at the vegetables. He looked at the housewife who was now pointing out a bird three miles away to her confused husband.

He looked at Oren.

"You're bad at this," Pip observed cheerfully.

Oren flinched. He looked down.

"Those carrots," Pip said, picking one up. "They're magic, aren't they? I can feel the hum. My hat is vibrating."

Oren shrugged helplessly.

"And you're selling them for two coppers?" Pip asked, incredulous. "That's robbery. You're robbing yourself."

Oren made a vague hand gesture that meant *'I don't know what else to do.'*

Pip hopped up onto the cart, sitting next to the turnips.

"I'm Pip," the boy said. "I'm with the Service. We handle... logistics. And negotiations."

He leaned in. "You need a manager. Someone to do the talking. Someone to make sure you get fair value. In exchange for..." Pip calculated. "Let's say, ten percent. And maybe a bag of those carrots. They look crunchy."

Oren looked at the boy. He looked at the terrifying crowd of people starting to gather, drawn by the rumors of the miracle vegetables. He felt the panic rising in his chest.

He nodded vigorously.

"Deal," Pip grinned. He stood up on the cart and cupped his hands around his mouth.

"Step right up!" Pip shouted, his voice trained in the noisy alleys of the capital. "For the finest produce in the kingdom! But be warned! These aren't just carrots! They are 'Visionary Roots'! Five coppers a bunch! No haggling! The Farmer is a genius of few words, so you talk to *me*!"

Oren shrank back, hiding behind a crate. But he felt a wave of relief. The loud boy was handling it.

Pip looked back at Oren's clothes—a burlap sack tied with rope and muddy boots with holes in the toes.

"We're going to need to fix that," Pip muttered to himself. "You can't be a genius farmer looking like a scarecrow. I know a guy. Well... I know a girl who knows a guy."

He pulled a small notepad from his pocket (Grey Service standard issue) and scribbled a note.

*Target acquired. Magic turnips. Silent genius. Needs wardrobe upgrade. Send Garrick's catalog. - P.*

He handed the note to a passing crow (he had a way with birds).

"Alright, Farmer," Pip said, turning back to the crowd. "Let's make some money."
