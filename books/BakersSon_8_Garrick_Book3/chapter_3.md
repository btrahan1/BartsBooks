## Chapter 3: The Dilution Solution

The next day, Oren’s farm had visitors.

Pip had brought backup. A tall man in serene gray robes walked through the muddy field as if he were strolling through a palace. He held a glowing staff.

Finn stopped by the turnip patch. He felt the immense life force radiating from the ground.

"By the Leaves," Finn whispered. "This isn't a garden. It's a mana font."

He turned to Oren, who was hiding behind a cow.

"You can't sell these raw," Finn said gently. "I just saw a squirrel eat a discarded beet top and then punch through a wooden fence. If a child eats a whole turnip, they might explode. Or ascend to godhood. Neither is ideal for a Tuesday."

Oren paled.

"We have to cut them up," Pip declared, sitting on the fence. "Dilute them. Stretch the product. Maximizes profit, minimizes explosions."

"Exactly," Finn agreed. "We need to make... 'Miracle Soup'. A broth. One turnip could feed a hundred people and cure a hundred colds. And the flavor..." He sniffed the air. "It smells like comfort."

Finn winked. "I know a chef or two. And a baker. We'll work on the recipe. You'll need a shop. A controlled environment."

Oren shook his head frantically. A shop meant people. Lots of people.

"Don't worry," Pip said. "You stay in the kitchen. I'll handle the front. Finn handles the... dosage."

"I know a Baker who might have some space," Finn mused. "Bran. His shop is expanding. He's been looking for a savory partner. 'Soup for the Soul, and Pastries for the Palette!'"

Pip frowned. "We'll work on the name. Maybe just 'The broth'."

Oren looked at his muddy clothes. He looked at the two strangers who were planning his life. He felt... safe? They seemed to know what they were doing.

"But first," Pip said, jumping down from the fence and eyeing Oren’s rags with professional disdain. "We can't have the creator of 'Miracle Soup' looking like a compost heap. We need fresh digs."

He pulled a measuring tape from his pocket (stolen from Garrick, naturally).

"Stand still, Farmer," Pip commanded. "I'm sending your measurements to the capital. We need something... earthy. But expensive earthy. 'Rustic Chic'."

Oren sighed. He just wanted to grow his carrots. But if it helped Ma and Pa...

He stood still.

The Crow arrived with a small package two days later. Inside was a tunic of heavy, durable canvas in a rich forest green, with reinforced leather patches on the shoulders and elbows. It was enchanted with **[Dirt Repellent]** and **[Photosynthesis]** (it literally soaked up sunlight to keep the wearer energized).

Oren put it on. He felt... sturdy. Like a tree.

"Now," Pip said, rubbing his hands together. "Let's go make some soup."

This is a lovely, quiet moment to end on. Oren isn't seduced by the gold or the fame; his motivation remains pure. His parents are happy. That’s the victory.

And the transition to **Milk**? That opens up the **Animal Husbandry** skill tree! Imagine cows eating his magic grass and producing "Spirit Milk" or "Mana Cream."

This sets up the next expansion perfectly without losing the cozy vibe.
