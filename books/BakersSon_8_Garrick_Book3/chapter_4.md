## Chapter 4: The Milky Way

The "Miracle Soup" shop was a sensation. Lines wrapped around the block, mingling with the lines for Bran's bakery. People came in coughing and left singing. Old men walked in with canes and walked out carrying them over their shoulders.

Pip handled the coin. Finn handled the recipes. Bran handled the bread bowls.

Oren stayed on the farm.

He sat on the porch with Ma and Pa. They were laughing. Ma was knitting a scarf with steady hands. Pa was chopping wood, humming a tune. They looked ten years younger. The roof was fixed. The larder was full.

Oren didn't understand the numbers in Pip’s ledgers. He didn't care about the gold coins piling up in the jar.

He understood happiness. And Ma and Pa were happy. That was good enough for him.

He walked his farm in the twilight. The vegetables were humming their sleepy night-song. He felt the connection to the land, deep and abiding.

He knew more than vegetables. The soil spoke to him, but so did the animals.

He leaned against the fence of the pasture. His single cow, Bessie, looked at him with big, soulful eyes. She had been grazing on the "Iron-Blade Grass" he had accidentally cultivated near the stream.

"They said the soup made people better," Oren whispered to Bessie.

Bessie mooed. It was a resonant, powerful moo.

Oren patted her flank. It felt solid. Warm.

"I bet they could use some good milk," Oren mused. "Strong milk. Milk for... bones."

He looked at the empty barn. He imagined chickens that laid golden (nutritional) eggs. He imagined sheep with wool so soft it healed the skin it touched (Garrick would love that).

He smiled. The garden was done. It was time to build a ranch.

"Hey, Pip!" Oren called out to the empty air, knowing the boy (or one of his spies) was listening. "I need a bigger bucket!"
