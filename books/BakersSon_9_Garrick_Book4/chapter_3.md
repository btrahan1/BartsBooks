## Chapter 3: The Hardwood Gym

The suit was coming together. It was a marvel of engineering—thousands of tiny, paper-thin mythril scales sewn onto a bodysuit of **[Adaptive Weave]**. It shimmered like fish scales. It weighed less than a winter coat.

But Tim watched the fitting with a critical eye.

"Make it flexible," Tim instructed, watching Galen flinch as a pin came too close. "He needs full range of motion. I don't have a lot to work with, but I will make him grow. At least enough so he doesn't blow away in a swift breeze."

Galen looked terrified. "Grow? How?"

"You have no foundation," Tim said, poking Galen's skinny leg. "You are top-heavy with ideals and bottom-light on stability. You will develop muscle. Or die trying."

Tim stood up, tossing his apple core into the bin with perfect aim.

"I can teach you how to move. Garrick can give you a shell. But you need to build the frame."

He grabbed Galen by the collar of his tunic.

"I know a guy," Tim said, a sadistic grin forming. "With a staff. He likes to put people to work. He thinks carrying things is a spiritual experience."

***

An hour later, Galen stood in the chaotic yard of **William's Wonders**. It was loud. It was dusty.

Elian stood there, holding his Iron-Root staff. He looked at the scrawny boy. He looked at Tim.

"You brought me a twig," Elian grumbled.

"I brought you a sapling," Tim corrected. "He needs roots. And bark."

Elian sighed. "Fine."

He pointed to a massive pile of raw lumber deliveries. "Those oak logs need to be moved to the seasoning shed. By hand. No carts."

Galen looked at the logs. They were huge. "But... I'm a Paladin Initiate!"

"Today," Elian said, thrusting a pair of heavy work gloves into Galen's chest, "you are a Mule Initiate. Lift with your legs. Breathe out on the effort. If you drop it on your foot, don't cry near the walnut; it's sensitive."

Tim sat on a stack of planks, watching. "Don't worry, kid. After you move the wood, we'll work on dodging. I'm going to throw wood chips at you."

"Why?" Galen wailed, struggling to lift the smallest log.

"Because undead don't fight fair," Tim said cheerfully. "And neither does wood."

Galen groaned and heaved. The log moved an inch.

"Good start!" Tim cheered. "Only five hundred to go!"

The training of the Lightweight Paladin had begun. And it involved a lot less praying and a lot more splinters.

This is the final piece of the "power-leveling" puzzle! We have the **Defense** (Garrick), the **Skill/Strength** (Tim/Elian), and now we need the **Fuel**.

Oren’s "Superfood" is the perfect way to explain how a skinny kid can gain muscle fast enough to matter. It's the magical equivalent of a protein shake, but with "Stat-Boosting" properties.

Let's bring in the soup.
