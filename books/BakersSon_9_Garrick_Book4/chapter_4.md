## Chapter 4: The Diet of Heroes

Two weeks of Elian's "Mule Training" had left Galen broken, bruised, and constantly exhausted. He was lifting the logs, yes, but he was burning more calories than he had. He looked like a skeleton wearing skin.

Tim watched him collapse after moving the last log. The boy had heart. He had willpower (WIL: 18). But his body was failing him.

"He's running on fumes," Tim muttered. "The engine is good, but the tank is empty."

He picked up the boy, throwing him over his shoulder like a sack of grain.

"Where are we going?" Galen mumbled, half-asleep.

"Lunch," Tim said.

They walked to the storefront next door. **"Soup for the Soul, and Pastries for the Palette!"**

The smell hit them first. Rich, savory, and vibrating with life energy.

Tim dumped Galen into a chair. Oren, who was wiping down a table, looked up. He saw the boy's gray skin and shaking hands.

He didn't need to be told.

Oren disappeared into the kitchen. He returned with a bowl of "Titan Stew"—the concentrated stuff, made with the purple turnips and the golden carrots.

"Eat," Oren whispered.

Galen took a spoonful. His eyes widened. The warmth spread through his chest, down to his toes. The shaking stopped.

Then, Oren brought out a mug. It was filled with a thick, creamy white liquid.

"Bessie," Oren said simply. "Calcium. For the bones."

It was **[Iron-Bone Milk]**.

Tim nodded approval. "Drink it all. You need protein. You need density."

Galen drank. It tasted like sweet cream and iron filings. As it hit his stomach, he felt a strange sensation. His bones... itched. They were hardening. His muscles, torn down by the heavy lifting, were knitting back together, but tighter. Stronger.

"More," Tim ordered.

For the next month, Galen's life was a cycle of torture and feasting. Lift logs until he dropped. Drink Bessie's milk until he glowed. Dodge wood chips until he was dizzy. Eat magic soup until he passed out.

He grew. Not into a hulk like Roric, but into something... dense. Like a sapling turning into ironwood. He was still lean, still light, but when he gripped the practice sword, his knuckles didn't turn white. He held it steady.

"He's ready," Elian grunted one morning, watching Galen move a heavy oak beam without wheezing.

"Not yet," Tim said, finishing his apple. "He's strong. He's fed. Now... he needs to learn how to shine."

He looked at the boy, who was wiping a milk mustache from his face.

"Get your shiny suit, kid," Tim said. "It's time to go hunting."

This is the "Tutorial Boss" moment. Galen has the gear, the stats, and the training. Now he needs the experience. The fact that his "staff" (probably a specialized Paladin weapon Elian made) has Jasmine's blessing is a great touch—it links him to Finn and the "Soul" magic system.

And Tim's "Remember... Balance!" snicker is the perfect callback.
