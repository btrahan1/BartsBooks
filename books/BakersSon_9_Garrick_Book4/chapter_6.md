## Chapter 6: The Zombie Shuffle

From the high ridge overlooking the crypt entrance, Finn watched. He leaned on *Jasmine*, who was vibrating with a mischievous energy.

"He handles one well enough," Finn murmured. "But the world doesn't attack one at a time."

He looked at the staff.

"Let's up the stakes a bit, what do you say, Jasmine?"

The staff glowed. A pulse of life-energy, concentrated and deliberate, shot down into the crypt. It wasn't an attack; it was a wake-up call. To the undead, life energy was irritating, like a loud noise in a library.

"Could you disturb a few more skeletons for us?" Finn whispered. "And throw in a couple of zombies? They smell terrible, but they're durable."

Below, the ground rumbled.

From the crypt entrance, a groan echoed. Not one skeleton. Ten. And behind them, two shambling, fleshy figures. Zombies.

Galen, who had been feeling pretty good about his dust pile, paled.

"Tim?" Galen squeaked. "There's... more."

Tim didn't draw a weapon. He just crossed his arms.

"Good," Tim said. "More targets means you don't have to aim as much. Don't let them corner you. Use the environment. And remember... zombies are slow, but they grab. Don't get grabbed."

The hoard shambled forward.

Galen took a deep breath. He remembered the logs. He remembered the soup. He remembered the suit.

He moved.

He was a blur of silver light. He dodged a skeleton's claw, pivoted, and smashed its skull. He ducked under a zombie's grasp, the rot-smelling arm passing inches from his face, and swept its legs (a move Tim had insisted on).

The zombie fell. Galen didn't stop. He slammed the mace onto its head. *Flash.* Purified.

He was dancing. It wasn't perfect. He took a glancing blow to the shoulder—the Mythril Weave absorbed it, turning a bone-breaking hit into a bruise. But he kept moving.

Up on the ridge, Finn smiled.

"He's got rhythm," Finn noted.

"He's got good gear," Jasmine seemed to hum back.

"He's got heart," Finn corrected.

Down below, the last skeleton fell. Galen stood panting, surrounded by dust and silence.

Tim walked over. He handed Galen a flask of Bessie's milk.

"Drink," Tim said. "You're dehydrated. And you missed a spot on your left flank."

Galen drank, wiping sweat from his brow. "Did I pass?"

Tim looked at the carnage. He looked at the boy who was still standing.

"For today," Tim said. "Tomorrow... we find a Ghoul."

**THE END (of Book Four)**

This is the ultimate convergence. We have **Galen** (The Paladin), **Tim** (The Cowled Monk), and **Prince William** (The Toddler Tyrant) all in one scene. It’s the Avengers Assemble moment, but weirder and funnier.

William commanding fully grown Paladins to fight Galen is perfect. It establishes the stakes and the hierarchy immediately.
