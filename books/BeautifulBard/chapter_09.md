# Chapter 9: The First Wake

The darkness wasn't empty. It was thick and cool, like velvet pressing against his eyelids. Bart didn't feel the pod anymore. He didn't feel the lavender light of the suite or the weight of his own body. He felt... a hum. A low-frequency vibration that resonated in his chest, synchronized with the rhythmic thump of a drum that he realized was his own digital heart.

*Immersion Level: 1/10.*
*Calibration Complete.*

Bart opened his eyes.

The sky above him was a pale, washed-out blue, the color of a faded memory. There were no clouds, only a soft, diffused glow that hinted at a sun waiting to be rendered. He was lying on a stone slab that felt surprisingly solid, its surface smooth and cold against the skin of his back. 

He sat up slowly, his movements feeling slightly sluggish, as if he were moving through invisible syrup. It was the first day of Spin-Up—the 7:1 ratio was active, but the sensory depth was still being layered in. The world looked like a high-end watercolor painting: the lines were sharp, but the colors were subdued, waiting for the system to inject the full richness of the Vitreous engine.

"Party check," a voice said, clear and resonant, slicing through the artificial silence of the waking chamber. It was coming from the slab to his right. "Tim, aka Timothy Healz online."

Bart turned his head. Tim was sitting up, dressed in simple, gray-white linen robes. He looked like himself, but... polished. His features were a little more symmetric, his posture a little more upright. He looked like the prototypical Cleric—a man who carried the weight of the library and the temple on his shoulders.

"Dastardly Dave online." The voice came from the shadows of a nearby pillar. David was already standing, checking the fit of a dark leather tunic that seemed to absorb the pale light. He moved with a grace that was already frighteningly efficient. He wasn't just David anymore; he was a silhouette given purpose.

"Mihwa, online," she said, her voice followed by a small, giddy laugh. She was standing near a fountain that was currently only producing a soft, static-like hiss instead of water. She wore practical green leathers, her hair braided with a strip of raw hide. "It was available, heh. Guess not many people wanted just 'Mihwa' on Server One."

"Beautiful Bart, checking in," Bart said, standing up and brushing off his tunic. He felt it—the Charisma. It wasn't just a number on a screen anymore. It was a pressure in the air around him. When he spoke, the acoustics of the chamber seemed to bow to his voice. He felt taller, more vibrant, even in the Level 1 immersion. He was the center of his own gravity.

"Gag!" A massive figure stood up from the slab on Bart's left. It was Nathan, but he had undergone the most dramatic transformation. He was broad-shouldered, towering over the others, his presence filled with the solid, immovable weight of a mountain. He wore the basic Gambeson of a Paladin, but even in the simple gear, he looked like a statue come to life. "The Protector, online! Man, it's great that we got in early!"

Nathan looked around the chamber, his eyes scanning the architecture with a DM’s practiced scrutiny. "Look at the occlusion culling. Even at Level 1, the draw distance is incredible. We’re in the Ascension Hall. This is the neutral zone where the Spin-Up happens."

"Timothy Healz, Dastardly Dave, Mihwa, The Protector, and Beautiful Bart," Bart recited, the names feeling like the lyrics to a new song. "The Merry Band of Miscreants has officially arrived."

They were in a circular hall of white marble, the ceiling high and open to the pale sky. In the center was a pedestal holding a single, glowing crystal that pulsed with the same indigo light Bart had seen in the pod. This was the anchor—the tether that kept their five-pod neural web synced during the first week.

"Six more days of Spin-Up," David said, walking toward the hall's exit, which led out into a vast, misty courtyard. "Six days to learn how to walk in 7:1 speed before they drop us into the actual starter zone. We need to find the training dummies. I want to see how the backstab window scales at 10% immersion."

"I want to see the trees," Mihwa said, trailing after him. She paused by the archway, her hand hovering over a vine that was carved into the stone—only to gasp when the 'stone' gave slightly under her touch. "If the engine can render the tension of a leaf at Level 1, I want to see what Day 7 looks like."

Tim stayed back for a moment, looking at Bart. He was checking a small, translucent HUD that only he could see, his fingers twitching as he navigated the Cleric's initial prayer list. "How do you feel, Bart? Any neural static? Headache? The manual said the first hour of Level 1 can feel like being underwater."

Bart shook his head, a wide, genuine grin on his face. He felt the weight of the guitar case on his back—or rather, the digital representation of it. It felt strangely light, yet present. "I feel... resonant, Tim. I feel like I'm finally standing on the stage I was born for. It's a bit quiet, though. Level 1 doesn't seem to have much of an ambient soundtrack. No birds, no wind rustle."

"That's because the soundtrack is you, brother," Tim said, clapping him on the shoulder. The contact felt solid, but lacked the 'warmth' of real skin. It was more like touching a very expensive mannequin. "We’re the only things in this zone that have 100% texture resolution."

Nathan walked over, his heavy boots echoing on the marble. "The Protector is ready. Timothy Healz is ready. Let's go find our first quest. Even if it's just 'Kill 5 Rats,' I want to hear how you sing to them, Bart."

"Oh, I'm not going to sing to the rats, Nathan," Bart said, stepping toward the misty exit. "I'm going to tell them a joke. And then, thanks to you, I'm going to use my Charisma to make them realize that dying for me is the greatest honor they'll ever have."

"God-tier ego," David muttered from the courtyard. "Definitely Beautiful Bart."

Bart laughed, the sound carrying further and clearer than it ever had in the real world. He stepped through the mist, the cool air of Vitreous Online hitting his face for the first time. It was Level 1. It was watercolor. It was quiet.

But it was theirs. 

And for Beautiful Bart and his miscreants, the show had finally, truly, begun.
