# Chapter 13: Twisting the Tune

The road to Oakhaven was a winding ribbon of cobblestone that cut through a forest of shimmering, silver-barked trees. At Level 1 immersion, the world still felt like a high-end watercolor painting, but the scale of the landscape was beginning to sink in. The sky was a vast, unblemished dome of pale blue, and the air carried the faint, crisp scent of pine and digital ozone.

"Formation 1, stay tight," Nathan commanded, his voice deep and resonant. He was leading the pack, his plastic-looking shield held high, his eyes scanning the thickets of glowing ferns that lined the path. "David, what do you see?"

David—Dastardly Dave—was a shadow moving between shadows. He was twenty yards ahead, his dark leather armor blending perfectly with the muted tones of the woodland. "Movement in the brush. Low-slung. Four, maybe five targets. They’re tracking our heat signatures."

"Vitreous Wolves," Tim said, checking his mini-map. "Standard starter mob. Level 1. High agility, low health. They like to swarm."

Bart grinned, his fingers already hovering over the strings of his plywood lute. "Swarm away. I’ve been waiting for a chance to test something."

"Test what?" Mihwa asked, her eyes fixed on the rustling leaves to their left. She had her gnarled staff raised, a faint green glow already beginning to accumulate at the tip.

"Hymn twisting," Bart said, his voice brimming with the confidence of an 18-Charisma powerhouse. "In the old tabletop manuals, Nathan used to talk about the 'Twist'. A hymn lasts ten seconds after you stop playing. If I switch tunes every five seconds, I can keep two going at once. Overlap the resonance."

Timothy Healz—Tim—looked at him, his brow furrowing. "That’s a heavy mana draw, Bart. You’re talking about double the upkeep."

"Who am I kidding, of course I can!" Bart cheered, a flash of 'Beautiful' arrogance crossing his face. "Lead on Nate, second verse, same as the first!"

The first wolf lunged from the ferns. It was a lean, silvery creature, its eyes glowing with a malevolent blue light that matched the Training Echoes they’d fought in the orchard. It snarled, a low, digital rasp, and bit deep into Nathan’s greave.

"Contact!" Nathan shouted, slamming his shield down on the wolf’s head. 

*Hymn Unleashed: Battle Hymn of the Miscreant.*

Bart struck the first chord, a driving, aggressive melody. The indigo energy rippled out, washing over the party. Nathan’s movements became sharper, the physical damage buff lighting up his plastic sword with a purple hue.

"Five seconds," Bart whispered to himself, his fingers racing. 

He didn't wait for the hymn to finish. He shifted his grip, his fingers sliding into a new chord progression—slower, more melodic, like the sound of a distant mountain spring.

*Hymn Unleashed: Recovery Hymn of the Resonant Soul.*

The indigo energy shifted, a ripple of soft white light overlapping the purple. Nathan, who had taken a second bite to the arm, felt the wound begin to stitch itself back together even before Mihwa could cast a spell.

*Status: Battle Hymn (5s remaining)*
*Status: Recovery Hymn (10s remaining)*

"It’s working!" Bart shouted over the music. 

"The damage is up, and I can feel the mana reservoir filling!" Tim called out, his eyes wide. "Bart, you’re creating a feedback loop!"

The wolves were fast, but they were predictable. They tried to flank, but Nathan was a mountain of plastic and leather that wouldn't be moved. David, meanwhile, was a ghost. He appeared behind a wolf that was trying to circle Nathan, his daggers flashing in the pale light. 

*Critical Hit: 24 damage.*

The wolf dissolved into blue pixels before it could even whine.

"Keep it up, Bart!" Nathan yelled, parrying a two-pronged attack from a pair of larger wolves. 

Bart was sweating now—not just the digital suggestion of it, but real, physical effort. His fingers were flying, switching between the aggressive Battle Hymn and the soothing Recovery Hymn every five seconds. The mental load was staggering. He had to keep the tempo in his head while monitoring the five-second windows, all while moving through the 30% physics drag of the world.

*Mana: 160/180.*
*Mana: 155/180.*
*Mana: 150/180.*

"Aggressive draw!" Bart gritted his teeth. "But the throughput is insane!"

The Battle Hymn made Nathan’s strikes lethal. The Recovery Hymn kept his health and the party’s mana pools from bottoming out. It was a tactical engine that was out-pacing the AI’s swarm logic. 

"Mihwa, root the one on the right!" Bart commanded, never missing a chord.

"Rooting!" Mihwa slammed her staff into the cobblestone. Silvery vines exploded from the ground, wrapping around the third wolf’s legs and pinning it in place. 

David didn't miss the opportunity. He lunged from the mist, his daggers carving a 'V' into the pinned wolf’s chest. Another explosion of blue particles.

Three down. Two to go.

The final two wolves hissed, realized they were out-matched, and tried to retreat into the ferns.

"Oh no you don't!" Bart shouted. He struck a booming, final chord of the Battle Hymn, the indigo energy reaching a crescendo. "David, catch them!"

David didn't need to be told twice. With the 5% movement speed buff from the Battle Hymn, he was a blur. He caught the first wolf at the edge of the road, sending it spinning into the trees. The second wolf turned to face him, but Nathan was already there, his plastic shield crashing into its skull.

The silence that followed was thick and heavy. 

*Combat Log Update:*
*Target: Vitreous Wolf (x5)*
*Total Base Experience: 500 XP*
*Party Modifier Applied: Vitreous Cohesion Bonus (1.2x)*
*Total Adjusted XP: 600 XP*
*Shared Experience: 120 XP per member.*

"Total XP: 168," Tim said, his voice reflecting a mix of exhaustion and triumph. "We’re nearly a fifth of the way to Level 2."

Bart leaned over, his hands resting on his knees, his plywood lute hanging loosely from its strap. His fingers were cramping, a dull ache that translated through the haptic mesh. "Twisting... is... exhausting."

"But it’s the most efficient thing I’ve ever seen," Nathan said, looking at his arm. The bites were gone, the Recovery Hymn having wiped the slate clean. "If we can keep that up, we can handle packs twice that size."

"We need more mana regen," Tim observed, looking at Bart’s pool. 120/180. "You’re burning through it fast. If we get into a long engagement—a boss fight or an elite pack—you’re going to hit the wall in five minutes."

"Then I’ll just have to make sure the boss dies in four," Bart said, standing up and flashing that arrogant, beautiful grin. He looked down the road, where the first stone chimneys of Oakhaven were finally becoming visible through the trees.

"Formation 1?" Nathan asked, already lining up at the lead.

"Formation 1," Bart confirmed. "And tell Oakhaven to get the stage ready. The Beautiful Bard has arrived, and he’s bringing the noise."

As they walked toward the village, the watercolor world felt a bit more detailed, the lines a bit sharper. They were learning the rhythm of the 7:1 life. They were learning the math of survival. And Bart, looking at his hands, knew that the plywood lute was just the beginning. 

He was going to be the most complicated thing in *Vitreous Online*. And he was going to love every second of it.
