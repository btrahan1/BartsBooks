# Chapter 17: Resonance Strike

The transition to Phase 2 was more than just a visual upgrade. As the group walked out of the *Thirsty Oak* and into the village square, the very ground felt different. The cobblestones, which had felt like smooth, underwater pillows the day before, now had a distinct, gritty texture under their boots. The morning air didn't just smell like pine; it felt... sharper. Cooler.

"Formation 1, stay tight," Nathan commanded, his voice now sounding like it was coming from a three-dimensional throat rather than a digital speaker. He was checking the latch on his shield, which now had a visible, tarnished sheen in the Level 2 light. "The drag is lighter, but the mobs are going to be faster too."

"Ready when you are," David said, his shadow stretching long across the square. He was moving with a fluid grace that had been impossible twenty-four hours ago. The 25% drag was still there—a ghostly weight on his limbs—but it was no longer a cage.

Bart was walking in the center of the group, his hands resting on the strap of his lute. He wasn't humming today. He was focused.

"Okay, Formation 1," Bart said, his voice carrying that crystal-clear resonance. "But, leave the last mob for me. You pull aggro on it, Nate. I need to test my melee damage."

Nathan glanced back, his brow furrowing. "Melee? Bart, you have a Constitution of 8. If one of these Phase 2 wolves catches you without a shield, you're going to be a blue pixel fountain."

"I have the Resonance Strike talent, remember?" Bart grinned, sliding his lute around to his front—not to play it, but to hold it by the neck like a club. "And I want to see what that melodic buff does for a physical hit. What was it called again, Tim?"

Timothy Healz—Tim—checked his HUD. "Battle Hymn of the Miscreant. It’s a +2 flat physical damage bonus. Plus, your Resonance Strike scales your attack and damage with Charisma instead of Strength."

"Right. My Strength is an 8, which is a minus-one modifier," Bart said, doing the mental math. "But my Charisma is an 18, which is a plus-four. That’s a five-point swing before we even count the hymn. I want to see if this plywood piece of junk can actually crack a skull."

They moved beyond the Oakhaven gates and back toward the silver-barked forest. The forest was different now. The trees were no longer semi-transparent; they were solid, their bark rough and mossy. The 'watercolor' mist had been replaced by a light morning fog that clung to the ferns.

*Status: Level 1 (168/1000 XP)*

"Targets ahead," David whispered, dropping into a crouch. "Three Vitreous Wolves. And look at them. They’re not just blue silhouettes anymore."

The wolves were leaner, their fur rendered in individual, silvery strands that rippled as they moved. Their eyes weren't just glowing; they had pupils—slits of predatory intelligence that were fixed on the group.

"Engagement in three," Nathan said. "Two. One. Go!"

Nathan slammed his shield into the first wolf, the impact producing a sickening, organic *thud* that made Bart wince. The Level 2 haptics were definitely stronger. David appeared behind the second wolf, his daggers carving deep, bloody gashes that flickered with blue particles.

"Nathan, pull the third one!" Bart shouted.

Nathan swung his sword in a wide, sweeping arc, catching the third wolf across its flank. The creature snarled and turned its attention to the Paladin, its teeth snapping inches from his greave.

"I’ve got it!" Nathan yelled, bracing his shield. "He’s all yours, Bart! Do the thing!"

Bart didn't hesitate. He didn't start a chant or a twist. He just gripped the neck of his lute with both hands and stepped forward.

*Hymn Unleashed: Battle Hymn of the Miscreant.*

The indigo energy rippled out, but this time, Bart focused it inward. He felt the resonance humming through the wood of the lute, turning the plywood into a conductor for his 18 Charisma.

"Eat this!" Bart roared.

He swung the lute in a massive, horizontal arc. Because of the Resonance Strike talent, the swing wasn't clunky or weak. It was fast—impossibly fast for a man with 8 Strength. The Charisma stat wasn't just a social value; it was a force of will that dictated how his body interacted with the haptic mesh.

The lute slammed into the wolf’s head just as it was preparing to lung at Nathan.

*CRACK.*

The sound was staggering. It wasn't the hollow thud of wood on bone; it was a resonant, musical explosion. The wolf’s head snapped to the side, and its digital form flickered violently as the damage value popped up in Bart’s HUD.

*Combat Log Update:*
*Resonance Strike: 28 damage.*

"Whoa!" David yelled, momentarily distracted from his own wolf. "Twenty-eight? My backstab only did twenty-four!"

"The scaling is real!" Bart shouted, his eyes wide with adrenaline. 

The wolf was dazed, its HP bar nearly empty. Bart didn't give it a chance to recover. He swung again, this time a vertical overhead strike.

*Hymn Bonus Added: +2 Physical Damage.*
*Resonance Strike: 31 damage.*

The wolf didn't even whine. It shattered into a million blue pixels, the impact sending a spray of digital light across Bart’s tunics.

The silence that followed was different than the one in the tavern. This was a silence of shock. Nathan, David, Tim, and Mihwa were all staring at Bart, who was still holding his lute like a battle-axe.

"You just... you just one-shot a Level 1 wolf with a lute," Tim said, his voice sounding faint. "Bart, that weapon has a base damage of 1-4. You’re outputting thirty per hit."

"Charismatic Casting," Bart said, breathing hard, his 180 Mana pool having barely dipped from the single Hymn. "It’s not just for talking. If I believe the hit is going to land, the engine makes it land. My Charisma is the variable."

"But the durability," Mihwa pointed out, stepping forward and touching the wood of the lute. The head of the instrument was cracked, a jagged line running through the lacquer. "Bart, you can't keep doing that. The lute isn't meant for melee."

"Then I need a better instrument," Bart said, a fierce light in his eyes. "A battle-lute. Something reinforced with iron or silver-bark. If my personality is the damage, I need a conductor that can handle the feedback."

"Formation 1 complete," Nathan said, though he was still looking at the spot where the wolf had been. "I think we just found our secret weapon. The Bard isn't just a buff-bot. He’s a tactical nuke."

"A beautiful tactical nuke," Bart corrected, wiping a smear of blue particles from his cheek. He looked at the cracked lute and then toward the Oakhaven gates. "The Summer Solstice is coming, guys. And I think I just found my solo."

*Combat Log Update:*
*Target: Vitreous Wolf (x3)*
*Total Base Experience: 300 XP*
*Party Modifier Applied: Vitreous Cohesion Bonus (1.2x)*
*Total Adjusted XP: 360 XP*
*Shared Experience: 72 XP per member.*

*Total XP: 240/1000.*

"We’re a quarter of the way there," Tim said, his analyst brain already calculating the time-to-level. "If we find an elite pack or a quest-boss, we hit Level 2 by sunset."

"Then let's go find one," Bart said, slinging the cracked lute over his shoulder. He looked at his friends, and for a moment, the Level 2 immersion felt almost perfect. "The noise is getting louder. And I love the way it sounds."

As they walked deeper into the forest, the 25% drag felt like a challenge rather than a burden. They were moving with a new confidence, a group of miscreants who had finally realized that in *Vitreous Online*, your stats weren't just numbers. They were weapons.

And Beautiful Bart was just getting started.
