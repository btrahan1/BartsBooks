# Chapter 18: The Level 2 Threshold

The forest beyond the Oakhaven perimeter was thick with the scent of damp earth and crushed mint. The Phase 2 transition had turned the once-silent orchard into a living, breathing landscape where the rustle of leaves was no longer a digital placeholder, but a distinct, multi-layered sound.

The group had just finished tearing through their third pack of Vitreous Wolves. The combat had been faster, thewolves more aggressive, but their coordination—the 'Vitreous Cohesion'—was holding steady. Nathan’s shield work was becoming instinctive, and David’s shadow-steps were cutting through the 25% physics drag with an efficiency that was terrifying to watch.

"Hold up," Bart said, raising a hand. He leaned back against a silver-barked oak, his cracked lute resting on his shoulder. "Short break. My legs feel like they’ve been through a gym session, and this physics drag is starting to tax my neural link."

They gathered in a small clearing, the dappled morning light filtering through the canopy. Tim immediately began his ritual of checking the HUD, his fingers dancing through the air.

"Status Check," Bart said, his voice a bit raspy from the earlier 'Resonance Strike' shout. "How are we on mana and health? I’m at about sixty percent MP, but I feel like I could hum a marathon."

"I'm at seventy percent MP," Tim reported, his eyes tracking the blue bars. "Nate’s HP is at ninety-five. You and David are at full. Mihwa is at eighty-five percent mana."

"I'm fine," Nathan said, sitting on a mossy rock and wiping sweat from his forehead—actual sweat, another Level 2 upgrade. "My stamina is the bottle-neck right now. That shield is heavier than it looks in the brochures."

"Also," Bart said, looking down at his tunics. He noticed a small, glowing icon in the corner of his vision that he’d been ignoring during the heat of the fight. "Are we getting any drops at all? I haven't been paying attention. Are we getting paid for this?"

David grinned, opening his own inventory. "I was wondering when you’d notice. Check your 'Party Wallet', Bart."

Bart tapped the icon. A window expanded, revealing a list of items and a numerical value that made his eyes widen.

*Party Inventory:*
*- Vitreous Wolf Hide (x12)*
*- Cracked Wolf Fang (x8)*
*- Essence Fragment (x2)*

*Party Currency:*
*- 42 Vitreous Bits (VB)*

"Vitreous Bits?" Bart asked. "What is that, like four cents?"

"One hundred Bits equals one Vitreous Coin," Tim explained, shifting his display so the others could see. "And Thorne said the suite was three silver a night. I’m guessing a Coin is roughly equivalent to a silver piece in Traditional D&D terms. We’ve managed to earn about half a silver in forty minutes of grinding."

"Fourty minutes for half a silver?" Bart scoffed. "If I played the tavern for an hour, I’d probably clear ten silver just in tips."

"But the hides, Bart," David pointed out, highlighting the twelve wolf skins. "Thorne specifically mentioned a bounty for these. In a fixed-population server, materials are the real currency. The bits are just a base-line to keep the economy from stalling. The hides... those are the tickets to your new lute."

"Auto-loot is a lifesaver," Mihwa said, leaning her staff against a tree. "I saw the bits flying toward us every time a wolf shattered. As long as we’re within twenty meters, the engine just handles the distribution."

"Shared materials, split currency," Bart muttered, closing the window. "I can live with that. Especially since I’m the one doing the heavy lifting with the plywood."

"And the XP?" Nathan asked, lookikng at Tim.

Tim’s expression shifted, a small, triumphant smile breaking through his analytical mask. "We’re at 928 XP each. The Vitreous Cohesion Bonus is doing most of the work. If we find one more pack—even a small one—we hit the threshold."

"Wait, 928?" Bart stood up, his fatigue momentarily forgotten. "That means we’re... seventy-two points away. One pack of three wolves is worth 360 XP for the group, shared five ways... that’s exactly seventy-two XP each."

"Exactly," Tim nodded. "One more pack. And we 'Ding' for Level 2."

The group stood up as one. The 'Watercolor' era was truly over. They were no longer the 'new blood' trying to navigate a simulation. They were a party on the edge of their first major milestone.

"Let's find them," Nathan said, his shield already braced. "Formation 1. Bart, you keep the songs twisting. David, you find the flank. Tim, Mihwa... keep us in the green."

They moved through the brush, their steps synchronized. The Level 2 physics felt like a partner now rather than an obstacle. 

They found the targets near a small stream: a pack of four Vitreous Wolves, led by a slightly larger alpha with a jagged scar across its muzzle. The alpha let out a low, textured growl that vibrated in Bart’s chest.

"Engagement!" Nathan shouted.

The fight was a blur of blue light and silver fur. Bart felt the resonance of his **Battle Hymn** pulsing through the group, the +2 damage bonus turning David’s backstabs into lethal eruptions of pixels. He was twisting his **Recovery Hymn** every five seconds, the 3-per-tick heals keeping Nathan’s HP bar locked at ninety percent despite the alpha’s vicious snaps.

*Mana Chant active.*

Bart’s mana was holding steady, the eerie hum supporting the rhythm of the lute. 

"The Alpha is down!" David yelled, his daggers burying deep in the creature’s neck.

The final wolf snarled and tried to retreat, but Mihwa’s **Verdant Pulse** had slowed its movements, the green energy of her staff clinging to its legs like spectral vines.

"Finish it!" Bart roared.

He stepped forward, gripping the cracked neck of his lute. He didn't use Resonance Strike this time; he simply let the Battle Hymn do the work as Nathan’s sword delivered the final, crushing blow.

*VICTORY.*

The wolf shattered. The blue bits flew toward the party wallet. And then, a sound erupted that was louder than any tavern cheer or server announcement. It was a golden, orchestral swell—the sound of a hundred trumpets and a celestial choir.

*CONGRATULATIONS!*
*YOU HAVE ATTAINED LEVEL 2.*

A brilliant pillar of light descended upon each of them, the energy washing away the neural fatigue and the physics drag. Bart felt his mana pool snap to full, and his health bar expanded as the engine recalibrated his stats.

*Status Update: Level 2 Bard*
*HP increased to 80.*
*MP increased to 180.*
*Attribute Point Gained: +1 CHA (Bart logic).*
*New Skill Unlocked: Dirge of Discord.*

"We did it!" Mihwa cheered, spinning around in the golden light. "Level 2! The world... it feels even sharper now."

"Total XP: 1000/3000," Tim reported, his voice filled with a rare excitement. "The curve just tripled. But our output is going to triple with it."

Bart looked at his cracked lute. The golden light of the level-up hadn't repaired the wood, but he didn't care. He looked at his friends—the miscreants who were now the first Level 2 party on Server One.

"Formation 1 success," Bart said, a wide, predatory grin on his face. "Now, let's go back to Oakhaven and see what Thorne has for a group of 'Quality' such as ourselves."

The noise of the forest was still there, but it was no longer a challenge. It was a soundtrack. And Beautiful Bart was ready for the next movement.
