# Chapter 20: Hallelujah

The village square of Oakhaven was bustling with the frantic energy of Day 2 registration-lock. Other players were running in disorganized packs, haggling with the blacksmith over iron scrap or comparing the dull stats of their starting gear. Nathan, David, Tim, and Mihwa had just finished a successful circuit of the vendors. Their party wallet, now containing several dozen Vitreous Coins from their wolf-kill bounties, felt heavy with potential.

"I saw a reinforced shield at the quartermaster's," Nathan said, his eyes bright with the prospect of actual defense. "If we pool the rest of our bits, we can get that and maybe a decent leather jerkin for David."

"The gear upgrade is essential for Level 2," Tim agreed, his HUD showing the predicted difficulty spike for the upcoming Orchard quests. "But where’s Bart? He said he was going to 'work some magic,' but he’s been in that tavern for an hour."

"He was looking pretty depressed about that lute," Mihwa said, her voice filled with genuine concern. "I’ve never seen him look that broken. I hope he didn't do something stupid like spend our rent on a repair kit."

"It’s Bart," David said, a small smirk playing on his lips as they approached the heavy doors of the *Thirsty Oak*. "Stupid and brilliant are the same stat for him. Let's go see what kind of mess he’s made."

They pushed open the doors, expecting to find Bart slumped at the bar or perhaps nursing a flagon in the corner. What they saw instead stopped them dead in their tracks.

The tavern was silent. Not the scripted silence of an empty room, but the heavy, expectant silence of a crowd that was witnessing something impossible. The center of the tavern had been cleared, the two tables Bart had used the night before pushed together once again to form a stage.

Bart was sitting in the exact center of that stage. But he wasn't slumped. He wasn't crying. He was holding something that looked like it had been woven from the night sky itself.

"What is that?" Nathan whispered, his hand going to the hilt of his training sword. 

The lute in Bart’s lap was a masterpiece of Level 2 rendering. The wood was a deep, shimmering obsidian, polished to a mirror finish that reflected the hearth fire in a way that plywood never could. The strings weren't nylon anymore; they were fine, silver filaments that seemed to hold their own internal starlight. As Bart caressed the body of the instrument, it didn't just vibrate; it *glowed*.

"He's got a Masterwork," Tim muttered, his eyes wide. "That’s not a repair. That’s an upgrade from the Silver Citadel tier. Where the hell did he get it?"

"Shhh," Mihwa hissed, stepping forward.

Bart didn't look up at them. He was staring at the strings, his 19 Charisma aura radiating from him like a physical heat. He struck a single chord. It wasn't a Battle Hymn. It was a sound so deep and resonant that the floorboards of the tavern seemed to sigh in relief.

Then, he began to play a song they all knew—a song of the world 'upside', a song that had followed them into the pods.

He started slow. The melody was mournful, a low, pulsing rhythm that matched the heartbeat of the tavern. Bart’s fingers moved over the silver strings with a delicacy that made the Star-Field Lute weep. It wasn't just music; it was a memory.

"He's singing..." Mihwa started, her voice a breathless whisper. "No way."

Bart’s voice rose, no longer cracking with the feigned grief he’d shown Thorne, but rich and powerful, amplified by the lute’s Masterwork resonance.

*“I've heard there was a secret chord, that David played and it pleased the Lord…”*

"Omigod," David whispered, his jaw dropping. "He's singing Hallelujah."

The tavern patrons—NPCs and players alike—were frozen. The 19 Charisma was performing a total overwrite of the server’s social environment. Players who had been shouting about quests were now standing still, tears standing in their eyes as the familiar lyrics washed over them in high-definition audio.

As Bart hit the first chorus, the tempo began to shift. It wasn't just a mournful dirge anymore. He was increasing the speed, his fingers beginning to blur over the starlight strings. The mournful melody was evolving into something triumphant, something defiant.

*“Hallelujah… Hallelujah… Hallelujah… Hallelu-u-u-jah!”*

With every repetition of the word, the starlight from the lute grew brighter. The silver strings were vibrating so fast they were leaving trails of light in the air—ghosts of notes that lingered in the Level 2 render. Bart was leaning into the performance, his 19 Charisma turning the tavern into a cathedral of sound.

"This isn't a hymn," Tim whispered, checking his HUD. "There’s no buff icon. No regen. It’s just... pure social pressure. He's breaking the game logic through sheer performance."

By the third verse, Bart was at full speed. He wasn't just strumming; he was attacking the lute, his movements a symphony of kinetic energy. The song had transformed from a prayer into a celebration, a roar of survival in a world that was trying to cage them.

The crowd erupted on the final 'Hallelujah'. It wasn't a cheer; it was a rhythmic stomping of feet that shook the tavern to its foundations. Thorne was standing behind the bar, his eyes fixed on Bart with a look of pure, unadulterated awe. The 'architect' hadn't just made the wood sing; he’d made it scream with life.

Bart struck the final, triumphant chord, the starlight from the lute flaring one last time before fading into a soft, midnight glow. He sat there in the silence that followed, breathing hard, his 19 Charisma-charged eyes finally lifting to meet those of his friends.

He didn't say a word. He just smiled—that same mischievous, beautiful smile that told them exactly how many secrets he was keeping.

"Formation 2," Bart whispered as the tavern finally exploded into a cacophony of applause and shouting. 

The group moved toward the stage, their shopping forgotten.

"Where did you get that?" Nathan asked, practically shouting over the noise of the crowd. "That lute... Bart, that has to be worth a thousand gold!"

"Gift from a fan," Bart said, standing up and slinging the Star-Field Lute over his shoulder with an effortless grace. He looked back at Thorne, who was already heading toward them with two flagons of the good ale. "Thorne and I had a... meaningful dialogue about the nature of sacrifice."

"Sacrifice?" Tim asked, narrowing his eyes. "Bart, what did you do?"

"I gave him a show he’ll never forget," Bart grinned. "And in exchange, he gave me the keys to the solstice. How was the quest turn-in? Did we get the bits for Nate’s shield?"

"We got the bits," David said, still looking at the lute. "But I think we just became the most famous players on Server One. People are recording this on their haptic logs, Bart. You're going to be a viral legend by sunrise."

"Viral is just another word for popular," Bart said, accepting a flagon from Thorne with a respectful nod. "And the Summer Solstice is only six days away. We’ve got Level 2 to master, a festival to head-line, and apparently, a lot of shopping to do."

"You really are a piece of work," Mihwa laughed, shaking her head as she leaned into his side. "Hallelujah, Bart? Really?"

"It seemed appropriate," Bart said, taking a sip of the ale. It tasted like triumph. "The world is getting louder, Mihwa. I might as well give it a chorus."

They sat back down at their corner table, the group of five together once more. The room on the third floor was waiting, the village of Oakhaven was theirs, and for the first time since the Spin-Up began, the 7:1 reality felt like a life worth living.

But as Bart looked at the Star-Field Lute resting against the table, he knew the 'Hallelujah' was just the beginning. 

The real music was still to come.
