# Chapter 21: The Shred Effect

The silence that followed the final chord of 'Hallelujah' was the loudest thing Bart had ever heard. It wasn't just the lack of sound; it was the total suspension of game logic. The patrons of the *Thirsty Oak* were still staring at him, their AI-scripted routines momentarily paralyzed by the sheer social pressure of 19 Charisma.

Bart exhaled, a long, slow breath that felt like it was clearing out the digital cobwebs from his brain. He slung the Star-Field Lute over his shoulder—the wood cool and smooth against his back—and stepped down from the stage.

His friends were waiting for him at the table, their expressions a mix of awe and deep, technical suspicion.

"Bart," Nathan said, leaning forward. "Where. Did. You. Get. That?"

"Thorne gave it to me," Bart said, sitting down and taking a long pull from his flagon. The ale was cold, the Level 2 immersion making the condensation on the pewter feel incredibly real. "We had a moment. A bonding experience over the nature of artistic sacrifice."

"And by 'bonding experience,' you mean you used your Level 2 attribute point to hit 19 Charisma and then emotionally manipulated him with a broken piece of plywood," Tim said, his voice flat. He wasn't even looking at Bart; he was staring at his own HUD.

"I prefer the term 'Narrative Engineering'," Bart grinned. Reachng for a piece of bread, he paused. A notification was blinking in the corner of his eye—not the golden level-up light, but a smaller, platinum-colored icon he hadn't seen before.

*Passive Attribute Increase Detected.*

Bart froze, his hand halfway to the bread. "Whoa. Wait. Guys, look at this."

He swiped his hand through the air, sharing his status screen with the group.

*Name: Bart (The Beautiful Bard)*
*Level: 2*
*Strength: 8*
*Dexterity: 11 (+1)*
*Constitution: 8*
*Intelligence: 12*
*Wisdom: 10*
*Charisma: 19*

"Wait," Bart stared at the '11' next to Dexterity. "I got +1 Dex! I know my stats are really low other than charisma, but I think I got that point from all that 'shredding' I been doing! Any of you bookworms looked into how that works yet?"

Tim’s eyes widened, his fingers immediately flying into a flurry of data-retrieval gestures. "I thought it was just a rumor from the pre-launch forums. Neural Adaptation."

"Neural what?" Nathan asked.

"Neural Adaptation," Tim repeated, his voice dropping into 'analyst mode'. "Vitreous Online isn't just a D&D clone. It uses a hybrid engine. Because it’s FIVR, the system monitors your neural pathways. If you perform a repetitive, high-intensity task that taxes your haptic sync, the engine concludes that your 'digital persona' needs better hardware. It physically re-wires your attribute weighting."

"So... because I was shredding on those silver strings at a hundred and twenty beats per minute," Bart said, a slow, predatory grin forming on his face. "The game decided my fingers needed to be faster?"

"Exactly," Tim nodded. "Dexterity isn't just about dodging arrows. It’s about fine motor control. That performance of Hallelujah wasn't just a song; it was a neural stress-test. You pushed the engine’s limit for Bardic performance, and it rewarded you with a permanent stat gain."

"Is there a cap?" David asked, his daggers already twitching in his hands. He was thinking about how many shadow-steps it would take to gain a point of Strength.

"According to the Deep Dive leaks, it’s capped at one point per week," Tim said. "To prevent players from just macroing activities or burning out their neural links. But still... that’s fifty-two points a year just from playing the game."

"One point a week," Bart whispered, looking at his hands. "If I can shred for Dex, and Nate can block for Strength, and Tim can... I don't know, calculate for Intelligence? We could be the most optimized party on the server without even fighting."

"But we *should* fight," Nathan stood up, his shield clutched tight. "I want to see what that starlight lute does in the field. If it boosted your Charisma that much in a social setting, what does it do to a Battle Hymn?"

"Agreed," Bart said, standing up with him. "Formation 1, but with more starlight. Let's go test the new hardware."

They walked out of the tavern and back toward the village gates. The Level 2 immersion was in full swing now, the physics drag feeling like a light, supportive resistance rather than a burden. The world looked incredibly sharp—the bark of the trees had individual ridges, and the morning fog was weaving through the silver orchard in a way that looked like a cinematic trailer.

They reached the 'Alpha Woods', a Level 2 area just beyond the initial tutorial zone. The air here was colder, and the Vitreous Wolves were larger, their silvery-blue fur glowing with a faint, predatory light.

"Target spotted," David whispered, dropping into the brush. "Vitreous Alpha. Level 3. And it’s got two pack-mates."

"Nate, you take the Alpha," Bart commanded, his voice carrying that new, 19-Charisma resonance. "Tim, keep the shields ready. Mihwa, slow the pack-mates. I'm going to open with the Star-Field resonance."

Bart reached back and pulled the obsidian lute from his shoulder. As he touched the silver filaments, the starlight flared, illuminating the dark undergrowth.

*Hymn Unleashed: Battle Hymn of the Miscreant.*

The indigo energy rippled out, but this time, it was laced with silver sparks. The sound was different—cleaner, sharper. Bart felt the resonance echoing through his bones, the Masterwork quality of the instrument turning his simple Level 1 hymn into a Tier 2 sonic event.

*Star-Field Bonus Applied: +2 to Bardic Resonance Scaling.*

"Whoa!" Nathan yelled as he charged. "I feel... lighter! My Strength modifier just doubled!"

"That’s the scaling!" Bart shouted, his fingers blurring over the strings. With his new 11 Dexterity, the 'Twist' felt effortless. He shifted into the **Recovery Hymn**, the silver starlight washing over Nathan as the Alpha Wolf’s teeth snapped against the Paladin's shield.

*Recovery Hymn: 5 HP/MP per tick.*

"Five per tick?" Tim gasped. "Bart, that’s double our baseline! At Level 2?"

"It’s the lute!" Bart roared.

He wasn't just playing anymore. He was 'shredding'. He was using the high-tempo rhythm of 'Hallelujah' to maintain the overlap of the hymns with a precision he’d never had before. The 11 Dexterity made the transition between notes seamless, creating a continuous wall of starlight that was turning the fight into a one-sided slaughter.

David appeared behind the Alpha, his daggers flashing. "The Alpha is at thirty percent! Bart, do the thing!"

Bart didn't hesitate. He shifted his grip, sliding into the **Resonance Strike** stance. This time, he didn't just swing the lute like a club. He struck a massive, power-chord that released a physical wave of starlight.

*Resonance Strike: 42 damage.*

The Alpha Wolf didn't even have time to howl. The sonic impact shattered its digital form, sending a shockwave of blue pixels through the clearing. The two pack-mates, dazed by the resonance, were quickly finished off by Nathan and Mihwa.

*VICTORY.*

The silence returned, but this time, it was filled with the soft, chime-like sound of the Star-Field Lute cooling down.

"Forty-two damage," David said, cleanng his daggers. "Bart, you just out-damaged my backstab again. With a *musical instrument*."

"The Neural Adaptation," Bart said, looking at his screen. "It’s not just about the stats, Tim. It’s about how the engine translates the performance. The more 'perfect' the song, the more 'perfect' the hit."

"Formation 1 complete," Nathan said, breathing hard. "But I think we need a new name for it. Formation 1 feels too... basic for how we’re playing now."

"Formation 19," Bart suggested, slinging the lute back over his shoulder. He looked at his friends, the starlight from his instrument still reflecting in their eyes. "For the Charisma that got us here."

"I can live with that," David grinned.

"Level 2 is going to be a breeze," Mihwa said, her green mana already pulsing as she prepared for the next hunt.

Bart looked at the sky, the Phase 2 sun-light feeling warm on his skin. He had his lute, he had his party, and he had a newfound respect for the 'shred'.

"Capped at one point a week," Bart whispered, a plan already forming in his mind for his next performance. "Let's see if I can get some Strength from all this lute-carrying."

The noise of Oakhaven was calling them back, but for the Beautiful Bart, the real music was just beginning.

*Combat Log Update:*
*Target: Vitreous Alpha (x1), Vitreous Wolf (x2)*
*Total Base Experience: 450 XP*
*Party Modifier Applied: Vitreous Cohesion Bonus (1.2x)*
*Total Adjusted XP: 540 XP*
*Shared Experience: 108 XP per member.*

*Total XP: 1108/3000.*

"One tenth of the way to Level 3," Tim reported. "And we did it in five minutes."

"Then let's find ten more Alphas," Bart said, his eyes fixed on the horizon. "I've got a festival to prepare for, and I want to be the loudest thing in the village."
