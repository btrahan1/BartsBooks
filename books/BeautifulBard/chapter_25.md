# Chapter 25: World First

The golden text of the announcement hung in the air of the quartz chamber for a full minute, pulsing with a frequency that matched the beating of Bart’s heart. 

*WORLD FIRST: MINIBOSS*
*Bart's Beautiful Band has achieved a World First!*

"We did it," Mihwa whispered, her hand trembling as she touched the spectral notification. "We actually did it. On Day 2."

"The whole server just saw that," David said, his eyes scanning the empty corners of the chamber as if expecting a crowd to materialize. "Bart... our names are on every screen in Oakhaven. Every screen in the reach."

Bart sat back down on his rock, the Star-Field Lute resting across his knees. His 19 Charisma was giving him a strange, sensory feedback—a 'Social Pressure' spike that felt like a localized thunderstorm. It wasn't just a notification; it was an event.

*Achievement Unlocked: World First Mini-Boss.*
*Party Title Awarded: Pioneer of the Silver Orchard.*
*Permanent Bonus: +2% XP Gain (All Members).*

"Pioneer of the Silver Orchard," Nathan read aloud, the title appearing as a shimmering silver prefix above his name on the party sheet. "That’s... that’s a decent chunk of reputation right there."

"Look at the XP," Tim said, his voice climbing an octave. "Bart, the bonus from the achievement just kicked in."

Bart looked at his HUD. 

*Combat Experience: 1,500 XP (Shared).*
*Achievement Bonus: +500 XP (World First).*
*Current Level: 2 (1,450 / 3,000).*

"We’re halfway to Level 3," Bart said, a slow grin spreading across his face. "And we haven't even seen the settlement yet. We’re going to be the highest-level players in the zone by a wide margin."

But as the adrenaline began to fade, the weight of the achievement started to settle in. In a game like *Vitreous Online*, where the physics were 7:1 and the physics drag was real, fame wasn't just a number. It was a target.

"Formation 19 is public now," Tim pointed out, his brow furrowing as he looked at the chat logs that were beginning to scroll at the bottom of his vision. "People are asking. In the global chat. 'Who is Bart? What is the Beautiful Band? How did they kill an Elite at Level 2?'"

"Let them ask," Bart said, standing up and slinging the lute. "They won't find the Silver Orchard easily, and even if they do, they don't have a Star-Field. Or a tank who can hold a golem on one knee."

He looked at his team. Their gear was in tatters—Nathan’s shield was literally held together by glowing pixels, and David’s daggers were dull and chipped. They looked like a group of survivors, not world-first pioneers.

"We need to move," Bart said, his voice taking on the commanding tone of a 19-CHA leader. "The announcement just gave away our position to anyone with a map and a brain. There will be players heading this way—some to congratulate us, sure, but most to see if they can scavenge what we missed."

"Or to see what kind of loot a world-first party carries," David added, his hand going to his broken hilt.

"Exactly," Bart said. "Mihwa, can you hide our trail? Use that Druid haptic-masking."

Mihwa nodded, her emerald eyes glowing as she swept her staff over the quartz floor. "I can blur the haptic signatures. They’ll find the chamber, but they won't know which way we went."

"Good," Bart said. "We head for Oakhaven. We sell the materials, we get Nate a shield that isn't made of cardboard, and we find a blacksmith who knows how to handle Silver-Bark."

As they left the chamber, the 'Pioneer' titles above their heads caught the fading starlight of the Alpha Woods. They moved with a new sense of purpose, the '7:1' drag feeling lighter than it ever had before. 

Bart's mind was already racing ahead. The Summer Solstice Gala was only a few days (subjective time) away. With the 'Pioneer' reputation and a 'World First' under their belts, the 'Social Engineering' portion of his plan was about to get a lot easier.

"Hey Bart," Nathan said, stepping in line behind him. "You think Thorne heard the announcement?"

"Thorne?" Bart laughed, the sound echoing through the silver trees. "Thorne probably just choked on his ale. He gave me a Masterwork instrument for ten bits and a promise. Now he finds out I used it to take down the first boss on the server."

"He’s going to want a cut," Tim predicted.

"He can have a front-row seat to the Solstice," Bart said. "That’s the deal. But for now... let's just enjoy the walk. We’re pioneers, guys. The woods belong to us."

They pressed on, the silver-barked trees closing in behind them as Mihwa's magic erased their footsteps. The Alpha Woods were still dangerous, and their gear was still broken, but for the first time since the Spin-Up began, they didn't feel like players in a game.

They felt like legends in the making.

*Navigation Log:*
*Destination: Oakhaven Settlement.*
*Estimated Arrival: 4 Hours (Subjective).*
*Status: EXHAUSTED but TRIUMPHANT.*

"Bart's Beautiful Band," Bart whispered to himself, the name appearing in gold on his HUD. "I think I like the sound of that."
