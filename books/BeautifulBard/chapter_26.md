# Chapter 26: The Oakhaven Welcome

The walk back to Oakhaven had been a blur of adrenalin and nervous energy. But as the silver-barked trees finally gave way to the manicured dirt roads of the settlement’s outskirts, the 'Watercolor' world of Day 1 felt like it had been replaced by a high-definition documentary. Even with the persistent haptic drag, the air felt sharper, the sun brighter, and the tension in the party higher.

As the gate of Oakhaven came into view, the group slowed. 

The streets weren't just busy. They were lined. 

On either side of the main thoroughfare, both NPCs and players were standing, their eyes fixed on the forest road. The NPCs were murmuring, their digital AI reacting to the 'World First' reputation flag that was now hard-coded into the party's identities. But the players... they were silent, their HUDs likely glowing with the same gold text that had rattled Bart’s pod four hours ago.

"Look at them," Nathan whispered, his shattered shield looking even worse in the afternoon sun. "They’re waiting for us."

Bart gripped the Star-Field Lute, his 19 Charisma giving him a strange, almost physical sensation of the crowd's focus. It was a 'Social Gravity' so thick he could almost lean against it. He was tempted—incredibly tempted—to strike a power chord and belt out a high-resolution version of "We Are the Champions." The irony of it would have been delicious.

But his thoughts were elsewhere. He looked at David’s chipped daggers, Tim’s flickering mana-ring, and Mihwa’s frayed druid robes. 

"Not today," Bart said, his voice quiet but carrying the weight of a leader. "Let's keep the performance for later. We have work to do."

As they crossed the threshold of the gate, a collective breath seemed to escape the crowd. An NPC guard snapped to attention—a scripted reaction to the 'Pioneer' title—and a few players tentatively clapped, while others whispered in hurried, private messages.

"Bart’s Beautiful Band," someone called out. "Is it true? You took down the Keeper?"

Bart didn't stop. He gave a sharp, professional nod to the crowd, his aura of presence pushing through the social pressure with ease. "The Orchard is open," he said, his voice amplified by the lute’s passive resonance. "But the road is still dangerous. Watch your gear."

He led the party straight through the gauntlet, his eyes fixed on the smoking chimney of the settlement’s forge.

"Let's get our gear sorted out first," Bart said to the group, ignoring the trail of onlookers following them at a respectful distance. "We sell the common hides, we reforge with the obsidian, and we get Nate a shield that can actually take a hit. Then, we head toward the tavern."

"The tavern?" Mihwa asked, catching the glint in his eye. "You already have the starlight lute, Bart. What else are we doing at a tavern besides celebrating?"

"Celebrating is part of it," Bart said, his grin returning—the one that usually preceded a massive shift in the local economy. "But I have a plan. A plan that involves the Solstice, the Mayor, and about forty-seven thousand people needing a reason to stay in this zone."

"Uh oh," Mihwa grinned, her earlier exhaustion replaced by a familiar sense of mock-dread. "This can't be good. Every time you say 'I have a plan,' my bar bill goes up and someone ends up losing their shirt."

"Not this time," Bart laughed. "This time, everyone wins. But first... shopping!"

They reached the forge, the heat of the fire hitting their faces with a haptic intensity that made the 7:1 world feel more real than the one they’d left behind. The blacksmith, a massive Dwarf with a beard that looked like it was rendered with individual hair-physics, looked up from his anvil. His eyes went straight to the 'Pioneer' titles above their heads, and then to the Star-Field Lute.

"Pioneers," the dwarf rumbled, his voice like grinding stones. "Heard the announcement. Didn't think I'd see the lot of you so soon. What’ve you brought me?"

"Obsidian Essence," Bart said, dropping the glowing shards onto the counter. "And Masterwork Silver-Bark. We need a 'World First' set of gear, Master Smith. And we need it before the sun sets."

"Obsidian..." the dwarf whispered, his hand hovering over the shards. "I haven't seen this since the Beta cycles. Right then. You provide the materials, I provide the sweat. But it won't be cheap."

"We have bits," Bart said, tossing a heavy pouch of Vitreous Bits onto the table. "And we have the reputation of the first band to survive the Orchard. I think that covers the premium, don't you?"

The dwarf chuckled, the haptic vibration of his laughter rattling the tools on the walls. "It does. Get your gear on the racks. I’ve a long night ahead."

As they stepped out of the forge, leaving their battered equipment behind, the party felt a strange sense of Nakedness. But Bart's stride was as confident as ever. He was looking toward the 'Oakhaven Arms', the largest tavern in the settlement, where the lights were already beginning to flicker on.

"Formation 19 is taking a break," Bart announced. "From here on out, it’s all social engineering. Mihwa, Tim... I hope you guys are ready to dance."

"I knew it," Mihwa sighed, though her smile was genuine. "Let's go, Chief. Show us the plan."

*Settlement Log: Oakhaven.*
*Status: RECOGNIZED.*
*Current Objective: Gear Reforging & Tavern Initiative.*

Bart led the way, his 19 Charisma lighting the path like a beacon in the digital dusk. The 'Beautiful Bard' was about to perform his most important set yet, and the stage was already set.
