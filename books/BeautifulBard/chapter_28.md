# Chapter 28: Signatures of the City

The Mayor’s Office in Oakhaven was a masterwork of digital architecture. Carved from the same silver-bark as the forest elite, the room smelled of cedar and old parchment, with high-resolution dust motes dancing in the afternoon light. It was a space designed to intimidate players with its formality, its large mahogany desk acting as a barrier between the common traveler and the settlement’s authority.

Mayor Weston sat behind that desk, his spectacles sliding down a nose that was rendered with precisely three tiny, high-definition liver spots. He was a Level 10 Civil AI, programmed for bureaucratic efficiency and a healthy dose of skepticism toward 'The Immersion-Born'.

Or at least, he was suppose to be.

The heavy oak doors of the office didn't just open; they were swept aside as if by a sudden, indigo-silver wind. Bart stepped into the room, the Star-Field Lute slung casually across his back, its starlight filaments pulsing in time with his stride. Behind him, Tim and Mihwa hovered near the threshold, looking like they were expecting the Captain of the Guard to materialize and arrest them for trespassing.

"Mayor Weston!" Bart exclaimed, his voice filling the room with a resonance that made the cedar walls hum. "Just the man I was looking for! How are you, my friend?"

The mayor looked like a deer in the headlights. He froze mid-inkwell-dip, his digital eyelids fluttering as he processed the 'Celebrity Aura' radiating from the figure in front of him. The 'Pioneer' title above Bart’s head was glowing with a gold light that seemed to overrule the room’s standard lighting.

"Fff... friend?" Weston stammered, his skeptical dialogue tree completely collapsing under the weight of Bart’s 19 Charisma. "I... I don't believe we've had the pleasure of... er..."

"Of course, Westy!" Bart said, closing the distance to the desk in three confident strides. He didn't wait for an invitation to sit; instead, he leaned one hip against the edge of the mahogany desk, looking down at the elder NPC with a warmth that felt like a localized heatwave. "You don't mind if I call you Westy, do you? I feel like we've known each other for ages!"

Tim, watching from the door, winced. "Westy?" he whispered to Mihwa. "He’s calling the highest authority in the zone 'Westy'?"

"Shh," Mihwa grinned, her eyes fixed on the Mayor’s HUD. "Look at the Mayor’s Favorability bar. It just turned emerald green. It’s actually vibrating."

Bart reached into his tunic and pulled out the Guild Charter, the spectral scroll unrolling across the Mayor’s ledger with a satisfying, high-fidelity snap.

"Listen, Westy," Bart said, his voice dropping into that smooth, performative baritone that had already charmed half of Oakhaven. "I’ve been thinking about the future of this settlement. After what my band did at the Silver Gate... well, Oakhaven is on the map now. Every player on the server knows where we are. That means tourism. That means trade. That means a whole lot of 'Vitreous Bits' flowing through your gates."

Weston’s digital pupils dilated. The 'Social Gravity' of the 19-CHA build was doing more than just bypassing a dialogue check; it was simulating a lifetime of rapport in a single interaction.

"Trade... yes," Weston muttered, his hand reaching for his quill as if by instinct. "The prosperity of Oakhaven is always my primary... er... Westy’s primary concern."

"Exactly," Bart said, tapping the first line of the Charter. "And to coordinate all that prosperity, we need a formal organization. A Guild of Pioneers. But a Guild needs signatures, and I wouldn't dream of starting one without the signature of the man who makes it all possible."

"Me?" Weston blinked, his skepticism finally being erased by the sheer 'Aura of Presence'. He looked at the scroll, and then at Bart. "You... you want *my* signature? On a Pioneer's charter?"

"Who else?" Bart laughed, the sound making the Star-Field Lute chime a soft, supportive chord. "You're a registered entity of this world, Westy. Your name carries weight. In fact, I’d say it carries more weight than a hundred common travelers."

Bart leaned in closer, his gold-starlight eyes locked onto the Mayor's pixelated ones. "What do you say, my friend? Shall we make history together?"

Weston didn't hesitate. His hand moved with a scripted fluidness that indicated a total override of his common logic. He signed the line in a bold, silver ink that hissed as it hit the spectral vellum.

*SIGNATURE RECORDED: MAYOR WESTON (ID: NPC_OAK_MAYOR_001).*
*PROGRESS: 6 / 100.*

"Splendid!" Bart beamed, rolling up the scroll with a flourish. "I knew I could count on you. We’ll be in touch about the Gala, of course. Keep that calendar clear!"

Before the Mayor could even find his voice to ask what a 'Gala' was, Bart was already halfway back to the door.

"See you later, Westy!" Bart called over his shoulder.

The doors slammed shut, leaving the Mayor staring blankly at his empty inkwell.

In the hallway, Tim and Mihwa were staring at Bart as if he’d just performed a literal miracle.

"That was devious," Tim said, his voice a mixture of horror and awe. "You just... you just gaslit the settlement’s mayor into skipping the entire player-signature requirement."

"It's not gaslighting, Tim," Bart said, his 19-CHA aura cooling down to a manageable simmer. "It’s high-fidelity networking. He’s a registered entity. He’s allowed to sign. I just gave him a reason to feel good about it."

"He called him 'Westy'," Mihwa muttered, still grinning. "I can't believe that worked."

"Ninety-four to go," Bart said, his eyes scanning the hallway. "And look! Here comes the Captain of the Guard. David, Nathan—you guys get the Sergeant at the gate. I’ll handle the Captain. By the time we hit the tavern tonight, I want this charter looking like a Who’s Who of Oakhaven."

As he approached the armored Captain, Bart’s stride didn't falter for a second. His fingers brushed the silver filaments of his lute, a soft, triumphant melody beginning to play itself out in the haptic air of the hallway.

"Captain Thorne!" Bart announced, his voice booming with a false intimacy that could have moved mountains. "How’s the shield-arm, my old comrade?"

Tim sighed, following his brother into the next 'attack'. "God help the developers," he whispered. "The Beautiful Bard is loose in the city."

*Guild Progress:*
*- Mayor Weston (Signed)*
*- 94 signatures remaining.*
*- Next Target: Captain Thorne.*

The 'Signature Raid' had officially begun. To the rest of the players on Server One, Oakhaven was a town of NPCs. To Bart, it was an untapped resource of ninety-four signatures, all waiting to be 'convinced' by the man who called the Mayor 'Westy'.
