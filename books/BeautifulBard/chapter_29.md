# Chapter 29: The Oakhaven Charter

The Oakhaven Arms was built for a hundred people, but as the evening sun dipped below the forest canopy, it felt like it was holding the entire population of Server One. The air was thick with the scent of roasted stag, spilled ale, and the electric ozone of a thousand active HUDs.

Bart didn't just walk to the center of the room; he *became* the center of the room. 

His 19 Charisma was no longer a passive stat; it was an active force of nature. As he stepped toward the cluster of heavy oak tables, the game engine scrambled to accommodate the 'Social Gravity' of his presence. In a series of sharp, haptic cracks that sounded like pistol shots, nine tables suddenly slid across the floor, snapping together with pixel-perfect precision to form an impromptu stage.

The tavern went silent. The NPCs stopped their scripted arguments, and the players froze, their tankards halfway to their lips.

"In the Round?" Bart whispered, a slow grin spreading across his face. "I can do that."

He unleashed the Star-Field Lute from his back, the silver filaments catching the lantern light and projecting a soft, indigo nebula onto the ceiling. He strummed a single chord, a sound so clear it felt like it was being played inside everyone’s ears simultaneously.

"Uh oh," Mihwa murmured from the back booth, her hand sliding over her face. "I've seen that look before. Karaoke night, three years ago. He did three Michael Jackson songs in a row and convinced the DJ to give him the keys to the booth."

Bart began to walk slowly around the edge of his new stage, nodding and smiling as he met the eyes of players and NPCs alike. His aura wasn't just visual; it was a 'Social Pressure' spike that made every person in the room feel like they were the only person he was looking at.

"My friends!" Bart announced, his voice carrying the warm, resonant weight of a 19-CHA leader. "My fellow adventurers! Citizens of this world!"

Tim, watching from the shadows, let out a low groan of admiration. "Wow. Just... wow. He’s actually doing it."

"I stand before you on the precipice," Bart continued, his voice dropping to a conspiratorial hum before swelling again. "On the precipice of something profound! Something that goes beyond levels, beyond gear, and beyond the gold we find in the dirt!"

He paused, letting the silence ring. The Star-Field Lute chimed a soft, supporting melody that seemed to pull the listeners closer.

"I want to send you a humble invitation," Bart said, his eyes scanning the crowd with a warmth that felt like a localized heatwave. "An invitation to be on the ground floor of something so real... something so BEAUTIFUL... that it would be selfish of me to keep it for myself."

He struck a chord—a bright, triumphant sound that triggered a global notification sound inside the tavern.

"Please check your messages," Bart said, a devious glint in his starlight eyes. "I send you... a humble... invitation. Please consider what you see before you as I play a few songs."

In that moment, a thousand pings echoed through the tavern. 

*NOTIFICATION: You have been invited to sign the Charter for the Guild: [Bart's Beautiful Band].*
*Requirement: Pioneer Status or Registered Entity Signature.*

"Signatures," Tim whispered, his eyes wide. "He’s mass-mailing the charter to every registered entity in the room. The players... and the NPCs."

Bart began to play. It wasn't just a song; it was a 'Social Engineering' masterpiece. He transitioned from a soulful, acoustic ballad to a high-tempo rhythmic shred that made the 7:1 physics of the tavern feel like a dance floor.

As he played, the signatures began to roll in.

*SIGNATURE RECORDED: PLAYER_JACK_RIPPER (ID: P_098_112).*
*SIGNATURE RECORDED: NPC_TAVERN_MAID_04 (ID: N_OAK_045).*
*SIGNATURE RECORDED: PLAYER_TITAN_GLOW (ID: P_044_221).*

"He’s using the performance to mask the exploit," Mihwa realized, watching the HUD scroll as players signed without even reading the fine print. They wanted more of the music. They wanted to be part of the 'Beautiful' thing he was describing.

Bart moved in rhythm with the lute, his 19 Charisma causing the indigo nebula on the ceiling to spin and flare. He was calling the Mayor 'Westy', he was calling the guards 'comrades', and he was calling the players 'legends'. 

By the time he hit the bridge of the third song, the progress bar on the Guild Charter was at 82 / 100.

"Just a few more," Bart sang, his voice blending with the lute’s resonance. "Join the pioneers. Join the band. Let's make Oakhaven our own."

The tavern was a sea of glowing signatures. NPCs, override by the 'Pioneer' reputation and the 'Social Gravity', were clicking 'Accept' on dialogue boxes they shouldn't have been able to see. Players, caught up in the celebrity of the World First party, were eager to have their names recorded on the same scroll as the winners of the Silver Orchard.

Bart hit the final, ringing chord, the Star-Field Lute projecting one last, brilliant flare of starlight that filled the room before fading into the amber glow of the lanterns.

The silence that followed was heavy, expectant, and utterly triumphant.

*GUILD FORMATION: 100 / 100 SIGNATURES.*
*FEE RECORDED: 1,000 VITREOUS BITS.*
*GUILD [Bart's Beautiful Band] HAS BEEN FORMALLY REGISTERED ON SERVER ONE.*

A golden banner unfurled in the center of the tavern, visible to everyone.

***CONGRATULATIONS!***
***Bart's Beautiful Band has achieved the WORLD FIRST: GUILD FORMATION.***
***Reward Awarded: Guild House Token (Oakhaven Region).***

Bart lowered the lute, his chest heaving with the haptic effort of the performance. He looked at the banner, and then at his team in the back booth.

"Mihwa," Bart said, his voice returning to its normal volume, though the resonance was still there. "I think it’s time to go look at our new house."

Tim sat back, his head spinning. "He did it. He actually did it. Day 2, and we have a guild house."

"I told you," Mihwa grinned, standing up and brushing the dust off her robes. "He did three MJ songs. Once he does the moonwalk, there’s no turning back."

Bart stepped off the stage, the tables snapping back into their original positions behind him with a final, satisfied click. The 'Beautiful Bard' had closed the deal. The city of NPCs and confused players was now the foundation of his empire.

"Let's go," Bart said, tossing the Guild House Token—a glowing, golden coin—into the air and catching it. "I hear the view from the hill is lovely."

*Guild Status: REGISTERED.*
*Headquarters: PENDING (Oakhaven District).*
*Current Objective: TOKEN REDEMPTION.*

The Oakhaven Arms erupted into cheers, the sound following the party out into the cool, night air of the settlement. Bart looked at the silver moon of *Vitreous Online*, and for the first time, he didn't see a game. He saw a home.
