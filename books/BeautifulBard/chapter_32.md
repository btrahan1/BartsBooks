# Chapter 32: Calling the Troops

The echoes of Eve’s departure were still vibrating in the Crystal-Sharp air of the Great Hall when Bart’s hands began to rub together with a frantic, rhythmic intensity. It was the sound of a plan—one that was already three steps ahead of the game’s actual code—slamming into place.

"Outstanding!" Bart breathed, his eyes wide and glowing with the starlight of his lute. "Absolutely outstanding!"

Mihwa turned from the stone stairs, looking at him like he had grown a second head. "Outstanding? Bart, did you hear a word she said? We 'broke' the development cycles. We’re being watched by the Mother of all AIs. This place was a *concept* until we dropped a token on it. Do you know what this means?"

"Of course I do," Bart said, his half-insane, half-ingenious smile spreading across his face. "It means the location of the Gala has changed. We aren't having it in the Oakhaven Square. We’re having it RIGHT HERE!"

Tim stopped his examination of the Universal Storage chests. "Here? In the Guild House? Bart, the square was chosen because of the NPC density. If we move it up the hill, we lose the casual crowd. We lose the 'Social Gravity' of the market."

"We don't need a casual crowd, Tim," Bart said, pacing the marble floor. "We have the Pioneer's Hall. We have Phase 3 rendering in a Phase 1 world. If we host the Solstice here, every player in the reach will have to climb the hill just to see what the 'ruckus' is about. It’s not just a party anymore; it’s a pilgrimage."

"And the logistics?" David asked, leaning against the obsidian forge. "There’s five of us. How do we decorate a three-story fortress and cater an event for five hundred people in forty-eight hours?"

Bart stopped pacing and unslung the Star-Field Lute. He looked at the Guild Charter, still resting on the hearth, glowing with the names of one hundred 'Registered Entities'.

"Good thing we have ninety-five other guild members to help us get ready, right?"

The silence that followed was broken only by the crackle of the magical hearth. 

"The signatures," Mihwa whispered. "Bart... they aren't just names on a scroll. They’re guild members. They have a 'Member' tag now."

"Exactly," Bart grinned. "And what does a guild leader do when there's work to be done?"

He didn't wait for an answer. He struck a single, ringing chord on the lute—a sound that bypassed the room's acoustics and traveled directly into the server's notification queue. The indigo filaments of the instrument flared, projecting a map of Oakhaven onto the ceiling.

"Build it," Bart whispered, his voice resonating with the 'Social Pressure' of a 19-CHA leader, "and they will come! It's time to call home the troops!"

He began to play. It wasn't a ballad or a battle hymn; it was a 'Summons'. The music was a rhythmic, pulsing cadence that mimicked the heartbeat of the settlement below. It carried the 'Varness' of their victory at the Silver Gate and the 'Celebrity Aura' of the World First achievement.

In the streets of Oakhaven, ninety-five AIs stopped what they were doing.

Mayor Weston, sitting at his desk, felt a sudden, inexplicable urge to check his calendar. Captain Thorne, patrolling the gate, felt a pull toward the northern hill that overrode his 'Standard Patrol' routine. The tavern maids, the blacksmith’s apprentices, and even the local woodcutters—all of them felt the 'Social Gravity' of the Beautiful Band tugging at their code.

It was a sonic recruitment. 

On the hill, the party watched as the first figures appeared at the bottom of the slope.

"Look," Nathan said, pointing.

A formation of Oakhaven Guards, led by Captain Thorne, was marching up the hill. Behind them came a line of carts belonging to the local merchants, loaded with silk banners, flower-vines, and barrels of the settlement's finest elderberry wine. And right in the center, looking both bewildered and strangely proud, was 'Westy' himself.

"They're coming," Mihwa whispered, her Druid-senses feeling the massive shift in the zone’s NPC density. "All of them!"

Bart didn't stop playing. He walked out onto the sapphire balcony, his music swelling to meet the arriving 'troops'. As the NPCs reached the marble courtyard of the Pioneer's Hall, their 'Member' tags began to glow in unison.

"My friends!" Bart’s voice boomed over the courtyard, reinforced by the hall’s acoustics. "Welcome to your new home! As you can see, we have a bit of a housing problem. The Summer Solstice is coming, and our hall looks a little... bare."

Mayor Weston stepped forward, his digital eyelids fluttering as he adjusted to the high-fidelity rendering of the fortress. "Bart... my friend. We heard the call. The prosperity of Oakhaven... and the guild... it requires our attention."

"It requires more than attention, Westy," Bart said, leaning over the railing. "It requires a miracle. I want this hill covered in silver-orchard lanterns. I want the Great Hall hung with the tapestries of the woods. I want the Masterwork Forge running twenty-four hours a day to craft the commemorative badges for our guests."

He looked at his team, who were now standing in the courtyard, staring at the sudden army of laborers.

"Nathan, you and Captain Thorne organize the security perimeter. I don't want any 'uninvited guests' or rival guilds sneaking in for a peak until we're ready. David, you work with the scouts—we need the perimeter of the reach watched. Tim, coordinate the merchants. I want a trade-hub rendered in the courtyard by tomorrow morning."

Bart turned his gaze to Mihwa. "And you, Mihwa. You said it was perfect. Now make it *Beautiful*. Use the guild members to bridge the gap between Oakhaven’s watercolor and this hall’s crystal. I want the entire hill to look like it’s ascending."

Mihwa grinned, her imagination finally finding a canvas large enough. "I can do that. I can definitely do that."

The NPCs didn't hesitate. Override by the 'Social Gravity' and the 'Pioneer' status, their AI behavior shifted into 'Organization Mode'. Carts were unloaded, banners were unfurled, and the quiet hill of Oakhaven was suddenly a hive of high-fidelity activity. 

Bart lowered his lute, the 'Summons' complete. He looked at the chaos below—the guards training in the courtyard, the merchants arguing over banner placement, and the Mayor directing the woodcutters to clear a vista for the sunset.

"Ninety-five members," Tim said, coming up to the balcony. "And they're all NPCs. Bart, you just automated the entire event-planning industry."

"It's not automation, Tim," Bart said, his 19-CHA aura cooling into a satisfied simmer. "It's community. If we're going to stabilize the neural sync like Eve said, we need the world to feel like it belongs here. And nothing builds a sense of belonging like a little manual labor."

He looked at the Golden Guild Token at the entrance. "Time to get to work."

*Preparation Phase: ACTIVE.*
*Guild Members Assigned: 95 NPCs.*
*Current Status: Hall Decoration (20%), Perimeter Security (45%), Logistics (15%).*
*Deadline: 42 Hours to Summer Solstice.*

The Oakhaven hill was no longer a meditation spot. It was a construction site for a legend. And as the sun set over Server One, the lights of the Pioneer's Hall didn't just flicker—they blazed, a beacon of high-fidelity ambition in a watercolor world.
