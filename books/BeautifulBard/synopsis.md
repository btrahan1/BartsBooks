Stuck in a cycle of dead-end jobs and mountain-high debt, Bart and his tabletop gaming crew spend their Tuesdays pretending to be heroes in a basement. But everything changes when a commercial airs for "Vitreous Online"—a full-immersion virtual reality world with permanent residency options where your imagination becomes your reality. Tired of being a performer with no stage, Bart decides to take the ultimate leap. He's going "Full Bard" in a world where music is actual magic. Join Bart and his friends as they leave the real world behind for the Grand Finale of their lives!
