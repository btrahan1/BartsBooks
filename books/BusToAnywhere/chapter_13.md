# Chapter 13: The Geometry of Conflict

The bar was called *Boots and Balls*.

The neon sign in the window featured a pair of cowboy boots (complete with spinning spurs) kicking a rack of billiard balls.

"Nice," Ben laughed when he saw it. "Literal. I like it."

Inside, the air was thick with country music, sawdust, and the *clack-clack* of pool balls.

They got a table near the back. Ben wore the blue t-shirt. Sarah wore a vintage NASA tank top.

They were both sharks.

Sarah played with aggressive precision—banking shots hard, snapping the cue ball back with heavy English. Ben played with fluid geometry—soft touches, using the rails to park the cue ball exactly where he needed it for the next shot.

They were tied, three games to three. They had a pitcher of Shiner Bock between them. It felt like a real date. The kind Ben hadn't had since... well, he couldn't remember.

Then, the variable entered the equation.

A large man in a grease-stained rodeo shirt stumbled past their table. He was holding a full mug of domestic lager, and he was steering like a boat with a broken rudder.

He lurched. The beer splashed. A wave of amber liquid sloshed directly onto Ben’s chest, soaking the blue shirt.

It wasn't entirely accidental. The guy had swerved *toward* Ben.

"Watch it, stick-boy!" the man slurred. He glared at Ben, steadying himself on the pool table. "You made me spill my drink."

The bar went silent in a ten-foot radius. This was Hank. Everyone knew Hank. Hank liked to fight after his fifth beer.

Sarah tensed. She gripped her pool cue like a bo staff. She had seen what Ben did to the guys in the parking lot. She knew Hank was about to have a very bad night.

Ben stood there, dripping. He looked at the stain. He looked at Hank.

He saw the violence in Hank’s eyes. He saw the sloppy stance. He calculated the vectors.
*Strike to the knee. Takedown. Pivot. Elbow to the jaw.*
*Result: Hank unconscious. Police involved. Night ruined. Sarah traumatized.*
*Efficiency: 0%.*

Ben looked the guy in the eyes. He took a deep breath and smiled.

"That's okay, partner," Ben said, his voice warm and genuine. "Looks like I owe you a beer!"

Sarah’s mouth dropped. 

Hank’s mouth dropped as well. His fists, which had been clenching, went slack. He blinked, trying to process the data. This wasn't the script.

"Uhm..." Hank stammered. "Sure... uh, that be awful nice of you, friend."

Ben walked to the bar. He ordered a fresh lager. He handed it to Hank.

"No harm done," Ben said. "Enjoy your night."

Hank took the beer. He looked at it, then at Ben. The aggression drained out of him, replaced by confused gratitude. "Thanks. Uh. Sorry about your shirt."

Hank wandered off, muttering to himself about "nice folks."

Ben walked back to the table. He grabbed a napkin and dabbed at his chest.

Sarah was staring at him.

"What was that?" she whispered. "I thought you were going to... you know. *Phoenix Eye* him."

Ben chalked his cue.

"Violence is inefficient, Sarah. A beer costs four dollars. Bail costs five hundred. Hitting him would have ruined the geometry of the evening."

He leaned over the table, lining up the 8-ball.

"Besides," he said, taking the shot. "I'm having a good time. I didn't want to stop."

*Clack. Thud.* The 8-ball vanished into the corner pocket.

"Game over," Ben said lightly. "Your break."
