# Chapter 18: The Hardware Upgrade

The next night, Sarah arrived at Ben’s apartment (room 204 at the *Starlight*, which he was now renting weekly for the discounted rate).

She didn't knock. She kicked the door with her foot.

"Open up," she called out. "My hands are full of justice."

Ben opened the door. Sarah walked in and dumped a large cardboard box onto his small dining table. The contents spilled out with a clatter of high-end plastic and glass.

Ben surveyed the inventory.
*Four high-resolution IP cameras.*
*Two passive infrared heat sensors.*
*Two solar-powered floodlights.*
*One automated License Plate Reader (LPR).*

"Salvaged from a defunct impound lot in Odessa," Sarah explained, dusting off her hands. "I reflashed the firmware this afternoon. They now run on my custom kernel."

She looked at him, her eyes fierce.

"It's time to get serious," she said, smiling.

Ben looked her up and down. She was wearing her 'serious' clothes: black jeans, combat boots, and a t-shirt that said *rm -rf / (sudo make me a sandwich)*.

"Oh!" Ben said, his face a mask of mock realization. "You meant about the case. My bad!"

He grinned evilly.

"I was wondering if you were going to set up one of 'those' kind of websites for a minute or two. You know, with the cameras and the lighting..."

Sarah froze. She blinked. Then she picked up a cat-6 ethernet cable.

"Ben," she warned, "I will strangle you with this patch cord."

"Inefficient," Ben noted, dodging the cable as she swung it at him. "Too much slack range. You want a zip tie for asphyxiation."

She successfully whipped his arm with the cable. "Shut up and help me untangle these wires. If that truck comes back to town, I want to know its GPS coordinates, its tire pressure, and what radio station they're listening to."

Ben grabbed the other end of the tangle.

"So we're building a dragnet," Ben said.

"We're building a Panopticon," Sarah corrected. "We're going to wire *Abernathy's*. It's right off the main highway. If they come back to hit another store, they have to pass it."

"And if they stop?" Ben asked.

Sarah picked up a screwdriver. She spun it in her fingers like a gunslinger.

"Then you do that Kung Fu thing. And I'll record it in 4K."
