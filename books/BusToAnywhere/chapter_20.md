# Chapter 20: The Intervention

The night was silent, until it wasn't.

The truck, the same primer-grey Ford F-150, drove through town first. It moved with all the stealth of a freight train, the muffler rattling against the chassis.

They cruised down Main Street, slowing down as they passed *Abernathy’s*. Ben held his breath in the shadows of the alleyway, every muscle coiled. But they kept going.

Then, about as sneaky as a rhino in a china shop, they turned around at the edge of town and came back through.

They pulled up to the curb in front of *The General Store*.

"Idiots," Ben whispered into his comms. "They probably wanted some snacks."

They didn't bother with lockpicks. They didn't bother with stealth. They were as subtle as a brick through glass, which is exactly what they did.

*CRASH.*

The sound of the plate glass window shattering echoed like a gunshot. The driver stayed in the truck, engine idling. The other two—the Shover and the quiet one—hopped out and crunched over the glass into the store. A simple smash and grab for beef jerky and cigarettes.

Ben watched patiently from the shadows of the awning across the street. He observed their movements. Sloppy. Uncoordinated. Confident in their impunity because they believed this town was soft.

He tapped his earpiece.

"Sarah," he said, his voice flat and cold. "Call the Sheriff. And an ambulance."

"An ambulance?" Sarah’s voice crackled in his ear. "Why?"

Ben stepped out of the shadows. The streetlight caught the matte black of his tactical gear. He adjusted his gloves.

"No more Mr. Nice Guy."
