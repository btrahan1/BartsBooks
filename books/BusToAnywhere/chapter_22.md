# Chapter 22: The Aftermath

The retreat was efficient.

Three zip ties. Two for the wrists of the conscious men (Driver and Shover), one for the ankles of the Knife Guy (who was too busy cradling his broken wrist to run anyway).

Ben left them there on the sidewalk, a pile of groaning, defeated malice.

He slunk into the shadows of the alley as the blue and red lights of Sheriff Dean’s cruiser bathed Main Street in a strobe-light disco.

"Ambulance is two minutes out," Sarah’s voice whispered in his ear. "Sheriff is on scene. You're clear, Batman."

Ben made his way back to the *Starlight*, moving through backyards and empty lots to avoid the spectacle. He climbed the fire escape and slipped through the window of room 204.

He landed on the carpet with a heavy thud.

Sarah was there instantly. She had turned the monitors off. The only light was the lamp by the couch.

She gasped.

"You're hurt!"

She was staring at his side. The black compression shirt was torn, and the fabric was soaked with dark, wet blood.

"It's nothing but a scratch," Ben wheezed, winced as he straightened up. "A big scratch. But did you see the other guys?"

He grinned goofily, the adrenaline crash hitting him like a physical weight.

Sarah didn't smile. She grabbed a first aid kit from the bathroom (she had stocked it herself, naturally).

"Sit down," she ordered. "And take that shirt off. If you bleed on my security deposit, I'm billing you."

Ben slid down onto the couch, exhausted. He peeled the shirt off. The cut was long—maybe four inches—but shallow. It looked worse than it was.

Sarah cleaned it with iodine. Her hands were steady, precise. Soldering iron steady.

"You broke his wrist," she said quietly, applying a butterfly bandage.

"He killed Hank," Ben murmured, his eyes closing. "I heard the snap. The geometry... it was just right."

Sarah finished the bandage. She sat back, looking at him. The "manager" who fixed ice machines. The "Kung Fu guy" who took down three killers.

"You're crazy," she whispered.

Ben opened one eye. "I'm efficient."
