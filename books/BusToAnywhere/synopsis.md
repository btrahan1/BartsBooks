Ben is a master of movement. Working at a massive fulfillment center, he turns the frantic scramble of sorting boxes into a Tai Chi form, becoming the most efficient worker on the floor. But in a world run by algorithms, perfection isn't enough. Fired for "Time Off Task" because he stopped to meditate after clearing his lane, Ben takes a bus to the furthest point fifty dollars will buy. Arriving in the dusty town of Pecos, Ben starts a new journey where his unique skills might be exactly what the vast, brown world needs. A tale of self-discovery, martial arts in mundane life, and finding your place when the system discards you.
