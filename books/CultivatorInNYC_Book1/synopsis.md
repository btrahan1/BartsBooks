Han Wei, an Outer Disciple of the Azure Cloud Sect, expected many things during his teleportation array practice—but waking up in Central Park was not one of them. Stranded in a "Desolate Realm" with toxic air (smog) and almost no ambient Qi, Wei must use his sect-trained mind and physical foundation to survive. When he discovers that the local strategy game (chess) is laughably simple compared to what he was taught, he begins his quest to conquer the city, one game at a time. Join the hilarious journey of a master cultivator navigating the strange world of New York City!
