Han Wei has conquered New York City, turning his "Park Sect" into a massive success. But success brings its own challenges. Feeling stagnant and trapped by the thin Qi of the modern world, Wei faces an existential crisis. That is, until a mysterious messenger from the Iron Blood Pavilion arrives with an invitation to the Sovereign's Tournament. Discovering that the world is much wider and full of hidden practitioners, Wei must prepare for a battle that will prove whether he is truly a dragon or just a loud lizard. The journey continues beyond the city limits!
