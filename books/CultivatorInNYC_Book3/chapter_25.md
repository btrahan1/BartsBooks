# Cultivator in NYC: Book 3 - Chapter 25: The Vermin Engine

The transition from the Engine Room to the third floor wasn't a slide or a fall; it was a rhythmic, high-frequency acceleration. 

As the Raid Group entered the service tunnels, the metallic blue light didn't just illuminate the walls; it pulsed with a frantic, electronic heartbeat. The service tunnels weren't rusted iron anymore; they were a labyrinth of spirit-forged chrome and pulsing fiber-optic cables that hummed with a cold, predatory intelligence.

Han Wei led the way, his new awareness of the Gilded Labyrinth's depth making the hairs on the back of his neck stand up. "Stay tight. The frequency has shifted again. We are no longer in the stomach. We are in the nerves."

Miller cracked his knuckles, his iron pipe resting on his shoulder. He looked at the sleek, chrome walls with a mixture of suspicion and eagerness. "nerves, huh? Well, they smell like ozone and expensive machinery."

Suddenly, the mechanical pulsing of the walls was joined by a series of sharp, rhythmic clicks. It wasn't the heavy metallic thud of the Sentinels or the wet rasp of the Goblins. This was the sound of precision engineering meeting an animal's hunger.

"Of course," Miller said, a grim smile spreading across his face. "What else could it be but SteamPunk rats?!"

From the shadows of the chrome rafters, the first wave descended. They were the size of small dogs, their bodies a terrifying fusion of sleek, spirit-refined steel and exposed copper plumbing. Miniature chimneys on their backs hissed with jets of blue steam, and their tails were long, serrated saws that whirred with high-frequency lethality.

"Alpha, Phalanx! Keep the Phalanx tight!" Miller roared. "Let's get to work!"

The rats didn't lunge in a mindless swarm. They moved in tactical packs, using the chrome conduits as tracks. Three rats would 'exhaust' a freezing jet of blue-vapor from their flanks to disrupt the delvers' vision, while two others would use their whirring saw-tails to strike at the gaps in the shield wall.

"They're faster than the goblins!" Spider shouted, his Team Beta Rogues struggling to keep up with the rats' erratic patterns. "And they're armored! My daggers are bouncing off the copper plating!"

"Suppression volley!" the lead Archer cried. 

A dozen arrows struck the chrome conduits, but the rats simply used the impact to 'pivot' their spirit-bodies, leaping over the projectiles with mechanical precision.

"They aren't just mobs," Wei noted, his aura expanding into a sharp, protective barrier. "They are 'Vermin Engines'. They are powered by the very flow of this floor."

"Sarah, the Anchor! We need floor-traction!" Tupi called out.

Sarah raised her new Staff of the Anchor, the emerald glass head glowing with a calm, cool light. "Purifying Field... FROST!"

Instead of moss, a layer of thick, rugged frost erupted from the chrome floor. It didn't just provide traction; the cold 'choked' the steam-pistons of the rats that got too close.

"Miller, now!" Wei commanded.

Miller surged forward, his pipe glowing a brilliant white-hot. He caught a leaping rat mid-air, the impact causing the creature's copper plumbing to rupture in a spectacular burst of blue pressure.

"Beta, go for the chimneys!" Spider commanded, noticing the weak point.

With the rats slowed by Sarah's frost and Miller's phalanx holding the center, Team Beta Rogues used the momentum to strike at the small, hissing chimneys on the rats' backs. For every chimney they shattered, the rat's spirit-core overloaded, turning the mechanical beast into a pile of harmless, steaming scrap.

"One room at a time!" Wei barked, his staff striking the ground to disperse a cloud of freezing vapor. "Alpha, pivot left! They're coming through the vents!"

The 'Vermin Engine' floor was a marathon of high-speed skirmishes. For every pack they cleared, two more seemed to emerge from the pipes. The Raid Group was forced to move in a constant, defensive circle, their Phalanx shifting and rotating as the rats used the 360-degree architecture to their advantage.

"Jax, status!" Wei panted, parrying a saw-tail that had nearly caught Tupi's sling.

"Pressure reaching ninety percent!" Jax reported, his camera eye following a rat that was currently trying to chew through a Magi-Tech power cable. "Master, the 'Magi-Tech' network is taking a hit! The rats aren't just attacking us; they're attacking the infrastructure of the floor!"

Wei looked at the chrome walls. The blue light was flickering, turning into a frantic, strobe-like red. 

"They're trying to out-speed our Intent," Wei realized. "The Labyrinth is using the 'Vermin' to consume the very logic we're trying to clear."

He looked at Sarah, who was holding the Staff of the Anchor with white-knuckled intensity. "Sarah, we need a 'Deep Freeze'. Stop the flow of the entire room!"

"Master, that'll drain me!" Sarah warned.

"The Staff will take the hit," Wei promised. "Anchor the floor and the walls! Stop the gears of the Engine!"

Sarah closed her eyes, her presence expanding until she seemed to merge with the emerald glass of her staff. She slammed the anchor-head into a central conduit.

"TOTAL... STASIS!" 

A massive wave of absolute zero energy erupted from the staff. For one heartbeat, the entire hall was silent. The flickering red light frozen in mid-pulse. The hissing steam turned to solid ice. The SteamPunk rats, caught in mid-leap, became a series of chrome statues, their chimneys silent.

Miller let out a long, shaky breath, his own armor covered in a thin layer of frost. "That... was... awesome."

"Spirit levels critically low," Jax noted, his voice sounding slightly mechanical as his own sensors recovered from the freeze. "Sarah is at ten percent. Miller at forty percent. But the room is clear. The 'Vermin Engine' is stalled."

Wei walked to the center of the hall, touching one of the frozen rats. It shattered into a thousand pieces of copper and ice. 

"Twenty targets neutralized," Wei said, his voice a steady anchor in the cold. "But we have only stalled the Engine. The 'Governor' of this floor is a Level 3.8 'Queen-Engine'. She will not be satisfied with a freeze."

He looked toward the end of the hall, where a massive, gear-driven door was currently encased in three feet of solid ice. 

"Rest," Wei commanded. "Five minutes. This floor is about the 'Speed' of the city. We cleared the first hallway, but the 'Queen' is already re-routing the pressure. We need to be faster."

As the Raid Group huddles around Sarah, using their own Intent to help her recover, Han Wei looks back at the chrome corridors. 

The 'SteamPunk Rats' was just the beginning. The Labyrinth was learning his pace. It was accelerating. 

"Jax," Wei said, "tell the world to watch their 'Speed'. The Labyrinth is no longer just a physical space. It's a clock. And the hands are moving faster than we expected."

"Understood, Master," Jax chirped. "The 'Rat-Race' has officially gone digital."

The third floor was live. And the Vermin Engine was just getting started.
