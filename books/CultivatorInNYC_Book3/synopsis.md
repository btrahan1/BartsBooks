Returning to New York City after a journey to the Amazon, Han Wei finds the city transformed. The ambient Qi has become electric and dense, turning the atmosphere violet and granting everyday citizens strange, uncontrollable power. As New Yorkers try to "cultivate" while commuting and technology fails due to magical interference, Wei must navigate a city that is rapidly rewriting the laws of physics. With his unique blend of sect knowledge and street smarts, the "Grandmaster of Queens" is back to prove that in a world where everyone is suddenly a genius, a little bit of mediocrity goes a long way!
