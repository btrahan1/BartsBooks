# Chapter 19: Full

The message from the Observation Pavilion was simple.
"Break him."
It wasn't malicious. The Elders needed to know the failure point of their new asset. They needed to know exactly how much punishment the "Living Wall" could take before it crumbled.

Feng arrived at the training corner with a serious expression. He wasn't carrying his practice spear. He was carrying his *real* spear—a shaft of azure spirit-metal with a tip that hummed with concentrated wind Qi.

"Gong," Feng said, his voice tight. "The Elders have ordered a stress test. I am not to hold back. I am to use Spirit Arts."

Gong looked at the glowing spear. He felt a prickle of danger on his skin.
"Okay," Gong said. He handed Maggie to Brother Cai. "Hold her tight. It will be windy."

Cai retreated to a safe distance, clutching the rhino. The crowd of spectators grew silent.

"Ready?" Feng asked.
"Ready."

There was no dance this time. Feng exploded.
*Piercing Wind Art: First Form - Gale Thrust.*

The spear didn't just move; it vanished. Boom.
Gong raised the Ugly Door.
*CRANG.*
The impact wasn't a push; it was a drilling force. The spear tip dug into the scrap metal, spinning like a drill bit. Sparks flew.
Gong skidded back ten feet, his boots carving deep trenches. His arms shook.

"Second Form - Tornado Cleave!"
Feng spun, the spear creating a vacuum blade of wind.
Gong slammed his shields together. *The Fortress.*
*WHAM.*
The force lifted him off his feet. He landed on his knees, grunting.

Inside Gong, something was happening.
Usually, when he took a hit, the vibration would disperse into the ground. But Feng was hitting too fast, too hard. The energy couldn't leave.
So it went *in*.
It packed down into his dantian.
*Sediment.*
*More Sediment.*
It felt heavy. Uncomfortably heavy. Like he had eaten a thousand rocks.

Feng didn't stop. He was a blur of azure light. He struck from the left, the right, above, below.
Gong blocked. Deflected. Slanted. Adjusted.
But he was losing ground. The Ugly Door was tearing apart. A chunk of oak flew off. A strip of copper peeled away.

"Final Form," Feng shouted, leaping into the air. "Meteor Pierce!"
He came down like a god of war, the spear point glowing like a star.

Gong looked up. He didn't have time to dodge. He only had time to brace.
He put every ounce of his heavy strength into his arms. He roared.
*THOOOOOM.*

The impact created a shockwave that knocked the front row of spectators flat. Dust billowed out in a mushroom cloud.

When the dust cleared, Gong was on his back.
The Ugly Door was in pieces. His arms were bruised purple.
Feng stood over him, panting, his spear tip hovering inches from Gong's throat.

The crowd gasped. The Wall had fallen.

Gong blinked against the sunlight. He looked at the spear.
Slowly, painfully, he lifted his left arm—the one with the buckler still attached. He gently pushed the spear tip away.

"Very good, Feng," Gong wheezed. "A moment to catch my breath, please."

Feng retracted the spear. He looked shaken. "You... you took a Meteor Pierce. Directly. And you still have arms."

Gong rolled over. He pushed himself to his knees. Then to one foot. Then standing.
He swayed slightly.
He dusted off his tunic.

"Good hit," Gong said.
Then he frowned. He put a hand on his stomach.
His face went pale.

"Gong?" Cai called out, running over with Maggie. "You okay, buddy?"

"Something weird is happening in my stomach," Gong whispered.
He looked at Feng with wide, confused eyes.
"It feels... full. Like... too full."

The air around Gong began to shimmer. Not with blue wind or red fire, but with a heavy, brown distortion.
The ground beneath his feet cracked. *Snap.*
Small pebbles around him started to float, hovering in the air as gravity itself seemed to get heavier around him.

"I don't know what it is!" Gong panicked. "Did I eat a bad turnip?"

Feng stared at the floating stones. His jaw dropped.
"No," Feng whispered. "Gong... I think you're breaking through."

"Breaking what?" Gong asked, clutching his belly. "I don't want to break!"

"Foundation Establishment," Feng said, awe in his voice. "But... you don't have a Foundation. You just have... dirt."

Gong groaned as the pressure inside him doubled.
Whatever was happening, it was heavy. And it was starting *now*.
