# Chapter 21: The Selection

The meeting in the Elder's Hall was quiet, save for the clicking of Go stones.
Elder Iron-Heart placed a black stone on the board. "The boy needs field experience. A training dummy who cannot move is useful for the sect, but it is a waste of a Foundation level asset."

Elder Fang grunted. He placed a white stone. "You still think he is an asset. I think he is a freak accident. A walking rock."

"Rocks are useful," Iron-Heart countered. "Walls are useful. Especially with the reports coming from the Southern Marshes. The Spirit Beasts are restless."

He leaned back.
"We will send a team. A standard 5-man cell. To the Fungal Caverns."

Fang raised an eyebrow. "The Caverns? That's a Stage 2 dungeon. Bit rough for a rookie, isn't it?"

"If he is what I think he is, he will be fine. If not... well, we lose a laborer."
Iron-Heart tapped the table.
"But the party composition is critical. We need to test Gong and Xiao. Obviously, they are practically paired at the hip. A Tank and a Healer."

Fang nodded. "A classic core. But who else? We cannot send elites like Feng. They would carry the team, and we wouldn't learn anything about Gong's limits. We need... competence."

"Competent, but not great," Iron-Heart agreed. "We need Average. Who do we have?"

He pulled a scroll of disciple rosters.

"First, a Scout. Someone fast. Someone who can find traps but perhaps... lacks finishing power."
Fang squinted at the list. "Pip. Agile Wind Division. He passed the same day Gong failed. Fast feet, good eyes, but terrified of direct combat. He fights like a rabbit."
"Perfect," Iron-Heart said. "Write him down."

"Second, Damage. Single target."
"Sister Lin," Fang suggested. "Rapier specialist. Fast, precise. She has been training with Gong recently. Her technique has improved, but she is fragile. If the enemy touches her, she folds."
"Good. She will rely on the Tank. Write her down."

"Third. Crowd Control. Or Area Damage."
Iron-Heart scanned the names.
"Zhi. Fire Division. He has a lot of mana and knows the 'Explosive Ember' chant."
Fang chuckled. "Zhi? The boy is a glass cannon. He casts one big spell and then passes out from exhaustion. He has zero stamina."
"Exactly," Iron-Heart smiled. "He needs protection while he casts, and he needs someone to carry him when he collapses. A perfect stress test for a Tank."

Iron-Heart rolled up the scroll.
"Gong (Tank). Xiao (Healer). Pip (Scout). Lin (Duelist). Zhi (Artillery)."

"It's a balanced party," Fang admitted. "But flawed. Each of them has a glaring weakness."

"And that," Iron-Heart said, picking up his tea, "is the point. Can Gong cover the cracks in the wall? Or will the whole thing tumble down?"

***

Gong was brushing Maggie when the summons came.
It was a red scroll, delivered by a messenger bird that Maggie tried to eat.

"Field Mission," Brother Cai read over Gong's shoulder. "Fungal Caverns. Difficulty: Stage 2."
Cai whistled. "They aren't starting you easy, buddy. That place is full of poison spores and grabby vines."

"Training?" Gong asked.

"No. Real," Cai said seriously. "Real monsters. They don't stop when you tap out. You get paid, though. Ten Spirit Stones."

Gong's eyes widened. That was a lot of turnips.

"You have a team," Cai continued. "Meeting at the East Gate in one hour."

Gong patted Maggie. "Stay with Cai. Be good. Don't eat the mace."
 Maggie snorted and headbutted his shin.

Gong packed his things. He didn't have much. A sack of dried meat. A water skin.
And his shields... he didn't have shields. The Ugly Door was gone.
He had the buckler, but it was small.

"Take these," Cai said.
He kicked open a crate. Inside were two heavy, rectangular shields. They were standard issue Iron Mountain Infantry towers. Dull grey steel. Nothing fancy, but solid.
"I... uh... 'found' them in the armory," Cai lied. "Consider it a loan. From your Manager."

Gong picked them up. They were lighter than his old scrap shield, but balanced.
"Thank you, Manager."

"Go," Cai said, looking unusually worried. "And Gong? Don't let them die. That's the Tank's job. You keep aggro, you keep them alive."

"I know," Gong said. "I am the Wall."

He walked to the East Gate.
Standing there were four others.
Xiao, looking nervous in her green robes.
A skinny boy bouncing on his heels (Pip).
A girl with a sharp sword and a sharper gaze (Lin).
And a boy in red robes who looked like he had barely slept (Zhi).

They looked at Gong. The massive, square-shaped boy with a shield on each arm.
"So," Pip grinned nervously. "We're the... A-Team?"

"No," Gong said seriously. "We are the Wall Team."
He looked at them. They looked soft. Breakable.
*Protect,* his core hummed. *Sediment.*

"Let's go," Gong said. "I need the stones."
