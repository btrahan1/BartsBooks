# Chapter 22: The Taunt

The Fungal Caverns smelled like wet socks and old cheese.
"Ugh," Lin wrinkled her nose. "My boots are going to smell like this for weeks."

Gong sniffed. "Smells like good soil. Lots of nutrients."

"Focus," Pip hissed from the front. He was crouching behind a stalagmite, vibrating with nervous energy. "Movement ahead. Myconid Warriors. Three of them."

"I got them!" Zhi shouted.
Before anyone could formulate a plan, the Mage stepped forward and unleashed a torrent of flame.
*FIREBALL.*

The explosion was impressive. It incinerated the three mushroom men instantly. It also incinerated the loot, singed Pip's eyebrows, and used up about half of Zhi's mana pool.
"Zhi!" Lin shouted. "You don't nukes trash mobs! Save it for the boss!"

"But they died, didn't they?" Zhi panted, leaning on his staff. "I'm... gonna sit for a second."

Gong watched them. They were strong individuals, but as a unit, they were a mess.
Pip was too far ahead. Lin was annoyed. Zhi was exhausted. And Xiao was trying to heal Pip's eyebrows.
Gong just stood there, shields ready, guarding... emptiness.
"We should walk closer together," Gong suggested. "Like the rhinos."

"Rhinos are slow," Lin scoffed. "We need speed."

They pushed forward.
The first floor was a maze of glowing tunnels.
They fought Spore-Rats (Lin stabbed them), Slime-Puddles (Pip kited them), and more Myconids (Gong blocked their spore clouds with his shields).

Then they reached the end of the floor.
A large cavern, dominated by a pool of bubbling green acid.
Sleeping on a mound of moss was the Floor Guardian.
A Moss-Back Bear.
It was twice the size of a normal bear, with fungal plates growing on its shoulders. Its claws dripped with venom.

"Okay," Pip whispered. "Plan?"
"I shoot it," Zhi volunteered.
"I flank it," Lin said.
"I... wall it?" Gong offered.

"Go," Lin ordered.

Zhi cast a Firebolt. It hit the bear in the nose.
The bear roared. It didn't look happy.
It charged.
Gong stepped forward to intercept. *The Fortress.*
But the bear didn't hit him. It swerved.
Animals were smart. It saw the big metal box and ignored it. It saw the fragile girl in green robes standing in the back (Xiao).
*Target: Healer.*

"No!" Gong shouted. He tried to chase, but he was too slow.
The bear bypassed Lin. It bypassed Zhi. It was barreling straight for Xiao.
Xiao froze. She raised her hands, but she was a healer, not a fighter.

Gong felt a familiar panic. The wolves. The memory of wolves eating his friend.
*Protect.*
He couldn't reach her. He couldn't block it.
He needed the bear to look at him.
He needed to be the most annoying thing in the room.

Gong stopped running. He set his feet.
He slammed his two tower shields together with every ounce of his Foundation strength.
*CLANG.*

The sound was deafening. It echoed off the cavern walls, magnified by the acoustics.
It wasn't just noise. It was a pulse of heavy, demanding Qi. A vibration that rattled teeth.

"YOU FACE ME!" Gong roared.

It wasn't a complex insult. It was an order.
The bear skidded to a halt. It shook its head, ears ringing.
It looked back at Gong.
It saw the big metal man. It heard the challenge. Instinct overrode strategy. The noise was hateful. The metal man was hateful.
The bear turned.
It forgot the healer. It wanted to silence the noise.

It charged Gong.
"Yes," Gong whispered. "Good bear."

He raised his shields. *The Fortress.*
*CRASH.*
The bear hit him like a derailment. Gong slid back ten feet, his boots sparking on the stone.
But he held.

"Now!" Gong grunted.

Lin appeared from the shadows, her rapier flashing. She struck the bear's exposed flank.
Zhi, having recovered some mana, cast a smaller, focused fire bolt into the bear's mossy back.
Pip threw a dagger at its eye.

The bear roared, swiping at them, but Gong slammed his shields again. *CLANG.*
"ME!" Gong shouted. "FACE ME!"

The bear turned back to him, distracted from the damage dealers.
It was a rhythm.
Clang. Bear hits Gong.
Stab. Burn. Slice.
Clang. Bear hits Gong.

Finally, the beast collapsed, dissolving into particles of light and loot.

The party stood there, panting.
Xiao ran to Gong. "Are you okay? That hit was massive."
"I am fine," Gong said, shaking out his arms. "The shields are good."

Lin looked at him differently. "That yell... you pulled aggro. Even when it wanted to kill Xiao."
"It didn't like the noise," Gong explained.

"That's a skill," Pip said, impressed. "That's a legit Tank taunt. Where did you learn that?"

Gong thought about it.
"Bertha," he said. "She hates loud noises."
