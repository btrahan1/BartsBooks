# Chapter 24: The Charge

The Floor 2 Boss wasn't sleeping. It was waiting.
It stood in the center of a grove of phosphorescent fungi. It was a Myconid Lord—the Spore-King. It was tall, thin, and capped with a massive, vibrant purple mushroom.
In its vine-like hands, it held a bow made of hardened stalk-fiber.

"Range," Pip warned, ducking behind a stalagmite. "It's an archer."

*THWIP.*
A spine, thin and hard as steel, slammed into the rock inches from Pip's nose.
*THWIP. THWIP. THWIP.*
The Spore-King was a machine gun.

"Gong!" Lin shouted. "Cover!"

Gong stepped out. *The Fortress.*
The arrows rained on him.
*Ping. Ting. Thud.*
They were heavier than normal arrows. They carried kinetic force.
And they exploded on impact, releasing clouds of choking yellow dust.

Gong coughed. His shields held, but the force was pushing him back.
One arrow found a gap in his armor, piercing his thigh.
"Ow," Gong grunted.
Another clipped his ear.
"That stings."

"Zhi! Fire!" Lin ordered, darting between shadows.
"I'm trying!" Zhi shouted. "But I can't get a clean shot! The dust is too thick!"

Then, the ground began to shake.
Around the periphery of the cavern, small mounds of dirt burst open.
Little mushroom men—Sprouts—popped out. Dozens of them. They carried spears and looked angry.

"Where did *those* come from?" Gong asked out loud, kicking one away.

"Adds!" Pip screamed, stabbing a Sprout. "They're called Adds! Dealing with them!"

The battle turned chaotic.
Lin and Pip were forced to defend Zhi from the swarm of Adds. They couldn't attack the Boss.
The Spore-King, free from pressure, focused entirely on Gong.
*THWIP-THWIP-THWIP.*
Gong was a pincushion. The arrows didn't go deep into his dense muscle, but they accumulated.
His HP (if he had a bar) was dropping.

Xiao was behind him, her hands glowing green. *Healing Light. Rejuvenation. Detox.*
She was sweating. Her qi reserves were bottoming out.
"Gong!" she gasped. "I can't keep this up! There's too much poison!"

Gong looked back. Xiao was pale. Her knees were shaking.
She was hurting. Because he wasn't stopping the damage. He was just taking it.
Blocking wasn't enough. Standing still wasn't enough.

block... block... block...
*Boring,* his core whispered.
*Heavy things should move,* the sediment rumbling in his stomach suggested.

Gong looked at the Boss across the cavern. It was smugly reloading its bow.
It thought Gong was a statue.
Gong tightened his grip on his shields.
He started to run.

It was a slow start. *Thud. Thud.*
But his weight was an advantage here. Once he got moving, physics took over.
He lowered his head. He clashed his shields together.
*CLANG.*

"RHINO CHARGE!" Gong shouted, for no real reason other than it felt right.

The Spore-King paused. It saw the metal boulder accelerating toward it.
It fired a volley.
Gong didn't stop. He didn't even block. He just slanted his shields, letting the arrows glance off his armored shoulders.
The arrows shattered.
Gong got faster.
He wasn't running anymore; he was rumbling. The ground shook with every step.

The Boss tried to move. But roots take time to pull up.
Gong hit it.
*CRUNCH.*

It was the sound of a pumpkin meeting a sledgehammer.
The Spore-King was lifted off its feet. It flew back twenty yards, crashed into the cavern wall, and slid down, leaving a smear of purple ichor.
Its bow was snapped in half.

The Adds froze.
Gong stood over the flattened mushroom. He was panting. He had arrows sticking out of his legs and arms.
"Target down," Gong wheezed.

Lin stared at him. "Did you just... tackle a boss?"
"Momentum," Gong explained, pulling an arrow out of his thigh. "Mass times velocity."

Xiao ran up to him, glowing with the last dregs of her mana.
"You idiot!" she cried, healing the hole in his leg. "You could have died!"
"But I didn't," Gong smiled. "And you stopped hurting."

Zhi poked the remains of the Spore-King. "Loot! Look at this Core! It's Huge!"
