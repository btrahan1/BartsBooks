# Chapter 25: The Mule

Floor 3 wasn't hard. It was just... long.
It was infested with Stone-Beetles.
They weren't smart. They didn't have arrows or magic. They just had numbers.
Thousands of them. Scuttling from every crack in the wall, a carpet of clicking black shells.

"Another wave!" Pip called out, his voice hoarse. "Ten... no, twenty of them!"

Gong didn't say anything. He just stepped forward.
*Clang.*
"Me."
He didn't even shout anymore. He didn't have the breath.
He just banged the shields and let them break themselves against him.
*Crunch. Crunch. Crunch.*

For four hours, it was a grinder.
Lin's arm was cramping from stabbing. Zhi was out of mana potions and was reduced to whacking beetles with his staff.
Xiao was leaning against a wall, judging the health of the party by how pale they looked.
But Gong didn't stop.
He was the wall. And the wall moved forward, one heavy step at a time.

Finally, the clicking stopped.
The tunnel opened up into a large antechamber. A massive stone gate stood closed at the far end. The Boss Room.

Gong looked at the gate. Then he looked at the floor.
He sat down.
It wasn't a graceful sit. It was a collapse of controlled gravity.
*THUD.*
Dust puffed up around him.

"Gong?" Xiao rushed over. "You okay? Are you hurt?"

"No hurt," Gong wheezed, wiping sweat from his eyes. "Just... heavy. A bit of a rest, perhaps?"

"We all need a rest," Lin agreed, sliding down the wall. "My wrist is killing me. I killed like... three hundred of those things."

"Three hundred and twelve," Pip corrected. "I counted."

Gong shifted. His pack clinked loudly.
"While we rest," Gong said. "Let's go through this loot."

"Loot?" Zhi blinked. "We didn't stop for loot. We were running for our lives."
"I stopped," Gong said.

He opened his sack. It was a standard burlap sack, the kind used for grain.
He turned it upside down.
*Clatter. Clink. Thump.*
Beetle cores.
Hundreds of them. Small, black, polished stones.
Then beetle shells. Stacks of them.
Then a couple of rusty daggers.
Then a mushroom he found on Floor 2.
Then a rock that looked cool.

The pile kept growing. It defied the physics of the sack.
Finally, Gong shook the bag. A last beetle core fell out.

The party stared at the mountain of loot. It was easily two hundred pounds of materials.
"You..." Pip pointed at the pile. "You carried that? While tanking?"

"It was on the ground," Gong explained. "Waste not, want not."

"But... the weight," Lin stammered. "You were moving slower. I thought you were tired. You were just... overloaded?"

"Momentum," Gong shrugged. "Once I get going, the bag just follows."

Zhi picked up a handful of cores. "These are Earth-Aspected. They sell for 10 copper each. There's... there's like 50 gold worth of cores here."
Gold. Real gold.
The eyes of the disciples went wide. A field mission usually paid 10 silver. This was a fortune.

"We split?" Gong asked.

"Yes," Lin said, looking at Gong with a mix of horror and adoration. "We split. You get an extra share. For... being the mule."

"Mule is good," Gong nodded, grabbing a strip of dried meat. "Mules are strong."
