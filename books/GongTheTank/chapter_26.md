# Chapter 26: The Retreat

The Floor 3 Guardian was a bad match.
It was a Magma Golem. A walking hill of molten rock and hatred.
Physical attacks bounced off specifically because they melted on impact. Gong’s "Clang" taunt worked, but being the focus of a creature made of lava was... unpleasant.

"It's too hot!" Lin screamed, her rapier glowing cherry-red. "I can't get close!"
"My fire heals it!" Zhi wailed. "Why does it have to be fire?!"
"Rocks don't bleed!" Pip yelled, throwing daggers that simply sank into the magma.

Gong stood in the center. His shields were glowing. His skin was blistering.
*Sediment.*
He packed the pain down. He held the aggro.
"Hit the... cool spots!" Gong choked out, inhaling ash.

It was ugly.
Gong went down first, overheated.
Then Zhi, knocked out by a flying lava rock.
Then Pip and Lin, scorched by an AOE blast.

It was Xiao who finished it.
She didn't attack. She healed... the boss.
She cast *Purify Water* on the golem's core.
Thermal shock.
*CRACK.*
The golem screamed as its core shattered from the rapid cooling. It crumbled into a pile of cooling slag.

Then Xiao collapsed.

***

Four hours later.
Gong woke up. His mouth tasted like charcoal.
The room was quiet. The air was still hot, but bearable.
Xiao was sitting against a wall, looking like a ghost. She had used every drop of Qi to stabilize them.
Lin was groaning. Zhi was unconscious. Pip was staring at the ceiling.

"We won?" Gong asked.
"Barely," Xiao whispered. "We survived."

Gong sat up. He looked at the heavy iron door that led to Floor 4.
He felt his core. It was heavy. Solid.
But his friends were cracked.
"We go home," Gong said.

"What?" Lin pushed herself up. "We cleared it. We can do Floor 4. We just need to rest."
"No," Gong shook his head. "We did good. But the job is done for today. If we go in there..." he pointed to the door, "...we break."

"But the mission..." Pip started.
"The mission is to clear. Not to die," Gong said simply. "We have loot. We are alive. That is a good shift."
He stood up, groaning as his burnt muscles protested. He picked up the massive sack of beetle loot.
"Come on. I will carry Zhi."

He hoisted the unconscious mage over one shoulder and the loot sack over the other.
"Let's go home."

***

In the Elder's Hall.
The Go board was untouched. The tea was cold.
"They are leaving," Elder Fang noted, watching the projection in the scrying mirror. "Floor 3. A respectable attempt."

"Respectable?" Iron-Heart scoffed. "It was a massacre. They only won because the Healer used her brain."

"Do you think they realize they can't beat the dungeon?" a third Elder, Elder Willow (Xiao's master), asked softy. "They are exhausted. A normal party would quit."

"Gong was given a task," Iron-Heart grunted. "To clear the Fungal Caverns. You know his file. He is simple. He is stubborn. He won't stop until the box is checked."

"If it was just him, I would agree," Elder Willow said, watching the image of Gong carrying his friend. "But he has a team now. He has things to protect."
She smiled.
"This will be interesting. Will his programming override his instinct? Or has he learned something new?"

Iron-Heart looked at the "Wall" trudging out of the dungeon.
"He learned to retreat," Iron-Heart said. "That is the hardest lesson for a Tank. To admit you aren't invincible."
He picked up a black stone.
"They will be back. And they will be stronger."
