# Chapter 27: The Paladin

The AAR (After Action Report) was held in the central hall.
Gong stood alone in the center of the room. His burns had healed into reddish scars. He wore fresh labor robes.
Xiao, Pip, Lin, and Zhi stood behind him, silent.

Elder Iron-Heart sat on the dais, flanked by Elder Fang and Elder Willow.
"Mission Evaluation," Iron-Heart began, reading from a scroll. "Five-man cell. Target: Fungal Caverns. Status: Failed."
He looked down at Gong.
"You retreated at Floor 3. Why?"

Pip stepped forward nervously. "Elder, the boss was—"
"Silence," Iron-Heart snapped. "I am asking the Tank."

Gong looked up. He didn't flinch.
"Failed," Gong repeated.
"Why?" Iron-Heart pressed. "Was the team too weak? Was the Healer too slow? Did the Scout trigger too many traps?"

It was a trap question. Fang leaned forward, expecting the laborer to point fingers. Everyone blames the DPS. Everyone blames the Healer.

"No," Gong said firmly. "I was not good enough."

The hall went silent.
"Explain," Iron-Heart said softness entering his voice.

"I am the Wall," Gong said. "The Wall broke. The fire came through. My friends got hurt."
He clenched his fists.
"I should be fireproof. I should be bigger. I let the rock melt me."
He bowed low.
"I am sorry. I need to learn more. I need to be... heavier."

Elder Willow smiled behind her hand. Elder Fang looked stunned.
"He... he actually means it," Fang whispered. "No excuses?"

"Accountability is the first step of power," Iron-Heart nodded approvingly. "You have the heart, Gong. But you lack the method."

"And learn you shall!" a booming voice echoed from the entrance.

The heavy doors groaned open.
Steps rang out on the stone floor. *CLANK. CLANK.*
A figure walked in. He was a giant, standing a head taller than even Gong.
He was clad in gleaming silver plate armor that shone with internal light. A white cape flowed from his shoulders.
On his back was a greatsword as long as a man. In his left hand, a shield that looked like it was made of diamond and starlight.

The Elders stood up. Even Iron-Heart bowed.

The figure stopped in front of Gong. He smelled of ozone and holy oil.
He took off his helmet, revealing a scarred, square-jawed face with eyes like blue steel.

"I watched your fight, boy," the giant rumbled. "You have terrible form. You have no footwork. You fight like a drunk rhinosaurus."
He grinned.
"But you have guts. And you know what the shield is for."

He slammed his fist against his chestplate.
"I am Gaul. Commander of the Silver Bastion. And I am your new trainer."

Gong looked at the shining man. He looked at the diamond shield.
"Hello, Gaul," Gong said politely. "Is your shield... edible?"

Gaul blinked. Then he threw his head back and laughed. "I like him! Come, student. We have work to do. First lesson: How not to melt."
