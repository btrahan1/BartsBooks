# Chapter 34: The Jog

The sun was barely up when the door to Gong's room (a converted storage closet near the Beast Pens) banged open.
"I hope you slept well!" Gaul shouted, looking sickeningly energetic in a lightweight track suit.

Gong groaned, rolling off his straw mat. It had only been a few hours since the Feast.
"Slept well," Gong muttered. "Full."

"Good!" Gaul slapped his hands together. "Because today we run!"

Gong looked at his boots. They were heavy. Iron-soled.
"Run?"
"Yes. Run. For distance."

***

And run they did.
It wasn't a sprint. It wasn't even a proper jog.
Gong ran like a boulder rolling downhill. It was a loping, shuffling motion. His feet barely left the ground, sliding forward with a *shhh-thud* rhythm that minimized impact and maximized efficiency.
It was ugly. It looked like a golem trying to dance.
But it didn't stop.

They ran out of the Sect gates. They ran through the Bamboo Forest. They ran up the winding path to the Cloud Peak and back down.
For four hours.
Gong didn't talk. He just breathed. *In-in-out. In-in-out.*

They stopped at a stream.
"Snack," Gaul announced, tossing Gong an apple.
Gong ate the apple. Core and all.
"Done," Gong said.

"Then we go back," Gaul grinned.

They ran for four more hours.
By the time they shuffled back into the training arena, the moon was rising.
Gong stopped. He didn't collapse. He just... turned off. He stood still, steam rising from his shoulders like a kettle.

Gaul walked around him, checking his pulse. It was slow. Strong.
"You touched a chicken," Gaul mused, pacing around his student. "That's the first step. It showed you have eyes."
He poked Gong's chest.
"Next, you need speed."
He watched Gong wobble slightly.
"Or... at least a semblance of it."

Gaul sighed. He had trained speedsters. He had trained duelists. He knew how to make people fast.
But Gong was built of sedimentary rock.
"It's a good thing you have endurance," Gaul admitted. "Because if you can't outrun them... you're just going to have to outlast them."

Gong nodded. "Outlast is good. Outlast means I am still here when they go home."

Gaul laughed. "Exactly. Now go sleep. Tomorrow... we add weights."
"Weights?" Gong asked, alarmed.
"You're a Tank, Gong," Gaul smiled. "Did you think running would be enough?"
