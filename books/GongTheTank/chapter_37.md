# Chapter 37: The Amazon

High above the ballroom, in the observation gallery, the Elders watched Gong trip over his own feet for the hundredth time.
"And what is the purpose of this?" Elder Iron-Heart asked, rubbing his temples. "He looks like a drunk bear in a silk curtain."

Gaul, who was snacking on a turkey leg, shrugged.
"Not my idea," the Paladin said. "It's hers."
He pointed to a figure standing in the shadows of the gallery.

She stepped forward into the light.
She was tall, nearly as tall as Gaul, with skin like burnished bronze and muscles that coiled like pythons.
She wore leather armor that emphasized mobility over protection—a barbarian style rarely seen in the Sect.
On her back was a long, cruel-looking spear. On her arm, a small, circular bronze shield.
She looked like a warrior goddess who had walked out of a myth to kick someone in the face.

"I am Vala," she announced. Her voice was deep and rough, like grinding stones.
Her class was unmistakably **Amazon Warrior**.

"You?" Iron-Heart blinked. "You want him to dance?"

"I want him to move," Vala corrected. "I want him to understand flow."
She looked down at Gong, who was currently apologizing to a marble pillar he had headbutted.
"I want to fight him," she declared, a hungry grin spreading across her face. "I will teach him the spear and shield. But first, he must not be a statue."

"Why?" Elder Willow asked. "There are many disciples who are faster. More skilled."

"Too weak," Vala spat. "I break them."
She gripped the railing, the metal groaning under her hand.
"I need someone to practice with. Someone who can take my full strength. Most men are too weak, and too slow. They snap like twigs."

She watched Gong stand up, shake off the impact, and resume his clumsy waltz.
"He is durable," Vala purred. "He is dense. I could fight with him for hours. I could stab him, bash him, throw him... and he would get back up."
Her eyes gleamed with a terrifying light.
"It will be glorious!"

Gaul toasted her with his turkey leg.
"She's been looking for a punching bag that hits back," Gaul explained to the horrified Elders. "Gong is the first one who fits the bill."

Vala leaned over the rail, shouting down at the ballroom.
"FASTER, WALL! OR I WILL COME DOWN THERE AND MAKE YOU DANCE!"

Gong looked up, terrified. "Who is the scary lady?"
"That's your next class," Elina whispered, looking pityingly at him. "Try not to die."
