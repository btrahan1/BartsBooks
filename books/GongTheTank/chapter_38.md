# Chapter 38: The Spear

A week of dancing with Elina had improved Gong's footwork from "Landslide" to "Controlled Avalanche."
Now, he stood before Vala, and he wished he was back in the ballroom.

He was wearing leather and fur. It was itchy. It exposed his arms and legs. He felt ridiculous. Like a barbarian who had lost his way to the buffet.
In his right hand, he held a spear. It was long, whippy, and balanced.
In his left hand, he held a shield.
If you could call it that.
It was round. It was bronze. It was half the size and a third of the weight of his beloved tower shields.
"It is a dinner plate," Gong muttered, looking at it dubiously. "What do I block with this? A grape?"

Vala circled him like a jaguar stalking a very large, very confused capybara.
She made appraising sounds.
"Hmmm."
"Ahhhh."
She poked his exposed calf with her spear tip. Gong flinched.

"I have been watching you for a week," Vala said, her voice low and dangerous. "You move like a pregnant yak."

Gong sighed. "Elina said 'Avalanche'."

"Avalanches go one way," Vala snapped. "Down. You need to go *every* way."
She stepped back, leveling her spear at his throat.
"I heard you couldn't really move at all and just stood there. That you let things hit you because you were too lazy to dodge."

"Not lazy," Gong corrected politely. "Sturdy."

"That won't do," Vala hissed. "In my tribe, if you stand still, you become a pincushion."
She spun her spear, the bronze tip blurring.
"This shield is not a wall, Gong. It is a deflector. It is a second fist. And this spear..."
She lunged, stopping an inch from his nose.
"...is your fang."

Gong tried to hide behind his dinner plate. It covered his chest, but left his head and legs exposed. He felt very, very naked.

"Prepare yourself!" Vala shouted.
She didn't wait for an answer. She unleashed a flurry of strikes.
*Clang. Ting. Thwack.*
Gong tried to block like a wall. He planted his feet and held the shield out.
Vala's spear snaked around the rim, rapping his knuckles, poking his shoulder, slapping his ear.

"Move!" she roared, sweeping his legs with the shaft of her spear.
Gong hit the dirt.

"Again!" Vala ordered, waiting for him to get up. "The yak must learn to dance, or the yak gets eaten!"
