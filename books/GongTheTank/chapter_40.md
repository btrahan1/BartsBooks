# Chapter 40: The Target

The next morning, Vala didn't take him to the arena. She took him to the Weapon Testing Grounds.
It was a long, narrow field lined with training dummies of various materials.

"Why are we here?" Gong asked, adjusting his itchy fur pauldron.

Vala turned to him. She wasn't smiling today.
"You are holding back," she said flatly.
Gong blinked. "I am bad. You said so."

"You are bad at *moving*," Vala corrected. "But when you strike... you pull your punches. I hear things, Gong. I hear you don't want to hit a girl."

Gong blushed. "Momma said never hit—"

"I AM NOT A GIRL!" Vala roared, slamming her spear butt into the ground. "I am a Warrior! When you hold back, you insult my steel! You insult my time!"
She stepped closer, poking a finger into his chest armor.
"You think you are protecting me? I could snap you in half, little fortress."

Gong looked at his boots. "Sorry."

Vala sighed. She walked over to a rack of spears.
"You are an idiot," she said, but her voice was softer. "But you are a strong idiot. You could be amazing with a spear, Gong. It is a simple weapon. It takes force and focuses it into a single point."
She traced the line of a spear shaft.
"With your strength... if you developed the right strike... you could pierce a mountain."

Gong looked at the spear in his hand. *Pierce a mountain?* It seemed unlikely. It was just a stick with a sharp bit.

"But you lack focus," Vala continued. "You lack killer instinct. So, I will give you a different instinct."

She pointed down the line of targets.
There were ten of them.
The first was pine wood.
The second, oak.
The third, stone.
The fourth, iron.
They got progressively darker and shinier, ending with a black, metallic dummy that looked terrifyingly hard. Adamantite.

"Those range from 'Peasant' to 'Dragon'," Vala explained. "Your job is to destroy them. Not hit them. *Destroy* them."

Gong looked at the Adamantite dummy. "It looks... tough."

"It is," Vala grinned.
She reached into a pouch and pulled out a dried meat strip.
"Every time you destroy a target, you get to eat."
She tossed the meat in the air and caught it in her mouth.
"Have fun!"

Gong’s eyes widened.
He looked at the wooden dummy.
He looked at the iron dummy.
He looked at the spear.
It wasn't a weapon anymore. It was a spoon. And the dummies were between him and the soup.

"Eat," Gong whispered.
He took a stance.
It wasn't the clumsy stance of a dancer. It was the grounded stance of a hungry rhino.
He lunged.
*CRACK.*
The pine dummy exploded into splinters.

"One!" Vala cheered, tossing a bun.
Gong caught it mid-chew and turned to the oak dummy.
This was going to be a good lunch.
