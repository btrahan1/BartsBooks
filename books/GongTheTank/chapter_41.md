# Chapter 41: Sturdy

The lunch break was... loud.
And dusty.

"Two!" Gong grunted, slamming the spear butt into the Oak dummy. It splintered down the center.
Vala tossed a drumstick. Gong caught it, ate it bone and all, and moved to the next one.

The Stone dummy was harder. It didn't splinter; it chipped.
*Thwack. Thwack. Thwack. Thwack. CRUMBLE.*
"Five!" Gong announced, shaking stone dust from his hair.
He accepted a pork bun.

The Iron dummy was annoying. It dented.
Gong had to use "The Adjustment" to find the weak points in the casting. He hammered the same spot, over and over, driving the spear tip in like a chisel.
*CLANG-CLANG-CLANG... SCREECH.*
Ten strikes. The dummy folded in half.
"Ten," Gong panted. He ate a bowl of rice.

The Steel dummy (Target 5) was a workout.
Twenty strikes. Full power. Using the "Rhino Charge" start-up for extra kinetic energy.
By the time it finally snapped at the waist, Gong was sweating properly. His arms burned. The spear shaft was starting to crack from the force he was putting through it.
"Twenty," Gong wheezed.

He ate an apple. He looked at the line.
He was halfway through.
He stepped up to the 6th target.

It wasn't shiny like the steel one. It was matte grey. It looked heavy. Dense. It absorbed the light rather than reflecting it.
Gong poked it.
*Thud.*
It didn't ring. It barely vibrated. It felt... dead.
He tapped it harder.
*THUD.*
"Ooh," Gong whispered. He ran his hand over the surface. It was cool to the touch.

He turned to Vala, his eyes wide with professional admiration.
"Vala," he called out. "What is this one made of? It looks very sturdy!"

Vala was leaning on her spear, picking her teeth.
"That," she said, "is Lead-Alloy. It's soft, but it absorbs impact. It doesn't shatter. It just eats the force."
She grinned.
"You can't break it with power, Gong. You have to pierce it. If you hit it blunt, it just laughs at you."

Gong looked back at the dummy.
"It laughs?"
"Metaphorically."

Gong frowned. He poked the lead man again.
"It is a good dummy," he complimented it. "Very tanky."
He braced his feet. He leveled his spear.
"But I am hungry."
