# Chapter 42: Through

*Thud.*
Impact 21.
The spear tip sank an inch into the soft, heavy metal, then stopped. The dummy absorbed the energy, shuddered, and pushed the spear back out.
*Thud.*
Impact 22.
Gong frowned. "It is chewing my spear."

*Thud. Thud. Thud.*
Impact 25.
Gong was panting. He wasn't tired, but he was frustrated. He was hitting it hard enough to crack stone, but the lead just... ate it.

Vala stepped forward.
"Stop," she commanded.

Gong lowered his spear. "It is cheating. It is soft but hard."

Vala didn't answer. She stood in front of the dummy. She breathed in.
Gong blinked.
He didn't see her move.
One moment, she was standing still. The next, she was behind the dummy.
There was a *thwip* sound.
A neat, circular hole appeared in the center of the lead chest. Sunlight shone through it.

Vala retracted her spear with a smooth motion and placed it back on her back.
"You are hitting *at* it," she said, turning to face him. "You are treating the spear like a hammer. You are trusting the weight to do the work."

She tapped the hole in the dummy.
"You can't defeat this target with strength. Lead laughs at strength. It drinks it."
She looked him in the eye.
"You need acceleration. You need to focus all that mass into a single point, infinitely small, moving infinitely fast."
She made a thrusting motion with her hand.
"You have to pierce *through* the target. Don't aim for the chest. Aim for the tree behind it."

Gong scratched his head. He looked at the dummy. He looked at the tree fifty yards away.
"But... the dummy is in the way."

"Exactly," Vala nodded. "It is an inconvenience. Do not let it stop you."

Gong stared at his spear.
"Through," he muttered.
He had spent his whole life stopping things. Being a wall. A wall doesn't go through things. Things go *into* a wall.
This was... backwards.
"I don't understand," he admitted.

Vala sighed. She pulled out a sticky bun from her pouch.
She held it behind the dummy.
"Gong," she said. "The bun is here."

Gong's eyes locked onto the bun.
"Through," he whispered.
