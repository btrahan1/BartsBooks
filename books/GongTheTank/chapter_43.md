# Chapter 43: Die Tree

*Thud.*
Impact 99.
Gong's arms were shaking. The lead dummy was covered in dents, like a piece of chewed gum, but it was still whole.
Vala was sitting on a rock nearby, eating the sticky bun.
"Seventy-five more times," she noted. "Still hitting like a wall. Walls don't go through things, Gong."

Gong wiped sweat from his eyes.
He looked at the dummy. He hated it.
He looked at the tree behind it. Vala had said to aim for the tree.
Why the tree? What did the tree do?
Maybe the tree was mocking him. Look at it, standing there with its leaves, waving comfortably while Gong starved.
The tree was the enemy. The tree was keeping him from his lunch.
The dummy was just... a distraction. A fog he had to punch through to kill the evil tree.

Gong gripped the spear. The wood groaned under his strength.
He didn't wind up. He didn't brace.
He just stared at the bark of the pine tree fifty yards away.
He visualized the spear tip burying itself in that bark.
The lead man didn't exist.
*It's just air,* he told himself. *Heavy, grey air.*

"Die," Gong whispered.
Then he screamed.
"DIE TREE!"

He exploded forward.
It wasn't a step; it was a launch. The 'Explosive Acceleration' kicked in.
His body became a projectile. The spear was the tip.
He didn't feel the impact.
There was no *thud*.
There was a *shhh-tunk*.

Gong stumbled, nearly falling on his face.
He blinked.
He was standing *behind* the lead dummy.
He looked at his spear. The tip was buried deep in the pine tree.
He looked back at the dummy.
There was a ragged, fist-sized hole punch through its center.

Vala stood up, dusting crumbs from her hands.
"Finally," she grinned. "You killed the tree."

Gong pulled his spear free. He looked at the hole in the dummy.
"It felt... slippery," he said, amazed. "Like cutting water."

"That is piercing," Vala nodded. "Welcome to the path of the Amazon."
She tossed him another bun.
"Next target."
