# Chapter 44: The Drill

Target 7 was weird.
It wasn't metal. It wasn't wood. It was a pale, fleshy pink color. It looked like a giant block of solidified sap, or maybe muscle.
Gong poked it. It was squishy.

"This target is different," Vala said, standing beside him.
She lunged.
*THWACK.*
Her spear sank deep into the material.
Gong watched, impressed. But then Vala withdrew her spear.
The hole didn't stay. The pink material squelched, flowed, and knitted itself back together. Within seconds, the surface was smooth again.

"It heals," Gong whispered. "Like a troll."

"Regenerative Armor," Vala corrected. "Sometimes, you can't defeat a target with 1 strike. No matter how hard you hit, if you stop, it undoes your work."

She took a stance.
"You have to hit the same spot in rapid succession. Like a hammer on a nail."

She twirled her spear and lunged.
But it wasn't one lunge.
*Thwip-Thwip-Thwip.*
It sounded like tearing canvas.
Her arm was a blur. She hit the target three times in the blink of an eye.
The first hit opened the wound.
The second hit deepened it before it could close.
The third hit punched through the back.

She withdrew her spear. The hole tried to close, but the damage was too deep, too structural. It remained a gaping, oozing tunnel.

"I drilled it," Vala said, wiping slime from her spear tip. "Three strikes. Same spot. Minimal delay."
She looked at Gong.
"Understand?"

Gong looked at his massive arms. He was used to one big swing. One big crunch.
"Fast poking," he summarized.
"Accurate fast poking," Vala emphasized. "If you miss the hole by an inch, you are just making two shallow scratches. You must be precise."

Gong nodded. He aimed his spear at the pink blob.
He took a breath.
He tried to be a woodpecker.
*Thwack... Thwack... Thwack.*
He hit it three times.
He made three separate holes, forming a neat little triangle.
All three healed instantly.

Vala sighed. "We are going to be here a while. Elina is going to be rich."
