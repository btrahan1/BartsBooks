# Chapter 45: Homework

By the time the sun dipped below the horizon, the pink dummy looked like it had a rash.
It was covered in shallow punctures, healed scars, and the occasional double-tap.

"Two!" Gong panted, pulling his spear back.
He had managed to hit the same spot twice. The hole was deeper, though it still hadn't pierced through.
It was happening about once every ten attempts.

"Better," Vala nodded, tossing him a canteen. "That's enough for tonight. You are starting to wobble."

Gong drank greedily. "It is hard work. Being a woodpecker is tiring."

Vala strapped her shield to her back.
"Keep practicing. I have to leave."
Gong lowered his canteen. "Leave?"

"Mission," Vala said vaguely. "I will be back in a week. While I am gone, you are to train here for four hours a day. Work on the Drill. Work on the Pierce. Do not let your muscles forget."

"Four hours," Gong repeated. "Okay."

"The rest of the time is yours," Vala added. She looked at him with a strange expression—something almost like kindness.
"I hear your pet rhino misses you."

Gong froze.
Maggie.
He had been so consumed by Gaul's endurance runs, Elina's dancing, and Vala's abuse that he hadn't been back to the Beast Pens in... nearly a month.
A month in a baby rhino's life was an eternity. She probably had horns by now. Or a husband.

"Maggie," Gong whispered, guilt washing over him.

"Go," Vala smiled, turning to walk away into the twilight. "She is probably eating the fence by now."
She waved without looking back.
"Don't get fat while I'm gone, Fortress!"
