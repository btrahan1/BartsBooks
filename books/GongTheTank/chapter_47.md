# Chapter 47: The Ward

Gong was brushing Maggie's flank, enjoying the rare moment of peace, when the gate to the Beast Pens creaked open.
It wasn't Brother Cai. It wasn't a customer.
It was an Elder.
He wore robes of shifting grey silk that seemed to vanish into the morning mist. He radiated an aura of quiet, terrifying authority.

Beside him stood a girl.
She was small, perhaps twelve or thirteen. She had large, dark eyes that observed the world with a mix of boredom and sharpness. She held a closed fan in her hand.

"Gong," the Elder stated. It wasn't a question.

Gong dropped his brush and bowed deeply. "Elder."
Maggie snorted and continued eating hay, unimpressed by authority.

"This is my niece, Mia," the Elder stated, gesturing to the girl. "She is now your ward."

Gong blinked. "My... ward?"

The Elder motioned to Brother Cai, who had scurried out of the office, trembling. He tossed a heavy sack of coins to the manager.
"You have been paid," the Elder said. "For the next month, you belong to her."

He turned his cold gaze back to Gong.
"You will spend every waking moment with her. You will guard her with your life, and beyond if need be."

Mia stepped forward. She looked Gong up and down, then looked at Maggie.
"Is the rhino part of the deal?" she asked. Her voice was light, but firm.
"If you wish it, it is," the Elder replied instantly.

He stepped closer to Gong, lowering his voice so only the big youth could hear.
"You have wondered why we pushed you. Why Gaul broke you. Why Vala beat you."
He placed a hand on Mia's shoulder.
"She is why. She is why we, The Elders, have taken an interest in you."
He looked Gong in the eye.
"She will be part of your team in the upcoming tournament. You are not just a shield, Gong. You are *her* shield."

Gong looked at the small girl. She looked harmless. But he knew better than to judge by size. Vala was smaller than him, and she was a nightmare.
"I will keep her safe," Gong promised. "Like a baby rhino."

The Elder didn't smile.
"Don't disappoint us."
He vanished in a swirl of mist, leaving Gong standing in the mud with a sack of gold, a confused manager, and a twelve-year-old girl who was currently trying to climb onto Maggie.
