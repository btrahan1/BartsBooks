# Chapter 49: The Partner

The sun was beginning to dip, casting long shadows across the Sect.
It was time for The Dance.

Gong dragged his feet toward the ballroom. His spear arm was throbbing from the Drill. His legs were tired from the Run.
Usually, this was the time he got stepped on by unhappy volunteers.
He hoped, secretly, that today nobody would show up. Maybe they all had strict parents. Or food poisoning.

He walked into the empty ballroom.
"Hello?" he called out, his voice echoing off the marble.
There was no line of sullen disciples. Just the empty floor.
Gong smiled. *Maybe I get a nap.*

"Ready?" a voice chirped.

Gong looked down.
Mia was standing there. She had changed out of her travel robes into something lighter—a practice dress that looked suspiciously professional. She had her fan snapped shut in her belt.

"Ready for what?" Gong asked. "To watch?"

Mia giggled. It was a terrifying sound.
"To dance, silly. I am your partner."

Gong froze.
He looked at the small girl. He looked at his giant boots.
"What? You are my dance partner for a month?" Gong groaned. The sound came from the depths of his soul.

"Yes!" Mia beamed. She did a little twirl. "Uncle said you needed help with your footwork. And I need a partner who won't break if I step on them."

Gong rubbed his face. "I am very breakable inside."

"Nonsense," Mia said, grabbing his hand. Her grip was surprisingly firm.
"I know 30 dances! The Cloud Step Waltz. The Crane's Courtship. The Storm Jig..."
Her eyes sparkled with manic energy.
"One for each day! Isn't that exciting?"

Gong looked at the calendar in his mind. Thirty days. Thirty dances.
No.
No, it wasn't.
It wasn't exciting at all.

"Can we do the one where we stand still?" Gong asked weakly.

"That's called a statue," Mia corrected. "And statues get pigeons on them. Come on! Day One: The River Flow!"
She yanked his arm.
Gong stumbled forward.
It was going to be a long month.
