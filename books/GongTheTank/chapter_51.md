# Chapter 51: The Encounter

They had gone into the Lower City to buy ribbons. Mia had insisted.
Now, on the way back, the alleyway felt too quiet.

Five men stepped out from the shadows.
They weren't sect disciples. They were rough, scarred, and smelled of cheap ale and violence. They held jagged blades and weighted clubs.
They had received a tip: A pretty girl, a foolish bodyguard, an easy ransom. Perhaps a bit of fun before the payout.

Gong stopped. He put the bag of ribbons down gently.
He unslung his round shield and leveled his spear.
He stepped in front of Mia, his large frame blocking her completely from view.

"Be on your way," Gong said. His voice wasn't his usual confused mumble. It was deep, resonating like a temple bell. "You don't want to be here."

The leader of the thugs laughed. "We are exactly where we want to be, big man. Move aside, and you might get to keep your teeth."

Gong didn't move. He didn't blink.
"She is my ward," he stated, as if that explained everything. "You should leave."

They didn't.
They charged.

Two went for Gong's legs. Two went for his arms. The leader hung back, laughing.
Gong moved. It wasn't the graceful dance of Elina. It was the violent lurch of a falling tree.
*CRUNCH.*
His shield met a club. The club shattered. The shield continued, meeting the thug's face.
*THWACK.*
His spear lashed out, not piercing, but bludgeoning. A thug folded over, gasping.

But there were too many. A knife scored Gong's thigh. A club clipped his shoulder.
Gong grunted, absorbing the pain. He grabbed a thug by the collar and used him to bludgeon another thug.
"Sturdy!" he roared, slamming them into the wall.

In the chaos, the fifth man—the leader—saw his opening.
He slipped past Gong's flank.
"Gotcha!" he snarled, reaching for Mia with a grimy hand.

Mia stood there, clutching her fan. Her eyes were wide.
But as the hand reached for her, she didn't freeze.
She stepped.
*One-two-three.*
It was the Waltz.
She spun under his arm. The thug grabbed empty air.
"What?" he snarled, spinning around.
Mia twirled again, her silk robes flowing like water. She was terrified, her heart hammering against her ribs like a trapped bird, but her body knew the rhythm.
He lunged. She pivoted.
He swiped. She dipped.
She danced around him like a butterfly teasing a bear.

"Gong!" she shrieked, part fear, part thrill.

Gong heard her.
He dropped the two unconscious thugs he was holding.
He turned. He saw the leader reaching for his ward.
"NO!"
Gong launched himself. Explosive Acceleration.
He didn't use a spear. He used his head.
*CRACK.*

***

Ten minutes later, the alley was quiet, save for the groans of five very broken men.
A healer—who had suspiciously just "happened" to be nearby—was tending to Gong's cuts.
"You'll live," the healer grumbled. "Though I can't say the same for these gentlemen's knees."

Mia was sitting on a crate, still clutching her ribbons.
She was shaking.
Gong looked at her, worried.
"Did I... did I fail?" he asked. "He got close."

Mia looked up. Her eyes weren't filled with tears. They were shining. Bright, manic through the fear.
"He couldn't touch me," she whispered.
She looked at her hands. She looked at the unconscious thug.
"He tried... but I was faster."
She looked at Gong, a wild grin spreading across her face.
"I felt... alive!"

Gong sighed, accepting a bandage from the healer.
"Elders are weird," he muttered. "Both of them."
