# Chapter 52: The Swap

"Adequate," Elder Iron-Heart admitted, looking at the report. "Gong took four blunt-force injuries and one slash. He neutralized four targets. Mia evaded capture for forty-five seconds against a superior weight class."

"She enjoyed it," Mist-Walker noted, his voice tight. "Too much."

"Then we test that enjoyment," Iron-Heart decided. "The alley was chaotic. We need a controlled environment. A gauntlet."

He unrolled a map of the Fungal Caverns.
"They retreated at Floor 3 last time. The Magma Golem beat them."

Mist-Walker studied the map. "A dungeon. It is dangerous, but predictable."

"Swap Mia in for one of the three on the team," Iron-Heart commanded. "Let's see how far they get this time."

***

An hour later, the team was assembled in the mission hall.
Gong looked nervous. Xiao looked tired.
Pip, Sister Lin, and Zhi looked confused.

Elder Iron-Heart stood before them.
"The Sect requires a new assessment of the Fungal Caverns," he announced. "You will deploy within the hour."

Pip let out a small squeak of terror. "Floor 3 again? But the Golem..."

"Pip," the Elder interrupted. "You are dismissed."

Pip blinked. "Dismissed? Like... I'm fired?"
"For this mission, yes. You will remain here."

Pip fell to his knees, weeping tears of pure joy. "Thank you! Oh, thank the Heavens! I don't want to be burned!"

"Your replacement is here," Iron-Heart gestured.

Mia stepped out from behind Gong. She was wearing a sleek, form-fitting combat robe of midnight blue, reinforced with leather. Her fan was in her belt. She looked ready for war.

"Hello," she chirped. "I am Mia. I will be your Scout."

The silence was heavy.
"A child?" Zhi asked, the fire mage frowning. "We are swapping our Scout for a child?"

"She is my ward," Gong said instantly, stepping in front of her. "And she is... fast."

"I am very fast," Mia agreed, peeking around Gong's elbow. "And I don't cry." She looked pointedly at Pip, who was currently hugging the Elder's boot in gratitude.

Sister Lin assessed Mia's gear. "She has no weapon."
"I have my fan," Mia smiled, snapping it open. A blade of wind sliced the air, severing a loose thread on Lin's sleeve.
Lin's eyes widened. "Okay. She stays."

"Gong," Iron-Heart barked. "Your mission is unchanged. Tank the damage.Protect the Healer. And now, Protect the Scout."

Gong looked at the dark tunnel entrance. He looked at Mia, who was practically vibrating with excitement.
"I will hold the door," Gong promised.
"Good," Mia grinned. "Because I'm going to kick it open."
