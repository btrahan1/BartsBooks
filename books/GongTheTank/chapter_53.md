# Chapter 53: The Gear

They did not send them in empty-handed.
The Elders spared no expense.

Gong stood in the prep room, marveling at himself in a polished shield.
Gone was the mismatched leather and the rusted iron.
He was clad head to toe in Elven Chain Mail. It shimmered like moonlight on water. It felt strange—light, fluid, almost like wearing a heavy silk robe. But when he tapped it with his knuckle, it didn't give. It was dense.
"It breathes," Gong whispered, flexing his shoulders. "And it doesn't pinch."

His head was covered in a matching coif that draped over his neck, leaving only his face exposed.
And the weapons...
On his back was his trusty Tower Shield, refurbished and reinforced.
On his left arm was the Amazonian Round Shield—the "Dinner Plate"—now made of a dark, star-flecked metal.
In his right hand was a spear. It was black, balanced perfectly, with a tip that looked like it could cut a shadow. It felt dangerous. It felt eager.

"I feel... invincible," Gong murmured.
"Don't get cocky," Xiao warned, checking her potion belt. "Invincible usually means 'about to fall in a hole'."

They entered Floor 1.
The Fungal Caverns were just as damp and smelly as before. The spore-clouds drifted lazily.
But the party was different.
Last time, they had stumbled. They had argued.
This time, they flowed.

Mia didn't hide behind Gong. She moved ahead, a shadow in her blue robes.
"Three Spore-Rats ahead," she signaled with her fan. "One on the ceiling."
"Pull them," Gong ordered.

He stepped forward.
*CLANG. CLANG. CLANG.*
He slammed the shaft of his spear against the rim of his round shield. The sound was sharp, rhythmic, and incredibly annoying.
The rats screeched and charged him instantly.
Gong didn't just block. He engaged. He caught the first rat on his round shield, deflecting it into the path of the second. He thrust his spear—*Thwip*—piercing the third.
Lin and Zhi cleaned up the rest before they could even flank.

It was efficient. It was brutal.
They swept through the floor like a broom.

Finally, they reached the Boss Chamber. The Moss-Back Bear.
It roared, rising on its hind legs, shaking the stalactites.
Last time, this roar had frozen them. Last time, Gong had been knocked flying.

"Taunt!" Zhi yelled.
Gong stepped forward.
*BANG-BANG-BANG.*
"Come here, rug!" Gong shouted.

The bear charged.
Gong planted his feet. He didn't just brace; he leaned into the impact, his Elven Chain absorbing the shockwave that would have rattled his old bones.
He caught the paw on his Tower Shield, then—using Vala's lessons—he pivoted.
He didn't just tank the hit; he let it slide off, opening the bear's flank.
"NOW!"

Mia was there instantly. Her fan flashed.
Lin's rapier blurred.
Zhi's fireball struck the exposed belly.
The bear didn't even get a second swing. It collapsed with a confused groan.

Silence fell over the chamber.
Gong lowered his shield. He wasn't even out of breath.
Xiao sat down on a mushroom, wiping sweat from his forehead—not from exertion, but from stress habit.
"Well," Xiao laughed nervously. "That was definitely harder the first time."

Gong patted his Elven Chain.
"Sturdy," he agreed.
Then he looked at the dark tunnel leading down.
"Floor Two," he said.
