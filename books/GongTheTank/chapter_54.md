# Chapter 54: The Pincushion

Floor Two was usually a slog of boulders and bats.
Today, it was a track meet.

"Trap!" Mia called out, flicking her fan. A gust of wind triggered the tripwire, sending the boulder crashing harmlessly while they were still twenty yards away.
"Bats!" Lin shouted.
Zhi didn't even stop walking. He snapped his fingers, and a wave of fire incinerated the ceiling.

They reached the Boss Chamber. The Spore-King.
A massive, bulbous mushroom creature surrounded by a legion of smaller saprolings.
Last time, they had hesitated. They had strategized. They had feared the swarm.

"Plan?" Xiao asked, uncorking a stamina potion.
"I kill the big vegetables," Gong said, lowering his visor. "You kill the little vegetables."

He didn't wait. He charged.
*CLANG-CLANG-CLANG.*
The sound of his spear banging his shield echoed like a war drum.
The Spore-King turned its faceless cap toward him. It tried to summon a cloud of poison.
Gong didn't care. His Elven Chain filtered the air, and his Constitution laughed at toxins.

He closed the distance.
He didn't use the Tower Shield to wall it off. He used the Round Shield to bat away its tendrils.
And then, the spear work began.
*Thwip-Thwip-Thwip.*
The Drill.
Gong targeted the central stalk. Three strikes, rapid fire. Purple ichor sprayed.
The Spore-King shuddered, trying to heal, trying to summon help.
But Gong didn't stop. He was a machine fueled by sticky buns and Vala's insults.
*Thwack. Pierce. Drill.*

Around him, the rest of the team was a whirlwind.
Mia danced through the saprolings, her fan slicing them apart like paper.
Lin's rapier moved so fast it hummed, dismantling the adds that tried to flank Gong.
Zhi rained fire on the perimeter, keeping the reinforcements at bay.

The Spore-King roared—a wet, gurgling sound—and tried to slam Gong with a massive root.
Gong didn't brace. He stepped. He pivoted.
The root missed him by an inch.
"My turn," Gong grunted.

He visualized the tree behind the boss.
"Die Tree!" he whispered.
He thrust.
The spear went in one side of the massive stalk and came out the other.
The Spore-King stiffened, then collapsed into a heap of rotting mulch.

The room fell silent, save for the sizzling of Zhi's leftover fire.
Gong pulled his spear free and wiped it on a relatively clean patch of moss.
"Soup is ready," he announced.
