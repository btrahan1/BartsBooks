# Chapter 55: The Gatekeeper

Floor Three was hot.
The air shimmered with heat rising from the lava vents below.
And then came the clicking. Thousands of legs.
The Obsidian Beetles.

Last time, Gong had been "The Mule," passively tanking the swarm while collecting loot. It had worked, but it was slow.
This time, he didn't put the loot bag on his back.
He unslung his Tower Shield on his left arm and his heavy Round Shield on his right.
"Formation," he ordered.

He stepped into the chokepoint of the tunnel.
The wave of beetles crashed into him.
*CRUNCH.*
Gong didn't budge. His Elven Chain clinked softly as claws raked it, but he ignored them.
He wasn't just a wall today. He was a door.

"Ready?" he called back.
"Ready!" Lin answered, her rapier poised.

Gong pivoted his left foot—a move Elina called the "Open Waltz."
He swung his Tower Shield out, creating a gap exactly wide enough for two beetles.
The insects surged through, chittering in triumph.
*SLAM.*
Gong stepped back, bringing his Round Shield across, sealing the gap instantly.
Behind him, Lin and Mia descended on the two isolated beetles.
*Slice. Snap.*
Dead.

"Clear," Mia called.

Gong opened the gate again.
Two more beetles.
*SLAM.*
Close.
Two dead beetles.

It was rhythmic. It was disciplined.
Open. Close. Kill.
Open. Close. Kill.

Gong's feet moved with intent. He wasn't stumbling or slipping. He was controlling the flow of the battle, turning a chaotic swarm into a manageable trickle.
"He's actually... dancing," Xiao whispered, impressed, while topping off Gong's hydration.

They cleared the floor in half the time.
No panic. No overwhelming odds. Just a steady march of efficiency.

Finally, the tunnel widened. The heat intensified.
They stood before the massive iron doors of the Boss Chamber.
Through the cracks, they could see the red glow of magma.
The Magma Golem.
The reason they had retreated last time.

Gong lowered his shields. He took a deep breath of the sulfurous air.
"Stop," he said, holding up a hand. "Assess."

The team gathered. They weren't broken. They weren't terrified.
They were professionals.
"We know its moves," Gong said. "Big smash. Lava vomit. Fire aura."
He looked at Mia.
"Can you dodge fire?"
"I can dodge anything," Mia said, though she gripped her fan tightly.

Gong nodded. He looked at the door.
"Then let's go break a rock."
