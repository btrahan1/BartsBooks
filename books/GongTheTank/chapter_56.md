# Chapter 56: Resistance

Gong reached for the heavy iron handle of the boss door.
"Wait," Mia said.

She reached into a pouch at her waist—one that looked far too expensive for a simple disciple—and tossed a small, glowing red vial to him.
Gong caught it. It was warm to the touch.
"Drink this," she ordered.

Gong uncorked it and sniffed. It smelled like spicy peppers and cold water. A contradiction.
"What is it?"
"Fire Resistance Potion," Mia explained. "High grade. It will coat your throat and your insights. You have ten minutes."

Ten minutes.
Last time, the fight had lasted three minutes before they were routing in panic.
Gong downed the potion in one gulp.
A chill spread through his chest, battling the ambient heat of the cavern. He felt insulated. Cool.
He smiled, tossing the empty vial aside.
"Plenty of time."

He shoved the doors open.
*GROOOAN.*
The Magma Golem rose from its pool of lava in the center of the chamber. Heat rolled off it in waves.
Gong didn't wait on the periphery this time.
He charged straight for the lava's edge.

The Golem roared and slammed a fist of molten rock down.
*BOOM.*
Gong caught it on his Tower Shield. The heat flared, licking around the edges, but the potion held. The Elven Chain held.
"Is that it?" Gong shouted, banging his spear against the shield. *CLANG.*

The Golem focused entirely on him. It wound up for a magma breath attack.
"Turn him!" Lin shouted.
Gong stepped—a clumsy pirouette—turning the Golem's back to the team.
The magma vomit washed over Gong's shield. It hissed and steamed, but Gong just gritted his teeth and held the line.
"Hot shower!" he yelled.

While the Golem poured its rage onto the unmoving tank, the rest of the team went to work.
Mia danced in, slashing the Golem's cooling knee joints with wind blades.
Lin pierced the cracks in its obsidian armor.
Zhi switched to lightning, cracking the superheated rock.

The Golem crumbled. It tried to smash, but Gong was always there, a wall of steel and magically chilled determination.
It roared one last time, a sound of crumbling stone, and fell apart into a pile of harmless, steaming rocks.

Gong stood amidst the rubble.
The cool sensation in his chest was fading.
"Eight minutes," Mia announced, checking a tiny hourglass.
Gong dusted volcanic ash off his shoulder.
"Efficient," he nodded. "I'm hungry."
