# Chapter 57: The Curse

Floor Four was different.
Animals were predictable. Mechanisms were logical.
Goblins were neither.

They used crossbows from the shadows. They set tripwires that dropped nets. They threw jars of biting ants.
By the time the team reached the Chieftain's Hall, they were bleeding.
Lin had a dart in her shoulder. Zhi was out of mana potions. Even Mia’s blue robes were stained with soot.

The Goblin Chieftain sat on a throne of bones. Shamanistic totems rattled around him.
He didn't roar. He chanted.

"Engage!" Gong yelled, his voice rasping.

The fight was chaos. Elite hobgoblins formed a shield wall, blocking Gong's advance.
"Break them!" Gong roared, slamming his Tower Shield into their line.
While Gong wrestled with the front line, the Chieftain stood up.
He pointed a gnarled staff not at the tank, but at the dancer.
A ball of sick, purple energy coalesced at the tip. It screamed as it flew, aiming straight for Mia's back.

Mia was busy dodging two daggers. She didn't see it.
Gong did.
"NO!"

He didn't think. He didn't calculate.
He triggered his Explosive Acceleration.
His boots gouged deep furrows in the stone as he launched himself sideways.
He threw himself into the path of the bolt.

*SPLAT.*
The curse didn't hit with force; it hit with sickness.
Gong felt his strength evaporate. It was as if his bones had turned to water. His vision swam.
The spear in his right hand felt like it weighed a thousand tons.
He dropped it.
*Clatter.*

"Gong!" Mia screamed, seeing the purple aura writhing over him.

"Focus!" Gong wheezed.
He couldn't attack. He could barely stand.
But he didn't need to attack.
He reached back and unslung his Round Shield.
Two shields. One wall.
"I am... The Mountain," he whispered.

He planted his feet. The hobgoblins hacked at him. The Chieftain threw fire.
Gong didn't move. He absorbed every hit, letting his Elven Chain and his sheer mass hold the line.
"The Mountain does not move," he gritted out, blood leaking from his nose.

His immobility bought them time.
Lin flanked the Chieftain.
Xiao poured her last mana potion into Zhi.
"Burn him!"
A pillar of fire consumed the throne. The Chieftain shrieked and fell.

The hobgoblins broke and fled.
The room fell silent.

Gong stood there for a moment longer, a battered statue covered in soot and purple residue.
Then, his knees hit the stone.
*Thud.*
Mia was there instantly, grabbing his arm. "Gong! Gong, look at me!"

Gong looked at her. Her face was blurry.
"Good dance," he mumbled. "I think... I'll take a short nap."

And then the Mountain fell.
