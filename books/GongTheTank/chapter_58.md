# Chapter 58: The Nap

The world was white. And it smelled of antiseptic herbs.
Gong blinked. His eyelids felt heavy, like rusty hinges.
He tried to sit up, but his muscles protested with a deep, aching throb.

"Don't move," a soft voice commanded.

Gong turned his head.
Mia was sitting in a chair beside his bed. She was still wearing her soot-stained combat robes. Her fan was folded in her lap, her knuckles white as she gripped it.
She looked terrible. Her hair was a mess, and her eyes were red.

About four hours later, Gong stirred.
"Did we win?" he croaked. His throat felt like he had swallowed a cactus.

Mia let out a breath that sounded like a sob.
"Yes, you big idiot. We won."
She reached out and lightly smacked his arm with her fan.
"You caught a Curse of Withering with your face. Who does that?"

"The Mountain," Gong whispered, a faint smile touching his lips. "The Mountain does not move."

"The Mountain almost died," Mia retorted, though there was no heat in her voice. "The Healers had to pour three cleansing potions down your throat just to stop the rot."

Gong considered this.
"Three? That is expensive."

"Uncle paid for it," Mia said. "He said... he said it was a good investment."
She looked down at her hands.
"You saved me, Gong. Again."

Gong managed to shift his arm, patting her hand with his large, calloused fingers.
"That is the job. Protect the Healer. Protect the Scout. Protect the... Ward."

Mia squeezed his hand. She didn't pull away.
For a moment, the room was quiet. The sun was streaming through the window, painting the white sheets in gold.
Gong took a deep breath. His lungs didn't rattle anymore. The coldness in his blood was gone, replaced by a dull, hungry emptiness.

"Mia?"
"Yes, Gong?"
"Is there... chicken?"

Mia stared at him for a second, then burst into laughter. It was a bright, genuine sound that chased the shadows from the room.
"Yes, Gong. I will get you a chicken. I will get you ten chickens."

"Good," Gong sighed, closing his eyes again. "Being a hero makes you hungry."
