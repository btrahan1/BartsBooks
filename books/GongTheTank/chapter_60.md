# Chapter 60: The Announcement

The summons came at dawn.
Gong was still bandaged, munching on a roasted chicken leg Mia had smuggled in, when the messenger arrived.

An hour later, the five of them stood in the Great Hall.
Gong, looking like a linen mummy.
Mia, pristine in her silk robes.
Sister Lin, polishing her rapier.
Brother Zhi, looking nervous.
And Sister Xiao, clutching her abacus like a shield.

Elder Iron-Heart and Elder Mist-Walker sat on the dais. Behind them, the Sect Leader's empty throne cast a long shadow.

"You have survived," Elder Iron-Heart began, his voice echoing off the vaulted ceiling. "And more importantly, you have succeeded where others failed."

He gestured to the group.
"You five will be representing our sect in the Beginners Tournament this year."

Xiao dropped his abacus. *Clack.*
"The... the Grand Tournament?" she squeaked. "Us? But we're... well, look at him." She pointed at Gong.
"He is sturdy," Iron-Heart said dismissively. "And you are the team that cleared Floor Four in record time."

Mist-Walker stepped forward.
"There will be penalties for failure, of course. But the rewards for victory are substantial. The Tournament hosts both individual and team competitions. We expect you to perform well in both."

He looked at Mia, then at Gong.
"To ensure this, your official duties are suspended effective immediately."
"Suspended?" Gong asked, swallowing a mouthful of chicken. "The Rhinos?"
"Someone else will shovel the dung," Iron-Heart assured him. "You have three months. Three months to train. Three months to prepare. Three months to become something more than a 'competent loser'."

Gong considered this.
"And the food?" he asked. "If I am not working, do I still eat?"
"You will be on the Elite Diet," Mist-Walker said. "Unlimited access to the Spirit Kitchens."

Gong's eyes widened behind his bandages.
"Unlimited?"
"Within reason," Iron-Heart clarified quickly.

Gong looked at his team.
Lin was grinning, already imagining the glory.
Zhi was calculating the magical items he could win.
Mia was beaming at him.
"We're going to be famous, Gong!"

Gong nodded slowly.
"We're going to be full," he corrected.
"Same thing," Mia laughed.
