# Chapter 64: Level Baby

The Grand Tournament Arena was a coliseum of polished white stone, capable of seating ten thousand disciples. Today, every seat was full.

"Block A, Match 14!" the magical amplifier boomed. "Gong of the Beast Pens versus Disciple Kael of the Gale Blade!"

Gong stepped into the ring. The sun was bright, the crowd was loud, and the sand under his boots was well-raked.
He wore his full Elven Chain, his tower shield strapped to his back, and his round shield on his arm. He looked like a moving fortification.
Trotting happily beside him was Maggie, wearing her leather boot-helmet and a new, sect-approved sash.

Disciple Kael, a lean young man with two curved swords, frowned. He looked at the judge.
"Objection!" Kael shouted, pointing his sword at Gong. "Two against one? Is this a joke?"

The Judge, a stern Elder with a white beard, floated down on a cloud of Qi. He looked at Gong. He looked at Maggie.
"Verify bond," the Judge commanded.

Gong reached down and placed his hand on Maggie's head. A soft, golden light pulsed between them—the undeniable mark of a Soul Link.
"Bond confirmed," the Judge nodded. He turned to Kael. "Rules Section 4, Paragraph 2: 'Participants in the Individual Bracket may be accompanied by one Bonded Soul Pet, provided said pet is below Rank 3.'"

He inspected Maggie.
"This creature is a Spirit Rhino Calf. Unranked. It is legal."

"It's a tank!" Kael argued. "It's huge!"

"She is a baby," Gong corrected, a rare, mischievous grin splitting his face. "Level 'Baby'."

Kael stared at him. "It's wearing armor."

"She gets cold," Gong lied. He patted Maggie's flank. "Don't worry. She is almost harmless."

"Almost?" Kael sputtered.

"Fight!" the Judge shouted, soaring back into the air.

Kael didn't hesitate. He was a speed fighter. He blurred forward, intending to bypass the slow tank and strike from behind.
He was fast. Very fast.
But he wasn't looking at the ground.

*Thump.*

Maggie, sensing the start of "playtime," took two steps to the left and sat down.
Kael, eyes locked on Gong's neck, tripped over three hundred pounds of "harmless" baby rhino.

He went airborne.
"Oof!"

Gong was waiting. He didn't use his spear. He didn't use his tower shield.
He simply leaned forward and met the flying disciple with the flat of his round shield.

*CLANG.*

It wasn't a hit. It was a collision. Kael bounced off the shield, flew backward ten feet, and landed in a heap in the sand. He groaned, tried to stand, wobbled, and collapsed.

"Winner: Gong!" the Judge announced.

The crowd erupted. Half of them were cheering, half were laughing.
Xiao, watching from the stands (eating popcorn since Healers had no individual bracket), cheered the loudest. "Good girl, Maggie! Good sitting!"

Gong bowed politely to the unconscious Kael.
"See?" he told the Judge. "Harmless. Use gravity."

Maggie huffed and nudged Gong's pouch. He fed her a turnip as they walked out of the ring.
"One down," Gong whispered to her. "Many lunches to go."
