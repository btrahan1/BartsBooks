# Chapter 66: The Recruiters

High above the arena, in the shadowed box reserved for the Sect Elders, tea was being served.

"Messy," Elder Mist-Walker commented, blowing on his cup. "Your fire mage nearly passed out. Your swordswoman took a cut to the face. Even my niece looked... frantic."

Elder Iron-Heart didn't look concerned. He was peeling a mandarin orange with his stone-calloused thumbs.
"They won," he stated.

"They struggled," Mist-Walker countered. "Against second-rate opponents. If they fight like that in the finals, they will be embarrassed. And we will be embarrassed."

"We didn't recruit them as individuals," Iron-Heart said, popping a slice of orange into his mouth. "We recruited them as a team."

He gestured to the empty arena below, now being raked by servants for tomorrow's events.
"Individually, they are flawed. Zhi burns too hot. Lin is too fragile. Mia is too light. And Gong..." He chuckled. "Gong is a rock. A rock is useless if you just walk around it."

"But together?" Mist-Walker asked.

"Together," Iron-Heart smiled, "they cover the cracks. It is good for them to realize they are stronger as a fist than as fingers. Today was a lesson. Tomorrow is the test."

Mist-Walker nodded slowly. "Gong could go far. His durability is becoming unnatural. And Mia... she is adapting to him. She is learning to use his stillness."

"Indeed," Iron-Heart agreed. "Let's see how they fare tomorrow in the team match."

He picked up the schedule scroll.
"Who are their first victims?"

Mist-Walker leaned over to read the calligraphy.
"Team 'Obsidian'," he read. "A defensive formation team from the Southern Peak. Shield users and Earth Mages."

Iron-Heart let out a bark of laughter.
"They are sending a defensive team against Gong? That isn't a fight. That's a siege."

"And Gong," Mist-Walker noted, "does not know how to be besieged. He only knows how to be the wall."

"Then tomorrow," Iron-Heart said, his eyes gleaming, "we will see what happens when an immovable object meets... another immovable object."
