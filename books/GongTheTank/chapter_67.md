# Chapter 67: The Siege Engine

The match began with a rumble.

"Fortify!" the captain of Team Obsidian shouted.
Three Earth Mages slammed their palms into the sand. Walls of stone erupted from the arena floor, fusing together to create a U-shaped bunker. Two shield-bearers locked their tower shields across the opening.
It was perfect. It was impenetrable. It was boring.

Five minutes passed.
Zhi threw a fireball. It blackened the stone but didn't crack it.
Lin poked the wall with her rapier. "It's solid," she confirmed.
Mia fanned herself. "They aren't coming out, are they?"

"They are waiting for us to make a mistake," Zhi grumbled. "Or die of old age."

The crowd was getting restless. "Do something!" someone yelled. "Fight!"

Gong stood at the front, Maggie beside him. He looked at the fortress. It reminded him of the targets Vala had set up. The ones he had to hit again and again.

"They are not moving," Gong observed.
"No," Lin agreed. "They're obstacles."
"Like the logs," Gong said.

He handed his round shield to Mia.
"What are you doing?" she asked, struggling under the weight.
"I need two hands," Gong said.

He gripped his black spear with both hands. He didn't hold it like a duelist. He held it like a battering ram.
He walked back twenty paces. He turned around.
He scraped his boot against the sand.

"Maggie," he said. "Charge."

Maggie let out a squeal of delight. She lowered her head and started running.
Gong started running too.
He wasn't fast like Lin. He wasn't graceful like Mia. He was heavy.
Every step shook the ground. *Thud. Thud. Thud.*
He began to pick up speed. His "Explosive Acceleration" kicked in. He became a runaway cart.

"He's charging the wall!" Zhi gasped. "Gong, stop! You'll break your neck!"

Gong didn't stop. He accelerated.
The Obsidian captain saw him coming through the gap in the shields. "Brace! He's just one man!"

Gong wasn't just one man. He was a projectile.
Ten feet from the wall, he roared.
"DIE WALL!"

He didn't slow down. He didn't brace for impact. He thrust the spear forward, putting every ounce of his momentum, every pound of his density, behind the tip.
He became the boulder. A boulder with a spear.

*CRACK-BOOM.*

The spear tip hit the center of the fusion point between the two tower shields.
The shields didn't just bend; they folded.
The impact transferred through the shield-bearers, through the earth wall, and into the foundation of their bunker.
The stone exploded.

Dust billowed into the air. Rock shards rained down on the arena.
When the dust cleared, the fortress was gone.
In its place was a pile of rubble and five very dazed members of Team Obsidian.
Gong stood in the center of the wreckage, his spear buried halfway into the ground. Maggie was beside him, happily chewing on a piece of shattered rock.

"Knock knock," Gong said politely.

The crowd didn't cheer immediately. They were too busy trying to figure out if the arena was still structurally sound.
Then, the roar began.

Up in the Elder's box, Iron-Heart was laughing so hard he spilled his tea.
"I told you!" he crowed. "A siege engine!"
