# Chapter 68: The Council

That evening, the team didn't celebrate alone.
They were summoned to a private dining room in the Spirit Kitchens. Waiting for them were three very different masters.

Gaul, the Commander of the Silver Bastion, sat at the head of the table, his silver armor polished to a mirror shine.
Elina, the dance instructor, lounged on a divan, sipping wine with elegant boredom.
Vala, the Amazon, was currently arm-wrestling Brother Cai (and winning) at the far end of the table.

"Sit," Gaul commanded, pointing to the empty chairs. "Eat."

Gong didn't need to be told twice. He sat. He reached for a roast duck.

"We watched the match," Gaul started, his voice grave. "The 'Siege Engine' maneuver."

"It was effective," Gong said through a mouthful of duck.

"It was reckless," Gaul corrected. "When you charged, you left your healer exposed for three seconds. A wind-element team would have bypassed you and taken her out."
He paused, then cracked a rare smile. "But the impact... that was good. You kept your shield angle perfect."

"He broke the turtle shell," Vala crowed, slamming Cai's hand onto the table with a wood-splintering *thud*. "Finally! I told you he had potential! Did you see the penetration? He didn't just push them; he went *through* them!"

She walked over and slapped Gong on the back hard enough to dislodge a bone.
"Next time," Vala advised, "don't aim for the connection point. Aim for the man holding it. He is softer than the shield."

"Vala," Elina chided gently. "We are trying to win a tournament, not create widows."
The dancer turned her gaze to Mia.
"You moved well, little butterfly. Your transitions were smooth. You used Gong's chaos to hide your own intent."

"Thank you, Master Elina," Mia bowed.

"And Gong..." Elina sighed, looking at the tank who was now dismantling a rack of ribs. "You... did not trip."

"Thank you," Gong said sincerely.

"It is an improvement," Elina admitted. "You move like a boulder rolling downhill. It has a certain... gravitational majesty. Ugly. But majestic."

"They are ready for the semi-finals," Gaul decided. "Tomorrow, you face Team 'Viper'. Poison and stealth."

"I hate poison," Zhi groaned.

"Don't get hit," Gaul said simply. "Gong, you will be the bait. They will try to kite you. Do not chase them."
"I don't chase," Gong promised. "I sit."

"Good," Gaul nodded. He raised his goblet.
"To the Wall and the Wind. And... whatever the Rhino is."

"The Terrain," Maggie squeaked from under the table, where she was eating the scraps Vala dropped.

"To the Terrain," the mentors agreed.
