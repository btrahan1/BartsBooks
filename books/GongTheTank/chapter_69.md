# Chapter 69: Resilience

The semi-finals began with a hiss.

As soon as the gong sounded, Team Viper vanished.
Five smoke bombs exploded simultaneously, filling the arena with a choking, green-tinted fog.
"Poison mist!" Zhi warned, coughing. "Hold your breath!"

From the fog, darts flew.
*Thwip. Thwip. Thwip.*
They pinged off Gong’s armor. They deflected off Mia’s fan. But one found a gap in Lin’s sleeve, grazing her arm.
"Numbing poison," Lin hissed, her arm twitching.

"They aren't engaging," Mia noted, her eyes scanning the swirling smoke. "They're kiting. They want us to chase them into the fog where they have traps."

Gong looked at the green cloud. He couldn't see anything. He couldn't hear anything except the soft footsteps of assassins repositioning.
"Gaul said don't chase," Gong remembered.

"So what do we do?" Zhi asked, creating a wind barrier to blow the smoke away from Xiao. "If we stand here, they'll turn us into pincushions."

"We wait," Gong said.

And then, in the middle of the most important match of his life, Gong sat down.
He unstrapped his tower shield and planted it in the sand like a monolith. He sat behind it.
"Everyone behind the Wall," he ordered.

Confused, but trusting him, the team huddled behind Gong and his shield. Maggie, delighted by this development, flopped down next to him.

The arena went silent.
The smoke swirled. The Vipers waited for their opponents to run around in panic.
They didn't run.
They sat.
Gong even pulled out a turnip and offered a bite to Maggie. *Crunch.*

The crowd began to murmur. Then, someone booed.
"Fight!" a spectator yelled. "Cowards!"
"Vipers! Go get them!"

The Captain of Team Viper, hiding in the smoke, gritted his teeth. His poison relied on exertion to circulate. If the enemy was sitting still, the toxin in Lin's arm would take hours to work. And the match had a time limit.
If time ran out, the judges would decide based on "Aggression and Control."
Sitting in a plyometric pile was not aggressive. But refusing to engage was worse.

"Fine," the Viper Captain whispered. "Flush them out."

The Vipers moved. They emerged from the smoke, abandoning their stealth for speed.
"Circle them! Strike from all sides!"

They acted like a pack of wolves, darting in to slash and retreat.
But Gong didn't have to chase them. They came to him.
As a Viper lunged at Xiao, Maggie’s horn flared amber. The assassin hit the barrier and bounced off, stunned.
*WHAM.*
Gong’s shield slammed down on him before he could recover.

Another Viper tried to jump over the shield wall.
Mia was waiting. She plucked him out of the air with her sash and slammed him into the ground.

It wasn't a battle. It was a meat grinder. The Vipers threw themselves against the rock, and the rock broke them.
Within five minutes, four Vipers were unconscious. The Captain stood alone, panting, his daggers hanging loosely at his sides.

Gong stood up. He dusted sand off his breeches.
"Are we done?" he asked politely.

The Captain looked at the mountain of metal, the deadly dancer, the angry fire mage, and the rhino who was currently eating an unexploded smoke bomb like it was a spicy treat.
He dropped his daggers.
"Yield."

"Winner: Gong!"

As the crowd cheered (mostly for Maggie burping a cloud of green smoke), Gong patted his stomach.
"Resilience," he told his team, "is mostly about being comfortable while you wait."
