# Chapter 73: The Flag

The arena had changed.
Gone was the flat sand pit. In its place, the Elders had activated the "Grand Illusion Array."
A dense forest had sprouted on the left. A rocky ruin dominated the right. And in the center, a river flowed.
It was a battlefield.

"Welcome," the Sect Leader's voice boomed from the sky, "to the Grand Finals."
Two pillars of light erupted at opposite ends of the field.
One Red. One Blue.
Floating inside each pillar was a flag.

"The rules are simple," the Sect Leader continued. "Capture the enemy flag. Bring it to your base. First to one capture wins."

Gong stared at the map.
"This is very big," he noted. "And there are trees."

"It's Capture the Flag," Mia said, her eyes lighting up. "This favors speed. This favors stealth. This is perfect for us."
She looked at the Golden Dragon team on the other side. They looked less happy. They were built for a duel, not a scavenger hunt.

"Strategy meeting," Gaul whispered, appearing behind them. (Mentors were allowed on the field for the prep phase).
"Gong," Gaul pointed to the Red Flag. "You are the anchor."

"I sit on the flag," Gong confirmed.

"Yes. You, Maggie, and Xiao stay here. Make a fortress. If Prince Jin comes, you stall him. You force a draw."
Gaul turned to the others.
"Mia, Lin, Zhi. You are the hunting party. You use the forest. You avoid their main force. You steal that flag and you run."

"What if they defend?" Lin asked.

"They won't," Vala interrupted, sharpening her dagger. "They are Golden Dragons. They are arrogant. They will attack with five. They will try to crush Gong and take the flag by force."

"So it's a race," Zhi said, nervously spinning his wand. "Can Gong hold off five of them?"

"I held off the Prince for an hour," Gong reminded him. "And Maggie is full of smoke bombs."

Maggie burped a small green cloud as if to confirm her readiness.

"Good," Gaul nodded. "The key is the switch. Once Mia has the flag, they will panic. They will turn back. That is when Gong..."

"No," Gong interrupted.
The team looked at him. Gong rarely interrupted strategy.
"If Mia takes the flag," Gong said, "They will catch her. Their Lancer is fast. Their Bard has speed buffs."

"So?" Mia asked.

"So," Gong pointed to himself. "I carry the flag."

"You?" Lin laughed. "Gong, no offense, but you're... efficient. Not fast."

"I am heavy," Gong corrected. "If I have the flag, who can take it from me?"

There was a silence.
They imagined the scenario. Gong, holding the flag, walking back to base.
Prince Jin hacking at him. The Paladin bashing him. The Lancer stabbing him.
And Gong just... walking.

"The Juggernaut," Vala whispered, a wicked grin spreading across her face. "He doesn't need to run. He just needs to not stop."

"It's risky," Gaul warned. "If they swarm you in the open field..."

"Then I need a distraction," Gong said. He pointed at the forest. "Make them look at you. Then I take the walk."

"The Walk," Mia tested the word. She liked it. "Operation: The Long Walk."

The horn sounded. The preparation phase was over.
"Ready?" Gong asked, tightening his shield straps.
"Ready," the team chorused.
"For the meat," Gong added.
"For the meat!"
