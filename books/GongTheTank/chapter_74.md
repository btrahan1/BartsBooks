# Chapter 74: The Switch

Prince Jin surveyed the battlefield from the high ground of the Ruins.
"Report," he commanded.

"Scouts are in position," his Lancer replied. "Gong is at their base. He has set up a perimeter. Mia and Lin have entered the forest."

"Typical," Jin scoffed. "He plays the turtle. He expects us to break our teeth on his shell while his thieves steal our flag."
Jin drew his sword.
"We will not give him the satisfaction. Lancer, Paladin, take the forest. Intercept the thieves. Bard, stay here and guard the flag. I will go to their base and keep the Turtle occupied."

"Understood!"

The Golden Dragon team split perfectly. Fast, efficient, disciplined.
Jin sprinted toward the enemy base, ready to engage Gong in another hour-long stalemate.
He reached the Red Base.
He saw the flag fluttering in the wind.
He saw Sister Xiao calmly drinking tea.
He saw Zhi, the fire mage, nervously guarding the perimeter.

He did not see the Rhino.
He did not see the Wall.

"Where is he?" Jin demanded, stopping his charge.
Xiao looked up. "Oh, hello Prince. Gong? He went for a walk."

***

MEANWHILE, IN THE CENTER LANE.

*THUD. THUD. THUD.*

The ground shook. Small birds flew out of the trees.
Gong was not sneaking. He was not using cover. He was running down the main road, his shields locked in front of him, Maggie galloping beside him.
He was a freight train made of iron and leather.

"Enemy spotted!" The Golden Dragon Bard screamed from his perch on the Blue Base wall. "It's... It's the Tank!"
"He's defending!" Jin's voice crackled over the communication crystal.
"No, he's attacking! He's attacking with the Rhino!"

Gong saw the Blue Base ahead.
"Maggie," he grunted. "Siege mode."

Maggie squealed and lowered her head. Her horn began to glow with amber light.
Gong lowered his shoulder.

"Stop him!" the Bard yelled, casting a *Song of Slumber*.
Musical notes floated toward Gong.
"Earplugs," Gong muttered, activating the wax plugs he had inserted before the match started. He couldn't hear the song. He couldn't hear the Bard screaming.
He only heard his own heartbeat. And the promise of the meat waiting at the victory banquet.

*CRASH.*

Gong and Maggie hit the front gate of the Blue Base.
The gate was reinforced wood. It didn't stand a chance.
It exploded inward.

The Bard fell off the wall. The Light Mage, who had been hiding in the back, shrieked.
Gong walked through the splintered remains of the gate.
He walked up to the Blue Flag.
He grabbed it.
"Mine," he said.

"PRINCE JIN!" the Bard screamed into his crystal. "BASE BREACHED! GONG HAS THE FLAG!"

In the Red Base, Jin froze.
"He switched?" the Prince whispered, horrified. "He left his base undefended to trade flags?"

"I'm undefended?" Xiao asked politely. "I have Zhi."
Zhi waved weakly. "Hi."

Jin looked at the Red Flag. He could take it. He could kill Zhi easily.
But if he took the flag, he would have to carry it back.
And Gong—the Juggernaut—was already halfway back with *his* flag.
Gong was slow. But he was unstoppable. If they crossed paths, Jin couldn't take the flag from him.
But if Jin let Gong reach the Red Base... it was game over.

"recall!" Jin shouted. "Everyone back to base! Stop the Tank!"

"Operation Long Walk," Gong muttered to himself, strapping the Blue Flag to his back. "Is a go."
