# Chapter 75: The Unstoppable Carrier

Gong was walking.
He wasn't running—he couldn't run with the extra weight of the flag and his armor. He was setting a pace. A heavy, rhythmic pace that shook the dust off the road.
*Step. Step. Step.*

"Incoming!" Zhi screamed from the treeline.

The Golden Dragon Lancer burst from the forest, his horse (a Spirit Steed) foaming at the mouth. He aimed his lance straight at Gong's back.
"Drop it!" the Lancer roared.

"No," Lin whispered.
She stepped out from behind a rock. She didn't have a weapon—her rapier was broken. She had a dagger.
She threw herself at the Lancer's horse. Not to kill it, but to spook it.
The horse reared. The lance went wide, missing Gong by inches.
The Lancer recovered instantly, swinging the shaft of his weapon to knock Lin unconscious.
"One down," the Lancer growled, turning back to Gong.

But Gong hadn't stopped walking. He was ten paces further away.

"Stop him!" Prince Jin ordered, dropping from the sky with a *Golden Descent*.
He landed in front of Gong.
"You are slow, Wall. You cannot outrun us."

"I don't need to outrun you," Gong grunted, shifting the flag. "I just need to outlast you."

Jin raised his sword. "Then I will cut you down."

"Dance with me first!" Mia blurred into existence between them. Her fan was open, her eyes fierce.
"Mia, don't," Gong warned. "He is too strong."
"Keep walking, Big Guy," Mia smiled. "I'll buy you ten seconds."

She engaged the Prince. It wasn't a fight; it was a suicide mission. She burned her mana, her stamina, everything she had to deflect Jin's strikes.
One second. Two seconds.
Jin snarled and unleashed a localized shockwave. Mia was blown into the bushes, unconscious before she hit the ground.
Five seconds.

Gong kept walking.

The Golden Dragon Paladin appeared, blocking the road. He was big. Almost as big as Gong.
"None shall pass!" the Paladin shouted, slamming his greatshield into the dirt.

Gong didn't slow down.
"Maggie. Terrain adjustment."

Maggie, who had been trotting faithfully beside Gong's leg, squealed. She accelerated. She was a Rhinoceros. She was a living battering ram.
The Paladin braced himself against Gong. He expected the man.
He didn't expect the beast.
*CRUNCH.*
Maggie hit the Paladin's shield with the force of a falling anvil. The Paladin went flying. Maggie bounced off, shook her head dizzily, and sat down hard. "Rest," she huffed.

"Good girl," Gong whispered. "Stay."

He was alone now.
Lin was down. Mia was down. Maggie was resting. Zhi was back at the base, probably hyperventilating.
Gong looked at the red gate in the distance. It was two hundred yards away.
He looked behind him.
Prince Jin was coming. The Lancer was coming.
They were angry. They were fast.

Gong took a deep breath. He adjusted his grip on the flag.
He didn't run.
He shuffled.
*Step. Step. Step.*
"Come and get it," he mumbled.
