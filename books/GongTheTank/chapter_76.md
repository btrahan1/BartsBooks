# Chapter 76: The Technicality

Gong was fifty yards from glory.
He was bruised. His armor was dented. He had an arrow sticking out of his left pauldron.
But he was still walking.

Behind him, Prince Jin was exhausted. He had struck the Wall a hundred times. He had tried to trip him. He had tried to blind him.
Nothing worked. Gong just kept shuffling.
*Thud. Thud. Thud.*

"He's going to make it," the Golden Dragon Lancer gasped, clutching his ribs where Lin had tackled him.

Gong saw the Red Base. He saw Zhi waving frantically.
"Hurry, Gong! Hurry!"

"I am hurrying," Gong grunted. This was his top speed. The flag on his back felt like it weighed a thousand pounds.
Thirty yards.
Twenty yards.

Then, a horn sounded. Not the end-of-match horn. The "Flag Taken" horn.
Gong looked up.
On the giant magical projection screen above the arena, he saw a blur of motion.
It was the Golden Dragon's fifth member—the Rogue. While Jin and the others had been keeping Gong busy, the Rogue had slipped past Zhi (who was too busy watching Gong) and snatched the Red Flag.

"It's a race!" the announcer screamed.

The Rogue was sprinting down the forest path. He was fast. He was unencumbered.
Gong was walking down the main road. He was heavy. He was tired.

Ten yards.
Gong took a step.
The Rogue vaulted over a log.

Five yards.
Gong took another step.
The Rogue sprinted past the halfway mark.

"Move!" Zhi screamed, trying to pull Gong forward with a wind spell.

Gong roared. He activated his "Explosive Acceleration" one last time. He launched himself forward, a cannonball of desperation.
He hit the base plate.
*CRASH.*

But the horn had already sounded.
*HOooooooNK.*

Gong lay face down on the capture plate, the blue flag underneath him.
Silence filled the arena.
The magical scoreboard flickered.

**Winner: Team Golden Dragon**
**Time Difference: 3 Seconds**

Gong lifted his head. He spat out some dirt.
He looked at the screen. The Rogue was celebrating in the Blue Base, holding the Red Flag.

"I lost," Gong whispered.

Prince Jin walked up to him. The Prince was limping. He looked like he had been in a war.
He looked at Gong, who was already standing up, dusting himself off.
Gong had defeated the Paladin. He had defeated the Lancer. He had outlasted the Prince. He had broken the gate. He had carried the flag.
He had done everything except be fast.

"You lost the match," Jin said quietly, extending a hand to help Gong up. "Because of a technicality."

Gong took the hand.
"A loss is a loss," Gong said simply. He looked at the Blue Flag in his hand. "At least I got a souvenir."

"You... you can't keep the flag," Jin said, feeling a migraine coming on.

"I captured it," Gong argued. "It is mine."

As the crowd erupted in a mix of cheers for the Dragons and chants of "WALL! WALL! WALL!" for Gong, the Elders looked down.

"He lost," Iron-Heart observed. "But he proved the concept."

"Indeed," Mist-Walker agreed. "He proved that if you want to beat him... you have to run away."
