# Chapter 77: The After Action Review

The cheering crowds were gone. The magical screens were dark.
Gong and his team sat in the quiet, candlelit office of the Elder Hall. They were cleaned up, healed by Sister Xiao, and wearing fresh robes.
But the silver runner-up medals around their necks felt heavy.

Elder Iron-Heart sat behind his desk. Elder Mist-Walker stood by the window.
"You lost," Iron-Heart said flatly.

"By three seconds," Zhi argued, his voice raspy from shouting.

"A loss is a loss," Iron-Heart countered. " In a real war, three seconds is the difference between reinforcements arriving and a massacre. You were too slow."

Gong nodded. "I am slow."

"Yes," Iron-Heart agreed. "You are."
He leaned forward.
"But you are also the reason the Golden Dragon team—the most talented generation of disciples we have seen in twenty years—spent the entire match terrified."

Mist-Walker turned from the window.
"Do you understand why you lost, Disciple Gong?"

"Because I couldn't run fast enough," Gong said.

"No," Mist-Walker corrected. "You lost because you tried to be a hero. You tried to be the Wall *and* the Carrier. You cannot be in two places at once."
The Elder paced the room.
"A hero tries to do everything. A Tank... a Tank does one thing so well that the enemy has no choice but to respect it."

"Prince Jin respected you," Iron-Heart added. "He respected you so much that he refused to fight you. He abandoned his honor, abandoned his duel, and ran past you to kill your support. That is the ultimate compliment for a defender."

Lin looked at her hands. "But we still lost."

"You lost the game," Iron-Heart grinned. "But you won the argument."
He pulled a scroll from his desk.
"The Sect Leader was impressed. He agrees with our assessment. The 'Wall and Wind' strategy is valid. The 'Siege Engine' is valid. And the Rhino..."
He looked at Maggie, who was currently asleep under Gong's chair.
"...is a valid piece of siege equipment."

"So?" Mia asked, hopeful.

"So," Iron-Heart tossed the scroll to Gong. "You are granted full access to the Inner Sect resources. Better pills. Better smiths. And, of course, the Level 2 Spirit Kitchens."

Gong's eyes widened. "Level 2?"

"High-grade Spirit Meat," Iron-Heart confirmed. "Braised Stalker-Cat. Fried Thunder-Bird. Steamed Dragon-Carp."

Gong stood up. He bowed deeply. Deeper than he had bowed to the Prince.
"Thank you, Elder. We will train harder. So we can eat more."

Iron-Heart laughed. "Go. The victory banquet is starting. Try not to bankrupt us."

As the team filed out, smiling and chatting about the menu, Mist-Walker looked at Iron-Heart.
"Do you think they are ready?"

"For the tournament? Yes," Iron-Heart said. "But the real war is coming. And for that... we're going to need a bigger shield."
