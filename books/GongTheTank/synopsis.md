In the village of Little Pond, known for its tough turnips and equally stubborn people, young Gong is an anomaly. He doesn't run or climb like other children; he moves with the inevitable force of a rolling boulder. When the village's meanest goat, "Old Iron", tries to bully him off a bridge, Gong proves exactly how dense and unstoppable he is. Discover the origin of a legendary "Tank" who simply refuses to be moved!
