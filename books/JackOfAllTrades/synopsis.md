Jack lives by a simple rule: good enough is, more often than not, perfect. He is the ultimate generalist in a world of specialists. Using the 80/20 rule, Jack can learn the basics of anything quickly—from fixing an espresso machine to changing brake pads or tuning a piano. When he's not helping his neighbors in Maplewood, he's navigating life with a calm, analytical mind. But when a local factory faces a crisis that the experts can't solve, Jack's unique synthesis of skills might be the only thing that can save the town's economy. A story of versatility, practicality, and the power of being "pretty good" at everything.
