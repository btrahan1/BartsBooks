# Synopsis - Kael's Law

Kael is a simple farm boy forced into military service by the "King's Law". However, he possess an incredible, innate talent that defies the understanding of veterans. He can master any weapon in minutes, heal wounds with a touch, and manipulate energy in ways that violate the laws of nature.

Discovered by a grizzled Staff Sergeant and a skilled Healer, and later studied by an obsessive Archmage, Kael proves to be a force of nature rather than a mere recruit. Despite his world-altering power, he remains innocent and unassuming, ultimately ending the Great War without spilling a drop of blood before asking for the only reward he truly wants: to go home to his farm.
