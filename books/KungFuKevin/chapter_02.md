# Chapter 2: The Dao of the Disco Beat

The arrogant youth, whose name Kevin had already decided was probably "Chad" or "Brad" in the subtitles of his mind, narrowed his eyes. The blue glow of his sword intensified, humming with a frequency that made the teeth of everyone in the front row ache.

Everyone except Kevin. To Kevin, it just looked like a really high-quality LED prop.

"You mock the Azure Sword Style?" the youth hissed. "I, Chen Wu, shall sever your meridian pathways!"

"Boring monologue!" Kevin yelled, interrupting him. "Action!"

In Kevin's head, the needle dropped. It was instantaneous. The mental cassette tape clicked, whirred, and the opening synth stabs of Carl Douglas's 1974 masterpiece exploded in his brain.

Oh-hoh-hoh-HOAH!

Kevin began to bob his head. To the gathered cultivators, this rhythmic nodding looked like a terrifying trance state.

*He is attuning his spirit to the heartbeat of the Heavens!* Elder Fan
thought, his beard twitching. *He is entering the State of the Empty Cup!*

Chen Wu roared and lunged. He moved faster than a cheetah, his sword trailing a ribbon of blue light aimed directly at Kevin's throat. It was a killing blow. A strike that had decapitated demon beasts.

But Kevin was grooving.

Everybody was Kung Fu Fighting!

Just as the sword tip arrived, Kevin did a jagged, funky side-step to the beat of the song in his head. *Dun-dun-dun-dun.* He threw his hands up in a "Jazz Hands" motion, inadvertently slapping the flat of the glowing blade.

CLANG!

The sound rang out like a temple bell. Chen's sword was knocked wide, the vibration traveling up his arm and numbing his shoulder. Chen stumbled, his eyes wide. "Impossible! You deflected my Qi with a bare-handed palm strike?"

"Those cats were fast as lightning!" Kevin sang out loud, spinning in a circle. He ended the spin with a double finger-gun point at Chen. "Pow! Pow!"

Invisible bullets of pure "Intent" fired from Kevin's fingers. They weren't real Qi. They didn't exist in the physical realm. But because Kevin believed he was shooting imaginary bullets, the Universe flinched.

Two puffs of dust exploded at Chen's feet as if struck by sniper fire.

Chen yelped and danced backward. "Invisible Qi Darts?! He has mastered formless energy!"

"Come on, Brad!" Kevin shouted, shuffling his feet in a movement that was 50% Ali Shuffle and 50% Riverdance. "Is that all you got? My grandmother kicks higher than that, and she has a hip replacement!"

Chen's face went purple. "Die, heretic!"

Chen leaped into the air, floating ten feet off the ground. He slashed his sword downward, unleashing a crescent wave of blue fire that tore up the marble floor tiles as it raced toward Kevin.

The crowd screamed. This was the *Azure Dragon Descends*, a technique meant to split boulders.

Kevin saw the pyrotechnics coming. *Whoa, big budget effects,* he thought. *Gotta sell the dodge.*

He waited until the last second, the absolute last fraction of a second,and dropped into the splits.

It wasn't a graceful split. It was the kind of split that tears jeans and pulls groin muscles. Kevin grimaced, his eyes watering, but he was low enough. The arc of blue fire sailed over his head, singing the tips of his spiky hair.

"In fact, it was a little bit fright'ning!" Kevin wheezed, straining to stand back up.

The fireball hit a jade statue behind him, exploding it into dust.

Kevin used the momentum of his struggle to stand up to launch himself forward. He was awkward, he was clumsy, but he was moving with the inexorable logic of a protagonist.

Chen landed, exhausted from the massive attack. He looked up to see Kevin rushing him.

"Hand of the Five Exploding Hearts!" Kevin screamed nonsensically.

He reached out. He wasn't aiming for a pressure point. He was aiming for a move he'd seen in a slapstick comedy.

Kevin's open palm slapped Chen on the forehead. At the same time, Kevin stomped on Chen's foot.

SMACK!

It was the "Stooge Style: Bonk and Stomp."

To the world of Cultivation, however, something else happened. The slap on the forehead disrupted the flow of Spirit energy in Chen's upper Dantian. The stomp on the foot grounded his Earth Meridian.

Chen's eyes rolled back in his head. His Qi rebelled, spinning backward. He stiffened, dropped his glowing sword, and fell backward onto the marble like a chopped tree.

Thud.

The music in Kevin's head faded out with a final kung fu flute trill.

Kevin stood over his fallen opponent, breathing hard. He wiped his nose with his thumb again. "Keep the change, ya filthy animal."

Silence descended on the Azure Cloud Sect.

A pin dropping would have sounded like a cannon blast. Thousands of disciples stared, mouths agape. They had just watched a stranger in yellow pajamas defeat the Inner Sect's top genius without drawing a weapon, using movement techniques that defied all logic, and finishing him with a technique so simple it was insulting.

Elder Fan, the floating old man, drifted down from his dais. He landed softly in front of Kevin. His eyes were wide with a mixture of fear and awe.

"Young... Young Master," Elder Fan stammered, bowing low. "That final technique... to sever the connection between Heaven and Earth with a single touch... surely that was the Lost Art of the Thunder Palm?"

Kevin looked at the old man. "Thunder Palm? Nah, old timer. That was just a little love tap."

Kevin looked around at the stunned crowd, then up at the floating mountains. He put his hands on his hips.

"So," Kevin said, flashing a million-dollar smile. "Did I get the part? Or do I need to audition for the bad guy too?"
