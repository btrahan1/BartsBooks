# Chapter 3: The Goddess and the Saxophone Solo

The dust from Chen Wu's unconscious body had barely settled when the temperature on the mountain peak plummeted.

Frost began to creep across the marble tiles. The disciples in the stands shivered, their breath turning to steam in the air. Elder Fan stopped bowing and looked toward the sky, his face pale.

"She comes," he whispered reverently.

Kevin looked up, shielding his eyes with a flat hand. "Who comes? The weather girl? It's getting chilly."

A streak of azure light tore through the clouds. It circled the arena once, leaving a trail of sparkling ice crystals, before diving toward the platform. The light coalesced, slowing down until it gently touched the ground.

The light faded to reveal a woman standing on a floating sword.

She was devastating. She wore robes of white silk embroidered with blue frost patterns that seemed to move on their own. Her skin was like jade, flawless and pale. Her hair was a waterfall of ink-black silk that reached her waist, defying gravity to float gently around her. Her eyes were cold, sharp, and beautiful enough to make a man forget his own zip code.

Kevin's jaw unhinged. It didn't just drop; it filed for divorce from his skull.

In his head, the kung fu fighting music screeched to a halt. It was replaced instantly by a sultry, echo-heavy saxophone solo.

Careless Whisper.

Kevin's heart hammered a frantic rhythm against his ribs. *Ba-dump. Ba-dump.*

"Whoa," Kevin breathed, his voice cracking. "Hello, high-definition heroine."

The woman stepped off her sword, which shrank and vanished into her sleeve. She ignored the unconscious Chen Wu. She ignored the groveling Elder Fan. Her gaze locked onto Kevin like a missile lock.

She walked toward him. Every step was graceful, as if she were walking on water.

Kevin panicked. *Okay, Kevin. Be cool. This is the romantic subplot. You've practiced for this in the mirror. Don't mess it up. Give her the* '*Smolder*'.

Kevin leaned back, putting his weight on one hip. He ran a hand through his hair, licked his lips, and narrowed his eyes. He thought he looked like a rugged movie star.

To the crowd, he looked like he was having a mild stroke.

The woman stopped three feet away from him. She smelled like winter rain and expensive flowers.

"I am Lin Xue," she said. Her voice was melodic, but cold as a glacier. "Core Disciple of the Azure Cloud Sect. You defeated Junior Brother Chen?"

Kevin's brain short-circuited. *Lin Xue. Even her name sounds like wind chimes.*

"Uh, yeah," Kevin managed, deepening his voice to what he thought was a sexy baritone. "Chen? Yeah, he tripped. Had some balance issues. I just... helped him down."

He winked. He actually winked.

Lin Xue did not blink. Her eyes scanned him, searching for his Qi signature. She found nothing. Absolutely nothing. It was like staring into a void.

*He has no cultivation,* she thought, her mind racing. *No spiritual
pressure. No leaking aura. He is completely perfectly contained. He has reached the realm of* '*Return to Origin.*' *He looks like a mortal, acts like a fool, yet defeated a Foundation Establishment genius with a slap. He must be... a monster.*

"You are a master of body refinement?" she asked, stepping closer.

Kevin leaned forward, invading her personal space with the confidence of a man who owns a Blockbuster Video membership card. "Body refinement? Baby, I work out. A little cardio, some crunches. You know, gotta keep the temple tight."

He flexed his bicep. It wasn't impressive.

Lin Xue stared at the small muscle. *He condenses his power into such a small form? The density of his muscle fibers must be diamond-hard.*

"Your presence disturbs the Dao," Lin Xue said. "I must test you."

"Test away," Kevin purred. "I'm an open book. A pop-up book. Fun for all ages."

Lin Xue's eyes flashed. She raised her hand, palm open, and unleashed her Spirit Pressure.

Usually, this technique would force a normal man to his knees, crushing him under the weight of her spiritual will. The air around them grew heavy, vibrating with power. The disciples in the front row fainted. Elder Fan clutched his chest.

Kevin felt... a little heavy?

*Whoa,* Kevin thought. *Is this... chemistry? The air is getting thick.
My knees feel weak. Oh my god, she's into me. The tension is palpable!*

He didn't kneel. He didn't buckle. He just smiled goofily, staring into her eyes, completely immune to the psychic crushing force because he just thought it was love.

Lin Xue's eyes widened. *He doesn't even flinch. My pressure washes over him like a breeze against a mountain.*

She retracted her aura and stepped forward, grabbing Kevin's wrist. Her fingers were cool and soft.

"Ooh," Kevin squeaked. "Moving fast. I usually like to buy dinner first, maybe some dim sum, but... I'm adaptable."

Lin Xue sent a pulse of Qi into his wrist to probe his meridians. She expected to find vast rivers of energy.

She found... nothing. No meridians. No core. Just blood, bone, and a very high level of caffeine.

She dropped his hand as if it burned her. She stepped back, looking at him with a mixture of confusion and horror.

"You..." she stammered. "You are Empty."

Kevin checked his pockets. "I mean, I left my wallet in my other pants, so yeah, technically I'm broke right now. But I'm rich in personality!"

Lin Xue turned to Elder Fan. "Elder. This man... he is an anomaly. He possesses no cultivation, yet the laws of the world seem to... bend around him."

"Bend?" Kevin interrupted, doing a quick hip swivel. "I'm flexible, darling. Very flexible."

Lin Xue looked at Kevin again. He was currently doing finger-guns at a terrified disciple in the stands.

*He hides his power so deeply that he simulates the ignorance of a
complete moron,* Lin Xue concluded. *This is the legendary* '*Mask of the Holy Fool.*' *If I stay near him, perhaps I can comprehend his secrets.*

She turned back to him, her face regaining its icy composure.

"You will come with me," she commanded. "The Sect Leader will want to see the man who humiliated his son."

Kevin's heart soared. "A date? Already? Should I change? These yellow pants are a bit 'combat casual'."

"Silence," Lin Xue said, stepping back onto her sword. "Follow."

She shot into the air.

Kevin stood on the ground, looking up. "Uh... wait! I can't fly! I haven't unlocked that DLC yet!"

Lin Xue paused, hovering twenty feet up. She looked down, sighing. With a wave of her sleeve, a ribbon of white silk shot down, wrapping around Kevin's waist.

"Whoa! Bondage style!" Kevin yelled as he was yanked into the air.

As he dangled upside down, flying toward the highest peak of the Azure Cloud Sect, Kevin looked at the beautiful, deadly woman towing him through the sky.

"Best. Day. Ever," he whispered.
