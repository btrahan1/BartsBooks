# Chapter 4: The Glitch in the Matrix

Being towed through the sky by a magical silk ribbon while hanging upside down was, Kevin discovered, not as romantic as *Aladdin* made it look.

For one, the blood was rushing to his head, making his face feel like an overripe tomato. For another, the wind at this altitude was freezing, cutting through his thin yellow track pants with disrespectful ease.

"Hey! Senior Sister Lin! Babe!" Kevin yelled against the wind, his voice wobbling. "Can we maybe pull over? I think I'm getting motion sickness! The craft services catering isn't sitting well!"

Lin Xue, standing imperiously on her flying sword ten feet above him, did not look down. She was busy meditating on the profound mystery of why the universe had burdened her with this idiot.

Kevin squinted. He reached out a hand and swiped at a passing cloud. It felt wet. Cold. He looked down at the ground, a dizzying mosaic of green forests and jagged rocks five thousand feet below.

A seed of doubt began to sprout in Kevin's mind.

*Wait a second,* he thought. *If this were a movie set, there would be
wires. I don't see any wires. And if this were a VR headset, I'd feel the weight of the goggles. But I feel... wind burn.*

He pinched his own arm. Hard.

"Ow!"

Okay. Pain is real. The cold is real. The nausea is very real.

Panic began to rise. "Okay, Kevin. Don't freak out. Maybe you're in a coma. Maybe you're dead. Maybe, "

Suddenly, a sound like a dial-up modem shrieked inside his skull.

SCREEE-ONK-BZZZT!

"Gah!" Kevin clutched his head, swinging wildly on the ribbon.

\[SYSTEM REBOOTING...\]

A bright blue, translucent rectangle popped into existence right in front of Kevin's eyes. It flickered and distorted, static running through the text.

**\[ERROR: SOUL WAVELENGTH UNKNOWN.\]** **\[DETECTING ORIGIN: EARTH
(Dimension 404-Null)\]** **\[ATTEMPTING TO SYNCHRONIZE WITH WORLD LAWS...\]**

Kevin blinked. The text remained, floating in his vision no matter where he looked.

"A HUD?" Kevin gasped, forgetting the nausea. "A Heads-Up Display? Oh, I get it now! This isn't a movie... it's a GAME! I'm in a super-advanced, fully immersive MMORPG!"

His confidence came rushing back like a tidal wave. If this was a game, he knew exactly what to do. He just needed to grind XP and find the loot.

**\[SYNCHRONIZATION COMPLETE.\]** **\[ANALYZING USER ATTRIBUTES...\]**

The blue box expanded, showing a silhouette of Kevin (complete with the bad posture and the headband).

**\[NAME: KEVIN\]** **\[CULTIVATION: NONE (Mortal Trash)\]**
**\[TITLES:** "**Basement Dweller**"**,** "**VHS Collector**"**,**
"**Delusional**"**\]**

"Hey!" Kevin shouted at the box. "That's rude! I prefer 'Cinephile'!"

The box flickered, the text deleting and rewriting itself as if struggling to translate Kevin's concepts into this world's reality.

**\[RE-CALCULATING BASED ON USER PSYCHE...\]** **\[LOADING...**
"**ACTION MOVIE LOGIC**" **PATCH v1.0\]**

\[NEW ATTRIBUTES GENERATED:\]

- **CORE STAT: SWAGGER.** (Replacing 'Spiritual Wisdom')
- **CORE STAT: PLOT ARMOR.** (Replacing 'Destiny')
- **CORE STAT: SOUNDTRACK.** (Replacing 'Qi Capacity')

**\[UNIQUE TALENT DETECTED: THE SUSPENSION OF DISBELIEF\]**
*Description: The user is so incredibly dense that reality warps to
accommodate his ignorance. If the User believes he is cool, the Universe agrees.*

Kevin grinned upside down. "Hell yeah. Swagger stat? I must be maxed out."

"Who are you talking to?" Lin Xue's voice drifted down, laced with irritation.

Kevin twisted his body to look up at her. "Just checking my stats, beautiful! Hey, do you know how to access the inventory? I need a parachute or maybe a jetpack!"

Lin Xue looked down. She saw nothing in front of Kevin. He was shouting at thin air.

*He communes with invisible spirits,* she reasoned. *Perhaps he is
negotiating with the wind demons to keep us aloft. His madness is his strength.*

"Be silent," she ordered. "We are approaching the Peak of Heavenly Judgment."

The blue box in front of Kevin's face dinged.

**\[QUEST ALERT:** "**MEET THE PARENTS**"**\]** *Objective: Survive the
meeting with Sect Leader Chen (The Villain's Dad).* *Difficulty: Nightmare.* *Reward: Not dying immediately.* *Bonus Reward: A lingering glance from the Heroine.*

"Nightmare difficulty?" Kevin laughed. "Please. I beat *Battletoads* on the NES. I eat nightmares for breakfast."

The interface glitched again, a red warning box flashing.

**\[WARNING: HOSTILE INTENT DETECTED.\]** **\[SOURCE: EVERYONE.\]**

As they crested the final mountain peak, a massive palace came into view. It was built of gold and obsidian, radiating power. Hundreds of disciples were lined up in the courtyard, weapons drawn.

Lin Xue began her descent.

"Alright, System," Kevin muttered to the floating text. "Let's see what you got. Activate... Cool Landing Mode!"

The System didn't respond, but Kevin didn't care. He started humming the
*Mission: Impossible* theme song.

Dun dun, dun dun, dun dun...

Lin Xue landed gracefully. The silk ribbon dissolved, dropping Kevin the last five feet.

He didn't stumble. He didn't fall. Prompted by the music in his head, he tucked his shoulder, rolled, and sprang up into a pose, hands bladed, looking fiercely at the confused army of cultivators.

**\[SYSTEM MESSAGE: STYLE POINTS +100\]** **\[EFFECT: INTIMIDATION AURA
ACTIVATED.\]**

A ripple of unease went through the gathered crowd. To them, Kevin hadn't just fallen; he had executed a *Ground-Breaking Heaven Fall* maneuver.

Kevin brushed his shoulder. "Ladies and Gentlemen," he announced, looking at the HUD which was now highlighting the biggest, angriest-looking guy in the room with a red 'BOSS' icon. "I hope you brought your stunt doubles."

He glanced at Lin Xue and gave her a thumbs up. "Don't worry, babe. I'll clear the level. You just look pretty."

Lin Xue's hand drifted to the hilt of her sword. She wasn't sure if she should protect him or stab him herself.

The Interface flickered one last message:

\[TUTORIAL COMPLETE. GOOD LUCK, IDIOT.\]
