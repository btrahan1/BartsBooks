# Chapter 5: The Forbidden Art of Hammer Time

"Idiot?" Kevin snorted, swiping the blue notification box away with the back of his hand. "Please. I've been called worse by twelve-year-olds on
*Call of Duty* lobbies. You gotta try harder than that, System."

The massive doors of the Obsidian Palace groaned open. A wind violent enough to strip paint from a car rushed out, carrying with it a voice that sounded like grinding boulders.

"WHERE IS THE ANT WHO DARED TO STRIKE MY SON?"

Out stepped Sect Leader Chen. He was seven feet tall, broad as a vending machine, and surrounded by a wreath of purple lightning. His eyes were glowing orbs of rage.

**\[BOSS ALERT: CHEN LONG - SECT LEADER\]** **\[POWER LEVEL: ??? (Run
away, you moron)\]**

Kevin whistled. "Whoa. Look at those particle effects. This game has an insane graphics engine."

The disciples surrounding the courtyard parted like the Red Sea, leaving Kevin standing alone in the center of the kill zone. Lin Xue wisely stepped to the side, her hand hovering near her sword, watching intently.

Sect Leader Chen glared down at Kevin. "You... a mortal? A mortal with no cultivation humiliated my heir? I will grind your bones to dust!"

He raised a hand, and the purple lightning coalesced into a giant, spectral tiger claw floating above Kevin's head.

"Die!" Chen roared, bringing his hand down.

"Not today, Satan!" Kevin yelled. "DJ, spin that track!"

Kevin's internal playlist skipped the dramatic orchestral score and went straight to the golden age of parachute pants.

Duh-nuh-nuh-nuh. Nuh-nuh. Nuh-nuh.

"STOP!" Kevin screamed, holding up a palm.

The spectral tiger claw froze inches from his nose.

The Sect Leader froze. The disciples froze. Even the wind seemed to pause in confusion.

"Hammer time," Kevin whispered.

The beat dropped.

Kevin exploded into motion. It was his masterpiece. The "Kata of the Funky Pants."

He initiated the Side-Shuffle. He slid rapidly to the left, his feet moving in a blur, his upper body perfectly rigid.

My, my, my, music hits me so hard!

The giant claw slammed into the ground where Kevin had been a millisecond before, shattering the stone. But Kevin was already ten feet away, shimmying his shoulders.

"Missed me!" Kevin taunted.

"Insolence!" roared the Sect Leader. "Seize him!"

Fifty disciples drew their swords and charged. It was a wall of steel closing in from all sides.

**\[SYSTEM: EVASION PROTOCOL ENGAGED.\]** **\[ACTIVATING SKILL: CAN'T
TOUCH THIS.\]**

Kevin didn't panic. He just kept dancing.

As the first sword swung for his neck, Kevin did a deep squat-wiggle, his knees popping in and out to the beat. The blade whistled harmlessly over his headband.

Can't touch this!

He spun, doing a running man step backward. Two disciples stabbed at his chest, but Kevin's erratic, rhythmic bouncing made him a moving target that defied physics. He was slipping through the gaps in their formation like water.

To the cultivators, this was terrifying.

*"Look at his footwork!"* a disciple screamed, swinging wildly at empty
air. *"He moves between the seconds! It is the Temporal Ghost Step!"*

*"My sword passed right through his afterimage!"* another cried. *"He is
creating illusions with pure speed!"*

Kevin was in the zone. He felt the flow. He moonwalked past a spear thrust, grabbed a passing hors d'oeuvre from a terrified servant's tray, and popped a dumpling into his mouth without breaking his rhythm.

Yo, sound the bell, school is in, sucker!

Sect Leader Chen was turning a shade of purple that matched his lightning. "Enough playing! **Heavenly Thunder Net!**"

Chen clapped his hands. A web of electricity erupted from the ground, covering the entire courtyard. There was nowhere to run. Nowhere to hide.

Lin Xue gasped. *He cannot dodge an area-of-effect attack!*

Kevin saw the floor glowing. "Oh, is the floor lava? I love this game!"

Duh-nuh-nuh-nuh. Can't touch this!

Kevin started doing the typewriter dance,moving his feet so fast they were a blur, lifting his knees high.

\[SYSTEM: LOGIC OVERRIDE. FEET MOVING TOO FAST TO CONDUCT ELECTRICITY.\]

The lightning crackled up Kevin's legs, but because he was stepping to the beat of an untouchable song, the electricity simply *refused* to shock him. It arced off his nylon track pants, grounding itself harmlessly.

Kevin did a spin, pointed at the Sect Leader, and then did the splits again.

"Break it down!" Kevin yelled.

He held the pose. The music in his head faded.

The courtyard was destroyed. There were scorch marks everywhere. Fifty disciples were panting, exhausted, tangled in a pile of limbs.

And in the center, untouched, unblemished, and sweating only slightly, was Kevin.

He stood up, brushed some dust off his shoulder, and checked his reflection in the polished steel of a fallen disciple's sword.

"Man," Kevin said, addressing the stunned Sect Leader. "You guys have terrible aim. You should check your hit-boxes. They're glitchy as hell."

Sect Leader Chen took a step back, his eyes trembling. He had used his ultimate technique. And this boy... this *thing*... had danced through it.

"Who..." Chen whispered, his voice shaking. "Who is your Master? What Sect teaches such terrifying movement arts?"

Kevin smirked. He brushed his nose with his thumb.

"My Master?" Kevin chuckled. "He was too legit to quit. You wouldn't know him."

**\[SYSTEM MESSAGE:\]** **\[REPUTATION UPDATE: AZURE CLOUD SECT NOW
FEARS YOU.\]** **\[NEW TITLE UNLOCKED: THE UNTOUCHABLE ONE.\]** **\[XP GAINED: 500 (STYLE POINTS)\]**

Lin Xue stared at Kevin's back. Her heart was racing. *He mocked the lightning. He commanded the thunder to halt. He is... magnificent.*

Kevin turned to her and winked. "So, did we pass the level? I'm ready for the cutscene."
