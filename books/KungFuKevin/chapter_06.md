# Chapter 6: The Rider

Sect Leader Chen was a man who had stared down dragons. He had meditated in volcanoes. He had eaten scorpions for breakfast. But as Kevin sauntered up to him, thumbs hooked in his elastic waistband, Chen felt a cold sweat trickle down his spine.

"Yo, bossman!" Kevin chirped, breaking the terrifying silence. "That was some cool stuff, yeah! Very atmospheric. The lightning? A bit much on the strobe effect, maybe dial it back for the sequel, but solid effort."

Chen blinked, his purple aura sputtering. "Boss... man?"

Kevin leaned in, invading the Sect Leader's personal space. He tapped the Sect Leader's chest plate, which was forged from star-metalwith a playful finger.

"Now, let's talk contracts," Kevin said, his voice dropping to a conspiratorial whisper. "You know I'm the man. The metrics don't lie. I just danced through your little light show without scuffing my sneakers. So, the question is..."

Kevin paused for effect. He threw a heavy, exaggerated wink at the terrifying warlord.

"What can YOU... do for ME?"

The crowd gasped. To wink at a Sect Leader was to invite death. But to Chen, who was currently watching his own worldview crumble, the wink seemed like a chaotic signal. A twitch of madness.

**\[SYSTEM ACTIVATED: NEGOTIATION MODE\]** **\[TRANSLATING**
"**HOLLYWOOD DIVA**" **TO** "**ANCIENT EXTORTION**"**\]**

Sect Leader Chen swallowed hard. *This monster... he is testing me. He has displayed his dominance, and now he demands tribute to spare our lives.*

"Ven... Venerable One," Chen stammered, bowing slightly. "The Azure Cloud Sect is... humble. But we have resources. What does the Senior require to... appease his spirit?"

Kevin pulled a crumpled receipt out of his pocket (from a Pizza Hut back in Ohio) and pretended to read from it.

"Alright, listen up. I'm gonna need a Rider. Standard stuff for a talent of my caliber."

**\[DEMAND 1:** "**A Trailer.**"**\]**

"First off," Kevin said, counting on his fingers. "I need my own crib. No roommates. I need privacy to work on my craft. Preferably with a view, and good ventilation. The basement smell is so last season."

Chen nodded frantically. "Yes! Of course! We shall grant you the
**Shattered Moon Peak**. It is a solitary mountain, surrounded by a
barrier of mist. No disciple shall disturb your... craft."

Kevin shrugged. "Sounds fancy. Does it have WiFi? You know what, never mind, I'll use my data."

**\[DEMAND 2:** "**Gym Access.**"**\]**

"Second," Kevin continued. "I need a place to pump iron. I'm talking heavy weights. Resistance training. I gotta keep the guns loaded." He kissed his bicep.

Chen's eyes widened. *He wishes to train his body further? Impossible. His body is already impervious to lightning.*

"We have the **Ten-Ton Gravity Chamber** in the depths of the mountain," Chen offered. "It is used to crush the bones of traitors, but... if you wish to use it for 'pumping iron'..."

"Perfect," Kevin said. "Love a hardcore gym environment. No pain, no gain, right?"

**\[DEMAND 3:** "**Craft Services.**"**\]**

"Third. Diet," Kevin said seriously. "I'm on a strict regimen. High protein. None of that vegan stuff. I need energy. You got shakes? Supplements?"

Chen looked at Elder Fan for help. Elder Fan stepped forward, trembling.

"We... we can provide the **Blood Pills of the Nine-Tailed Beast**," Elder Fan squeaked. "And... and the meat of Spirit Boars cooked in medicinal herbs!"

Kevin nodded approvingly. "Spirit Boar? Is that like organic pork? I'm down. As long as it fits my macros."

**\[DEMAND 4:** "**The Assistant.**"**\]**

Kevin turned and pointed a finger directly at Lin Xue, who was standing like a statue by the pillar.

"And her," Kevin said, flashing a grin. "I need a PA. Someone to handle my schedule. Carry my water bottle. Wipe my brow when I get too sweaty. You know, a Handler."

The entire Sect held its breath. Lin Xue was the Ice Goddess of the Sect. The proudest daughter of the heavens. To ask her to be a servant?

Lin Xue's hand tightened on her sword. Her eyes narrowed.

But then she remembered the lightning. She remembered the "Hammer Time." She remembered the absolute emptiness of his cultivation.

*If I stay by his side,* she thought, *I can observe his Dao. I can
learn the secrets of the Untouchable One. Even if I must endure his... idiocy.*

She stepped forward and bowed stiffly. "I... accept the challenge."

"Challenge?" Kevin laughed. "Babe, it's not a challenge, it's a partnership! We're gonna be a power couple! Like Kim and Kanye, but with more kicking!"

Kevin turned back to Sect Leader Chen and clapped his hands together. "Alright, Bossman. Sounds like a deal. Where do I sign? Do you have an iPad?"

Chen hesitated, then produced a scroll made of dragon skin and a brush dipped in gold ink. He held it out with trembling hands.

Kevin took the brush. He held it like a crayon.

With a flourish, he scrawled a giant, messy signature across the ancient, priceless artifact.

KUNG FU KEVIN

He even drew a little smiley face next to it.

**\[SYSTEM MESSAGE:\]** **\[CONTRACT BOUND:** "**GUEST ELDER**" **STATUS
ACQUIRED.\]** **\[REWARDS ADDED TO INVENTORY: SPIRIT PEAK KEY, GRAVITY CHAMBER ACCESS, UNLIMITED MEAT.\]** **\[RELATIONSHIP UPDATE: LIN XUE (STATUS: SKEPTICAL BABYSITTER)\]**

Kevin tossed the brush back to Chen.

"Pleasure doing business with you," Kevin said. He threw an arm around the stiff, frozen shoulders of Lin Xue.

"Alright, sugar," Kevin said, pointing toward the floating mountains. "Take me to my trailer. Daddy needs a nap before his evening workout."

Lin Xue sighed, a sound that carried the weight of a thousand years of regret.

"Follow me... Elder Kevin."
