# Chapter 9: The Dao of Frankie Goes to Hollywood

Kevin wriggled deeper into the neon-green bean bag, the synthetic fabric screeching against his nylon track pants. It was a symphony of friction.

He looked up at Lin Xue. She was standing perfectly still in the center of the hall, her posture rigid, her hand still resting on the hilt of her spirit sword. She looked like she was expecting the bean bag to attack her at any moment.

"Lin," Kevin said, shaking his head. "Babe. You are a bit too uptight. You're vibrating. Are you cold? Or just stressed?"

"I am maintaining my vigilance," Lin Xue replied, eyeing the bean bag suspiciously. "This... 'chair'... it has no legs. It is a formless blob. Is it a slime beast?"

"It's ergonomic luxury, Lin," Kevin corrected. "But look at you. Shoulders up to your ears. Frown lines. You're gonna get wrinkles before you hit the Golden Core stage."

He patted the empty space on the bean bag next to him. "Let me lay some wisdom on ya."

Kevin sat up slightly, adopting the tone of a guru he'd seen in a documentary about Woodstock.

"See, life is like... a river. Or a really long movie. If you spend the whole time worrying about the plot holes, you miss the cool explosions."

He leaned back, putting his hands behind his head.

"Sometimes," Kevin said, his voice dropping into a rhythmic cadence, "you just need to *Relax*."

**\[SYSTEM MUSIC QUEUE:** "**RELAX**"** - FRANKIE GOES TO HOLLYWOOD\]**

The opening synth bass line began to thump in Kevin's head.
*Dun-dun-dun-dun.*

"Don't do it," Kevin sang softly, pointing a finger at her. "When you want to go to it."

Lin Xue blinked. "Go to... where? What is 'it'?"

"Relax!" Kevin shouted, throwing his arms wide. "Don't do it! When you want to come!"

Lin Xue took a step back, her face flushing a delicate shade of pink. "Come? Come where? Your words are... confusing. And suggestive."

"It's about letting go, Lin!" Kevin said, standing up and starting to sway his hips. "You gotta let the flow take you. You guys here, with your meditation and your 'holding your breath for ten years' stuff... you're doing it wrong. You're trying to control the universe. You gotta let the universe buy you a drink first."

He moonwalked around the ancient bone altar.

"You feel me?" Kevin asked, spinning to face her.

Lin Xue watched him. She tried to analyze his movements.

*He moves with no pattern,* she thought. *He sings of relaxation, yet
his energy is chaotic.* '*Don't do it... when you want to go to it.*'
*Is this a mantra? Is he saying that desire creates suffering? That one
must resist the urge to act (*'*do it*'*) in order to reach the destination (*'*go to it*'*)?*

Her eyes widened.

By the Heavens. He is teaching me the Dao of Wu Wei! Action through inaction! The Paradox of the Relaxed Mind!

"I... I think I feel you," Lin Xue whispered, her mind reeling with the sudden epiphany. "To relax is to conquer?"

"Exactly!" Kevin finger-gunned her. "Now you're getting it! chill out, max out, and all that jazz."

He flopped back onto the bean bag.

"Now," Kevin said, yawning. "I'm gonna take a power nap. Wake me up if any ninjas attack. Or if the pizza guy gets here."

He pulled his headband down over his eyes.

Lin Xue stared at him. A mortal man, sleeping defenselessly in the middle of a Forbidden Zone, surrounded by demons, on a green sack, having just casually shattered her understanding of cultivation philosophy with lyrics from the 1980s.

She slowly sat down on the cold stone floor, crossing her legs into the lotus position. She closed her eyes.

*Relax,* she told herself. *Don't do it.*

For the first time in her life, the spiritual energy in the air didn't feel like a heavy weight she had to pull. It felt like a song. And it was dancing around her.

Blue light began to shimmer around her body. The frost on the floor melted, replaced by blooming flowers.

Kevin snored loudly. *Hrrkk-shooo.*
