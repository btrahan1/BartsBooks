# Chapter 10: The Pizza Dao

Kevin woke up with a groan and a crick in his neck. The legendary-grade bean bag chair, while stylish, offered zero lumbar support.

His stomach gave a growl that sounded like a tectonic plate shifting.

"Man," Kevin mumbled, pulling the headband off his eyes. "I'm starving. I could eat a horse. Or a Spirit Beast. Whatever."

He looked around. Lin Xue was still meditating, surrounded by a gentle whirlwind of blue flower petals. She looked serene, powerful, and totally zoned out.

"Hey, Sleeping Beauty," Kevin called out. "Rise and shine. Daddy needs sustenance."

Lin Xue's eyes snapped open. The blue light vanished instantly. She looked at him with a new intensity, a mix of reverence and confusion.

"Elder Kevin," she said, bowing from her sitting position. "Your wisdom... it unlocked a knot in my heart. I have ascended to the pseudo-stage of the Golden Core."

"Cool, cool," Kevin waved his hand dismissively. "Glad I could help. Now, about lunch."

A few minutes later, a trembling servant scuttled into the hall carrying a tray. On it was a porcelain bowl of clear broth with a single, sad-looking white noodle floating in it, and a side of what looked like pickled tree bark.

"What is this?" Kevin asked, poking the noodle with a chopstick.

"It is Spirit Ginseng Broth," the servant squeaked. "And aged Cloud Root. It is the purest food for cultivation."

Kevin took a sip. It tasted like hot water that had once thought about a vegetable.

"All right..." Kevin sighed, pushing the bowl away. "These noodles don't hit the spot, know what I mean? It's missing the grease. The salt. The
*soul*."

He stood up, towering over the terrified servant.

"Send the cook in," Kevin commanded. "I gotta teach him how to make a pizza, since there doesn't seem to be a Domino's around here!"

Ten minutes later, the Royal Chef of the Azure Cloud Sect stood in the kitchen of the Shattered Moon Peak. His name was Chef Wang, and he was famous for cooking dishes that could cure poison. He wore a tall white hat and held a ladle made of meteor iron.

He looked insulted.

"Pizza?" Chef Wang scoffed. "I have never heard of this dish. Is it a type of medicinal pill?"

"It's a circle of happiness, Wang," Kevin said, tying a dirty apron over his yellow track suit. "And you and I are going to revolutionize the culinary world."

Kevin looked at the ingredients available.

"Okay. We need dough. Flour? Water? Yeast?"

"We have Spirit Wheat flour," Wang grunted. "And water from the Heavenly Spring."

"Good enough. Now, sauce. I need tomatoes."

"We have Fire-Fruit," Wang said, pointing to a basket of red, glowing orbs. "But they are spicy enough to melt iron."

"Perfect," Kevin said. "Crush 'em up. Add some garlic. Do you have garlic? Or something that smells like feet but tastes good?"

Wang produced a bulb of Ghost-Root. Kevin sniffed it. "Close enough."

"Now, the most important part. Cheese."

Wang looked blank. "Cheese?"

"Curdled milk? Aged dairy? The sticky-icky?"

Wang's face soured. "We have the fermented milk of the Thunder Yak. It is a solid block. It smells of ozone."

"Grate it!" Kevin ordered. "We're making history here!"

EXPERIMENTAL CUISINE\]** **\[CHANCE OF EXPLOSION: 40%\]**

Kevin began to toss the dough. He didn't know how to toss dough. He just threw it in the air and punched it.

"Hi-yah! Hwah!"

The Spirit Wheat dough was tough. It fought back. It was elastic and filled with Qi. When Kevin punched it, it bounced off his fist and slapped him in the face.

"It's resisting!" Kevin yelled. "Wang, hit it with the ladle!"

Wang, caught up in the madness, slammed his meteor iron ladle onto the dough. *BANG!*

The dough flattened into a perfect circle.

"Nice assist!" Kevin cheered.

They assembled the monstrosity. The Fire-Fruit sauce sizzled when it hit the dough. The Thunder Yak cheese sparked with static electricity. For toppings, Kevin threw on chunks of the Spirit Boar meat he had demanded earlier.

"Into the oven!" Kevin pointed to the massive stone kiln used for refining pills.

"That is an alchemy furnace!" Wang protested.

"It's a pizza oven now, Wang! Slide it in!"

They shoved the pizza into the magical fire.

Three minutes later, a smell wafted through the kitchen. It smelled of burnt crust, spicy magma, and heaven.

Kevin pulled it out. The cheese was bubbling and popping with blue sparks. The crust was golden.

"Behold," Kevin whispered. "The Supreme Disc."

He sliced it into triangles. He picked up a slice. The cheese stretched, a long, gooey string of electric dairy.

"Bottoms up."

Kevin took a bite.

The spice of the Fire-Fruit hit him like a punch to the tongue. The Thunder Yak cheese zapped his gums. The Spirit Boar meat was savory and rich.

It was chaotic. It was painful. It was delicious.

"Oh yeah," Kevin mumbled with his mouth full. "That's the stuff."

He handed a slice to Chef Wang. "Try it."

Wang hesitated. He was a master of delicate flavors. This thing looked like a culinary car crash. But the smell...

He took a bite.

Wang's eyes went wide. His hat flew off.

*The explosion of flavors!* Wang thought. *The Yin of the bread
balancing the extreme Yang of the sauce! The chaotic lightning of the cheese stimulating the taste buds! It is... barbaric. It is crude. It is... MAGNIFICENT!*

"This..." Wang whispered, tears forming in his eyes. "This is the Taste of the Mortal World! It is so... greasy! I love it!"

"That's called cholesterol, buddy," Kevin grinned. "It's the flavor of freedom."

Kevin grabbed the rest of the pizza and walked out into the courtyard, where Lin Xue was waiting.

"Hey, Babe!" Kevin shouted. "Dinner is served! Grab a slice. But blow on it first, the cheese is literally lightning."

Lin Xue looked at the glowing, sparking triangle of dough.

"You cooked... in the alchemy furnace?" she asked.

"Innovation, Lin," Kevin said, taking another bite. "I'm disrupting the industry. Now eat up. We got training to do. I need to work off these carbs."

Kevin wiped tomato sauce from his chin with his thumb. "Man, I should franchise this."
