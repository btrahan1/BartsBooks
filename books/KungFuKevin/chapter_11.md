# Chapter 11: The Franchise Wars

Kevin slapped Chef Wang on the back, nearly sending the robust man face-first into a sack of Spirit Wheat.

"Chef, my man! Let's do this," Kevin said, his eyes gleaming with the light of capitalism. "How much you think those tighty-whitey folks will pay for this delicacy? I'm talking about the disciples. Those guys in the robes who look like they've never tasted anything with more than two grams of sodium."

Chef Wang wiped a tear of joy from his cheek. He was still chewing on the crust, savoring the crunch.

"The disciples?" Wang mused. "They eat flavorless grain pills to suppress their desires. They are starving for sensation. For something like this... a slice that stimulates the senses and provides Yang energy? They would pay in Spirit Stones. Perhaps... five Low-Grade stones per slice?"

"Five?" Kevin scoffed. "We're talking premium, artisanal, small-batch pizza here, Wang. Ten stones. Minimum. Plus tip."

Kevin spun around to face Lin Xue, who was gingerly holding a slice of Thunder Pizza by the crust, examining the sparking cheese like it was a hazardous material.

"I've got tons more recipes!" Kevin declared, counting on his grease-stained fingers. "Buffalo Wing calzones. Meat Lovers lasagna. Garlic knots,oh man, wait till you taste the garlic knots. We're gonna blow this market wide open."

He pointed around the circle.

"Let's three-way the profit! You, me, and my sweet thang right here!"

Lin Xue choked on the air. "Three-way... the profit?"

"Yeah! The split!" Kevin clarified. "I'm the Visionary. Wang is the Talent. And you, Lin..." He winked. "You're the Face. The Brand Ambassador. Imagine it: 'Senior Sister Lin loves the Thunder Pizza, and you will too!'"

Lin Xue looked at the pizza. She took a tiny, hesitant bite.

The flavors exploded in her mouth. The spicy sauce warmed her dantian. The lightning cheese sent a pleasant shiver through her meridians. It was... shockingly effective at circulating Qi.

*It acts as a stimulant,* she realized. *It forces the body to react, to
defend against the spice and the shock, thereby strengthening the constitution. It is not just food. It is edible cultivation training.*

"It is... acceptable," Lin Xue said, trying to maintain her icy demeanor while licking a drop of sauce from her lip. "But Sect rules forbid unauthorized commerce on the mountain."

"Rules?" Kevin laughed. "Lin, look at me. I'm wearing yellow nylon pants in a world of silk. I *am* the exception to the rule. Besides, we'll cut the Sect Leader in. Call it a... franchise fee."

**\[SYSTEM QUEST:** "**THE WOLF OF WUXIA STREET**"**\]** **\[OBJECTIVE:
SELL 100 SLICES BEFORE SUNSET.\]** **\[REWARD: UNLOCKING** "**CAPITALISM**" **SKILL TREE.\]**

"Alright, Wang," Kevin barked, clapping his hands. "Fire up the furnace. We need volume. Crank out twenty more pies. Lin, grab your sword. We're doing delivery."

An hour later, the main training grounds of the Azure Cloud Sect were in chaos.

Usually, this time was reserved for silent meditation and sword drills. Today, however, the air smelled of oregano and burnt cheese.

Kevin had set up a booth made of two floating shields and a "borrowed" banner that he had painted over with red sauce.

**KEVIN'S KICKIN' KITCHEN** *Specific Tastes for Specific Cultivators*

"Hot and fresh!" Kevin yelled, spinning a pizza box (made of folded parchment) on his finger. "Get your Thunder Pizza here! Guaranteed to shock your taste buds and maybe your heart rhythm! Only ten Spirit Stones!"

A crowd of young disciples had gathered. They were hesitant at first, but the smell was intoxicating.

"Is it... safe?" one disciple asked, eyeing the pizza.

"Safe?" Kevin laughed. "Kid, is walking the Path of the Sword safe? Is fighting a demon beast safe? Live a little! Eat the cheese!"

Kevin shoved a slice into the disciple's hand.

The disciple took a bite. His eyes bulged. Steam shot out of his nose.

"IT BURNS!" the disciple screamed. "BUT... I FEEL SO ALIVE!"

He frantically dug into his robe and threw a handful of glowing blue stones on the table. "Another! Give me another!"

The floodgates opened.

"I want the Spicy Magma!" "Do you have anything with extra Spirit Sausage?" "Take my money! Take my sword!"

Chef Wang was in the background, working the portable furnace like a madman, laughing maniacally as he tossed dough into the air.

Lin Xue stood by the booth, looking incredibly uncomfortable. She held a sign that Kevin had forced upon her.

It read: "**Even Ice Queens Melt for Kevin's Pizza.**"

"Kevin," she hissed, leaning toward him as he collected a mountain of Spirit Stones. "This is undignified. I am a Core Disciple. I should not be peddling... snacks."

"You're not peddling snacks, babe," Kevin said, counting the loot. "You're selling a lifestyle. Look at them! They're happy! They're energized! We're boosting morale!"

Suddenly, a shadow fell over the booth.

The crowd went silent and parted instantly.

Standing there was Young Master Chen,the guy Kevin had humiliated earlier. He was bandaged, limping, and looked furious. Behind him stood two hulking enforcers from the Disciplinary Hall.

"You!" Chen spat, pointing a shaking finger at Kevin. "You turn our sacred training ground into a... a marketplace? You peddle this poison?"

Kevin stopped counting stones. He looked at Chen. He looked at the pizza.

"Poison?" Kevin picked up a slice of 'Meat Lovers'. "This is pure protein, bro. Maybe if you ate some, you wouldn't have folded like a lawn chair in our duel."

The crowd gasped. *Sick burn.*

Chen's face went purple. "Seize him! Confiscate this... filth! And bring me that chef's head!"

The Enforcers stepped forward.

Kevin sighed. "Always a critic. Wang! Keep cooking! Lin, hold my fanny pack."

He tossed the bag of spirit stones to Lin Xue.

"Time for some customer service," Kevin said.

He stepped out from behind the booth. He didn't draw his sword. Instead, he grabbed two fresh, uncooked pizzas,still raw dough and sticky sauce, one in each hand.

"Alright, boys," Kevin said, spinning the dough discs like shuriken. "You want a piece of me? Or do you want a piece of the pie?"
