# Chapter 12: Finish Him (With Extra Cheese)

The Disciplinary Enforcers were huge. They were built like brick outhouses stacked on top of other brick outhouses. They wielded heavy iron staffs and wore expressions that said they hadn't smiled since the previous dynasty.

"Surrender the dough, criminal!" the Left Enforcer bellowed.

Kevin smirked. He tapped his foot.

Dun-dun-dun-dun-dun-dun...

**\[SYSTEM MUSIC QUEUE:** "**TECHNO SYNDROME**" **(MORTAL KOMBAT
THEME)\]**

"MORTAL KOMBAT!" Kevin screamed at the top of his lungs.

The beat dropped. Kevin exploded.

He didn't run. He slid. He utilized the **Grease Glide** technique, surfing on a patch of spilled mozzarella oil.

The Left Enforcer swung his staff down, a blow meant to crush skulls. Kevin limboed under it, his back inches from the ground.

"Toasty!" Kevin falsettoed.

As he slid past, he slapped the raw, sticky pizza dough right over the Enforcer's face.

SPLAT.

The Enforcer blinded by pepperoni and tomato sauce, stumbled backward, flailing. "My eyes! The spice! It stings the soul!"

The Right Enforcer roared and charged. "You dishonorable cur!"

Kevin popped up from his slide. He reached into his pocket (which was somehow filled with flour) and threw a handful into the air.

"Pocket Flour!"

A white cloud engulfed the Right Enforcer. While the man was coughing and hacking, Kevin spun around and delivered a **Spinning Back-Kick**... right into the Enforcer's gut.

But Kevin wasn't wearing combat boots. He was wearing cheap canvas shoes. The impact hurt his toe more than the Enforcer.

"Ow! Ow, ow, ow!" Kevin hopped on one foot.

The Enforcer recovered, looming over Kevin. He raised his staff.

"Die!"

Suddenly, a streak of white lightning shot past Kevin.

THWACK.

A stale baguette, hard as a rock, hit the Enforcer square in the forehead. The giant man's eyes rolled back, and he crumpled.

Kevin looked back. Chef Wang was standing by the oven, holding another baguette like a javelin. He gave Kevin a nod.

"Nice shot, Chef!" Kevin pointed. "Now that's using your loaf!"

Young Master Chen, seeing his goons defeated by baked goods, lost his mind.

"Useless trash!" Chen screamed. He drew a new sword, this one glowing red. "I will kill you myself!"

Chen channeled his Qi. Flames erupted from his body. He prepared the
**Scorching Heaven Slash**.

Kevin looked at the glowing red sword. Then he looked at the pizza peel (the giant wooden paddle) leaning against the booth.

"Oh, it's on," Kevin said.

He grabbed the pizza peel. He held it like a spear.

"Get over here!" Kevin shouted in a demonic, gravelly voice.

He hooked the flat end of the pizza peel behind Chen's ankle. He yanked.

Chen, who was in the middle of a dramatic power-up sequence, wasn't expecting a low sweep. His feet flew out from under him.

"WAAAAH!"

As Chen fell, Kevin didn't let up. He spun the pizza peel and, using it like a giant spatula, scooped the falling Young Master out of the air.

"Alley-oop!"

Kevin launched Chen toward the booth.

Chen flew through the air, flailing, and landed face-first into a massive bowl of uncooked dough.

FLOOMPH.

Silence fell over the training grounds.

Young Master Chen sat up. He was covered in sticky white dough. He looked like a furious, abominable snowman. A single slice of pepperoni was stuck to his forehead.

The music in Kevin's head reached its crescendo.

**\[SYSTEM ALERT: ENEMY STUNNED.\]** **\[FINISH HIM!\]**

Kevin walked up to Chen. He looked down. He raised his hand.

The crowd held its breath. Was he going to use a lethal strike? The Five Finger Death Punch?

Kevin reached out. He grabbed the pepperoni slice from Chen's forehead.

He ate it.

"Fatality," Kevin whispered. "Flawless Victory."

He turned to the crowd, arms raised.

"Who's next? And who wants extra garlic sauce?"

The crowd erupted.

"KEVIN! KEVIN! KEVIN!"

Lin Xue watched from the side, clutching the bag of money. Her heart was pounding.

*He humiliated the Young Master... with groceries,* she thought. *He
turned a battle of life and death into a slapstick comedy. And yet... no one died. He diffused the killing intent with... cheese.*

She looked at the cheering disciples. She looked at Kevin, who was currently high-fiving Chef Wang.

For the first time in ten years, the corners of Lin Xue's mouth twitched upward.

It wasn't a smile. Not yet. But it was close.

"Kevin," she murmured. "You truly are... something else."

Kevin bounded over to her, wiping flour off his track pants.

"Did you see that, Lin? The pizza peel scoop? That was NBA jam level stuff!"

He grabbed the money bag.

"Alright, franchise secured. We made bank today. Now, we gotta hide before Chen's dad shows up and tries to turn me into a lightning rod again."

He looked at the dough-covered Chen, who was sputtering and trying to stand up.

"Hey, Chen!" Kevin yelled. "Nice costume! You look like the Pillsbury Doughboy's angry cousin!"

Kevin grabbed Lin Xue's hand.

"Run!"

**\[SYSTEM QUEST COMPLETE:** "**THE WOLF OF WUXIA STREET**"**\]**
**\[PROFIT: 500 SPIRIT STONES.\]** **\[RELATIONSHIP STATUS: LIN XUE
(AMUSED)\]** **\[RELATIONSHIP STATUS: YOUNG MASTER CHEN (HOMICIDAL)\]**

As they sprinted away toward the Shattered Moon Peak, Kevin laughed.

"Best. Job. Ever."
