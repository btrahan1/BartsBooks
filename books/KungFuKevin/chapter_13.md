# Chapter 13: The Devil Wears Prada (And Spirit Armor)

Kevin stood before the cracked bronze mirror in his spooky new mansion, inspecting the damage. The yellow nylon track suit, once a beacon of unparalleled style, was looking rough. It was stained with tomato sauce, scorched by lightning, covered in flour, and ripped at the knee from that time he tried to slide on a gravel path.

He picked at a piece of dried mozzarella stuck to his collar.

"I think it's time for some new threads," Kevin announced, turning to Lin Xue. She was currently trying to clean the bean bag chair with a purification spell. "Whadda ya think, Babe? You got a royal tailor or something around here? I need a look that says, 'I saved the world, but I'm still approachable.'"

Lin Xue wrinkled her nose. "You smell like a burning bakery. Yes. New garments are... essential. Follow me."

They descended from the peak (Kevin surfing on his sword, Lin Xue flying properly) and arrived at the **Pavilion of the Thousand Silks**. It was a building draped in shimmering fabrics that floated in the wind without tethers.

Inside, an elderly man with six arms (a result of a specific Spider-Style mutation) was weaving on a loom made of light. This was
**Grandmaster Weaver**, the sect's premier armorer.

He stopped weaving when Kevin walked in. All six of his hands paused. He lowered his spectacles and stared at the dirty yellow track suit.

"By the Ancestors," the Weaver whispered, looking physically ill. "What... is *that*?"

"It's vintage, Versace," Kevin said, strutting in. "Poly-blend. Breathable. But it's seen better days. I need an upgrade. I got cash."

Kevin slammed his bag of 500 Spirit Stones onto the counter.

The Weaver's eyes narrowed. "I do not sew for commoners. I create Spirit Vestments that protect the soul."

"Good," Kevin said. "Because my soul is very precious. Now, listen closely. I have a vision."

Kevin grabbed a piece of parchment and a charcoal stick. He began to draw. It was a terrible drawing. Stick figures with exaggerated muscles.

"I don't want robes," Kevin explained, sketching furiously. "Robes are a tripping hazard. I need something dynamic. I'm thinking... *The Matrix* meets *Bloodsport*."

The Weaver looked at the drawing. "You want... a sleeveless vest made of cow skin? And trousers that are... loose in the hips but tight at the ankles?"

"Exactly!" Kevin pointed. "They're called 'Action Jeans.' The gusseted crotch is non-negotiable. I need full range of motion for high kicks. If I split my pants, I'm suing you."

The Weaver sighed, looking at Lin Xue. She shrugged. "He has money, Elder. And... he defeated Chen Long."

The Weaver's six arms twitched. "Fine. But I will not use cow skin. I will use the hide of the **Iron-Scaled Drake**. And for the trousers...
**Silk of the Void Spider**. It stretches infinitely."

**\[SYSTEM MUSIC QUEUE:** "**SHARP DRESSED MAN**"** - ZZ TOP\]**

The montage began.

The Weaver measured Kevin with a magical tape measure that snaked around his body like a constrictor. *Kevin flexed.* *The Weaver slapped him.*
*Kevin did a blue steel look.* *The Weaver rolled his eyes.*

Fabrics flew through the air. Needles danced. Spirit fire welded the seams.

Thirty minutes later, the music faded.

Kevin stepped out from behind the changing screen.

Lin Xue gasped.

Gone was the yellow track suit.

In its place, Kevin wore: 1. A sleeveless, jet-black vest made of dragon scales that looked like distressed leather. It was open at the chest, showing off his (very average) abs. 2. Baggy, black martial arts pants with a red stripe down the side. They flowed like water but looked tough as nails. 3. A pair of heavy, black boots that stomped when he walked.
4. A new red headband, embroidered with gold thread that read *"BAD BOY"*
in ancient runes.

But the *piece de resistance* was on his face.

"Are those..." Lin Xue squinted. "Are those smoked crystals?"

"They're Aviators, doll," Kevin said, adjusting the dark, round glasses Weaver had crafted from Obsidian Crystal. "They block UV rays *and* killing intent."

He looked in the mirror. He looked ridiculous. He looked like an extra from a straight-to-DVD action movie in 1992.

To Kevin, he looked like a God.

**\[SYSTEM UPDATE: NEW GEAR EQUIPPED\]** \* **CHEST:** *Vest of the
Renegade* (+50 Defense, +100 coolness). \* **LEGS:** *Pants of the Hammer* (+20 Agility, Immune to Ripping). \* **FACE:** *Shades of the Blind Dog* (Allows user to see 'cool stuff' and ignore 'haters').

Kevin turned to the Weaver. "You outdid yourself, six-arms. I look dangerous."

The Weaver pocketed the Spirit Stones, weeping silently. "I have defiled my art. Please... never tell anyone I made this."

Kevin turned to Lin Xue. He lowered his sunglasses, looked over the rim, and then pushed them back up.

"So," Kevin grinned, striking a pose with his thumbs hooked in his belt. "How do I look? Ready for the red carpet? Or the octagon?"

Lin Xue walked around him. She touched the dragon-hide vest. It was impenetrable. The pants were woven with top-tier defensive wards.

"You look..." she searched for the word. "Like a rogue cultivator who lost his mind. But... the defensive properties are adequate."

"Adequate?" Kevin laughed. "I'm stunning. Now come on. I got new boots. I wanna go kick something."

He headed for the door, his boots clunking heavily.

"Wait," Lin Xue called out. "You forgot your... 'Board'."

Kevin snapped his fingers. "Right! Can't leave the ride."

He grabbed the rusty iron sword. It clashed horribly with his new black outfit.

"Note to self," Kevin muttered. "Need to get this thing painted. Maybe some flames? Or a skull decal?"

He threw the sword into the air, jumped on it, and hovered two feet off the ground.

"Let's roll, Lin! I feel a training montage coming on!"
