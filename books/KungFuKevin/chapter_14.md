# Chapter 14: Pimp My Blade

"Normally, I'd franchise these threads," Kevin said, smoothing down his dragon-scale vest as they flew toward the smoking crater of the sect's foundry. "But I wanna be unique, ya know? Limited Edition. One of one."

He looked down at his feet. The rusty iron sword, 'Old Iron', was rattling ominously. Rust flakes were peeling off in the wind, staining his new black boots.

"But this board..." Kevin shook his head. "It could use an upgrade. I mean, it's functional and all. It gets me from A to B. But it doesn't fit the vibe, ya dig? It's like driving a rust-bucket Honda Civic when I'm dressed for a Ferrari."

He looked at Lin Xue, who was flying serenely on her elegant, frost-covered blade.

"Let's go visit the swordsmith, or whatever you call the weapon dude. You can tell a lot about a man by his wheels, comprendez?"

Lin Xue sighed. "The 'Weapon Dude' is Elder Tie. He is a Master of the Divine Flame. He is... temperamental. He threw a disciple into a volcano last week for asking for a 'shiny' handle."

"Perfect," Kevin grinned. "I love a guy with passion."

**The Volcanic Forge Peak** lived up to its name. The ground was black
rock, veins of lava flowed like rivers, and the air smelled of sulfur and burnt eyebrows.

Inside the massive cavern, a giant of a man was hammering a piece of glowing red metal. Elder Tie was seven feet of muscle, soot, and beard. He hit the metal, *CLANG*, and the entire mountain shook.

Kevin strolled in, his obsidian aviators reflecting the magma.

"Yo! Is this the chop shop?" Kevin shouted over the noise.

Elder Tie stopped hammering. He turned slowly. His eyes were like burning coals.

"Who dares disturb the Sacred Hammer?" Tie roared.

"The name's Kevin," Kevin said, stepping up to the anvil. He unclipped the rusty sword from his belt and slammed it down. "And I need you to pimp my ride."

Elder Tie looked at the rusty slab of iron. His face twisted in disgust.

"This..." Tie spat. "This is scrap metal. It is an insult to the art of smithing. It has no soul. It is garbage."

"One man's trash is another man's treasure, Big Guy," Kevin said. "But you're right. It's lacking... pizzazz. I need a makeover. Total overhaul."

Kevin pulled out his charcoal stick and began drawing directly on the anvil.

"Okay, look here. First, the shape. It's too pointy. I need a wider deck. More surface area for the feet. And curve the ends up, front and back. That's called a 'kicktail'. Essential for aerodynamics."

Elder Tie frowned, leaning in. "Curved tips... to channel the wind flow? An interesting theory."

"Exactly," Kevin lied. "Now, traction. The metal is slippery. I need 'Grip Tape'. You got any rough sandpaper? Or maybe the skin of a sandpaper shark?"

"We have the hide of the **Granite Ray**," Tie muttered. "It is rough enough to strip flesh from bone."

"Perfect. Glue it to the top side," Kevin ordered.

"And finally," Kevin leaned in close, lowering his sunglasses. "Underglow."

"Under... glow?" Tie asked.

"Yeah. I want the bottom of the sword to glow. Neon Green or Electric Blue. When I fly at night, I want people to think a UFO is landing. It's an intimidation tactic."

Elder Tie stroked his soot-stained beard. "To infuse the blade with
**Luminous Spirit Stones**... it would create a phantom aura. It would
make the enemy unsure of the blade's true position. Genius."

Tie grabbed his hammer. A manic grin spread across his face.

"I have not forged a weapon this strange in centuries," Tie bellowed. "Bring me the Granite Ray! Stoke the fires! We forge the... the Kick-Tail!"

**\[SYSTEM MUSIC QUEUE:** "**IRON MAN**"** - BLACK SABBATH\]**

The montage was intense.

- Kevin held the tongs while Tie hammered.
- Tie poured molten star-metal over the rusty iron, widening the blade
  into a perfect skateboard shape.
- Kevin applied the Granite Ray skin to the top with adhesive resin made
  from tree sap.
- They embedded glowing blue stones along the bottom edge.

Three hours later, it was done.

It didn't look like a sword anymore. It looked like a heavy metal, bladed longboard from the future. It was wide, black, wrapped in rough gray skin on top, and pulsed with a sick neon blue light underneath. The edges were still razor sharp, but the shape was unmistakably skate-ready.

Elder Tie held it up. "It is... an abomination. I love it."

Kevin took the board. It hummed in his hands.

**\[SYSTEM UPDATE: WEAPON EVOLVED\]** **\[OLD IRON -\> THE RADICAL
SHREDDER\]** **\[CLASS: FLYING BOARD\]** **\[STATS: +50 SPEED, +200 STYLE, +100% CHANCE TO DO A KICKFLIP\]** **\[SPECIAL ABILITY: NEON NIGHTMARE (BLINDS ENEMIES AT NIGHT)\]**

"Beautiful," Kevin whispered. He dropped it to the ground.

It hovered instantly, the blue underglow casting an eerie light on the black rock.

Kevin hopped on. The Granite Ray grip tape held his new boots perfectly. He wiggled the board back and forth. It was responsive. Agile.

"Smooth," Kevin said. "Real smooth."

He turned to Elder Tie. "You're an artist, Tie. A true visionary."

Kevin tossed a bag of 100 Spirit Stones to the smith. "Buy yourself something nice. Maybe some beard oil."

Tie caught the bag, grinning. "If you ever need... 'spoilers'... come back."

Kevin kicked the tail of the board. The front lifted up.

"Let's go, Lin," Kevin said, revving the board like an engine (making
*vroom vroom* noises with his mouth). "I feel the need... the need for
speed."

Lin Xue stared at the glowing, flat, bladed monstrosity.

"It... it has no point," she noted. "You cannot stab with it."

"Stabbing is so medieval, Babe," Kevin said, launching himself toward the tunnel exit. "I'm gonna **grind** their faces."

He blasted out of the volcano, leaving a trail of blue light and confused blacksmiths in his wake.

"To the skate park!" Kevin yelled. "I mean... the training grounds!"
