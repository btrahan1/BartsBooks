# Chapter 15: Half-Pipe to Heaven

"Woo hoo!"

Kevin's shout echoed off the cliffs of the Azure Cloud Sect as he tore through the sky.

He wasn't just flying; he was carving. He dipped his left shoulder, leaning hard into the turn. The *Radical Shredder* responded instantly, banking sharply. The blue underglow left a streak of neon light in the air, like a Tron light cycle drawn by a calligraphy master.

Lin Xue flew behind him, struggling to keep up. Her traditional sword flight relied on mental commands and linear trajectories. She flew in straight lines and gentle arcs.

Kevin flew in zig-zags.

He spotted a large, curved roof of an ancient library pavilion ahead.

"Target acquired!" Kevin yelled.

He accelerated. Instead of flying over it, he dropped altitude.

CLACK.

The metal trucks (or in this case, the reinforced blade-guards) of his board hit the ridge of the roof.

"Grind it out!"

Sparks, literal showers of blue and gold sparks, erupted as Kevin slid along the spine of the ancient building. The sound was deafening, a high-pitched metallic screech that sounded like a dragon screaming.

He rode the entire length of the roof, balanced perfectly, arms out, sunglasses reflecting the terrified faces of the scholars below.

At the end of the roof, he stomped on the tail.

Pop.

The board launched into the air. Kevin grabbed the nose of the board with his left hand, a **Mute Grab**, tucked his knees, and spun 360 degrees.

"Stalefish!" Kevin screamed nonsense names of tricks that no cultivator had ever heard.

He landed back on the air currents, the board stabilizing instantly under his boots.

Lin Xue watched, her mouth slightly open. The wind whipped her hair, but she was too focused to notice.

*His agility...* she thought, her mind racing to analyze the physics.
*He uses the environment not as an obstacle, but as a fulcrum! By
sliding along the roof, he conserved his momentum while changing direction. And that spin... by tucking his body, he reduced his wind resistance to zero, allowing for rapid rotation!*

It is not flight. It is... dancing with gravity.

"How is he doing that?" she whispered. "He has no wind cultivation. No levitation spells. He is simply... falling with style."

Kevin looped back around, pulling up beside her. He was grinning like a maniac.

"Did you see that grind, Lin? That was a fifty-fifty! I need to find a handrail. Or a really long dragon's spine."

He looked at her, noticing her stare.

"What's the matter, Babe? Never seen a pro skater before?"

Lin Xue shook her head, her eyes wide. "Your mobility... it defies the Dao of Flight. We are taught to be like the arrow, straight and true. You are like the... the drunk butterfly. Erratic. Unpredictable."

"Butterflies are hard to catch," Kevin said, winking behind his shades. "And arrows are boring."

He looked down at the Sect grounds. They were approaching the **Pillars of the Sky**, a training area consisting of massive stone columns of varying heights, used for practicing jumping and lightness skills.

To the disciples, it was a rigorous obstacle course.

To Kevin, it was a skate park.

"Oh. My. God," Kevin gasped. "Look at that layout. It's got vert ramps. It's got gaps. It's heaven."

He pointed the nose of the *Radical Shredder* down.

"Drop in!"

Kevin dove. He plummeted toward the tallest pillar. Just before impact, he pulled up, transitioning the vertical fall into horizontal speed along the side of the pillar. He rode the vertical wall of the stone column, defying gravity with sheer centrifugal force.

\[SYSTEM SKILL: WALL RIDE\]

He shot off the side of the pillar, launched across a fifty-foot gap, and landed on the next one. He didn't stop. He chained the moves together.

Jump. Grind. Wall ride. Manual.

Disciples on the pillars stopped their training. They watched a black blur with neon blue lights tearing through their sacred course.

*"Is that... a demon?"* one asked. *"No! It is the Pizza Lord!"* another
shouted.

Kevin hit the final pillar, a massive, curved structure meant to deflect wind.

He rode up it until he was vertical, then pushed higher, launching into the air above the clouds.

He hung there for a second, a silhouette against the sun, board grabbed in one hand, legs kicked out.

**\[SYSTEM ACHIEVEMENT: THE BIG AIR\]** **\[HEIGHT: 500 FEET\]**
**\[STYLE: IMPECCABLE\]**

He fell back down, landing on the transition perfectly.

"Clean!" Kevin yelled.

He cruised over to where Lin Xue had landed. He hopped off the board, kicked the tail so it flipped up into his hand, and tucked it under his arm.

He wasn't even out of breath.

"Okay," Kevin said, adjusting his shades. "That was a good warm-up. But I think I scuffed my new boots."

Lin Xue looked at the pillars. He had traversed the entire "Course of the Nine Deaths" in under thirty seconds. It usually took elite disciples ten minutes.

"Kevin," she said, her voice serious. "You must teach me this technique. The 'Grind'. The 'Stalefish'. I must learn the Dao of the Board."

Kevin grinned. "You wanna be a skater girl? I dunno, Lin. It takes attitude. It takes grit. And most importantly..."

He reached into his pocket and pulled out a spare red headband.

"It takes accessories."

He tossed her the headband.

"Put that on. We start training tomorrow at dawn. Bring your own board. Or use your sword. Whatever."

Lin Xue caught the headband. She looked at it as if it were a sacred artifact.

*To learn the movement of the erratic butterfly...* she thought. *This
is the key to breaking through to the Nascent Soul stage!*

She tied the red strip of cloth around her forehead.

"I am ready," she said fiercely. "Let us... shred."
