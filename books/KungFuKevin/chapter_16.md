# Chapter 16: The Ali Doctrine

The dawn light washed over the **Pillars of the Sky**, painting the stone columns in shades of pink and gold. It was a serene, majestic morning.

Or it would have been, if not for the sound of grinding metal and 80s rock music echoing from a magical boombox Kevin had commissioned from the artifact hall.

"Lean into it!" Kevin shouted. "Bend your knees! Lower your center of gravity!"

Lin Xue was standing on her pristine, white flying sword. She wore her flowing azure robes, but now, a red headband emblazoned with "*BAD BOY*" was tied firmly around her forehead.

She was trying to do a "kickturn" on a vertical stone wall.

"It is... unnatural!" Lin Xue grunted, wobbling dangerously. "The sword wishes to go forward! You ask it to pivot on its axis!"

"Don't ask it, Lin!" Kevin hovered nearby on the *Radical Shredder*, effortlessly doing circles around her. "Tell it! You're the boss! Make the metal submit to the funk!"

Lin Xue gritted her teeth. She forced her will into the blade. She shifted her weight to her back foot, lifting the nose of the sword.

Pivot.

The sword swung around 180 degrees. She didn't fall.

"Yes!" Kevin cheered. "You are getting it, babe! That was clean!"

Lin Xue exhaled, a small smile touching her lips. She felt a rush of adrenaline she had never experienced in standard meditation.

Kevin cruised up next to her, resting one foot on a gargoyle head jutting from the pillar.

"Alright," Kevin said, lowering his shades. "You've got the movement down. Now we need to talk about combat application. Mobility is great, but you gotta know how to use it in a fight."

He hopped off his board and stood on the gargoyle, assuming a boxer's stance, bouncing lightly on the balls of his feet, hands up guarding his face.

"Let me drop some more wisdom on you," Kevin said, his voice dropping to a serious whisper. "This comes from the Greatest. The King. A man who punched people for a living and wrote poetry while doing it."

Lin Xue leaned in, her eyes wide. "A warrior poet? Like the Sage of the Drunken Brush?"

"Better," Kevin said. "Cassius Clay. Now, repeat after me."

He threw a quick left jab, then danced back.

"Float like a butterfly!"

Lin Xue mimicked him, throwing a graceful, fluid palm strike while drifting backward on her sword. "Float... like a butterfly."

"Sting like a bee!"

Kevin lunged forward, throwing a straight right cross that snapped with surprising speed.

"Sting like a bee!" Lin Xue repeated. She thrust her sword forward, channeling a sharp burst of Qi that pierced the air with a buzzing hum.

"Exactly!" Kevin clapped. "See, everyone here fights like a tank. They stand still, charge up their lasers, and blast each other. It's boring. It's static."

He began to shuffle around the pillar top, moving his feet in the **Ali Shuffle**.

"But if you float," Kevin said, blurring his feet, "they can't hit you. You're everywhere and nowhere. You're a ghost. And when they get frustrated, when they swing and miss..."

Kevin stopped shuffling and threw a lightning-fast punch at the air.

"STING! You hit 'em in the nose. You disrupt their rhythm. You break their cool."

Lin Xue looked at her sword. She imagined fighting a duel, not by overpowering her opponent with massive waves of energy, but by dancing around them, tapping them, annoying them, stinging them until they collapsed from exhaustion and confusion.

"The Dao of the Bee," Lin Xue mused. "Small. Fast. Lethal. And the Butterfly... chaotic. Beautiful. Untouchable."

"You got it," Kevin grinned. "Now, let's spar. Come at me, Lin. Give me your best shot. But remember..."

He jumped back onto his board and hovered five feet away.

"Your hands can't hit what your eyes can't see."

Lin Xue narrowed her eyes. The competitive fire lit up in her chest. She tightened her headband.

"Prepare yourself, Elder Kevin," she announced.

She surged forward. Instead of flying straight, she banked left, drifted right, and spun, floating like a butterfly.

Kevin laughed and kicked his board into gear. "That's it! Catch me if you can!"

They tore through the pillars, a game of cat and mouse played at eighty miles per hour. Lin Xue thrust her sword, aiming for his shadow. Kevin did a **McTwist** over her head, slapping her on the shoulder as he passed.

"Tag! You're it!"

For the first time in the history of the Azure Cloud Sect, the morning training sounded less like a war and more like... laughter.

But hidden in the shadows of the lower peaks, Young Master Chen watched with a spyglass. His face was covered in dried dough and healing salves.

"Float like a butterfly..." Chen muttered, his voice trembling with rage. "I will crush that bug. I will crush him flat."

He turned to his new henchman, a dark, cloaked figure radiating ominous green smoke.

"Is the **Soul-Eater Beast** ready?" Chen asked.

The cloaked figure nodded.

"Good," Chen hissed. "Let's see him dance his way out of this."
