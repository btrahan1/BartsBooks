# Chapter 17: The Nunchuck Nexus

Kevin landed on the platform, his boots making a satisfying *thud*. He was sweating, but he looked energized. The sparring session had been exhilarating. Lin Xue was a quick learner; she had almost clipped his ear with a frost-blade during a particularly aggressive barrel roll.

"A'ight babe," Kevin panted, wiping his forehead with his wristband. "That was solid. You're getting faster. But I realized something up there."

He mimed a quick strike, followed by a block.

"My hands are empty. I mean, I got the 'Guns'." He kissed his biceps. "But I need reach. I need rhythm."

He looked at Lin Xue seriously.

"We need to go visit that weapon dude again! He does great work, really understands the 'Rule of Cool'. But I need to up my Bruce Lee!"

Lin Xue sheathed her sword. "Bruce... Lee? Is this another ancient master?"

"The Grandmaster," Kevin said reverently. "The Dragon. And to honor his style, I need a specific tool. I need some numb chucks!"

Lin Xue frowned. "Numb... chucks? A weapon that numbs the enemy?"

"No, no. Nunchaku. Two sticks connected by a chain. It's the ultimate weapon of flow. It's basically a flail, but for people with style."

Back at the **Volcanic Forge Peak**, Elder Tie was busy polishing a massive war hammer. When he saw Kevin enter, he didn't scowl. He actually smiled.

" The Rider returns!" Tie boomed. "How fares the *Radical Shredder*?"

"She purrs like a kitten, Big Guy," Kevin said, giving the giant smith a fist bump (which felt like punching a brick wall). "But I'm here for a custom order. Something special."

Kevin pulled out his charcoal stick again. He drew two rectangles connected by a line.

"Two batons," Kevin explained. "Short. Heavy. Made of something dense. Maybe... **Star-Iron** core wrapped in **Spirit Wood** for grip?"

"Simple," Tie grunted. "Two clubs."

"Ah, but here's the kicker," Kevin tapped the drawing. "The chain. It needs to be flexible but unbreakable. And the bearings... they need to spin. Fast. I'm talking supersonic speeds."

Tie nodded, stroking his beard. "To create a rotational force that can deflect arrows... yes. I can forge the chain from the **vertebrae of a Void Snake**. It moves fluidly in all directions."

"Void Snake vertebrae. Metal," Kevin nodded approvingly. "And one last thing. They need to make a sound."

"Sound?"

"Yeah. When I swing 'em. Like a *Whoosh-Whip-Crack*. It's gotta sound intimidating. Sonic warfare."

Tie's eyes lit up. "I can hollow out the wood slightly and carve wind-runes inside. When spun, they will scream like a banshee."

"Music to my ears," Kevin said. "Let's bake this cake."

An hour later, Kevin held the weapon in his hands.

They were beautiful. Ebony wood handles with gold end-caps, connected by a shimmering, silver bone-chain. They felt perfectly balanced.

**\[SYSTEM UPDATE: WEAPON ACQUIRED\]** **\[ITEM: THE TWIN DRAGONS
(NUNCHAKU)\]** **\[CLASS: BLUNT / FLEXIBLE\]** **\[STATS: +80 SPEED, +50 CONFUSION\]** **\[RISK: 20% CHANCE TO HIT SELF IN FACE\]**

Kevin took a deep breath. He assumed the stance. Feet wide. One handle tucked under his armpit, the other held out in front.

He twitched his wrist.

WHOOSH.

The nunchucks blurred. Kevin spun them around his hand, over his shoulder, behind his back, and caught them under his other arm.

WHIP-CRACK.

The sound was incredible. A high-pitched, warbling howl that cut through the noise of the forge.

"Ki-Ya!" Kevin shouted.

He unleashed a flurry of strikes at an imaginary opponent.

Left! Right! Spin! Catch!

He moved with the chaotic, terrifying grace of a man who has watched
*Enter the Dragon* frame-by-frame since he was six years old.

Elder Tie watched, mesmerized. "It is... a continuous circle of violence. There is no beginning, no end. Only the spin."

Kevin finished with a dramatic pose, wrapping the chain around his neck and striking a thumbs-up.

"Perfect," Kevin said. "Thanks, Tie. Put it on my tab."

He turned to Lin Xue.

"Alright, Lin. I got the threads. I got the wheels. I got the sticks."

He lowered his sunglasses.

"I think we're ready for the sequel."

Suddenly, the ground shook.

A massive roar echoed from the valley below, a sound far deeper and hungrier than any forging hammer.

Green smoke began to rise from the direction of the main sect gate.

Kevin's HUD flickered red.

**\[WARNING: RAID BOSS APPROACHING.\]** **\[ENEMY TYPE: SOUL-EATER
BEAST.\]** **\[THREAT LEVEL:** "**GAME OVER**"**\]**

Kevin sighed. "Can't a guy just enjoy his new toys for five minutes?"

He spun the nunchucks one last time.

"Looks like the critics are here early."
