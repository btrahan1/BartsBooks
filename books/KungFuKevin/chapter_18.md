# Chapter 18: The Smackdown Symphony

The green smoke billowed over the mountain peaks, blotting out the sun. In the center of the toxic cloud, a creature emerged that looked like a cross between a dinosaur and a garbage disposal. It had three heads, scales made of rotting moss, and drool that sizzled when it hit the ground.

It was the **Soul-Eater Beast**. And it was currently stepping on the sect's flower garden.

"Oh, absolutely not," Kevin said, adjusting his dragon-scale vest. "Those petunias are endangered."

He turned to Lin Xue, who was staring at the beast with pale horror.

"Lin!" Kevin snapped his fingers. "Focus! You're the DJ. Find me a track for monster slaying! It's time to lay the smack down!"

Lin Xue blinked, snapping out of her fear. She reached into the magical boombox Kevin had strapped to her back (a modified artifact called the
*Box of Resonant Thunder*).

"A track... for slaying?" she murmured. She scrolled through the 'Mental Playlist' Kevin had tried to explain to her. "Heavy... Metal? Or perhaps... The Eye of the Tiger?"

"Too slow!" Kevin yelled, hopping onto his board. "I need intensity! I need adrenaline! Think *Doom*! Think *Electric Guitar Solos*!"

Lin Xue closed her eyes and tapped the rune.

**\[NOW PLAYING:** "**THUNDERSTRUCK**"** - AC/DC\]**

Ah-ah-ah-ah-ah-ah-ah-ah!

The guitar riff tore through the air, amplified by the sect's natural acoustics.

"Yes!" Kevin screamed. "That's the stuff!"

He looked at the beast. Then he raised one eyebrow, tilted his head, and sniffed the air dramatically.

"Can you smell..." Kevin bellowed, his voice echoing across the valley. "...what the Rock... is cooking!"

He kicked the tail of the *Radical Shredder*. The board shot forward.

Kevin didn't fly high. He flew low, skimming the ground, weaving between the panic-stricken disciples.

The Soul-Eater Beast roared, its three heads snapping at him.

SNAP. SNAP. SNAP.

Kevin dodged left. He dodged right. He did a kickflip over a snapping jaw.

"Too slow, ugly!"

He reached into his belt and pulled out **The Twin Dragons**.

WHOOSH-CRACK.

The nunchucks sang their banshee song.

Kevin flew straight toward the beast's massive leg.

"Kneecap check!"

As he passed the leg, he spun the nunchucks with furious speed. The Star-Iron baton smashed into the monster's knee joint.

CRUNCH.

The beast howled. It wasn't a lethal blow, but it hurt. It stumbled.

"Lin! Drop the bass!" Kevin shouted over the music.

Lin Xue, hovering safely above, channeled her Qi into the boombox. The volume spiked.

THUNDER!

The sonic wave hit the beast, disorienting it.

Kevin used the distraction. He flew up the monster's back, grinding along its spinal ridges like a skateboard ramp.

SCREEEEEECH.

The blue underglow of his board burned a neon line up the monster's scales.

He reached the shoulders. The three heads turned to look at him.

"Sup, fellas," Kevin said.

He leaped off the board.

Mid-air, he unleashed the **Helicopter of Pain**.

He spun the nunchucks in a blur, striking all three heads in rapid succession.

BONK. BONK. BONK.

"That's for the flowers! That's for the bad breath! And that's just because I felt like it!"

He landed back on his board as it looped around to catch him.

But the beast was tough. It shook its heads and unleashed a breath of green poison gas.

**\[WARNING: TOXIC CLOUD.\]** **\[AVIATORS DEFENSE: ACTIVATED.\]**

"My shades block UV *and* halitosis!" Kevin yelled, diving through the cloud unharmed.

However, he spotted something on the ground. Young Master Chen was standing on a nearby ridge, controlling the beast with a glowing black amulet.

"Aha!" Kevin pointed a nunchuck at him. "The controller is unplugged!"

Kevin banked hard. He wasn't aiming for the monster anymore. He was aiming for the gamer.

"Hey Chen!" Kevin shouted. "Check out this combo move!"

He accelerated to max speed. He hit a small rock, using it as a ramp.

He launched into the air, flying directly at Chen.

"Ki-Ya!"

Kevin prepared the **Flying Dragon Kick**.

But at the last second, he realized a kick was too simple.

He grabbed the nunchucks with both hands, wrapping the chain around the front of his board. He pulled back, tensioning the chain like a slingshot.

"Eat wheels!"

He released the tension, swinging the board around in a massive arc.

The *Radical Shredder* slammed into the glowing amulet in Chen's hand.

SMASH.

The amulet shattered into a thousand pieces.

The Soul-Eater Beast froze. Its eyes went dull. It looked around, confused, like a dog that forgot why it walked into a room. Then, it sat down and started scratching its ear.

Chen stared at his empty hand. He looked up at Kevin, who was hovering above him, spinning his nunchucks casually.

"Game Over, man," Kevin said. "Game Over."

The music faded out on a final power chord.

Kevin landed. He walked up to the terrified Young Master Chen.

He leaned in close.

"Now," Kevin whispered. "Go to your room. And don't come out until you learn how to play nice."

He turned to Lin Xue, who was descending like an angel of rock and roll.

"How was that for a smackdown?" Kevin asked, grinning.

Lin Xue looked at the subdued monster, the shattered amulet, and the crying villain.

"It was..." she paused, searching for the right words. "It was... Totally Tubu-lar?"

Kevin froze. A tear rolled down his cheek beneath his sunglasses.

"She speaks the language," Kevin choked out. "She speaks the language of the Gods."

He threw an arm around her.

"Come on, DJ. Let's go get some pizza. I'm starving."
