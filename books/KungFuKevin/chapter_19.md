# Chapter 19: Road to the Kumite

Sect Leader Chen rubbed his temples. He stood amidst the wreckage of the training ground, looking at the unconscious form of his son (who was still covered in dough) and the Soul-Eater Beast (which was currently chasing its own tail like a giant, mossy puppy).

"I should be angry," the Sect Leader muttered. "I should execute someone. But... the beast is tamed. My son is alive. And the pizza... smells delicious."

He turned to Kevin, who was leaning against a pillar, cleaning his nunchucks with a silk handkerchief.

"Elder Kevin," the Sect Leader boomed. "You have saved the Sect from embarrassment. But a greater threat looms."

Kevin looked up over his obsidian shades. "Aliens? Zombies? The IRS?"

"The **Grand Celestial Tournament**," the Sect Leader announced gravely. "Every ten years, the Five Great Sects gather to test their disciples. It is a brutal contest of strength, will, and survival. The **Iron Fist Sect** has vowed to destroy us this year."

Kevin's eyes widened behind the sunglasses. He stood up straight, the wind catching his dragon-scale vest.

"Tournament?" Kevin whispered, a smile spreading across his face that was wider than the Grand Canyon.

He spun his nunchucks and tucked them under his armpit.

"I love tournaments! The brackets! The underdogs! The montage where I get beaten up but then find the inner strength to win at the last second!"

He grabbed Lin Xue by the shoulders.

"Lin! This is it! This is my *Bloodsport*. My *Mortal Kombat*. My
*Karate Kid Part III*!"

He struck a pose, flexing his arms.

"I can be the dude of dudes! The King of the Ring! The Sultan of Swat!"

He looked at his legs.

"Van Damme..." Kevin muttered. "I must channel the spirit of the Muscles from Brussels."

He dropped into a split.

RIIIIIP.

Luckily, the **Pants of the Hammer** were immune to ripping. Kevin sank all the way to the ground, wincing only slightly as his hamstrings screamed in protest.

"Let's rock and roll!" Kevin shouted from the ground. "Where do I sign up? Is there a weight class? I'm a featherweight with the heart of a heavyweight!"

Lin Xue looked concerned. "Kevin, this is not a game. The Iron Fist Sect... they dip their hands in molten metal to harden them. They kill their opponents."

"Molten metal hands?" Kevin scoffed, standing up (with some difficulty). "Sounds like they need moisturizer. Don't worry, Babe. I got a secret weapon."

**\[SYSTEM ALERT: TOURNAMENT ARC INITIATED.\]** **\[QUEST:** "**ENTER
THE KUMITE**"**\]** **\[OBJECTIVE: DON'T DIE IN THE FIRST ROUND.\]**
**\[SECRET OBJECTIVE: WIN USING ONLY 80s MOVIE QUOTES.\]**

"Come on," Kevin said, jumping onto his *Radical Shredder*. "We need to go to the Registration Hall. I need to pick my fighting name. 'Kung Fu Kevin' is good, but I need something punchier. Maybe ' The American Ninja'? No, that's taken."

"How about 'The White Lotus of Chaos'?" Lin Xue suggested, actually trying to help.

"Too flowery," Kevin dismissed. "I'm thinking... **The Kumite Kid**."

**The Registration Hall** was packed. Disciples from rival sects were
posturing, glowing with Qi, and staring each other down.

There were guys with giant axes. Women with poison snakes wrapped around their necks. And a group of massive, shirtless men with hands that looked like anvils, the Iron Fist Sect.

Kevin strolled in, his board under his arm, his aviators reflecting the room.

The chatter stopped. Everyone looked at the guy in the black vest and sunglasses.

One of the Iron Fist disciples, a guy the size of a vending machine named
**Boulder**, stepped in Kevin's way.

"You look soft," Boulder grunted, cracking his knuckles. "Do you even cultivate?"

Kevin looked up at the giant. He smirked.

"I cultivate pure awesomeness, my friend," Kevin said.

He poked Boulder in the chest.

"And you," Kevin whispered, channeling his inner Bruce Lee. "You have offended my family. And you have offended the Shaolin Temple." (Note: Kevin had no family here, and the Shaolin Temple was in another dimension).

Boulder frowned. "What is a Shaolin?"

"It's a place where we break bricks with our heads," Kevin said. "Observe."

Kevin looked around for a brick. He saw a stone tablet on the registration table.

"Ki-Ya!"

Kevin slammed his head onto the stone tablet.

THUD.

It hurt. It hurt a lot. Birds circled Kevin's vision. The tablet did not break.

However, the **System** intervened.

**\[GLITCH DETECTED: USER ATTEMPTING IMPOSSIBLE FEAT.\]** **\[APPLYING**
"**PROP PHYSICS**"**.\]**

Suddenly, the solid stone tablet crumbled into dust as if it were made of styrofoam.

The room gasped.

*"He pulverized the Ancestral Stone with a headbutt!"* someone screamed.
*"His skull is harder than diamond!"*

Kevin shook his head, clearing the dizziness. He pointed a shaking finger at Boulder.

"Boards... don't hit back."

Boulder took a step back, terrified.

Kevin turned to the registrar, a trembling old man.

"Sign me up," Kevin said, leaning on the desk. "Name: **Jean-Claude Van Kevin**."

He looked back at Lin Xue and winked.

"Let the games begin."
