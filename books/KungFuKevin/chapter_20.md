# Chapter 20: Blind Fury

The Grand Celestial Tournament Arena was a colossal coliseum floating on a cloud island. Thousands of spectators from the Five Great Sects filled the stands, their cheers sounding like thunder.

In the center of the ring stood **Jean-Claude Van Kevin**.

He was facing not one, but *ten* disciples from the Iron Fist Sect. They had surrounded him, their metal-hardened hands glowing red with heat. They looked angry. They looked lethal.

Kevin looked bored.

He yawned, stretching his arms high above his head.

"Is this it?" Kevin called out to the judges' box. "I asked for a challenge, not a warm-up session! This doesn't even seem fair."

The Iron Fist disciples growled. "Arrogant fool! We will melt your bones!"

Kevin held up a hand. "Stop! Collaborate and listen!"

He pointed to a female disciple in the front row of the audience. She was wearing a lovely red silk scarf.

"You there! Give me your scarf!"

The disciple, blushing furiously, threw the scarf into the ring. Kevin caught it with a flourish.

"You think your eyes are your greatest weapon?" Kevin shouted, pacing the ring. "Wrong! Your eyes deceive you. Don't trust them."

He wrapped the red silk scarf around his head, covering his obsidian sunglasses.

"Tie this around my eyes!" Kevin declared as he knotted it tight at the back of his head. "I will fight all of you! Blind!"

The crowd went insane. *"He is mocking them!"* *"No! He is entering the State of the Mind's Eye!"* *"He is insane!"*

Lin Xue, watching from the VIP box, buried her face in her hands. "Kevin... why must you always raise the difficulty setting?"

Kevin stood in the center of the circle, blindfolded. He couldn't see anything. It was dark.

*Okay, Kevin,* he thought. *This seemed like a good idea a second ago.
Now I can't see the giant lava-hands coming at me. Time to rely on the Force. Or echolocation.*

He tilted his head.

"Marco!" Kevin shouted.

The Iron Fist disciples looked at each other, confused.

"Polo!" one of them whispered reflexively.

Kevin spun toward the sound.

**\[SYSTEM ACTIVATING: DAREDEVIL MODE\]** **\[SONAR DETECTED. TARGET
LOCK: 4 O'CLOCK.\]**

"Found you!"

Kevin launched into a **Blind Tornado Kick**.

He spun blindly, his leg extended. By sheer luck (and System manipulation), the disciple who had whispered "Polo" had just stepped forward to attack.

Kevin's heel connected with the disciple's jaw.

POW.

The disciple went flying out of the ring.

"One down!" Kevin yelled. "Who's next? I can smell your fear! It smells like... burnt toast and regret!"

The remaining nine disciples charged at once.

"Kill him!"

Kevin stood still. He waited until he felt the wind of their movement.

"Wax on! Wax off!"

Kevin began to wave his hands in frantic circles. He blocked a punch to his left. He parried a kick to his right.

He was using the **Miyagi Defense**.

Block. Parry. Slap.

He slapped a disciple in the face. "No touching the hair!"

He ducked a sweep. "Low bridge!"

He grabbed the arm of one attacker and the leg of another.

"Dosi-do!"

Kevin spun them around, causing them to crash into each other. Their metal hands collided with a loud *CLANG*, knocking them both unconscious.

"Three down! Four down!"

The crowd was roaring so loud the floating island was vibrating.

Kevin was in the zone. He couldn't see, but he felt the rhythm. The rhythm of the fight.

Bam-ba-lam! Whoa, Black Betty!

He started dancing. He dodged punches by doing the **Moonwalk**. He avoided kicks by dropping into the **Worm**.

"Can't touch this!"

Finally, only the leader of the group remained. The massive disciple named **Boulder** (from earlier).

Boulder was furious. He charged up a massive fireball in his hands.

"I will incinerate you!" Boulder screamed.

Kevin stopped dancing. He turned his blindfolded face toward Boulder.

"You talk too much," Kevin said.

He reached into his pocket and pulled out a handful of **Thunder Pizza** crusts he had saved for a snack.

"Pocket Pizza!"

He threw the crusts.

They hit Boulder in the face. The spicy sauce got in his eyes. The lightning cheese zapped his nose.

"GAAAH! MY EYES!" Boulder screamed, clutching his face.

"Now we're even!" Kevin shouted. "We're both blind!"

Kevin ran forward. He didn't know exactly where Boulder was, but he followed the screaming.

"Crane Kick!"

Kevin stopped, raised one leg, spread his arms, and hopped.

He unleashed the kick.

His foot connected with Boulder's stomach.

OOF.

Boulder doubled over.

Kevin followed up with a **Double-Fist Hammer Blow** to the back.

Boulder collapsed face-first into the dirt.

Silence.

Kevin stood over the pile of defeated enemies. He adjusted the blindfold.

"Is it over?" Kevin asked the silence. "Did I win? Or did everyone just leave?"

The announcer's voice boomed, shaking the heavens.

"WINNER! JEAN-CLAUDE VAN KEVIN OF THE AZURE CLOUD SECT!"

The crowd exploded.

Kevin grinned. He untied the blindfold and whipped it off, revealing his sunglasses underneath.

He raised his arms in victory.

"I told you!" Kevin shouted to the cheering masses. "You don't need eyes to see victory! You just need... GUTS!"

He turned to the judges.

"Now, where is my trophy? And does it come with a cash prize? Mama needs a new pair of shoes!"

Lin Xue watched him, shaking her head, a smile finally breaking through her icy facade.

"He is an idiot," she whispered. "But he is *our* idiot."

Kevin basked in the glory. He felt the Dao of the Blockbuster flowing through him.

He was Kung Fu Kevin. And he was just getting started.
