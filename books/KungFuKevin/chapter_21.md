# Chapter 21: The Swole-ivation Begins

The roar of the tournament crowd had faded, replaced by the eerie silence of the **Ten-Ton Gravity Chamber**.

It was a cavern deep beneath the mountain, lit only by glowing red runes etched into the obsidian walls. The air was thick, heavy, and smelled like old iron and despair.

Kevin stood in the center of the room, shirtless, wearing only his new
*Pants of the Hammer* and his obsidian aviators. He was examining
himself in a polished bronze shield leaning against the wall.

"All righty, Babe," Kevin said, flexing a bicep that was... respectable, but not exactly threatening to tear any fabric. "That tournament was fun and all. I styled on 'em. But I think it's time to get serious."

He poked his stomach. It rippled slightly. Not from muscle, but from the three Thunder Pizzas he had eaten to celebrate his victory.

"I think you said something about some gravitation chamber or something? And some muscle-building pills?" Kevin turned to Lin Xue, who was standing by the control panel, looking amused.

"I know you dig this..." Kevin gestured vaguely to his 'physique' with a double finger-gun motion. "The 'Lean Mean Pizza Machine' look. But I think I could add a few pounds. Get that *superhero* density. What do you think?"

Lin Xue looked him up and down. "You defeated ten men while blindfolded. Your technique is... unorthodox. But your physical vessel is indeed fragile. If Boulder had connected with that fireball, you would be ash."

She walked over to a stone pedestal holding a small jade jar.

"These are the **Marrow-Cleansing Bone-Forging Pills**," she explained. "They are made from the ground bones of a Diamond Ape. They are incredibly painful to consume. They tear your muscles apart and rebuild them instantly."

"Sounds like a intense pre-workout," Kevin nodded. "I'll take two."

"And this," she gestured to the room, "is the Gravity Array. Currently, it is set to 1x gravity. I can increase it to crush a boulder into dust."

Kevin cracked his knuckles. He grabbed the jade jar and popped the cork.

"Crank it up, Lin," Kevin said, dumping a handful of the pills into his mouth. They tasted like chalk and burning tires. He swallowed them dry. "Let's go for... Hyperbolic Time Chamber levels."

"I will start at 2x," Lin Xue said cautiously, turning the dial.

HUMMMMMM.

The red runes flared. The air shimmered.

Kevin felt his knees buckle. It felt like someone had suddenly strapped a refrigerator to his back. His blood felt like mercury.

"Oof," Kevin grunted, forcing his legs to straighten. "Okay. That's heavy. That's... that's good."

**\[SYSTEM ALERT: GRAVITY INCREASE DETECTED.\]** **\[MUSCLE FIBER
TEARING IMMINENT.\]** **\[ACTIVATING** "**TRAINING MONTAGE**"
**PROTOCOL.\]**

"Music!" Kevin wheezed. "I need the beat!"

Lin Xue, already anticipating this, hit the play button on the boombox.

**\[NOW PLAYING:** "**GONNA FLY NOW**"** - ROCKY THEME\]**

Dun-dun-dun... Dun-dun-dun...

Kevin's eyes lit up behind his shades. The pain transformed into fuel.

"Yo Adrian!" Kevin shouted (strained). "I did it!"

He dropped to the floor.

"Push-ups!"

He lowered himself. The gravity tried to smash his face into the stone. He pushed back.

One.

His arms shook. The pills in his stomach exploded with heat, burning through his veins.

Two.

"Lin! Increase the weight!" Kevin screamed.

"Kevin, you will die!"

"Do it! No pain, no gain!"

Lin Xue bit her lip and turned the dial. **3x Gravity.**

*CRACK.* The floor beneath Kevin's hands spiderwebbed.

Kevin roared. He didn't collapse. He pushed harder.

He started doing burpees.

Down. Kick back. Push up. Jump.

With every jump, he barely left the ground, but the effort was Herculean. Sweat poured off him, evaporating instantly on his superheated skin.

He moved to the corner where a massive iron block sat.

"Deadlifts!"

He grabbed the block. It weighed five hundred pounds normally. In here, it was fifteen hundred.

He strained. His veins bulged like garden hoses. His face turned purple.

"LIGHT WEIGHT, BABY!" Kevin screamed, quoting Ronnie Coleman. "AIN'T NOTHIN' BUT A PEANUT!"

He lifted the block. He slammed it down.

He did it again.

Hours passed. The music cycled. *Eye of the Tiger. You're the Best Around. Maniac.*

Kevin's body was changing. The Diamond Ape pills were working. His muscles were tearing, healing, tearing, and healing thicker. Harder.

His skin began to take on a faint, metallic sheen.

Lin Xue watched in awe. *He is not using Qi to reinforce his body. He is using pure will. He is torturing himself into greatness.*

Finally, Kevin collapsed. The music stopped.

Lin Xue rushed over and deactivated the gravity.

Kevin lay on the floor in a pool of sweat. He was gasping for air.

But as he sat up, something was different.

His shoulders were broader. His chest was thicker. His abs were no longer a suggestion; they were a topographical map of granite.

He stood up. He felt heavy, but powerful. Like a tank.

He walked over to the bronze mirror.

He flexed.

RIIIIIIP.

This time, the Dragon-Scale Vest couldn't hold it. The seams popped.

Kevin grinned. He looked at Lin Xue.

"How's that for gains?" Kevin asked, his voice deeper, resonating in his chest.

He struck a pose.

"I think I'm ready for the beach episode."
