# Chapter 22: The Beach Bod and the Chicken Chase

Kevin peeled off the remnants of his dragon-scale vest, which now looked more like a dragon-scale bib. He tossed the shredded leather to Lin Xue.

"Take these to be repaired," Kevin said, flexing his pecs individually.
*Left. Right. Left. Right.* "And bring me some tights. Spandex. Lycra.
Something that appreciates the glutes. And some chickens."

Lin Xue caught the vest, blinking. "Chickens? For... eating? Or for another pizza experiment?"

"No, Babe. Live chickens. Fast ones. I gotta do the beach scene."

He walked toward the exit of the gravity chamber, the heavy iron door screeching as he pushed it open with one hand.

"Babe, cue 'Gonna Fly Now'!" Kevin commanded. "We're doing this old school. Sly, lend me your strength, and speed!"

**The Azure Cloud Sect's Private Beach** (actually the shore of the
**Spirit Lake**) was a serene place. The water was crystal clear, the
sand was white diamond dust, and giant lotus flowers floated peacefully.

That peace was shattered by a trumpet fanfare.

Dun-dun-dun-DAAAAAH!

Kevin burst onto the beach. He was wearing a pair of neon pink compression shorts (Lin Xue had to specially order them from a confused silk worm farmer) and nothing else. His skin glistened with sweat and oil.

Behind him, Lin Xue released a cage full of **Wind-Chaser Roosters**. These were Spirit Beasts known for running so fast they could traverse water.

"Go!" Kevin shouted.

The chickens exploded out of the cage. They didn't run; they blurred.

Kevin sprinted after them.

The sand exploded under his feet. He wasn't using lightness techniques. He was using raw, explosive power.

"Come here, nuggets!"

He dove for a rooster. It zig-zagged. Kevin planted his foot, tearing a trench in the sand, and pivoted. His agility, honed on the *Radical Shredder*, combined with his new strength was terrifying.

He grabbed the rooster with one hand.

"Gotcha!"

He tossed it (gently) into a coop and went for the next one.

He ran through the shallow water, his legs churning like pistons, creating a wake that capsized a nearby duck.

He did one-armed push-ups in the surf as the waves crashed over him.

He punched the water. *Splash. Splash. Splash.*

"Adriaaaaaan!" Kevin screamed at the horizon.

Disciples gathered on the cliffs above, watching.

*"Is he... fighting the ocean?"* one whispered. *"No. He is training his
spirit against the relentless tide."* *"Why is he wearing pink small-clothes?"* *"Do not question the Dao of the Master."*

After an hour, all ten Wind-Chaser Roosters were back in the coop. Kevin stood knee-deep in the lake, chest heaving, water dripping from his muscles.

He felt incredible. He felt like he could punch a hole in a mountain.

He waded out of the water, shaking his hair like a wet dog.

Lin Xue was waiting on the beach with a towel and a protein shake (Spirit Soy Milk and Raw Eggs).

"You caught them all," Lin Xue noted, impressed. "Those roosters are faster than Foundation Establishment cultivators."

"Speed is key, Lin," Kevin said, chugging the shake. "Speed and power."

He wiped his mouth.

"But we're not done. The beach scene isn't complete without the slo-mo run."

Kevin turned and looked down the long stretch of white sand.

"System," Kevin muttered. "Activate Cinematic Mode. Frame rate: 24fps. Play *Chariots of Fire*."

He began to jog. Slowly.

To Lin Xue, it looked like he was just... running weirdly. Taking long, exaggerated steps. Making a weird face of intense concentration.

But in Kevin's mind, it was glorious. The synthesized music swelled. His hair bounced in slow motion. The water droplets flew off him like diamonds.

He ran toward the sunset.

"I am infinite," Kevin whispered. "I am... Kevin."

He tripped on a crab.

OOF.

The slow-motion spell broke. Kevin face-planted into the sand.

He lay there for a second.

"Cut!" Kevin yelled into the sand. "We'll fix it in post!"

He rolled over and looked up at Lin Xue, who was trying very hard not to laugh.

"Help me up, Babe. I think I got sand in places sand should never be."
