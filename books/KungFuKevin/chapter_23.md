# Chapter 23: The Wing Commander

Kevin limped into the kitchen, dusting sand off his pink compression shorts. He was carrying two crates of squawking, panicked Wind-Chaser Roosters.

"Chef!" Kevin bellowed, kicking the door open. "Clear the decks! It's time for innovation!"

Chef Wang looked up from a pot of simmering tortoise soup. He eyed the frantic chickens and Kevin's attire.

"Elder Kevin," Wang said, bowing respectfully but keeping his eyes averted from the neon shorts. "More... pizza?"

"No, Wang. We've saturated the pizza market. We need diversity. We need finger food. We need..." Kevin slammed the crates onto the prep table. "...Chicken Wings!"

Wang peered into the crate. "You wish to cook the Wind-Chaser Roosters? Their meat is tough. Stringy. It is meant for soup stock."

"That's because you're boiling it, man!" Kevin shook his head. "You gotta fry it. Deep fry. Double fry. Make it crispy enough to shatter teeth."

Kevin began raiding the pantry.

"Use my spoils from the beach! And bring the hot sauce! We gonna make a killin' on these!"

**\[SYSTEM QUEST:** "**LORD OF THE WINGS**"**\]** **\[OBJECTIVE: CREATE
THE PERFECT BUFFALO WING.\]** **\[HIDDEN OBJECTIVE: DON'T CAUSE A GREASE FIRE.\]**

Kevin and Wang got to work.

"First," Kevin instructed, wielding a cleaver. "We separate the flats from the drums. Nobody likes the tips. Throw those in your soup."

Chop. Chop. Chop.

"Now, the breading. Flour. Salt. Pepper. And..." Kevin produced a pouch of red powder. "Dragon-Chili Dust."

"That is used to make explosives," Wang noted.

"It adds kick," Kevin said.

They tossed the wings in the explosive flour mixture.

"Now, the oil. Get that cauldron bubbling!"

Wang stoked the magical fire under a massive wok filled with **Golden Peanut Oil**.

Kevin dropped the wings in.

SIZZLE.

The sound was like rain on a tin roof. The smell of frying chicken filled the air, a scent so pure and holy that a passing monk outside stopped chanting and started drooling.

"While those cook," Kevin said, grabbing a saucepan. "The sauce."

"Butter. Lots of it. Melt it down. Add the vinegar. Add the chili paste."

Kevin stirred the bubbling red concoction. He dipped a pinky in and tasted it.

His face turned purple. His eyes watered. He coughed.

"Perfect," Kevin rasped. "It hurts so good."

The wings came out golden brown and crispy. Kevin tossed them in a metal bowl with the sauce.

Shake. Shake. Shake.

The wings were coated in a glistening, angry red glaze.

"Plating," Kevin ordered. "We need a cooling agent. Celery sticks? Blue cheese?"

"We have **Ice-Stalk Celery**," Wang offered. "And the moldy cheese of the Yak."

"Close enough."

Kevin arranged a dozen wings on a platter. He carried them out to the courtyard where Lin Xue was waiting (still wearing her "BAD BOY" headband).

"Sample time," Kevin announced. "Be careful. These are nuclear."

Lin Xue picked up a wing. She examined the red glaze.

"It smells of... violence," she said.

She took a bite.

CRUNCH.

The crispy skin gave way to the juicy meat. Then the heat hit.

Lin Xue's eyes went wide. She grabbed a stick of Ice-Stalk Celery and crunched it frantically.

"Hot! Hot!" she gasped. "But... addictive! The pain... it clears the sinuses! My Qi is circulating faster to combat the heat!"

"That's the capsaicin rush, babe," Kevin grinned, popping a whole wing into his mouth and stripping the meat off the bone in one motion.

"Chef Wang!" Kevin yelled back into the kitchen. "Start the franchise! We're calling it **Kevin's Killer Wings**! Slogan: 'They'll blow your mind... and your tastebuds!'"

He turned to Lin Xue.

"We need a delivery system. The board is fast, but we need volume. Can we train those Spirit Cranes to carry buckets?"

Lin Xue wiped sauce from her lip. "To use sacred cranes for food delivery... it is heresy."

She reached for another wing.

"But... they *are* fast."

Kevin laughed. "Capitalism, Lin. It conquers all. Even the Dao."

He looked at the pile of bones.

"Now," Kevin said, patting his stomach. "I need a napkin. And maybe a fire extinguisher for my mouth."
