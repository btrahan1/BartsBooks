# Chapter 24: The Godfather of the Golden Crust

The sun was setting over Shattered Moon Peak, casting long shadows across the courtyard. It was a peaceful evening, punctuated only by the clinking of Spirit Stones.

Lots of Spirit Stones.

Kevin sat at a stone table, now covered in a velvet cloth (black, naturally). He was still wearing his aviators, despite the twilight.

Lin Xue sat to his right, efficiently stacking the glowing blue stones into neat towers of ten.

Chef Wang sat to his left, looking exhausted but ecstatic, his white hat slightly askew.

And in the center of the table lay a massive pile of wealth.

"Alright," Kevin said, leaning back in his legendary bean bag chair (which he had dragged outside). "Let's review the quarterly earnings. The Pizza franchise is steady. The Wings expansion? Through the roof. We sold out in twenty minutes at the Outer Sect alone."

"The disciples are addicted," Lin Xue reported, her voice professional. "They claim the spice helps them stay awake during night meditations. We have pre-orders for three hundred buckets tomorrow."

"Beautiful," Kevin nodded. "Supply and demand, baby."

He reached out and began to divide the pile.

"Okay. The split."

He pushed a large pile toward Chef Wang. "Wang, this is for operations. Ingredients. Staffing. Maybe buy yourself a new ladle. A gold one."

Wang bowed, his eyes shining. "Thank you, Boss Kevin. I shall name my firstborn after the Pepperoni."

Kevin pushed another pile toward Lin Xue. "Lin, this is for management. Marketing. And hazard pay for dealing with me. Buy yourself something nice. Maybe a sword that doesn't look like an icicle."

Lin Xue touched the stones. "I will use this to repair the Sect's defensive arrays. And... perhaps some new ribbons."

Kevin pulled his own pile toward him. "And this is for the Visionary. R&D. And maintaining the 'Action Jeans' inventory."

He looked at the remaining pile in the center. It was substantial.

"And finally," Kevin said, a smirk playing on his lips. "The tax."

He looked up toward the floating Obsidian Palace high above them, where Sect Leader Chen lived.

"Four-way split," Kevin declared. "One for the 'Big Guy'."

He picked up a heavy leather bag and swept the remaining stones into it.

"You gotta pay the Don," Kevin whispered in a raspy voice, doing his best Marlon Brando impression. "I'm gonna make him an offer he can't refuse."

He tossed the bag to Lin Xue.

"Lin, deliver the package. Tell him it's a 'consulting fee'. And include a Supreme Meat-Lover's Pizza. Extra olives. He looks like an olive guy."

Lin Xue took the bag. "You are bribing the Sect Leader."

"It's not a bribe," Kevin corrected, pointing a finger. "It's *profit sharing*. We're partners. Silent partners. Very silent."

He stood up and stretched.

"Man, being a tycoon is tiring. But we're not done yet."

He walked to the edge of the cliff and looked out over the sect. Lights were flickering on in the disciple dormitories. He could almost smell the faint scent of garlic knots drifting on the wind.

"We conquered the stomach," Kevin said. "We conquered the tournament. We conquered the wardrobe."

He turned back to his crew.

"But I feel like we're missing something. Every great movie has a montage where the hero builds something. A base. A fortress."

He looked at the dilapidated pagoda behind him.

"This place is still a dump. I want renovations. I want a hot tub. I want a statue of me out front doing a kickflip."

Chef Wang stood up. "I know a mason who owes me a favor."

Lin Xue stood up. "I can inscribe runes for climate control."

Kevin grinned. He put his arm around both of them.

"Then what are we waiting for?"

He pulled out his nunchucks and spun them once.

"Let's build the **Dojo of Dopeness**."
