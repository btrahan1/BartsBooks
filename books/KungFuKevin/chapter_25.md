# Chapter 25: The Art of the Awkward Yawn

The **Dojo of Dopeness** was complete.

It was... spectacular. In the way a car crash involving a disco ball and a neon sign factory is spectacular.

The ancient, rotting pagoda had been transformed. The wood was polished to a shine. The paper windows were replaced with translucent purple crystal that gave the whole room a "Miami Vice" nightclub vibe.

In the center of the courtyard stood a ten-foot marble statue of Kevin. He was mid-air, on his board, doing a kickflip while holding a slice of pizza. It was majestic.

Kevin stood on the balcony overlooking his domain. He was wearing a silk smoking jacket (black and gold dragon pattern) over his action jeans. He held a goblet of sparkling grape juice (fermented Spirit Grapes).

He looked at the view. Then he looked at Lin Xue.

She was leaning against the railing, the wind playing with her hair. She looked radiant. Dangerous, deadly, and absolutely terrifyingly pretty.

Kevin swallowed hard. It sounded like a golf ball moving down a drainpipe. *Gulp.*

*Okay, Kevin,* he thought, his palms sweating against the crystal
goblet. *You fought a Soul-Eater Beast. You humiliated a Young Master. You invented pizza. You can do this.*

He cleared his throat. It came out as a squeak. He tried again, dropping his voice an octave.

"So," Kevin said, leaning casually against the railing and missing his mark slightly, causing his elbow to slip. He recovered with a clumsy stumble. "Nice view, huh? Almost as nice as... the view right here."

He pointed finger-guns at her.

Lin Xue looked at the landscape, then back at him. "The geography is standard for a floating mountain, Elder Kevin. Though the neon lights you installed do illuminate the nocturnal bats quite effectively."

"Yeah, yeah, the bats love it," Kevin mumbled. He shuffled closer.

He could feel his heart hammering against his ribs. *Ba-dump. Ba-dump.*

"Listen, Lin," Kevin said, swirling his grape juice. "We've been through a lot lately. The training. The fighting. The culinary revolution. We make a pretty good team. Like... *Turner and Hooch*. Or *Tango and Cash*."

"I am unfamiliar with these clans," Lin Xue said. "But our combat synergy is... adequate. Your chaotic style complements my precision."

"Synergy. Right," Kevin nodded. "But I was thinking... maybe we could be more than just business partners. You know?"

He took a step closer. The scent of her, winter rain and lotus, was overpowering.

"I mean," Kevin stammered, losing his cool facade entirely. "You're... you're pretty radical, Lin. Like, totally tubular. You got that... that
*je ne sais quoi*."

Lin Xue tilted her head. "I do not speak the tongue of the demons. What is *je ne sais quoi*?"

"It means..." Kevin panicked. "It means you kick butt. And you look good doing it."

He looked at her shoulders. They were right there.

*Do the move, Kevin,* his brain screamed. *The classic move.*

Kevin unleashed a massive, fake yawn.

"YAAAAAAWN. Man, being a tycoon is exhausting."

He stretched his arms out wide. And then, with the grace of a rusty crane, he dropped his right arm.

His hand landed on her shoulder.

Lin Xue stiffened. Her natural instinct was to flip him over the railing and break his arm in three places.

But she didn't.

She looked at the hand on her shoulder. It was warm. Heavy.

"Kevin," she said slowly. "Are you... attempting to transfer Qi to me? Is your shoulder injured?"

"No," Kevin squeaked. "Just... resting. It's a... support thing. You know. Lean on me. Bill Withers style."

He waited for the rejection. He waited for the icy glare.

Instead, Lin Xue relaxed. Just a fraction of an inch. She leaned back, ever so slightly, into the crook of his arm.

"It is... acceptable," she whispered. "The wind is cold tonight."

Kevin's internal monologue exploded in fireworks.

**\[SYSTEM ACHIEVEMENT UNLOCKED: THE SMOOTH OPERATOR\]** **\[ROMANCE
LEVEL: HOLDING PATTERN\]** **\[MUSIC QUEUE:** "**TAKE MY BREATH AWAY**"** - BERLIN\]**

Kevin grinned. He squeezed her shoulder gently.

"Yeah," Kevin said, his voice finally finding its smooth rhythm again. "Cold wind. Good thing I'm radiating pure machismo heat."

He looked up at the twin moons of this strange world.

"Hey, Lin?"

"Yes, Kevin?"

"You think they have drive-in movies in this world? Because if not... I think I just found our next project."

Lin Xue looked at him, her eyes reflecting the purple neon of the dojo. For the first time, she smiled, a real, genuine smile.

"I do not know what a 'drive-in' is," she said softly. "But if it involves your strange snacks and sitting like this... I am willing to learn."

Kevin took a sip of his grape juice.

"Groovy," he whispered. "Totally groovy."
