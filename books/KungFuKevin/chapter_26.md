# Chapter 26: The Box Office Smash

The next morning, Kevin stood in the center of the Shattered Moon Peak courtyard, now renamed **The Starlit Plaza**, with a megaphone made from a rolled-up scroll and a minor amplification rune.

"Alright people, listen up!" Kevin shouted. "We've fed the masses. We've clothed the masses. Now, we are going to *entertain* the masses!"

Chef Wang, wearing a new gold-threaded apron, nodded enthusiastically. Lin Xue, wearing her "BAD BOY" headband (she rarely took it off now), stood ready with a clipboard.

"Operation Entertainment is a go!" Kevin announced. "We are building...
**The Drive-In Sword Theatre**!"

He swept his hand across the horizon.

"Picture this: Disciples flying in on their swords, hovering in rows. A massive illusion array projecting images in the sky. It's the ultimate date night!"

"We do not have 'movies'," Lin Xue pointed out. "We cannot project recorded images with such clarity."

"Who needs recordings?" Kevin scoffed. "We're going **Live**, baby! Live Action Plays! Kung Fu Demonstrations! We'll hire disciples to act out the great legends! And for the romance lovers..."

Kevin winked. "Cultivation Love Poetry. The mushy stuff. '*Oh, Junior Sister, your eyes are like the Nine Nether Lakes.*' That garbage sells like hotcakes!"

"And snacks?" Chef Wang asked, his eyes gleaming.

"Glad you asked, Chef." Kevin pulled out a bag of kernels. Not normal corn. These were **Spirit Ember Kernels**.

"Dragon Fire Popcorn," Kevin whispered. "Popped in the breath of a Wyvern. Dusted with sweet moon-sugar and spicy salt. It's light. It's airy. It's dangerous."

The construction began immediately.

Kevin directed the terraforming. They carved a massive amphitheater into the side of the mountain. They set up "Parking Rows", floating jade rings where cultivators could dock their flying swords.

For the "Screen", Kevin hired a team of Illusion Masters.

"Bigger!" Kevin yelled at them. "I want that illusion to be fifty feet tall! If the hero cries, I want the audience to drown in tears!"

They built a stage. They built a concession stand that looked like a pagoda but smelled like butter.

Kevin held auditions for the actors.

"Next!" Kevin shouted from his director's chair (the bean bag).

A timid disciple walked onto the stage. "I... I will recite the ballad of the Weeping Willow."

"Boring!" Kevin buzzed him. "Next! I need action! I need passion! Someone give me a death scene!"

A dramatic disciple threw himself to the ground, thrashing and screaming, "My meridians! They burn! Avenge me!"

"Gold!" Kevin stood up and clapped. "You're hired. You're dying in Act
3."

Opening Night.

The sky above the Azure Cloud Sect was clogged with traffic. Thousands of flying swords, floating gourds, and magical carpets descended on Shattered Moon Peak.

The neon sign buzzed to life: **KEVIN'S SWORD-IN CINEMA**.

Kevin stood at the entrance, wearing a tuxedo he had the Weaver make (black dragon-silk with tails). He was taking tickets (Spirit Stones).

"Enjoy the show! Keep your swords within the designated parking rings! No flash photography!"

The disciples parked their swords in the air, creating tiered rows of seating. They bought buckets of glowing Dragon Fire Popcorn and cups of
**Fizzy Spirit Soda** (carbonated spring water).

The lights dimmed (Kevin signaled Lin Xue to lower the cloud cover).

**\[SYSTEM MUSIC QUEUE:** "**THUS SPOKE ZARATHUSTRA**" **(2001: A SPACE
ODYSSEY THEME)\]**

A massive illusion flickered into existence in the sky.

"Welcome," Kevin's amplified voice boomed. "To a world... of adventure."

The play began. It was a retelling of the sect's founding, but Kevin had "punched up" the script.

- The Founder didn't just meditate; he did a training montage.
- The Demon King didn't just lose; he got roundhouse kicked into a
  volcano.
- There was a dance number.

The audience was captivated. They cheered. They gasped. They threw popcorn.

During the intermission, Kevin took the stage for a **Kung Fu Demonstration**.

"And now!" Kevin shouted. "Watch closely as I demonstrate the
**Technique of the Shadowless Kick**!"

He jumped on his board, flew into the air, and did a flurry of kicks while spinning. He shattered ten clay pots hanging from strings.

SMASH. SMASH. SMASH.

"Ki-Ya!"

The crowd roared.

Finally, for the grand finale, the **Cultivation Love Poetry**.

Two actors, a handsome youth and a beautiful maiden, floated in the center of the illusion.

"Oh, Chen," the maiden wept (amplified to deafening volumes). "My heart beats only for you! But our sects are enemies!"

"Forget the sects!" the youth shouted. "Our love is the Dao! It is the true path!"

They embraced. Fireworks exploded (Kevin literally set off fireworks).

In the back row of the parking, on a secluded floating cloud, Kevin and Lin Xue sat on the *Radical Shredder*.

Kevin had his arm around her. They were sharing a bucket of popcorn.

"Look at them," Kevin whispered, gesturing to the thousands of mesmerized cultivators. "They love it. They're crying. They're laughing."

Lin Xue watched the show. She ate a piece of popcorn.

"It is... manipulative," she noted. "The music swells to force emotion. The dialogue is melodramatic. The explosions are unnecessary."

She leaned her head on his shoulder.

"But... it is not boring."

Kevin squeezed her shoulder.

"That's show business, baby."

He looked at the screen, then at the girl, then at his empire of pizza, wings, and movies.

"You know," Kevin said thoughtfully. "I think we need a theme park next. Roller coasters. But with real dragons."

Lin Xue sighed, but she didn't move away.

"One thing at a time, Kevin. One thing at a time."

Kevin smiled.

"Roll credits."
