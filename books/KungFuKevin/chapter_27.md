# Chapter 27: The Matrix Has You (But It's Chill)

The credits of the illusion-play were rolling, literally, scrolling text of fire in the sky listing the cast, the crew, and giving special thanks to Chef Wang for the snacks.

The crowd began to disperse. Flying swords zipped away like fireflies in the night.

Kevin stayed on his board, floating gently in the cool night breeze. The adrenaline of the show was fading, replaced by a strange, quiet melancholy.

He looked at his hands. They were calloused from gripping the nunchucks. He looked at his clothes, the dragon-scale vest, the ridiculous pants. He looked at Lin Xue, who was now busy directing the cleanup crew with efficient, sharp commands.

"I wonder when I'm going to wake up," Kevin whispered to the moon.

He reached up and touched his face. He could feel the stubble on his chin. He could smell the lingering scent of spicy popcorn and ozone.

"This has been the greatest dream ever," he murmured. "I mean, seriously. Pizza tycoon? Kung Fu master? The girl? It's too perfect. It's too... scripted."

He pinched his arm. Hard.

Ouch.

It hurt. But dreams could hurt, right?

"Could all of this..." Kevin looked around at the floating mountains, the glowing runes, the sheer impossibility of it all. "...all of this... be my 'real life'?"

\[SYSTEM NOTIFICATION\]

A blue box popped up, hovering softly in his vision. It wasn't the usual aggressive, flashing alert. It was calm.

**\[QUERY: EXISTENTIAL CRISIS DETECTED.\]** **\[ANALYSIS: USER IS
QUESTIONING REALITY.\]**

Kevin stared at the text. "Yeah, System. I am. Is this a coma? Did I fry my brain on that breaker box in the basement? Am I drooling on a carpet somewhere in Ohio right now?"

The text shifted.

\[ANSWER: DOES IT MATTER?\]

Kevin blinked. "Does it matter? Uh, yeah! If I'm a vegetable, that sucks!"

**\[SYSTEM RESPONSE:\]** **\[IN YOUR OLD LIFE: YOU WERE A CLERK AT A
VIDEO STORE. YOU LIVED IN A BASEMENT. YOUR HIGHLIGHT WAS** '**TACO TUESDAY**'**.\]**

\[IN THIS LIFE: YOU ARE AN ELDER. YOU RIDE A FLYING SKATEBOARD. YOU HAVE A HOT GIRLFRIEND (ALMOST). YOU ARE HAPPY.\]

\[CONCLUSION: REALITY IS WHAT YOU MAKE OF IT. THE DAO IS IN THE DOING.\]

Kevin read the words. *The Dao is in the doing.*

He looked over at Lin Xue. She sensed his gaze and turned. She flew over to him, her expression softening.

"Kevin?" she asked. "You are quiet. Are you calculating the profits? Or planning to build a rollercoaster?"

Kevin looked at her. Really looked at her. He saw the way the moonlight caught her eyes. He saw the slight tiredness in her posture from working all day to help him achieve his crazy vision.

He realized something. Even if this *was* a dream... she felt real. The way she laughed at his bad jokes felt real. The way the pizza burned his tongue felt real.

"No," Kevin said, shaking his head and smiling. "Just thinking."

He stood up on his board and adjusted his sunglasses, even though it was pitch black.

"I was thinking that I don't care if I wake up."

He held out his hand to her.

"Come on, Lin. Let's go home. I have an idea for a breakfast burrito that's going to change the world."

Lin Xue took his hand. Her grip was strong. Grounding.

"Burrito?" she asked as they turned toward the Dojo. "Is it another explosive food?"

"Oh yeah," Kevin grinned. "It's the bomb."

He looked back one last time at the empty theater, at the stars, at the world he had accidentally conquered.

"Real life," Kevin whispered. "Yeah. I'll take it."

He kicked the tail of his board.

"Let's ride."
