# Chapter 29: The Steam Engine of Destruction

An hour later, Chen, now sporting a fresh black robe (Kevin insisted he ditch the "boring sect whites") and his new gold-rimmed aviators, stood in the courtyard of the **Dojo of Dopeness**.

Kevin circled him like a shark, nodding appreciatively.

"A'ight, Iceman!" Kevin said, pointing a finger gun. "So, lookie here. You go by Iceman, it's gonna confuse your enemies, you dig! It's psychological warfare 101."

Chen crossed his arms, looking skeptical but undeniably stylish. "Confuse them? How?"

"Think about it!" Kevin exclaimed, making an exploding motion with his hands. "They see you coming. They sense the heat. But then they hear the name... *Iceman*. Their brains short circuit! They start thinking... 'Wait, he's FIRE... but now... he's ICE? What kind of cultivation guru is he! Is he dual-element? Is he a transcendent being?'"

Chen tilted his head, considering this. "Deception is a key tenet of war..."

"Exactly!" Kevin continued, pacing faster. "But it can't just be a name. You gotta embody the contradiction. You need to add some **COOL** to your **FIRE**."

Kevin stopped and looked Chen dead in the eye.

"And you know what that makes?"

Chen frowned. "Obsidian? Ash?"

"No!" Kevin leaned in close. "Fire... plus Cool (which is basically water in attitude form)... equals..."

Kevin paused for dramatic effect.

"**STEAM**."

"Steam?" Chen repeated flatly. "Like... from a kettle?"

"Yah! You feel me?" Kevin shouted, grabbing Chen's shoulders. "Not kettle steam, bro. I'm talking **Locomotive Steam**. I'm talking **Pressure Cooker** energy! You compress that fire inside you. You keep it cool on the outside, looking chill, looking icy... but inside? You are building massive pressure."

Kevin mimed a piston pumping. "Chugga-chugga-chugga... CHOO CHOO!"

**\[SYSTEM ALERT: CULTIVATION THEORY GENERATED.\]** **\[THEORY:** "**THE
THERMODYNAMIC DAO**"**\]** **\[PROBABILITY OF SUCCESS: LOW.\]**
**\[PROBABILITY OF AWESOMENESS: HIGH.\]**

Chen's eyes widened behind his glasses.

"To compress the Yang fire..." Chen muttered, his mind racing. "To restrain the explosion until the moment of impact... creating a pressurized burst of superheated vapor..."

He looked at his hand. Usually, he let his fire rage uncontrollably. That was the way of the Red Lotus. It was wasteful. It was messy.

"Like a geyser," Chen whispered. "Silent... until it erupts."

"Bingo!" Kevin snapped his fingers. "You're getting it! The Silent Killer. The Steampunk Assassin."

Kevin ran over to a pile of scrap metal left over from the theatre construction. He picked up a thick iron pipe.

"Try it," Kevin challenged, tossing the pipe to Chen. "Don't melt it. Pressurize it. Make it scream."

Chen caught the pipe. He closed his eyes. He focused on his core. Instead of letting his Qi explode outward, he held it in. He wrapped his intent in a layer of "Cool". He imagined he was an iceberg... with a volcano inside.

The pipe didn't glow red. It didn't melt.

Instead, a high-pitched whining sound began to emanate from the metal.

eeeeeeeeEEEEEEEEEEEE!

White steam began to hiss violently from the ends of the pipe, jetting out like thrusters. The pressure was immense.

Chen opened his eyes. They were glowing with a pale, white light instead of angry red.

"Choo... Choo?" Chen asked tentatively.

"Ki-Ya!" Kevin yelled. "Hit something!"

Chen thrust the pipe forward. He released the pressure.

HISSSSS-BOOM!

A concentrated blast of superheated steam fired from the pipe, punching a clean hole through a stone statue of a lion ten feet away. The edges of the hole weren't melted; they were blasted clean by sheer kinetic force.

Chen stared at the destruction. "It... it penetrated the stone defenses. Fire usually splashes against rock. But this... this pierced it."

"That's the power of Steam, baby!" Kevin cheered, doing a victory lap around the courtyard. "You ain't just burning people anymore. You're cooking them. Like a lobster. Or a dumpling."

Kevin stopped and put an arm around his new Frenemy.

"Welcome to the Industrial Revolution, Iceman."

Chen looked at the smoking pipe, then at Kevin. A slow, dangerous grin spread across his face.

"Steam," Chen said, adjusting his aviators. "I feel... pressurized."

"Good," Kevin said. "Keep a lid on it. We're gonna need that pressure."

Kevin looked toward the horizon, his expression turning serious for a moment.

"Because I have a feeling the **Iron Fist Sect** isn't gonna be happy about losing their title. And when they come back... they're gonna come heavy."

He tapped Chen's chest.

"And you, my steamy friend, are gonna be our frontline defense."

Chen nodded. "Let them come. I will boil them alive."

"That's the spirit!" Kevin laughed. "Now, let's go get some lunch. All this science talk made me hungry. You like burritos?"
