# Chapter 30: The Trojan Horse of Snacks

Kevin led Chen into the bustling kitchen of the Dojo, where Chef Wang was currently trying to teach a sous-chef how to properly bread a chicken wing.

"Chef!" Kevin announced. "Table for two. And prepare the **Tortilla Press**. We are entering Phase Two of the menu."

He sat Chen down at the marble counter. Chen looked around suspiciously. This kitchen was unlike any alchemy lab he had ever seen. It smelled of grease and victory.

"So," Kevin began, grabbing a handful of raw flour. "You asked about the burrito."

"I did not," Chen corrected. "You simply dragged me here."

"Details," Kevin waved his hand. "Listen. The burrito is not just an oblong sandwich. That's rookie thinking. A sandwich is boring. It's open on the sides. Stuff falls out. It's structurally unsound."

Kevin slapped a ball of dough onto the counter.

"But a burrito?" Kevin's eyes gleamed. "It's a self-contained tactical unit. It's sealed. Portable. Aerodynamic. It can be breakfast, lunch, or dinner! It all depends on what you put inside!"

He flattened the dough into a large, thin circle.

"Breakfast? Eggs, Spirit Sausage, Cheese. Boom. Energy for the day." "Lunch? Beans, Rice, Carnitas. Boom. Sustenance." "Dinner? Steak, Guacamole, Sorrow."

"Sorrow?" Chen asked.

"If you run out of guacamole," Kevin clarified somberly. "Now, watch."

Kevin began to pile ingredients onto the tortilla. He used **Thunder Yak Cheese**, **Spirit Boar Carnitas** (slow-cooked for 12 hours), and
**Void-Bean Paste**.

"Now, here is the genius part," Kevin leaned in, his voice dropping to a conspiratorial whisper. "The secret ingredient? The sauce!"

He pointed to a row of small glass vials Chef Wang had prepared. They were glowing in various colors: red, green, purple, and a disturbing neon yellow.

"You can have dozens of different powerups... all disguised as a harmless snack!"

Kevin picked up the Red Vial.

"**Salsa of the Raging Bull**. Made from Fire-Peppers. Increases strength by 20% for thirty minutes. Side effect: Mild sweating."

He picked up the Green Vial.

"**Verde of Vitality**. Made from healing herbs and lime. Regenerates health. Good for hangovers or stab wounds."

He picked up the Neon Yellow Vial.

"**Queso of the Quick-Step**. We don't really know what's in this one. Wang found a glowing mushroom in a cave. But it makes you run really fast. Or hallucinate. It's a gamble."

Kevin looked at Chen. "You see the potential, Iceman? We aren't just feeding the sect. We are **buffing** them. We are turning every disciple into a super-soldier, one bite at a time."

Chen stared at the glowing sauces. "Edible alchemy... mass-produced... and hidden inside a flour shell?"

"The Trojan Horse of Snacks," Kevin nodded. "The enemy won't know what hit 'em. They'll think we're just eating lunch, and suddenly, BAM!, we're all Hulking out on burrito power."

Kevin wrapped the burrito. His technique was flawless, the tuck, the fold, the roll. It was tight as a drum.

"Chef Wang!" Kevin yelled. "Flash fry this bad boy! Make it chimichanga style!"

Wang dropped the burrito into the fryer. *PSSSHHH.*

Minutes later, a golden, crispy cylinder was placed in front of Chen.

"Eat," Kevin commanded. "I put the **Steam Sauce** in there just for you. Ghost Pepper and Mint."

Chen picked up the chimichanga. It was hot. Heavy.

He took a bite.

CRUNCH.

The flavors hit him. The savory pork. The creamy beans. And then, the sauce.

It was icy cold and burning hot at the same time. It rushed through his meridians, hitting his core. He felt his new "Steam Qi" expanding, pressurizing.

Steam shot out of Chen's nose. *Pfffft.*

"Oh," Chen whispered, his eyes widening behind his gold-rimmed glasses. "Oh my."

He took another bite. Faster this time.

"This is..." Chen chewed aggressively. "This is superior to the Pill of the Golden Dragon! It is delicious... and I feel my power rising!"

"That's the burrito magic, baby," Kevin grinned, leaning back. "Welcome to the future of warfare."

Kevin grabbed a napkin and wiped a speck of oil from the counter.

"Now," Kevin said, his tone shifting to business. "We got the troops fed. We got the defenses up. But if the Iron Fist Sect is coming... we need intel."

He looked at Chen.

"You know their territory. The **Metal Wastelands**. What are we walking into?"

Chen put down the half-eaten burrito. His expression darkened.

"The Wastelands are a graveyard of swords," Chen said. "The air is filled with metal dust. Breathing it shreds the lungs of the weak. And their Sect Leader... **Ironclad Ivan**..."

"Ivan?" Kevin raised an eyebrow. "Sounds like a Bond villain."

"He is worse," Chen said. "He has grafted spirit metal to his own skin. He is invulnerable. Swords shatter against him. Fire washes over him."

Kevin tapped his chin. "Invulnerable, huh? Metal skin?"

He smirked.

"Sounds like he hasn't met the **Can Opener** yet."

"The... Can Opener?"

"It's a move I'm working on," Kevin said mysteriously. "But first, we need to do some recon. You up for a road trip, Iceman?"

Chen finished his burrito in one massive bite. He stood up, steam still drifting from his nostrils.

"With this power?" Chen adjusted his vest. "I am ready for anything."

"Good," Kevin said. "Because where we're going... we don't need roads."

(Narrator note: They absolutely needed roads, or at least a map, because Kevin had no sense of direction.)
