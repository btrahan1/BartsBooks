# Chapter 31: The GPS (Global Path of Spirit)

Kevin stood at the edge of the sect's boundary, looking out into the endless sea of clouds. To the north lay the jagged, grey silhouette of the Metal Wastelands.

"Alright," Kevin muttered, squinting. "North. I think. Or is that just a really dark cloud?"

He tapped the side of his head.

"System! Where is my map! All good RPGs have a map! Come on. Snap snap! And don't forget my waypoints!"

**\[SYSTEM PROCESSING...\]** **\[MAP APPLICATION NOT FOUND.\]**
**\[DOWNLOADING** "**GOOGLE MAPS: SPIRIT REALM EDITION**"**...\]**

A loading bar appeared in Kevin's vision. It spun for an agonizing three seconds.

**\[DOWNLOAD COMPLETE.\]** **\[LAUNCHING** "**THE PATHFINDER**"**.\]**

A translucent, 3D holographic map unfolded in front of Kevin's eyes. It was glorious. It showed the terrain in wireframe neon green. It had icons for "Safe Zones" (Blue), "Danger Zones" (Red), and "Good Loot" (Gold).

"Finally," Kevin sighed. "Civilization."

He zoomed in on the Metal Wastelands.

**\[DESTINATION SET: IRON FIST SECT CITADEL.\]** **\[ESTIMATED TRAVEL
TIME: 4 HOURS (BY FLYING SWORD).\]** **\[TRAFFIC: HEAVY (FLOCK OF DEMON VULTURES AHEAD).\]**

A glowing blue line appeared on the ground in front of him, stretching off into the sky.

"Okay, boys and girls," Kevin announced to his team.

The team consisted of: 1. **Lin Xue** (The Brains/The Sword). 2.
**Chen** "**The Iceman**" (The Muscle/The Steam). 3. **Kevin** (The
Wildcard/The Pizza Lord).

"We have a route," Kevin said, pointing at the invisible line only he could see. "Follow the blue brick road."

He jumped onto the *Radical Shredder*.

"Lin, you take point. Chen, watch our six. I'll navigate."

"Navigate?" Lin Xue raised an eyebrow, hovering on her pristine white sword. "You mean you will stare at the air and shout directions?"

"It's called a HUD, Lin. Get with the program."

They launched into the sky.

Flying toward the Metal Wastelands was less like a majestic journey and more like flying into a sandblaster.

The air grew heavy with metallic dust. The wind howled, sounding like grinding gears.

"Recalculating!" Kevin shouted over the wind as the blue line on his HUD flickered. "System says there's a speed trap ahead! Slow down!"

"A speed trap?" Chen yelled back, his steam-aura deflecting the metal dust. "What is that?"

"It means there's a high-level Spirit Beast waiting to ambush anyone moving fast!" Kevin explained. "Drop altitude! We gotta go stealth mode!"

They dove down into a canyon of rusted iron spikes.

The canyon was a maze. Sharp, jagged metal jutted out from the walls.

"Turn left in 200 yards!" Kevin commanded.

Lin Xue banked hard left, narrowly missing a giant rusted sawblade embedded in the cliff.

"Kevin!" she shouted. "This route is suicidal!"

"It's the scenic route!" Kevin argued. "Plus, there's a loot chest nearby! System shows a Gold Icon!"

"We are not stopping for 'loot'!" Lin Xue insisted.

"But it could be a Legendary helmet! Or a coupon for free spirit stones!"

Suddenly, a massive shadow passed over them.

**\[WARNING: ENEMY DETECTED.\]** **\[TYPE: IRON-WINGED GRIFFIN.\]**
**\[LEVEL: BOSS.\]**

A screech echoed through the canyon. A creature made of feathers that looked like knives dove at them.

"Contact!" Kevin yelled. "Evasive maneuvers! Do a barrel roll!"

Lin Xue rolled. Chen blasted a jet of steam to propel himself sideways.

Kevin... simply stopped.

He stomped on the tail of his board, pulling a **Powerslide** in mid-air. The board shrieked as it turned 90 degrees, halting his momentum.

The Griffin overshot him, its metal talons grasping empty air.

"Ha! You missed!" Kevin taunted.

The Griffin turned around, screeching furiously.

"Okay, maybe I shouldn't have taunted the giant metal bird," Kevin admitted.

"Iceman! You're up! Steam cleaning time!"

Chen grinned. He clapped his hands together.

HISSSSSS.

He pressurized his Qi. His body began to vibrate.

"Choo Choo," Chen whispered.

He thrust his palms forward.

\[STEAM CANNON!\]

A massive column of white, superheated vapor erupted from Chen's hands. It hit the Griffin square in the chest.

The steam didn't just burn; it expanded rapidly, knocking the massive beast backward into the canyon wall.

CRASH.

The Griffin shook its head, wet and confused. Its metal feathers were hot and warped. It decided that this prey was too spicy and flew away squawking.

"Nice shot!" Kevin high-fived the air. "Now, back to the mission. System says we have arrived at... waypoint one."

He pointed to a small, unassuming cave entrance hidden behind a waterfall of mercury.

"The Secret Tunnel," Kevin said, reading the HUD. "Leads directly into the Iron Fist Sect's ventilation shaft. Classic spy movie trope."

Lin Xue looked at the mercury waterfall. "That liquid is toxic. If we touch it, we die."

"No problem," Kevin said. "I brought umbrellas."

He pulled three **Spirit-Umbrellas** (made of treated silk) from his inventory.

"Pop 'em up, team. We're going in Mary Poppins style."

Lin Xue sighed. "I do not know who Mary is, but I assume she was a powerful cultivator."

" The most powerful," Kevin nodded solemnly. "She could fly with just an umbrella. Respect the Poppins."

They opened their umbrellas and flew through the toxic waterfall, entering the dark, metallic belly of the enemy sect.

**\[QUEST UPDATE:** "**INFILTRATION**" **STARTED.\]** **\[OBJECTIVE:
DON'T SNEEZE.\]**

"Shhh," Kevin whispered, putting a finger to his lips. "Be vewy, vewy quiet. We're hunting wabbits. I mean... Iron Men."
