# Chapter 32: Metal Gear Solid (But With Snacks)

The tunnel behind the mercury waterfall was dark, damp, and echoed with the distant rhythmic clanging of hammers. It smelled like pennies and body odor.

Kevin landed softly, his boots making a faint *click* on the metal floor. He collapsed his Spirit Umbrella and tucked it away.

"A'ight Team," Kevin whispered, crouching low and making a slicing motion with his hand. "It's Ninja Time. Engage stealth mode!"

Lin Xue landed silently beside him, her movements naturally graceful. She channeled a technique that dimmed her aura, making her presence feel like a shadow.

Chen landed with a heavy *THUD*. His boots were reinforced with iron plates. Steam hissed loudly from his vents.

PSSSHHHHT.

Kevin winced. "Chen! Shhh! You sound like a tea kettle having a panic attack! Turn down the pressure!"

"I am trying," Chen whispered back, his face strained. "My cultivation... it is loud by nature. The steam must escape!"

"Hold it in!" Kevin hissed. "Think cool thoughts. Think... snow cones. Penguins. The other side of the pillow."

Chen squeezed his eyes shut. The hissing subsided to a low, barely audible whistle.

"Acceptable," Kevin whispered. "Now, follow me. Stay low. Watch the corners."

Kevin pulled his sunglasses down over his eyes. Even in the dark, the
**Shades of the Blind Dog** gave him a wireframe view of the tunnel.

**\[STEALTH MODE ACTIVE.\]** **\[VISIBILITY: LOW.\]** **\[NOISE LEVEL:
MEDIUM (THANKS TO CHEN).\]**

They moved deeper into the complex. The tunnel opened up into a massive ventilation shaft. Below them, through a grate, they could see the Iron Fist Sect's main foundry.

Hundreds of disciples were working. They were dipping their hands into vats of molten liquid, screaming, and pulling them out coated in gleaming metal.

"Barbaric," Lin Xue whispered, looking down. "They sacrifice their flesh for power."

"Yeah, not exactly OSHA compliant," Kevin muttered. "System, scan for the Boss Room."

**\[SCANNING...\]** **\[TARGET LOCATED: THE IRON THRONE ROOM. 500 METERS
NORTH.\]**

"Okay, target acquired," Kevin said. "We need to cross this catwalk without being seen."

He pointed to a narrow metal walkway suspended high above the foundry floor.

They crept forward. Kevin moved with surprising stealth for a guy in leather pants. He utilized the **Silly Walk of Silence**, lifting his knees high and placing his toes down first.

Suddenly, a guard appeared at the end of the catwalk. He was massive, wearing full plate armor.

Kevin froze. Lin Xue's hand went to her sword.

"Wait," Kevin whispered. "No killing. We need to be ghosts."

He reached into his pocket.

"Distraction tactic."

He pulled out a **Wind-Chaser Wing** (Leftover from lunch). It was cold, but still smelled delicious.

"Go long," Kevin whispered.

He threw the chicken wing.

It sailed through the air in a perfect arc. It landed with a wet *slap* on the helmet of a disciple working below the catwalk.

SLAP.

The disciple looked up. "What? Who threw meat?"

He saw the wing. He sniffed it. "Is that... Buffalo sauce?"

The guard on the catwalk turned to look at the commotion below.

"Hey! No snacking on the job!" the guard yelled, leaning over the railing.

"Move! Move! Move!" Kevin urged.

They sprinted past the distracted guard. Kevin did a commando roll purely for effect. Chen lumbered past, holding his breath to stop the whistling. Lin Xue glided like a ghost.

They made it to the other side and slipped into a side corridor.

Kevin leaned against the wall, breathing hard.

"That was close," he said. "Good thing they have terrible discipline. And hunger."

"We are approaching the inner sanctum," Chen warned, pointing to a massive set of double doors made of black steel. "Ironclad Ivan will be inside."

"Great," Kevin said. "Time for the dress rehearsal."

He looked at his team.

"Lin, you take the left flank. Chen, you take the right. I'll go up the middle and distract him with my charisma."

"Distract him?" Lin Xue asked. "He is a warlord. He will kill you."

"Not if I confuse him first," Kevin grinned. "I'm going to use the
**Diplomacy of the Dao**."

He straightened his dragon-scale vest. He checked his hair in the reflection of his nunchucks.

"System," Kevin muttered. "Equip the 'Negotiator' title. And lend me some swagger."

**\[TITLE EQUIPPED: THE SMOOTH TALKER.\]** **\[CHARISMA: +10.\]**
**\[INTELLIGENCE: -5 (RISKY).\]**

Kevin kicked open the massive steel doors.

BANG.

Inside, sitting on a throne made of melted swords, was **Ironclad Ivan**. He was seven feet tall. His skin was literally metal, silver and gleaming. He was holding a goblet of molten gold.

He looked up, surprised.

"Who dares enter the Iron Sanctum?" Ivan roared, his voice sounding like grinding gears.

Kevin strolled in, hands in his pockets, aviators reflecting the torchlight.

"Knock knock," Kevin said loudly.

Ivan blinked. "What?"

"Knock knock," Kevin repeated.

"Who... who is there?" Ivan asked, confused by the ritual.

"Tank," Kevin said.

"Tank who?"

"You're welcome!" Kevin shouted, finger-gunning him.

Ivan stared. The silence was deafening.

"That... that does not make sense," Ivan growled, standing up. "You are the Pizza Lord. The one who mocked my sect."

"Guilty as charged," Kevin said, walking closer. "But I come bearing gifts. And a proposition."

He pulled a **Super-Supreme Burrito** out of his pocket. It was still warm.

"Ivan, baby," Kevin said smoothly. "Why fight? Why coat your skin in metal? It chafes. It's heavy. Look at me. I'm flexible. I'm free."

He tossed the burrito to Ivan.

"Eat this. It's got beans. It's got cheese. It's got love."

Ivan caught the burrito. He crushed it in his metal hand. The filling exploded everywhere.

"I DO NOT EAT BEANS!" Ivan roared. "I EAT STEEL!"

He grabbed a sword from his throne and took a bite out of the blade.
*CRUNCH.*

Kevin winced. "Okay. Tough crowd. That's bad for your teeth, man."

"Kill them!" Ivan commanded.

From the shadows, two dozen Elite Iron Guards emerged.

Kevin sighed. "So much for diplomacy."

He pulled out his nunchucks.

"A'ight team. Plan B."

"What is Plan B?" Lin Xue asked, drawing her sword.

"**Plan Beatdown**," Kevin yelled. "Everybody was Kung Fu Fighting!"

**\[SYSTEM MUSIC QUEUE:** "**KUNG FU FIGHTING**" **(TECHNO REMIX)\]**

The brawl began.
