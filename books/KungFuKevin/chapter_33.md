# Chapter 33: The Ice Queen Cometh

The techno beat dropped, shaking the iron walls of the sanctum.

Kevin spun his nunchucks, ready to engage. Chen pressurized his steam, his fists trembling.

But then, a voice cut through the chaos. Cool. Calm. Commanding.

"Boys... step back. I got this."

Kevin froze mid-spin. He looked at Lin Xue.

Usually, Lin Xue was the voice of reason. The strategist. The one who told him *not* to jump off cliffs. But right now, she looked different.

She wasn't calculating angles. She wasn't analyzing Qi signatures. She had entered the **Zone**.

She tightened the red "*BAD BOY*" headband around her forehead. She sheathed her sword.

"Wait," Kevin whispered. "Sheathed? Lin, they have metal skin! You need the sharp pointy thing!"

Lin Xue ignored him. She closed her eyes.

She remembered the lesson on the pillar. *Float like a butterfly. Sting like a bee.*

She remembered the feeling of the **Radical Shredder** under her feet. The chaotic flow. The rhythm.

She opened her eyes. They were glowing azure blue.

"Hit it," she whispered.

She didn't wait for the music. She *became* the music.

Twelve Elite Iron Guards charged her at once. They were heavy, slow, and powerful.

Lin Xue moved.

She didn't fly. She didn't run. She **glided**.

It looked like she was on ice skates. Her feet barely left the ground as she slid forward to meet the charge.

The first guard swung a massive hammer.

Lin Xue dropped into a split, Kevin style, but with infinite grace. The hammer passed over her head.

As she came up, she didn't strike with her fist. She struck with her
**finger**.

TAP.

She touched the guard on the inside of his elbow. Not hard. Just a sting.

"Boop," she whispered.

The guard's arm went numb. He dropped the hammer on his own foot.

CRUNCH.

"AAAAH!"

Lin Xue was already moving. She spun, her robes fluttering like butterfly wings. She weaved between three guards, dodging their clumsy strikes by mere millimeters.

She wasn't overpowering them. She was dancing around them.

Float.

She leaped onto the shoulders of a guard, used his metal head as a springboard, and backflipped over the line.

Sting.

Mid-air, she flicked three needles of condensed frost Qi. They hit three guards precisely in the gaps of their armor, right in the neck.

THWIP. THWIP. THWIP.

The guards froze instantly, encased in blocks of blue ice.

Kevin watched, his jaw on the floor.

"She's doing it," Kevin whispered. "She's actually doing it. The Ali Shuffle. The Miyagi Wax On."

Chen adjusted his glasses. "She is terrifying. I am glad she is on our side."

Ironclad Ivan watched his elites fall like bowling pins. He roared in frustration.

"Enough dancing! I will crush you myself!"

The giant metal man charged. He was unstoppable. A juggernaut.

Lin Xue stood her ground. She waited.

"Come on, Tin Man," she said softly, adopting Kevin's vernacular.

Just as Ivan reached her, winding up a punch that could shatter a mountain, Lin Xue moved.

She didn't dodge. She stepped **into** his guard.

She placed one hand on his metal chest and one hand on his metal abdomen.

"The Dao of the Microwave," she whispered.

She channeled her Qi. But instead of blasting it out, she vibrated it. Fast. Oscillation at a molecular level.

**\[SYSTEM ALERT: PHYSICS EXPLOIT DETECTED.\]** **\[SKILL: RESONANCE
SHATTER.\]**

HUMMMMMMMM.

Ivan stopped. His metal skin began to vibrate.

"What... what are you doing?" Ivan stammered. "It tickles!"

"Wait for the ding," Lin Xue said.

The vibration pitch increased. The metal couldn't handle the frequency.

PING!

Cracks appeared on Ivan's chest.

Lin Xue stepped back and snapped her fingers.

CRACK-BOOM.

Ivan's metal plating shattered. Shards of silver flew everywhere. The invincible warlord was left standing in his undergarments, his metal skin lying in a pile around his feet.

He looked down at his pink, vulnerable skin. He shivered.

"I feel... naked," Ivan whimpered.

Lin Xue dusted off her hands. She adjusted her headband.

She turned to Kevin and Chen, who were staring at her in stunned silence.

"That," Lin Xue said coolly, "is how you clear the level."

Kevin slowly started to clap.

"Bravo," Kevin said. "Encore. That was poetry. Violent, icy poetry."

He walked up to her and offered a fist bump.

"You really are a Bad Boy, Lin."

Lin Xue looked at the fist. She smiled. She bumped it.

"I learned from the best," she said.

She looked at the shivering Ivan.

"Now," Lin Xue said, her voice dropping to a dangerous tone. "About that surrender..."

Ivan fell to his knees. "I yield! I yield! Just give me a blanket! It's cold!"

Kevin stepped forward, pulling another burrito from his pocket.

"Eat the burrito, Ivan," Kevin said gently. "And join the franchise. We have dental."

Ivan took the burrito with trembling hands. He took a bite.

"Beans..." Ivan whispered, tears forming in his eyes. "They are... not terrible."

Kevin winked at his team.

"Another satisfied customer."
