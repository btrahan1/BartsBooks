# Chapter 34: The MLM (Martial Lord Marketing) Scheme

The Iron Sanctum had been repurposed. The throne of melted swords was gone, replaced by a massive, round conference table Kevin had transmuted from the scrap metal (using Chef Wang's alchemy furnace and a lot of swearing).

Ironclad Ivan sat at the table, wrapped in a fluffy blanket, eating his third burrito. He looked less like a warlord and more like a confused toddler at a slumber party.

"A'ight... Ivan," Kevin said, pacing around the table with a piece of chalk in hand. He stopped and rested a hand on Ivan's shivering shoulder. "My new brother from another mother... let me explain the concept of a franchise."

He turned to the blackboard he had set up.

"Right now, you're in the **Violence Business**. And let's be honest, the margins are terrible. You spend money on armor, swords, medical bills for when your guys lose fingers... it's a money pit."

Kevin drew a frowny face on the board.

"But imagine this."

Kevin erased the frowny face and drew a pyramid.

"The **Pizza Business**. Low overhead. High demand. Infinite scalability."

He pointed to the top of the pyramid.

"This is me. The CEO. The Visionary."

He pointed to the next level down.

"This is you, Ivan. You are a **Regional Manager**. You control the Metal Wastelands territory. You convert your foundries into ovens. You convert your sword-smiths into dough-spinners. Instead of dipping hands in molten steel, they dip wings in molten sauce!"

Ivan stared at the pyramid. "So... we stop killing people?"

"We stop killing them *physically*," Kevin corrected. "We kill them with
**Flavor**. We conquer their stomachs. A customer who is full of cheese
is a customer who doesn't rebel."

Ivan nodded slowly. "Cheese... as a suppression tool. Brilliant."

"Exactly!" Kevin drew dollar signs (or Spirit Stone signs) all over the board. "And here's the kicker. You get 10% of all gross sales in your region. Plus, you get unlimited access to the **Gravity Chamber Gym** back at my place. You can get that metal skin back, but the natural way. Through gains."

Ivan's eyes lit up. "Gains? I can become... swole?"

"The swolest," Kevin promised. "You'll be Ironclad Ivan: The Man of Steel Abs."

Ivan stood up, the blanket falling from his shoulders. He slammed his fist on the table.

"I accept!" Ivan roared. "The Iron Fist Sect is no more! We are now...
**The Iron Crust Pizzeria**!"

"I love the rebranding," Kevin said. "Very industrial chic."

Kevin turned to Lin Xue and Chen, who were watching from the sidelines.

"See?" Kevin whispered. "Diplomacy. It works every time."

"You essentially tricked him into a pyramid scheme," Lin Xue noted dryly.

"It's not a scheme, Lin," Kevin winked. "It's a **Multi-Level Marketing Opportunity**. Now, let's get out of here. This place smells like wet socks."

The Ride Home.

They flew back toward the Azure Cloud Sect. The mood was triumphant. They had neutralized the biggest threat in the region without starting a war.

Kevin rode the *Radical Shredder*, doing lazy S-turns through the clouds.

"Man," Kevin sighed contentedly. "Life is good. We got the North secured. We got the food supply chain locked down. What's next? Space travel? Time travel?"

Suddenly, the sky darkened.

Not like a storm. Like ink spilling into water.

The sun was blotted out. The temperature dropped fifty degrees in a second.

**\[SYSTEM WARNING: CRITICAL ERROR.\]** **\[REALITY BREACH DETECTED.\]**
**\[SOURCE: UNKNOWN DIMENSION.\]**

A massive rift tore open in the sky above them. It wasn't a cultivation rift. It looked... digital. Pixelated.

Static noise screeched through the air.

KZZZRT-BEEP-BOOP.

From the rift, a voice boomed. It wasn't a booming cultivator voice. It sounded synthesized. Autotuned.

"**ERROR 404: PLOT ARMOR NOT FOUND.**"

Kevin stopped his board. He lifted his sunglasses.

"Uh oh," Kevin said. "That doesn't sound like a side quest."

A figure descended from the rift.

It wasn't a man in robes. It was a figure made of chrome and neon light. It wore a suit that looked like it was cut from the fabric of the universe itself.

It held a glowing clipboard.

"**USER: KEVIN,**" the figure droned. "**YOU HAVE VIOLATED THE GENRE CONVENTIONS. YOU HAVE INTRODUCED TOO MANY ANACHRONISMS. THE NARRATIVE INTEGRITY IS COMPROMISED.**"

The figure pointed a stylus at Kevin.

"**I AM THE EDITOR. YOU ARE BEING AUDITED.**"

Kevin looked at Lin Xue. "Did we pay our taxes?"

"I thought you handled the bribes!" Lin Xue hissed, drawing her sword.

"**PREPARE FOR DELETION,**" The Editor said.

Kevin cracked his knuckles. He spun his nunchucks.

"Deletion?" Kevin laughed nervously. "Buddy, I'm the main character. You can't delete me."

He looked at his team.

"A'ight guys. This looks like the Season Finale cliffhanger. You ready?"

Chen pressurized his steam. Lin Xue iced her blade.

Kevin assumed the stance.

"Let's show this guy what happens when you mess with the Pizza Lord."
