# Chapter 36: The Dao of the Crunch

Safe inside the kitchen of the **Dojo of Dopeness**, Kevin stood before a spread of ingredients that would make a Luchador weep with joy.

"Now..." Kevin began, holding up a disk of corn dough. "The taco. It is messy... but delicious. It comes hard, or soft."

He looked at Chef Wang, who was dutifully taking notes with a calligraphy brush.

"But we've got burritos already," Kevin reasoned. "Burritos are soft. They are the pillow of the food world. So, we gonna go hard taco. We need texture contrast."

He tossed the dough into the deep fryer. He used a pair of tongs to fold it in half as it cooked, forming the classic U-shape.

SIZZLE.

"You'll love the crunch!" Kevin promised.

He pulled the golden shell out. It was rigid. It was greasy. It was perfect.

"Now, the filling," Kevin said. "Ground Spirit Beef. Seasoned with cumin, paprika, and the ashes of a Phoenix (for that smoky flavor)."

He spooned the meat into the shell.

"Cheese. Lettuce. Tomato. Simple. Elegant."

He handed the taco to Chen.

"Iceman. Do the honors."

Chen held the hard taco gingerly. It was fragile.

"It feels... brittle," Chen observed. "Like the defenses of a weak sect."

"Just bite it," Kevin urged. "But lean forward. The 'Taco Tilt' is essential to keep the filling from falling on your new vest."

Chen leaned over the counter. He tilted his head 45 degrees. He took a bite.

CRUNCH.

The sound echoed through the kitchen. It was loud. Satisfying.

Chen chewed. His eyes lit up.

"The texture!" Chen exclaimed. "The shell shatters like glass, but the meat inside is tender! It is the duality of Yin and Yang! Hard and soft! Chaos and order!"

"And messy," Kevin added as a piece of lettuce fell onto the counter. "But that's part of the charm. It forces you to be present. You can't multitask while eating a hard taco. It demands your respect."

"It demands napkins," Lin Xue noted, wiping a spot of salsa from Chen's cheek.

"Precisely!" Kevin clapped. "Now, Wang. Mass production. We need thousands of shells. We're launching **Taco Tuesdays** at the Cinema. Half price if you bring a date."

Kevin walked out to the balcony, taco in hand. He looked over his empire.

He had defeated rivals. He had tamed beasts. He had tricked the Gods.

And he had done it all without learning a single actual martial art.

"Real life," Kevin whispered again, taking a crunchy bite.

He looked at the horizon, where the sun was setting in a blaze of orange and purple.

"I wonder what sushi tastes like with Kraken meat?"
