Kevin is nineteen, weighs 140 pounds, and is a self-taught lethal weapon—at least in his parent's basement. His martial arts journey takes a shocking turn when a failed stunt sends him crashing into the breaker panel. Transported to a literal floating mountain tournament in a cultivation world, Kevin thinks he's having a vivid hallucination. Armed only with movie quotes and unearned confidence, he faces off against real immortal warriors who think his ridiculous antics are secret, high-level techniques!
