# Chapter 5: A Hero of a Thousand Names

Before Sal could even begin to translate the gibberish hand signals, Bruce took matters into his own hands. He took a deep breath, puffed out his chest, and projected his voice with the force of a drill sergeant.

"We have the place surrounded!" he boomed, cupping his hands around his mouth for imagined effect. "Release the hostage! We are sending in a negotiator... namely... me!"

"Oh, you have got to be kidding me," Sergeant Miller whispered, raising his pistol again.

Without waiting for a response that would never come, Bruce pivoted. He didn't use the handle on the shattered door. Instead, he drew his foot back and delivered a perfect side-kick to the door's edge, sending it swinging inward with a protesting groan of bent metal. He strode through the doorway, crunching over the broken glass with the swagger of a man walking into his own throne room.

The scene inside was tense. A young man, barely out of his teens, with frantic, darting eyes and a cheap pistol, was holding a terrified, middle-aged cashier by the collar. The air was thick with the smell of spilled soda and fear.

Bruce came to a stop in the middle of the snack aisle, his hands held up in a placating, yet powerful gesture. "All right," he said, his voice now calm and resonant. "No one has to get hurt here today. Let's talk this out."

The gunman and the cashier both stared at him. The gunman's arm trembled, his grip on the cashier's shirt slackening for a moment out of sheer astonishment.

"Who the hell are you?" the gunman stammered, his voice cracking. "Are you a cop?"

A slow, knowing smile spread across Bruce's face. He let out a soft chuckle, a sound full of condescending wisdom.

"Who am I?" he asked, the question hanging in the air. "Who AM I?"

He took a dramatic step forward, his shadow stretching long across the linoleum floor.

"I am the cry for help in the dead of night. I am the silent guardian on the city walls. I am the thunder that awakens the sleeping dragon of justice and the lightning that strikes the heart of evil!"

The gunman just blinked, his confusion overriding his fear.

"In the forgotten temples of the East, they call me 'The Fist of Urgent Care,'" Bruce continued, his voice rising in a crescendo. "In the neon-drenched alleys of the West, I am known only as 'The Night Rider.' I am the Unflinching Spirit, the Last Responder, the Janitor of Justice who mops up the filth of crime! I am the deliverer of deliverance! I... AM... THE KUNG FU MEDIC!"

He struck a dramatic pose, one hand held forward, the other pulled back to his waist, a classic martial arts stance. The silence that followed was absolute, broken only by the hum of the Slurpee machine.

The gunman stared for a long, silent moment. The veins in his neck pulsed. His frantic energy finally found a focus, hardening from confusion into pure, unadulterated rage at the sheer absurdity of the man standing before him.

"You're a dead man," the gunman hissed. He shoved the cashier away from him, the clerk stumbling and falling behind the counter. The gun, which had been aimed at the cashier, now swung around, its black, unblinking eye pointing directly at the center of Bruce's chest.

Hidden from the gunman's view, the cashier slowly, silently, reached a hand under the counter, his fingers brushing against the cool, familiar wood of a baseball bat.

This is it. The culmination of a thousand movies, a million shadow-boxed punches, a lifetime of delusion. The moment of truth.
