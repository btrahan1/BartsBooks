# Chapter 8: The Dregs of the Vat

The Hall of Rebirth was a place of steam and whispers. Massive, bronze-lined vats dotted the cavernous, stone chamber, each filled with a shimmering, turquoise liquid that smelled faintly of ozone and medicinal herbs. This was the terminus for souls deemed worthy of a second chance in the Iron-Blood Realm, a place where the fallen were remade.

Lin, a junior acolyte of the Healing Hand Sect, moved between the vats with an air of practiced weariness. It was her job to process the "newborns"---pulling their soul-slates, checking their assigned roles, and sending them off to their new lives with a standardized orientation pamphlet they rarely read.

A soft chime echoed from Vat 37, a newer model tucked away in the back corner usually reserved for low-priority souls. Lin sighed, wiping a stray strand of black hair from her forehead. "Another one already?" she muttered.

She glided over to the vat's control pedestal, a smooth, obsidian pillar humming with contained energy. She tapped its surface, and a holographic slate of light shimmered into existence, displaying the soul's core data. Her eyes first landed on the physical projection forming within the vat.

A tall, well-built man floated serenely in the glowing liquid. He had a strong jawline and an almost comically heroic physique, a far cry from the wiry, battle-scarred soldiers or withered old men she usually processed. A small, unprofessional thought sparked in her mind.

"Okay, what do we have here? Oh my, he's kind of cute."

She shook her head, focusing back on the data slate. It was time to see what kind of hero she was dealing with. She scanned the stats, her professional curiosity turning to mild disappointment.

`STR: 11, DEX: 12, CON: 11...`

"Decent physicals," she mused aloud. "Not brilliant, but sturdy. A warrior trainee, perhaps? Could be useful on the front lines as a shock trooper."

She continued scrolling down the slate, looking for his class designation. She expected to see "Swordsman, Grade 1" or "Pikeman, Recruit." What she found instead made her blink. And blink again. She leaned closer, convinced the slate was malfunctioning.

`DESIGNATED CLASS: ASSISTANT TO THE ASSISTANT FIELD MEDIC.`

Lin stared at the title. She read it a third time, mouthing the words slowly. "Assistant... to the *Assistant*... Field Medic?"

She had worked in the Hall of Rebirth for five years. She had seen souls assigned to be everything from "Grand Imperial Alchemist" to "Dragon Fodder, Grade 3." She had never, in her entire career, seen a title so bafflingly, so pitifully, specific and subordinate. It was a job title for the person who carried the bags for the person who carried the bags.

"Is that even a thing?" she whispered, tapping the slate as if to clear an error. The title remained, stark and official. This wasn't a glitch. Some higher power had looked at this well-built, heroic-looking soul and designated him to be the lowest rung on a ladder that was already on the floor.

With a heavy, world-weary sigh that was far too old for her years, Lin resigned herself to the bizarre reality of her job. Another day, another cosmic weirdo.

She tapped a final rune on the control pedestal, initiating the draining sequence for Vat 37. The glowing turquoise liquid began to recede, and the man inside stirred for the first time.

"Alright, handsome," Lin muttered, grabbing a standard-issue burlap tunic from a nearby shelf. "Let's wake you up and figure out what to do with you."

Here we go. The legend awakens.
