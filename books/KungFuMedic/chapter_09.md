# Chapter 9: Please Enter Your Designation

The last of the turquoise liquid drained away with a final, gurgling sigh. Air, cool and smelling of damp stone, hit Bruce's skin. His eyes snapped open.

There was no confusion. No gentle reawakening. One moment he was staring into a divine, golden light, the next he was here. A hostile environment. An unknown chamber.

His action-hero instincts, honed by a lifetime of cinema, took over completely.

In a single, explosive movement, he launched himself out of the vat. He didn't climb out; he sprang, his body a coiled spring of muscle. He hit the stone floor, tucking his shoulder into a perfect tactical roll that absorbed all the momentum. He came up out of the roll not on his feet, but by planting his hands and kicking his legs up into a handstand, using the centrifugal force to spin his body around. He landed silently on the balls of his feet in a low crouch, his head snapping up to scan for threats.

His eyes locked onto the only other person in the room: a young woman in simple robes, holding a burlap tunic.

The final move in the sequence was a jump spinning back kick, a blur of motion designed to neutralize the closest threat. It was fast, powerful, and executed with surprising precision. The heel of his foot sliced through the air, stopping a hair's breadth from Lin's nose, the displaced air making her bangs flutter.

"Woah! Big Boy! Calm down!" Lin yelped, taking a single, sharp step back. Her professional calm was shattered, replaced by a jolt of genuine shock. She hadn't even had time to blink. "I'm Lin. Do you remember your name?"

Bruce held his kick-in-progress pose for a dramatic beat before slowly lowering his leg. He narrowed his eyes, still assessing her. But his attention was suddenly pulled away by something far stranger.

A translucent blue rectangle shimmered into existence in front of his face, hovering in the air about two feet away. It was lined with a simple, glowing border, and text appeared on it, written in a language he somehow understood implicitly.

`WELCOME, REINFORCEMENT UNIT #734. PLEASE ENTER YOUR DESIGNATION.` `FIRST NAME: [________________]` `LAST NAME: [__________________]`

Bruce blinked. He looked at Lin, then back at the floating blue box. He reached out a hesitant finger and poked the screen. It rippled slightly at his touch. This was real.

"Your name?" Lin repeated, her voice a little more cautious now. She was watching him poke at what, to her, was completely empty air.

Bruce ignored her. He was focused. A glowing keyboard appeared below the entry fields. He tentatively poked the 'B'. It appeared in the "First Name" box. He grinned. He poked 'R', 'U', 'C', 'E'. *Bruce*. That was his name. He remembered it. But..."Johnson?" It felt... wrong. Lifeless. It was the name of the guy who drove the ambulance. It wasn't the name of the hero who caught a bullet with his teeth.

No. He had a new life. He deserved a new name. A *real* name.

His fingers began to move with newfound purpose. He backspaced over 'Bruce' and began to type, a look of intense concentration on his face. He poked at the letters deliberately.

`FIRST NAME: [BRUCELEEROY]`

He paused, admiring his work. Then he moved to the second field. He had to think for a moment, but then it came to him, the perfect, most heroic title he could imagine.

`LAST NAME: [THEMAGNIFICENT]`

A massive, triumphant grin, the kind that splits a face in two, spread across his features. It was the grin of a man who had just discovered his true purpose in the universe. He jabbed a final, decisive finger at the `[CONFIRM]` button at the bottom of the screen. The blue box flashed once, brightly, and then vanished.

He was whole. He was reborn.

He finally turned his full attention back to the still-wary Lin. He stood up straight, puffed out his chest, and gave her a slight, heroic bow.

"The name," he announced, his voice filled with the weight of a legend, "is BruceLeeroy. BruceLeeroy TheMagnificent."

Let's capture that moment of dawning horror for poor Lin.
