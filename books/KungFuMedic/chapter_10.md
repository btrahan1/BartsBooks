# Chapter 10: The Name on the Slate

Lin's professional demeanor, already cracked by the near-decapitation, finally crumbled into open disbelief. She let out a short, incredulous laugh.

"You can't be serious," she said, shaking her head. "You don't just
*get* a title. Titles are earned. Bestowed by the Sect Masters or the
Emperor himself for acts of great valor. And who has a title of 'The Magnificent'? That's... that's not even a real title."

Bruce simply crossed his powerful arms over his chest, completely unfazed by her skepticism. He looked at her as if she were the one being ridiculous.

"Pfft," he scoffed. "Who says so? It's not a title. It's my name. Says so right there." He jabbed a thumb at the empty air where his registration screen had been.

Lin's eye twitched. The sheer, unadulterated confidence of this man was staggering. He was like a block of granite; logic and reason simply broke against him. She had dealt with arrogant nobles and boastful warriors, but this was something else entirely. This was a fundamental misunderstanding of how reality was supposed to work.

"Alright, 'Magnificent'," she said, her voice dripping with sarcasm. "We'll see what The System has to say about that. Don't move."

She raised her hand, palm forward, towards Bruce. She closed her eyes for a second, muttering a single, clear word: "Inspect."

A soft, golden light emanated from her palm. From her perspective, a new slate, far more detailed than the one at the vat's pedestal, materialized between them. It was a direct query to the soul's core data, an incorruptible record of who and what he was. She scanned it, a smug look on her face, fully expecting to see the name `JOHNSON, BRUCE` and a flag for mental instability.

The smug look faltered. Her eyes widened. She read the first line, then read it again.

`NAME: BRUCELEEROY THEMAGNIFICENT` `CLASS: ASSISTANT TO THE ASSISTANT FIELD MEDIC` `LEVEL: 1`

`STATS` `STR: 11` `DEX: 12` `CON: 11` `INT: 8` `WIS: 7` `LCK: 6`

The name was there. It was real. It was locked into his soul slate, as official as the Emperor's seal. Somehow, this infuriating, handsome, muscle-bound idiot had successfully registered the most absurd name she had ever seen as his actual, factual designation. The System, the infallible, all-knowing System, had just shrugged and said, "Sure, why not?"

The golden light from her palm flickered and died. She lowered her hand slowly, a profound groan escaping her lips. It was the sound of a woman who had just seen the orderly, predictable rulebook of her life set on fire. She stared at the man now known as BruceLeeroy TheMagnificent, not as a cute but confused newborn, but as an omen. An agent of cosmic chaos sent specifically to ruin her day.

"Oh no," she whispered, her head falling into her free hand. "What have I gotten myself into?"
