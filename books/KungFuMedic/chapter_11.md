# Chapter 11: The Janitor of Jolts

Lin took a deep, centering breath, the kind her instructors had taught her for dealing with particularly gruesome battlefield injuries. It didn't help. She decided to reclaim some semblance of control, starting with his name.

"Okay, 'BruceLeeroy'," she said, her voice firm and clipped. "I am not going to be adding your..." she paused, raising her hands and wiggling two fingers on each in a gesture of pure contempt, "...'last name'."

She dismissed the inspection slate with a wave and picked up the burlap tunic she had dropped. "Right. It says here you are to be a Field Medic. Kind of." She tossed the rough garment at him. Bruce caught it out of the air with one hand, a perfect, reflexive snatch.

His heroic grin vanished, replaced by a look of profound offense, as if she'd just suggested he take up knitting.

"What? No!" he exclaimed, the tunic clutched in his fist. "That's a clerical error. A mix-up in the great cosmic switchboard. I am an epic fighter! A hero of... unknown renown!"

He puffed out his chest, striking a new, even more dramatic pose. "I am the Quake Fist that Shatters Mountains! The Shadowless Kicker of Injustice! The Mauler of Malevolence!"

Lin pinched the bridge of her nose, a dull throb starting to pulse behind her right eye. "The slate says you're an assistant's assistant medic. You carry bandages."

Bruce seemed not to hear her. He was on a roll now, the words flowing from a deep wellspring of cinematic nonsense.

"They call me the Crimson Cyclone of Calamity! The Unstoppable Juggernaut of... uh... Jugger-naut-ing! I am the Defibrillating Destroyer, the Janitor of Jolts, the man who puts the 'harm' in 'pharmacist'!"

He finished the declaration with a powerful "Kiai!" shout, chopping his hand through the air. The sound echoed in the stone chamber.

Lin's headache, no longer a dull throb, flared into a sharp, stabbing pain. Each ridiculous title was like a tiny hammer blow against her skull. This wasn't just a simple soul with an overactive imagination. This was a force of nature. An unstoppable tidal wave of pure, concentrated delusion.

She was the first person in the Iron-Blood Realm to encounter him, and she was already beginning to understand the cautionary note Zorp had attached to his file. Supervision wasn't just recommended; it was a desperate, and likely futile, attempt to contain the inevitable chaos he would bring.
