# Chapter 12: The Ladder of Legends

The throbbing in Lin's temples was becoming a real problem. Arguing with this man was like trying to punch the ocean; it was pointless and exhausting. She decided to switch tactics. Her voice softened, losing its sharp, sarcastic edge and taking on the gentle, placating tone one might use on a spooked horse or a very large, confused child.

"Okay," she began, taking a small step closer. "I understand that you might be a bit disappointed. And... chin up." She gave him what she hoped was an encouraging smile. "You may be an Assistant to an Assistant today, but that's the beauty of The System. You can work your way up!"

*(Though I highly doubt it,)* she thought, a wave of pity washing over
her. *(This cosmic charity case will be lucky to survive his first week. Still, if it gets him to put on the tunic and follow me, it's worth a try.)*

"...So, let's get you settled in, what do you say?"

The effect was instantaneous and profound.

Of her entire, carefully crafted speech, Bruce's brain only registered three words: "work your way up." They were the most powerful and motivating words in his entire vocabulary. The clouds of heroic indignation parted, and the bright sun of ambition shone through. His entire posture changed from defiant to dazzlingly optimistic.

"Well, why didn't you say so!" he boomed, his infectious grin returning at full force. He looked at her as if she were a wise and benevolent master, finally revealing the secret to his quest. "That's all you had to say! The sky is the limit! It's a classic underdog story!"

Before Lin could respond, he began pacing the stone floor, his mind already racing, planning out his glorious career path.

"Okay, so first things first, I master this 'Assistant to the Assistant' thing. Probably take me, what, a day? Two, tops," he said, waving a hand dismissively. "Then I get promoted to just 'Assistant.' Then, before you know it, I'll make 'Lead Medic.' From there, I'll establish a new division and become 'Commander of the Emergency Response Valkyries'!"

Lin's encouraging smile became fixed and strained. "We don't have valkyries..."

"Then I'll create them!" he declared, not missing a beat. "And after I've revolutionized battlefield medicine, I'll move into a management role. I'll become the 'Supreme Arch-Healer of the Five Continents'! No, wait---the 'Ultimate Grandmaster of Life and Kicking Butt'!"

He stopped pacing and pointed a finger at the ceiling, a look of prophetic vision in his eyes.

"And when I've done all that," he said, his voice dropping to a near-whisper, filled with awe for his own brilliant destiny. "They'll have no choice but to name me Emperor. Emperor Leeroy The Magnificent! It's a foolproof plan!"

He beamed at her, radiating an aura of absolute certainty. He had just laid out a life plan that started with carrying bandages and ended with ruling the world, and he saw no flaws in his logic.

The headache was back. It had brought friends.

"Right," Lin said, her voice a flat, hollow monotone. "An emperor. Let's... let's just get you your uniform first."
