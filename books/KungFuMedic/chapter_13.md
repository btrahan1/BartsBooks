# Chapter 13: The Wink of the Magnificent

Caught up in the grandeur of his own imperial destiny, Bruce finally stopped pacing. He glanced down to admire his heroic physique, and it was only then that a crucial piece of information finally penetrated his consciousness: he was standing in a strange stone room, talking to a young woman, while being completely and utterly naked.

"Whoa," he said, his eyes widening slightly. The thought of his own nudity hadn't bothered him until the exact moment he became aware of it. With a sudden burst of modesty, he hastily shook out the burlap tunic and threw it on over his head.

The garment was rough, shapeless, and dyed a rather unheroic shade of beige. It settled on his muscular frame like a potato sack. But to Bruce, a uniform was a uniform.

"Okay, not bad," he announced, testing the fit. He immediately dropped to the floor and banged out ten pushups with perfect form, the speed of them making the tunic flap. "Good shoulder mobility."

He sprang back to his feet and went into a series of deep lunges and stretches. "Plenty of room in the hips for roundhouse kicks."

To complete the assessment, he threw a few shadow-boxed punches---a jab, a cross, an uppercut---and finished with a crisp, high kick that whistled through the air.

"Nice and flexible," he concluded with a satisfied nod. "Just like me."

He turned his full, beaming attention back to Lin, who had been watching the entire bizarre fitness demonstration with a look of stunned paralysis. He pointed at her with two finger guns.

"Lead the way, Master Lin! Destiny awaits!" He took two steps toward her, then stopped, a mischievous glint in his eye. "By the way," he said, his voice dropping to a low, conspiratorial murmur. "Did I mention you're kind of cute?"

He followed this with a wink.

It wasn't a subtle, charming wink. It was a facial event. A full-scale muscular contraction that involved his entire left eye, cheek, and a significant portion of his forehead. It was the kind of wink you'd see in a cartoon, accompanied by an audible "ping!" and a glint of light off a tooth. It was the wink to end all winks.

Lin's brain, already battered by his monologues and imperial ambitions, short-circuited. Every instinct she had screamed that this man was an absolute, grade-A, world-class moron. He was arrogant, delusional, and had the tactical awareness of a turnip.

And yet...

*Oh my gods,* she thought, a wave of horrified self-awareness washing
over her. *How can someone so dumb be so adorable, in an idiotic, puppy-dog kind of way?*

She felt a faint blush creep up her neck and ruthlessly suppressed it. This man was her responsibility. He was a walking disaster. He was also, unfortunately, a little bit charming in the same way a golden retriever that keeps running into a glass door is charming.

"Just..." she stammered, pointing a shaky finger towards a large stone archway. "Just follow me, Magnificent. And try not to kick anything. Or anyone."
