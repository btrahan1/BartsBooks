# Chapter 15: The Truce of the Stew Pot

Lin looked from Bruce's genuinely hungry face to the steam rising from the massive stew pots, then back to his face. She opened her mouth to argue, to explain the rules, the protocols, the entire social structure he was so casually ignoring. Then she closed it.

What was the point? Arguing with him was like trying to teach a mountain to sing. The mountain wasn't listening, and she was just wasting her breath. Besides, he had a point. He had just been willed into existence from nothingness. The energy expenditure must have been significant.

Her shoulders slumped in utter defeat. A long, weary sigh escaped her lips, carrying with it the weight of a thousand failed arguments.

"Fine," she said, the word coming out flat and tired. "You're probably right. Help yourself. I'll... I'll get you some bread and cheese to go with it."

Bruce's face lit up as if she had just granted him a knighthood. His hungry focus instantly transformed into pure, radiant joy.

"Awesome! You're the best, Master Lin!" he boomed, giving her a thumbs-up so enthusiastic it nearly knocked over the wiry archer in front of him. The archer shot them both a venomous glare before shuffling forward.

Lin simply shook her head, a faint, involuntary smile touching her lips for the first time. The man was a menace, a walking, talking headache, but his simple, honest delight was surprisingly disarming.

"Don't call me 'Master'," she muttered, already turning away. "Just... stay here. Don't talk to anyone. Don't challenge anyone to a fight. Don't try to 'fix' the stew. Just get the food."

She navigated through the crowd towards the station where bread was being handed out, leaving Bruce to face his first true quest in the Iron-Blood Realm: acquiring the big ladle of the brown stuff.
