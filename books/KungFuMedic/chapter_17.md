# Chapter 17: A Date with Destiny

BruceLeeroy shoveled the last of the bread and cheese into his mouth, chewed it with gusto, and swallowed. He leaned back on the bench, patted his stomach with a loud, satisfied slap, and then unleashed a belch of truly heroic proportions. It was a deep, rumbling sound that echoed across the nearby tables, causing several conversations to halt mid-sentence.

Without missing a beat, he leaned forward, cupped a hand to his mouth as if sharing a secret with the entire cafeteria, and loudly proclaimed, "Well, ex-squeeze me!"

Before anyone could react to this breach of etiquette, he was in motion. He vaulted over the bench, landing lightly on the balls of his feet. He immediately dropped to the stone floor and, to the utter bewilderment of his new fan club, hammered out another ten pushups with blinding speed.

He sprang back up, dusted off his hands with two sharp claps, and flashed a grin at the stunned warriors at the table.

"Smell you later, guys!" he announced, jabbing a thumb back towards a thoroughly mortified Lin. "I've got a date with destiny, if you know what I mean!"

He then turned to Lin, the full force of his personality focused into a single, dazzling expression. It was a smile so bright, so confident, so utterly devoid of self-awareness, that it looped past idiocy and landed somewhere in the vicinity of adorable. It was the smile of a golden retriever who had just successfully figured out how to open the refrigerator, unaware of the mess he was about to cause.

Lin felt her headache, her frustration, and her sense of impending doom all warring with the undeniable, infuriating charm of that stupid, stupid smile. She was going to have to follow this walking disaster to the infirmary, where he would undoubtedly try to "heal" someone by putting them in a headlock.

Her life, she realized with a sinking heart, was now officially a comedy. And she wasn't even the main character.
