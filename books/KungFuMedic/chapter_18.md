# Chapter 18: The Inefficiency of Compassion

After a winding journey up a set of stone stairs, Lin finally led Bruce to a wide, arched doorway. The air that flowed out was heavy with the scent of antiseptic herbs and linen. It was a quieter space than the main hall, filled with the low sounds of pained groans, soft whimpers, and the rustle of medics moving between cots.

"Okay, so here we are," Lin said, her voice dropping to a more respectful, hushed tone. "The Field Hospital. Now, this is where they generally take those with minor wounds that don't require a master's touch, or sometimes... those with no hope for survival. Not all wounds can be healed by magic."

She gestured towards the rows of simple cots that filled the large, well-lit room. Most were occupied by weary-looking soldiers with bandaged limbs or exhausted-looking acolytes. But a small section, off to the side, was different.

"And," Lin continued, her voice softening further, "this is where they bring most of the children. Children have a low health pool, so most of the senior healers won't bother with them. It's not considered an efficient use of their mana."

She said it as a simple, unfortunate fact of life. In a world of constant war and limited resources, a master healer's time and energy were better spent saving a powerful warrior who could turn the tide of a battle than fixing a child's broken arm. It was cold, but it was logical.

To BruceLeeroy TheMagnificent, it was blasphemy.

His cheerful, goofy demeanor vanished. The dazzling smile was replaced by a hard, flat line. He turned to Lin, his eyes blazing with a fire she hadn't seen before---not the fire of theatrical bravado, but of pure, undiluted rage.

"WHAT?" he growled, his voice low and dangerous, causing a nearby medic to look over in alarm. "They won't heal *children*? What kind of a messed-up place is this!"

"Shhh! Bruce, keep your voice down!" Lin hissed, grabbing his arm.

He shook her off, not violently, but with a finality that was absolute. He strode into the center of the room, taking the attention of every conscious person within it. He planted his feet, puffed out his chest, and became a beacon of righteous indignation.

"LISTEN UP!" he roared, his voice booming off the stone walls. "I've been in this world for less than an hour, and I'm hearing things that make my blood boil! I hear you have 'healers' who think they're too important to help a child in need! 'Healers' who measure life in 'mana efficiency'!"

A senior medic, a stern-looking man with a long grey beard, started walking towards him. "You will be silent, newcomer!"

"I WILL NOT BE SILENT!" Bruce bellowed, pointing a finger at the bearded medic. "You call yourselves healers? You're accountants! You're bean counters with magic wands! A hero doesn't check the math! A hero helps those who need helping! That's the only rule!"

He turned his gaze towards the corner where the children lay on their small cots.

"These so-called masters have forgotten their purpose! But that's okay. Because a new day has dawned! A new hero has arrived! And class... is now in session!"

Without another word, he turned his back on the stunned medics and marched with unwavering purpose toward the children's section, leaving a wake of shocked silence and a mortified Lin who was desperately trying to become invisible.
