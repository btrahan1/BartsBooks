# Chapter 19: By the Power of Greyskull!

Bruce stormed into the children's section like a hurricane of righteous purpose. His eyes scanned the small cots, each one holding a child with some minor ailment---a cough, a fever, a bandaged arm. His gaze landed on the first cot, occupied by a small boy with tear tracks on his dusty cheeks, looking forlornly at a bloody scrape on his knee.

An assistant medic, a young, nervous-looking acolyte barely older than Lin, was dabbing the scrape with a cloth. He looked up in alarm as Bruce's massive shadow fell over him.

"You there!" Bruce commanded, his voice echoing with the thunder of a dozen movie heroes. He pointed a dramatic, trembling finger at the boy. "What is wrong with this child? This poor, innocent child? Tell me! Tell me now!"

The young acolyte flinched, nearly dropping his cleaning cloth. "Uh... he... he has a skinned knee, sir," the boy stammered. "He fell while playing near the training yard."

Bruce leaned in, examining the scraped knee with the intensity of a master surgeon diagnosing a fatal disease. He saw the scraped skin, the welling blood, the dirt. He saw an injustice that could not be allowed to stand.

"A skinned knee..." Bruce repeated, his voice a low, dangerous whisper. He straightened up, his face a mask of grim determination. "Well, we'll see about that!"

He gently nudged the terrified acolyte aside. He knelt down beside the cot, placing one hand on the boy's small shoulder. He closed his eyes, his face contorting with effort as he reached deep inside himself, searching for a power he did not know how to use but was absolutely certain he possessed. He needed a battle cry. A powerful incantation. The most powerful one he knew.

His eyes snapped open. He raised his free hand to the heavens, fingers splayed wide.

"BY THE POWER OF GREYSKULL!" he bellowed, his voice shaking the very foundations of the infirmary.

He slammed his open palm down on the boy's forehead.

For a split second, nothing happened. Then, a tiny, almost apologetic blue flash flickered between his hand and the child. The boy let out a small grunt, more surprised than anything else.

Bruce held the pose, his hand still on the boy's head, the echo of his cry fading into the stunned silence of the room. He felt... drained. Utterly and completely empty. But he also felt a profound sense of accomplishment.

He removed his hand and rose to his feet, turning his back on the child. He took a deep, world-weary breath.

"My job is done here," he announced to the room at large.

He took one heroic step.

Then a second.

And then, with all the grace of a felled tree, he pitched forward and collapsed flat on his face, out cold. The sound of his unconscious body hitting the stone floor was the only thing that broke the spell of silence, followed by Lin's perfectly timed, soul-deep groan. The magnificent hero had made his grand entrance, and his first healing act was a resounding, face-planting success.
