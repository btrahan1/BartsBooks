# Chapter 20: The Whispers of a Power Unknown

The world was a fuzzy, grey blanket. Sounds reached Bruce as if from the bottom of a deep pool, distorted and distant. He was lying on his back, staring up at a blurry stone ceiling. The scratchy texture of a cot was beneath him, and the air smelled of herbs and confusion.

He was fading in and out, drifting on a sea of profound exhaustion. In the moments of clarity, snippets of conversation pierced the fog.

"...never seen anything like it. He just... collapsed."

"...the boy's knee, though. Look. Not just clean. The skin is completely regenerated. Not a scar."

*Victory...* Bruce thought, a faint, delirious smile touching his lips.
*A flawless... procedure...*

He drifted away again, into a dream of shadow-boxing with ninjas on a speeding train. Then another voice, sharp and familiar, pulled him back.

"I'm telling you, Master Elara, he just yelled. Something about a...'grey skull'?" It was Lin. She sounded stressed.

A deeper, calmer voice responded. It was the bearded medic from before. "A grey skull? An ancient deity, perhaps? A forgotten incantation? It is not in any of our texts. The channeling was crude, unfocused... pure brute force. It's a miracle it didn't scramble the boy's chi permanently."

*My technique...* Bruce's mind grasped the words. *They're analyzing...
my secret technique... They don't... understand its power...*

Another wave of exhaustion pulled him under. When he surfaced again, they were talking about him.

"His Wisdom is only a seven!" Lin's voice was a strained whisper. "He shouldn't be able to channel spiritual energy at all, let alone manifest a healing effect!"

"And his designation," the old medic, Master Elara, mused. "'Assistant to the Assistant'... The System must be malfunctioning. It makes no sense. To expend one's entire mana pool for a single point of healing... it is the most inefficient use of power I have ever witnessed."

Bruce's woozy brain latched onto the fragments. *Wisdom of the heavens... The System is malfunctioning... because of my power... They've never witnessed... such power...*

It all made perfect sense. He was an anomaly. A prodigy. A hero whose power was so immense it defied their understanding and broke their rules. He had taken on the great and terrible foe known as "Skinned Knee" and, though the battle had cost him dearly, he had emerged victorious.

As he drifted into a deeper, more peaceful sleep, a familiar blue screen flickered into existence in his mind's eye.

`[COMBAT ACTION SUCCESSFUL]` `NEW SKILL ACQUIRED: LESSER MINOR HEAL (LV. 1)` `WISDOM STAT INCREASED BY 1.` `CURRENT WISDOM: 8`

A final, triumphant thought echoed in his consciousness before the darkness took him completely.

*Leveling up... Just as planned...*
