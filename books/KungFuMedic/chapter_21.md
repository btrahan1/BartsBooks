# Chapter 21: The Second Law of Motion

Hours later, consciousness returned to Bruce not as a gentle tide, but as a lightning strike. The memory of the blue screen, the confirmation of his newfound skill, the glorious +1 to his Wisdom---it was all there, a jolt of pure, unadulterated victory. His mana-hangover was gone, replaced by the exhilarating buzz of a successful quest.

His eyes snapped open. The stone ceiling was sharp and clear. The distant sounds of the infirmary were no longer a muddled dream, but a call to action. There were more children to heal! More legends to be forged!

He swung his legs over the side of the cot, a grin of pure determination plastered on his face.

"Welp!" he declared to the room, his voice a booming announcement of his recovery. "Time to get to work!"

He didn't just stand up. He exploded off the cot, attempting to launch himself directly into a heroic, three-point landing in the middle of the floor.

His spirit was willing, but his body, having just been used as a conduit to drain every last drop of his life force, had not received the memo.

The heroic leap became an ungraceful plummet. His legs, having absolutely no energy left to give, buckled instantly. The powerful spring became a boneless collapse. He hung in the air for a fraction of a second, a perfect picture of failed ambition, before gravity took over completely.

He hit the floor with the dull, ignominious thud of a sack of potatoes. Face first. Again.

He didn't move. He just lay there, a beige-tunic-clad heap on the cold stone. A few seconds of pained silence passed.

"Maybe I'll just rest here for a few more minutes," a voice mumbled, the words muffled and distorted by the dusty stone floor.

From across the room, where she'd been watching with a sense of dreadful anticipation, Lin let out the longest, most soul-weary sigh of her entire life.
