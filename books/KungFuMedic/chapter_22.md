# Chapter 22: All Right, All Right, All Right

A gentle but insistent shaking pulled Bruce from the strange, dreamless sleep of the utterly exhausted. It was Lin's voice, not distant this time, but right next to his ear, filled with a mix of concern and exasperation.

"Bruce? Bruce? Bruce! Wake up, your mana is full!"

His eyes fluttered open. He was no longer on the floor, but back on the cot. Lin was leaning over him, one hand on his shoulder, her brow furrowed with worry. The exhaustion was gone, replaced by a fresh, thrumming energy that hummed just beneath his skin. His mana pool was full. He was back in business.

A slow, impossibly cool grin spread across his face. He looked up at Lin, his eyes half-lidded, and in a perfect, laid-back Texas drawl, he spoke.

"All right, all right, all right."

He started to sit up, ready to leap back into action, but Lin pressed down firmly on his shoulder, holding him in place.

"No! Stop!" she ordered, her voice firm. "Bruce, you have to be more careful! You can't just throw your energy around like that. You nearly died! You only have a Wisdom of 7. You don't have the capacity for..."

"Eight," Bruce interrupted, his voice returning to its normal, booming timbre. The McConaughey persona vanished as quickly as it had appeared.

Lin stopped, her train of thought completely derailed. She stared at him, confused. "What?"

"My Wisdom," Bruce clarified, as if explaining the simplest thing in the world. He tapped his temple with a knowing finger. "It's 8 now. I leveled up."
