# Chapter 23: The Flawless Logic of +1

Lin recoiled as if he had slapped her. "That's impossible! No one levels up after casting their first spell! It takes months, sometimes years, of meditation and practice to raise a core stat by a single point!"

But even as the words of denial left her lips, a seed of doubt was planted in her mind. *Well,* a small, traitorous voice in her head whispered, *no one casts their first spell with 7 Wisdom, either.* Every rule she knew, every fundamental law of magic and metaphysics, seemed to bend and warp around this infuriating, magnificent man.

Bruce, seeing the conflict on her face, misinterpreted it as simple, heartfelt concern. He sat up, this time slowly, and placed a hand on Lin's shoulder. His expression became one of grave, paternal seriousness. It was a look he'd copied from a war movie general comforting a young soldier before the final charge.

"Darling," he said, his voice a low, gentle rumble. "I appreciate your concern, I really do. But a man's gotta do what a man's gotta do."

He gave her shoulder a firm, reassuring squeeze. "Besides," he continued, a look of flawless, unshakeable logic in his eyes, "I have 8 Wisdom now! That's a whole extra point of power. I probably won't even pass out this time!"

The word "probably" hung in the air, a testament to his complete and utter lack of understanding of the forces he was playing with. He saw his single-point increase not as a marginal improvement, but as a complete game-changer, the equivalent of upgrading from a slingshot to a cannon.

He swung his legs off the cot and, this time, stood up successfully. He looked down at Lin, who was still sitting, stunned into silence by his terrible, terrible reasoning. He gave her a condescendingly warm smile, patted her on the head like a small child, and then gestured grandly to the rest of the children's ward.

"Now, Nurse," he said, his voice brimming with renewed purpose. "Please show me to my next patient. There are skinned knees that need vanquishing."
