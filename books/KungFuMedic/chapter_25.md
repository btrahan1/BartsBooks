# Chapter 25: The Goddess GreySkull

Bruce leaned back over the cot, his voice a low, soothing murmur that commanded the little girl's complete attention.

"Now there, Mei," he began, "I need you to be brave. I am a professional. I have set more than a few broken arms in my time."

*(Well, two, if he was being honest, and one of them was on a training
mannequin whose arm he'd accidentally snapped off during a particularly enthusiastic CPR demonstration.)*

He ignored the silent correction from his own conscience and continued, laying out the plan with the calm authority of a seasoned veteran.

"Here's what I'm going to do," he explained, pointing a thumb at a horrified-looking Lin. "This pretty lady here is going to hold you tight, give you a big hug, so you feel safe. Then, I'm going to do a real, quick pull on your arm to get the bones lined up. After that," he leaned in closer, his voice dropping to a conspiratorial whisper, "I'm going to invoke our great and powerful Goddess of Healing..."

He looked left, then right, as if sharing a universe-altering secret. "...(psst... her name is GreySkull)... and then you will be all better. Two seconds, max! Can you be brave for two seconds?"

Mei, caught up in the sheer, undeniable confidence of his plan, looked from Bruce's earnest face to Lin's. The fear in her eyes was replaced by a flicker of determination. She gave a small, hesitant nod.

Lin, on the other hand, was frozen. She had been referred to as a "pretty lady," enlisted as an accomplice in a highly unorthodox medical procedure, and was now being introduced to a goddess that sounded suspiciously like a skull. Her day had gone from strange to surreal.

"I... I don't think..." she started to protest, but Bruce was already waving her over impatiently.

"No time to waste, Nurse!" he whispered urgently. "The patient is prepped and ready! Cuddle positions, stat!"

With a sigh that surrendered her last remaining shred of professional dignity, Lin moved to the head of the cot and gently wrapped her arms around the little girl, holding her close. Mei squeezed her tight, burying her face in Lin's tunic.

Bruce gave them both a satisfied nod. He took a firm but gentle grip on Mei's splinted forearm.

"Okay, Mei," he said, his voice now a focused, steely command. "On the count of three. One... two..."

He didn't wait for three.
