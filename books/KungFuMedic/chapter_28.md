# Chapter 28: The Magnificent Promotion

Lin found him exactly where she expected to: not resting in the dorms, but in a deserted corner of the training yard, long after the evening drills had ended. He was shirtless, his burlap tunic folded neatly on the ground, going through a series of slow, deliberate martial arts forms under the moonlight. He looked focused, powerful, and, for a moment, almost wise.

Then he punctuated a move with a loud "Kee-ya!" and tried to kick a leaf off a tree branch five feet above his head, missed, and almost fell over. The moment of wisdom passed.

Lin cleared her throat. "BruceLeeroy."

He spun around, not startled, but as if he'd been expecting her. "Master Lin! Come to join my midnight meditation? A warrior must hone his vessel, even when the moon sleeps!"

"It's just 'Lin'," she said, the correction now a weary reflex. "And no. I'm here on official business. Master Elara and Matron Hwei have reviewed your... performance... from today."

Bruce's eyes lit up with anticipation. He puffed out his chest. "Ah, the review. I assume they were impressed."

Lin took a deep breath. "They have decided to promote you. You are no longer an Assistant to the Assistant. You are now, officially, Assistant Field Medic TheMagnificent."

The reaction was instantaneous. He threw his head back and let out a triumphant laugh.

"A promotion! YES!" he roared, punching the air. He jogged in a small, celebratory circle. "Took me a bit longer than I thought, I'll be honest. At least a whole day! But you know what they say," he stopped and pointed a sagely finger at her, "Rome wasn't built in a day!"

Before Lin could even begin to process the sheer audacity of that statement, he was off, his mind already charting a new, glorious course to the top.

"Okay, so this changes things," he said, pacing back and forth. "The whole 'Emperor' plan was good, but it was yesterday's plan. It was too political. I've been thinking, and I have a new vision. A better one."

Lin braced herself.

"First, 'Assistant,'" he began, ticking a point off on his finger. "I'll master this role by sunrise. Then, I'll leverage my new position to create a new kind of entertainment. 'Wuxia-on-Ice'! It's sword fighting, but with skates. The crowd will love the triple axel into a dragon-tail sweep!"

Lin just stared, her face blank. "We don't have ice..."

"We'll make it! With magic!" he countered, waving away the logistical hurdles. "Once I'm the world's greatest Wuxia-on-Ice champion, I'll launch my own line of lifestyle products. 'Magnificent's Moon-Chi Tea,' 'Leeroy's Lethal Liniments'! We'll make a fortune!"

He stopped pacing and grabbed her by the shoulders, his eyes wild with visionary fire.

"And then---and this is the best part---I'll use that fortune to buy a private mountain, hollow it out, and build the world's first combination dojo and animal sanctuary! We'll rescue injured spirit-beasts and teach them the ways of the Drunken Master! It's foolproof!"

He beamed at her, utterly convinced of the genius of his new, completely different, and equally impossible life plan.

Lin didn't say a word. She just closed her eyes and felt the familiar, gentle thrum of a headache beginning its slow, agonizing return. It felt, she thought, a bit like coming home.
