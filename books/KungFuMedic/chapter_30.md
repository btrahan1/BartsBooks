# Chapter 30: The Magnificent's New Life

Lin found him at dawn, not in the dorms, but back in the children's ward, quietly folding a piece of paper into an intricate-looking dragon. The room was peaceful; all the cots were empty, the children having been declared healthy and sent back to their families. He looked up as she approached, a bright, ready-for-action smile on his face.

"Master Lin! What's the mission for today? Got a platoon of bruised knees that need a talking to? A battalion of sniffles that need vanquishing?"

Lin held up a hand, a gesture she was getting very good at. "BruceLeeroy... today is your day off."

Bruce blinked, the concept clearly foreign to him. "Day... off? What's that? Is that some kind of secret training?"

"No," Lin said patiently. "It's a day of no work. No complaints. No sick children today. No cuts, no broken arms, no fevers. You've healed them all. For now. You pushed yourself to the absolute limit yesterday, and if you don't let your mind recuperate, you will be useless."

She took a deep breath, steeling herself for his reaction to the next part.

"So today..." she sighed, the sound heavy with resignation. "...today we are going to study."

Bruce's eyes went wide with a look of genuine, profound horror, as if she'd just sentenced him to a thousand years of torture. "Study? Like... with books? And... reading?"

Seeing the panic on his face, Lin softened her approach. She stepped closer, her voice becoming earnest and persuasive.

"Bruce, listen to me," she said, trying to appeal to the hero, not the himbo. "I know what you are doing is frankly, quite remarkable. I've never seen anything like it. But don't you think you could do more if you... like... actually knew a bit more about how things work?"

She saw a flicker of interest in his eyes and pressed her advantage.

"Think about it. How mana works? So you could heal more people before needing a nap on the floor? How spells work? So maybe you could learn something even more powerful than 'Lesser Minor Heal'?" She leaned in, delivering the final, crucial point. "Maybe a little... anatomy? So you know exactly *what* you're healing?"

The gears in Bruce's head were visibly turning. He hated the idea of "studying." It sounded boring. But "learning more powerful spells" and "knowing anatomy"... that sounded like a power-up. It sounded like unlocking a new skill tree.

He stroked his chin, a look of deep contemplation on his face. "So what you're saying is... this isn't studying. This is... a tactical knowledge-enhancement session. A mission to upgrade my internal database."

Lin let out a breath she didn't realize she'd been holding. "Yes, Bruce," she said, latching onto the lifeline he'd thrown her. "Exactly. A mission to upgrade your internal database."

He slapped his hands together, his enthusiasm restored. "Well, why didn't you say so! Let's do it! To the library-dojo! Lead the way, Sensei Lin!"

A silent, horrified scream echoed in the chambers of Lin's mind. '*Sensei Lin.*' *Oh no. What have I done? Goddess GreySkull, if you're real, please help me through this day.*

She forced a tight, brittle smile onto her face. "Of course. To the... library-dojo... we go."
