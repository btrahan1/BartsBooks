Bruce is a six-foot-two EMT who believes he is a lethal weapon and a martial arts master. His partners, the weary veteran Sal and the sharp-witted Maya, view him with the exasperated affection usually reserved for a very large, very clumsy golden retriever. When a priority one call comes in for an armed robbery, Bruce sees it not as a dangerous medical emergency, but as his destiny to be a city hero. Join the hilarious and chaotic adventures of Unit 73's most dangerous (and deluded) medic!
