# Chapter 23: The Last Quiet Place

Brenda's office became the nerve center as planned. A large map of the town was pinned to her wall, and she used different colored thumbtacks to track the movements of the tourists—the "friendlies."

I stopped in on the fifth day. The phone rang while I was there. Brenda answered, listened for a moment, and made a note.

"That was Will," she said after hanging up. "A silver Mercedes with California plates just pulled up to the Inn. One man, business suit. Looks like he's here to stay."

***

"Did Eleanor's team make contact?" I asked.

"Not yet," Brenda said, her expression serious. "He walked right past them. Didn't even take the muffin."

A chill went down my spine. *The muffins were our first line of defense. This was different. This wasn't a tourist. This was the next wave. This was the developer. The plan had worked, but its success had just attracted a much bigger shark.*
