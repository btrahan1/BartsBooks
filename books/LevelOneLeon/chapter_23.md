# Chapter Twenty-Three: The Variable of Dimples

The adrenaline of the first match was fading, replaced by a cerebral
buzzing.

Leon sat on his bench in the holding pen, the black cat curled next to
his thigh. He was trying to visualize the bracket.

"Next opponent," Leon muttered, drawing lines in the dirt with his
staff. " Likely 'Swiftfoot Silas.' Level 5 Rogue. High Agility build.
Probably uses daggers and dirty tricks."

*Strategy:* The Muffler caps Leon's Agility at 9. That was fast, but a
specialized Rogue might be faster in short bursts. Leon couldn't rely on
being faster. He had to be *smarter*.

"If he goes for a backstab," Leon reasoned, "I need to pivot 180 degrees
and utilize the \[Sweep\] mechanic. But I have to be careful not to
break his shins. Shins are fragile."

He closed his eyes, trying to simulate the combat geometry.

Vector A intersects Vector B...

"Leon?"

The simulation shattered.

Leon opened his eyes. Clara was standing there again. She wasn't holding
a basket this time; she was holding a damp towel and a waterskin. Her
cheeks were flushed from the heat of the crowd (or perhaps from
cheering), and a few strands of strawberry-blonde hair had escaped her
bonnet, framing her face in soft, chaotic curls.

Leon's **Intellect 8** immediately tried to pull up the combat stats for
'Swiftfoot Silas.'

Instead, his **Charisma 7** (and perhaps his human heart) pulled up a
new file: *Clara_CloseUp.png.*

"Oh," Leon said. "Hello, Clara."

"I brought you water," she said, offering the skin. "My uncle isn't mad,
by the way. He said sitting on him was a 'boss move.' He's actually
buying drinks for everyone and bragging that it took a 'Level 4 Titan'
to pin him."

Leon took the waterskin. His fingers brushed hers.

*Data Point: Skin temperature warm. Texture soft.* *System Alert: Heart
rate increasing. Focus compromised.*

"That is... generous of him," Leon said. He took a drink, but his eyes
stayed on Clara.

He had always known Clara was "nice." She was efficient at the bakery.
She was polite to her elders. But looking at her now, framed by the dust
motes of the arena waiting room, Leon realized something else.

She was symmetrical. Very symmetrical.

Her nose had a slight, upward tilt that was aerodynamically pleasing.
Her eyes were the color of toasted hazelnuts. And when she smiled---like
she was doing right now---her cheeks did a folding thing.

*Dimples,* Leon identified. *Structural depressions caused by muscle
contractions. Why are they so... effective?*

"Are you nervous about the next one?" Clara asked, stepping a little
closer. She reached out and dabbed a smudge of dirt off his shoulder
with the towel.

The gesture was intimate. Domestic.

Leon's brain stopped processing Rogue evasion tactics entirely.

"I..." Leon started. He cleared his throat. "I am... calculating."

"Calculating what?" Clara tilted her head.

"The... variables," Leon murmured. "You have dimples, Clara."

Clara froze. Her hand hovered over his shoulder. Her face went bright
red. "I... yes? I do."

"They are very efficient," Leon said, his voice serious. "They increase
the surface area of your smile. It makes the expression... significantly
more radiant."

Clara let out a breathy laugh, covering her mouth with her hand. "Leon!
You say the strangest things."

"I'm just observing," Leon defended weakly. "It's data."

He realized he wasn't thinking about the tournament. He wasn't thinking
about the System. He was thinking about how nice it would be to sit on a
porch and watch those dimples happen over a plate of brioche.

*Is this attraction?* Leon wondered. *It feels like a mild fever. But
pleasant.*

He found himself leaning in. Just an inch. Drawn by the gravity of the
hazelnut eyes.

HISS.

A sharp pain dug into his thigh.

Leon jumped, nearly dropping the waterskin.

The black cat had extended its claws and dug them into his leg. It was
staring at him with narrow, judgmental slits. Its ears were pinned back.

*Focus, you idiot,* the cat seemed to vibrate. *You are about to fight a
man with knives.*

Clara jumped back, startled. "Oh! The kitty is grumpy."

"The kitty is a coach," Leon realized, rubbing his leg. "It sensed my
concentration dropping."

He looked at Clara. He forced his brain to reboot.

"Clara," he said, straightening his posture. "Thank you for the water.
And the towel. But I need to... I need to review my geometry. If I lose
focus, I might get stabbed. Getting stabbed would make it difficult to
dance later."

Clara's eyes widened. "Right! Of course! Don't get stabbed!"

She backed away, clutching the towel. "I'll be cheering for you! Aim for
the... geometry!"

She turned and ran back toward the stands, looking back once to wave.

Leon watched her go. He sighed.

"She is cute," he admitted out loud. "Very cute. Like a prize-winning
piglet, but... better."

The cat meowed---a dry, sarcastic sound.

"I know," Leon told the cat. "Focus. Rogue. Daggers. Don't die."

"Group C! Match Two!" The announcer bellowed. "Leon the Farmer versus
Swiftfoot Silas!"

Leon stood up. He cycled his Ki, trying to flush the "Romance Fever" out
of his system and replace it with "Combat Cold."

But as he walked toward the gate, he found a new motivation settling in
his gut.

He didn't just want to win for the fifty silver anymore.

He wanted to win because Clara was watching. And he really, really
didn't want to get stabbed in front of a girl with dimples like that.

"System," Leon whispered. "Maximize Agility within public parameters. We
need to be flashy."

**\[Public Level: 4\]** **\[Agility: 9 (Ready)\]**

Leon gripped his staff. "Let's go dance."
