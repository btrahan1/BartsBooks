# Chapter Twenty-Four: The Velocity Glitch

Swiftfoot Silas lived up to his name.

The Rogue was a blur of leather and steel. He didn't run; he flickered.
One moment he was in front of Leon, the next he was sliding through the
dirt to the left, his dual daggers flashing like silver teeth.

Swish. Swish.

Leon parried. *Clack. Clack.*

The walnut staff moved in a tight defensive perimeter. Leon was
sweating.

Unlike Gorlag, who was a stationary object, Silas was a variable that
refused to solve.

"Too slow, Farmer!" Silas taunted, bouncing on the balls of his feet. He
feinted left, dropped his shoulder, and spun right.

Leon's **Wisdom 9** saw the feint. Leon's **Agility 9** (Muffler Capped)
tried to react.

He pushed off his back foot to pivot. But Silas used a Skill: **\[Sprint
Burst\]**. His speed doubled instantly.

The dagger was coming for Leon's ribs. The Muffler was screaming,
holding Leon back to "Level 4" speeds. If he didn't move faster, he was
going to get cut.

Leon didn't think. He didn't consult the algorithm. He just *pushed*.

He poured Ki into his ankles. He forced the "Safety Latch" of the System
to bend.

CREAK.

**\[System Alert: High-Speed Evasion Required.\]** **\[Public Parameter
Limit Exceeded.\]** **\[Adjusting Agility...\]**

\[Agility: 9 \> 10 (Override)\]

The update hit Leon like a hiccup in gravity.

He intended to slide two inches to the left. Instead, his frictionless
joints and upgraded speed launched him *two feet* to the left.

"Whoa!" Leon yelped.

He over-rotated. His boots lost traction on the loose dirt. He didn't
look like a master martial artist; he looked like a man slipping on a
banana peel.

He flailed. The staff swung wildly.

Silas, expecting a standard dodge, lunged into empty air where Leon was
supposed to be. But Leon wasn't there. Leon was currently horizontal,
falling backward.

*Opportunity in chaos,* Leon's brain supplied.

As he fell, Leon didn't try to catch himself. He surrendered to the
momentum. He planted the end of the staff into the ground and used it as
a pivot point, turning his fall into a clumsy, lopsided cartwheel.

His legs swung around.

Silas was recovering from his missed lunge, his back turned for a split
second.

Leon's boot caught Silas behind the knee.

It wasn't a powerful kick. It was a trip. A classic "Sheep Hook"
maneuver.

"Ack!" Silas squawked.

The Rogue's legs went out from under him. He hit the dirt face-first,
sliding through the dust.

Leon landed on his feet---barely. He wobbled, took two stumbling steps
to regain his balance, and spun around, bringing the staff down.

He stopped the tip of the wood one inch from the back of Silas's head.

"Yield?" Leon panted, his chest heaving.

Silas groaned, spitting out dirt. He looked at the staff hovering over
his skull. He looked at Leon, who looked just as surprised as he was.

"You... you crazy bastard," Silas wheezed. "You cartwheeled? Who
cartwheels in a duel?"

"It was an improvised rotational evasion," Leon corrected breathlessly.
"Yield?"

"I yield, I yield."

"Winner! Leon the Farmer!"

The crowd went ballistic. They hadn't seen the System glitch. They
hadn't seen Leon slip. They had seen a miraculous, drunken-master style
dodge followed by a decisive counter-attack.

"L.O.L.! L.O.L.!" The chant rocked the stands.

Leon pulled the staff back and offered a hand to Silas.

Silas took it, and Leon hauled him up. The Rogue dusted himself off,
checking his daggers.

"You're fast," Silas admitted, shaking his head. "I popped \[Sprint
Burst\] and you still cleared the zone. What's your Agility?"

"Ten," Leon beamed. He felt giddy. The adrenaline was singing in his
blood. He had almost fallen on his face, but he hadn't! He had solved
the puzzle! "Just hit ten."

"Ten?" Silas's eyes widened. "At Level 4? That's human peak. You're a
freak, Farmer."

"Thank you!" Leon said, shaking Silas's hand enthusiastically. "You were
incredible! That feint to the left? The torque you generated on your
ankle was textbook perfection. I nearly didn't see it."

Silas blinked. He was used to winners gloating, or being stoic. He
wasn't used to the winner looking like a kid who just got a puppy.

"Uh... thanks?" Silas said. "You're... you're alright, Leon."

Leon turned to the crowd. He raised his staff.

He saw Clara jumping up and down. He saw the Baker cheering.

He felt a bubble of pure laughter rise in his chest. This wasn't
farming. This wasn't quiet contemplation. This was chaos. It was messy,
dangerous, and required instantaneous math.

And he loved it.

"That was fun!" Leon shouted to no one in particular, a wide grin
splitting his face. "That was really fun!"

He walked out of the ring, his step light, his Agility 10 making him
feel like he was walking on springs.

He found his bench in the waiting area. The black cat was still there.
It opened one green eye.

"I know," Leon said to the cat, sitting down and wiping his face. "I
slipped. I over-calculated the friction coefficient."

The cat yawned, stretching its claws.

"But I won," Leon said, his eyes shining. "And did you see that trip?
Just like catching a runaway lamb."

The cat purred. A deep, rumble of approval.

Leon leaned back against the wall, his heart still racing. He looked at
his hand. It wasn't shaking from fear. It was shaking from excitement.

"One more match," Leon whispered. "I wonder what happens if I hit
Strength 11?"
