# Chapter Twenty-Seven: The Resume of a Workaholic

The sun hadn't quite breached the horizon, painting the sky in a bruised
palette of indigo and grey. The air was cold, the kind that froze the
breath in the lungs.

Leon stood at the property line of the farm, gripping the handles of the
travel cart.

It was a magnificent cart. The suspension was reinforced with
dungeon-ash wood. The wheels were banded with iron he had tempered
himself. And in the back, sitting comfortably on a bed of premium straw
and surrounded by sacks of feed, was Bessie the Cow.

She chewed her cud, looking regal and entirely unbothered by the fact
that she was about to be hauled across the kingdom by a human.

"Are we ready, Inspector?" Leon asked.

The black cat sat on top of Bessie's head, nestling into the warm fur
between her ears. The Inspector yawned, showing a flash of pink tongue
and white fangs. *Proceed.*

Leon looked back at the farmhouse. A single candle burned in the kitchen
window. Grandpa Silas stood on the porch, leaning on his new walnut
staff. He didn't wave. He just raised the staff an inch off the ground
and thumped it once. A warrior's salute.

Leon nodded back. He turned his face to the road.

"Hup," Leon grunted.

He leaned into the harness. His **Strength 10** (Public) engaged. The
cart, weighing easily a thousand pounds with the cow and supplies,
rolled forward smoothly.

*Squeak-thrum.* The leaf springs took the weight.

Leon took one step. Two.

He crossed the invisible line where the farm's property ended and the
King's Road began.

DING.

It wasn't a normal notification. It was a cacophony. It sounded like
someone had dropped a box of silverware down a flight of stairs inside
his head.

Leon stumbled, bracing himself against the cart handles.

**\[System Alert: Zone Transition Detected.\]** **\[Leaving Tutorial
Area: Oakhaven Farmlands.\]** **\[Entering Open World.\]**

**\[Initiating Skill Consolidation Protocol...\]** **\[Scanning Subject
Memory for Repetitive Actions...\]** **\[Analyzing Muscle Memory...\]**
**\[Analyzing Crafting History...\]**

The blue screens began to cascade in front of Leon's eyes, scrolling so
fast they blurred.

*... 10 years of Hoeing detected ...* *... 50,000 hours of Soil
Management ...* *... 5,000 hours of Blacksmithing (Non-System) ...* *...
2,000 hours of Unarmed Grappling (Livestock Class) ...*

"What is happening?" Leon muttered, blinking rapidly. "It feels like my
brain is being reorganized by a librarian on caffeine."

**\[Processing Complete.\]** **\[Legacy Skills Quantified.\]**
**\[Updating Status Sheet...\]**

The scrolling stopped. A massive, multipage window hovered in the air,
blocking his view of the road.

"Oh my," Leon whispered.

**\[Name: Leon\]** **\[Public Level: 4\]** **\[True Status: ERROR (See
Adjudicator Note 74-B)\]**

**\[Combat Skills:\]** \* **Blunt Weapon Mastery:** Level 42 (Master) \*
*Passive: +40% Stun Chance. +20% Armor Penetration against Rigid
Structures.* \* **Unarmed Combat (Grappling):** Level 48 (Grandmaster)
\* *Passive:* '*Iron Grip*'* - Opponents with lower Strength cannot
break hold.* \* *Active:* '*Pacify*'* - Non-lethal takedowns yield +10%
Social Credit.* \* **Evasion:** Level 35 (Expert) \* *Passive:*
'*Frictionless*'* - Movement speed unaffected by terrain.*

**\[Life Skills:\]** \* **Blacksmithing:** Level 50 (Grandmaster) \*
*Specialty: Molecular realignment (Ki Infusion).* \* **Leatherworking:**
Level 40 (Master) \* *Specialty: Waste-Free Harvest.* \* **Herbalism:**
Level 45 (Master) \* *Specialty: Intuitive Mixture.* \* **Cooking:**
Level 30 (Journeyman) \* *Buff:* '*Comfort Food*'* - Meals provide
mental health regeneration.*

**\[Unique Skill:\]** \* **Agrarian Arts:** Level 99 (ZENITH) \*
*Description: You are one with the soil. The cycle of life and death is
your tool. You can grow crops in stone. You can till the earth with your
intent. Nature recognizes you as a peer.*

Leon stared at the list.

"Level 99 Agrarian Arts?" He frowned. "That seems high. I still have
trouble with aphids on the squash."

He looked at the Combat Skills.

"Grappling Level 48? Grandmaster?" He looked at his hands. "I just hold
things until they stop moving. Is that really a martial art?"

The Inspector let out a loud *meow* from atop the cow. The cat seemed to
be reading the screen over Leon's shoulder. It looked impressed,
specifically by the '*Comfort Food*' buff.

Leon swiped the screen to the side, minimizing it to a small icon in his
peripheral vision.

"This is... a lot of data," Leon admitted. "The Adjudicator said she was
patching me. I didn't realize she was going to write a biography."

He gripped the cart handles again. He felt different. Before, he just
knew how to do things because he had done them. Now, the System
*acknowledged* that he knew them.

He looked at a pothole in the road ahead.

Usually, he would just walk around it.

Now, a red trajectory line appeared in his vision, calculating the
optimal path for the cart wheels to minimize vibration for the cow.

\[Skill Activation: Agrarian Arts (Logistics) - Pathfinding
Auto-Correct.\]

"Okay," Leon breathed. "That is useful."

He adjusted his grip. He felt the knowledge of the *Blunt Weapon
Mastery* humming in the back of his mind. It told him exactly where to
hit a bandit to knock them out without causing permanent brain damage.

"It's just a list," Leon reminded himself, grounding his thoughts. "It
doesn't change who I am. I'm just a farmer with a very detailed resume."

He leaned forward. The cart rolled. Bessie chewed. The Inspector watched
the tree line for threats.

"To Ironhold," Leon said to his crew. "And if anyone asks, I'm just a
Level 4 porter delivering a cow."

He set a steady pace, his boots crunching on the gravel, walking away
from the sunrise and into the shadow of the great mountains ahead. The
world was big, scary, and full of monsters.

But Leon had a stick, a cow, and Level 99 Farming.

He liked his odds.
