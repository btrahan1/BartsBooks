# Chapter Forty-Six: The Administrative Over-Correction

The cart was heavy.

It wasn't just heavy; it was laden with roughly two tons of rusted iron
plates, bent axles, and jagged scrap that Foreman Grolm had happily let
Leon haul away.

Bessie was walking behind the cart today, tied to the tailgate, munching
on clover. Leon was in the harness.

He was pulling the two-ton load up the incline to Blackwood Farm. He
wasn't running, but he wasn't struggling. He was just leaning into the
work, treating the massive weight like a mild inconvenience.

DING.

DING.

DING.

The sound wasn't a bell this time. It sounded like a slot machine paying
out a jackpot inside his skull.

Leon stopped. The cart groaned as the leaf springs settled.

**\[System Notification: XP Backlog Processed.\]** **\[Achievement:
Industrial Titan (Consolidated).\]** **\[Achievement: Restoration of the
Cursed Soil.\]** **\[Achievement: Economic Disruption (Positive).\]**

**\[Level Up!\]** **\[Level Up!\]** **\[Level Up!\]**

\[Public Level: 11 \> 14\]

Leon blinked. He wiped the sweat from his eyes with Clara's
handkerchief.

"System?" Leon asked out loud. "Three levels? At once?"

The air above the scrap pile shimmered. Adjudicator Beta appeared. She
was sitting on a rusted breastplate, rubbing her temples. She looked
like she hadn't slept since the dawn of time.

"Mr. Leon," she sighed. "Do you have any idea how much data you
generated in the last 72 hours?"

"I fixed some spears," Leon said modestly. "And I pulled some weeds."

"You processed four thousand units of military hardware," Beta
corrected, scrolling through a holographic list that stretched to the
ground. "And you terraformed a Class-4 Cursed Zone into a productive
agricultural plot using nothing but 'Good Vibes' and 'Ki.' The System
couldn't decide if you were a Blacksmith, a Druid, or a Paladin, so it
just dumped all the XP into 'Journeyman' and hoped for the best."

She tapped the screen.

"Here. Look at your new Public Sheet. We had to widen the parameters
again."

**\[Name: Leon\]** **\[Class: Journeyman Wanderer\]** **\[Public Level:
14\]**

**\[Stats Adjusted for Narrative Consistency:\]** \* **Strength: 15 \>
18** (Threshold: Can now wrestle large equestrian animals). \*
**Agility: 12 \> 14** (Threshold: Can catch arrows if you really try).
\* **Constitution: 16 \> 18** (Threshold: Immune to common disease;
Poison resistance +50%). \* **Intellect: 10 \> 12** (Threshold: Advanced
Logistics). \* **Wisdom: 11 \> 13** (Threshold: Danger Sense extended to
50 meters). \* **Charisma: 12 \> 13** (Threshold: People instinctively
trust your directions).

Leon stared at the numbers.

"Eighteen Strength?" Leon frowned. "And Eighteen Constitution?
Adjudicator, is this... wise?"

Beta looked at him over her rimless glasses. "Wise?"

"It seems unbalanced," Leon argued. "If I get too strong, I might break
the farm tools. And Intellect 12? I feel... overly processed. I find
myself calculating the structural density of this scrap pile instead of
just enjoying the walk."

"It is necessary," Beta snapped. "You are entering the 'Mid-Game,' Leon.
Level 14 is the tipping point."

She stood up and floated down to eye level.

"Listen to me. You are in Ironhold now. The threats here aren't bandits
with rusty knives. We have Ogre mercenaries. We have unstable magical
constructs. We have *politics*."

She gestured to his stats.

"If you walk around with Level 10 stats but perform Level 50 feats, the
local reality engine starts to glitch. Walls might clip. Gravity might
forget to apply to you. By bumping you to Level 14, we create a buffer.
You can now legally lift a horse without the universe filing a bug
report."

"I have no intention of lifting a horse," Leon said. "Bessie would find
it undignified."

"You say that now," Beta warned. "But the 'Rot' you heard about? It's a
Level 20+ threat vector. It's creeping North. By the time it hits
Ironhold, you need to be at least Level 15 on paper to survive the AoE
(Area of Effect) checks."

She faded slightly, her form turning transparent.

"Consider this a software patch for your soul, Leon. We're hardening
your code because the virus is coming."

"Virus?" Leon asked.

"The Rot," Beta said grimly. "Keep building that farm, Leon. You might
need the walls sooner than you think."

She vanished with a static *pop*.

Leon stood alone on the road. The Inspector, who had watched the whole
exchange from the top of the scrap pile, let out a low meow.

*Eighteen Strength,* the cat mused. *You can carry more salmon.*

"It's not about the salmon, Inspector," Leon said, gripping the cart
handles.

He pulled.

The cart felt lighter. Significantly lighter. With **Strength 18**, the
two tons of iron felt like a wheelbarrow of dry leaves.

"It feels... disconnected," Leon muttered. "But efficient."

He looked up the hill toward Blackwood Farm. He could see the roof of
the barn he had fixed. He could see the wall of vines.

"Intellect 12," Leon whispered, testing his new mind.

Immediately, a schematic overlaid his vision.

- *Scrap Pile Analysis:* 60% Iron, 10% Steel, 30% Rust.
- *Project:* Reinforce Perimeter Wall.
- *Required Time:* 4.5 hours at current work rate.
- *Optimal Strategy:* Smelt slag into rebar; reinforce granite masonry.

Leon blinked. The world looked like a blueprint.

"Okay," Leon nodded slowly. "I can work with this. It takes the
guesswork out of the mortar mix."

He leaned forward, his boots digging into the road.

"Hup, Bessie. We have a fortress to build. And apparently, I am now
strong enough to wrestle a horse. Let's try not to test that theory."

He marched up the hill, a Level 14 Journeyman with a cart full of
garbage and a head full of blueprints, ready to turn a ruin into a
stronghold.
