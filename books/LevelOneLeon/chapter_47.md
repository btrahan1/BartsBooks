# Chapter Forty-Seven: The Resonant Frequency of Belonging

It had been three weeks since Leon bought Blackwood Farm. In that time,
the landscape had shifted from "Gothic Horror" to "Aggressive
Agriculture."

The farmhouse roof was repaired with golden-hued cedar shingles. The
barn stood straighter than a soldier on parade. And the perimeter...

The perimeter was a masterpiece.

Leon stood by the main gate, holding a granite lintel block that weighed
roughly eight hundred pounds. He wasn't using a crane. He was holding it
over his head with one hand while he used his other hand to trowel
mortar onto the pillar.

**Strength 18** made masonry feel like playing with wooden blocks.

"A little to the left, Boss!" Kip squeaked from the top of the wall. The
Kobold was wearing a tool belt that was too big for him and a straw hat
that Leon had woven.

"Precision is key, Kip," Leon said calmly, sliding the massive stone
block into place. *Thud.* "If the lintel is off-level, the gate won't
swing silently. Squeaking hinges are a sign of neglect."

"It's level!" Kip cheered, checking it with a plumb line. "It's
perfect!"

Leon wiped his hands on a rag. He stepped back to admire the entrance.
The Razor-Vines had been trained to weave around the stone archway,
their thorns retracting into a dormant state unless Leon (or the
Inspector) gave the signal.

"It looks... welcoming," Leon decided. "But secure."

The Inspector, sitting on a stack of bricks, rotated his ears. *Visitor
inbound. South road. Familiar scent.*

Leon froze.

His **Wisdom 13** expanded his senses. He didn't just hear footsteps; he
felt the vibration of boots on the gravel. He smelled pine soap, ozone,
and the metallic tang of dried monster blood.

He turned.

Standing just outside the range of the Razor-Vines was Lyra.

She looked exhausted. Her green cloak was tattered at the hem, and there
was a bandage wrapped around her left hand. But she wasn't looking at
the vines in fear. She was looking at them with a critical, appreciative
eye.

The vines sensed her. They sensed the green ribbon in her hair---the one
Leon had bought.

Instead of lashing out, the vines bloomed. Tiny, purple flowers opened
instantly along the tendrils, releasing a sweet, spicy scent.

"They like you," Leon said softly.

Lyra looked up. She saw him standing in the courtyard.

She blinked. She blinked again.

She had left a Level 10 Journeyman. She was looking at a Level 14 Titan.

Leon wasn't just bigger; he felt *denser*. His presence seemed to warp
the air around him slightly, a gravitational pull of sheer stability. He
wore a heavy leather apron over a tunic that strained at the shoulders,
and he stood with the casual confidence of a man who had just
bench-pressed a piece of architecture.

"Leon?" she asked, stepping through the archway.

"Hello, Lyra," Leon smiled. It was the smile that had charmed the
Silt-River market, but deeper. "You're early. I haven't finished the
walkway."

Lyra walked up to him. She stopped three feet away, looking him up and
down. She reached out and poked his arm. It was like poking a tree
trunk.

"You've grown," she whispered, her eyes wide. "A lot. I leave you for a
few weeks to kill goblins, and you turn into a fortress."

"I ate well," Leon said simply. "And the work was... engaging."

Lyra looked around the farm. She saw the repaired house with smoke
curling from the chimney. She saw the field of winter wheat that was
growing with unnatural vigor, glowing faintly with blue mana. She saw
the Kobold waving at her from the wall.

"I heard rumors in the city," she said, shaking her head in disbelief.
"The brokers were saying a madman bought the cursed Blackwood place.
They said it was a death trap."

She looked back at him, her eyes softening, the exhaustion melting away
into relief.

"I expected a ruin, Leon. And I arrive home to this?"

Home.

The word hung in the air.

Leon felt it hit his chest like a physical blow. It bypassed his Logic
Gates. It ignored his Social Algorithm. It went straight to the **Zenith
Agrarian Core** of his soul.

Home.

She didn't say "your house." She said *home*.

Leon's control slipped. Just for a fraction of a second. His Ki, usually
kept in a tight, pressurized loop, surged outward into the ground.

RUMBLE.

The earth heaved.

It wasn't a violent quake. It was a *pulse*.

The ground under their feet vibrated with a deep, resonant bass note.
The dust on the path jumped three inches into the air. The water in the
trough rippled into standing waves. The shingles on the roof rattled in
unison.

Kip squeaked and grabbed the wall. "Earthquake!"

The Inspector merely dug his claws into the brick, unimpressed.

Lyra grabbed Leon's arms to steady herself as the ground hummed beneath
them.

"Leon!" she gasped. "What was that? Is the dungeon acting up?"

The vibration stopped as quickly as it had started, leaving the air
charging with static electricity.

Leon looked down at his boots. He looked at Lyra, his face flushed a
deep, embarrassed red.

"No," Leon muttered. "That was... me."

"You?" Lyra stared at him. "You just shook ten acres of land because..."

"Resonance," Leon explained hurriedly, his hands fluttering slightly.
"I... the word you used. It created a harmonic feedback loop in my
meridians. I lost containment for a moment. I apologize. It was
unprofessional."

Lyra stared at him.

He had just caused a seismic event because she called his farm "home."

A slow, delighted smile spread across her face.

"You shook the ground," she teased softly. "Because I said I was home?"

"It is a very powerful word," Leon defended earnestly. "It carries
significant structural weight."

Lyra laughed. She threw her arms around his neck, burying her face in
his chest. He smelled of mortar, crushed pine, and safety.

"Well," she mumbled into his tunic. "I guess I better be careful with my
vocabulary. If I say 'I love you,' you might level the mountain."

Leon froze. He wrapped his massive arms around her, holding her with
that terrifyingly gentle strength.

"I would try to aim it away from the structures," he whispered into her
hair.

**\[System Notification: Emotional Bond Deepened.\]** **\[Land Affinity:
Synced.\]** **\[The Farm recognizes** '**Lyra**' **as Co-Owner.\]**

Leon looked over Lyra's head. He saw the Inspector watching him.

The cat gave a slow blink. *Smooth. Very smooth.*

"Welcome home, Lyra," Leon said, and this time, he managed to keep the
ground still. "Now, let me look at that hand. I have a salve for goblin
bites."
