# Chapter Forty-Eight: The Human Photosynthesis

They sat at the heavy oak table in the kitchen. The room was warm,
smelling of the pine logs crackling in the hearth and the drying herbs
hanging from the rafters.

Leon unwrapped the bandage on Lyra's left hand with the delicacy of a
jeweler handling a cracked diamond.

The wound was ugly.

It was a jagged gash across her palm, surrounded by a spiderweb of black
veins. The skin around it was grey and fever-hot to the touch.

"Goblin blade," Lyra whispered, wincing as the air hit the raw nerves.
"They coat them in sewer sludge. I drank an antidote, but the necrosis
is stubborn. It usually takes a week to clear."

Leon frowned. His **Wisdom 13** scanned the injury, but it was his
**Agrarian Arts** that analyzed the data.

To Leon, the black veins didn't look like an infection. They looked like
*blight*. They looked like the leaf-rot that attacks tomato plants after
a heavy rain. It was a corruption of the vascular system, choking off
the flow of healthy sap---or in this case, blood.

"It is inefficient," Leon murmured. "The tissue is trying to repair
itself, but the poison is creating a barrier."

He placed his right hand underneath hers, cradling it. He placed his
left hand over the wound, hovering just an inch above the skin.

He meant to just check the heat, to gauge the fever.

But as he focused on the damage, that familiar sensation in his
core---the Neutron Star---stirred.

It wasn't the explosive power he used to fight bears. It wasn't the
rigid stability he used to hold up barns.

It was... warm.

It started as a gentle hum in his solar plexus, like a seed germinating
in the sun. Then, it grew hotter. It moved down his arm, rushing through
his meridians like liquid gold.

It was the urge to *Grow*. The urge to *Restore*.

Leon didn't think to stop it. It felt natural. If a plant is wilting,
you water it. If a fence is broken, you mend it. If a hand is hurt...

"Fix," Leon whispered.

He didn't cast a spell. He didn't pray to a deity. He simply opened the
valve on his core and let the heat flow.

Lyra gasped.

"Leon? It's... hot!"

She tried to pull her hand away, but Leon held firm.

"Hold still," he murmured, his eyes losing focus, turning that strange,
luminous gold. "The roots need to reconnect."

The heat surged. It wasn't burning her skin; it was burning the
*sickness*.

Lyra watched in wide-eyed horror and fascination. Visible steam rose
from her palm. The black veins began to retreat, sizzling as if they
were being cauterized by invisible fire. The grey skin flushed pink. The
jagged cut didn't just scab over; the edges knit together, weaving cell
by cell with terrifying speed.

It felt like sticking her hand into a bucket of pure adrenaline.

The sensation rushed up her arm, hitting her chest. Her fatigue
vanished. Her mana bar, which had been sitting at 10%, refilled in a
blink and slammed into the cap.

ZAP.

A spark of static electricity jumped between their hands, loud as a whip
crack.

Leon jerked back. The heat in his core subsided, leaving him feeling
pleasantly empty.

Lyra stared at her hand.

It was perfect.

Not just healed---*pristine*. The callous she had from her bowstring was
gone. The skin was soft, new, and glowing faintly. She flexed her
fingers. The stiffness was gone. In fact, her grip felt stronger than it
had before the injury.

"What..." Lyra looked up at him, breathless. "What was that? That wasn't
a \[Lesser Heal\]. That wasn't even a \[Great Heal\]. That felt like you
injected raw mana into my bloodstream."

Leon rubbed his hands together. They were tingling.

"I treated it like a graft," Leon explained, trying to rationalize what
he had just done. "I flushed the blight and stimulated the cellular
growth. Like using bone meal on a sapling."

DING.

The System notification appeared between them. It looked confused. The
text was flickering slightly.

**\[System Alert: Biological Anomaly Detected.\]** **\[Subject**
'**Leon**' **attempted Medical Intervention without \[Medicine\] or
\[Holy Magic\] skills.\]**

**\[Resolving Action...\]** **\[Action Categorized as: Aggressive
Fertilization.\]**

**\[Skill Created: Life-Graft (Unique)\]** \* *Description: You can
force raw Life Essence into a target to repair structural damage. Note:
May cause side effects such as extreme energy, temporary
invulnerability, or the sudden urge to photosynthesize.*

Lyra read the screen. She read it twice.

"Aggressive Fertilization," she repeated flatly. "You fertilized me?"

"It worked," Leon pointed out. "The structural integrity of your
epidermis is restored to 100%."

Lyra looked at her hand, then at Leon. She started to laugh. It was a
borderline hysterical sound.

"I'm dating a man who treats me like a prize pumpkin," she wheezed. "And
the worst part is... I feel incredible. I feel like I could punch a
stone wall."

"Please don't," Leon said seriously. "I just fixed that wall. The mortar
is still curing."

Lyra stood up. She walked around the table and grabbed his face with
both hands---her newly healed hand feeling warm against his cheek.

"Thank you, Leon," she said softly, the laughter fading into tenderness.
"You really can fix anything, can't you?"

"I try," Leon said. "Broken things take up space."

Lyra leaned down and kissed him.

It wasn't a peck on the cheek this time. It was a real kiss. It tasted
of the peppermint tea they had been drinking and the ozone of raw magic.

Leon froze for a split second---his **Intellect 12** trying to calculate
the social protocol---before his **Wisdom 13** told him to shut up and
kiss her back.

He wrapped his arms around her waist, pulling her closer. He was careful
with his strength, mindful of his density, but Lyra didn't seem to mind.
She pressed against him, grounding herself in his stability.

For a moment, the war in the South, the rot, and the System didn't
exist. There was just the kitchen, the fire, and the farmer who had
finally brought his Ranger home.

From the top of the cupboard, the Inspector watched. The cat yawned,
stretching its claws.

*Finally,* the cat thought. *Now maybe he will stop talking to the
vegetables.*
