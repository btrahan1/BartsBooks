# Chapter Forty-Nine: The Breakfast Buff

The smell of coffee was the first thing to reach Leon's consciousness.
It was rich, dark, and carried a hint of chicory---exactly how he liked
it.

He opened his eyes. The morning sun was streaming through the newly
installed window (double-paned for thermal efficiency), casting a warm
rectangle of light across the quilt.

For a moment, it was perfect. He was warm. He was rested. He could hear
Lyra humming in the kitchen.

Then, the world *chimed*.

It wasn't the polite ding of a skill increase. It was the heavy, brassy
gong of a significant structural update.

Leon groaned, rubbing his eyes. "Not before breakfast."

He tried to sit up, but something heavy was pinning him down. A blue
window, dense with text, was hovering inches from his nose.

**\[System Notification: Overnight Calculation Complete.\]** **\[Event
Processed: Symbiotic Resonance (Class 5).\]** **\[Event Processed:
Successful Life-Graft (High Efficiency).\]** **\[Event Processed:
Emotional Anchor Established.\]**

**\[Calculation:\]** \* *Subject successfully merged internal Ki with
external biological entity (Lyra).* \* *Subject successfully stabilized
local reality via* "*Home*" *concept.* \* *Subject slept for 8 hours
with \[Comfort\] buff active.*

\[XP Threshold Shattered.\]

**\[Level Up!\]** **\[Level Up!\]**

\[Public Level: 14 \> 16\]

Leon stared at the number.

Sixteen.

He hadn't lifted a hammer. He hadn't pulled a weed. He hadn't even
engaged in a polite conversation. He had literally just slept.

"I leveled up from napping," Leon whispered, horrified. "That feels
lazy."

He scanned the stat adjustments. The Adjudicator had clearly been busy
while he was unconscious.

**\[Stats Adjusted for Level 16:\]** \* **Strength: 18 \> 19**
(Threshold: Dangerously close to \[Heroic\] tier. Please grip things
gently). \* **Agility: 14 \> 15** (Threshold: Reflexes now operate
faster than conscious thought). \* **Wisdom: 13 \> 15** (Threshold:
\[Nature's Voice\] unlocked. You can hear the grass complaining). \*
**Charisma: 13 \> 14** (Threshold: Magnetic Personality. Small animals
may follow you involuntarily).

Leon sat there, frozen.

In the doorway, Lyra froze too.

She was holding two steaming mugs of coffee. She was wearing one of
Leon's spare tunics, which hung on her frame like a dress, her hair
messy and loose. She looked soft, happy, and domestic.

Until she saw Leon's face.

He had the "Thousand-Yard Stare." His eyes were fixed on a point in
empty space, tracking text that only he could see.

"Leon," Lyra said slowly, her grip tightening on the mugs. "Why do you
have that look?"

Leon didn't blink. "I didn't do anything."

"That's the look you get when you break physics," Lyra accused, walking
closer. "Did you accidently renovate the bedroom in your sleep? Is the
bed suddenly made of diamond?"

"No," Leon said, his voice hollow. "I leveled up. Twice."

Lyra stopped at the edge of the bed. She set the coffee down on the
nightstand with a *clack*.

"Twice?" she repeated. "You were Level 14 yesterday. You're telling me
you're Level 16 now? Before coffee?"

"The System cited 'Symbiotic Resonance'," Leon explained, swiping the
screen so she could see. "And the 'Life-Graft.' Apparently, fixing your
hand yielded high experience returns."

Lyra looked at the screen.

**\[Public Level: 16\]** **\[Strength: 19\]**

"Nineteen Strength," Lyra whispered. She looked at her own hand---the
one he had healed. She looked at the man sitting under the quilt.

Level 16 was the threshold for "Veteran Commander." It was the level
where adventurers started getting invited to the Royal Court. It was the
level where you stopped fighting Goblins and started fighting Giants.

And he had reached it by holding hands and sleeping in.

"You're broken," Lyra decided, shaking her head. But she was smiling. It
was a bewildered, affectionate smile. "You are completely broken."

"I feel... tuned," Leon admitted. He flexed his hand. The air popped
audibly around his fingers. "My Wisdom is fifteen. The System says I can
hear the grass complaining."

"Can you?"

Leon tilted his head. He listened.

Outside, the wind rustled the winter wheat.

Thirsty... thirsty... roots cold...

"Yes," Leon nodded solemnly. "The wheat is thirsty. And it dislikes the
north wind."

Lyra sat on the edge of the bed. She picked up her coffee and took a
sip, watching him.

"You know what this means, right?" she asked quietly.

"That I need to water the wheat?"

"No. It means you aren't just a Journeyman anymore, Leon. Level 16 is...
it's serious. If you go to the Front with me..." She hesitated. "You
won't be in the back lines fixing catapults. With stats like that,
they'll put you on the wall."

Leon swiped the blue screens away. The text vanished, leaving only the
morning light and the woman he loved.

He reached out and took his coffee. The ceramic mug felt fragile in his
hand---like an eggshell. He had to consciously dial down his grip
pressure.

"I know," Leon said. His voice was calm, anchored by that massive Wisdom
stat. "If I go South, I go to work. Whether that work is fixing a wall
or... holding it up."

He looked at her.

"But I have a farm to finish first. And you have a report to file."

Lyra sighed, leaning her head on his shoulder. "I do. The Guild Master
is going to scream when he sees my report on the goblin tunnels. And
when I tell him my 'Guide' was a Level 16 Farmer who treats necrosis
with 'Aggressive Fertilization'."

"Tell him I am a Consultant," Leon corrected. "It sounds more
expensive."

The Inspector, who had been sleeping under the bed, crawled out. He
jumped onto the mattress, looked at Leon, and sniffed.

*You smell like ozone and authority,* the cat judged. *Also, the wheat
is right. It is dry.*

"I'm on it," Leon told the cat.

He finished his coffee in one gulp (Constitution 18 allowed him to drink
boiling liquid without flinching).

"Two levels," Leon muttered, swinging his legs out of bed. "I guess I
really do need to expand the barn. At this rate, I'll be Level 20 by the
time I finish the fence."

Lyra watched him stand up. He blocked out the sun. He was a force of
nature in linen trousers.

"Leon?"

"Yes?"

"Just... warn me before you level up again," she said. "I nearly dropped
the coffee."

"I'll try," Leon promised. "But the System is very erratic."

He walked to the door, grabbing his staff.

"I'm going to water the wheat. And then... I think I need to forge a
bigger shovel."
