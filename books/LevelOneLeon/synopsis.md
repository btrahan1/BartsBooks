Leon is a Level 1 Farmer who is capped at level 1 but has incredible "Cultivated" strength and Ki control from years of doing chores and meditation. Surrounded by high-level adventurers who rely on the System for everything, Leon proves that hard work, skill, and understanding mechanics can overcome any level gap. A story about a humble farmer who is secretly the most competent person in the world, fighting "hazards" and proving that level isn't everything.
