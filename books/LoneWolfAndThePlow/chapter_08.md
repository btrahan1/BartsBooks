# Chapter 8: The Commander\'s Price

As the disgraced soldier, his face a mask of shame, began to retreat
towards the broken tavern doorway, the commander had seen enough. *The
humiliation of his man, the open defiance, the utter shattering of
protocol---it was an inferno in his gut.*

\"So,\" Commander Vance\'s voice was a blade of ice, slicing through the
quiet courtyard. \"You think you have what it takes to beat up on a
useless soldier, do you?\"

With a fluid, practiced motion that spoke of a thousand training drills
and a dozen real battles, he unsheathed his own sword. The whisper of
steel was sharp, clean, and deadly serious. It was not the clumsy rasp
of the soldier\'s draw; it was the sound of a master at his work. The
blade, polished and perfectly balanced, caught the noon sun, flashing
like a sliver of vengeful light.

His eyes, hard as chips of flint, were locked on Steven. *This was no
longer about a boy\'s transgression. This was about the fundamental
order of the realm. A farmer did not assault a soldier. A commoner did
not defy a Commander of The Order. It was an intolerable breach that had
to be corrected, publicly and decisively.*

Steven, who had been about to take another step toward the tavern,
stopped. He didn\'t turn quickly. He turned with a weary slowness, the
movement conveying not fear, but a profound sense of annoyance. He let
out a quiet sigh, a small puff of air that was somehow more insulting to
the commander than any curse could have been.

His gaze left the tavern doorway and settled on the armed commander. The
cold fire in his eyes didn\'t flare with new anger; it simply changed
its focus. *He had been a collector coming for a debt. Now, he was a
stonemason facing a large, noisy rock that had rolled into his path and
needed to be cleared.*

From the edge of the courtyard, Vanera's lips curved into the faintest
hint of a smile. *The true test had arrived. Untamed power versus
trained discipline. This was worth the wait.*

The crowd, which had begun to breathe again, gasped as one. *This was no
longer a dispute. This was rebellion. To even raise a hand against the
Commander of The Order was a death sentence.*

Commander Vance settled into a classic duelist's stance, the tip of his
sword pointing directly at Steven\'s heart, a perfect, unwavering point
of deadly intent. \"You have assaulted an officer of The Crown and
defied a direct command,\" he declared, his voice ringing with the
weight of the law. \"Yield now, or be cut down.\"

The words were a formality. *He had no intention of accepting a
surrender. Honor demanded blood.*

Steven did not raise his fists. He did not adopt a brawling stance. He
simply stood there, his hands loose at his sides, and asked a simple
question, his voice still chillingly calm.

\"Are you with them?\"
