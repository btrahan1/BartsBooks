# Chapter 9: The Unwanted Path

Steven began to walk slowly towards the commander. It was not the
arrogant strut of a challenger, nor the hesitant shuffle of a coward,
but the purposeful, rhythmic advance of a man with a job to do. Each
step was solid, rooted to the earth, a confirmation of his right to be
there.

*He had reigned in the inferno of his anger. The blinding, white-hot
rage that had erupted at the sound of his sister's cry had been cooled
and forged into something harder, colder, and far more dangerous:
resolve.* The aura that shimmered around him was still not fully
contained. It was no longer a tidal wave of fury, but a low hum of
pressure, a visible distortion in the air around his massive frame that
made the sane part of the brain scream to run.

He did not want this. The commander, the soldiers, The Order
itself---they were a complication. A diversion from the simple, clean
line of justice he intended to draw from his fist to the face of the man
who had harmed his sister. This confrontation was a waste of time, a
tedious obstacle.

He stopped a respectful distance from the commander, close enough for
his words to be heard clearly, yet far enough away not to be an
immediate physical threat. He met the man\'s furious gaze without
malice, his own eyes holding only a deep, weary certainty.

\"I have no quarrel with you,\" Steven stated. *It was not a plea for
mercy or a request to stand down. It was a simple statement of fact,
offered with the hope that the commander was a reasonable man who could
understand a simple truth. He was clarifying his intent, separating this
unwanted standoff from his true purpose.*

To Commander Vance, however, this calm was the ultimate arrogance. *He
was a hammer, and this boy was a nail that refused to lie flat.* To be
approached so calmly by an unarmed farmer while he stood with a drawn
sword was a profound insult to his rank, his skill, and the very symbol
of authority he represented.

\"Your quarrel is not with me, boy,\" Vance\'s voice was low and tight
with fury. \"Your quarrel is with the law. It is with The Order itself.
And that is a quarrel you will not survive.\"

From the sidelines, Vanera watched, her expression unreadable. *She saw
what the commander did not. She saw the boy\'s attempt to de-escalate,
to isolate the conflict to its source. He was not challenging The Order;
he was finishing a personal task. But Vance, blinded by duty and pride,
could no longer see the distinction.*

The commander shifted his weight, settling deeper into his fighting
stance. The tip of his sword did not waver. \"This is your final
warning. Kneel.\"

Steven did not move. He simply held the commander's gaze and gave a
slow, almost imperceptible shake of his head. *He had offered the man a
path away from this. The commander had refused to take it. The debt
would still be paid. The path to the tavern just had one more stone that
needed to be cleared.*
