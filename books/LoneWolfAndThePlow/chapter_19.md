# Chapter 19: The Farmer\'s Pitch

Steven began to walk slowly up and down the line of the remaining
fifteen boys. He didn\'t inspect them for muscle or height. He simply
made eye contact with each of them, a quiet acknowledgment of their
choice to stay.

\"I stood where you are now just a few weeks ago,\" he began, his voice
calm and conversational. \"I didn\'t want to be there. I wanted to get
back to my farm\... but\...\" He paused, a small, wry smirk touching his
lips. \"It seems fate has other plans.\"

The boys relaxed visibly. This was not the speech they had expected.

\"I am not a soldier,\" Steven continued, his honesty a disarming force.
\"I am a farmer. But I am learning that deep within all of us, there is
a possibility of more. But to find it, it takes WILL. It takes the
decision, every morning, to be better than you were the day before.\" He
kept walking, his voice gaining a quiet intensity. \"It takes HEART. The
courage to stand for something when it would be easier to run. And it
takes INNER STRENGTH. The kind that holds you together when the world is
trying to tear you apart.\"

He stopped in the middle of the line and turned to face them all. His
voice softened, becoming almost a whisper, yet it seemed to carry a
profound weight.

\"\...and it takes love.\"

The word hung in the air, a shocking, beautiful anomaly in the context
of a military recruitment.

\"Love of your family,\" he said, his eyes distant for a moment. \"The
ones who gave you life and whose faces you will carry in your heart.
Love of your town. The people and the very stones of the place that made
you. Love of your country, the soil that connects us all. And\... and
yes, this one is last, but no less important, love of yourself. Enough
to know that you are worthy of defending all the rest.\"

He let the silence settle, allowing the full import of his words to sink
in.

\"That is what The Order truly needs. Not just bodies to hold a spear,
but hearts to hold a kingdom. If you think you have what it takes, and
you are willing to put in the work\... please, take a step forward.\"

There was no hesitation. No shuffling of feet. As one, fifteen boys,
their faces now set with a new and solemn determination, took a single,
unified step forward.

On the sideline, the Commander simply shook his head in wonder. Vanera
watched, a slow, dawning realization on her face. *It wasn\'t magic. It
was alchemy. He wasn\'t giving them orders; he was giving them a piece
of his own soul, and in doing so, he was turning boys into the
foundations of men.*
