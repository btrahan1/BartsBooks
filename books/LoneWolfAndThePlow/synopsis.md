Steven was a giant of a man who wanted nothing more than a quiet life on his farm. Possessing immense strength and a sharp intellect, he spent years "toning it down" to avoid frightening his neighbors. But when his sister is assaulted in a local tavern by an arrogant merchant's son, the performance ends. Revealing his true, terrifying power, Steven steps in to protect his family. After effortlessly disarming the local Commander of The Order, he invites the authorities to his farm for tea to discuss the law. A story of hidden power, family loyalty, and a man who just wants to be left alone.
