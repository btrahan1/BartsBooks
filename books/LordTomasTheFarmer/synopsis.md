Tomas is a farm boy who needs to avoid conscription by finding an apprenticeship. But farming doesn't count! So his parents send him to Uncle Silas ("The Grinder"), a retired adventurer. Silas trains him brutally, helping him unlock skills like Grit, Dodge, and Danger Sense. Tomas proves to be a natural, evolving his class to Aegis Initiate and learning to control the rhythm of battle. He eventually goes to the army, becomes a legendary commander, and returns to build an Academy to train leaders. A story about a farm boy who becomes a master builder of men.
