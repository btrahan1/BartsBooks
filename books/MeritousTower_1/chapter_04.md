# Chapter 4: The Sling

Jace paced around the small campfire. The mud squelched beneath his boots, a rhythmic, wet sound that irritated him.

"Range," he muttered. "I can't hit them if I can't reach them."

He looked at the leftover scraps of rat hide and the pile of sinew he’d stripped from the tails.

"Bella," he said, stopping his pacing. "The Highborn have bows, right?"

"Compound bows," Bella answered from her spot near the roots, where she was studying the movement of a glowing swamp-moth. "Carbon fiber. Some have wands. One of the Valerius group has a [Wind-Caller] flute."

"Fancy," Jace spat. "I don't need fancy. I need velocity."

He sat down and grabbed the longest, toughest strip of rat leather. He cut a diamond shape in the center—a pouch. Then, he took the sinew, braided it three strands thick for durability, and tied it to the ends of the pouch.

A sling.

It was the weapon of shepherds and street rats. In the Rust Bottom, kids used them to knock cans off fences. Or knock pigeons out of the sky for dinner.

He picked up a heavy, smooth river stone the size of a lemon. He stood up and walked to the edge of the light.

"You're going to practice now?" Bella asked, rubbing her eyes. "It's pitch black."

"Target practice is for later," Jace said. "Right now, I'm practicing the *mechanic*."

He loaded the stone. He spun the sling. *Whoosh. Whoosh.* The centrifugal force built up.

*Snap.*

He released one end of the cord. The stone vanished into the darkness with a terrifying hum. A second later, a loud *CRACK* echoed off a distant tree trunk.

**[Skill Unlocked: Primitive Ballistics (Lvl 1)]**
*Description: The art of throwing rocks really, really hard.*
*Effect: +5% Range with Slings/Thrown weapons.*

"It works," Jace noted. "But it's not enough. If I hit a plated knight with a rock, I'll just annoy him. I need force. I need **Strength**."

He dropped the sling and looked at his hands. He felt the **Null-Iron Body** humming. The *Internal Furnace* was still active, keeping him warm, but he realized something.

When the furnace was hot, his body felt... heavier. Like his bones were lead.

"The mana," Jace whispered. "I absorb it. It doesn't go out. It stays in. Mass."

He looked at Bella. "I'm going to try something stupid. If I pass out, make sure I don't drown in the mud."

"Jace, wait—"

Jace dropped to the ground in a push-up position.

Usually, a push-up was easy. He was skinny. But now, he took a deep breath, sucking in the damp, mana-rich air. He visualized the *Internal Furnace* roaring to life. He didn't burn the mana for heat this time; he just *held* it. He saturated his muscles with it.

The effect was instantaneous. Gravity seemed to double. His small frame suddenly felt like it weighed three hundred pounds. His arms trembled violently just holding the plank position.

**[SYSTEM WARNING]**
**[Mana Saturation at 80%. Body Density Increasing.]**
**[Muscular Stress: CRITICAL.]**

"One," Jace gritted out.

He lowered himself. It felt like a mountain was on his back. His triceps screamed. The veins in his neck bulged.

He pushed back up. It took three seconds of agonizing effort.

"Two."

"Jace, your skin is turning grey," Bella warned, leaning forward. "Like... like iron."

"Good," Jace wheezed.

He did another one. And another.

It was a cheat. A glitch in his own physiology. By overloading his body with mana he couldn't expel, he was effectively training in high gravity without leaving the ground. He was bench-pressing his own soul.

**[Strength Check: FAILURE]**
**[Muscular Micro-tearing Detected.]**
**[Regeneration (Internal Furnace): ACTIVE.]**

He collapsed into the mud after ten reps, gasping, sweat steaming off his body. As he exhaled the mana, his body weight returned to normal.

Then he waited for the *Internal Furnace* to knit the muscle fibers back together, fueled by the rat meat and the ambient magic.

The pain faded. The strength remained.

**[Strength +1]**

Jace grinned, his face caked in mud. "Again."
