# Chapter 5: The Grind

***

For the next four hours, while the swamp slept, Jace tortured himself.

**Set 1: Gravity Push-ups.** (Targeting Chest/Triceps)
**Set 2: Rebar Swings.**
He took the rusted metal bar. He didn't just swing it. He practiced the *stop*. He swung it at a tree root and stopped the metal fully an inch before impact.
*Control. Momentum. Grip.*

**[Skill Unlocked: Blunt Weapon Mastery (Lvl 1)]**
*Effect: +5% Damage with maces, hammers, and improvised clubs.*

**Set 3: The Sling.**
He threw stones into the dark until his shoulder joint felt like it was grinding glass.
*Load. Spin. Snap.*

By the time the grey light of dawn began to filter through the mist, Jace was a wreck. He was covered in sweat, mud, and rat blood. His tunic was in tatters.

But when he stood up, the rebar didn't feel heavy anymore. It felt like a twig.

He checked his status.

**[Name: Jace]**
**[Level: 2]**
**[Strength: 4 -> 7]** (The growth curve for low stats was fast, especially with the "Gravity Training" exploit).
**[Agility: 9 -> 10]**
**[Constitution: 4 -> 6]**

He had nearly doubled his physical power in one night. The Highborn kids had slept to get their "Rested XP" bonus. Jace had grinded his bones to dust and rebuilt them.

"Bella," Jace rasped. He kicked the dying embers of the fire.

Bella jerked awake, clutching her makeshift dagger (a sharpened bone). "What? What is it?"

"Dawn," Jace said. He picked up his rebar and slid the sling into his belt. "The tutorial protection ends at sunrise. That means player-versus-player damage is enabled."

He looked toward the distant rock formation where Valerius and his group were camped.

"They're going to move toward the Gatekeeper Boss today," Jace said. "They need to beat the boss to get to Floor 2."

"And us?" Bella asked, standing up and brushing off the moss.

Jace smiled. It wasn't a nice smile.

"We're going to get there first. And we're going to steal the kill."

"Don't walk. Creep," Jace whispered, his voice a dry rasp in the pre-dawn chill. He held out an arm, stopping Bella before she took another step. "Like this."

He demonstrated, bending his knees low, almost in a crouch. He placed his foot down toe-first, testing the ground for stability before committing his weight. It was a silent, fluid motion.

The darkness was absolute. The moon, if there was one, was smothered by the perpetual mist of the Silt. The only light came from the faint glow of distant, alien-looking fungi.

"We move for ten paces," Jace instructed. "Then we stop. And we stare. We stare until our eyes bleed. Understand?"

"Stare at what?" Bella whispered back, her voice trembling.

"At nothing. At everything," Jace said, his intensity palpable. "The System rewards intent. Our intent is to *see*. We're telling it what skill we want. We're forcing its hand."

They moved. Ten agonizingly slow steps through the sucking mud. The sounds of the swamp were a constant, low-level threat—the buzz of insects, the plop of something unseen falling into water, the rustle of leaves that could be wind or could be teeth.

"Stop," Jace breathed.

They froze. Jace’s body was a statue, his head unmoving, his eyes wide open, scanning the impenetrable blackness in front of them. He wasn't just looking; he was willing the darkness to give up its secrets. He focused on the space between the glowing fungi, trying to perceive the shapes that connected them.

**[Attempting to Perceive in Low-Light Conditions...]**
**[Optical Strain Detected.]**
**[Focus Detected.]**

His eyes began to burn. Little black spots danced in his vision. He ignored them. He kept staring.

Beside him, Bella was doing the same. She wasn't looking for monsters; she was trying to define the edges of the roots, the line where mud met water. She was looking for the geometry of the darkness.

**[Willpower Check: Passed]**
**[Willpower Check: Passed]**

A soft chime, almost inaudible, rang for both of them.

**[Skill Unlocked: Low-Light Acuity (Lvl 1)]**
*Description: Your eyes have begun adapting to the gloom. You perceive shapes and outlines in near-darkness.*
*Effect: In low-light environments, vision is rendered in grayscale. Minor details are lost, but movement and large objects are discernible.*

The world shifted.

It wasn't like a light turning on. It was like the black ink of the swamp was being diluted with grey paint. The solid wall of darkness dissolved into a thousand distinct shapes and shadows. The root Jace had almost tripped over a minute ago was now a clear, pale silhouette against the darker mud. He could see the gleam of water in a deep puddle to their left. He could see the outline of a thick, gnarled tree fifty feet away.

"Whoa," Bella breathed.

"It worked," Jace said, a grim smile touching his lips. He flexed his hands. "It worked."

He looked at Bella. Her face, previously invisible, was now a pale oval in the gloom. "What do you see?"

"The contrast," she said, her voice full of awe. "The edges are sharper. I can see... a path. Not a real path, but a line of stable-looking roots we can use as stepping stones."

She pointed. Jace followed her finger. She was right. It was a treacherous, winding route, but it was solid ground.

"Good," Jace said. "Keep that path in your head. My job is to watch for movement."

They continued, their pace doubling. They were no longer blind. They moved through the pre-dawn murk with a confidence that bordered on supernatural. They were a pair of ghosts, their bare feet making almost no sound on the damp wood and packed earth.

They passed a sleeping Silt-Reaver, its chitinous back looking like a mossy boulder in the gloom. Jace put a hand on Bella's shoulder, guiding her around it.

They saw a hunting party of Dredge-Rats—a dozen of them—moving in a tight, disciplined pack. Jace pulled them into the shadow of a giant mangrove until the rats had passed, their claws clicking on stone.

Jace stopped again, holding up a hand. He stared at a thick, low-hanging vine covered in thorns.

Bella focused on it for three seconds.

**[Weakness Detection: Active]**

"The base," she whispered. "It's barely connected to the branch. It's a trap. A Snapper-Vine. If we touch it, it'll coil around our legs."

Jace nodded. Without their new vision, they would have blundered right into it. The gamble had paid off. They hadn't just avoided a broken ankle; they had avoided certain death.

"We're close," Jace said, pointing with his rebar. Ahead, the ground began to rise, the swampy mud giving way to solid stone. "That's the entrance to the Gatekeeper's arena. Valerius will come charging in with the sunrise, thinking he has the advantage of light."

He looked at Bella, his eyes glowing with a faint orange intensity that was now clearly visible in the gloom.

"He's wrong. The darkness was never a handicap. It's our home."
