# Chapter 7: The Thief

Running was Jace’s first and greatest skill. He’d outrun guards in the market, bigger kids in the alleys, and the gnawing hunger in his own stomach. His legs were thin, but they were pistons forged in the furnace of desperation.

Valerius and his crew were not. They were strong, yes. Their stats were higher. But they had been trained in the dojo, on flat, matted floors. They were fencers, not sprinters.

"HEY!" Jace screamed, his voice raw and taunting. He didn't run for the portal. He ran along the curved wall of the cistern, making himself the most obvious, most infuriating target imaginable. "THANKS FOR THE HELP! COULDN'T HAVE DONE IT WITHOUT YOU!"

"Kill him!" Valerius shrieked, his composure shattering like glass. "Forget the loot, forget the portal! *KILL HIM*!"

The Highborn kids charged. Marcus, the tank, led the way, his face a mask of grim determination. He was fast for a big kid, but he ran with heavy, predictable steps.

Jace slid to a stop, the water spraying up around him. He already had a stone in his sling. He whipped it around his head, the leather humming a deadly tune.

*Snap.*

The stone flew. He didn't aim for Marcus's armored chest. He aimed for his feet. The stone skipped off the water like a flat rock and slammed squarely into Marcus's ankle.

"ARGH!" The big kid stumbled, his perfect charge broken as he pitched forward into the water with a huge splash.

**[Skill Unlocked: Tripwire Shot (Lvl 1)]**
*Description: You don't aim to kill; you aim to inconvenience. Hitting an opponent's legs with a ranged attack has a 10% chance to cause [Stumble].*

Jace was already running again, laughing. It was a cold, sharp sound. "Having trouble, big guy? Too much mud for you?"

He glanced over his shoulder. Bella was halfway down the vines. Good.

Valerius pointed his stick-sword at Jace. "Don't chase! Flank him! Cut him off at the north wall!"

They were learning. They started to spread out, using their numbers. Jace felt a flicker of real danger. He couldn't outrun a pincer movement. He needed to break their formation again.

He scrambled on top of the dead Croaker’s immense, rubbery back, giving himself the high ground. "Hey, Valerius! Is this the part of the lesson your dad paid for? The one where a Dreg with a rusty spike makes you look like a fool?"

Valerius’s face went from red to purple. He broke his own order and charged straight at the corpse. "I'll kill you myself!"

"Bad move," Jace grinned.

He let Valerius get close. As the blond kid leaped onto the corpse, Jace stomped hard on one of the still-glowing, un-vented pustules on the Croaker's back.

*FWOOMP.*

A geyser of thick, foul-smelling, non-damaging slime erupted directly into Valerius's face. It wasn't acid, but it was slick, blinding, and utterly humiliating. Valerius slipped on the bile-covered skin and fell, landing in a heap of flailing limbs and curses.

Jace used the momentum to leap off the other side of the corpse, landing in the water and sprinting again. He saw Bella hit the ground and start running for the portal. She was almost there.

*Just a little more time.*

The other Highborn were momentarily confused, their leader covered in monster goo. Jace didn't let them recover. He scooped up handfuls of the thickest, blackest mud and began pelting them.

It did no damage. But it splattered across their faces, got in their eyes, and stained their pride.

**[New Title Gained: Pest]**
*Description: You have mastered the art of being profoundly annoying. Enemies targeting you suffer a minor penalty to [Focus].*

"Come on!" Jace yelled, backpedaling toward the portal. "I thought you were the Elites! The best! My grandmother hits harder than you, and she's dead!"

He saw Bella disappear through the shimmering blue light.

*Time to go.*

His taunting had a purpose. He had baited them all to one side of the arena. The path to the portal was now clear.

Jace stopped his retreat. He turned his back on them and sprinted.

"He's getting away!" Elara screamed.

It was a straight race now. Jace pushed his legs harder than he ever had before. The world narrowed to the glowing blue rectangle in front of him. He could hear them splashing furiously behind him, their angry shouts fueling his speed.

He was five feet from the portal.

A sharpened stick flew past his ear, thrown like a javelin. Close.

He didn't look back. He poured every ounce of his will, every point of his stamina, into that final burst.

He dove.

The world dissolved into a silent, blue, liquid light. He felt a moment of weightlessness, the angry shouts of the Highborn vanishing as if a door had been slammed shut.

He tumbled out onto a floor of cool, smooth, dry stone, landing in a heap at Bella's feet.

He was on Floor 2.

He lay on his back, gasping for air, a wide, triumphant, and utterly feral grin on his face. They had done it. They had stolen the prize and escaped with their lives.

**[Achievement Unlocked: Giant Slayer]**
*Criteria: Land the killing blow on a Boss-type enemy at least 10 levels higher than yourself.*
*Reward: +5 to all base stats. You have been recognized by the Architect.*

Jace pushed himself into a sitting position, his body screaming with a dozen different kinds of pain. He looked around. They were in a circular chamber of perfectly carved grey stone. The air was cool, dry, and clean, a stark contrast to the humid rot of the Silt. A single, wide staircase spiraled up the wall, leading to a higher level.

"Too bad we didn't get the loot," Jace said, still panting but unable to suppress a wide grin. "But that was pretty awesome!"

He looked at Bella. She was leaning against the wall, catching her breath, but she wasn't looking at him. She was looking at something in her hands.

Bella smiled shyly, a flicker of pride in her eyes that Jace had never seen before.

"You mean THIS loot?"

She held out her hands. In one palm was a pulsing, sickly green orb the size of his fist. It felt warm and vaguely slimy even from a distance. In her other hand was a small, leather-bound amulet on a cord, with a single, perfectly preserved Silt-Reaver tooth hanging from it.

Jace’s jaw dropped. He stared from the items to her face. "How... When... I was running!"

"You were distracting," Bella corrected him, her voice gaining confidence. "When you jumped on the Croaker and stomped on its face... they weren't looking at the portal anymore. They were only looking at you."

She walked over and sat down beside him. "When the monster died, these things... they just appeared out of the air right where it fell. Valerius and his group, they were angry. And angry people are stupid. They forgot the most important rule."

"What rule?" Jace asked, still stunned.

"Secure the objective," Bella said simply. "You were the new objective. So, while you were leading them on a chase, I... took a detour."

She handed him the green orb. As soon as he touched it, a System notification appeared.

**[Gatekeeper's Bile-Core (Rare)]**
*Description: The condensed essence of the Floor 1 Gatekeeper. Contains a massive amount of raw, chaotic energy.*
*Effect: Can be absorbed to gain a one-time, massive boost to Constitution or used as a core component for poison-based crafting.*

Jace looked at the orb, then back at Bella, a slow, dawning sense of awe on his face. He hadn't just created an escape. He had created the perfect opening, and she had been smart enough to see it and seize it.

"And this," Bella said, holding up the amulet.

**[Swamp-Warder's Charm (Uncommon)]**
*Description: An amulet imbued with the miasma of the Silt.*
*Effect: +10% Resistance to Poison and Acid damage. Negates the [Slippery] status effect from mud and slime.*

Jace started to laugh. It began as a low chuckle and grew into a full-throated, joyous roar that echoed in the stone chamber. He had been so focused on the fight, on the taunts, on the run, that he hadn't even considered the reward. He had played the part of the berserker, while she had played the part of the silent professional.

"You're not just the brains, Bella," Jace said, shaking his head in disbelief. "You're the thief."

Bella blushed, but she didn't deny it. "We earned it. Now... what do we do with this core? It says 'absorb'."

Jace looked at the green, pulsing heart in his hand. Then he looked at the staircase leading up into the unknown dangers of Floor 2.

"We get stronger," Jace said, his grin turning sharp and dangerous. "We get a lot stronger."
