# Chapter 9: The Anomaly

Jace was still marveling at the feeling of his newly reinforced body when a different kind of notification flashed in his vision. It wasn't the standard blue alert box. This one was golden, shimmering at the edges, and it appeared for both him and Bella simultaneously.

**[SYSTEM ANOMALY DETECTED...]**
**[ANALYZING USER JACE'S RECENT ACTIONS: KILL-STEAL (NEGATIVE), TEAMWORK (POSITIVE), RISK-TAKING (NEUTRAL), WILLFUL ENDURANCE OF PAIN FOR ALLY'S BENEFIT (UNPRECEDENTED)]**

Jace blinked. "Unprecedented?"

**[SYSTEM TRAIT RECOGNIZED: SELFLESSNESS (TIER 1)]**
**[SYSTEM TRAIT RECOGNIZED: LEADERSHIP (TIER 1)]**
**[SYSTEM TRAIT RECOGNIZED: CHARISMA (POTENTIAL)]**

**[STANDARD ATTRIBUTES (STRENGTH, AGILITY, INTELLIGENCE...) ARE INSUFFICIENT TO QUANTIFY THIS BEHAVIORAL AXIS.]**
**[INITIATING NEW PROTOCOL...]**

**[NEW STAT UNLOCKED: REPUTATION]**

Jace and Bella stared at the golden text, reading the description that unfolded beneath it.

*   **[Reputation]:** *A measure of your soul's weight within the Spire. It is the story others tell about you when you are not there. This attribute is not raised through training, but through deeds. It influences social interactions, the loyalty of allies, the fear of enemies, and the Architect's perception of your 'worth'.*

**[Current Reputation: 2 (Dreg Scrapper -> Respected Partner)]**
*Note: A positive Reputation may unlock unique social quests and interactions. A negative Reputation may unlock paths of intimidation and fear.*

The golden box faded, leaving a new line in Jace's status screen, nestled right below his Willpower.

Jace stared at it, utterly bewildered. "What the hell is Reputation? Can I punch things with it?"

Bella, however, understood immediately. Her eyes, now sharp with her enhanced Intellect, were wide with the massive implication of what had just happened.

"No, Jace," she said, her voice full of awe. "You can't punch things with it. I think... I think it's how you get other people to punch things *for* you."

She looked at him, truly seeing the shift that had occurred. He wasn't just a tough kid anymore. The System itself had branded him.

"Valerius and the Highborn... their power comes from their families, their training," Bella reasoned, thinking aloud. "It's an external power. This... this is different. This is a power you *made*. The Spire itself just acknowledged that you're more than just a Dreg. You're a leader."

Jace looked at his hands, then at the new line item on his stat sheet. He didn't fully understand it, but he understood the look in Bella's eyes. It was a look of absolute, unwavering trust.

"Huh," Jace grunted, a slow smile spreading across his face. "So if we keep doing things our way... we get stronger in ways they can't even measure."

He looked up the massive, spiraling staircase that was Floor 2.

"Okay then, Analyst," Jace said, cracking his knuckles. "Let's go raise our Reputation."

The fight was a blur of matted fur, sharp claws, and guttural hisses. The creatures—a pack of five Chitter-Kin, small, stooped humanoids with rodent-like faces—were faster than anything on Floor 1.

Jace met them head-on, a whirlwind of brutal efficiency. He smashed the first with his rebar, the impact crushing bone. He used his new [Tripwire Shot] to send a second one tumbling, finishing it with a stomp. But there were too many. They swarmed.

"The leader!" Bella called out from behind a pillar. "The one with the scarred ear! Its left leg is weak!"

A shimmering red aura, visible only to Jace, highlighted the Chitter-Kin leader's knee. Jace lunged. As he engaged the leader, two others closed in on him, clawing at the crude rat-hide armor on his back. He grunted, ignoring the shallow cuts.

But the fifth one, the smallest of the pack, suddenly broke off. It ignored Jace completely. Its beady eyes fixed on Bella. It had seen her point. It had identified her as the source of the tactical weakness.

"Jace!" Bella screamed as the creature screeched and charged her hiding spot.

Jace’s blood ran cold. He couldn't disengage; the leader and its two guards had him pinned. He made a choice. He ignored a claw swiping for his face and took the hit, a deep gash tearing open his bicep, in exchange for a single, desperate moment to act.

**[CRITICAL DAMAGE DETECTED]**

He spun, whipping his sling with his uninjured arm. The stone flew, not at the creature charging Bella, but at the pillar right beside it. The stone shattered, spraying rock chips into the Chitter-Kin's face. It flinched, momentarily blinded, giving Jace the second he needed.

He roared, a sound of pure fury, and shoulder-checked the Chitter-Kin leader, sending it stumbling back. He then lunged past it, intercepting the smaller creature just as it leaped for Bella. He didn't have time to use his rebar. He just caught it, wrapping his arms around its thrashing body and slamming it into the stone floor with all his might.

The battle was over thirty seconds later. The remaining two, their leader dead, broke and fled into the darkness of the upper floors.

Jace stood panting amidst the carnage, his arm dripping blood onto the clean stone. Bella rushed over, her face pale, tearing a fresh strip from her already ragged tunic.

"I'm sorry," she whispered, her hands shaking as she tried to wrap his arm. "It looked at me. As soon as I used the skill, it just... it knew."

Jace winced as she tightened the bandage. "Not your fault," he grunted, though his face was a mask of frustration. "You did your job. I didn't do mine."

They found a small, defensible alcove off the main staircase to rest. Jace sat heavily, leaning against the wall, staring at the opposite wall as if replaying the fight on its surface.

"I can take the hits," he said, thinking aloud. "My Constitution is high, the Furnace heals me. I can kill them. But I can't make them fight me. That's the problem."

He looked at his hands. "When those things came at me, I was just a wall. An obstacle. But you..." he looked at Bella. "You were a *threat*. Your brain is a bigger weapon than my rebar, and they knew it."

This was a fundamental flaw in his plan. He couldn't just be a wall. A wall can be walked around. He had to be a *dangerous* wall. A wall with spikes. A wall that screamed.

"Aggro," he muttered, using a term he'd overheard the Highborn kids using. "I need to... to hold their attention. To make them so angry they can't think straight."

How did a street rat make someone angry? You didn't just stand there. You got in their face. You insulted their mother. You kicked sand at them. You became the most infuriating, unavoidable problem in their world.

"My [Pest] title," Jace said, a flicker of an idea in his eyes. "The System said it makes enemies lose focus. I've been using it by accident. What if I use it on purpose?"

He stood up, ignoring the throbbing in his arm. He looked around the alcove. A small, crystal-shelled lizard, a passive mob, was clinging to the wall, tasting the air with a forked tongue.

"Hey, ugly!" Jace yelled at it.

The lizard blinked, its head tilting.

**[Attempting to apply [Intimidation]... FAILURE. Target is not intelligent enough to be intimidated.]**

"That's not it," Jace muttered. He needed to focus his intent. Not fear. Annoyance.

He walked over to the lizard. He didn't touch it. He just leaned in close and made a series of loud, obnoxious clicking sounds with his tongue, mimicking the Chitter-Kin. He waved his hand in front of its face, blocking its view. He was being a bully.

The lizard hissed, its frills flaring in agitation. It took a step back, but its eyes were locked on Jace. It didn't see him as a predator. It saw him as a profound nuisance.

A new notification, sharp and clear, appeared.

**[User Intent analyzed: To Provoke. To Irritate. To Monopolize Attention.]**
**[Synergy Detected: [Pest] Title + [Reputation] Stat]**

**[New Skill Unlocked: Goad (Lvl 1)]**
*Description: You weaponize your obnoxious presence. By focusing on a single enemy, you can force it to recognize you as the primary threat on the battlefield.*
*Effect: Forces a single target to focus its attacks on you for 10 seconds. Target suffers a -5% penalty to its Defense as it grows angry and sloppy.*
*Cost: 10 Stamina.*

Jace’s eyes lit up. He looked at the new skill, then at Bella, a confident, almost dangerous grin spreading across his face.

"Okay, Analyst," he said, flexing his bandaged arm. "I think I just figured out how to be a proper tank."
