# Chapter 10: The Barrier

"But first," Jace said, his voice grim as he stared at the bandage on his arm. "I have another idea. And you're not going to like it... at first."

He walked away from their alcove and began methodically gathering stones from the floor of the massive chamber. Not large rocks, but smooth, palm-sized pebbles, worn down by ages of disuse. He piled them near their resting spot until he had a small mound of them.

Bella watched him, a knot of anxiety tightening in her stomach. "Jace, what are you doing?"

Jace sat down opposite her, about fifteen feet away. He picked up one of the stones, weighing it in his hand.

"In the last fight, I lost aggro," he said, not looking at her. "I couldn't get to you in time. I can't let that happen again. You need a shield."

"A shield?" Bella asked. "I can't carry one, I'm not strong enough. And we don't have the materials to—"

"Not a real shield," Jace interrupted, finally meeting her gaze. His orange-flecked eyes were deadly serious. "A shield made of your mind. Made of your mana. But the System won't just give you a skill like that. You have to prove you need it. You have to *desperately* need it."

He lifted the stone. "It's going to hurt a bit."

Bella's blood ran cold. "You... you're going to throw rocks at me?"

"Pain is a good teacher," Jace said flatly. "When that Chitter-Kin came at you, what was the first thing you did?"

"I screamed!"

"Wrong. The first thing you did was flinch. You threw your hands up. Your body *tried* to make a shield. We're going to teach your mana to do the same thing." He softened his voice, just a fraction. "Bella, I'm not going to break your bones. But it has to be real. Your intent has to be real. When I throw this, don't just flinch. Don't just try to block it with your hands. *Push back*. With your mind. Imagine a wall between you and this rock."

Bella shook her head, terrified. "I can't."

"You can," Jace said, his voice hard again. "Ready?"

He didn't wait for an answer. He tossed the stone underhand. It wasn't fast, but it was accurate. Bella yelped and threw her hands up, just as he'd predicted. The stone smacked squarely against her forearm. It stung, a sharp, blooming pain that made her eyes water.

"Pain," Jace said, picking up another stone. "Now you know what you're trying to stop. Again. Don't use your arms. See the rock. *Feel* it coming. Build the wall."

He threw the second stone.

This time, Bella squeezed her eyes shut. She didn't raise her hands. She pictured a pane of glass in front of her. She poured her will into it. *Stop. Stop. Stop.*

The rock hit her in the shoulder. She cried out, stumbling back.

**[MANA POOL UNSTABLE. INTENT FORMED, BUT NO STRUCTURE.]**

"Closer!" Jace said, a flicker of excitement in his voice. "The System felt it! You tried. But a thought isn't a wall. You're the Analyst, Bella. Use your brain! Structure it! Give it edges! Give it a thickness!"

Bella was crying now, tears of frustration and pain mixing. "I'm trying!"

"Try harder!" Jace yelled, picking up a third stone. "The world doesn't care if you're trying! It only cares what you DO! SEE THE WALL!"

He threw this one a little harder.

This time, something was different. Bella's fear was eclipsed by a flash of pure, defiant anger. *No.*

She didn't just imagine a wall. She used her [Purity of Thought]. She saw the trajectory of the stone as a clean, geometric arc. She pictured a single point in the air where that arc would intercept her body. And at that point, she focused every ounce of her mana and willpower.

A faint, hexagonal shimmer, no bigger than her hand, flickered into existence a foot in front of her face.

*Thwack.*

The stone hit the shimmer and... stopped. It didn't bounce off. It just lost all momentum and dropped harmlessly to the floor. The hexagonal shield vanished a second later.

Bella stared at the pebble on the ground, then at her hands, panting. She felt dizzy, drained.

Then, the notification came, glowing with a soft, protective silver light.

**[Skill Unlocked: Mana Barrier (Lvl 1)]**
*Description: By focusing your will, you can manifest a small, temporary shield of pure mana to block incoming attacks.*
*Effect: Creates a 1ft x 1ft barrier with 20 HP. Drains Mana per second while active. Cannot be moved.*
*Requirement: User must be stationary.*

Jace’s grim expression broke into a wide, triumphant grin. He dropped the rest of the stones.

"See?" he said, his voice laced with pride. "I told you you could do it."

Bella looked at him, wiping the tears from her eyes with the back of her hand. She was exhausted and her arm and shoulder throbbed. But beneath the pain, a new feeling was blooming. Power.

"Okay," she said, her voice shaky but firm. "Again."

The alcove became a training ground. For the next hour, the only sounds were the scrape of Jace picking up stones, the whistle of them cutting through the air, and the sharp, crystalline *thwack* as they met Bella’s shimmering shield.

At first, her barriers were clumsy. They would flicker and die, costing her huge chunks of mana. A few stones got through, leaving new bruises. But Bella was an Analyst. She wasn't just enduring the pain; she was processing the data.

She learned that a wider shield was less stable. She learned that holding it for too long was a waste of energy. She began to anticipate the arc of Jace's throws, manifesting the hexagonal barrier at the last possible second, minimizing the mana drain. She got better. Fast.

Jace noticed the shift. She wasn't wincing anymore. Her expression, once fearful, was now one of intense, almost academic concentration. Then, he saw the corner of her mouth twitch. A small, devious smile.

He threw a stone, a little faster this time.

Bella’s barrier snapped into existence. But instead of being flat, it was angled, tilted slightly to the left. The stone didn't just drop; it ricocheted off the hard-light surface and zipped past Jace's head, smacking into the far wall with a loud *crack*.

Jace froze, the next stone halfway to his shoulder. "Huh." He stared at the impact mark on the wall, then back at Bella. Her smile widened.

*A fluke,* he thought. *A lucky bounce.*

He threw the next one with more purpose. He aimed right for her center mass.

The angled shield appeared again, this time tilted in the opposite direction. The pebble shot back, humming through the air, and embedded itself in the dirt floor right next to his foot.

Jace stared at the pebble, then slowly looked up. Bella was practically beaming, her eyes sparkling with newfound confidence. He picked up one last stone, holding it up for her to see. A silent challenge.

He threw it.

Bella’s shield materialized with perfect, instantaneous precision. It wasn't just angled; she gave it a subtle, concave curve. The stone hit the center of the shimmering hexagon and shot back with almost the same velocity Jace had thrown it.

*Thud.*

The rock bounced harmlessly off the thickest part of his rat-hide vest. It didn't hurt, but the message was unmistakable.

Jace looked down at the pebble that had fallen at his feet, then back up at Bella, a look of utter disbelief on his face.

"Did you just reflect that rock back at me?"

Bella's shy demeanor was gone, replaced by the confident smirk of someone who had just solved a very difficult puzzle. "You said to build a wall," she said, her voice clear and steady. "You never said which way it had to face."

As if on cue, a silver notification shimmered in the air between them.

**[SYSTEM ANALYSIS: UNCONVENTIONAL APPLICATION OF [Mana Barrier] SKILL DETECTED.]**
**[User Intent: Not to Block, but to Return.]**
**[User has successfully weaponized a defensive ability through geometric innovation.]**

**[Skill Modified!]**
**[Mana Barrier (Lvl 1) -> Riposte Barrier (Lvl 1)]**
*Description: A refined mana shield angled to deflect incoming projectiles back at their source.*
*Effect: Blocks incoming physical projectiles. On a successful block, 25% of the projectile's kinetic force is reflected back at the attacker.*
*Note: Costs 15% more mana than a standard block.*

Jace stared at the notification, then at Bella, and then he let out a loud, barking laugh. It was a sound of pure, unadulterated pride. He had wanted to give her a shield, and in the process, she had forged herself a sword.

"Okay, Analyst," Jace said, kicking the pile of pebbles aside. "New rule. From now on, when they shoot at you... you shoot back."
