# Chapter 11: Sanguine Offering

The victory was hollow. The last of the Chitter-Kin lay dead, but the cost had been high. They had been sloppy, tired, and it had almost cost Bella her life.

They huddled together in the alcove, the silence broken only by Bella’s soft, pained whimpers. She had a deep gash on her leg and her arm was bent at an unnatural angle. Jace, by contrast, was already knitting back together. His own cuts were scabbing over, the bruises on his torso fading as the *Internal Furnace* burned mana to fuel his regeneration.

**[HP: 85/110... 86/110...]**

He watched his own health bar tick slowly upward. Beside him, Bella’s was static, a terrifyingly low number.

**[HP: 18/65]**
**[STATUS EFFECT: BROKEN BONE (LEFT ARM)]**
**[Natural Healing Severely Impeded.]**

"She will still get hurt, eventually," Jace thought, the frustration a bitter taste in his mouth. "And I can only heal myself... or... is that really true?"

He looked at his own arm, where the deepest cut was now just an angry red line. His body was a factory. It took in mana and churned out repair. What was the delivery mechanism? What carried that repair to the damaged tissue?

*Blood.*

The realization was a cold, sharp shock. His blood wasn't normal anymore. It was supercharged with the energy of his furnace. It was a liquid regenerative agent.

A terrible, desperate idea began to form in his mind.

"Bella," he said softly.

She looked up, her face pale and streaked with tears and grime. "It hurts, Jace."

"I know," he said, his voice gentle. He unsheathed the sharpened bone he used as a dagger. "I'm going to try something. I need you to trust me. Like with the rocks."

Her eyes widened in fear as she saw the blade. "What are you doing?"

"My furnace heals me. It fixes my body," he explained, his words careful and deliberate. "That healing... it's in my blood. What if... what if it can fix you, too?"

It was insane. A Dreg's desperate, uneducated guess at battlefield medicine. But in Bella's pained, exhausted eyes, he saw the flicker of trust that had been forged in the Silt. She gave a tiny, almost imperceptible nod.

Jace didn't hesitate. He drew the bone dagger across the palm of his uninjured hand. A deep, clean cut. Blood, thick and dark, welled up instantly. In the dim light of the chamber, it seemed to have a faint, ember-like glow.

**[HP: 84/110]**

"Here," Jace said, holding out his hand. "Drink it."

Bella stared, horrified and mesmerized. The smell of his blood was strange, not just coppery, but with an underlying scent of ozone and heat, like a blacksmith's forge after the fire has been quenched.

Swallowing her revulsion, she leaned forward. She closed her eyes and pressed her lips to the cut, taking a small, hesitant sip.

The effect was instantaneous.

A wave of pure, clean warmth spread from her mouth down into her chest, chasing away the cold shock of her injuries. It wasn't a potion that numbed the pain; it was a tide of vitality that began actively knitting her broken body back together. She could feel the fire in his blood fighting the damage in hers.

**[HP: 19/65... 20/65... 21/65...]**

Her health bar was moving.

Jace watched, his expression a mixture of awe and grim satisfaction. His own health ticked down as hers ticked up. He was literally pouring his life force into her.

A golden notification, the Architect's signature shimmer, appeared before them.

**[SYSTEM ANALYSIS: UNPRECEDENTED BIOLOGICAL INTERACTION DETECTED.]**
**[User Jace is converting his own refined Life Force into a consumable healing agent for an ally.]**
**[This process is inefficient and physically damaging to the user.]**
**[Synergy with 'The Crucible' Title confirmed.]**

**[New Skill Created for User Jace: Sanguine Offering (Active)]**
*Description: You sacrifice your own vitality to mend the wounds of another.*
*Effect: Convert your HP into a healing draught for an ally. Conversion Rate: 2 HP (Jace) -> 1 HP (Ally). Cannot be used on self.*
*Warning: Excessive use can lead to [Anemia] or [Stamina Depletion].*

Bella pulled back, wiping her mouth, her eyes wide with shock. The sharpest agony in her arm had dulled to a manageable throb. The bleeding from her leg had stopped.

"Jace..." she breathed. "It worked."

He just nodded, wrapping his bleeding palm with another strip of his ruined tunic. He felt dizzy, drained, but a fierce, protective resolve settled over him.

He couldn't be everywhere at once. He couldn't block every hit. But now, he had one final, desperate defense for her. If the wall broke, he could become the medicine. And in the brutal meritocracy of the Spire, that was a power no Highborn could ever buy.
