# Chapter 12: Tactical Survey

The alcove was quiet for a long time after Bella’s wounds had closed, leaving only a dull ache and the stark reality of their near-failure. Jace sat with his back against the wall, watching the faint orange glow of his own veins under his skin as his body slowly refilled its stolen vitality.

He was a walking, breathing fortress. He could take a beating, regenerate, and even pour his own life into his partner. A true leader helps improve his team. He was trying his best.

But Bella needed more.

He had taught her to build a wall of force. He had become her emergency healing potion. But both of those were solutions for when things had already gone wrong. They were reactions. And reacting meant they were always one step behind.

What could she learn next? How to throw a rock back? How to create a bigger shield?

Jace stopped. The thought felt... wrong. Arrogant.

*Who was he kidding?*

He was the Dreg who fought with a spike and his fists. His solutions were always going to be some version of "hit it harder" or "get hit less." She was the one who saw patterns. She saw the math in the chaos.

He had been trying to teach a grandmaster of chess how to be a better pawn.

"Bella," he said, his voice quiet, breaking the silence.

She looked up from inspecting her newly-mended arm.

"I've been an idiot," he stated, not as an apology, but as a simple statement of fact. "I've been trying to make you tougher. Trying to give you armor. A shield. Healing. I've been teaching you how to survive a beating."

He shook his head. "That's my job. I'm the one who gets beat up. Your job... is to think. And I've been trying to do your job for you. That's stupid."

He leaned forward, his gaze intense and full of a respect he hadn't known how to show before.

"So, I'm asking you. What do *you* need? Forget my ideas. You're the Analyst. Analyze our problem. We're two people against an entire tower. How do we win?"

Bella was taken aback. For the first time, he wasn't giving her a command or a piece of desperate advice. He was asking for a strategy. He was handing her the reins.

She looked around their small, safe alcove. She looked up the spiraling staircase that led into more danger. She thought about the Chitter-Kin swarming them, about how they had been forced to fight on the creature's terms, in an open space where numbers mattered.

"You're right," she said slowly, her mind beginning to hum with the clarity her new stats provided. "Reacting is a losing game. We can't win a fair fight, Jace. So... we have to stop fighting fair."

Her eyes lit up with a spark of pure, intellectual fire.

"We need an advantage before the first punch is ever thrown," she continued. "We shouldn't be looking for monsters. We should be looking for *traps*."

"Traps?" Jace asked.

"Not just monster traps," Bella clarified, standing up and beginning to pace. "The environment. This entire tower is a weapon. We've just been walking on it. We need to learn how to *use* it. Why fight five creatures in an open room when we can make them follow us down a narrow hallway? Why trade blows with a monster when we can drop a piece of the ceiling on its head?"

She stopped and looked at him, her idea fully formed. "I don't need to be tougher, Jace. I need to be *smarter*. I need to learn how to read the battlefield itself. I need to see the loose pillar, the unstable floor, the chokepoint we can defend. *That's* my shield. An unfair advantage."

Jace stared at her, a slow, dangerous grin spreading across his face. "So... how do you learn that?"

Bella’s eyes scanned their surroundings with new purpose. "I stop looking at the threats. And I start looking at the opportunities."

She walked to the edge of their alcove and ran her hand along the stone wall, feeling for cracks. She crumbled a piece of mortar between her fingers, testing its consistency. She stared up at the ceiling, not just looking at the rock, but at the way it was joined, the stress lines, the shadows that might indicate a hollow space. She was mapping the world in three dimensions, not as a place, but as a machine.

She was showing the System her intent.

A pale, silver light began to glow around her, the System responding to the sheer force of her concentration.

**[SYSTEM ANALYSIS: USER BELLA IS ATTEMPTING TO DECONSTRUCT THE BATTLEFIELD ENVIRONMENT ON A TACTICAL LEVEL.]**
**[INTELLIGENCE CHECK: PASSED]**
**[WILLPOWER CHECK: PASSED]**

**[New Skill Unlocked: Tactical Survey (Active)]**
*Description: You read the environment as a schematic of violence, seeing not just what is, but what could be.*
*Effect: When activated, highlights interactable environmental objects within a 30-foot radius. (e.g., Unstable Rocks, Weak Walls, Slippery Surfaces, Chokepoints).*
*Cost: 5 Mana per second.*

Bella gasped, her vision shifting. The world was suddenly overlaid with a faint, shimmering grid. The pillar near the staircase pulsed with a soft yellow light. A patch of moss on the steps ahead glowed with a faint green sheen. High above them, a cluster of stalactites was outlined in a menacing red.

"Jace," she breathed, pointing a trembling finger upward. "If we make them angry... and we stand right here... I can make the ceiling fall down."

Jace looked up at the glowing red stalactites, then back at Bella. He wasn't looking at a scared girl from the Professional class anymore. He was looking at his general. He was the hammer. But from now on, she would be the one who told him exactly where to strike.

"Now *that's* how you're supposed to fight," Jace said, a low chuckle rumbling in his chest. "With your head... and some well-timed blows, of course."
