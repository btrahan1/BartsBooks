# Chapter 13: The Aegis Beetles

Their new strategy was put to the test sooner than they expected. The spiraling staircase of Floor 2 opened into a series of interconnected caverns, vast and echoing. Patrolling these caverns were creatures that made the Chitter-Kin look like pests. They were **Aegis Beetles**, hulking insectoids the size of sheep, covered in thick, overlapping plates of black chitin that looked like solid stone.

The first time they saw one, Jace instinctively tightened his grip on his rebar, preparing for a brutal, head-on assault. But Bella put a hand on his arm.

"Wait," she whispered, her eyes already glowing with the pale silver light of her new skill.

**[Tactical Survey: Active]**

Her gaze wasn't on the beetle. It was on the cavern around it. The beetle was patrolling a wide, circular path around a deep, dark fissure in the center of the room. A narrow, natural stone bridge, barely three feet wide, spanned the chasm. The entire bridge was glowing with a faint yellow light in Bella's vision. A single point on their side of the bridge, a large, cracked keystone holding it to the cavern floor, pulsed with a brighter, more insistent red.

"There," Bella said, her voice steady and sure. "That's the kill zone."

She looked at Jace. "It's too heavily armored for a straight fight. We'd break our weapons before we broke its shell. But it's heavy. And it's stupid."

Jace's feral grin returned. "So the plan is...?"

"You make it angry," Bella said, picking up a sharp-edged stone for her own sling. "You make it so angry it forgets there's a giant hole in the floor. You lead it onto the bridge. And I'll handle the rest."

Jace nodded, tossing his rebar from hand to hand. "Time to be a pest."

He stepped out from behind their cover. "HEY, SHELL-FOR-BRAINS!" he roared.

The Aegis Beetle stopped, its multifaceted eyes swiveling to focus on him. It let out a low, chittering hiss of annoyance.

Jace activated his own new skill, focusing his will, his very presence, into a single, infuriating point of light in the beetle's primitive mind.

**[Goad: Active]**

"COME AND GET ME, YOU OVERGROWN ROACH!"

The effect was instantaneous. The beetle's low hiss became a furious shriek. It abandoned its patrol route, its heavy legs scrabbling against the stone as it charged, single-mindedly, at the source of its irritation. Its Defense stat, as promised, dropped as its rage climbed.

Jace didn't stand his ground. He did what he did best. He ran.

He led the creature on a perfect, looping path, straight for the narrow stone bridge. The beetle, blinded by the magical rage of [Goad], followed without hesitation. Jace was light on his feet, his [Agility] carrying him across the bridge in seconds.

The beetle was not. Its immense weight caused the stone bridge to groan in protest. Dust and small pebbles rained down into the chasm below.

"BELLA, NOW!" Jace screamed as he reached the far side.

Bella was already in motion. She had been waiting, her sling a blur. The moment the beetle's full weight was on the center of the bridge, she released her stone. It wasn't a powerful shot. It didn't need to be. It was precise.

*CRACK!*

The stone slammed squarely into the glowing red keystone. A spiderweb of fractures erupted from the point of impact. With a deafening groan of tortured rock, the entire bridge gave way.

The Aegis Beetle had a single, confused moment where the ground vanished beneath its feet. Then, with a final, surprised shriek, it plunged into the darkness of the fissure, its heavy shell ensuring it would fall fast and far. A few seconds later, a faint, distant crunch echoed up from the depths.

Jace and Bella stood on opposite sides of the new, wider chasm, panting in the sudden silence. They hadn't taken a single point of damage. They had expended a handful of stamina and a few points of mana. It was the most efficient, most elegant, and most brutally effective fight of their lives.

Jace looked across the gap at Bella, who was lowering her sling, a look of fierce, intellectual pride on her face.

He simply nodded, a sign of profound respect. This was their new rhythm. This was how they would win. Not with rage and desperation, but with a plan. He was the bait, she was the trigger, and the Spire itself was their weapon.
