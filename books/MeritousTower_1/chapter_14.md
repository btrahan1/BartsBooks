# Chapter 14: The 24-Hour Notification

They stood in the echoing silence, the chasm a stark reminder of their new, brutal efficiency. Just as Jace was about to search for a way around the gap, a low, resonant chime filled the air. It wasn't the sharp ping of a skill-up or the urgent alert of a system warning. This was different. Deeper. It felt ancient.

A transparent screen, this one tinged with a neutral grey, materialized in front of both of them.

**[System Notification. You have survived for 24 hours. Well done!]**
**[Now... some information you might find useful.]**
**[At Level 5, you will find access to a System Store. Maybe.]**
**[At Level 10, you are granted the option to leave the Spire.]**
**[More information is coming. Try not to die.]**

The screen faded, leaving the two of them in a profound silence.

Jace stared at the spot where the text had been, a deep frown on his face. He kicked a loose stone into the chasm.

"Maybe?" he spat, the word dripping with the cynicism of a life spent dealing with empty promises. "What the hell does 'maybe' mean? You find it, or you don't. There's no maybe in survival."

Bella, however, was processing it differently. Her mind, humming with its new power, was racing through the implications.

"Twenty-four hours," she whispered, a shiver running down her spine. "How many... how many of the others are left to even see this message?"

"Not our problem," Jace said, though the thought was a grim one. He started walking along the edge of the fissure, looking for a way across. "Level 5. A store. That's a real target. A store means gear. Better weapons. Real armor, not... this." He plucked at his stitched-together rat-hide vest.

"And Level 10," Bella added, her voice soft with a fragile, dangerous thing: hope. "Jace. It said we can *leave*. An exit. A way out of all of this."

Jace stopped and looked back at her across the chasm. "Leave and go where, Bella? Back to the Silt? Back to the alleys? What's out there for us?" For him, the Spire wasn't just a prison; it was the first place in his life where the rules, however brutal, were clear. Effort was rewarded. Here, he wasn't a Dreg. He was a survivor.

"I don't know," she admitted. "But... it's a choice. We didn't have a choice when we were brought here."

Jace grunted, seeing the logic in that. He found a spot where the chasm narrowed and, with a running start, leaped across, landing with a heavy thud on her side.

"Alright," he said, dusting himself off. "We can worry about leaving when we get to Level 10. Right now, we have a new mission."

He checked his status screen. After the beetle kill, he was Level 4. Bella was almost there, too.

"We hunt," Jace said, his eyes burning with a renewed, ferocious purpose. "We use our heads, we use the damn walls, and we grind. I want to see what's in this 'maybe' store."

He looked at Bella, and for the first time, his grin wasn't just feral; it was hungry for something more than survival. It was hungry for power.

"Let's go find out what a Dreg can buy when he can finally afford it."
