# Chapter 15: The Sepulcher

Floor 3 was a graveyard. A vertical city of the dead called the Sepulcher.

Unlike the organic chaos of the first two floors, this one was eerily ordered. Mausoleums and crypts were carved directly into the Spire's inner wall, connected by a network of crumbling stone staircases and precarious ledges that crisscrossed a dizzying, thousand-foot drop. The air was still, cold, and heavy with the scent of ancient dust.

They were moving slowly, Jace in the lead, his rebar held at the ready, while Bella scanned their surroundings, her [Tactical Survey] a constant, low-level drain on her mana.

"Jace... what is that?"

Her voice was a quiet breath, barely disturbing the silence. Jace froze, dropping into a low crouch. He followed her gaze, his own eyes, now accustomed to the gloom, scanning the wall of a large, ornate tomb they were passing.

"I don't see anything," he squinted. "Just rock."

"No, not on the rock. *In* the rock," Bella insisted. She took a step closer, her head tilted. "There's a... a thing. A glowing yellow thingee. It's faint, like a symbol drawn in sunlight, but it's right there." She pointed to a section of the tomb's wall that looked completely unremarkable to Jace. "You don't see that?"

A look of dawning comprehension crossed her face. "Oh. Oh, that's part of my Analyst skill. It's not red for danger, or yellow for an unstable object..." Her voice dropped to a whisper of pure, unadulterated excitement. "I think... I think I can find... *treasure?*"

Jace's skepticism warred with the absolute trust he now had in her perception. He walked to the wall and ran his hand over the spot she had indicated. The stone was smooth, cold, and solid. There was no seam, no crack, nothing.

"Right there?" he asked, pressing his palm flat against the stone.

"A little to the left," Bella guided him. "It looks like one of the carved flowers in the pattern is... brighter than the others."

Jace looked closer. The wall was covered in a repeating motif of carved stone lilies. To him, they all looked identical. But trusting Bella, he took the pointed end of his rebar and pushed against the center of the lily she was focused on.

*Click.*

The stone flower depressed into the wall with a soft grinding sound. A section of the wall beside it, no bigger than Jace's head, slid inward, revealing a small, dark cavity.

A system notification, glowing with a soft golden light, appeared for Bella.

**[SYSTEM ANALYSIS: A new function of the Analyst Class has been discovered.]**
**[New Passive Skill Unlocked: Keen Eye (Lvl 1)]**
*Description: Your mind is attuned to anomalies. You can now perceive not only tactical opportunities, but also hidden objects, secret passages, and items of potential value.*
*Effect: Objects of interest will be highlighted with a golden aura, visible only to you.*

Jace ignored the notification he couldn't see and reached into the hole. His fingers brushed against something dry and leathery. He pulled it out.

It was a small scroll, bound with a simple cord. There was no writing on the outside.

"A book?" Jace said, a hint of disappointment in his voice. "We need a new axe, not a book."

"Let me see," Bella said, her hands trembling with excitement. She took the scroll, her fingers tracing its surface. As an Analyst, she instinctively understood that information was a far deadlier weapon than any axe. She unrolled it.

The scroll was blank for a moment, then words began to glow into existence on the parchment, written in the System's familiar font.

**[Skill Scroll: Identify]**
*Teaches the user the [Identify] skill.*
*Description: Reveals the basic properties, stats, and hidden attributes of an item, creature, or object.*
*Class Requirement: Analyst, Scholar, or Sage.*

Bella gasped, her mind reeling with the implications. "Jace," she breathed, her voice filled with awe. "This is better than an axe. This is... a key. This is the key to everything."

He looked at her, confused. "What's it do?"

"Remember the Gatekeeper's core? We didn't know what it did until we touched it. With this, I can *know* beforehand. I can look at a monster's claw and know if it's poisonous. I can look at a piece of armor and see if it's cursed. I can look at a potion and know if it'll heal us or kill us."

She crushed the scroll in her hand. It dissolved into motes of golden light that flowed into her, warm and empowering.

**[Skill Learned: Identify (Lvl 1)]**
*Cost: 5 Mana per use.*

Jace’s eyes widened as he finally understood. Every fight, every piece of loot, every strange plant they found... she would now have an answer. They would never have to guess again.

He looked at the hole in the wall, then back at the sprawling, silent city of tombs.

"How many more of these are there?" he whispered.

Bella smiled, a new and predatory gleam in her eyes. "Let's find out."
