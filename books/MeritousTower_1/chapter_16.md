# Chapter 16: Making Bags

The hunt had changed. They were no longer just hunting monsters. They were hunting secrets.

The single scroll felt heavy with possibility, a weight that had nothing to do with its physical mass. Jace looked at Bella, who was carefully re-rolling it, then at their empty hands. They had beast cores in their pockets and a few sharpened bones in their belts. That was it.

"This is a problem," Jace stated, kicking at a loose piece of flagstone. "You find one of these, and it's a treasure. You find ten, and it's just garbage we have to carry. We find something big, we have to leave it behind."

He stopped and looked at the pile of supplies they'd laid out in a small, defensible crypt. Among the stones and bones was a small stack of stiff, cured hides from the Chitter-Kin they'd killed. They were ugly, patchy things, the fur coarse and wiry.

"We need a bag," Jace declared. "Maybe two bags. Or a knapsack. Let's see if we can put some of these pelts to good use."

He grabbed the hides, his bone needle, and the last of his sinew thread. "Time to level up my tailoring."

He sat down, crossing his legs, and laid the materials out. He wasn't just slapping pieces together like he did with his first bracer. He was thinking. A bag needed a strong bottom. It needed straps that wouldn't snap. It needed a way to close so things wouldn't fall out. He was trying to engineer a solution, not just patch a hole.

Bella watched him, her mind working. "Wait, Jace. Let me try something."

She focused on the pile of pelts at his feet, her brow furrowed in concentration. She activated her new skill.

**[Identify: Active]**

A screen of text, visible only to her, materialized over the hides.

**[Chitter-Kin Hide (Common)]**
*Description: A tough, mangy pelt from a scavenger humanoid.*
*Properties: High tensile strength (difficult to tear). Low puncture resistance (easy to pierce). Slightly water-resistant. The thickest, most durable section is along the creature's spine.*

"The spine," Bella said aloud. "The System says the toughest part of the hide is the strip from the back. Use that for the bottom and the straps."

Jace paused, a hide held taut in his hands. He looked at the distinct line of thicker, darker fur running down the middle. He'd just been about to cut it randomly. He nodded, a slow smile of appreciation spreading across his face. "Good thinking, Analyst."

He began his work with new precision, using the sharpest rock flake he could find to carefully cut the patterns. He stitched with a focus that was almost meditative. He wasn't just sewing; he was pouring his intent for durability into every single stitch. The System responded.

**[Your understanding of materials and construction has deepened through focused effort.]**
**[Skill Leveled Up: Jury-Rigging (Lvl 2)]**
**[New Perk Unlocked: Reinforced Stitching.]**
*You can now create double-stitched seams that are 25% more durable than standard stitching.*

Jace grunted in satisfaction as he felt the new knowledge settle into his hands. He went back over the main seams of the bag, his bone needle flying with a new, more confident rhythm.

An hour later, he was done. He had two lopsided, ugly, and undeniably rugged bags. They smelled faintly of rodent and dust, but the seams were thick and strong. He'd even fashioned a simple flap for the top that could be tied down with a length of sinew.

He tossed the slightly smaller one to Bella. The System chimed as she caught it.

**[Scavenger's Haversack (Common)]**
*Quality: Sturdy.*
*Description: A crude but effective satchel, jury-rigged from common beast hides. Built for function, not for comfort.*
*Capacity: 10 Item Slots.*
*Durability: 25/25*

"It's... perfect," Bella said, slinging the strap over her shoulder. It was the first proper piece of equipment either of them had owned.

Jace slung the larger one over his own back. It felt good. Solid. The weight of potential. He looked around the vast, silent Sepulcher, at the thousands of tombs that lined the walls, each one a potential source of secrets and power.

"Alright, Analyst," Jace said, his voice echoing in the stillness. He cracked his knuckles.

"Time to fill these up."
