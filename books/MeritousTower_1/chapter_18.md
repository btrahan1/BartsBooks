# Chapter 18: Bella's Triumph

"Wow!" Bella breathed, the single word a mix of exhaustion, terror, and pure, undiluted triumph. She slid down the pillar she was leaning against, her legs finally giving out now that the adrenaline was gone.

She shared her notifications with him, a new feature of their growing party synergy. The glowing text boxes appeared in Jace's vision, clear as day.

**[Player 'Bella' has successfully performed a PERFECT PARRY against a Boss-level attack.]**
**[Skill Leveled Up: Riposte Barrier (Lvl 2)]**
**[New Perk Unlocked: Kinetic Overload.]**
*When successfully parrying a projectile, you can now spend an additional 20 Mana to add your [Intelligence] bonus to the reflected damage.*

**[Achievement Unlocked: Return to Sender]**
*Defeat an enemy by reflecting its own attack back at it.*
*Reward: Willpower +2. You have proven that the best defense is a good offense.*

Bella looked up at him from the floor, her face smudged with dust and grime, but her eyes were shining with a light brighter than any Grave-Light.

"I did good, yes!" She was practically glowing. The shy, terrified girl from the Induction was gone, replaced by a confident, deadly battlefield tactician who had just single-handedly landed the killing blow on a Floor Guardian.

And Jace... Jace was proud.

He walked across the rubble-strewn floor, his footsteps the only sound in the vast chamber. He looked at the shattered lantern, at the pile of debris that had been a fearsome monster moments before, and then he looked down at her.

He was proud, so fiercely proud that it was an ache in his chest. She hadn't just used the skill he'd forced her to learn; she had mastered it, evolved it, and used it with a brilliance he could never have imagined. She was his partner. His equal.

And he had to admit, a bit enamored.

He saw her not just as the Analyst, the brains of their operation, but as Bella. Bella, who had been brave enough to trust a dirty Dreg in the mud. Bella, who had seen the math in the chaos. Bella, who had just faced down a fifteen-foot-tall monster and didn't even flinch.

A slow, genuine smile spread across Jace's face. It wasn't his usual feral grin or taunting smirk. This one was different. Softer.

He reached down, offering her a hand.

"Good?" he said, his voice a low rumble. "Bella, that wasn't good."

He pulled her to her feet. She was still unsteady, but his grip was firm.

"That was perfect."
