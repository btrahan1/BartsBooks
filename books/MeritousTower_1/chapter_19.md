# Chapter 19: The Ashen Foundry

The obsidian archway on Floor 3 led them to a world of oppressive heat and hammering noise. Floor 4 was the Ashen Foundry, a vast, multi-leveled chamber filled with dormant furnaces, massive gears, and a labyrinth of iron pipes that hissed with steam. The air tasted of coal and rust. In the center of the foundry, a massive gate of brass and iron blocked the path to the next level. A hulking, four-armed automaton forged from blackened steel and glowing embers stood before it—the Gatekeeper.

"This is it, Bella," Jace said, his voice low, a stark contrast to the distant, rhythmic clang of machinery. "We defeat this Boss, and we get to see Level 5. We get to look for the shop."

He hefted his rebar—now scarred and dented from its trip through the Amalgam's core—and prepared to ask for the strategy. But before he could, Bella's hand shot out and clamped onto his arm, pulling him back.

"Jace..." she whispered, her voice tight with alarm. "Down there. By the main furnace."

Jace followed her gaze. A group was emerging from a side tunnel, moving with a disciplined, confident stride that Jace recognized instantly. They were clad in mismatched but effective-looking armor made from beetle chitin and scavenged metal. Their weapons were no longer sharpened sticks but cruel-looking spears and swords fashioned from monster parts.

"Oh no..." Jace breathed, his blood running cold. He grabbed Bella's hand and pulled her behind a bank of massive, cold steam pipes, their bodies instantly swallowed by the shadows and swirling mist. "Hide! It's the Elites!"

Valerius led the group. The arrogant sneer was gone, replaced by a cold, hard focus. He was taller, leaner, and the driftwood branch had been replaced by a wicked-looking sword crafted from a Chitter-Kin's sharpened spine. Marcus was beside him, his turtle-shell shield now reinforced with bands of iron. Elara and two others followed, their faces grim and hardened by days of constant battle. They were no longer panicked children. They were a hunting party.

"Check the perimeter," Valerius commanded, his voice sharp and clear in the foundry's din. "The *Codex* says the Forgemaster Automaton is dormant until the central bellows are activated. We gear up, we prepare, and we take it down before those Dreg thieves have a chance to show up and steal another prize."

Marcus grunted. "If they do show up, we kill them first. The boss can wait."

From their hiding spot, Jace felt a surge of rage, but Bella squeezed his hand, a silent command to stay put. She wasn't just hiding; she was observing. Her eyes glowed with silver light.

**[Identify: Active]**

A text box flared in her vision as she focused on Valerius's new weapon.

**[Chitter-Lord's Spineblade (Uncommon)]**
*Description: A serrated blade crafted from the spine of an Alpha Chitter-Kin.*
*Effect: +12 Physical Damage. Causes a [Bleed] effect on critical hits.*

Bella's eyes widened. They were out-geared. Horribly. A straight fight was suicide.

She shifted her focus, activating her other skill.

**[Tactical Survey: Active]**

The foundry lit up. The Automaton boss was a massive red threat. The central bellows Valerius mentioned pulsed with a yellow 'interactable' light. But so did a series of pressure valves on the steam pipes scattered around the chamber, including one right next to their hiding spot. A large chain hoist holding a massive, half-forged ingot of steel directly above the brass gate glowed with a critical, tempting red.

Valerius's group began setting up, checking their gear and drinking potions, completely unaware they were being watched.

"They have a plan," Jace whispered, his voice tight with frustration. "They know the mechanic. We don't."

"We do now," Bella whispered back, her mind racing, connecting the dots her skills had revealed. She looked at the pressure valve, then at the chain hoist, then at the confident Elites. "They're going to wake the boss up and fight it on their terms."

Jace looked at her, seeing the familiar, dangerous spark in her eyes. "What are you thinking, Analyst?"

Bella gave him a slow, devious smile. "They're going to fight the boss for us, Jace. And we're going to make sure they do it the hard way."

"Okay," Jace whispered, his knuckles white around a fist-sized piece of iron slag he’d picked up. "Let's do this."

Bella gave a single, sharp nod. Her hand was already resting on the large, rust-red wheel of the pressure valve. "They're getting ready to buff," she murmured, her eyes glowing as she analyzed their movements. "Elara is preparing a group [Fortitude] chant. When I release the steam, it will block their view and probably deafen them for a few seconds. That's your chance to get to the hoist control on the other side. Don't stop for anything."

Jace looked across the foundry. The chain hoist control was a large lever on a platform near the main furnace, a good fifty-yard sprint through a maze of pipes and machinery. It was a suicide run. Perfect.

"On my mark," Bella said.

Jace took a deep breath, and then bellowed, his voice echoing off the iron walls. "HEY, VALERIUS! DID YOU MISS US?"

He hurled the piece of slag. It didn't hit anyone. It clanged loudly against the brass gate right behind them, a perfect, infuriating distraction.

The five Elites spun around, their preparations forgotten. Valerius's face contorted with pure hatred. "You!" he snarled, raising his Spineblade. "I knew it! Fan out! Find them and kill them!"

But they never got the chance.

"NOW!" Bella screamed, and threw her entire body weight into turning the valve.

With a deafening roar, a superheated geyser of steam erupted from a series of pipes right in the middle of the foundry. It was a solid, blinding white wall, instantly splitting the Elite's formation. Marcus and another were on one side; Valerius, Elara, and the fifth were on the other, completely cut off.

The sudden, violent change in pressure and the concussive sound triggered something in the foundry. A deep, resonant *CLANG* echoed from the central gate. The Forgemaster Automaton’s ember-eyes flared to life. It took a grinding step forward, its four arms whirring and clicking into combat positions.

The Elites' controlled boss fight had just become a chaotic scramble for survival.

Jace was already a ghost in the machine.

The moment the steam erupted, he was sprinting. He vaulted over a low pipe, slid under a massive gear, his movements fluid and silent amidst the cacophony. He saw Marcus, confused, trying to peer through the steam. Jace didn't engage. He just ran.

"The Dreg is on the west flank!" Elara shrieked, catching a glimpse of his movement.

Valerius turned, his eyes wild with fury. "Forget the boss! Get him!"

It was too late. Jace was a blur. He scrambled up the side of the furnace, using vents as handholds, and leaped onto the control platform. The heavy iron lever for the hoist was right in front of him. Below, the battle was a confused mess. The Automaton had engaged Valerius's half of the group, its massive steel fists swinging in powerful, predictable arcs.

Bella, from her safe vantage point, had her focus locked on the chaos.

**[Tactical Survey: Active]**

She wasn't looking at Jace. She was looking at the boss. The Automaton raised two arms to bring them down on Valerius, and in doing so, its head, a polished sphere of steel, was momentarily exposed directly beneath the hanging ingot.

"NOW, JACE!" Bella's voice cut through the noise. "DROP IT!"

Jace didn't hesitate. He grabbed the lever with both hands and threw his entire weight downward.

With a screech of protesting metal, the chain hoist released. The half-forged, multi-ton block of steel and slag plummeted from the ceiling.

It didn't just fall. It crashed down with the force of a meteor.

The ingot slammed directly onto the Automaton's head, driving its entire torso down into the iron floor with a cataclysmic **BOOM** that shook the entire foundry. The Automaton didn't even have time to register the attack. It was instantly decapitated and destroyed.

But the ingot's impact didn't stop there. A massive shockwave erupted from the point of impact, buckling the iron floor plates and sending Valerius and Elara flying. The brass gate itself was bent and torn from its hinges, half-buried under the cooling metal.

The path to Floor 5 was open.

In the ringing silence that followed, Jace stood on the platform, looking down at the utter devastation. The Elites were scattered and stunned. The boss was a crater of scrap metal.

They hadn't just stolen the kill. They had used the Elites as the bait, the boss as a distraction, and the foundry itself as the weapon. This wasn't a fight. It was a demolition.

Jace didn't linger to admire his handiwork. He leaped from the control platform, landing in a crouch amidst the twisted metal. The Elites were just starting to pick themselves up, their ears ringing, their faces masks of stunned disbelief. They weren't angry anymore; they were broken. They had been so focused on Jace, the Dreg, that they had never once considered the ceiling.

"Bella! Go!" Jace yelled, already sprinting for the ruined gate.

Bella didn't need telling. She was already moving, her small frame darting through the settling dust and steam. Jace caught up to her, grabbing her hand not just to pull her along, but as a silent, shared signal of their victory.

They ran past the crater where the boss had stood, the air still shimmering with heat. Valerius pushed himself to his knees, his expensive Spineblade lying forgotten a few feet away. He watched them go, his face pale with a humiliation far deeper than any physical wound.

"Dregs..." he finally managed to choke out, the word a weak, pathetic hiss. "Filthy... Dregs..."

Jace stopped right at the threshold of the shimmering blue portal that had materialized within the mangled gate. He turned, his face split by a wide, utterly triumphant grin. He looked at the defeated Elites, at their broken formation and their shattered pride. He gave Bella's hand a small squeeze.

Then, with all the arrogance and swagger of a king, he shouted back into the foundry, his voice echoing off every pipe and furnace.

"That's *Mr. and Mrs. Dreg* to you!"

Bella's face, already flushed from the run, turned a shade of crimson so deep it was visible even in the dim light of the foundry. Her jaw dropped, and she let out a tiny, mortified squeak.

Jace just laughed, a loud, free sound, and pulled her with him through the portal. She stumbled after him, blushing furiously, but she didn't let go of his hand as the world of fire and iron dissolved into the cool, silent light of Floor 5.
