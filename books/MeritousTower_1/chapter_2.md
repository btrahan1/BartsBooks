# Chapter 2: The Furnace

Valerius watched the Dreg drag himself into the mangroves. He checked the time on his internal System clock.

**[Time since Induction: 00:04:12]**

"Inefficient," Valerius muttered, dismissing the notification of the Dreg's survival. "He traded 60% of his health pool for a single trash-tier kill. He’ll be dead of sepsis or stamina depletion within the hour."

He turned his back on the swamp and clapped his hands sharply. The sound cut through the whimpering of the few "Professional" class kids lingering at the edge of their rock formation.

"Eyes on me!" Valerius commanded. His voice cracked slightly—he was only twelve, after all—but he pitched it lower, mimicking the tone of his House’s Master at Arms.

The seven other Highborn children snapped to attention. They were scared, shaking even, but training took over. They fell into a loose circle.

"We have exactly ten minutes before the System spawns the second wave," Valerius said, pacing the small perimeter of dry stone. "You all memorized the *Codex of Early Ascension*. Stop crying, Cassius. Tears dehydrate you."

He pointed to **Marcus**, a boy who was already pushing six feet tall, built like a brick outhouse. His father was a Vanguard for the Ivory Sect.
"Marcus, front and center. You need to unlock the **Bulwark** class before we leave this rock."

"But I don't have a shield, Val," Marcus stammered.

"You don't need a shield to register the intent," Valerius snapped. He walked over to a jagged rock formation. "Beat your fists against this stone. Do it until your knuckles bleed. The System registers 'Self-Inflicted Conditioning' as a precursor to [Thick Skin]. Go."

Marcus hesitated, then nodded. He began punching the stone. *Thud. Thud.*

Valerius spun to **Elara**, a slight girl with sharp eyes.
"Elara. Marcus is going to take damage. I want you to tear a strip off your tunic. Wrap his hands. Do not use magic—you don't have it yet. Use the cloth. The System rewards 'Medical Triage' with the [Field Medic] skill if performed within the first hour."

"Understood," Elara said, already ripping the hem of her grey shirt.

Valerius looked at the remaining four.
"The rest of you, sit down. Legs crossed. Lotus position. Close your eyes."

"We're sitting?" one boy asked. "There are monsters out there."

"There are *Level 1* monsters out there," Valerius corrected. "We are not hunting rats. We are unlocking our mana pools. The atmospheric mana density on Floor 1 is low, but if you hyperventilate slightly and focus on your sternum, the System will pity-unlock [Mana Sensitivity]."

He looked at them with a critical eye. This was the difference between the Noble Houses and the riffraff. The riffraff reacted. The Nobles prepared.

Valerius walked to the edge of the rock. He needed a weapon. Not a rusted piece of junk, but something that would trigger the **Tactician** or **Fencer** tree.

He found a straight, heavy branch of driftwood. It wasn't perfect, but it was balanced. He held it out, arm extended, perfectly still.

**[SYSTEM QUERY]**
**[User is maintaining a 'Guard Stance' without an enemy present.]**
**[Do you wish to initiate a Katas Routine?]**

"Yes," Valerius whispered.

He began to move. Not the desperate, flailing swings Jace had used. Valerius moved through the *Form of the Falling Leaf*, a standard warm-up routine he had practiced since he was four years old.

*Thrust. Parry. Step. Pivot.*

Blue boxes began to pop up around the group, silent and efficient.

**[CONGRATULATIONS!]**
**[Player 'Marcus' has unlocked skill: Minor Durability.]**
**[Player 'Elara' has unlocked skill: Bandage.]**

Valerius smirked as he finished his form.
**[Skill Unlocked: Basic Swordsmanship.]**
**[Attribute Increased: Precision +1]**

He looked at his team. In five minutes, they had a Tank, a Healer, and a DPS (Damage Per Second) leader. They hadn't killed a single thing, yet they were already statistically superior to everyone else in the swamp.

"Valerius!" Elara called out, tying off the bandage on Marcus's bloody knuckles. "I have the notification. I can see health bars now."

"Good," Valerius said. He pointed toward a cluster of shivering 'Worker' kids huddled in the mud nearby. "Now we farm. Marcus, go over there. Tell them they can stand on our rock if they give us their rations."

"And if they say no?" Marcus asked, flexing his newly toughened hands.

"Then shove them back into the water," Valerius said coldly. "We need the calories. And you need the XP for 'Unarmed Combat.' It’s a win-win."

As Marcus lumbered toward the terrified kids, Valerius looked back toward the dark mangroves where Jace had vanished.

*Survival of the fittest,* Valerius thought. *And we are the fittest because we know the rules.*

The mangroves were a tangled nightmare of roots and rot, but to Jace, they were just another version of the cramped ventilation shafts he’d grown up in. He pressed his back against a slimy trunk, sliding down until he was submerged up to his chest in the dark water, hidden by the shadows.

He held a finger to his lips, signaling the girl to be silent.

His eyes were closed, but his ears were twitching. This was his oldest trick. In the slums, hearing the landlord’s heavy boots or the whistle of a rival gang meant the difference between sleeping indoors or bleeding in a gutter.

Across the water, the voice of the blond boy, Valerius, carried clearly. The mist seemed to amplify it.

*“...System registers 'Self-Inflicted Conditioning'...”*
*“...tear a strip off your tunic...”*
*“...hyperventilate slightly and focus on your sternum...”*

Jace’s eyes snapped open. A slow, predatory grin spread across his face.

**[Skill Unlocked: Eavesdrop (Lvl 1)]**
*Description: Information is currency. You know how to listen to the silence between words.*
*Effect: +10% Hearing Range. Ability to filter background noise.*

"They're cheating," the girl whispered, shivering. She was hugging her knees, her face pale.

"No," Jace whispered back, his voice tight with pain. "They're just reading the manual. And we just stole it."

He looked at his mangled left arm. The blood was dark in the gloom. He needed that bleeding to stop, but he also needed what Valerius had promised: *Skills.*

"Hey," Jace said. "What's your name?"

"B-Bella," she stammered.

"Okay, Bella. Listen to me. Tear a strip off your shirt."

Bella blinked, horrified. "What? Why?"

"Just do it. Like the rich kid said." Jace let go of his arm, wincing as fresh blood welled up. He reached down and ripped the hem of his own tunic with his teeth, tearing off a long, ragged strip of grey hemp. "We’re going to play their game."

He tossed his strip of cloth to her. "Wrap my arm. Tight. Don't just cover it—apply pressure. The System wants to see you *trying* to heal."

"I... I don't know how," Bella said, her hands trembling as she caught the bloody cloth.

"Figure it out," Jace snapped, though not unkindly. "Wrap it. Knot it. If you do it right, you get a skill. You want to survive? Make yourself useful to the System."

Bella nodded, swallowing her fear. She moved closer, wading through the muck. Her hands were soft, unused to labor, but she was careful. She wound the cloth around the puncture wounds from the Silt-Reaver. As she pulled the knot tight, a soft chime rang in the air.

Her eyes went wide. "I... I got it. **[First Aid]**."

"Good," Jace grunted. The pressure hurt, but the bleeding slowed.

Now it was his turn. He looked at Bella. She had a nasty scrape on her cheek where she’d fallen against the rocks earlier. It wasn't life-threatening, but it was an injury.

"Give me the piece you tore off," Jace demanded.

Bella handed him her strip of cloth. Jace reached out. His hands were dirty, but he tried to be precise, mimicking the way the infirmary doctors in the slums used to work. He dabbed the mud away from her cheek, then pressed the cloth pad against it, securing it by tying the ends behind her head like a headband.

He focused on the intent. *I am fixing this. I am stopping the damage.*

**[Skill Unlocked: First Aid (Lvl 1)]**
*Description: The ability to treat minor wounds.*
*Effect: +5% healing speed on bandaged injuries.*

"Yes," Jace hissed. It worked.

He didn't stop there. Valerius had mentioned something else. *Mana.*

Jace leaned back against the tree roots. The pain in his arm was a dull throb now, manageable. He closed his eyes again.

"What are you doing?" Bella whispered, touching the bandage on her face.

"Shut up," Jace murmured. "I'm breathing."

*Hyperventilate slightly. Focus on the sternum.*

Jace began to take short, sharp breaths. In, out. In, out. He focused his mind on the center of his chest. At first, he felt nothing but the damp cold of the swamp and the hunger in his belly.

But he was stubborn. He was a "Dreg." He didn't have a Spirit Root or a Golden Core, but he had the **Null-Iron Body**—though he didn't know the name of his constitution yet, he knew his body felt *heavy*.

He pushed his awareness inward. He imagined a fire in his chest.

*Come on,* he thought. *If the golden boy can do it, I can do it.*

A spark.

It wasn't a roar of power. It felt like swallowing a hot coal. A burning sensation flared right in the center of his ribcage. It hurt, but it was a different kind of pain—it was electric.

**[SYSTEM ALERT]**
**[Mana Sensitivity Unlocked.]**

**[WARNING: ANOMALY DETECTED]**
**[User Constitution: Null-Iron Body]**
**[External Mana Projection: IMPOSSIBLE.]**
**[Internal Mana Cycle: INITIATED.]**

**[New Skill Unlocked: Internal Furnace (Passive)]**
*Description: You cannot cast spells. You cannot shape the world. You can only shape yourself. Mana absorbed is trapped within your flesh.*
*Effect: Mana points are converted directly into Stamina and Health regeneration at a 1:1 ratio. You burn hot.*

Jace gasped, his eyes flying open. Steam curled from his lips. The cold water didn't feel cold anymore. His body was heating up like a boiler.

He looked at his Health bar.

**[HP: 18/40... 19/40... 20/40...]**

It was ticking up. Fast.

Jace looked at his hands. Veins of faint, ember-like orange light traced under his skin for a second before fading.

"Valerius uses magic to hit things," Jace whispered, a terrifying realization dawning on him. "I think... I think I just use magic to *keep going*."

He looked at Bella. "Stay close. We’re not done. I heard him say something about 'Self-Inflicted Conditioning.'"

He looked at the rough bark of the mangrove tree. He raised his uninjured right fist.

"I need a Shield skill," Jace muttered. And he punched the tree.
