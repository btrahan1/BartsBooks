# Chapter 20: The Safe Zone

They tumbled through the portal, landing on cool, polished marble that seemed to drink the light of a thousand softly glowing crystals embedded in the high, vaulted ceiling. The air was clean, still, and silent. Gone was the hammering clang of the foundry, replaced by a profound, cathedral-like peace. They were in a vast, circular nexus, with multiple archways leading off into unknown corridors. This was Floor 5. This was the safe zone.

Jace was still laughing, high on the adrenaline of their impossible victory. "See? What did I tell you? We—"

He was cut off.

A sound, deep and resonant, echoed not from the walls, but inside their very skulls. It was a voice that wasn't a voice, a proclamation that bypassed the ears entirely and was simply *known*. It was the voice of the Spire itself, and for the first time, it wasn't a private notification. It was a broadcast.

**[SYSTEM-WIDE ALERT!]**

The voice boomed with the weight of eternity, a message delivered to every single climber still alive, from the deepest mud of Floor 1 to the scorched iron of Floor 4.

**[MR. AND MRS. DREG ARE THE FIRST TO REACH FLOOR 5!]**

The silence that followed was more profound than any noise.

Jace froze mid-laugh, his triumphant grin slowly melting into a look of slack-jawed disbelief. He stared at nothing, his brain trying to process what had just happened. The Spire... the all-powerful, god-like Architect of their reality... had just officially recognized his sarcastic, throwaway taunt. It had taken his joke and carved it into the history of the climb.

Bella, who had just started to get the color back in her cheeks, went utterly, bone-white pale. Her eyes were wide with a horror that eclipsed any monster she had ever seen. Her mouth opened, but no sound came out. The most embarrassing moment of her life had just been broadcast to the entire world.

A new notification, this one a shimmering, radiant gold, appeared only for them.

**[Achievement Unlocked: The Trailblazers]**
*You are the first Ascendants to reach the first designated Safe Zone.*
*Your [Reputation] has been permanently set and is now known to all survivors of the Induction.*

**[Current Reputation: 15 (Mr. and Mrs. Dreg - Legendary Kill-Stealers / Tactical Geniuses)]**

Jace’s shock finally broke, replaced by a slow, spreading grin that was more terrifying than any of his previous ones. It was a look of pure, unholy delight. He wasn't Jace the Dreg anymore. He was Jace of the infamous "Mr. and Mrs. Dreg." The System didn't just approve of his methods; it was actively making him a legend for them.

Bella finally found her voice. It was a strangled, horrified whisper. "What... did... you... *do*?"

Jace just threw his head back and roared with laughter, the sound echoing through the pristine, silent nexus of Floor 5. They were no longer anonymous survivors. They were celebrities. And every single person below them now knew their name.
