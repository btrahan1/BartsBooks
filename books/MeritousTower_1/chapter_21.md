# Chapter 21: The Catch

Jace finally managed to choke back his laughter, wiping a tear from his eye. He looked at Bella, who was practically vibrating with mortification. Her face was still beet red, but now it was mixed with a growing, horrified realization of what this meant.

"Sorry about that," Jace said, his voice still laced with amusement. He met her mortified gaze, his grin softening just a fraction. He knew he had pushed it, but the sheer absurdity of the situation, the *legend* the System had just created for them, was intoxicating.

"I know we hardly know each other," he continued, echoing her shame, but twisting it into something genuine. He saw her flinch as he spoke, her mind probably racing with a thousand scenarios of how this would make their lives impossible. He held up a hand. "But honestly... I think I'm a pretty good catch!"

He meant it. He saw her analytical mind, her courage, her quick thinking. He saw the person who had faced down monsters and Elites right beside him, and who had the intelligence to save his life and forge a new path. He wasn't just impressed; he was starting to genuinely respect and, yes, *like* her.

Bella just stared at him, speechless. Her mind was a whirlwind of embarrassment, the broadcast humiliation, and the dawning realization that he wasn't just being flippant. He was... acknowledging her. As an equal. As a partner.

Suddenly, a new notification flashed in their shared vision, this one sharp and urgent. It wasn't a legend or a title. It was a threat.

**[SYSTEM ALERT: HOSTILE ENTITIES DETECTED IN THE NEXUS.]**
**[TYPE: DEFILED SOULS (LVL 10 - ELITE)]**
**[NUMBER: 3]**
**[POSSIBLE ORIGIN: RECENTLY DEFEATED HIGHBORN CLANS.]**

Jace's playful demeanor evaporated instantly. His eyes narrowed, and the playful grin was replaced by a predatory glint. "Hey! OUCH!"

He yelped as Bella, her humiliation briefly forgotten in the face of imminent danger, delivered a sharp, stinging slap to his arm. "Not now, Jace! Focus!"

He rubbed his arm, but he understood. The legend of Mr. and Mrs. Dreg had not gone unnoticed. And it seemed some of the Elites they had so thoroughly dismantled hadn't *quite* died. Or perhaps their families were now hunting them.

"Right," Jace said, shaking his head to clear it. He looked around the vast nexus. "Three of them. Elite. That means they're not just brute strength. They're smart."

Bella was already scanning. "Two are heading towards the western arch. One is coming this way, towards the northern one... they're trying to flank us. They know we're here."

Jace’s mind was already working, piecing together his new skills with Bella’s tactical genius. He had the strength and the ability to take hits. Bella had the intelligence and the precision. And now, thanks to the Architect’s warped sense of reward, they had each other.

"Okay, Analyst," Jace said, his voice low and serious, his hand instinctively going to his rebar. "Let's show them what Mr. and Mrs. Dreg can *really* do."

The playful moment was gone, replaced by the ice-cold focus of a predator. Jace closed his eyes for a fraction of a second, not in fear, but to access the one thing that truly mattered. His status. He quickly reviewed the rewards, the stats, the arsenal of skills he had earned through blood, pain, and sheer, stubborn grit.

Impressive.

**[Name: Jace]**
**[Title: Mr. Dreg (Unofficial)]**
**[Level: 9]**
**[Reputation: 15 (Legendary Kill-Stealers)]**

**[Stats]**
*   **Strength: 18**
*   **Agility: 16**
*   **Constitution: 25**
*   **Intelligence: 10**
*   **Willpower: 19**

**[Notable Skills]**
*   **[Goad (Lvl 1)]:** Weaponize annoyance.
*   **[Gutter-Fighting (Lvl 2)]:** +15% Damage with Improvised Weapons.
*   **[Sanguine Offering (Lvl 1)]:** Convert HP to heal an ally.
*   **[Internal Furnace (Passive)]:** Convert Mana to Stamina/HP regen.
*   **[Toxin Conversion (Passive)]:** Convert Poison Damage to Stamina.

This wasn't the stat block of a scrawny street rat anymore. This was the stat block of a monster. A tank. His Constitution was absurdly high, a fortress built from absorbing a Gatekeeper's very essence. His Willpower was nearly unshakable. And his Reputation... that was the wildcard.

It was time to take it up a notch.

He opened his eyes, the faint orange glow of his internal furnace now a steady, confident light. "Bella. That single one coming north. I'm going to pull it. I'm going to make it fight me right here, in the open."

"But the other two will collapse on us," Bella warned, already positioning herself near a large marble pillar.

"That's the point," Jace said, a grim smile touching his lips. "I'm not going to be dodging. I'm going to be an anchor. You are the damage. You watch the other two. Use your [Riposte Barrier] to keep them off me. When I give the signal, you focus fire on my target. We burn them down one by one. Fast."

This was a new strategy. Not a trap, not a trick. A coordinated, aggressive assault. He was confident enough in his own durability to become the anvil, so she could be the hammer.

Bella nodded, understanding immediately.

A shimmering, translucent figure drifted into the nexus from the northern archway. It looked like one of the Elites, its face a silent scream of spectral energy. It carried a ghostly echo of a sword, and its movements were unnaturally smooth and fast. A Defiled Soul.

Jace stepped forward, deliberately making himself the only target.

"[Goad]!" he mentally commanded.

The Defiled Soul's head snapped toward him, its ethereal eyes flaring with a sudden, overwhelming hatred. It let out a psychic shriek and shot toward him, a phantom blade aimed at his heart.

Jace didn't move to dodge. He braced himself, lowering his center of gravity. Just before the Soul reached him, it seemed to hesitate for a split second. Its hateful gaze flickered, not with confusion, but with a flicker of recognition. It *knew* him. The legend, "Mr. Dreg," was a tangible weight in the air.

Jace exploited that flicker of hesitation without mercy.

He lunged forward, not away, and swung his rebar in a brutal, horizontal arc aimed not at the phantom sword, but at the creature's legs.

**[Cheap Shot Activated!]**

The blow connected with a sound like shattering glass. The Defiled Soul was thrown off balance, its attack pattern broken. Jace didn't let up. He followed through with a vicious, upward thrust, aiming for the center of its chest.

At the same moment, the other two Souls appeared from the western arch, raising their spectral weapons to fire bolts of necrotic energy at Jace's exposed back.

"Bella!"

Two perfectly formed, concave shields of silver light snapped into existence behind him, intercepting the bolts. The necrotic energy hit the barriers and was instantly reflected, a portion of its power shooting back at the attackers, forcing them to scatter.

The first Soul, now fully recovered and enraged, swung its blade wildly at Jace. He took the hit on his shoulder. It felt like ice and fire at the same time, but his rat-hide armor and sheer Constitution held. The damage was minimal.

He grinned, his eyes burning.

"NOW!" he roared.

Bella, her duty of defending his back done for the moment, whipped her sling. A single, sharp stone, imbued with her will and intelligence, shot across the nexus. It struck the Defiled Soul Jace was fighting directly in its screaming face.

The combined assault was too much. The creature destabilized, flickering violently before dissolving into wisps of malevolent smoke.

One down.

Jace spun to face the remaining two, his rebar held at the ready. He felt no fear. Only a cold, thrilling certainty.

"Alright," he said, his voice low and dangerous, a promise and a threat. "Who's next?"
