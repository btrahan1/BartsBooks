# Chapter 22: The Retreat

Jace stood ready, a solid, unmovable anchor in the center of the pristine nexus, his rebar humming with the kinetic energy of the last blow. He braced for the charge, for the coordinated pincer attack from the remaining two Defiled Souls.

But it never came.

The two ethereal figures, who had been poised to strike from the western archway, simply… stopped. Their spectral forms wavered, not with instability, but with something Jace had never seen in a monster before: hesitation.

They looked at their fallen comrade, a dissipating wisp of angry smoke. They looked at Bella, a small, unassuming figure who had just demonstrated the ability to reflect their most powerful attacks with contemptuous ease. Then, they looked at Jace.

They saw the glowing eyes of the Furnace. They saw the blood- and grime-stained Dreg who had dismantled the best-equipped party in the Induction with nothing but scrap iron and a plan. And in the very fabric of the Spire's reality, they felt the crushing, intangible weight of his [Reputation]. They weren't just fighting a climber. They were fighting a legend. A monster in his own right.

And they made a calculated decision.

Without a sound, the two Defiled Souls simultaneously turned and drifted back into the shadows of the western archway, their forms fading into the gloom until they were gone. They didn't die. They just left.

Jace stood there, his rebar half-raised, utterly baffled. The battle-high adrenaline coursing through his veins had nowhere to go.

"They ran?" he said aloud, the words echoing in the sudden, complete silence of the nexus. He lowered his weapon, a look of profound confusion on his face. He took a step toward the archway, peering into the darkness. "They can do that?"

Bella walked up beside him, her own expression a mixture of relief and astonishment. She deactivated her [Identify] skill, the silver glow in her eyes fading.

"I... I think so," she said, her voice full of wonder. "My skill… it didn't just show me their stats. It showed a descriptor. 'Affiliation: Valerius Argent.' They were the spirits of the Elites we beat in the Foundry. They weren't mindless monsters, Jace. They were… vengeful. But not suicidal."

Jace stared into the empty corridor, the pieces clicking into place in his mind. The hesitation. The recognition. It wasn't just his Goad or his strength that had won the fight. It was the story. The broadcast.

"Mr. and Mrs. Dreg," he whispered, a slow, dawning realization spreading across his face.

He had become the monster that other monsters were afraid of.

"Huh," Jace said, turning away from the archway. He looked at Bella, a strange, thoughtful expression replacing his confusion. "So, the Spire isn't just about fighting harder. It's about fighting smarter... until you don't have to fight at all."

He finally allowed himself a small, weary grin. The adrenaline was fading, leaving a deep-seated exhaustion in its wake. "Well, that was anticlimactic. Now, where's that store?"
