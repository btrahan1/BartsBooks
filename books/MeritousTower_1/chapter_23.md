# Chapter 23: The System Store

The exhaustion hit Jace like a physical blow. Now that the adrenaline was gone, a deep, gnawing hunger clawed at his stomach, a beast far more persistent than any Defiled Soul.

"The Store," he said, the words a gravelly rasp. "That message said 'maybe'. How do we find 'maybe'?"

"Look for the thing that doesn't belong," Bella answered instantly, her Analyst's mind already working. She scanned the vast, symmetrical nexus. Her eyes passed over the glowing crystals, the identical archways, and then stopped. In the exact center of the marble floor, there was a single, waist-high pedestal of flawless, non-reflective black stone. It was the only object in the entire chamber that didn't glow.

They approached it cautiously. As Jace got within five feet, the top of the pedestal shimmered. A soft, blue light projected upwards, forming a holographic screen.

**[SYSTEM STORE: WELCOME, TRAILBLAZERS.]**
**[Please present Beast Cores for Merit Point conversion.]**

Jace didn't hesitate. He and Bella unslung their scavenged haversacks and dumped their gruesome treasury onto the floor at the base of the pedestal. It was a motley pile: dozens of dull grey rat cores, a handful of larger Chitter-Kin cores, the still-pulsing green heart of the Bile-Croaker, the shattered lantern of the Amalgam, and the heavy, metallic core of the Forgemaster Automaton.

A beam of light scanned the pile. One by one, the cores dissolved into particles of light that flowed into the pedestal.

**[CONVERSION COMPLETE.]**
**[TOTAL MERIT: 1,124 MP]**

Jace let out a low whistle. A thousand points. It felt like a king's fortune.

The holographic screen changed, displaying a list of categories.

*   `Weapons`
*   `Armor`
*   `Consumables`
*   `Utility`
*   `Skill Tomes`

"Dang, I'm hungry," Jace muttered, and tapped `Consumables`.

The list that appeared made his mouth water. Iron Rations. Purified Water Canteen. MREs (Meal, Ready-to-Eat).

**[Item: Hearth-Baked Bread (Loaf)]**
**[Cost: 2 MP]**

**[Item: Roasted Boar Shank]**
**[Cost: 5 MP]**

"I'll take ten of each," Jace said without a second's thought.

**[10x Hearth-Baked Bread, 10x Roasted Boar Shank purchased. -70 MP]**

Two stacks of items materialized on the floor beside the pedestal, glowing softly before becoming solid. The smell of warm bread and savory, roasted meat filled the air. Jace grabbed a loaf, tore it in half, and handed a piece to Bella. He then seized a boar shank and took a massive bite.

The taste was overwhelming. It wasn't just food; it was warmth, safety, and civilization. It was the furthest thing from the greasy rat meat he'd choked down by a sputtering fire. He ate with a ferocity that bordered on violence, the hunger of a lifetime finally being sated.

After they had both eaten their fill, feeling the [Well-Fed] buff refresh with a pleasant warmth, Jace turned back to the store, his eyes gleaming.

"Time for an upgrade." He tapped `Weapons`.

He scrolled past the swords and daggers. He needed something with weight. Something that could shatter bone and break shields. And then he saw it.

**[Item: Foundry-Forged Quake Mace (Uncommon)]**
*Description: A brutal, heavy-headed mace forged in the heart of a chaotic foundry. Each strike feels like an impact from a factory hammer.*
*Effect: +25 Physical Damage. +10% Stun chance against armored enemies.*
*Requirement: 15 Strength.*
**[Cost: 350 MP]**

"Mine," Jace breathed, tapping the purchase button.

A heavy, black iron mace materialized. The head was a solid block of pitted steel, the handle wrapped in thick leather. He gripped it. The weight was perfect. His rebar was a toy compared to this.

Bella, meanwhile, was browsing `Armor`. She found what she was looking for under the 'Cloth' sub-category.

**[Item: Analyst's Cowl (Uncommon)]**
*Description: A deep-hooded robe of woven mana-fiber. The intricate patterns help focus the wearer's mind, sharpening their perception.*
*Effect: +5 Defense. +2 Intelligence. +1 Willpower.*
**[Cost: 300 MP]**

She bought it, and a simple but sturdy set of dark blue robes appeared. When she put them on, pulling the deep hood over her head, she looked less like a lost girl and more like a mysterious scholar.

They pooled their remaining funds for two sets of basic, essential protection.

**[Item: Hardened Leather Cuirass (Common)]**
**[Cost: 150 MP x2 = 300 MP]**

They equipped the new armor, strapping the tough, boiled leather chests over their tunics. It was a world away from the patchy, smelly rat hides.

Finally, they looked at their remaining balance: `104 MP`. They had gone from kings to paupers in under five minutes. But it was worth it.

Jace stood, his new mace resting on his shoulder. Bella adjusted her hood, her eyes sharp and focused in the shadows. They were no longer the ragged, desperate survivors who had stumbled out of the Silt. Clad in leather and armed with real, purpose-built gear, they looked like what the Spire intended them to be.

They looked like Ascendants.
