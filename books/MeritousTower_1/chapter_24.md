# Chapter 24: The Verdant Atrium

They stepped through the northern archway, leaving the sterile marble of the Nexus behind. The air instantly became warm, humid, and alive with the scent of rich soil and blooming flora. They had entered the Verdant Atrium, a massive, multi-tiered biodome lit by softly glowing moss and strange, bioluminescent flowers. A gentle waterfall cascaded down a wall of green stone into a crystal-clear pond. It was beautiful, and after the grim realities of the lower floors, it was deeply unsettling.

Bella, her new cowl pulled low, was scanning the area with her [Tactical Survey] when she saw a flicker of movement near the base of a giant, pulsating mushroom. It wasn't the menacing red of a hostile or the tactical yellow of an object. It was a soft, neutral white.

She lowered her hood, her curiosity piqued. She crept closer and peered behind the mushroom stalk. Curled up there was a small creature, no bigger than her two fists put together. It had the body of a rodent, but its fur was the color of polished obsidian and shot through with veins of what looked like shimmering crystal. It had large, amethyst-purple eyes and was currently nibbling on a shard of glowing moss.

A genuine, unforced smile bloomed on Bella's face. All the tension, all the fear, seemed to melt away for a single, precious moment.

"It's so cute!" she whispered, her voice filled with a wonder she hadn't felt since before the Induction. She knelt down, her movements slow and non-threatening. "What are you doing here, little guy?"

The creature paused its nibbling, its crystal-flecked ears twitching. It looked at her, its purple eyes wide with a cautious curiosity.

Jace came up behind her, his new mace held loosely in his hand. His first instinct was threat assessment. *Is it edible? Is it poisonous? Does it have valuable parts?* But his [Identify] skill wasn't Analyst-level; he got no data. It was just... a thing. A seemingly useless, harmless thing.

Then he saw the look on Bella's face. He saw the bond she was trying to form. And his mind, a machine now hardwired to see systems and opportunities, saw a different angle. This wasn't a monster. It wasn't a resource. It was an *asset* waiting to be acquired.

He knelt beside her, his voice a low, strategic command.

"Feed it," he said.

Bella looked at him, startled. "What?"

"Feed it," he repeated, his eyes locked on the creature. "It's a neutral mob. Its guard is down. Establish trust."

Bella hesitated, then understood. This wasn't about being kind; it was about being smart. She rummaged in her new haversack and broke off a small, thumbnail-sized piece of the hearth-baked bread. She offered it on her open palm.

The crystal-furred creature sniffed the air, its nose twitching. It crept forward, snatched the bread with its tiny paws, and retreated to its spot to nibble on the unexpected treat.

"Talk to it," Jace instructed, his voice never rising above a whisper. "Use a low tone. Let it get used to your voice. Associate you with food and safety."

Bella felt a strange mix of annoyance at his cold calculus and admiration for his cunning. She began to murmur softly to the creature, talking about the pretty lights and the warm air.

"Pet it," Jace said, his final command. "Slowly. Back of the hand first. Let it scent you."

Bella reached out a trembling hand. The creature watched her approach, then seemed to decide she was not a threat. It nudged her knuckles with its wet nose, then allowed her to stroke its surprisingly soft, crystalline fur. It let out a soft, purring vibration.

As her fingers made contact, a System notification shimmered into life before them.

**[SYSTEM ALERT: You are attempting to form a bond with a Neutral Beast (Glimmer Vole).]**
**[Taming Process Initiated. Affinity: 15/100]**
**[To increase Affinity, provide food, grooming, and protection.]**

Jace let out a low, satisfied grunt. His theory was correct. "Own it."

Bella looked from the purring Glimmer Vole in her lap to Jace's intense, focused expression. He hadn't seen a cute animal. He had seen a new game mechanic, a new path to power. And as much as she hated the cold logic of it, she knew he was right. This little creature wasn't just a pet. In the brutal world of the Spire, it was a new weapon in their arsenal.
