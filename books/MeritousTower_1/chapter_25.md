# Chapter 25: Naming JJ

Bella gently stroked the Glimmer Vole’s back, the crystalline fur humming with a soft, contented vibration under her fingers. The little creature, now full of bread and feeling safe, was curled in her lap, its large purple eyes blinking slowly.

"It needs a name," Bella murmured, a genuine warmth in her voice. She was completely captivated. "Hmmm... Cuddles... Blackie... Sparkly?"

"No," Jace’s voice cut through her reverie, sharp and incisive. He was crouched nearby, his expression uncharacteristically serious as he studied the interaction. "Names surely mean something here. The System recognizes intent. A name is the ultimate intent. It’s a definition."

He met her gaze, his own eyes burning with a familiar intensity. "Yes... it's cute. But we don't need cute. We need power. We need an edge. Every single thing we do, every choice we make, has to make us stronger."

Jace looked past Bella, his focus locking onto the Glimmer Vole. He stared deep into its amethyst eyes, seeing not a pet, but a tool. A potential.

"You will be..." Jace began, his mind already assigning a title of function and ferocity. *Breaker. Shatter. Fury.*

"I know," Bella said, her voice soft but firm, cutting him off.

She wasn't looking at Jace. She had locked eyes with the vole, a perfect, silent understanding passing between them. Jace’s logic was sound, but it was incomplete. He saw the need for power, but she saw the source of it. The creature wasn't a weapon to be wielded. It was an ally to be nurtured. Its strength wouldn't come from a fearsome name, but from the bond they were forging. The name had to reflect that bond.

She held the Glimmer Vole up, bringing it to eye level.

"Your name is Jace Junior"

There was a moment of absolute, profound silence in the Verdant Atrium, broken only by the gentle splash of the waterfall.

Jace stared, his mouth hanging slightly open. All his carefully constructed theories about intent, power, and the symbolic weight of names shattered into a million confused pieces. Of all the possible outcomes, this was one his strategic mind had utterly failed to predict.

He pointed a trembling finger at the tiny, purring creature. "You named it... after *me*?"

Bella looked up at him, and for the first time, the shy, devious smile she'd worn after her first Riposte Barrier was back, but this time it was aimed squarely at him. It was a weapon of pure, unadulterated teasing.

"Well," she said, her voice laced with false innocence as she tickled the vole under its chin. "He's small, surprisingly tough, eats anything you put in front of him, and I'm pretty sure he's going to spend most of his time getting us into trouble."

The Glimmer Vole—Jace Junior—let out a happy squeak of agreement.

A golden notification flared to life.

**[Bond Finalized. You have named the Glimmer Vole: Jace Junior.]**
**[The name is... unusual. The Architect is amused.]**
**[Reputation +1 (For exhibiting a sense of humor in the face of grim reality)]**
**[New Pet Skill Unlocked: [Loyal Squeak] - Jace Junior can now alert you to unseen threats.]**

Jace was speechless. He had been outmaneuvered, outsmarted, and was now the namesake for a sparkling rat. He opened his mouth, then closed it again, utterly defeated.

"Okay," he finally managed to say, a slow, grudging smile spreading across his face. "Okay, Analyst. You win this round."
