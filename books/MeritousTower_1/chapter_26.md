# Chapter 26: Training JJ

Jace paced a small, agitated circle near the crystal-clear pond, his new mace resting on his shoulder. He kept glancing over at Bella, who was sitting contentedly under a giant mushroom, teaching Jace Junior how to perch on her shoulder. The tiny Glimmer Vole seemed to be enjoying the attention, occasionally letting out a happy squeak.

Jace, however, was not content. He was muttering under his breath, a low, continuous stream of words.

"Jace Junior the Terrible," he mumbled, testing the sound of it. "Jace Junior the Fierce... The Destroyer... Jace Junior, Eater of Worlds..."

He was trying to retroactively apply the gravitas he felt the name so desperately needed. If the System recognized intent, maybe he could... amend the record. Add a subtitle. Give the sparkling rat a title worthy of its association with *him*.

"Jace Junior the Quake-Footed... The Adamantium-Clawed..."

"What are you mumbling over there?" Bella's voice, laced with amusement, cut through his monologue. She had been watching him pace for the last five minutes, a small, knowing smile on her face.

Jace stopped pacing and straightened up, trying to look nonchalant. "Nothing. Just... working out a battle strategy."

Bella raised a skeptical eyebrow. Jace Junior, perched on her shoulder, mimicked the expression, tilting its tiny head. "Really? Because it sounded an awful lot like you were trying to give my pet a scary nickname."

Jace scowled, his pride stung. "It's not a 'pet'," he corrected her gruffly. "It's a 'tactical beast companion'. And it has my name. It needs to have a reputation. What are the other climbers going to say when they hear we were saved by something named 'Jace Junior'?"

"They're going to say we were smart enough to tame a creature that could help us," Bella countered, her voice calm and reasonable. "And they're going to be jealous." She gently stroked the vole's back. "Besides, I think the name is perfect. It'll keep our enemies guessing."

Jace opened his mouth to argue, but he couldn't find a flaw in her logic. He sighed, the sound a mix of frustration and grudging acceptance. He walked over and sat down beside her, admitting defeat.

"Fine," he grumbled. "Jace Junior it is."

He leaned in, looking the Glimmer Vole directly in its big purple eyes.

"But you'd better live up to it, you hear me?" he said, his voice a low, threatening whisper. "You represent the family name now."

Jace Junior just blinked, then let out a defiant, high-pitched squeak, as if to say, "You got it, boss."

Jace couldn't help it. A small, genuine chuckle escaped him. The situation was ridiculous. He, the fearsome 'Mr. Dreg', the bane of the Elites, was now co-parenting a crystal rat named after himself. He shook his head, a real, honest-to-goodness smile finally breaking through his scowl.

"Okay," he said, looking at Bella, at the vole, at this strange little family they had become. "Okay. This is fine."

The grudging smile on Jace’s face quickly faded, replaced by his default expression: tactical analysis. He leaned forward, inspecting the Glimmer Vole as if it were a new piece of gear from the System Store.

"Well," he said, the pragmatist in him taking over. "At least we need to get him leveled up. A Level 1 anything is just a liability. I wonder what skills it has... does it have any built in?" He looked at Bella expectantly. "Does it understand intent? I mean, other than squeaking?"

Bella understood immediately. She gently lifted Jace Junior, holding him in her cupped hands. She focused, channeling a small thread of mana into her eyes. "Let's see what the System says."

**[Identify: Active]**

A detailed screen of text, far more complex than the one for the Chitter-Kin hides, materialized in her vision.

**[Name: Jace Junior]**
**[Type: Familiar (Neutral Beast)]**
**[Level: 1 (0/100 XP)]**
**[Bond: Bella (100%)]**
**[Stats]**
*   **HP: 20/20**
*   **Stamina: 50/50**
*   **Armor: 5 (Crystalline Fur)]**

**[Skills]**
*   **[Loyal Squeak (Passive)]:** Alerts bonded user to the presence of hostile or hidden entities within a 15-foot radius.
*   **[Geode Sense (Passive)]:** Can sense the location of hidden mineral deposits, gems, or crystalline magical signatures through walls or underground.
*   **[Crystalline Flash (Active)]:** Consumes a significant amount of stamina to vibrate its crystalline fur at a high frequency, creating a blinding flash of multi-colored light. All hostiles within a 10-foot radius are afflicted with [Disorient] for 3 seconds. Cooldown: 5 minutes.

Bella's eyes went wide as she read the list. "Jace," she breathed, her voice filled with excitement. "He's not a pet. He's a utility tool. A scout. He can find hidden treasure for us... and he has a flashbang."

Jace's eyebrows shot up. A flashbang. That was a game-changer. A three-second window of disorientation was an eternity in a fight. "Okay. That's... really good. Can you make him do it? The flash thing?"

Bella looked down at the vole. "I don't know. Let's try." She focused on the little creature. "Okay, Jace Junior. Do the... flash thing. Crystalline Flash. Now."

Jace Junior just blinked at her, then nuzzled her thumb. Nothing happened.

"It doesn't understand words," Jace grunted, a hint of frustration in his voice. "It has to be intent. Like with your barrier. You didn't just say 'shield', you *felt* the need for a shield."

Bella closed her eyes, thinking. How do you project the need for a flashbang? She couldn't just think "danger," because that might make him squeak or run. She needed to convey the specific tactical need for a disorienting burst of light.

She focused. She pictured the fight with the Mausoleum Amalgam. She pictured the moment the two other Defiled Souls were about to attack Jace's back. She remembered the feeling of desperation, the need to create an opening, to buy a single, precious second. She didn't just think the thought; she *felt* the memory, the adrenaline, the tactical urgency. And through her bond, she pushed that feeling, that complex packet of information, toward the Glimmer Vole in her hands.

Jace Junior stiffened. Every crystal in its fur began to vibrate, emitting a low, rising hum.

"It's working!" Jace warned, taking a step back and shielding his eyes.

With a silent, concussive burst, Jace Junior's body erupted in a blinding, kaleidoscopic flash of purple, silver, and gold light. It was so intense it left phantom hexagons burned into Jace's vision even through his arm. The air shimmered for a moment, and then it was over.

Jace Junior looked exhausted, slumping in Bella's hands and letting out a tired little squeak. His stamina bar, which Bella could now perceive through their bond, was almost completely empty.

Jace lowered his arm, blinking spots from his eyes. A slow, deeply impressed grin spread across his face.

"Okay," he said, the last of his skepticism gone. "So I tell you what we need. And you *show* him what we need." He looked at the tiny, panting vole. "Alright, Jace Junior the Fierce. Welcome to the team."

Jace found a thick, moss-covered log and propped it up against a stone, draping one of their old, tattered rat-hide vests over it to create a crude training dummy. He then turned to the Glimmer Vole, who was perched expectantly on Bella's shoulder.

"Okay, JJ," Jace began, the new nickname feeling surprisingly natural on his tongue. He could live with JJ. It was efficient. "This is what you are going to do. You are going to mirror me, you see? What I do... you do."

He crouched down to be at the vole's eye level. "But you stay on the back side, and strike from behind. Be nimble, be quick." He looked over at Bella. "You're on translation duty. Make him *feel* it. The intent to distract, to harry, to be the annoyance they can't ignore."

Bella nodded, placing a hand on JJ's back and closing her eyes, ready to act as a psychic conduit for Jace's lesson.

Jace took a deep breath and then exploded into motion. He didn't charge the dummy head-on. He feinted left, then darted right, a blur of motion. He circled around the log in a heartbeat, silent and low to the ground. From the dummy's "blind spot," he drove the heavy pommel of his Quake Mace into the rat-hide vest with a solid *thump*. The entire maneuver took less than three seconds.

"See?" he said, stepping back. "In. Strike. Out. Now you."

Bella focused, projecting the entire sequence—the feint, the circling, the concept of the blind spot, the sharp, surprising strike—into JJ's mind.

JJ hopped off her shoulder. He looked at the dummy, his crystalline ears twitching. He took a hesitant step, then seemed to understand. He didn't run in a wide circle like Jace; he was too small for that. Instead, he darted straight under the log, a flicker of shadow, and reappeared behind it. He then stood on his hind legs and gave the dangling rat-hide a tentative nip with his sharp little teeth.

A notification appeared, visible to all three of them.

**[Jace Junior has performed a Coordinated Flank Maneuver.]**
**[New Skill Unlocked: Harrying Bite (Lvl 1)]**
*Description: A quick, distracting bite aimed at an enemy's blind spot. Deals minor damage but has a high chance of drawing the enemy's attention.*
**[XP Gained: 5]**

"Sloppy," Jace critiqued immediately, though his eyes held a flicker of approval. "Too slow on the entry, and the bite was weak. The goal isn't just to bite him, JJ. It's to make him *turn around*. To make him forget about the big guy with the mace who's about to crush his skull. It needs to be sharp. Annoying."

Bella translated the refined intent: not just an attack, but a *provocation*.

"Again," Jace commanded.

This time, JJ didn't hesitate. As Jace feinted left, JJ was already a flicker of shadow moving right. He zipped behind the dummy, his movements silent and precise. Instead of a tentative nip, he leaped and delivered a sharp, vicious bite to the very bottom edge of the vest—the equivalent of a human's heel—before instantly darting back into the shadows.

**[Harrying Bite: CRITICAL SUCCESS!]**
**[XP Gained: 15]**

Jace watched the entire sequence, a slow, deeply satisfied smirk spreading across his face. The little creature was a natural. It understood the core tenets of Gutter-Fighting instinctively.

"Now that's what I'm talking about," he said, giving Bella an appreciative nod. "The Dreg family has a new rogue."

Bella couldn't help but smile, feeling a swell of pride. She was also a little horrified. Jace was successfully turning her cute, sparkling pet into a tiny, backstabbing assassin. And it was working perfectly.

The opportunity came as they navigated a narrow path between a cluster of giant, pulsating puffball mushrooms. Two creatures, looking like a cross between a boar and a porcupine, were rooting in the soft soil ahead. They were Thorn-Backed Grovellers, their hides a solid mat of thick quills.

"Okay... 2 targets. They could handle this," Jace whispered, pulling back behind a mushroom cap. He looked at Bella, his eyes sharp with tactical focus. "JJ takes the one on the right. I'll take the left. Remember, Bella, when they turn on JJ, shields up!"

Bella nodded, her hand already resting on JJ's back, who was perched on her shoulder, his crystal-flecked ears twitching in anticipation.

Jace didn't wait. He burst from cover, his mace held low, and let out a guttural roar. "HEY!"

The Groveller on the left looked up, startled, and charged with a low, rumbling snort. Jace met it head-on. He didn't try to block; he sidestepped the initial charge and brought the Quake Mace down in a brutal, crushing arc onto the creature's flank.

**BOOM!**

The impact sounded like a hammer hitting an anvil. The Groveller squealed, stumbling sideways, a visible stun effect shimmering around its head for a split second.

At the exact same moment, the second Groveller turned to charge Jace as well. But before it could take two steps, Bella pushed her intent to JJ. *Distract. Harry. Annoy.*

JJ was a flicker of black and crystal. He shot from Bella's shoulder, a silent streak across the mossy ground. He zipped under the Groveller's belly and, just as he'd been taught, delivered a sharp, vicious [Harrying Bite] to its back left heel.

The Groveller let out a roar of pure, infuriated surprise. It forgot all about the big, noisy threat with the hammer. Its entire world narrowed to the tiny, sparkling pest that had just bitten it. It spun around, its back quills flaring, and lowered its head to gore the tiny vole.

"Shields up!" Jace yelled, already moving to press his advantage on the stunned creature.

The Groveller lunged at JJ. But a foot in front of the vole, a shimmering, concave hexagon of silver light snapped into existence.

**[Riposte Barrier: Active!]**

The Groveller slammed headfirst into the unyielding wall of force. The impact sent a shockwave through its body, stunning it for a critical moment. It was completely exposed, its attention locked on an enemy it couldn't touch.

That was all the opening Jace needed. Having dispatched his first target with another crushing blow to the skull, he was already in motion. He crossed the few feet between them in two long strides.

"Thanks for the assist, JJ," he grunted.

He brought the Quake Mace down directly onto the back of the second Groveller's neck. The impact was sickeningly final. The creature collapsed without another sound.

The entire fight was over in less than ten seconds.

Jace stood over the two bodies, breathing a little heavily but completely unharmed. Bella walked over, scooping up a triumphant-looking JJ and placing him back on her shoulder.

"The plan worked," she said, a note of wonder in her voice at their own brutal efficiency.

Jace smirked, resting his mace on his shoulder. "Told you he'd live up to the name."
