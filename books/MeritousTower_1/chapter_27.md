# Chapter 27: The Sentinel

They dragged the Groveller carcasses off the path, stripping them of their quills and tough hides—more materials for Jace's jury-rigging. They found a defensible spot in a small grotto behind the waterfall, the sound of the cascading water providing a curtain of privacy and masking any noise they might make.

While Bella fed JJ a piece of roasted boar as a reward, Jace sat on a smooth, damp stone, his new mace resting against his leg. He closed his eyes, focusing his intent inward. He needed a full picture, a complete assessment of their progress. He called up the Party Status screen.

The familiar blue text materialized in his mind's eye, neat and orderly. Jace was reviewing the party stats...

**[Reputation: 17 (Mr. and Mrs. Dreg - Legendary Kill-Stealers / Tactical Geniuses)]**

Jace opened his eyes, a slow, satisfied exhale escaping his lips. He processed the data. He was a walking fortress, a wall of meat with a hammer attached. His Constitution was his most monstrous stat, a testament to the risks he’d taken. Bella was a glass cannon of pure intellect, a razor-sharp mind that could see and exploit the world's hidden rules. And JJ... JJ was their rogue, their wildcard, a tiny engine of chaos and opportunity.

The synergy was undeniable. They weren't just surviving anymore. They were thriving.

"Not bad," he murmured to himself, the words a profound understatement.

He looked at his own level again. Level 10. The number the System had mentioned. The number that came with a choice.

He looked over at Bella, who was laughing as JJ tried to climb the folds of her new cowl. For the first time since they had arrived in this violent, meritocratic hell, a path forward that didn't just involve the next fight seemed possible. And that was a far more terrifying thought than any monster.

Jace stared at the number on his status screen. **Level: 10**.

He thought back to the System's cryptic message in the Nexus. *At Level 10, you are granted the option to leave the Spire.* The words echoed in the quiet grotto, a strange, unsettling counterpoint to the gentle splash of the waterfall. Leave? What would that even look like? Go back to being a nameless, starving Dreg in the Rust Bottom?

Bella, watching him from her seat, saw the distant, conflicted look in his eyes. She knew exactly what he was thinking.

"I don't think that's right, Jace," she said softly, her Analyst's mind always working to find the most logical interpretation. "I think it means Floor 10 of the tower, not individual level. Reaching the tenth floor seems like a much bigger milestone for an offer like that."

She paused, then added with a small, knowing smile, "Besides... you weren't thinking about leaving, anyway."

Jace's head snapped up. He opened his mouth to argue, to deny it, to say *of course* he wanted to leave this death trap. But the words died on his lips because, as usual, she was right. For the first time in his life, he wasn't just surviving; he was winning. He had a purpose. He had a partner. Why would he leave?

Before he could respond, a sound like a distant, golden clarion call chimed in the air around them. A new notification, more radiant and imposing than any they had seen before, blazed into existence in their shared vision.

**[SYSTEM-WIDE ANNOUNCEMENT!]**

**[CONGRATULATIONS TO MR. DREG! HE IS THE FIRST ASCENDANT TO REACH LEVEL 10!]**

**[ADVANCED CLASS SYSTEM UNLOCKED!]**

The world seemed to hold its breath. Jace and Bella stared at the golden text, their argument forgotten, their minds reeling.

Bella was the first to react, a sharp gasp escaping her lips. "I was... I was wrong." Her logical deduction had been completely overturned. It *was* individual level. And Jace was the first.

Jace, however, was fixated on the last three words. *Advanced Class System.* It was a concept so far beyond their current understanding that it felt like a gateway to a whole new reality of power. The Spire wasn't just about getting stronger stats anymore. It was about *evolving*.

A new, personal notification immediately followed the announcement, this one for Jace alone.

**[As the first to reach this milestone, you are granted a choice.]**
**[Your actions have defined your path. Your base class, 'Survivor', is ready to specialize.]**
**[Please select your Advanced Class:]**

*   **[JUGGERNAUT]**
*   **[BERSERKER]**
*   **[SENTINEL]**

Jace stared at the three glowing options, his heart hammering in his chest. This was a choice that would define the rest of his climb. This was the true reward for reaching Level 10. Not an exit. A promotion.

Jace stared at the three glowing words, each one a potential future, a different path of violence and survival. Juggernaut. Berserker. Sentinel. The weight of the choice pressed down on him, heavier than any golem's shield. This was it. This was the moment he would define himself.

Before he could even begin to analyze the options, the grotto was once again filled with the brilliant, world-altering light of a golden notification. It was another system-wide broadcast, a clarification and a coronation all in one.

**[Attention. As a reward for reaching level 10, Mr Dreg has received the title "Forerunner!"]**

**[Also, to clear up any confusion, the first opportunity to leave the tower is at FLOOR level 10... after you defeat the Boss, of course.]**

The light faded, leaving the new information hanging in the air between them.

Jace blinked. "Forerunner." The word felt heavy, solid. It wasn't a joke like "Mr. Dreg." It was a formal, undeniable acknowledgment from the Spire itself. It was a crown. He was the one leading the way, the first to breach this new frontier of power. A surge of fierce, undeniable pride swelled in his chest.

He then processed the second part of the message and glanced at Bella.

She was looking at him, a single, perfectly smug eyebrow raised. The corner of her mouth was turned up in a triumphant little smirk. She didn't need to say a word. Her expression screamed, "I told you so."

"See?" she finally said, unable to resist. Her voice was calm, but laced with the satisfaction of a proven theory. "Floor 10. My logic was sound. The System just needed a minute to catch up."

Jace let out a short, sharp laugh, the tension of the last few minutes breaking. "Yeah, yeah, you were right, Analyst. Happy?" The clarification was a relief. The choice to leave wasn't some immediate, agonizing decision. It was a distant goal, a prize to be earned after five more floors of battle. It meant he didn't have to think about it. He could focus on what was right in front of him.

He turned his attention back to the personal notification, the three glowing class choices that had been patiently waiting. The new title, "Forerunner," seemed to give them even more weight.

"Okay, Analyst," Jace said, his voice dropping into a serious, tactical tone. He looked at her, not just for her opinion, but for her insight. This was a party decision, even if the choice was ultimately his.

"Juggernaut, Berserker, or Sentinel. Give me the breakdown."

Bella’s eyes unfocused for a moment, the smugness of being right instantly replaced by the cool, silver glow of pure analysis. She wasn't just looking at the words; her mind was cross-referencing them with every fight they'd ever had, every skill Jace had ever learned, every weakness they had ever exploited. The options weren't just titles; they were data points in a complex strategic equation.

She looked up at him, her expression serious. "Okay. The System is giving you a choice based on your core attributes: your insane Constitution, your high Strength, and your surprisingly effective Willpower."

She ticked off the points on her fingers.

"**Juggernaut** is the wall. Pure and simple. It's an evolution of what you're already doing. It takes your [Constitution] and makes it your primary weapon.
*   **You'd probably get skills like:** [Unstoppable Charge] to break enemy lines, or an 'Aegis' ability that lets you absorb a percentage of the damage I take.
*   **Pros:** You become the ultimate protector. Unkillable. Unmovable. You could probably stand in a fire and just be annoyed.
*   **Cons:** You might lack the raw killing power of the other options. Your job is to survive and protect, not necessarily to end the fight quickly."

"**Berserker** is the hammer. It takes your 'Gutter-Fighting' roots and your [Strength] and dials them up to eleven. It's a high-risk, high-reward path.
*   **You'd probably get skills like:** A [Rage] mechanic where you deal more damage as you lose health, or a lifesteal ability like [Blood for Blood].
*   **Pros:** The highest potential damage output. You'd chew through enemies like a machine. Fights would be fast and brutal.
*   **Cons:** You'd be a glass cannon, relatively speaking. I'd be healing you with [Sanguine Offering] constantly. You'd be harder to control, and if you get overwhelmed, you'd go down fast. It’s a pure DPS class."

She paused, her gaze becoming more intense as she described the final option.

"**Sentinel**... this one is different. It's not just a wall or a hammer. It's a fortress. It's a psychological class. It combines your [Constitution] with your [Willpower] and your new [Reputation]. It's not just about taking hits; it's about *commanding* the battlefield.
*   **You'd probably get skills like:** A fear-based aura that weakens enemies just by being near you, or a 'Vigilant's Rebuke' that automatically damages anyone who attacks me or JJ. It’s an area-denial and control class.
*   **Pros:** It synergizes perfectly with our current strategy. It weaponizes your 'Forerunner' title and our 'Mr. Dreg' legend. You'd control the flow of every single fight, forcing enemies to engage you on your terms. It's the smartest option.
*   **Cons:** It might be the most complex. It relies on more than just hitting things; it relies on positioning, timing, and using your presence as a weapon."

Bella finished, letting the information hang in the air. She looked him in the eye, her analysis complete.

"So," she summarized, flawlessly echoing his own thoughts back at him in her own words. "In short... do you want to specialize in being a Tank, being a mindless wrecking ball, or a Tower of Terror?"

Jace stroked his chin, a thoughtful, deliberate gesture that would have been more effective if he'd had even a hint of a beard. He paced the small grotto, the three choices seeming to hang in the air around him like spectral guides.

"So..." he mused aloud, his voice a low rumble. "I can sort of feel the Juggernaut... but it's not flexible enough. It's just a better wall. And we've learned that a wall can be walked around." He dismissed the first option with a shake of his head. It was safe, but safety wasn't how they had gotten this far.

"As a Berserker... we can kill fast, reducing the danger," he continued, the idea clearly tempting him. The thought of pure, unadulterated power, of ending fights in a blur of mace-swinging fury, was appealing. "But you're right. It's reckless. It puts everything on you to keep me alive. If you go down, I go down a second later. It's a single point of failure." He stopped pacing, his gaze turning inward.

"But... Sentinel..." He said the word differently. It wasn't just a class; it felt like a destination. "I can feel it calling to me. It's not just about me being stronger. It's about making *us* stronger. Controlling the field... using fear... protecting you and JJ just by standing there... I think it will be important going forward. Not just against monsters, but against... other climbers."

The finality in his tone made it clear. The decision was made.

He looked up at Bella, a new resolve hardening his features. "Sentinel it is."

He was about to confirm the choice when a stray thought hit him. He paused, his focus shifting.

"Hey," he asked, his brow furrowing slightly. "Did I get anything for the Forerunner title?"

Bella blinked, then her Analyst's mind immediately accessed the log. "Let me check... The broadcast said it was a reward..." Her eyes went wide. "Oh. Oh, wow. I missed this in all the chaos."

She shared the notification with him. It was a single, elegant line of golden text that had been attached to the title announcement.

**[Title Reward: Forerunner's Insight]**
*   **[Effect: As the first to unlock the Advanced Class system, you are granted a deeper understanding of your chosen path. When you select your Advanced Class, it will begin at Level 2 instead of Level 1.]**

Jace's jaw dropped. A free level. The System wasn't just rewarding him; it was giving him a massive, unprecedented head start on his new path. The choice wasn't just important anymore; it was supercharged.

A slow, dangerous grin spread across his face. He looked at the glowing [SENTINEL] option, his decision now reinforced with the certainty of a System-sanctioned destiny.

"Okay," he said, his voice brimming with a newfound power. "Let's make this official."

He focused his will, his entire being, on the glowing word.

"I choose Sentinel."

The moment Jace confirmed his choice, the world seemed to narrow to a single point of light centered in his chest. It wasn't the hot, chaotic burn of his furnace; this was a cool, heavy, and immensely powerful light, like the birth of a star made of solid iron.

He felt a profound shift, not just in his body, but in his very presence. The air around him grew heavier. The shadows in the grotto seemed to deepen, flocking to him as if he were their source. His [Reputation] and [Willpower] stats flared, merging with his [Constitution] to forge something new, something more.

He didn't just feel stronger. He felt... inevitable.

A cascade of notifications, shimmering with a silver-grey light, flooded his vision. He didn't even have to read them all to feel the changes settling into his bones.

**[Advanced Class Selected: SENTINEL]**
**[Forerunner's Insight: ACTIVE. Sentinel Class begins at Level 2.]**
**[Base Stats Adjusted. +2 Constitution, +2 Willpower per Sentinel Level.]**
**[Your presence on the battlefield is now a tangible force.]**

**[New Skill Unlocked (Sentinel Lvl 1): Aura of Dread]**
*   **[Type: Passive Aura]**
*   **[Effect: Enemies within a 20-foot radius of you suffer a penalty to their [Willpower] and [Focus] stats, proportional to your [Reputation]. Low-level enemies may become afflicted with [Fear].]**

**[New Skill Unlocked (Sentinel Lvl 2): Vigilant's Rebuke]**
*   **[Type: Triggered Retaliation]**
*   **[Effect: When a party member within your [Aura of Dread] is struck by an attack, you automatically retaliate, dealing 25% of your [Strength] as Force damage to the attacker. This retaliation cannot be dodged and has no travel time.]**

Jace’s eyes snapped open, wide with a stunned, almost giddy disbelief. He let out a low, shaky breath. "Wow!"

He immediately shared the skill descriptions with Bella. "Take a look at this, Bella!"

Bella's jaw dropped as she read. Her Analyst's mind, usually so calm and collected, was sent reeling by the sheer, game-breaking power of what she was seeing.

"[Aura of Dread]..." she whispered, her eyes huge. "Jace, that's not just a skill. It's a debuff field. You don't have to *do* anything. You just stand there, and the things trying to kill us get weaker and stupider. The higher our reputation gets, the stronger the effect becomes. It's... it's a perpetual psychological attack."

But it was the second skill that truly stole her breath.

"[Vigilant's Rebuke]..." She looked at him, her expression a mixture of awe and dawning horror for anything that ever tried to fight them again. "If something hits me... you hit it back. Instantly. No matter where you are, as long as I'm near you. It's a perfect, automated defense. I don't need a shield anymore. *You* are my shield."

Jace slammed his fist into his open palm, a wide, predatory grin spreading across his face. He could feel it now, a low, almost sub-sonic hum radiating from his body. It was the Aura, a tangible projection of his will. He was no longer just a wall. He was a walking fortress that shot back.

He looked at Bella, his eyes glowing with a new, profound confidence.

"Exactly," he said, his voice a low, dangerous rumble. "Now, let's go find something to test it on."
