# Chapter 28: The Canopy Stalkers

The Verdant Atrium wasn't just beautiful; it was dense. A thick, jungle-like canopy formed a ceiling a hundred feet above, and the path forward was a winding trail through gargantuan ferns and roots as thick as Jace's body. It was a perfect place for an ambush.

And they had just walked into one.

From the canopy above, half a dozen figures detached themselves from the shadows. They were long-limbed, primate-like creatures with mottled green-and-brown fur and claws that looked like obsidian shards. They moved with a silent, terrifying grace, positioning themselves on the thick branches that overhung the path, effectively cutting off any retreat.

Six of them. A coordinated pack.

Jace's hand tightened on the grip of his mace, the cool leather a familiar comfort. He took a half-step in front of Bella, his body instinctively shielding her.

"Okay, Analyst," he said, his voice a low, dangerous rumble that didn't betray a hint of fear. "Map out our game plan. Six enemies is... substantial. Hope this Sentinel class lives up to its name!"

Bella didn't panic. Her mind was a whirlwind of silver light, processing the sudden, overwhelming threat.

**[Identify: Canopy Stalker (Lvl 9)]**
**[Type: Beast]**
**[Armor: Low]**
**[Agility: Very High]**
**[Weakness: Low Willpower. Susceptible to Fear and Intimidation effects.]**

**[Tactical Survey: Active]**
The area lit up in her vision. The six Stalkers were red threats. The path ahead narrowed significantly, a chokepoint between the gnarled roots of two massive, ancient trees. High above that chokepoint, a thick, heavy tangle of parasitic vines, glowing with a faint yellow light, hung precariously from a dead branch.

The plan snapped into her mind, fully formed.

"We don't fight them here," she said, her voice steady and sure. "They have the high ground and room to flank. We fall back. Twenty paces. To the ironwood roots."

Jace nodded, trusting her implicitly. They began a slow, deliberate retreat. The Canopy Stalkers followed, leaping from branch to branch, their chittering screeches echoing through the atrium. They were confident. They were herding their prey.

They reached the chokepoint. The path was barely wide enough for two people to stand abreast. "Here," Bella commanded. "This is the kill zone. Jace, you are the cork in the bottle. You hold this line. Let your aura do the work."

She then looked at JJ, who was clinging to her cowl, his crystalline fur bristling. She projected her intent: *Climb. Wait for the signal. Chew.* JJ seemed to understand, leaping from her shoulder and scrambling up the trunk of one of the ironwood trees, disappearing into the leaves above the path.

"Ready?" Bella asked, nocking a stone in her sling.

Jace just grinned. "Let's see what happens."

The first two Canopy Stalkers dropped from the branches and charged into the narrow pass. The moment they entered the 20-foot radius of Jace's new aura, the effect was visible and immediate.

**[Aura of Dread: Active]**

Their aggressive, confident charge faltered. Their movements became hesitant, their chittering screeches becoming unnerved and questioning. They weren't just facing a boy with a hammer anymore; they were facing a tangible projection of dread, a psychic weight that pressed down on their weak wills.

Jace capitalized on their hesitation without mercy. He took one step forward, his mace a blur, and brought it down on the first Stalker's head. The creature collapsed, its skull caving in.

The second Stalker, seeing its partner fall, shook off the fear and lunged, not at Jace, but at Bella behind him.

Before Jace could even turn, the Stalker was thrown backward as if hit by an invisible fist, a grunt of pain punched from its lungs.

**[Vigilant's Rebuke: Active!]**

A ghostly grey ripple in the air was the only sign of the attack. The Stalker clutched its chest, stunned and confused by the unavoidable retaliation. Jace finished it with a brutal backswing.

The four remaining Stalkers screeched in fury and confusion from the branches. One of them, seeing JJ climbing above them, ripped a shard of bark from a tree and hurled it like a throwing knife. The shard spun through the air and sliced into JJ's flank.

JJ let out a pained squeak.

The Stalker who threw it was instantly slammed sideways by another incorporeal blow from [Vigilant's Rebuke], nearly knocking it from its perch.

"NOW, BELLA!" Jace roared, bracing himself.

Bella didn't need the command. The moment JJ was hit, she was already pushing the final command to him. *Chew! Now!*

High above, JJ sank his teeth into the dead vine's anchor point. With a final, tearing sound, the entire massive tangle of heavy, wet vegetation gave way.

It fell like a net, a hundred-pound curtain of green and brown, directly onto the two Stalkers who were preparing to leap. They were swatted from the branches, landing in a heap on the path, hopelessly entangled in the thick, ropy vines.

The last Stalker, the one nursing its rebuked ribs, hesitated for a split second, its nerve broken. That was a fatal mistake. A stone from Bella's sling cracked against its skull, and it dropped from the branch like a stone.

Jace calmly walked over to the writhing, trapped Stalkers and dispatched them with two clean, efficient blows.

The battle was over. Six hostiles, eliminated. Jace had a minor scratch. JJ was bleeding slightly, but Bella was already tending to the wound. It was a flawless victory.

Jace stood in the narrow pass, surrounded by the quiet dead. He looked at the humming, almost invisible aura surrounding his body, then back at the carnage. He let out a low, satisfied breath.

"Yeah," he said to no one in particular. "I think it lives up to the name."
