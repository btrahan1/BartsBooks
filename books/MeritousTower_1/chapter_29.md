# Chapter 29: Meeting Carol

The grotto behind the waterfall was a perfect sanctuary, but it wasn't the path forward. After tending to JJ's minor wound and taking a moment to rest, they ventured back into the lush, humid growth of the Atrium. As they rounded a bend choked with giant, blood-red orchids, Jace held up a hand, his body going tense.

"Do you hear that?" he whispered.

Bella strained her ears, filtering out the hum of insects and the drip of water. Faintly, she could hear it too. The sharp, rhythmic *clang* of metal hitting something hard, punctuated by a desperate, strained yell. It wasn't the guttural screech of a monster. It was human.

"It sounds like fighting," Bella confirmed, her voice barely audible. "Who could be here before us? The broadcast said we were the first."

"It didn't say we were the only ones *on* the floor," Jace countered grimly. "Just the first to reach the Nexus."

They moved forward with practiced stealth, Jace in the lead, mace at the ready. They peeked through a curtain of thick, purple ferns into a small, sun-dappled clearing.

There, a lone figure was locked in a desperate battle with a vicious-looking, six-legged creature that resembled a praying mantis, its forelimbs ending in scythes of razor-sharp chitin. The figure was a girl, clad in a mismatched set of leather and metal plating, wielding a simple short sword and a buckler shield. She was skilled, her movements economical and precise, but she was clearly being worn down.

"Oh, she's alone!" Bella whispered, a note of surprise in her voice. A solo climber on Floor 5 was almost unheard of.

Jace’s eyes narrowed, but not at the fight. He was looking at the girl, a flicker of something he couldn't quite place nagging at him. He turned to Bella, his gaze a silent command. Bella nodded, already focusing her [Identify] skill on the mysterious fighter.

The information flared in her vision, and she let out a small, confused gasp.

**[Name: Carol]**
**[Level: 11]**
**[Title: The Coward]**

She immediately shared the notification. Jace read the title, his brow furrowed in utter confusion. "Carol the Coward? Who the heck is she?" Level 11? How was that even possible?

"I don't understand," Bella murmured, diving deeper into the analysis the System provided. "Her entire build... it's based on avoidance. Her highest stats are Agility and Stamina. Her skills are things like [Disengage], [Feint], [Evasion]. She has almost no offensive abilities."

"So how is she Level 11?" Jace asked, watching as Carol expertly dodged a scythe-swipe but made no move to counter-attack, instead just creating more distance.

"She... runs," Bella said, the pieces clicking together in her mind with a dawning, slightly distasteful sense of understanding. "She never initiates a fight. She finds other climbers already engaged with monsters, waits for them to do most of the damage, and then when they're weak or forced to retreat, she swoops in to finish the nearly-dead creature for the full XP. She survives by letting others take all the risks."

Jace stared at the girl, his expression shifting from confusion to a grim, almost contemptuous recognition. She was another kind of scavenger. A different kind of kill-stealer. One who waited in the shadows not for an opportunity to strike, but for an opportunity to pick at the scraps left behind by the brave or the foolish.

The mantis-creature screeched and lunged, finally catching Carol's shield and sending her stumbling backward, her stamina clearly failing.

"So," Jace said, his voice a low growl as he looked from the struggling girl to Bella. "What do we do, Analyst? Help the Coward?"

The debate was short and purely academic. Jace might be a ruthless pragmatist, but he wasn't a monster. And Bella's compassion wouldn't let them stand by and watch someone be torn apart, title or no title.

Jace stepped into the clearing, his [Aura of Dread] washing over the mantis-creature like a physical wave. The beast, already focused on Carol, flinched violently, its aggression faltering as a primal fear wormed its way into its insectoid mind.

That was all the opening Carol needed. Seeing the monster hesitate, she executed a flawless [Disengage], rolling backward and creating a precious ten feet of space.

Jace charged in, his mace a blur. The mantis, already weakened and now terrified, was no match for a coordinated assault. Jace broke its armored legs while Bella and JJ provided harrying fire. The fight was over in seconds.

Carol stood panting, leaning on her buckler, and stared at them with wide, disbelieving eyes. She looked at Jace's brutal efficiency, at Bella's calm precision, at the sparkling rat on her shoulder.

"Who... who are you?" she asked, her voice raspy.

"We were about to ask you the same thing," Jace said, his tone not unkind, but wary. He rested his mace on his shoulder. "The System says you're Level 11. The broadcast said we were the first to get to this floor."

Carol let out a short, bitter laugh that turned into a coughing fit. "First... of *your* class, maybe." She finally caught her breath and looked at them with eyes that held a deep, ancient weariness that no twelve-or-thirteen-year-old should possess.

"I've been here for over a year," she said, the words heavy with a profound and terrible sadness. "I'm from *last year's* Induction."

Jace and Bella exchanged a look of pure shock. The idea that someone could survive that long, alone, was staggering.

"My team..." Carol's gaze drifted to a massive, vine-choked gate at the far end of the clearing—the Floor 5 boss gate. "We wiped on the Gatekeeper. It... it killed them all. I was the scout. I was supposed to find a way out. But I just... I ran. And I've been running and surviving ever since."

She slumped down onto a mossy rock, the fight completely gone from her. "The System is cruel. For my... defining act... it gave me a title." She shared the full description with them.

**[Title: The Coward]**
*   **[Effect: You have abandoned your comrades in their moment of greatest need. You suffer a permanent -50% penalty to all damage dealt. Your presence inspires [Doubt] in party members, slightly lowering their offensive capabilities.]**

Bella gasped, her hand flying to her mouth. It wasn't just a title; it was a curse. A permanent debuff that ensured she could never truly fight her way forward.

"I... I specialized as a Spellsword," Carol continued, her voice a hollow whisper. "I'm a Level 11 mage with the offensive power of a Level 5 brawler. I'm stuck. I can't beat the Gatekeeper alone, and no one would ever team up with 'Carol the Coward'." She tapped her temple. "It's not just a title. It's what I am. In my own mind."

Jace and Bella stood in silence, the tragic reality of the Spire laid bare before them. It wasn't just a place that killed you. It was a place that could break your spirit so completely that you were left to wander its halls as a ghost, trapped not by walls, but by your own failure. They were looking at a grim possible future, a cautionary tale made of flesh and bone.

Jace listened to her story, his usual hard expression unreadable. He looked at the girl—not much older than them, maybe fourteen—slumped in apathetic defeat. He saw her high level, her decent gear, and the crushing weight of the scarlet letter the System had branded onto her soul. He saw a resource. A wasted one.
