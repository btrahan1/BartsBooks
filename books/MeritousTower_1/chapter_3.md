# Chapter 3: The Brute & The Brain

Jace didn't wait for an answer. He pointed to a hollow beneath a massive, arching root system. It was elevated slightly above the water line—dry enough to keep her from freezing, dark enough to hide her.

"Sit," Jace commanded, his voice barely a whisper. "Don't make a sound. Just watch. Learn how they move."

Bella nodded, scrambling into the hole. She pulled her knees to her chest, her eyes wide, reflecting the faint bioluminescence of the swamp fungi.

**[Bella has entered 'Stealth' (Passive)]**
**[Bella is using 'Observation' (Uninitiated)]**

Jace turned away. He adjusted his grip on the rusted rebar. It was heavy, unbalanced, and crude. Perfect.

He scanned the immediate area. Now that he wasn't panicking, the swamp was alive with noise. The *Internal Furnace* burning in his chest kept the cold at bay, and his heightened [Eavesdrop] skill filtered the chaotic noise into distinct layers.

*Water lapping against wood.*
*Distant shouting from the Highborn rock.*
*Scrabbling claws on bark.*

*There.*

About twenty feet away, a **Dredge-Rat** was gnawing on a floating log. It was the size of a bulldog, its fur matted with slime, its tail naked and pink.

Jace lowered his center of gravity. He didn't know the "Walking on Clouds" footwork the Highborn kids probably learned. He used **The Shuffle**. It was how you walked on creaky floorboards in a squat without waking up the addicts passed out in the hallway. You roll the foot, outside edge to inside.

He moved through the water. It was waist-deep, which actually helped. It muffled his steps.

**[Stealth Check: Passed]**

He came up behind the rat. It paused, sniffing the air. Jace froze. He held his breath, stopping the *Internal Furnace* cycle for a split second to lower his presence. The rat went back to gnawing.

Jace didn't hesitate. He didn't aim for a fair fight. He aimed for the kill.

He lunged, driving the rebar down with both hands.

*Thwack.*

The metal spike punched through the rat’s spine, pinning it to the floating log. The creature let out a high-pitched squeal that was cut short as Jace twisted the bar, severing the cord.

**[CRITICAL HIT!]**
**[Sneak Attack Bonus: x2 Damage]**
**[Target Eliminated: Dredge-Rat (Lvl 2)]**

**[XP Gained: 25]**

Jace yanked the bar free. The body slid into the water.

*Too loud,* he criticized himself. *The squeal was too loud.*

He looked back at Bella. She flinched as he made eye contact, but she hadn't looked away. She was learning.

Jace moved to the next target. A pair of rats fighting over a dead fish near a root cluster.

This time, he needed to be faster.

He crept closer. The *Internal Furnace* was humming again, repairing the micro-tears in his muscles from the exertion, stamina refilling almost instantly. He felt... tireless.

He waited until the rats were fully engaged in their tug-of-war.

*Strike.*

He impaled the first one through the ribs. The second one hissed and leaped at his face.

Jace didn't dodge. He used his free hand—the left one, with the fresh bandage—and backhanded the rat mid-air.

*CRACK.*

It wasn't a martial arts strike. It was a street brawl slap. But with his Strength at 4 and the momentum of his body, it sent the rat spinning into the tree trunk. It hit with a wet thud, stunned.

Jace finished it with a stomp of his heel, crushing its skull into the mud.

**[Target Eliminated: Dredge-Rat (Lvl 2)]**
**[Target Eliminated: Dredge-Rat (Lvl 2)]**

**[Skill Unlocked: Cheap Shot (Lvl 1)]**
*Description: Honor is for the living. You prefer to win.*
*Effect: First strike against an unaware or stunned opponent deals +15% Damage.*

Jace stood over the carcasses, breathing heavily. The steam from his breath mingled with the mist. He felt the System pinging him again.

**[Achievement: The Exterminator (Tier 1)]**
*Kill 10 Vermin-type enemies.*
*Reward: +1 Agility.*

"Seven more," Jace muttered. He looked at the rebar. It was coated in gore.

He waved Bella over. She hesitated, then crawled out of her hiding spot, splashing quietly toward him.

"Did you see?" Jace asked.

"You... you hit the one while it was eating," Bella whispered. "And you stepped on the other one's head."

"Ugly, right?" Jace asked.

"Effective," she said, her voice trembling but certain.

Jace smirked. "Good. Now, look here." He pointed to the rat's lower back. "The kidneys. The spine. Soft spots. If you fight these things from the front, they bite you. If you fight them from behind, you eat tonight."

He crouched down and dug out the small, meager cores from the rats. He tossed one to her.

"Put that in your pocket. That's money."

"Jace?" she asked, clutching the bloody stone.

"Yeah?"

"Your eyes. They're... glowing a little bit. Orange."

Jace touched his face. He felt the heat radiating from his skin. The **Null-Iron Body** was eating the ambient mana of the swamp and turning it into pure biological fuel. He wasn't casting spells; he was becoming a furnace.

"Don't worry about it," Jace said, turning back to the dark swamp. "Stay close. We need seven more before we rest."

The seventh rat had died just like the others—quickly, violently, and without honor.

**[Achievement Unlocked: The Exterminator (Tier 1)]**
**[Reward: +1 Agility]**

Jace felt the change immediately. His muscles twitched, feeling lighter, more responsive. It wasn't a massive superhero transformation, but the sluggishness of malnutrition faded just a fraction. He could snap his fingers faster. He could turn his head quicker.

He dragged the carcasses to a relatively dry patch of root clusters, well away from the water line.

"Okay," Jace said, looking at the pile of wet, furry bodies. "Now the hard part."

Bella looked at the rats, then at Jace, her face turning a shade of green that matched the swamp algae. "We... we aren't really going to..."

"The Highborn have rations," Jace said, sitting cross-legged on the roots. "They have protein bars and mana-water. They’ll be full for two days. Maybe three." He looked at her seriously. "But this tower is infinite, Bella. What happens on day four?"

He didn't wait for an answer. He began scouring the mud around the roots. He needed a tool. The rebar was a bludgeon and a spike, not a knife.

He found a large, jagged fragment of a clam shell. The edge was serrated and razor-sharp.

**[Mud-Clam Shell (Trash)]**
**[Durability: 2/5]**

"Good enough," Jace muttered.

He picked up the first rat. It was still warm. He grit his teeth. He’d seen animals butchered in the market, but he’d never done it. He just had to remember the *intent*. The System rewarded intent.

*Separate the skin. Remove the guts. Save the meat.*

He sliced into the belly. The smell was atrocious—hot copper and sewage. Bella gagged and turned away, covering her mouth.

Jace didn't turn away. He forced his hands to be steady. If he punctured the stomach, he’d taint the meat. He worked with a grim, surgical focus, peeling back the hide.

**[Skill Unlocked: Field Butchery (Lvl 1)]**
*Description: You aren't picky, but you are precise.*
*Effect: Increases yield of usable meat from Beast-type enemies by 10%. Reduces chance of tainting resources.*

"See?" Jace whispered to himself. "Another skill."

By the third rat, his hands were covered in gore, but he had a neat pile of pinkish, stringy meat strips and a separate pile of usable bones (for potential needles or tools).

"Fire," Jace said, wiping his hands on the moss. "We need fire."

This was the tricky part. The swamp was soaked. Every twig, every leaf was waterlogged.

Jace gathered a handful of grey, fibrous moss. He squeezed the water out of it, but it was still damp. It wouldn't catch a spark like this.

He looked down at his chest. The *Internal Furnace* was humming. He could feel the heat radiating off his skin—a fever that didn't make him sick.

He unbuttoned the top of his tunic and shoved the damp moss directly against his bare skin, right over his sternum.

"Jace?" Bella asked, looking back. "What are you doing?"

"I'm a radiator," Jace grunted. "Give me a minute."

He sat there, eyes closed, focusing on the heat. *Burn.* He pushed the mana into his skin. The moss began to steam. Slowly, the dampness evaporated, driven out by his unnatural body heat. Ten minutes later, he pulled the moss out. It was dry and brittle. Warm to the touch.

He set it down on a flat stone. He took his rusted rebar and found a piece of hard flint in the mud.

*Strike. Strike. Strike.*

Sparks showered down. The dry moss caught instantly, glowing orange. Jace gently fed it small twigs, then larger branches he had dried near his body while he worked.

**[Skill Unlocked: Firestarter (Lvl 1)]**
*Description: The ability to create warmth in hostile environments.*
*Effect: Fires you build last 10% longer and are harder to extinguish by wind/rain.*

Jace skewered the rat meat on thin bone slivers and set them over the small, smokeless flame.

The smell changed. The sewage scent burned away, replaced by the universal, primal smell of cooking meat. Fat dripped into the fire, hissing.

Jace watched the meat turn brown. He wasn't rushing. Valerius and the Highborn were probably miles away by now, chasing the Level 5 mobs, desperate to hit the Leaderboards. They were sprinting.

Jace sat back, watching the fire.

**[Achievement Unlocked: The Self-Sufficient]**
*Criteria: Successfully hunt, butcher, and cook a meal within 1 hour of Induction without using Consumables.*
*Reward: Constitution +1. Stomach Capacity increased.*

"Eat," Jace said, handing a skewer to Bella.

She looked at it. It was a rat on a stick. It looked gross. But she hadn't eaten since breakfast before the Induction. Her stomach growled, betraying her.

She took a bite. She chewed, winced, but swallowed.

"It... tastes like chicken and dirt," she said.

"Protein," Jace corrected. He took a bite of his own. It was tough, stringy, and gamey. It was the best thing he had ever tasted.

**[Consuming Cooked Rat Meat]**
**[Satiety: +15%]**
**[Status Effect: Well-Fed (Minor)]**
*Regeneration of HP increased by 5% for 2 hours.*

Jace chewed slowly. "Let them run, Bella. Let them burn their potions and their mana bars. We’re going to eat. We’re going to sleep. And tomorrow, when they’re hungry and tired, we’re going to keep walking."

He looked at the fire, his orange-tinted eyes reflecting the flames.

"I think... I think I want to make some armor," Jace said thoughtfully, looking at the pile of rat hides he had set aside. "I bet there's a skill for that, too."

Jace sat close to the fire, the *Internal Furnace* keeping his body temperature high enough that the damp air didn't stiffen his fingers. He was working with a sharpened sliver of rib bone, punching holes through the stiff, dried rat hides. For thread, he was using the dried sinew he’d stripped from the tails.

It was ugly work. The smell of curing leather—accelerated by Jace pressing the skins against his fever-hot chest to dry them out—was pungent.

"I don't know," Bella whispered, hugging her knees. She was staring into the fire, watching the way the smoke curled. "My dad wanted me to be a Ledger-Keeper. For the Merchant Guild. Count the grain, tally the taxes."

She picked up a pebble and drew a straight line in the mud. Then another, perfectly parallel.

"I liked it," she admitted, her voice small. "I liked... knowing where things belong. In the books, if a number is wrong, you find it, and you fix it. Everything has a place."

Jace didn't look up from his stitching. He grunted, forcing the bone needle through three layers of hide. "Order. You like Order."

"It's safe," Bella said. "But here? There’s no order. It’s just... chaos. Monsters and mud."

"Wrong," Jace said. He bit the end of the sinew and pulled the knot tight. "This whole place is nothing *but* rules, Bella. The System. Experience points. Cooldowns. Physics."

He held up the crude object he had made. It was a bracer. Ugly, patchy, and smelling of burnt hair, but it was thick.

**[Item Created: Rat-Hide Vambrace (Common)]**
**[Quality: Poor]**
**[Defense: +2]**
**[Durability: 8/10]**

**[Skill Unlocked: Jury-Rigging (Lvl 1)]**
*Description: You can make something out of nothing. It won't be pretty, but it will work.*

Jace tossed the bracer to her. "Put that on your left arm. It’ll stop a bite."

Bella caught it. She looked at the crude stitching. Then she looked at Jace.

"You're making order," she realized. "You took a dead rat and made it into armor. That's... logic."

"That's survival," Jace corrected. "But back to you. You like finding the wrong numbers. You like fixing the books."

He picked up another hide. "When we fought the rats, you saw them first. You saw the one eating. You saw the pattern."

Bella nodded slowly. "They move in triangles. The two small ones flank, the big one waits. It’s... a formation. It’s just math."

Jace stopped sewing. He looked at her, his orange-flecked eyes intense. "Most people just see teeth, Bella. The Highborn see targets. If you see *math*... then you aren't a Healer. And you aren't a Ledger-Keeper."

He gestured to the darkness beyond the firelight.

"Don't look at the monster," Jace said. "Look at the *rules* of the monster. If you can tell me where they're going to be before they get there... I can kill them."

Bella stared at the line she had drawn in the mud. She drew a third line, connecting them into a triangle. She tapped the center.

"The System," she whispered. "It gives us information. But it hides it, too. Like... like a puzzle."

She closed her eyes. She didn't try to look for monsters. She tried to look for the *grid*.

**[Focusing Intent...]**
**[User Class Affinity Detected: Intelligence / Perception]**

"I don't want to be a soldier," Bella said firmly. She opened her eyes. "I want to be... the one who holds the map. The one who knows the rules better than the things trying to kill us."

A soft chime resonated in the air above her head. It wasn't the harsh blue alert of a combat notification. It was a pale, silver light.

**[Class Seed Unlocked: Analyst]**
*Requirement met: Analyze a threat pattern without engaging in combat.*
*Current Status: 1/3 Requirements for Class Change.*

**[New Skill Unlocked: Weakness Detection (Lvl 1)]**
*Description: You see the cracks in the armor.*
*Effect: By focusing on a target for 3 seconds, you can highlight a 'Critical Point' for your allies. Hitting this point guarantees a Critical Hit.*

Bella gasped. "Jace. I... I can see it. On you."

Jace raised an eyebrow. "What do you see?"

"Your left shoulder," she pointed. "You favor it. The muscle is tight. If something hits you there, your balance will break."

Jace touched his shoulder. She was right. It was stiff from the cold water.

"Useful," Jace grinned, showing teeth. It was a feral expression, but proud. "That's very useful."

He tossed the remaining rat skins onto his lap.

"Okay, *Analyst*. If you're going to be the brains, I need to be the wall. I'm going to make a vest next. You tell me where to put the extra padding."

Bella scooted closer to the fire, her fear replaced by a sudden, intense focus. She poked the pile of skins. "Not there. That's belly skin, it's too soft. Use the back strips. And you need to double layer the ribs, because you don't dodge, you tank."

Jace chuckled, threading the bone needle. "Now we're talking. We aren't going to just climb this tower, Bella. We're going to solve it."

**[Party Synergy: Activated]**
**[The Brute & The Brain]**
*XP Gain increased by 5% when working in tandem.*

Jace felt a thrill that had nothing to do with survival. They were a team. A Dreg and a Nerd against the world.

"Tomorrow," Jace said, pushing the needle through the tough hide, "we hunt something bigger."
