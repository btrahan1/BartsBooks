# Chapter 31: The Heartwood

***

As the last mote of the Blightwood's body disintegrated into the rich soil, a profound silence fell over the clearing, broken only by Carol's ragged, gasping breaths. The adrenaline that had fueled her suicidal charge vanished, and her legs gave out. She didn't just fall; she collapsed into a heap on the mossy ground, burying her face in her hands as a year's worth of repressed fear, grief, and shame finally broke free in a series of deep, shuddering sobs.

They weren't the whimpers of a coward. They were the raw, cleansing tears of a survivor who had just won her hardest battle.

Bella was at her side in an instant, kneeling and placing a gentle hand on her shoulder. She didn't say anything. There was nothing to say. She just offered her presence, the quiet, steady support of the person who had believed in her when she couldn't believe in herself. JJ hopped from Bella's shoulder and nudged Carol's hand with his tiny, crystalline head, letting out a soft, comforting purr.

Jace stood guard, his mace held loosely, his gaze sweeping the perimeter out of pure, ingrained habit. He watched the scene, his expression unreadable, but the [Aura of Dread] that normally surrounded him seemed to recede, replaced by a quiet, protective stillness. He was the Sentinel, and right now, his duty was to guard this moment of fragile, hard-won peace.

As always, the System was the first to move on. A series of notifications chimed, their cool, impersonal light a stark contrast to the raw emotion of the moment.

**[Floor 5 Gatekeeper: Grasping Blightwood - DEFEATED!]**
**[Party has demonstrated exceptional teamwork and unconventional strategy.]**
**[Reputation +3 (For redeeming a fallen Ascendant)]**

**[Loot Generated:]**
*   **[Blightwood Heartwood (Rare)]**: *A core of extremely resilient, self-regenerating wood. Can be used to craft powerful defensive gear.*
*   **[Skill Tome: Thorn Volley (Uncommon)]**: *Teaches a ranged skill that launches a spray of sharp thorns.*
*   **[Alchemist's Pouch (Magic)]**: *A small pouch containing a variety of rare herbs and fungal spores from the Verdant Atrium.*

Jace looked at the loot notifications, then at the two girls. He walked over and picked up the heavy, still-pulsing piece of Heartwood. He looked at Carol, whose sobs were finally quieting, and then at Bella.

"This is hers," he said, his voice a low, definitive rumble. He held out the Blightwood Heartwood to Carol. "She earned it."

Carol looked up, her face tear-streaked, and stared at the rare crafting material. It was a tangible symbol of the very monster she had just helped vanquish. It was the proof. Taking it felt like the final step. With a trembling hand, she accepted it.

"Now," Jace said, looking at the newly opened, vine-free gate that led to Floor 6. "We rest. We level up. And then, we decide what the three of us do next."
