# Chapter 34: The Silent Scribe

The boss of the Silent Archive was a nightmare of academia. A **Silent Scribe**, a ten-foot-tall vortex of swirling, razor-edged pages, animated scrolls, and whips of sentient ink, it made no sound save the whisper of a thousand turning pages.

Jace was in the thick of it, his mace a blur, deflecting torrents of paper that threatened to inflict a thousand tiny cuts. His [Aura of Dread] was less effective here; the creature had no mind to fear, only a cold, bibliographic purpose.

A thick tendril of black ink lashed out from the vortex, aiming for his blind spot.

"Behind you, Lover Boy!" Bella’s voice was sharp, tactical, but the nickname was unmistakable, a deliberate, well-aimed barb.

Carol snickered from the flank where she was methodically slicing through the outer layers of the paper storm. Jace, hearing the taunt and the laugh, didn't scowl. He dodged the ink whip with a fluid roll, coming up with a stupid, irrepressible grin on his face. He’d endured their relentless teasing for the entire floor, and somewhere along the way, his embarrassment had curdled into a kind of grudging acceptance. It was their new normal.

"Keep your eyes on the books, not the boy!" he yelled back, deflecting a volley of hardened scroll-spikes.

The Scribe shifted its attention, focusing its entire swirling mass on Carol. It launched a dense cloud of ink, a blinding smokescreen.

"Wipe that grin off your face, Mr. Gigolo!" Carol shouted, her voice laced with amusement as she expertly leaped back, her Heartwood blade cutting a clean arc to dissipate the ink cloud before it could envelop her.

"Ouch... that was harsh!" Jace winced as a few stray pages caught his arm, drawing thin lines of blood. He retaliated with a crushing blow to the base of the vortex, trying to break its concentration.

"I found it!" Bella called out, her [Identify] and [Tactical Survey] finally piercing the chaos. "In the center! There's a single, ancient-looking tome with a glowing spine! That's the core! We break the book, we break the boss!"

The Scribe seemed to understand her, and the entire vortex contracted, protecting its core, and unleashed a massive wave of paper in every direction.

Jace planted his feet, becoming the anchor. Bella's [Riposte Barrier] snapped into existence, deflecting the wave aimed at her. But Carol charged *into* it, her [Valiant] title glowing as she initiated a [Duel], forcing the Scribe's attention onto her. Pages swirled around her, slicing at her armor, but she ignored them, her eyes locked on the glowing spine at the heart of the storm.

"Jace! Opening! Now!" she roared.

Jace needed no other command. With a bellow, he charged, not at the Scribe, but at Carol's flank, his mace swinging. He wasn't attacking her; he was using the Quake Mace's impact to blast away the paper wall she was fighting, creating a momentary hole in the vortex.

Through the gap, Carol saw her target. She lunged, her entire body a single, focused spear, and drove her glowing green blade through the paper storm and deep into the ancient, glowing tome.

There was a final, mournful sigh of rustling pages, and the Silent Scribe collapsed, the thousands of pages and scrolls fluttering harmlessly to the floor in a silent, papery snowstorm.

They stood in the knee-deep pile of inert literature, catching their breath. Bella walked over, a proud, teasing smile on her face.

"Nice work, Casanova."

Jace just groaned, leaning on his mace. He looked at the quiet, inert pages, then at the glowing portal to Floor 8 that was forming where the boss had been.

*Thank God,* Jace thought with every fiber of his being. *This level is finally over!*
