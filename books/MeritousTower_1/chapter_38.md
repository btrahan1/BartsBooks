# Chapter 38: Floor 11: Sanctuary City


They were standing on a bustling street.

Cobblestones, worn smooth by the passage of countless feet, paved a wide avenue. Buildings made of warm, honey-colored stone rose on either side, their windows glowing with soft, welcoming light. The air was filled with the murmur of conversation, the distant clang of a blacksmith's hammer, and the smell of roasting meat from a nearby tavern. Lanterns hung from ornate poles, casting a permanent, cheerful twilight over the entire scene. And in the center of the main plaza, a massive, multi-tiered structure of crystal and polished wood pulsed with a gentle blue light, its purpose unmistakable.

The first thing they noticed on Level 11... was the store. And it was glorious. It wasn't a pedestal; it was a cathedral of commerce, an emporium.

And... people? What were all of these people doing here? It was like a small town.

Dozens of climbers, Ascendants, milled about the plaza. They were clad in an eclectic mix of powerful-looking armor and strange, exotic gear. A woman with shimmering, insectoid wings bargained with a dwarf whose beard was braided with glowing runes. A trio of beast-men, their fur a patchwork of different monster hides, laughed boisterously as they exited the tavern. These weren't children from the recent Inductions. They were veterans. Hardened, powerful survivors from years, maybe decades, past.

This wasn't a floor. This was a city. A hub. The first true safe zone for those who had chosen the path of Ascension.

Jace, Bella, and Carol stood at the entrance to the town, their epic gear and hardened expressions marking them as newcomers, but not as novices. Their collective [Reputation] preceded them, a silent, weighty announcement of their arrival. A few of the veterans turned to look at them, their eyes lingering on Jace's Sentinel shield and Carol's Valiant cloak. There were murmurs. Whispers.

"...is that them?"
"...'Team Casanova'?"
"...first to clear Floor 10 in years..."

A grizzled old man with a long, white beard and an eyepatch, who was leaning against a fountain, took a long drag from his pipe and looked them over. He wasn't impressed. He was appraising.

"Fresh meat," he grunted to his companion, a woman with sharpened, filed teeth. "Think they'll last?"

The woman just grinned, a sharp, predatory expression. "We'll see."

Jace felt the eyes on them, the weight of a hundred different stories and a thousand deadly battles. He looked at the bustling town, at the powerful, legendary figures who called this place home, and then he looked at his team.

A slow, confident smirk spread across his face. He connected to them with the Nexus Stone, his thought a clear, silent broadcast.

*Well, team. It looks like we've finally made it to the big leagues.*

The murmurs and stares continued as they walked deeper into the town, the weight of their new, unwanted fame a tangible thing. Jace was sizing up the veterans, Bella was mapping the layout of the town, and Carol was trying her best to look like she belonged.

A figure detached itself from the shadows of an alleyway, moving with a quiet, efficient grace that spoke of long experience. He was clad in dark, functional leather armor, scuffed and worn but meticulously maintained. His face was sharp, pragmatic, and unreadable. He wasn't old, but he wasn't a kid either; a veteran of some long-forgotten Induction. He stopped a few feet in front of them, his arms crossed.

"Team Casanova," he said, the name sounding less like a joke and more like a simple, factual designation. "Quite the entrance you made."

Jace instinctively moved his hand closer to his mace. Bella tensed, ready to activate a barrier.

The man held up a hand, a gesture of peace. "Relax. I'm not here for a fight. Just thought the new celebrities could use a proper guide." He gestured vaguely at the bustling plaza.

"So you see," said the adventurer clad in dark leather, his voice a low, even tone, "this is your bind point. After clearing any level, you can return here, or go on to the next one. Rest, re-arm, trade with the crafters. The next bind point is Floor 20."

He gestured to the tavern, where the sound of raucous laughter spilled out. "Lots of folks like it here. They find their limit, get a taste of the good life, and decide not to push their luck. They become residents. It's not a bad life, all things considered."

He leaned in closer, his voice dropping. "And oh, one more thing... The System didn't mention it, and the Elites already know this, so I figure you should too. At Floor 25, there is a hidden way to leave. It's super tough, a trial that makes a standard Gatekeeper look like a Dredge-Rat. But if you can do it, you can leave the tower and be qualified to become a Professional in the world below."

The three of them exchanged a silent, instantaneous look, their minds connected by the Nexus Stone.

*Jace: Professional is better than Worker. But what's better than Professional? Why is he telling us this?*

*Bella: A base of operations. This changes everything. We can resupply, gather information... A second, better exit is a new variable. But information is never free.*

*Carol: Leave... as a Professional? Not a Dreg, not a Worker... A real life. Could we do it? Together?*

Bella was the one to voice their collective suspicion. "Why are you telling us this? What do you get out of it?"

The man's unreadable expression finally broke into a small, weary smile. "Because I was a Dreg, once. And the Elites, with their family grimoires and their secret knowledge, have had the advantage for too long. My group... we call ourselves the Unseen. We try to level the playing field."

He looked them over one last time, a calculating glint in his eye. "You three made a lot of noise on your way up. You embarrassed a major Elite clan. That makes you interesting to people like me."

He nodded, a gesture of respect. "The name's Kaelan. Think about what I said. When you're done playing tourist, find me. There's more to this tower than just climbing."

With that, he turned and melted back into the shadows of the alley, leaving them standing in the middle of the bustling plaza with a wealth of new, game-changing information and a choice that was far more complex than just 'stay or go'.

The weight of Kaelan's information settled over them, a new, complex layer to their reality. The Spire wasn't just a vertical gauntlet anymore; it was a society, with factions, secrets, and a political landscape they had just stumbled into.

Carol looked down at her crimson cloak and Heartwood blade, then at the veterans milling around in armor that seemed to be woven from solidified shadow or glowing with internal starlight.

"I feel underdressed," she murmured, a sentiment that perfectly captured their sudden transition from big fish in a small pond to fresh-faced newcomers in an ocean of legends.

"Agreed," Jace grunted, his epic-quality gear suddenly feeling... standard. "Let's take a look at that shop. Properly this time. And while we're at it..." He looked between the two of them, his gaze serious. "Let's share our stat screens. I want to see the full picture. See where the Sentinel class took me and what being 'Valiant' really did for you, Carol."

They found a quiet corner in the plaza, away from the main thoroughfare, and settled onto a stone bench. With a shared act of will through the Nexus Stone, they brought up their personal status screens, overlaying them in their shared vision so they could all see the full, detailed breakdown of their collective power.

They absorbed the data in silence. Jace's Constitution and Willpower were monstrous, the perfect stats for a Sentinel. Bella's Intelligence had hit a staggering 40, making her mind their single most powerful weapon. And Carol... Carol was a terrifyingly balanced fighter, a true hybrid of speed, strength, and surprising magical aptitude. She was the perfect DPS, able to adapt to any situation.

"Okay," Jace said, the first to break the silence. His voice was low, filled with a newfound respect for the sheer, quantifiable power they represented. "That's our baseline. Now... let's go spend some money and make those numbers even bigger."

He stood up, his gaze fixed on the glowing, multi-tiered emporium that dominated the plaza. It was time to stop feeling underdressed. It was time to start gearing up for the real climb.

The interior of the System Store was even more impressive up close. It was a vast, open-atrium building, with holographic displays of legendary weapons floating in mid-air and specialized vendor-constructs standing behind counters dedicated to everything from alchemy to enchanting.

They walked past a display of glowing swords, their eyes drawn to a section labeled 'Armorsmith'. Full suits of armor stood on silent, unmoving mannequins, each piece radiating a faint aura of power.

"Let's focus on getting a set of armor," Jace said, his voice low and serious. "It would make sense to get full coverage, and maybe some bonuses. Hope we can afford what we want."

He paused, then turned to the girls, a flicker of his old, awkward charm returning.

"If it's okay with you ladies," he said, "let's just consider our funds 'Team Casanova' funds." He gave them an exaggerated wink, a clear, self-deprecating olive branch.

Carol rolled her eyes, but a genuine smile broke through. "Fine," she said, crossing her arms. "But if we go broke, *you're* the one hunting space rats for dinner, Casanova."

Bella, ever the pragmatist, just nodded. "Resource pooling is the most efficient strategy for party progression. The logic is sound." She did, however, allow a small, amused smile to touch her lips. The gesture was accepted.

They approached the armorsmith construct, a silent golem of polished bronze, and began browsing its inventory. Their eyes were immediately drawn to a matching set of dark grey, steel-plated armor, trimmed with hardened leather and etched with faint, silver runes. It wasn't as ornate as some of the legendary gear the veterans wore, but it was sleek, functional, and looked incredibly durable.

Bella, naturally, took the lead. "[Identify]."

**[Warden's Resolve Armor Set (Rare)]**
*   **[Description: A full set of armor designed for frontline guardians and their support. The silver runes are designed to create a protective ward when allies are near.]**

*   **[Set Bonus (2 pieces):]** *+10% Resistance to Physical Damage.*
*   **[Set Bonus (3 pieces):]** *Unlocks the passive skill [Guardian's Bond]. All party members within your [Aura of Dread] radius gain bonus Armor equal to 10% of your total [Constitution] stat.*

Bella's eyes went wide. She immediately shared the data.

"Jace," she said, her voice filled with analytical excitement. "Look at the three-piece bonus. It keys off *your* aura. With your current Constitution of 40, it would grant all three of us an extra 4 points of Armor. For free. The higher your Constitution gets, the stronger our entire team's defense becomes."

Jace saw it instantly. It was the perfect Sentinel set. It turned his personal toughness into a team-wide defensive buff. "Okay," he said, his voice grim with determination. "That's the one. How much?"

The construct displayed the price. Each piece—helm, cuirass, and greaves—cost 500 MP. A full set was 1,500 MP. For all three of them, the total came to a staggering 4,500 MP. After cashing in their recent loot, their 'Team Casanova' fund stood at 4,650 MP.

It would wipe them out.

They exchanged a look, a silent conversation passing between them through the Nexus Stone.

*Carol: It's everything we have. But that bonus...*
*Bella: The long-term defensive scaling is optimal. It's a sound investment.*
*Jace: We'll be broke. But we'll be alive. We'll earn more.*

The decision was unanimous.

Jace turned to the construct. "Three full sets of the Warden's Resolve."

**[4,500 MP has been deducted. Your balance is 150 MP.]**

Nine pieces of gleaming, runed armor materialized before them. They spent the next ten minutes equipping their new gear. The transformation was profound. Gone were the mismatched pieces of leather and scavenged parts. Now, they were a unit. Clad in matching, dark grey steel, they looked like a professional, elite team of Ascendants.

Jace settled his new helmet onto his head, the world appearing through the narrow visor. He looked at his partners, their faces now shielded, their forms clad in identical, purpose-built steel.

"Alright, Team Casanova," he said, his voice slightly muffled by the helm, a new, harder edge to it. "Let's go earn some money."

Clad in their new, matching Warden's Resolve armor, they stepped out of the armorsmith, the dark steel gleaming in the soft, magical light of the plaza. The transformation was not just physical; it was a statement. They were no longer a ragtag group of survivors. They were a unit, a force to be reckoned with, and the change did not go unnoticed.

"Well... don't you all look nice."

The voice was smooth, confident, and laced with a teasing amusement. They turned. Leaning against a nearby pillar was a woman who seemed to embody the very concept of a veteran Ascendant. She was tall, easily six feet, with long, silver hair braided with what looked like captured starlight. Her armor was a form-fitting, midnight-blue material that seemed to shift like liquid shadow. A wicked-looking, curved blade was strapped to her back.

Bella's [Identify] was already at work, a reflex now.

**[Name: Seraphina]**
**[Level: 15]**
**[Class: Nightblade]**
**[Title: The Swift]**

She was gorgeous, powerful, and radiating an aura of casual, predatory confidence that made the hair on the back of Jace's neck stand up.

Seraphina pushed off the pillar and sauntered over, her movements fluid and impossibly graceful. Her eyes, the color of twilight, scanned each of them, lingering for a moment on Jace's Sentinel shield and Carol's Valiant cloak.

"A full set of Warden's Resolve," she noted, a flicker of professional appreciation in her voice. "Good choice. Smart. Means you're thinking about the long game." She gave them a dazzling, predatory smile. "So, the real question is... are you lot recruiting?"

The question hung in the air, a test and an offer all in one. Bella and Carol both looked at Jace. This wasn't a tactical decision; it was a leadership one.

Jace stepped forward, his body automatically positioning himself slightly in front of the others. He felt the woman's gaze on him, a palpable weight. He swallowed hard, the sudden dryness in his throat a stark reminder of his Dreg roots. This was a whole different league of interaction.

"Not just yet," he said, his voice coming out steadier than he felt, muffled slightly by his new helmet. He was respectful, but firm. "We're noobs here. Still figuring things out." He gave a slight nod of his head. "But thank you for offering."

It was the perfect answer. Humble, but not weak. Cautious, but not fearful. It acknowledged her status without diminishing their own.

Seraphina's smile widened, a flicker of genuine respect in her twilight eyes. "A smart leader, too. Good." She tapped a single, elegant finger on the pommel of her blade. "The name's Seraphina. When you're done 'figuring things out' and decide you want to run with a real crew, find me. The Unseen are always looking for talent that knows how to think."

She had just confirmed Kaelan's faction. They were being scouted.

With a final, lingering look that was equal parts appraisal and warning, she gave a casual salute and melted back into the crowds of the plaza, leaving the three of them standing in the sudden, quiet aftermath of their first real political encounter in the city of Ascendants.

The moment Seraphina was out of earshot, the tension that had gripped them finally broke. Jace let out a slow, shaky breath, the encounter having been more stressful than any monster fight.

Carol, however, was not about to let the moment pass. She sidled up to Jace, her helmet tilted, and nudged him with her elbow.

"You could have added one to our Harem, Casanova," she smirked, her voice a teasing whisper that echoed just between the three of them through the Nexus Stone's link.

Jace, who had just managed to regain his composure, visibly flinched. He looked from the spot where the stunning Level 15 Nightblade had stood, then back to Carol's amused, visor-clad face.

He shook his head, a weary, almost paternal sigh escaping his lips. "If we *do* add another," he said, his voice a low, serious rumble that completely shut down her teasing, "it won't be because she's powerful or looks good."

His gaze shifted to Bella, a silent acknowledgment of her role, then back to Carol.

"We could really use a healer," he stated, his mind instantly shifting from social anxiety to pure, pragmatic strategy. "Someone who can mend bones so Bella doesn't have to waste mana on barriers. Someone who can cure poisons so my [Toxin Conversion] isn't our only line of defense."

He turned and began walking toward the central plaza, his new armor clanking with a heavy, purposeful rhythm.

"It takes more than a pretty face to stoke my libido," he finished, the words a blunt, almost brutal declaration of his priorities. His focus wasn't on romance or reputation. It was on survival. On building the perfect, unbreakable team.

Bella and Carol exchanged a look behind his back. Carol's smirk faded, replaced by a look of profound, grudging respect. Bella just gave a single, almost imperceptible nod of approval.

He was still a fool. He was still their Casanova. But he was, without a single doubt, their leader. And he had his priorities straight.
