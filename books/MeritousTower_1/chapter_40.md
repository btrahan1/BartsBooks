# Chapter 40: The Trial Run

They were back in the Sky-Reaches, the howling wind a familiar, menacing song. Lia huddled behind a weathered stone pillar, her heart hammering against her ribs. The battle was a blur of motion and violence, just a few feet away.

Jace was a fortress, his Sentinel's Vigil shield taking the brunt of a Harpy Matriarch's furious claw-strikes. His [Aura of Dread] made the lesser Harpies hesitant, their attacks sloppy. Carol was a whirlwind of crimson and steel, her Heartwood blade flashing as she dueled a particularly large Harpy Striker, trading blows with a joyful ferocity.

It was working. They were a machine. But they were taking damage. Small cuts, glancing blows, the inevitable price of combat.

Carol grunted as the Striker's claws slipped past her parry, tearing a gash in her arm. Her health, which Bella could see in her party interface, dipped.

**[Carol HP: 60%]**

"Now," Bella whispered, her voice a calm, steady anchor in Lia’s ear. She hadn't moved from her spot beside the terrified healer. "Carol is at 60% health. Tap her off. Siphon from Jace."

Lia peeked around the pillar. Jace was at nearly full health, his [Internal Furnace] already regenerating the minor damage he'd taken. The concept was sound, but her hands were shaking.

"I... I can't," she stammered. "If I start casting, they'll see me. They'll come for me."

"Don't worry about aggro," Bella reassured her, her voice never wavering. "Jace has a taunt. If those mobs so much as *look* your way, he will make them regret it. Trust the Sentinel."

Trust. The word echoed in Lia’s mind. These people, this team, they trusted each other implicitly. They were asking her to do the same.

She took a deep, shaky breath. She closed her eyes, focusing her intent. She reached out with her mind, not with fear, but with the pure, clinical purpose of her class. She felt Jace's deep, steady reservoir of vitality, a calm, warm ocean of life force. She felt Carol's, a smaller, more volatile pool, currently draining.

She became the bridge.

A thin, ethereal tendril of pale green light flowed from Jace's back, invisible to the enemy. It connected to Lia, then branched out, a second tendril reaching across the battlefield and touching Carol's shoulder.

**[Vitality Transfer: Active]**

Jace felt a slight, almost pleasant coolness, a faint drain on his massive health pool. Carol, in the middle of a parry, felt a sudden, invigorating warmth spread through her body, knitting the gash on her arm closed, her fatigue washing away.

**[Carol HP: 60%... 70%... 80%...]**

It worked. She was doing it. She was healing in the middle of a fight.

One of the lesser Harpies, noticing the green glow around Carol, let out a piercing shriek and broke off its attack on Jace, turning toward the source of the magic—Lia.

Lia's eyes flew open in terror.

Before the Harpy could take two steps, the crimson eye on Jace's shield flared with a baleful, commanding light.

**[Gaze of the Warden: Active!]**

The Harpy froze mid-stride, its shriek cut off. Its head snapped back toward Jace as if pulled by an invisible chain, its mind completely enslaved by the magical taunt. It let out a confused, enraged screech and charged the Sentinel, completely forgetting the healer it had been about to attack.

Jace met the charge with a contemptuous shield bash that sent the creature tumbling.

Lia stared, her heart in her throat. Bella was right. She was safe. The Sentinel was her shield, just as he was for the others.

A new feeling bloomed in her chest, pushing aside the fear. Confidence.

She held the channel, pouring Jace's vitality into Carol until she was back at full health. For the first time, she wasn't a liability cowering in the back. She was a part of the machine. She was a healer.

The trial run turned into a full-blown clearing operation. Fight after fight, Lia's confidence grew, and with it, her skill. The initial, paralyzing fear of drawing aggro was replaced by a cool, calculated understanding of the battlefield's rhythm. She started to get the hang of it.

She learned to "manage her aggro." She stopped waiting for Bella’s commands and began to anticipate the flow of battle herself. She learned that a sudden, massive heal created a huge spike of threat. Instead, she became a master of subtlety. She would start channeling her [Vitality Transfer] when someone dropped to around 70% health, and she would only heal them to 90%, leaving Jace’s passive regeneration to handle the rest. This created a smaller, less noticeable "healing footprint," often going completely unnoticed by the mobs focused on the whirlwind of steel and fury at the front.

She also learned to use the environment. She’d position herself behind cover, breaking the line of sight from the mobs, and channel her healing from safety. She started to think like a member of the team, not just a passenger.

During a particularly intense fight against a trio of Crystal Scorpions on Floor 13, Carol took a heavy hit that poisoned her, her health dropping rapidly.

Lia didn't panic.

While Jace locked down two of the scorpions with his shield and Bella provided covering fire, Lia focused. She saw Carol's health plummeting past the 50% mark. She needed a big heal, now. But a big heal meant big aggro.

She made a split-second decision. She channeled a massive surge of vitality from Jace, but she didn't just heal Carol. She split the stream, sending a smaller, secondary trickle to Jace himself, who was uninjured. The System, registering a heal on the person with the highest threat, got confused. The scorpion fighting Carol, which had been about to turn on Lia, hesitated for a crucial second, its primitive mind unable to decide which target was the true source of the healing.

In that second of hesitation, Carol chugged an anti-venom potion and buried her Heartwood blade in the scorpion's head.

The battle was won. As the last scorpion fell, a bright, chiming sound, a sound Lia hadn't heard in what felt like a lifetime, echoed in her mind.

**[Your understanding of battlefield medicine and threat management has deepened through practical application.]**
**[Skill Leveled Up: Vitality Transfer (Lvl 2)]**
**[New Perk Unlocked: Triage.]**
*You can now split your healing stream between two targets. The secondary stream operates at 30% effectiveness.*

Lia stared at the notification, her eyes wide with a joy and pride she had forgotten she could feel. She was getting pretty good, and her skill had leveled for the first time in ages.

She looked up at the team, who were giving her nods of impressed approval. A genuine, confident smile spread across her face. She wasn't the clumsy, shy girl from the workshop anymore. She was Lia the Mender, the Field Chirurgeon of Team Casanova. And she was right where she belonged.

They were back in Sanctuary, the bustling town on Floor 11 feeling more like home with every return trip. Their latest haul from the upper floors had been substantial, and they were at the System Store, their Merit Point balance once again looking healthy.

This time, their focus was on Lia.

While Jace was at the blacksmith's, using their collected materials to upgrade their Warden's armor, and Carol was browsing the latest weapon enchantments, Bella and Lia were deep in the 'Skill Tomes' section of the store.

"We need to augment your healing," Bella said, her finger tracing down the holographic list of available skills. "Your [Vitality Transfer] is perfect for single-target burst healing, but you have no way to handle sustained, party-wide damage."

"What is a HOT?" Carol asked, having wandered over, her curiosity piqued by a term she'd seen on the list.

Bella's eyes lit up with analytical zeal. "A Heal-Over-Time effect. It's a low-mana, 'fire-and-forget' spell. Instead of a single, large heal, it applies a buff that heals the target for a smaller amount every second for a set duration. It's incredibly efficient for managing predictable damage." She pointed to a specific, glowing tome on the virtual shelf.

**[Skill Tome: Mending Aura]**
*   **[Type: Heal-Over-Time]**
*   **[Class Requirement: Healer-type]**
*   **[Description: Teaches the user [Mending Aura]. You imbue an ally with a gentle, persistent aura of restorative energy.]**
*   **[Effect: Target regenerates 2% of their maximum HP every 3 seconds for 30 seconds. This effect can stack up to two times.]**
**[Cost: 750 MP]**

Carol's eyes went wide. "Ohhh... nice," she breathed, instantly grasping the tactical advantage. "So... before a big fight, she could cast this on me and Jace. We'd walk in already regenerating health, on top of Jace's furnace. We'd be practically unkillable."

Lia stared at the skill description, her mind racing. With this, she wouldn't have to constantly manage their health bars. She could apply the aura as a buffer, a safety net, freeing her up to focus her direct healing on whoever was taking the most damage. It would change her entire playstyle.

"You definitely want that!" Carol said, nudging Lia with a grin.

Lia looked at the price tag, her face falling. "750 Merit... that's almost everything I have."

Before she could even finish the thought, Jace walked over, wiping grease from his hands. He'd overheard the conversation. "Don't worry about it," he said, his voice a low rumble. He tapped the central console, accessing the Team Casanova funds.

**[Mending Aura has been purchased. -750 MP]**

The tome materialized in the air. Jace simply nodded at Lia. "Buy it. We need it."

Lia looked at the three of them—at Jace's simple, unquestioning support, at Carol's enthusiastic encouragement, at Bella's logical approval. This wasn't just her buying a skill for herself. This was the team investing in their collective strength.

With a trembling, grateful hand, she reached out and absorbed the tome. The knowledge flowed into her, warm and empowering. She could feel the new spell settling into her mind, a gentle, persistent hum of restorative power. She now had a proactive tool, not just a reactive one. She was becoming a true master of her craft.

The next few runs through Floors 12 and 13 were a masterclass in efficiency. Lia, now armed with her [Mending Aura], had transformed. Before every major engagement, she would cast the gentle, persistent healing buff on Jace and Carol. They would enter battles already regenerating, a constant, reassuring trickle of green numbers ticking upwards on their health bars.

This freed her up immensely. She was no longer just a reactive health battery; she became a proactive force multiplier. She learned to time her [Vitality Transfers] with Bella's [Exploit Weakness] calls, ensuring Carol was always at peak health right before she needed to unleash a massive burst of damage. She even started using her Triage perk to keep JJ topped off, making the little Glimmer Vole a more durable and effective distraction.

They stood now at the entrance to the boss chamber of Floor 13, a cavern of jagged, obsidian spires where a massive, winged demon with lightning crackling between its horns—the **Voltaic Abomination**—slumbered on a throne of black glass.

They had done their reconnaissance. Bella had a complete schematic of its attack patterns. Carol had a duel strategy mapped out. Jace had identified the best choke points to anchor the fight. But this time, the final, crucial piece of their strategy was Lia.

Jace turned to her, his helmet off, his expression serious but filled with a quiet confidence. The shy, fumbling girl he had met in the workshop was gone, replaced by a calm, focused member of his team.

"Alright," Jace said, his voice a steady, reassuring rumble. "I think you've got the process down, Lia. I think it's time we took on the Boss."

He looked her directly in the eye, his gaze unwavering.

"You up for it?"

It wasn't a command. It was a question. An offer of trust. He was making her an equal partner in this, the most dangerous fight of her life.

Lia looked past him at the slumbering demon, its form crackling with immense, destructive power. The old Lia would have run. She would have dissolved into a puddle of fear.

But this was the new Lia. Lia the Mender. Healer of Team Casanova.

She took a deep breath, the familiar, gentle hum of her [Mending Aura] already wrapping around Jace and Carol. She looked at her three teammates—her tank, her tactician, her champion—and felt not fear, but a profound sense of belonging.

A small, determined smile touched her lips.

"I'm ready."
