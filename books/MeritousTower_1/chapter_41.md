# Chapter 41: The Abomination Fight

The moment they stepped across the threshold, the Voltaic Abomination’s eyes snapped open, blazing with arcs of pure, white lightning. It let out a roar that was the sound of a thunderstorm condensed into a single, deafening peal.

But Team Casanova was already in motion, their plan unfolding with the precision of a master clockwork.

At the start of the battle, Lia, positioned safely at the back of the chamber behind a spire of obsidian, did not panic. She calmly cast her [Mending Aura] on Jace, the gentle green buff instantly offsetting the crackling, ambient lightning damage the boss was already putting out. Then, she did something new. She cast the second stack of the Heal-over-Time on herself. A faint, reassuring warmth enveloped her, a safety net of her own making.

Then, Lia sat down, crossed her legs, and began to meditate, a recent skill she had picked up from a tome in the Sanctuary store. Her eyes closed, her breathing deepened, and her entire focus turned inward.

**[Skill Active: Manafont Meditation]**
*   **[Effect: While meditating, you cannot move or cast other spells, but your Mana Regeneration is increased by 300%.]**

Up at the front, Jace planted his shield, the [Gaze of the Warden] flaring to life and locking the Abomination's furious attention onto him. The beast charged, its claws wreathed in lightning.

Carol, already buffed and regenerating, flashed a grin. She looked back at the meditating Lia, a picture of serene, untouchable calm in the heart of the storm.

"Chop chop," Carol yelled over the roar of the thunder, her voice filled with a joyful, battle-hungry confidence. "Daylight's wasting!"

She charged, her crimson cloak billowing behind her, ready to dance with the storm. The plan was in motion. Lia was the battery, charging her power for the burst healing she knew would be needed later. Jace was the lightning rod, absorbing the raw fury of the beast. And Carol was the storm itself, ready to unleash a torrent of steel and fire. The fight for Floor 13 had begun.

This Boss was tough.

It wasn't a puzzle like the Golem or a psychological test like Carol's redemption. The Voltaic Abomination was a pure, unadulterated wall of stats. It hit like a freight train, moved with shocking speed, and its passive lightning aura meant that the entire party was constantly taking damage.

This was a battle of attrition, a grueling symphony of managed decay. It was a test of health management, and aggro management, and mana management.

And Bella was on top of it all.

From her position at the rear, her Grimoire floating open beside her, she was a battlefield conductor. The Nexus Stone had transformed from a communication device into her command-and-control center.

*<"Jace, health at 50 percent, shield taking heavy damage,"> her thought was a calm, precise broadcast. <"Lia, channel a 30 percent transfer. Now.">*

Lia, who had finished her meditation, didn't hesitate. A stream of green vitality flowed from Jace's already-regenerating pool, shoring up the damage from a massive claw swipe. Jace grunted, planting his shield, the impact barely fazing him now that his health was stable.

The Abomination roared and unleashed a new attack: a bolt of chain lightning that arced off Jace's shield and slammed into Lia.

*<"Aggro spike on the healer!"> Bella's warning was instantaneous. <"Carol, [Duel]! Pull its attention for five seconds!"> *

Carol was already moving. "Hey, ugly!" she roared, her Valiant title flaring as she locked the beast into a one-on-one challenge. The Abomination, forced by the System, turned its furious gaze on her, forgetting the healer completely.

*<"Lia, your mana is at 40 percent,"> Bella projected. <"Stop all HoTs. Direct transfers only. Conserve your pool for the burn phase. Carol, your duel is ending in three... two... Jace, [Gaze of the Warden]! Re-establish threat! NOW!"> *

Jace’s shield flared, and the Abomination, its duel with Carol ending, was immediately locked back onto him, its attention flawlessly managed and passed between the two tanks.

The fight dragged on, a brutal, ten-minute grind. Their new armor was the only thing keeping them in the fight, absorbing dozens of hits that would have been fatal otherwise. But resources were dwindling.

Then, it happened. The Abomination’s horns began to glow with a blinding intensity. Its health was low. It was entering its final, desperate phase.

*<"ENRAGE PHASE! IT'S GOING TO UNLEASH A FIELD-WIDE NOVA! THIS IS IT!"> Bella's mind was a fortress of calm in the rising storm. <"Lia, dump every last point of your mana into Jace. Get him to absolute full. Carol, get behind him! JJ, on my mark!"> *

Lia poured her remaining energy into Jace, his health bar hitting 100% just as the Abomination threw its head back and unleashed a blinding, room-filling explosion of pure lightning.

Jace roared, planting his Sentinel's Vigil shield into the obsidian floor. He took the full, cataclysmic blast. His health plummeted—90, 70, 50, 30 percent—but he did not move. He was the lightning rod. He was the wall. He held.

The blast subsided, leaving the Abomination momentarily spent, steam rising from its horns.

*<"Its core is exposed! Blind it, JJ!"> *

JJ, who had been hiding behind Bella, zipped out and unleashed his [Crystalline Flash], a blinding, kaleidoscopic burst of light that struck the exhausted demon head-on.

*<"NOW!"> Bella's final command was a psychic scream. <"[Exploit Weakness] active! Its core! BURN IT!"> *

A massive, glowing red rune appeared on the Abomination's chest. Carol, already charging, her cloak blazing with maxed-out [Momentum] stacks, and Jace, pushing off the floor from his defensive stance, struck at the exact same moment.

Their combined, perfectly timed, critically-boosted assault shattered the creature's core in a single, explosive instant.

The battle was won. They stood panting in the silent chamber, their health and mana bars nearly empty. But they were alive. They were victorious.

Bella closed her Grimoire, a small, satisfied smile on her face. No worries.

They stood in the echoing silence of the boss chamber, the smell of ozone and burnt obsidian thick in the air. Lia was on her knees, completely drained of mana but alive. Carol was leaning on her sword, panting but grinning. Jace stood firm, his shield arm trembling from the effort of weathering the final blast.

It was in this moment of quiet, hard-won victory that the Spire's voice descended, not with a simple announcement, but with a proclamation that carried an undertone of genuine, systemic surprise.

**[ATTENTION!]**

**[FOR THE FIRST TIME IN OVER 10 YEARS, THE VOLTAIC ABOMINATION HAS BEEN DEFEATED BY A PARTY OF FEWER THAN 5 MEMBERS!]**

The four of them exchanged a look of stunned surprise. They had known the fight was hard, but they hadn't realized they were attempting something that hadn't been done in a decade.

The voice of the Spire continued, its tone shifting to one of profound respect.

**[TEAM CASANOVA HAS SHATTERED THE PREVIOUS RECORD. THEY HAVE RECEIVED THE TITLE OF "OVERACHIEVERS!"]**

A new, shared title materialized in their party status screen, glowing with a brilliant, almost defiant light.

**[Party Title: The Overachievers]**
*   **[Effect: Your team has proven its ability to overcome challenges far beyond the expected parameters. When fighting any 'Boss' or 'Elite' type enemy, all party members gain a 5% bonus to all stats.]**

Jace let out a low, shaky laugh of disbelief. A permanent, 5% stat buff for every major fight from now on. The reward was absurd. It was a testament to their flawless execution, a prize for not just winning, but for winning *impossibly*.

"Well," Carol said, finally catching her breath and straightening up. She looked at the new title, a wide, predatory grin spreading across her face. "I guess we're not just famous anymore."

Bella, ever the analyst, was already processing the long-term implications. "This buff... it's a compounding advantage. The stronger we get, the more powerful the bonus becomes. The System is actively rewarding our efficiency."

Jace just shook his head, a feeling of profound, almost overwhelming pride washing over him. He looked at his team. The brilliant Analyst who was their rock and their guide. The peerless Valiant who was their sword. The quiet Mender who was their lifeline.

"Overachievers," he said, the word tasting of victory. "Yeah. I can live with that."

They had come a long way from the Silt and the alleys. They weren't just climbers anymore. They were record-breakers. And the entire Spire knew it.
