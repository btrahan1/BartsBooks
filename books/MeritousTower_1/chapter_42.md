# Chapter 42: Stacking Buffs

They were back in their preferred corner of the Laughing Hydra, the sounds of Sanctuary a comforting, familiar backdrop to their post-battle recovery. The victory over the Voltaic Abomination felt different. It was a milestone, a definitive statement of their power and synergy. The new "Overachievers" title was a tangible mark of their elite status.

Jace took a long drink from a heavy wooden mug of ale, the cool liquid a welcome relief. He slammed the mug down on the table, a thoughtful, calculating glint in his eyes.

"I've been thinking," he began, the words immediately drawing the others' full attention. "Since we're overachievers and all..." He said the word with a self-deprecating grin, but there was a serious undercurrent to his tone. "You know what we need?"

He leaned forward, his voice dropping conspiratorially. "Buffs. Not just Lia's HoTs or Carol's courage aura. I'm talking about stacking buffs. Like... potion buffs, and food buffs, and item buffs. Like rings and necklaces and bracers and stuff!"

He looked around the table, his enthusiasm growing. "We've been focusing on our core gear—armor and weapons. But the veterans out there? They're covered in enchantments. They glow. We need to start thinking about the small things. The one-percent advantages that add up."

He pointed a thumb at his own chest. "I can cook... sort of!" he admitted, recalling the greasy but life-saving rat meat from Floor 1. "My [Jury-Rigging] skill is mostly for armor, but I bet I can make some stat-boosting food if we get the right ingredients. Bella, your alchemy is already giving us potions of haste. What else can you make? Lia, can your enchanting skill be used on rings?"

Bella's eyes lit up, her mind already racing through the combinatorial possibilities. "The concept is sound," she confirmed, pulling out her Grimoire. "Stacking temporary, non-conflicting buffs is a cornerstone of high-level raiding strategy. My alchemy primer has recipes for potions that boost Strength or Intelligence for a short duration. If we combine those with food that boosts Constitution or Stamina..."

"...then we walk into a boss fight with a 10% or even 15% stat advantage before the first punch is thrown," Jace finished, his grin widening. "On top of our Overachiever bonus."

Carol, who had been listening intently, leaned forward, her expression alight with a new kind of hunger. "So, we're not just going to out-fight them and out-think them. We're going to out-prepare them, too?"

"Exactly," Jace said. "We're going to walk into every fight so buffed up, so overpowered, that the boss will feel like *it's* the one who got ambushed."

The idea settled over the table, a new, exciting layer to their strategy. They had mastered teamwork, tactics, and gear. Now, it was time to master the art of preparation. It was time to become true overachievers.

The workshop had become their second home. The rhythmic *clang* of Jace's blacksmithing hammer was the new heartbeat of their team, a steady, industrious sound that set the pace for their preparations.

Jace was at a heavy iron stove, a new addition to his crafting repertoire. He was focused intently on a sizzling pan, a stack of slightly lopsided, golden-brown griddlecakes resting on a nearby plate. The air was filled with the surprisingly pleasant smell of roasted nuts and sweet syrup.

"So... it seems to me like this 4-man group could be a thing," Jace mused aloud, skillfully flipping what looked like a pancake. He'd been cross-referencing Bella's Grimoire with his new cooking skills. These weren't just pancakes; they were [Ironcakes], a recipe that granted a small, temporary buff to Constitution.

Carol, sharpening her Heartwood blade at a nearby whetstone, looked up. "Yeah," she agreed, her voice thoughtful. "The System made a big deal about us being 'fewer than 5'. It seems like we could get some more titles if we stick with 4. A smaller, elite strike team." She paused, then shot a pointed, teasing look across the workshop at Jace. "So... JACE... stop scouting the hot babes!"

She went back to her task, a satisfied smirk on her face. Jace just grumbled, his focus entirely on not burning breakfast.

Bella was at her alchemy station, carefully grinding a rare, glowing crystal into a fine powder. She was attempting a Potion of Major Intellect, a complex and expensive recipe. Her focus was absolute.

Nearby, Lia was practicing her new craft. She sat at a small, well-lit desk, her brow furrowed in concentration. She was trying to weave a simple enchantment—a minor stamina regeneration effect—into a leather bracer Jace had made. A small, glowing needle of pure mana danced between her fingers as she guided a silver thread through the leather. It was delicate, intricate work, a perfect fit for her patient, meticulous nature.

She was so focused that she hadn't really been listening to the banter. But a single, strange word had caught her ear. She looked up from her enchanting, her large, innocent eyes blinking.

"What's a harem?"

The question, spoken in her soft, genuinely curious voice, dropped into the industrious atmosphere of the workshop like a stone into a silent pond.

The rhythmic grinding of Carol's whetstone came to a sudden, screeching halt.

Bella's hand, holding a delicate glass vial, froze mid-air.

Jace, who had been about to plate another perfect Ironcake, fumbled his spatula, the pancake flying through the air and landing on his head with a soft *splat*.

Silence.

The three of them turned to look at Lia, who was just sitting there, looking back at them with a completely straight, inquisitive face.

Carol was the first to recover, a slow, wicked, and utterly merciless grin spreading across her face. She looked at Jace, who was now frantically trying to remove the sticky, syrup-covered cake from his hair.

"Well now, Casanova," Carol purred, her voice dripping with pure, unadulterated amusement. "It seems the newest member of your... *collection*... has a question for you."

The silence in the workshop stretched, thick with a mixture of Lia's innocent curiosity and the monumental weight of Jace's impending doom. Carol was practically vibrating with amusement, and Bella was watching the scene with the detached, analytical interest of a scientist about to witness a fascinating social explosion.

Jace, with the warm, sticky Ironcake still slowly sliding down the side of his face, knew he had to say something. Running was not an option this time. He was trapped.

"Well..." he began, his voice cracking slightly. He cleared his throat, trying to project an air of worldly knowledge he absolutely did not possess. "You see... a harem is more of a 'concept' than a real thing."

He peeled the pancake off his head, trying to sound as academic and detached as possible, as if he were explaining a complex boss mechanic.

"So, uh... it's kind of like," he continued, desperately searching his brain for a safe, non-incriminating analogy, "when a group of people decide to be... exclusive. Like in teamwork."

He looked at Lia, who was listening with rapt attention, her brow furrowed in concentration.

"Ummm... they don't work with other teams," he stammered, feeling the burning intensity of both Carol and Bella's stares. "And stuff. They, uh, pool all their resources. Share all their loot. It's a... a very, very tight-knit party structure." He finished lamely, hoping the sheer, boring mediocrity of his explanation would be enough to defuse the situation.

Lia considered this for a moment, her head tilted. "Oh," she said, a look of dawning understanding on her face. "So... we're a harem?"

Carol let out a choked sound that was halfway between a laugh and a snort of derision.

"YES!" Jace said, far too quickly and loudly. He seized on her innocent conclusion like a drowning man grabbing a life raft. "Exactly! We are! Because we're an exclusive, four-person team. That's what it means."

He looked around, a bead of sweat tracing a clean line through the soot on his forehead. "See? It's just a System term for an elite strike team. A weird one, but yeah. That's all it is."

Lia's face brightened with a genuine, happy smile. "Oh, okay! That makes sense. I like being in a harem!"

She then turned back to her enchanting, her concentration restored, leaving the other three in the wake of her innocent acceptance of Jace's disastrously clumsy explanation.

Jace met Carol's gaze. Her eyes were narrowed, and she was shaking her head slowly, a look of profound, mocking pity on her face. Bella just sighed, a long, weary sound, and went back to her alchemy, deciding that some things were simply beyond the scope of logical intervention.

Jace had survived. Barely. And in doing so, he had accidentally solidified "harem" as their official, in-universe team descriptor.
