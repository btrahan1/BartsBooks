# Chapter 6: The Gatekeeper

The entrance to the Gatekeeper’s arena was a collapsed archway, a maw of crumbling stone leading into a wide, sunken cistern. The floor was a shallow pool of black water, and in the center, a huge, moss-covered platform of rock rose like an altar.

"Up there," Jace whispered, pointing to a ledge halfway up the cistern wall, shrouded in thick, hanging vines. "Good sightline. Good cover."

Getting there was a silent, vertical climb, using cracks in the ancient stonework as handholds. Jace went first, testing each grip. Bella followed, her smaller frame fitting easily into the crevices. They settled into the nest of vines, a perfect sniper's perch overlooking the entire arena. From here, they were just another patch of shadow.

"You're the eyes, I'm the knife," Jace murmured, laying the rusted rebar beside him. "You watch the Highborn. You watch the monster. You find the seam. Don't tell me to strike until you're absolutely sure they've done all the work for us."

Bella nodded, her knuckles white as she gripped a root. Her fear was a cold stone in her stomach, but her mind was buzzing, a current of pure, focused energy. She activated [Weakness Detection].

They didn't have to wait long.

As the first true rays of dawn pierced the mist, Valerius's group marched into the cistern. They moved like a well-oiled machine. Marcus, the big tank, was in front, holding a makeshift shield he’d fashioned from the shell of a giant swamp turtle. Elara, the healer, and two others with sharpened sticks stood in the back. Valerius was in the center, his driftwood branch held like a king's scepter.

"Standard formation!" Valerius shouted, his voice echoing in the stone chamber. "Marcus, provoke it! Ranged, watch for the bile sacs! Elara, focus heals on Marcus only!"

A deep, guttural croak reverberated from the central platform. The water churned. A creature of nightmare hoisted itself onto the rock. It was a toad the size of a carriage, its skin a warty landscape of green and brown pustules. This was the **Bile-Gorged Croaker**.

The fight began.

Marcus slammed his shield and roared, drawing the beast's attention. The Croaker's throat swelled, and it spat a glob of sizzling green acid. Marcus caught it on his shield, which smoked and hissed, its durability plummeting.

From their perch, Bella watched, her focus absolute.

"The joints," she whispered to Jace. "The armor on its back is thick, but the skin around its knees and elbows is stretched thin. They're hitting the shell. Wasting energy."

Jace just grunted, his eyes flicking between the monster and Valerius's group. He was studying their rhythm, their timing. He loaded a sharp, heavy stone into his sling, letting it rest in his lap.

The battle was a stalemate. Marcus would block, the others would dash in to stab at the creature's legs, and Elara would chant, a soft green light enveloping Marcus every time he took a hit. They were whittling it down, slowly, methodically, and arrogantly.

"They're getting tired," Jace observed. "Look at the big one. His shield arm is dropping."

"Wait," Bella said, her eyes narrowed. "Something's changing. The pustules on its back... they're glowing."

As if on cue, the Croaker let out a deafening roar and slammed its belly onto the platform. A wave of noxious gas erupted from the glowing pustules. The Highborn kids choked, stumbling back.

**[STATUS EFFECT: POISONED]**

"Now!" Valerius screamed. "It's vented its poison! Its defenses are weakest now! Full attack!"

They rallied, charging in for what they thought was the final push. They hacked and slashed, ignoring defense for pure offense. The Croaker’s health bar, visible only to them, must have been dropping into the red.

"Bella," Jace said, his voice deadly calm.

Bella’s gaze was locked on the beast. It was swaying now, bleeding from a dozen shallow wounds. Valerius was moving in, his stick held high for the final, glorious blow.

"The throat!" Bella hissed. "When it roared, it tore the skin under its jaw! It's a flap! They can't see it from their angle, but from up here... it's wide open!"

That was all Jace needed.

He stood up. He spun the sling once, twice, a humming blur of motion.

*Snap.*

The stone flew. It didn't fly at the monster. It flew at the wall right next to Valerius’s head.

*SMACK!*

The impact startled the Highborn leader for a split second. He flinched, turning toward the sound. That split second was an eternity.

Jace was already moving. He didn't climb down. He leaped. He dropped twenty feet, landing in a crouch in the shallow water, the impact jarring his bones. He ignored the pain, the *Internal Furnace* already working to repair the damage.

He was inside their perimeter.

"What—" Marcus started to turn.

Jace was a blur of motion. He sprinted past the tank, past the stunned Valerius. The Croaker was rearing its head back for a final, desperate attack.

And there it was. A pale, torn patch of skin under its chin. A kill-zone.

**[Cheap Shot Activated]**
**[Gutter-Fighting Activated]**

Jace didn't just stab. He jumped, planting a foot on the beast's warty leg and using it as a springboard. He drove the rusted rebar up and in, plunging it deep into the soft tissue of the Croaker’s throat.

The beast froze. A wet, gurgling sound was its only reply.

Jace put his entire body weight onto the bar, twisting it like a key.

**[CRITICAL HIT!]**
**[WEAKNESS EXPLOITED!]**

The light in the Croaker's eyes died. Its massive body slumped, crashing to the platform with a final, shuddering exhalation.

Silence.

Then, the System notification that only Jace could see, brighter and more glorious than any he had ever received.

**[FIRST KILL: GATEKEEPER - BILE-GORGED CROAKER]**
**[SERVER-WIDE ANNOUNCEMENT PENDING...]**
**[MASSIVE XP AWARDED!]**
**[LEVEL UP!]**
**[LEVEL UP!]**
**[LEVEL UP!]**

"You..." Valerius whispered, his face a mask of pure, unadulterated fury. "You... *DREG*!"

Jace ripped his rebar free. Behind the dead monster, a shimmering portal of blue light had begun to form—the entrance to Floor 2.

"Bella, run!" Jace screamed.

He didn't wait. He didn't look at the loot that was beginning to materialize from the monster's corpse. He turned and sprinted for the portal, splashing through the water as the Highborn kids began to scream in rage. He was the thief, and he had just committed the greatest heist on Floor 1.
