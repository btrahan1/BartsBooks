# Chapter 8: The Core

"Did you get the bonus to stats?" Jace asked, rolling his shoulder and feeling the new, profound sturdiness in his frame. "I got 5 to each. The Architect really liked that kill."

"I got 3 to each," Bella replied, a genuine smile on her face. "But you did the killing, so I am happy! It's more than fair."

Jace nodded, satisfied. He looked down at the sickly green orb pulsing in his palm. It felt like a living, angry thing. "Okay then. I'm going to try something. I'm going to absorb this, but I want you to hold it while I do."

Bella's eyes went wide. "What? Jace, no. The System said *you* could absorb it. It might hurt you! What if it hurts me?"

"We climb together, or not at all," Jace said, his voice leaving no room for argument. He held the core out. "You found the weakness. You grabbed the loot. Your hands were on the prize as much as mine were. I'm hoping we can share this somehow. Trick the System. Just... put your hands over mine. If it starts to hurt, let go."

Hesitantly, Bella knelt in front of him. She placed her small, cold hands over his, sandwiching the Bile-Core between their palms. The orb pulsed faster, its light intensifying.

Jace closed his eyes and focused his intent. He didn't just think it; he projected the thought at the System with all his will. *Absorb. And share.*

The reaction was immediate and violent.

A searing, toxic heat flooded Jace's body. It wasn't the clean burn of his *Internal Furnace*; this was a greasy, chemical fire. Green light shot up his veins, visible even through the skin of his arms. It felt like being injected with pure poison.

**[WARNING: ABSORBING UNREFINED CHAOTIC ENERGY.]**
**[Constitution Check: CHALLENGING...]**
**[CONSTITUTIONAL ANOMALY DETECTED: NULL-IRON BODY.]**
**[REFINING ENERGY...]**

Jace grunted, his teeth clenched so hard his jaw ached. His body was a crucible. The *Internal Furnace* roared to life, not just creating heat but *fighting* the toxic energy, burning away the impurities. He felt the poison warring with his own nature, trying to rot him from the inside out. He held on, enduring the agony because he could feel something else happening.

Through their connected hands, the energy was flowing into Bella, but it was different. She gasped, but not in pain.

For her, the energy wasn't hot and toxic; it was cool and clean. A wave of mental clarity washed over her, sharpening the edges of the world. The grey stone of the chamber seemed to resolve into a million more shades. The faint hum of the portal behind them separated into distinct frequencies. It was like a thick fog had been lifted from her mind.

The green light in Jace’s veins flickered, and a trickle of pure, silver energy flowed from his palms into hers. He was filtering the chaos.

The process lasted ten agonizing seconds for Jace and ten enlightening seconds for Bella. Then, the Bile-Core crumbled into dust between their hands.

Jace collapsed backward, steam pouring off his body. His skin had a faint greenish tint that slowly faded back to its normal color.

Bella scrambled over to him. "Jace! Are you okay?"

"Peachy," he rasped, a single bead of green sweat rolling down his temple. He sat up, feeling a profound, deep-seated resilience he'd never known. The ache in his bones from the jump was gone. He felt... solid.

A cascade of notifications filled their vision.

**Jace's Log:**
**[Bile-Core successfully refined and absorbed.]**
**[Constitution +15]**
**[New Skill Unlocked: Toxin Conversion (Passive)]**
*Description: Your Internal Furnace can now render most biological poisons into pure Stamina.*
*Effect: 50% of Poison Damage is converted into Stamina Regeneration.*
**[New Title Gained: The Crucible]**
*Description: You have willingly taken pain upon yourself to purify power for an ally.*

**Bella's Log:**
**[Received Refined Energy via 'The Crucible']**
**[Intelligence +8]**
**[Willpower +4]**
**[New Skill Unlocked: Purity of Thought (Passive)]**
*Description: Your mind, washed by refined energy, is resistant to corruption and confusion.*
*Effect: +20% Resistance to Mental Status Effects (Confusion, Fear, Charm). Your [Weakness Detection] skill can now occasionally spot non-physical weaknesses, such as flaws in an enemy's attack pattern.*

Jace stared at his notifications, then looked at Bella, whose eyes were wide with wonder.

"It worked," Bella whispered, touching her head. "Jace, it's like... I can think clearer than ever before. I can see the dust motes in the air."

Jace pushed himself to his feet. He felt rooted to the floor, an unmovable object. He had taken the raw power and physical agony, and in return, he had become a fortress. Bella had received the distilled essence, and it had honed her mind into a razor.

"See?" Jace said, offering her a hand up. "Brains and Brawn. We just made it official."
