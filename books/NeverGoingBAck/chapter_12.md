# Chapter 12: The Introduction

The silence stretched, tight as a piano wire. Sal’s grip on his clipboard tightened. The three men behind the driver shifted, waiting for the signal.

Then, the driver laughed.

 It wasn't a cruel laugh. It was loud, open, and broke the tension like a dropped glass.

"Hey...," the driver said, holding up his hands, palms open. "Relax, Amigo! We didn't come here to start a war."

He stepped closer, the aggression in his posture melting into a swagger. "We just wanted to introduce ourselves to the new 'homie' in the neighborhood. Word travels fast, you know? A new face shows up, people get curious."

Pedro didn't move immediately. He breathed in slowly, smelling the exhaust of the Charger and the cheap cologne the driver wore. He exhaled. He was taking it all in—the eyes, the hands, the way the other three men relaxed their shoulders the moment the boss laughed.

The threat was gone. The test remained.

Pedro stepped forward. He extended his hand.

The driver took it. It wasn't a business handshake. Pedro rotated his wrist, locking thumbs, sliding into the forearm grip—the standard "prison" greeting. It was intimate and firm, a code that said *I know who you are, and I know who I am.*

Pedro pulled the man in for a quick, hard hug and a pat on the back, then released him.

"Thanks for checking in on me," Pedro said, his voice calm, reframing the intimidation tactic into a neighborly visit. "It's good to know there is a tight community in this town. People looking out for each other."

The driver blinked, surprised by the pivot. He looked at Pedro with a newfound respect. "Yeah. Something like that."

Pedro turned slightly, gesturing to the man standing guard by the office door.

"I'm Pedro," he said. "And this man right here is my boss. His name is Sal."

Sal nodded, his face stoic.

"He's been good to me," Pedro continued, looking the driver in the eye. "He gave me a shot when nobody else would. He's solid. He's one of us."

*One of us.* The phrase hung in the air. It claimed Sal. It put him under the umbrella of protection.

"Sal," the driver nodded, more respectfully this time. "Good to meet you."

Pedro walked over to the Charger. He rested a hand on the warm hood, claiming the space.

"If you want a place that you can trust to do good work on your baby," Pedro said, tapping the fender. "Honest work. No games. You bring it here. I guarantee it."

The driver watched him. He looked at his crew, then back at Pedro. The "Ghost" wasn't just a mechanic; he was a diplomat.

"You guarantee it, huh?" the driver asked, a smirk playing on his lips.

"I put my name on it," Pedro said. "NGB."

The driver nodded slowly. "Alright. We might just do that."

Pedro smiled. It was the first time he had smiled at them.

"Speaking of which," Pedro said, leaning in slightly. "You got a little valve tick on the passenger side bank. Mind if I give it a once over, free of charge? While you wait?"

The driver looked at his car, then at Pedro. He tossed the keys.

"Knock yourself out, Homie."
