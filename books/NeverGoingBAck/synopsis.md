After five years in prison for a joyride that turned into a felony, Pedro Rodriguez is finally free. Stepping out into the blinding Texas sun with forty-two dollars and a clear plastic bag of possessions, Pedro has a simple list of goals: a shower without shoes, a real steak, and the dignity of paying rent. Most of all, he wants to never go back. But the world outside can be as unforgiving as the world inside, and Pedro must fight to remain the man he wants to become. A gritty, realistic story of redemption and starting over.
