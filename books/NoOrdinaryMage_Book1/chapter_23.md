# Chapter 23: The Efficiency of Synergy

Tobin stood by the well, his hands gripping the iron-banded rims of the water barrels. It was his third day of this specific chore, and usually, by the second barrel, his shoulders would be screaming. 

But as he heaved the first barrel toward the bakery, something was fundamentally different. 

"How can the barrels be easier to carry just because I have more intelligence?" Tobin muttered to himself, his brow furrowed in concentration.

It wasn't that his muscles were stronger—his Strength was still 11, and he hadn't earned a new point since the bakery job started. But his '13' in Intelligence was acting like a high-performance engine tucked inside his skull. Every step he took was now preceded by a lightning-fast calculation. He wasn't just walking; he was optimizing his center of gravity. He was adjusting the tension in his core to offset the dynamic slosh of the water. He was finding the exact angle of his ankles to minimize the impact on the grey slate.

By the time he delivered the fifth barrel, he wasn't even out of breath. He felt... efficient. Like a machine that had just been oiled and calibrated.

"You're faster today, ghost," the Baker grunted, wiping his floury hands on his apron. "And you didn't spill a drop. Not one. Did the promotion to '13' come with a side of grace?"

Tobin chuckled. "It came with a lot of math, Sir. Apparently, if you calculate the resonance of the water, it doesn't try to pull you over as much."

He didn't wait for a reward. He headed straight for the smithy.

Inside the forge, the heat was already a vibrant, cherry-red wall. Master Thurgood was holding a thick slab of ironwood-steel, his hammer ready. Tobin grabbed the bellows handle and began his slow, steady draw.

"How can pumping these bellows be easier?" he whispered as the fire roared into a white-hot intensity.

Again, it was the integration. With his Perception at 40% and his Intelligence at 13, he wasn't just pumping a handle; he was observing the thermal-transition of the charcoal. He was timing his draws to the exact millisecond when the oxygen would provide the most efficient burn. He was using the physics of momentum to let the weight of the handle do half the work for him.

"Synergy," Tobin said, the word clicking into place like a key in a lock. "It must be the synergy."

At the Empire’s Academy, the mages were taught that mental stats were for magic and physical stats were for labor. They were separate silos, divided by the System's specializations. A Warrior didn't need Intelligence to swing a sword, and a Mage didn't need Strength to cast a spell. The System provided the 'scaffolding' that bridged the gaps.

But for a NOM, there were no silos. There was only the territory. 

Because Tobin was unassisted, every part of him had to work with every other part. His high Intelligence wasn't just a reservoir for mana; it was a processor for his Strength. His Agility was a function of his Perception. Every attribute was a thread in a single, complex weave—the same kind of pattern he’d seen in Mistress Elara’s shop.

"Master Thurgood!" Tobin called out, his voice steady over the roar of the forge. "The fire is at 1800 degrees. The transition is stable."

Thurgood stopped, his hammer resting on the anvil. He looked at the boy, then at the white-hot core of the forge. He let out a long, slow whistle. "1800, eh? You've got eyes like a hawk, boy. And a heart like a furnace."

He tossed Tobin another iron pebble, this one smaller and more refined. "Go. You’ve done your work for the morning. The Warden is waiting on the ledge, and I think your brain is starting to itch."

Tobin caught the pebble, his grin wide. He was No Ordinary Mage, and he was finally starting to understand that 'No' meant he didn't have to follow the Empire's rules of separation.

***

The Summit ledge was bathed in the harsh, clear light of midday. Sir Gregory was sitting on his stone stool, his silver armor catching the sun like a mirror. Elana was standing at the edge, her arms crossed, watching the mist recede from the valley.

Tobin sat on the granite slab, placing his iron plate in front of him. He didn't pick up the chalk. He didn't look at the equations he’d scribbled the day before. 

He didn't have to. The '13' in Intelligence had already solved them.

"Ready, ghost?" Gregory asked, his voice a low rumble.

Tobin didn't answer with words. He closed his eyes and went into the silence. 

The *Focused Meditation* was instantly accessible now, a mental ritual that took seconds instead of an hour. He pushed the world away. He found the reservoir. He found the point of state-transition. 

And there was the pre-spark. 

But it wasn't a tiny, jagged crack in the darkness anymore. It was a stable, humming node of resonance. With his new Intelligence, Tobin could see the gaps in the static. He could see the exact frequency required to bridge the distance between his intent and the physical world.

He didn't try to out-calculate the storm. He used the Synergy he’d found at the well and the forge. He aligned his Will with his Intelligence, and his Intelligence with his Mana Perception. He wove the threads together, holding the tension just like Mistress Elara had taught him.

He reached out a finger, pointing at the center of the blackened iron plate.

*Resonance. Frequency. Compression.*

The variables clicked into place. The arithmetic balanced to zero. 

A tiny, brilliant point of white light appeared at the tip of his finger. It wasn't a spark from a flint; it was a miniature star, a silent, absolute manifestation of unassisted power. 

It hung in the air for a heartbeat, two heartbeats, three. It was stable. It was perfect. It was a Spark.

**[First Unassisted Spell Manifested: Spark - Level 1]**
**[Attribute Milestone Achieved!]**
**Intelligence: 13**
**Mana Manipulation Unlocked: Level 1**
**[System Note: The arithmetic is correct. You have moved from the map to the territory. Well done, Scrivener.]**

Tobin opened his eyes. The spark was still there, a tiny, glowing jewel of light against the grey stone. It didn't flicker. It didn't fade. It only vanished when Tobin consciously let the resonance go.

He let out a breath he hadn't realized he was holding. He looked up at Gregory and Elana.

Sir Gregory was nodding, a look of profound, quiet satisfaction on his weathered face. Elana was staring at the spot where the spark had been, her mouth slightly agape.

"Two days," Elana whispered. "It took him two days."

"He didn't just calculate it," Gregory said, standing up. He walked over to Tobin and placed a heavy hand on the boy’s shoulder. "He integrated it. He found the synergy between the labor of the body and the labor of the mind."

He looked at Tobin, his silver armor reflecting the boy’s triumph. "You've earned your first lumen, Tobin. But remember, a spark is just a way to see the darkness. Now that the light is on... you're going to see just how deep that darkness really is."

Tobin stood up, his '13' Intelligence humming, his '12' Endurance steady. He looked out over the valley, the iron pebbles in his pocket a reminder of the heat and the water that had bought him this moment.

"I'm ready," Tobin said.

He wasn't a Mage yet. He was just a boy who had made a single point of light. 

But as the sun reached its zenith over the Ironwood Summit, Tobin of Oakhaven realized that with Synergy, a single spark was more than enough to start a revolution. 

And he was just getting started.
