**Chapter 21: A Wager in the Dark**

The divine argument raged.

\"He left them alive!\" Kael bellowed. \"Weakness! A true warrior would
have eliminated the threat!\"

\"He eliminated the threat without weakening himself,\" Selyra countered
calmly. \"That is a victory on every conceivable level. It is the very
definition of balance.\"

\"It\'s boring!\" a new voice sang out, the sharp, discordant clang of a
cracked bell. The gods turned. The Jester lounged on his throne of
shifting possibilities. \"Oh, don\'t get me wrong,\" he said with a
theatrical sigh. \"It was a *lovely* little morality play. The hero
shows mercy, the thief questions her life choices. Very heartwarming.
But it proves nothing.\"

He leaned forward, his grin sharp and unsettling. \"He hasn\'t faced a
true monster, a thing of pure hunger for which mercy is not an option.
He hasn\'t been forced to kill when his own life depends on it. His
pretty little balancing act works fine against desperate thugs. Let\'s
see how it holds up against a real nightmare.\"

\"You will not interfere,\" Selyra warned, her voice losing its serene
edge.

\"Interfere?\" The Jester held up his hands in mock surrender. \"My
dear, I would never break the rules! I can\'t create a monster. But\...
the sea is full of them already, isn\'t it?\" His gaze drifted to the
map in the mortal world below, to the words **HERE BE SERPENTS**. \"All
I\'m going to do,\" he purred, \"is ring the dinner bell.\"

Back in the Salt-Stained Gryphon, Captain Kaelen unrolled the map, his
scarred finger tracing their route. \"The Sunken Door,\" he murmured.
\"Figured it was just a sailor\'s yarn. This part of the sea is called
the Serpent\'s Maw for a reason. Storms, and things in the deep older
than this city. My price is my price, but I want double if we make it
back alive.\"

\"Fair enough,\" Silas agreed.

As the two veterans discussed provisions, The Jester made his move. He
found the faint, unique energy signature that clung to Gabriel---the
touch of Selyra\'s favor, the hum of his Quad-Class potential---and he
amplified it. He twisted it, warped it, making the subtle hum into a
screaming, irresistible beacon that punched down into the deepest,
darkest trenches of the Azure Sea. He added a flavor to the signal, a
taste of divine energy and burgeoning power, like the scent of blood to
a shark.

Miles below the surface, in a lightless abyssal trench, something
ancient stirred. It had slept for a century. But this scent on the
ethereal currents was new. It was not the taste of mere flesh. It was
the taste of *power*.

A single, colossal eye, the size of a ship\'s wheel and glowing with a
cold, hateful phosphorescence, snapped open in the crushing dark. The
Scyllan Sea-Drake, a relic from a forgotten age, unfurled its immense,
serpentine coils.

It was hungry. And for the first time in centuries, it began its slow,
inexorable ascent from the depths, drawn by a sweet, irresistible
promise from the world of light above.
