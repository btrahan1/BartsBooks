**Chapter 25: A Shattering of Precedent**

The divine palace was in an uproar. Kael, the God of War, was on his
feet, his iron throne rattling. He stared at the scrying pool, and for
the first time in millennia, he was utterly speechless.

\"Impossible!\" the God of Lore finally managed, his voice a choked
whisper. \"That is not a spell! There is no incantation for what he just
did! He\... he just *made* it!\"

\"He created a Prime Ice construct,\" a goddess of magic corrected, her
voice trembling. \"That is an archmage-level feat! He\'s a Level 1
Aspirant! He shouldn\'t be able to do more than chill a mug of ale!\"

The chamber erupted into a cacophony of divine chaos. What they had just
witnessed was not a mortal performing an act of power. It was a mortal
breaking the very laws of power that governed their reality.

The Jester sat bolt upright on his throne, his face a flickering,
chaotic mural of emotions. First, **Shock**. He had not expected the boy
to stand and fight a god-beast on its own terms. Then, **Rage**. The boy
had ruined his perfect test! This was supposed to be a lesson in brutal
reality. Instead, the boy had punched the universe in the eye. This was
followed by a cold, unfamiliar trickle of **Fear**. What *was* this
mortal? Beings who could break the rules were dangerous.

Finally, a slow-blossoming, wicked **Delight**. His test may have been
ruined, but the results were far more entertaining than he could have
ever hoped for! The boy wasn\'t just a player. He was a wild card, a
chaos agent in his own right.

Amidst the divine shouting, one being was perfectly still. Selyra, the
Goddess of Twilight and Balance, simply sat on her throne, a serene,
knowing smile on her lips. She felt the other gods\' gazes turn to her,
their expressions a mixture of awe and accusation.

Kael found his voice. \"This is your doing! What have you unleashed,
Selyra?\"

She could have gloated. She could have reminded them of their scorn, of
their dismissal of her \"scrawny orphan.\" But she didn\'t. This was not
her moment to preen. This was not her victory to claim.

This was Gabriel\'s.

She simply met Kael\'s furious gaze and said, her voice calm and clear
amidst the chaos, \"I have unleashed nothing. He has.\"
