**Chapter 27: The Unaccountable Boon**

The divine palace, which moments before had been a cacophony of outrage,
was now shrouded in a heavy, suspicious silence. The gods stared at the
scrying pool, their focus on the afterimage of the blue screens, their
impossible numbers seared into the divine consciousness.

Fifty. Thousand. Experience.

The number was obscene. It was a king\'s ransom, a dragon\'s hoard of
pure advancement. No single act in the history of The System had ever
yielded such a reward.

\"Fifty thousand,\" Kael the War God finally rumbled, his voice low and
dangerous. He was no longer shouting; he was accusing. \"The System is
not that generous. That number is\... a violation.\"

\"The \'Leviathan\'s Bane\' feat is a plausible, if unprecedented,
explanation for the base reward,\" the God of Lore conceded, his voice
tight. \"But it would not be fifty thousand. Perhaps a fifth of that, at
best.\"

All eyes in the hall turned to The Jester.

The trickster held up his hands, a look of pure, theatrical innocence on
his face. \"Don\'t look at me!\" he exclaimed. \"I make the game harder,
not easier!\"

The gods\' gazes shifted.

\"And the bonus stats,\" the Goddess of Magic added sharply. \"An
increase of +1 to Intelligence and Wisdom, awarded directly. That
has *never* happened. The System allows mortals to *earn* their stats.
It does not simply grant them as a gift. That is a direct intervention.
Someone broke the prime directive.\"

The accusation hung heavy in the air. To directly tamper with a
mortal\'s core attributes was the ultimate taboo.

Kael\'s furious gaze landed on Selyra. \"You,\" he snarled. \"This is
your champion. You gave him this boon.\"

Selyra met his glare without flinching, her serenity an unshakable
shield. \"I did not,\" she said, her voice ringing with absolute truth.
\"I have never once reached into the world and altered his fate or his
person. To do so would be to invalidate his entire journey.\"

The silence returned, deeper and more unsettling. If Sely inra hadn\'t
done it, and The Jester was innocent, then who had? They were so
consumed by their own politics that they failed to consider the most
terrifying possibility. They had all assumed the boon had been a
gift *from* one of them. It never occurred to a single one that it had
been a payment *to* The System itself, a price paid by a hidden player
for a service rendered.

Far away, in his blighted lands, Malakor felt the tremor in the cosmic
balance. He recognized the pattern. Long ago, he too had learned that
The System was not just a set of rules; it was a sentient, ancient, and
deeply transactional entity. For the right price, it could be bargained
with.

He hadn\'t been the one to interfere this time. But he knew what it
meant. Someone else, someone of immense power and unfathomable motive,
was also playing the game. They had just placed a massive wager on the
orphan boy.

A slow, terrible smile spread across Malakor\'s blighted face. A rival
Scion, a hidden patron, and a pantheon of squabbling gods. The game was
far grander than any of them realized.
