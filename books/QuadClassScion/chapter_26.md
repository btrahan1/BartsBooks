**Chapter 28: The Price of a Point**

The rest of the sea journey was tense but uneventful. The tale of the
boy who had blinded the Scyllan spread through the ship like wildfire,
turning Gabriel into a figure of myth. The sailors, who had once seen
him as a charming farm boy, now gave him a wide berth, their gazes a
mixture of profound respect and superstitious fear.

Gabriel paid them no mind. He spent the days in a state of deep, focused
introspection, contemplating the vast, intimidating pool of 50,000
experience points he now possessed. He found a quiet spot at the ship\'s
stern, the endless blue of the ocean a calming backdrop to the complex
mathematics unfolding in his mind. He pulled up his status screen.

**\[EXP: 50,123.5 / 2,000\]**

He had enough for twenty-five stat points. He could raise all four of
his primary attributes from 10 to 16 right now. But Silas\'s words
echoed in his memory. *The curve gets steeper.*

He focused on the \"Attribute Up\" button.

**\[Select an Attribute to improve.\]**\
**\[STR: 10\] \[DEX: 10\] \[WIS: 11\] \[INT: 11\]**\
**\[Cost for next point: 2,000 EXP\]**

Remembering the bonus points, he decided to start there, to bring his
Strength and Dexterity up to match. He selected Strength and confirmed.
A warm, invigorating pulse flowed through him. The screen updated.

**\[STR increased to 11.\]**\
**\[EXP: 48,123.5 / 2,500\]**

His eyes widened. The cost for the next point had increased by 500. He
spent the next hour doing the calculations. To get all four stats from
11 to 12 would cost 10,000 EXP. From 12 to 13\... a staggering 12,000
EXP. In total, to reach the starting line of 13 in all four primary
stats, he would need to spend 42,000 experience points.

It was an astronomical sum, but he had it. He could do it right now. He
paused, his will hovering over the confirmation. A quiet, insistent
whisper of Silas\'s hard-won wisdom gave him pause. Was it right to
spend it all at once? He made a decision. He would raise his Dexterity
to 11, balancing his core stats. But he would hold the rest in reserve.
The points weren\'t going anywhere. He needed to understand the world
more before making such a monumental choice.

After spending the 2,000 EXP, his four primary stats stood in perfect,
balanced harmony at 11. He closed the screen, a vast reserve of 46,123.5
experience points humming within him, a secret reservoir of incredible
potential.

His nights had also changed. His dreams were now darker, more
unsettling. He dreamed of twisted, screaming trees, of a shadowy throne,
and of a cold, malevolent presence that knew his name. They were
whispers, warnings of a danger he did not yet understand but could feel
approaching.

On the tenth day, land was sighted. Not their final destination, but the
bustling port city of Maridia, a midway point to restock. As the *Sea
Serpent\'s Pride* docked, a figure detached himself from the shadows of
a waterfront warehouse. He was tall and cloaked, two longsword hilts
rising above his shoulders. Pinned to his cloak was a small, silver
insignia: a snarling wolf\'s head. He was a member of the Claws of
Malakor, the Blighted Scion\'s elite hunters. He had been waiting for
them. And his orders were simple: kill the boy.
