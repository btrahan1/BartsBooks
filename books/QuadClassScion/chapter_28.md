**Chapter 30: The Price of a Soul**

Silas grunted, a look of profound, agonizing surprise on his face. He
staggered forward, his sword clattering to the floor as the second
assassin pulled his poisoned dagger free.

Time seemed to stop. Gabriel saw the black, viscous poison already
spreading from the wound. He saw his mentor, his friend, the only family
he had ever known, begin to fall.

Something dark, something ancient and deeply terrifying, erupted in
Gabriel\'s soul. The careful balance, the mercy, the restraint---it all
shattered. All that was left was a white-hot, singular point of pure,
unadulterated rage.

He didn\'t draw his sword. He didn\'t cast a spell. He simply looked at
the second assassin and squeezed his hand into a fist. It was not a
thought. It was a command issued to reality itself. *Die.*

The assassin\'s head didn\'t just explode. It imploded, then erupted in
a silent, grotesque fountain of red mist and bone fragments.

Gabriel didn\'t even register the man\'s death. He was already at
Silas\'s side, catching him before he hit the floor. \"Silas!\"

The old man was gasping, his face pale, his lips already tinged with
blue. The poison was devastatingly fast. Gabriel\'s hands glowed with
the pearlescent light of his healing magic. He poured all of his will
into his mentor. The wound closed, but the poison remained, a black web
spreading beneath the skin. Healing couldn\'t fight it.

Silas was dying. Despair, black and absolute, threatened to drown
Gabriel. But then, the rage returned. The same defiant fire that had
sustained him in the orphanage, the same will that had forged a spear of
ice. He would not accept this. This was not how the story ended.

He placed one hand on Silas\'s chest. He looked up at the tavern\'s
ceiling, but he was seeing the heavens beyond. He thrust his other hand
upward, a fist clenched against the gods and fate itself.

\"Take it!\" he screamed, his voice raw with anguish and fury. \"Take it
all! The experience! Everything! I DON\'T CARE! SILAS WILL NOT DIE!\"

He opened his will, his very soul, and made an offer not to a single
god, but to The System itself. A sacrifice of pure, unimaginable
potential.

At that exact moment, all across the kingdom, forty-two souls felt it.
An old librarian, a forgotten fisherman, a quiet blacksmith---men and
women who had long ago chosen the fool\'s path. They felt a sudden,
inexplicable pull, a desperate plea that resonated in the core of their
unique class. In his throne room, King Theron stood, his hand going to
his chest. He felt it too. A call from a brother of the path. A
desperate sacrifice. And a gift, a choice that had to be made.

The Gods, who had been watching the brutal tavern fight, now recoiled in
unison.

\"What is he doing?\" Kael roared.

\"He\'s\... he\'s trying to bargain with The System,\" the Goddess of
Magic whispered, her voice filled with horrified awe.

Selyra could only watch, her heart aching for her champion. He was about
to try and rewrite the rules of life and death. And the price would be
everything he had.
