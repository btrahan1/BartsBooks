**Chapter 31: The Soul-Giver**

Silas gasped, a ragged breath that shuddered through his body. The fire
of the poison was gone, replaced by a profound, bone-deep weariness. He
looked up.

Gabriel was frozen in his defiant pose, a column of pure, blinding white
light connecting him to\... somewhere else. It was not a spell; it was a
river of raw potential flowing out of him and into the unseen fabric of
the world.

The light pulsed once, a silent shockwave that made the very air in the
tavern hum. Then, it vanished.

Gabriel's arm fell. His eyes, which had been blazing with incandescent
fury, went blank. He swayed for a moment, then crumpled to the floor,
lost in a sleep so deep it resembled death.

Silas, his strength slowly returning, pushed himself into a sitting
position. He was alive. He looked at the boy. What had he done? A
flashing blue screen appeared in his own vision, a sight he had not seen
in over fifty years.

**\[EXP GAINED: +1,000\]**

For what?

All across the land, the other forty-two felt it too. The pull ceased,
the plea was answered. And in its place, a quiet, impossible gift of a
thousand experience points. In his throne room, King Theron stared at
the same notification, his heart pounding. What had The System wrought?

And then, for the first time in living memory, The System spoke.

It was not a voice that entered the ear. It was a voice that bloomed in
the center of every sentient mind in the world, from the lowest goblin
to the highest god. It was a voice of absolute, unimpeachable authority.

**\[A new precedent has been established.\]**

**\[A mortal has sacrificed an unquantifiable measure of personal
potential to avert the designated death of another.\]**

**\[This act has created a debt within The System.\]**

**\[To rebalance the scales, a new path has been forged.\]**

**\[CLASS UNLOCKED: THE SOUL-GIVER\]**\
**\[PREREQUISITE: QUAD-CLASS\]**\
**\[PREREQUISITE: VOLUNTARY SACRIFICE OF ALL ACCRUED EXPERIENCE IN AN
ACT OF PURE ALTRUISM\]**

**\[The Soul-Giver may channel their own life force and experience to
empower or resurrect their designated allies.\]**

**\[The scales are balanced. The precedent is recorded.\]**

The voice vanished, leaving behind a world reeling in stunned, terrified
silence.

The divine palace was in a state of cataclysmic shock. The gods could
only stare, their power rendered meaningless. They were not the masters
of The System; they were merely its oldest inhabitants.

The Jester was, for the first time in his existence, not smiling. What
had he done? He had wanted to test a hero. He had, instead, midwived the
birth of a living saint, a being with the potential to defy the very
concept of death.

Selyra looked at the image of Gabriel on the tavern floor, and tears,
bright as starlight, traced paths down her divine cheeks. He had faced
the ultimate test, not of power, but of love. And he had not just passed
it; he had shattered it, and in doing so, had unlocked a power greater
than any of them had dreamed possible.

Silas stared at Gabriel\'s sleeping form, the words of The System
echoing in his soul. *Voluntary sacrifice of all accrued
experience.* The fifty thousand points\... gone. All of it, burned away
in a single, selfless act to save his life. He looked at his own screen
again. The thousand experience points. It wasn\'t just a gift. It was a
tithe. A tiny fraction of the immense sacrifice Gabriel had made,
distributed among all the brothers and sisters of his path.

He knelt beside the boy, his old, cynical heart breaking and healing all
at once. He placed a trembling hand on Gabriel\'s shoulder.

\"Gabriel,\" he whispered, his voice thick with an emotion he hadn\'t
felt in sixty years. \"What have you done?\"
