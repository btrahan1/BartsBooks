**Chapter 32: A Scion\'s Vow**

Gabriel dreamed.

He floated in a void of quiet gray, the chaos of the past day a distant,
fading echo. He saw it all play out again: the flash of steel, the
poisoned dagger, the blinding white light, the feeling of his hard-won
potential pouring out of him like water from a shattered vessel. He felt
it all drain away, leaving him empty, hollowed out, and at peace.

And in his dream, he saw Silas take a clean, untroubled breath.

Gabriel smiled. Silas lived. That was the only thing that mattered.

What was experience? A number on a screen. A tool. A means to an end. He
had used it to buy the one thing he could not bear to lose. It was the
best bargain he had ever made. There would be more. The Sunken Door. The
Time Dilation Dungeon. It waited for him, promising the one resource he
needed to earn it all back: *time*.

He would work. He would train. He would sacrifice. And, he now
understood, he would kill.

He had crossed a bridge in that grimy tavern. He had offered mercy, and
it had been thrown back in his face. He saw now that there was evil in
the world, a conscious, deliberate malice that could not be negotiated
with. They had made their choice when they took up the assassin\'s
blade.

Now, Gabriel had made his. He would be a shield for the innocent. He
would be a healer for the wounded. He would be a light in the darkness.
But when he faced the true, unrepentant dark, he would be a sword. His
mercy was not a weakness, but it was not a suicide pact either. It would
all be on his terms. His path. His balance.

Gods be damned.

A new strength, one that had nothing to do with stats or skills, settled
into his soul. It was the strength of purpose.

In the waking world, Silas watched over the boy\'s sleeping form as
Captain Kaelen and his crew cleaned up the grisly mess, their silence a
testament to the awe and fear they now held for the two quiet travelers.

Silas checked Gabriel\'s status. The numbers were a testament to the
boy\'s sacrifice and The System\'s profound response.

The experience was gone. The cost for his next point had even increased.
But he had unlocked something new, something that had redefined
reality. *Soul-Giver.* And his Charisma, once his lagging attribute, had
been raised by The System itself, a quiet acknowledgment of an act that
had touched the souls of his scattered brethren.

Silas looked at the boy, who was smiling faintly in his deep, healing
sleep. He was no longer just a boy with a dream. He was a force of
nature, a new piece on the board that answered to no one, not even the
gods.

He was a Scion. And his journey was just beginning.
