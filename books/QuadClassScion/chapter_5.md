**Chapter 5: The Jester\'s Price**

The fire crackled, filling the sudden, heavy silence. The old man\'s
shock was so profound it seemed to suck the air from the small cottage.

\"You\... saw the Sunken Door,\" Silas breathed, the name a reverent
whisper. \"The legends are true.\" He leaned back in his chair, a slow,
weary wonder dawning in his eyes. He looked at Gabriel, then down at his
own gnarled knuckles.

\"For sixty years,\" Silas said, his voice distant, \"I have lived on
this farm. But when I was eighteen, I was like you. Full of fire and a
foolish, impossible dream.\"

He held up his hand, and for the first time, Gabriel saw four faint,
almost invisible tattoos on the inside of his wrist---a tiny sword, a
coiled serpent, a flickering flame, and a single drop of water. The
symbols of the four paths.

\"I chose the Quad-Class,\" Silas stated, the words landing with the
weight of a dropped anvil. \"My stats were good. Better than good.
Strength 13, Dexterity 13, Wisdom 13, Intelligence 13. I met the
prerequisites on my eighteenth birthday. I was arrogant. I thought the
world would fall at my feet.\"

He let out a short, bitter laugh. \"The System is a harsh mistress, boy.
The experience penalty\... ten times the cost for every single level.
It\'s a wall of sheer time that no mortal can climb in one lifetime.\"

Gabriel\'s heart, which had been soaring, began to plummet. \"So\... you
never leveled?\"

\"Oh, I leveled,\" Silas grunted. \"Once. It took me twenty years of
adventuring just to reach Level 2. The other members of my party were
reaching Level 15, becoming heroes, while I was still\... me.\"

He flexed his hand, the memory of it all clear in his eyes. \"It has its
benefits. I can mend a wound that would otherwise fester. I can light a
fire with a word. My body is still strong, my hands still steady. But I
am a master of none. A jack-of-all-trades in a world that rewards
specialization. Most of us who chose this path end up like
this---retired, forgotten, living quiet lives.\"

Gabriel\'s dream, which had seemed so bright, was suddenly cast in a
grim, realistic shadow. Was this his future? To spend a lifetime of
impossible effort only to end up a slightly more versatile farmer?

\"There are more of us than you\'d think,\" Silas continued, his voice
low. \"Dozens, maybe hundreds, scattered across the kingdom. We chose
the path of balance and were rewarded with stagnation. But we all cling
to the same rumor\... the same sliver of hope.\"

He leaned forward again, his eyes locking onto Gabriel\'s. \"The King.
It is whispered that King Theron is a Quad-Class. And that he did the
impossible. They say he reached Level 10 and unlocked the true potential
of our path. An advanced class so powerful no one even knows its name.
It is why he is the King.\"

The pieces clicked into place. The Time Dilation Dungeon. It wasn\'t a
myth. It was the key. The one tool that could break through the wall of
time that had stopped Silas and all the others.

\"The dungeon,\" Gabriel said, his voice filled with renewed conviction.
\"It\'s real. And it\'s the only way.\"

Silas stared at him, the weary resignation in his face slowly being
replaced by a spark of the fire he must have had as a young man. He saw
the same impossible dream in Gabriel\'s eyes, but with one critical
difference. This boy hadn\'t just dreamed of the path. He had been shown
the gate.

\"All my life, I thought it was just a story to give us fools a reason
not to despair,\" Silas murmured, shaking his head. \"If what you say is
true\... if you can find that place\...\"

He stood up, the aches and pains of sixty years of labor seeming to fall
away. The crotchety farmer was gone. In his place stood a mentor.

\"Your chores are done,\" Silas said, his voice now ringing with an
authority Gabriel had never heard. \"Your *real* training begins at
dawn.\"
