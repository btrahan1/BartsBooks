**Chapter 8: The Mocking Jester**

On the morning of what the Ministry had decreed was his eighteenth
birthday, Gabriel awoke not to the crowing of the rooster, but to a
silent, insistent light.

It hung in the air before him, a pane of luminous blue only he could
see, shimmering in the pre-dawn gloom of the loft. The lettering was
stark and impossibly clear.

**\[You have reached the age of choosing. Select your path.\]**\
**\[Choose a Class.\]**

He focused his will, calling up his own status. A new screen
materialized, displaying the results of two years of sweat, pain, and
unwavering focus.

- **NAME:** GABRIEL

- **AGE:** 18

- **CLASS:** NONE

- **LEVEL:** 1

- **STATS:**

  - **STR:** 10

  - **DEX:** 10

  - **WIS:** 10

  - **INT:** 10

  - **CON:** 10

  - **CHA:** 8

A slow, brilliant smile spread across his face. He had done it. From a
slate of balanced mediocrity to a foundation of solid competence. A
quiet, fierce pride swelled in his chest.

The Class Selection screen returned, the choices laid out before him. He
saw the standard
options: **\[FIGHTER\]**, **\[ROGUE\]**, **\[HEALER\]**, **\[MAGE\]**.
He even saw suggestions for dual-class paths he was close to qualifying
for. A respectable life. A noble path. But it was not his path.

His gaze searched the screen, pushing past the obvious choices. And
there, tucked away at the very bottom, separated from the others as if
it were a warning, he found it.

- **\[QUAD-CLASS\]** - The Path of the Scion.

Next to the words was a small, animated icon: a jester\'s mask, its
painted smile fixed in a look of perpetual mockery. This was The
System\'s final judgment. Choosing this path was not just difficult; it
was a joke. It was the choice of a fool, and The System itself was
laughing at him.

Gabriel felt a flash of defiant, hot fire. He had been laughed at by
Roric. He had been dismissed by the Ministry Guard. Now, the very fabric
of his reality was mocking him. He didn\'t care. He focused his will
upon the choice.

A new screen immediately replaced the old one, the text glowing a
warning crimson.

**\[WARNING: You do not meet the base stat prerequisite of 13 in STR,
DEX, WIS, and INT for this class. Choosing this path will lock you into
the \'Aspirant\' stage. You will be UNABLE TO LEVEL UP until all four
primary stats reach 13.\]**

**\[WARNING: This path carries an EXTREME 10x experience penalty upon
reaching Level 2 and beyond. This choice is IRREVERSIBLE.\]**

**\[ARE YOU CERTAIN?\]**

There was no hesitation. This was the promise he had made to himself in
the dark of the orphanage. This was his answer to the mocking jester.

*Yes.*

The warning screen shattered into a thousand shards of red light, which
then coalesced into a simple, elegant blue.

**\[CLASS CHOSEN: QUAD-CLASS (ASPIRANT)\]**\
**\[LEVEL: 1\]**\
**\[EXP: 0 / 2,000\]**

The EXP bar to reach Level 2 was double the standard amount, just as
Silas had warned. The true test would come after that.

The screen faded. The ladder to the loft creaked and Silas\'s head
appeared, his old armor slung over one shoulder. He was ready.

\"Well?\" the old warrior asked.

Gabriel stood, feeling like a new man, his quiet pride now forged into
unbreakable resolve.

\"I\'m ready.\"
