# Synopsis - The Quad-Class Scion

Gabriel is a sixteen-year-old orphan who dreams of becoming a legendary "Scion" by walking all four paths of power: Fighter, Rogue, Mage, and Healer. While others specialize, Gabriel chooses the difficult "Quad-Class" path, which locks him at Level 1 until he can raise all his primary stats to 13, all while carrying a crushing 10x experience penalty.

Working on a remote farm, Gabriel finds an unwitting mentor in Silas, a retired warrior who also chose the path of balance but succumbed to the wall of time. Together, they discover a map to the mythical "Time Dilation Dungeon," the only tool that can break the limits of a human lifetime. Gabriel's journey is one of relentless grind, sacrifice, and a vow to become a shield for the innocent and a sword against the dark.
