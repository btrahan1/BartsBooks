# Chapter 9: The Green and the Gold

The Old Docks of Silverhaven were a place where the city’s architectural elegance gave way to the brutal reality of decay. Here, the stone wasn't polished marble or sturdy granite; it was crumbling limestone, slick with the black slime of centuries and scarred by the iron teeth of abandoned salvage hooks. It was a labyrinth of rotting timber pilings, rusted chains, and the pervasive, heavy smell of stagnant water and ancient grease.

Ren Ito skidded to a halt at the edge of the descent, his *Balance Mastery* humming in his ankles. Before him, a narrow, moss-slicked ladder of iron rungs disappeared into the shadows of the lower wharf. 

"Moon-Moss," Ren whispered, checking the mental map Velda had etched into his *Pathfinder’s Sense*. "Bottom of the pilings. Before the tide."

He didn't use the rungs. Instead, he activated *Balance Mastery* and stepped directly onto the vertical stone wall of the quay. Gravity felt like a distant memory as he ran straight down the shadows, his blue-and-silver light cutting a narrow path through the gloom.

The air grew colder as he descended, the sound of the Grand Canal’s main current replaced by the rhythmic, hollow *slap* of water against hollow wood. This was a place where the city’s law didn't reach—where even the sunlight seemed to hesitate.

As he reached the bottom-most level, just above the waterline, his *Pathfinder’s Sense* flared with a new, greenish tint. 

"There!"

Tucked into the deep fissures of the ancient wooden pilings was a luminescent, pale-green moss. It pulsed with a faint, rhythmic light, mirroring the slow heartbeat of the ocean. This was Moon-Moss, the rare catalyst Velda needed for the *Silt-Soothe* tea.

Ren reached into his satchel and pulled out a small, curved harvesting knife Velda had lent him. He approached the moss with a courier's focus, but as his knife touched the first strand, a notification shimmered in the air.

**[NOTIFICATION: NOVICE OBSERVATION RECOGNIZED]**
**[CRITERIA MET: HARVESTING IN A HIGH-DANGER ZONE]**
**[CRITERIA MET: IDENTIFICATION OF RARE MUNICIPAL FLORA]**

**[SKILL GRANTED: HERBALISM (NOVICE)]**
*Effect: Increases the chance of gathering high-quality plant materials and allows for the basic identification of medicinal properties in the wild.*

"Herbalism?" Ren blinked at the blue pane. "I'm a courier, not a gardener!"

But as the skill integrated into his mind, the moss changed. It wasn't just 'green stuff' anymore. He could see the veins of potency running through the fibers. He saw where the roots were deepest and where the medicine was most concentrated. His hands moved with a new, instinctive precision, sliding the knife beneath the moss and peeling it away in perfect, intact sheets.

He was halfway through his second pilings when the shadows around him shifted.

A low, wet hiss echoed from beneath the pier. Ren froze. *Predictive Twitch* flared at the back of his skull—a sharp, staccato warning. 

A *Dock-Creeper*, a creature made of salt-encrusted scales and too many spindly legs, lunged from the darkness. It was a scavenger of the deep, a monster that fed on the refuse of the city and anything unfortunate enough to fall into its domain.

Ren didn't fight. He wasn't a warrior. He was a courier.

He kicked off the piling, his *Balance Mastery* allowing him to pivot mid-air. The creeper’s claws scraped against the wood where his head had been a second before. Ren landed on the side of a nearby timber, ran three steps vertically to gain height, and then vaulted over the creature.

"Too slow!" Ren chirped, though his heart was racing. 

He didn't run away. He ran to the next piling. He had a mission.

For the next twenty minutes, the Old Docks became the site of a high-speed game of tag. Three more creepers joined the hunt, their wet slaps echoing through the underside of the wharf. Ren moved like a silver needle, weaving between the rotting timbers, sliding under rusted chains, and using the vertical faces of the quay to stay out of reach.

He wasn't unscathed. A jagged splinter from an old crate caught his shoulder as he tumbled beneath a low beam, and a lucky swipe from a creeper left a stinging scratch across his thigh. But his pouch was filling. Sheets of Moon-Moss, bundles of *Salt-Wort*, and even a few rare *Barnacle-Blooms* were stuffed into his satchel.

As the first cold wave of the rising tide splashed against his boots, Ren knew he was out of time.

He launched himself upward, running straight up the vertical face of the primary quay. The creepers hissing below him grew faint as he reached the sunlight of the upper district. He didn't stop to catch his breath. He ran.

When he burst into Velda’s apothecary, his tunic was torn, his leg was bleeding, and his silver-blue light was flickering with exhaustion.

Velda looked up from her boiling vat, her thick spectacles reflecting the blue light of Ren's entrance. Her eyes widened as he slammed his satchel onto the counter and poured out the harvest.

"The Moon-Moss," Velda whispered, her voice losing its usual bark. She reached out with trembling fingers, touching the high-quality sheets of moss. "And Salt-Wort? Even Barnacle-Blooms? Boy, this is... this is enough for fifty batches of the centrifuge!"

She looked at Ren—at the scratches on his arm and the grime on his face. "You encountered the creepers, didn't you?"

"They weren't very fast," Ren panted, a tired grin spreading across his face. 

"Fast enough to bite, it seems," Velda muttered, but there was a new warmth in her gaze. She grabbed a clean rag and a bottle of stinging blue liquid. "Sit down. Let me clean those. Then... then you're going to help me."

"I have to deliver these?" Ren asked, hoping for another run.

"No," Velda said, handing him a silver mortar and pestle. "The people are arriving at the Magister’s triage center in an hour. I can't brew this alone. You brought the ingredients, now you're going to help me awaken the medicine."

For the next two hours, Ren’s speed was channeled into a different kind of precision. Under Velda’s stern direction, he ground the Moon-Moss into a fine, glowing paste. He measured out drops of *Ghost-Leaf* extract and stirred the bubbling tinctures with a rhythmic intensity that matched the silver hum of his courier energy.

He learned the delicate balance of the heat, the way the colors shifted from a sickly yellow to a deep, soothing gold when the reagents were perfectly combined. He felt the silver shimmer of his magic interacting with the chemical reactions in the glass.

As the final batch of *Silt-Soothe* was poured into small vials, the System chimed one last time.

**[NOTIFICATION: CROSS-DISCIPLINARY PROFICIENCY RECOGNIZED]**
**[CRITERIA MET: SUCCESSFUL COMPLETION OF A HIGH-STAKES SYSTEM TASK]**
**[CRITERIA MET: CHANNELING CLASS ENERGY INTO PRODUCTIVE REAGENTS]**

**[SKILL GRANTED: ALCHEMY (NOVICE)]**
*Effect: Allows for the basic refinement of medicinal tinctures and increases the potency of brewed items by 5% when using manual stirring.*

Ren stared at his hands, which were now stained a deep, medicinal purple. "Alchemy? First Herbalism, now Alchemy? Papa’s going to think I'm quitting the Express!"

Velda let out a rare, sharp bark of a laugh. She packed the vials into a sturdy leather carrying case and handed them to Ren.

"You're not quitting anything, little Flash," Velda said, her voice gruff with respect. "You're just becoming a courier who actually knows what’s inside the boxes he carries. That makes you dangerous."

She tapped the silver wing on his patch. "Now, go. The people in the Warehouse District are waiting. Deliver the cure with the same speed you delivered the justice."

Ren grabbed the case, the weight of the gold-tinted liquid feeling more important than any chest of coppers. His exhaustion vanished, replaced by a surge of Level 5 energy.

"The Silverhaven Express!" Ren shouted as he dove out the door. "Special Delivery: Hope!"
