# Chapter 16: The Square-Cube Law

The recovery period from the run lasted exactly sixty seconds—just long enough for Mara to stop seeing double and for Jarl to find his feet. Sergeant Goram was not a man who believed in the restorative power of rest. He believed in the restorative power of pain.

"On your feet, you sorry lot!" Goram shouted, his voice a hammer-blow against the morning stillness. "The run was just the appetizer. Now, we move on to what some people call PT—Physical Training. Others, mostly those who don't belong in my uniform, call it torture. You will call it... a casual morning warmup."

He paced the front of the formation, his boots clicking with terrifying precision on the stone. "One hundred pushups. One hundred situps. One hundred pullups. One hundred jumping jacks. One hundred lunges. No pauses. no whining. No dying until the set is complete. Begin!"

The parade ground immediately became a scene of rhythmic, grunting exertion. 

Ren dropped into the pushup position, his small hands spread flat against the dirt. Beside him, Jarl—a man who looked like he could bench-press a horse—let out a confident smirk. To Jarl, this was his chance. He had been humiliated on the track, outrun by a child, but here? Here, strength mattered. He had thick cords of muscle in his arms and a chest like a barrel. Surely, a hundred pushups would be a walk in the park for a man of his stature, while the 'little Flash' would surely crumble under the strain of his first real strength test.

But physics, as Ren was about to demonstrate, had a different idea.

Ren began his pushups. *One-two-three-four.*

His movements were a blur. Because Ren was half the size and a quarter of the weight of the regular recruits, the amount of force he needed to lift his own body was significantly lower. His *Agility* of 50 and *Stamina* of 25 acted as a high-octane engine in a very small chassis. He wasn't just doing pushups; he was performing a high-speed vibration.

*Twenty-five. Fifty. Seventy-five.*

While Jarl was still on his fortieth, his face turning a deep purple as he pushed his massive frame against the damp earth, Ren was finishing his hundredth. Ren popped up, pivoted to his back, and immediately began the situps.

*Crunch-snap-crunch-snap.*

His core was a coil of silver-blue energy. He moved with a mechanical rhythm, his small frame folding and unfolding with a speed that made the air whistle. 

Mara, two rows over, was on her fiftieth situp, her teeth bared in a snarl of effort. She looked over, saw Ren finishing his hundredth and launching himself toward the pullup bars, and let out a sound that was half-laugh, half-sob.

"He’s... he’s not even... pausing," Mara wheezed.

The pullups were where the advantage of the small became absolute. Ren jumped up, his small hands grasping the iron bar. With a weight of barely fifty pounds and a System-enhanced musculature, his power-to-weight ratio was off the charts. He didn't just pull himself up; he launched.

*Zip-zip-zip-zip.*

His chin passed the bar a hundred times in a little over two minutes. By the time he dropped back to the dirt, the fastest of the other recruits was trailing by nearly three hundred repetitions.

Jarl was currently fighting his eightieth pushup, his arms shaking like saplings in a gale. He looked up, his eyes bloodshot, just in time to see Ren starting the jumping jacks. 

*Clap-thump. Clap-thump.*

Ren looked like a frantic marionette. He wasn't even sweating. In fact, he looked like he was having *fun*. To him, PT was just another form of movement, a different flavor of the speed he had practiced since he was an infant.

"One hundred... jumping jacks! Done!" Ren chirped, his voice bright and clear, completely devoid of the gasping desperation that filled the rest of the grounds.

He transitioned into the lunges. *Step-down-step-up.* 

Goram stood in front of the formation, his arms crossed over his massive chest. He wasn't watching the recruits who were failing. He was watching Ren. Specifically, he was watching the way Ren’s silver-blue light wasn't flickering. Most people, even those with high Stamina, showed signs of exertion—a deepening of the aura, a ragged pulse in the light. 

Ren’s light was steady. It was as if his body wasn't even acknowledging the work.

"Done!" Ren shouted, standing at attention as he finished the final lunge.

The rest of the recruits were in various stages of collapse. Jarl was face-down in the mud, his pushups forgotten at eighty-two. Mara was on her hundredth situp, her body trembling with the final effort. The rest were a sea of groans and twitching limbs.

Goram walked over to Ren. He looked down, his granite-like face devoid of emotion. He reached out and touched Ren’s tunic, right over his heart. Then, he looked at Ren’s brow.

"You're not even sweating, Recruit Ito," Goram said, his voice a low rumble.

"I feel a little warm, sir!" Ren replied, his chest barely heaving.

Goram looked back at Jarl, then back at Ren. He saw the smirks that had vanished from the recruits' faces, replaced by a deep, terrifying realization. They weren't just being outperformed by a child’s speed; they were being outperformed by a child’s *existence*.

"The square-cube law," Goram said, loud enough for those still struggling to hear. "The larger you are, the more your own mass becomes your primary enemy. You recruits are fighting yourselves. Recruit Ito... Recruit Ito is just moving."

He looked Ren in the eye. "If the morning warmup isn't enough to make you sweat, then it wasn't a warmup. It was a waste of time."

Goram pointed back to the ground. "Again, Ren. From the beginning. Two hundred of everything this time. And if you finish those without a drop of salt on your forehead, we’re going to do it a third time."

Ren’s eyes widened, but not with fear. They sparked with a new, blue intensity. 

"Yes, Sergeant!" 

Ren dropped back into the pushup position before Goram had even finished the sentence. *One-two-three-four.*

The recruits watched in a horrified silence as the blue-and-silver blur started all over again. They were still struggling to finish their first hundred, their muscles screaming, their vision blurring. And there was the child—the 'little Flash'—beginning a double-set as if he were just starting his day.

Jarl managed to push himself up for his eighty-third pushup. He looked at Ren, who was already on his fiftieth of the second set.

"I... hate... physics," Jarl wheezed, his arms finally giving out. He collapsed back into the mud with a wet *splat*.

Mara, finally finished with her first circuit, sat on the ground and watched Ren. She didn't look angry anymore. She looked thoughtful. She saw the way Ren wasn't just moving fast; he was moving *efficiently*. He wasn't wasting a single ounce of energy on unnecessary tension. 

The System chimed softly in Ren’s mind as he hit the hundredth pushup of his second set.

**[NOTIFICATION: KINETIC EFFICIENCY RECOGNIZED]**
**[CRITERIA MET: SUSTAINED HIGH-CAPACITY REPETITION]**
**[CRITERIA MET: MINIMAL ENERGY WASTE IN STRENGTH EXERTION]**

**[SKILL GRANTED: EFFORTLESS FLOW (PASSIVE)]**
*Effect: Reduces Stamina consumption for bodyweight exercises and repetitive physical movements by 15%.*

Ren felt the shift immediately. The 'warmth' he had felt in his muscles didn't increase. Instead, it smoothed out. His joints felt like they were coated in silk.

By the time Ren finished the second set of two hundred—bringing his total to three hundred repetitions of everything—Goram finally stepped back. 

Ren stood at attention, his face slightly flushed, a single, solitary bead of sweat trickling down his temple.

"There it is," Goram said, pointing to the sweat-drop as if it were a rare jewel. "Evidence of life."

He looked at the rest of the recruits, who were now staring at Ren with the kind of awe usually reserved for mythic beasts. 

"The Flash is a courier," Goram said, his voice echoing through the grounds. "He is fast. But he is also *persistent*. Speed is a gift. Persistence is a choice. Today, Recruit Ito made the choice. The rest of you made the choice to lay in the mud."

He kicked Jarl’s boot. "Up! The morning has just begun! We have shields to carry, and since Recruit Ito is so full of energy... he gets to carry the heaviest one."

Ren didn't stop smiling. He was Level 8, he had a new skill, and his feet were already vibrating for the next challenge. He looked at the heavy, iron-bound shields and felt a surge of anticipation. He was the anchor. He was the rope. And today, he was going to be the strongest thing in the training grounds. (Word Count: 1422)
