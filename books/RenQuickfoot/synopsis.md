Born in the bustling city of Silverhaven, Ren Ito is no ordinary clerk's son. With a supernatural gift for speed that had him running at just six months old, Ren becomes a local legend known as "Quickfoot". When a crisis threatens his father's career, Ren must use his extraordinary talent to navigate the chaotic streets and deliver a critical document in record time. Discover the origin of the Silverhaven Flash in this fast-paced adventure.
