# Book 2: Chapter 30: The System's Summary

The departure from the High-Hall was not the cold, formal affair that Ren's arrival had been. He was no longer a "sprout" to be observed from behind ventilation slits; he was a guest of the throne, and more importantly, a comrade of the hearth.

Thrain and Balin, the two scouts who had first intercepted him in the Obsidian Maw, served as his honor guard. As they walked through the Under-Roads—the massive, rune-etched conduits that connected the mountain's heart to the surface—they talked with an easy, professional familiarity.

"You really put the 'stand' back in Stone-Kin, Ren," Thrain said, his voice echoing off the basalt walls. He adjusted the strap of his ocular device, looking at the boy with a mix of awe and genuine liking. "Krag hasn't stopped talking about that pike-thrust. He’s already petitioning the Master-Smiths to refine the balance of his own shield."

"And Master Gror," Balin added with a chuckle. "He’s been trying to replicate that 'summer afternoon' flavor in every batch of stew since you left. He’s nearly gone through our entire supply of mountain-peppers trying to find a substitute for your southern spice."

Ren smiled, the basalt-slab treaty tucked into a custom-fitted leather sleeve within his **[Ring of Holding]**. The weight of it was more than physical; it was the weight of a promise kept.

"I’ll send more 'Cinnabar-Peel' with the first supply caravan," Ren promised. "Bram has a whole crate of it in the Silverhaven stores. Consider it a down-payment on the Iron Accord."

They reached the threshold of the Obsidian Maw—the massive iron gate that served as the final barrier between the pressurized warmth of the mountain and the cold, damp winds of the outer world. The Shadow-Gaunts that usually haunted the Maw were silent, held back by the increased patrols of the Stone-Guard.

Standing by the gate was Krag. He didn't say a word, but as Ren approached, the massive Dwarf pounded his fist against his chest in the formal salute. Ren returned it, his small fist striking his own chest with a steady, resonant thud. It was a silent acknowledgment between two warriors who had found the "marrow" in each other.

"Run fast, child," Krag rumbled, his voice like the shifting of tectonic plates. "And don't look back. The Stone will be waiting at the border."

Ren nodded, took a final deep breath of the mineral-rich air, and stepped out into the grey light of the mountain's "Skin."

As the iron gate ground shut behind him, the world changed. The rhythmic hammer-strikes and the low hum of the mercury-lamps were replaced by the whistling of the wind through the basalt pillars and the distant, lonely cry of a mountain-hawk. Ren was back on the road.

He hadn't taken ten steps before a sudden, familiar coldness bloomed behind his eyes. It was a sensation he hadn't felt in weeks—not since his time in the high meadows. It was "The System," finally waking up to account for the ripples he had created in the world's structure.

**[NOTIFICATION: MISSION COMPLETED]**
**[OBJECIVE: THE IRON ACCORD - SECURE DWARVEN SUPPORT]**
**[STATUS: CRITICAL SUCCESS]**

Ren stopped, leaning against a soot-stained basalt pillar as the blue-tinted windows of logic began to scroll across his vision.

**[SUMMARY OF ACHIEVEMENTS]**
*   **Negotiated [The Iron Accord]**: Formal pact secured between Silverhaven and the Iron Mountains. 
*   **Military Commitment**: Secured three legions of Stone-Guard for surface-border defense.
*   **Logistic Breakthrough**: Opened the Under-Roads for prioritized supply transport.
*   **Combat Evolution**: Successfully demonstrated and refined the [Iron Stand] philosophy against a Master-level opponent.
*   **Diplomatic Breakthrough**: Utilized [Secondary Skills: Culinary] to bridge cultural resonance.
*   **Aknowledgement**: Currently holding the status of [Acknowledged Messenger of the Stone-Kin].

Ren watched the summary with a mix of pride and a strange, detached curiosity. To the System, his 30-minute duel and his hours in the kitchen were just variables to be calculated and filed. But to him, they were moments of bone-deep effort and connection.

**[REWARDS GRANTED]**
*   **Experience Gained**: 15,000 XP
*   **LEVEL UP!**: Ren Ito is now Level 22.
*   **LEVEL UP!**: Ren Ito is now Level 23.
*   **LEVEL UP!**: Ren Ito is now Level 24.

Ren felt the surge of energy—the literal expansion of his mana-capacity and the tightening of his muscles. It was a physical rush that made the cold wind of the Maw feel like a warm breeze.

**[SKILL INCREASES]**
*   **[Balance Mastery]**: Level 18 -> Level 22 (New Utility unlocked: [Kinetic Redirection])
*   **[Diplomacy]**: Level 12 -> Level 15 (Bonus: [Resonant Rapport])
*   **[Culinary: Prep]**: Level 10 -> Level 15 (Bonus: [Blade Fluidity])
*   **[Sense Resonance]**: Level 15 -> Level 18 (Increased range and frequency sensitivity)
*   **[Iron Stand]**: Level 1 -> Level 8 (Passive Bonus: [Anchored Foundation])

**[STAT POINTS GRANTED: 15]**
**[DISTRIBUTION SUGGESTED: HIGH AGILITY / STAMINA]**

Ren looked at the stat points. Usually, he would have dumped them all into **[Agility]** or **[Intelligence]** to feed his speed and his mana-pool. But his time with Krag had taught him the value of "Earth." He needed to be more than just fast; he needed to be durable.

He allocated 8 points to **[Stamina]**, 4 points to **[Agility]**, and 3 points to **[Intelligence]**.

He felt his breathing deepen, his heart-rate slowing to a steady, powerful rhythm that felt like it could sustain him for days. His legs felt like tempered springs, and his mind was a razor-sharp instrument, mapping the path ahead with a level of clarity that made the grey mountain-scape look like a vibrant, detailed map.

**[FINAL SYSTEM REMARK]**
"The Pebble has found its weight. The 'Silverhaven Express' is no longer just a delivery service; it is a catalyst for the stabilization of the world's essence. run fast, Ren Ito. The Shadow is no longer merely watching. It is hunting."

The windows vanished, leaving Ren alone in the whistling silence of the mountains. 

The Shadow was hunting. He had felt it in the High-Hall, that strange, targeted frequency of the rot. It wasn't just a disease anymore; it was an adversary. And Ren was the one carrying the cure—the promise of iron that could hold the line.

He adjusted his pack, feeling the basalt slab shifting within the ring. He checked his boots, his seaxes, and the small vial of Master Bram's biscuits. He was leveled up, he was reinforced, and he was "Acknowledged."

Ren Ito sank into his starting crouch, his eyes glowing with the silver-blue light of his consolidated mana. He didn't look back at the Iron Mountains. He looked toward the horizon, where the green of the forest was a thin, distant line against the grey stone.

"Let them hunt," Ren whispered, his voice caught and carried by the wind. "I have a word to deliver."

With a sudden, explosive burst of resonance, the "Silverhaven Express" launched into the Maw. He didn't just run; he 'arrived', his feet moving with the new **[Kinetic Redirection]** that turned every impact into forward momentum. He was a streak of white and blue, a blur of motion that left a trail of vibrating stone in its wake.

The journey home had begun. (Word count: 1230)
