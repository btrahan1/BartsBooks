Ren Ito is now seven years old, a Level 8 "Omni-Apprentice", and the busiest kid in Silverhaven. Balancing the demands of four powerful mentors—a battle-hardened guard sergeant, a master chef, a brilliant apothecary, and a mysterious shadow-walker—Ren is pushing his supernatural speed and learning capacity to the absolute limit. Follow the continued adventures of the Silverhaven Express as he deconstructs every skill and proves that he isn't spreading himself too thin—he's just getting started!
