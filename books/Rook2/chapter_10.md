# Chapter 10: The Declaration


The sudden, absolute silence in the hangar was more deafening than any
explosion. The screens were black, save for a single, stark, text-only
message that had just appeared on the main display, a final, ghostly
postscript from a man who had just severed all ties with his own
species.

It was addressed to no one in particular. It was a declaration.

THE MEN YOU SENT THROUGH WILL BE FINE. BETTER THAN FINE, ACTUALLY.

Rostova and Carter stared at the words. Better than fine? What did that
mean?

BUT, THEY ARE MINE NOW.

The claim was so audacious, so absolute, it was breathtaking. He wasn't
rescuing them. He was *conscripting* them.

**I WILL** '**CURE**' **THEM... SO TO SPEAK.**

The quotation marks around the word "cure" were deeply, profoundly
unsettling. It was not a promise of healing. It was a promise of
transformation. He was not going to fix them. He was going to reforge
them.

YOU HAVE 48 HOURS TO CLEAR THE AREA. A 5-MILE RADIUS AROUND THE PORTAL
SITE. EVACUATE ALL PERSONNEL. EVERYTHING.

Rostova's jaw clenched. "He can't be serious. He has no authority to---"

The next line of text appeared, as if Rook had heard her objection from
across the dimensions and was answering it directly.

**AND BEFORE YOU COMPLAIN, AND GO ON ABOUT** "**AUTHORITY**"**, AND**
"**PROTOCOL**"**... KNOW THIS...**

There was a long pause, a moment of pure, dramatic tension.

**...THERE ARE** '**OTHER**' **THINGS IN HERE THAT YOU DON'T WANT TO
COME THROUGH. THE GREY MEN ARE THE LEAST OF YOUR WORRIES.**

The final, terrifying revelation dropped into the room like a bomb. They
had been so focused on the Grey, on their own ghosts, that they had
never considered what else might live in this dead, silent world. The
Grey weren't the predators. They were the scavengers.

48 HOURS.

The message concluded with the final, non-negotiable command, and a
chilling glimpse into Rook's new, grander, and utterly insane purpose.

THEN I AM COLLAPSING THE TUNNEL. PERMANENTLY. FROM THIS SIDE. I HAVE A
WAR TO WAGE AGAINST CREATURES FROM ANOTHER PLANET THAT YOU DIDN'T EVEN
KNOW EXISTED.

The final line was not a plea for help. It was not a warning. It was a
statement of pure, possessive, and territorial fury.

THEY SHOULD HAVE NEVER COME TO MY WORLD.

The screen went blank.

The transmission was over.

Rostova, Vance, and Carter stood in the silent hangar, grappling with
the terrible, magnificent truth. They hadn't sent a soldier on a suicide
mission. They had sent a king into his kingdom. And he had just declared
war, not on their behalf, but on his own.

He had found his "Stillness." He had found his perfect, quiet world. And
he would kill anything, human or alien, that threatened to disturb it.
