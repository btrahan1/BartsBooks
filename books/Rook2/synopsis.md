Marcus "Rook" Riley is a broken weapon. A decorated soldier with severe PTSD, he sits on death row awaiting execution. But when government agents offer him a pardon in exchange for a suicide mission through an interdimensional portal, Rook accepts—on his own terms. On the other side lies a desolate world controlled by "The Grey," psychic parasites that feed on traumatic memory. But the agents didn't realize that Rook's intense trauma would make him invisible to the predators. Armed with only his boots, a knife, and a hyper-analytical mind, Rook begins to map the world, rescue lost soldiers, and wage a private war against the dark. A gripping military sci-fi thriller about an anti-hero who finds his peace in a world of nightmares.
