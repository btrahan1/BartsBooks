# Chapter 24: The Final Deletion

The elite guards charged, their movements a blur of brutish power. They
were the pinnacle of their kind, engines of slaughter.

Jin-woo moved.

It was a paradox of motion, a combination of a whisper and a scream. His
body flowed forward with the silent, inevitable grace of ink sinking
into paper. But his hands, his intent, were a piercing shriek of pure,
focused violence. His placid blue Ki erupted from his palms, condensing
and hardening into two glowing, razor-sharp spikes of pure destruction.
They were not weapons; they were erasers, designed to delete flawed text
from the page of the world.

He did not dodge. He did not block or parry. The Scribe's Insight had
already mapped the entire 60-second conflict. He saw the trajectory of
every clumsy sword swing, every thrusting spear, as a series of flawed
geometric lines. His path was simply the most efficient, brutal line
drawn through their errors.

The first guard swung a massive, notched cleaver at his head. A lethal
blow. Jin-woo did not step back. He stepped *in*, allowing the flat of
the blade to slam into his shoulder with a meaty thud that would have
broken another man's collarbone. He barely registered it. The impact was
a calculated cost. His forward momentum brought him inside the
creature's guard, and his right hand, sheathed in its spike of light,
moved not with a warrior's slash, but with a scribe's final, definitive
period. He tapped the center of the goblin's chest. The blue Ki
discharged, and the creature's black, curdled core was not just pierced,
but utterly and instantly nullified. It collapsed like a puppet with its
strings cut.

A spear lanced at his side. A crude sword hacked at his leg. He moved
through the attacks, his robes shredding, his skin tearing. The wounds
were shallow, irrelevant data points. The Scribe's Aegis was already at
work, a cool blue fire knitting the flesh and purging the poison from
the blades before it could take root. He shrugged off the blows as a man
shrugs off rain.

His movement was a devastatingly efficient dance. He flowed between the
last two guards, a whisper of motion. His left hand erased the second,
his right erased the third. It was not a fight. It was a final,
merciless edit.

Sixty seconds.

The flap of the command tent was thrown open. The Goblin Shaman emerged,
a sneer of arrogant power on its twisted, intelligent face. It expected
to see the lone human being torn to pieces by its elite guard.

The sneer dissolved, replaced by a mask of pure, uncomprehending horror.

It surveyed the scene. Its guards were not just dead; they were broken,
dismantled things, their foul Ki extinguished from the world. And
standing among them was the human. His simple robes were shredded,
crisscrossed with shallow cuts from which a faint blue steam rose as
they healed. His face was a mask of terrifying, otherworldly calm.

Jin-woo looked at the source of the rot, the author of the pain.

"You are unwelcome here," he said, his voice as cold and quiet as the
grave. "I think it's time you moved on."

He took a single step forward, the Ki-spikes on his hands flaring with a
brilliant, final intensity.

"...permanently."
