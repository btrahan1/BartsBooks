# Chapter 28: The Second Test

They fashioned a makeshift litter from spears and cloaks, laying their
unconscious master upon it with a reverence usually reserved for a king.
The journey back was a somber, urgent procession. The elation of their
victory was gone, replaced by a heavy, gnawing anxiety. Every shallow
breath Jin-woo took was a small victory against the crushing silence.

They marched hard and fast, their own exhaustion forgotten. As they
finally crested the familiar wooded rise that overlooked their town, the
anxiety in their hearts curdled into pure, ice-cold horror.

A pillar of greasy black smoke climbed into the afternoon sky, rising
from the direction of the town's western wall. It was a sight Kael knew
all too well. It was the smoke of burning homes.

And yes, their home. The thought was not a conscious one; it was a deep,
instinctual certainty that hit all eighteen men at once. This place was
no longer a temporary sanctuary. It was theirs. And it was under attack.

A low, animalistic growl of rage rippled through the war party. Their
faces, already grim, hardened into masks of murderous fury. For a
moment, they were the raiders of their past lives again, the hunger for
violence a coppery taste in their mouths.

Kael, marching at the head of the litter, saw it. He felt the familiar,
chaotic crimson flaring in his men's Ki. He looked at the fragile, still
form of Jin-woo, at the master who had taught them to be more than just
a raging river. He looked at the smoke. The teachings of the past
weeks---the lessons of balance, purpose, and control---crashed against
the primal instinct to simply charge and kill.

The lesson won.

"Hold!" Kael's voice was not a roar, but a sharp, cracking command that
cut through the rising bloodlust. The men stopped, their knuckles white
on their spear hafts.

"Listen to me!" Kael commanded, his eyes sweeping over them, fierce and
clear. He was no longer just a captain; he was the temporary guardian of
his master's entire philosophy. "Panic is a goblin's weapon! Rage is a
raider's tool! We are the Watch! We are the wall! And we will act like
it!"

He pointed at Roric. "Roric! Team One! The fastest of you! You are
needed!" His voice dropped, taking on the resonant, instructional tone
of Jin-woo himself. "Pull upon your souls! Remember the weight of the
shackles! Remember the lightness of your freedom! Use what our master
has taught us!"

He raised his spear, pointing it directly at the town. "Do not be a
flood! Be a spear! Go, now! Assess the threat, reinforce Borin, and
break them! GO!"

Roric and the five men of his team didn't need a second command. The
unfocused rage in their eyes had been given a purpose, a direction. They
were no longer a mob; they were a surgical instrument. With a single,
unified cry, they broke from the group, sprinting down the slope toward
the smoke with a speed and silence that was not just born of strength,
but of profound, disciplined training.

Kael turned to the remaining twelve men. "The rest of you, with me! We
get the master to safety, and then we are the hammer. Move!"

They followed, carrying their broken leader toward a home under siege,
their hearts filled with a terrible, clarifying purpose. The students'
final exam had begun.
