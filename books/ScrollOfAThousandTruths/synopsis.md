Jin-woo was a monk and a scholar who became the living embodiment of the "Scroll of a Thousand Truths". Transformed by the scroll's absorption, he now perceives the world as a symphony of raw data and Ki flows. With the "Scribe's Insight", he can see the flaws in any opponent and the truth in any soul. Choosing the "Way of the Brush" over the "Way of the Fist", Jin-woo sets out on a journey to live the truth rather than just record it, starting by helping a small town oppressed by thugs. A story of power, perception, and a search for balance.
