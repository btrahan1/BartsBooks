# Chapter 14: A True Warrior

The return to the North Mine was, initially, far less majestic than Dalton had envisioned. 

Armed with his new iron sword and militia shield, he had expected to slice through the darkness like a hot knife through butter. Instead, the first thirty minutes of his third descent were defined mostly by the sound of wood clattering against stone, steel scraping against chitin, and Dalton periodically cursing as he bashed his own shins with the edge of his shield.

"A knight's armaments are... weighty," Dalton grunted, stumbling slightly as he swung the rusted sword in a wide, clumsy arc that completely missed a Level 3 Corrosive Slime. The momentum carried him forward, and he ended up hitting the slime with the flat of his shield instead.

"You're swinging it like a club, Dalton," Sam sighed from his safe vantage point near the ceiling. "It's a sword. You're supposed to use the edge. And keep that shield up! You look like a tavern sign swinging in the wind."

"The transition requires a period of adjustment, Master Sam!" Dalton retorted, recovering his footing. He thrust the sword forward this time, the rusted tip piercing the slime's core with a satisfying *pop*. "Behold! Progress!"

Despite the awkward start, Dalton was nothing if not persistent. His theatrical training had given him a certain degree of physical endurance, even if it lacked martial precision. He fought through the upper tunnels, his swings growing slightly more measured, his footwork a fraction less chaotic. He learned that the shield was not just a wall to hide behind, but a tool to bash, deflect, and control the space around him.

The true test came when they encountered a Level 5 Cave Centipede. It was smaller than the Iron-Jaw monstrosity that had killed him, but it was still a terrifying, barbed menace that moved with sickening speed.

The centipede lunged, its mandibles snapping. This time, Dalton didn't try to sidestep or parry with his weapon. He planted his feet, braced his left arm, and brought the militia shield up.

*CRACK!*

The impact shuddered up his arm, making his teeth rattle, but the shield held. The centipede recoiled, temporarily stunned by the solid wall of pine and iron. 

**[ SKILL UNLOCKED: BLOCK (LEVEL 1) ]**
**[ EFFECT: SUCCESSFUL BLOCKS NEGATE 20% OF INCOMING PHYSICAL DAMAGE ]**

"Aha!" Dalton cried, his eyes lighting up as he saw the blue text. "The fortress holds!"

Before the beast could recover, Dalton drove the rusted sword forward, driving it deep into the gap between the centipede's armored segments. The creature shrieked, a sound like tearing metal, and dissolved into a cloud of grey mist.

As the mist cleared, two blue panes of light flared into existence simultaneously, illuminating the dark tunnel.

**[ LOOT ACQUIRED: RUSTED IRON HELMET ]**
**[ LEVEL UP! ]**
**[ DALTON IS NOW LEVEL 5 ]**
**[ MILESTONE REACHED: BASE CLASS SELECTION UNLOCKED ]**

Dalton ignored the level-up prompt for a moment, his attention entirely captured by the heavy, dented iron bowl resting on the stone floor where the centipede had died. 

"Master Sam," Dalton said, picking up the helmet. "It appears our many-legged foe was carrying a piece of armory. How peculiar. Do these beasts often hoard the trappings of men?"

"Arcadia's loot system is a mystery, Dalton," Sam said, fluttering down to inspect the helm. "Don't question it. Just assume the centipede ate a guardsman a few years ago and couldn't digest the hat."

Dalton placed the helmet on his head. It was a tight fit over his messy hair, and the rust smelled faintly of old blood, but it completed the ensemble. With his rat-fur vest, iron sword, pine shield, and rusted helm, he looked like an actor who had raided a particularly chaotic prop department. 

**[ ITEM EQUIPPED: RUSTED IRON HELMET ]**
**[ EFFECT: +3 DEFENSE ]**

"And now, for the grandest prize of all!" Dalton announced, turning his attention to the second blue screen. 

**[ PLEASE SELECT A BASE CLASS: ]**
*   **[ WARRIOR ]**
*   **[ ROGUE ]**
*   **[ RANGER ]**
*   **[ MAGE (UNAVAILABLE - INT/WIS TOO LOW) ]**

Dalton didn't even read the other options. He pressed his finger against the word 'Warrior' with the solemnity of a king signing a treaty.

**[ CLASS SELECTED: WARRIOR ]**
**[ CLASS BONUS APPLIED: +1 STRENGTH PER LEVEL ]**
**[ RETROACTIVE BONUSES APPLIED: +4 STRENGTH ]**
**[ 1 ATTRIBUTE POINT ACQUIRED. PLEASE ASSIGN. ]**

Dalton stared at the screen, a slow, magnificent smile spreading across his face beneath the rusted brim of his helmet. "Master Sam! Do you see this? The path of the Warrior yields a passive boon to my might! The very act of leveling now increases my strength! I am becoming a titan!"

"You're becoming slightly less pathetic," Sam corrected, though even the imp sounded mildly impressed. "That retroactive bump to your Strength is going to help a lot with that sword. Now, assign your free attribute point. And please, think about it."

Dalton raised his finger, fully intending to tap 'Strength' for the fifth time in a row. "A titan requires titanic power, Master Sam! I shall further fortify my striking arm!"

"Balance, Dalton. Balance!" Sam yelled, flying directly in front of Dalton's face and pointing to the newly bruised shins peeking out from under his rat-fur leggings. "You have the hitting power now, but you're still tripping over your own feet! A sword requires finesse. A shield requires agility. Put the point in Dexterity before you accidentally decapitate yourself!"

Dalton paused. He looked at his bruised shins. He looked at the heavy, awkward sword in his hand. He remembered the feeling of the centipede's mandibles snapping around his waist. 

"A knight must be a hurricane," Dalton mused, lowering his finger to the second stat on the list. "Powerful, yes, but also swift. The stage demands choreography as much as it demands volume."

He tapped the screen.

**[ ATTRIBUTE ASSIGNED: DEXTERITY ]**
**[ DEXTERITY: 12 ]**

**[ CURRENT STATS: ]**
*   **STRENGTH: 16 (BASE: 12 + CLASS: 4)**
*   **DEXTERITY: 12**
*   **CONSTITUTION: 12**
*   **INTELLIGENCE: 10**
*   **WISDOM: 10**
*   **CHARISMA: 12 (PENALTY INCLUDED)**

"Behold!" Dalton boomed, his voice echoing down the dark tunnel. The rusted helmet gave his voice a slight, metallic reverb that he found deeply satisfying. "I am no longer a vagrant! I am a Warrior of Arcadia! The first true step upon the path to Knighthood has been taken! Let the beasts of the deep tremble, for Sir Dalton has found his class, and his blade thirsts for justice!"

Sam rubbed his ears. "Great. You're a warrior. Now, can we please be quiet? The Iron-Jaw is down here somewhere, and I don't think it cares what your character sheet says."

"Let it come!" Dalton declared, banging his sword against his shield. The loud, metallic *CLANG* rang out through the mine. "The protagonist is finally ready for the main event!"
