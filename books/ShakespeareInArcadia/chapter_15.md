# Chapter 15: Rematch

The deeper tunnels of the North Mine were no longer a terrifying labyrinth to Sir Dalton; they had become his rehearsal space. With his new class and boosted Strength, the rusted iron sword felt less like an awkward piece of metal and more like an extension of his own arm. He moved through the gloom with growing confidence, his footwork stabilizing as his Dexterity aided his balance.

After a dozen more kills—a mixture of oversized spiders and a few particularly aggressive rock moles—a new, brilliant blue pane flared before his eyes.

**[ SKILL UNLOCKED: HEROIC STRIKE (LEVEL 1) ]**
**[ EFFECT: DELIVERS A POWERFUL BLOW DEALING 150% WEAPON DAMAGE. COSTS 10 STAMINA. ]**

Dalton stopped dead in his tracks, staring at the screen as if it were a love letter from the Muse herself. 

"Master Sam!" Dalton whispered reverently. "The phantom has recognized my true nature! A 'Heroic Strike'! It is a title befitting a final act! A maneuver of such profound justice that the very system bows to its narrative weight!"

He immediately tested it on a passing Level 3 Cave Spider. Dalton lunged forward, raising his rusted sword high. 

"Heroic Strike!" he bellowed, his voice echoing off the stone walls with enough force to dislodge dust from the ceiling. 

The blade came down, surrounded by a faint, momentary glow of white light. It hit the spider with devastating force, cleaving through its carapace and instantly dissolving it into mist. 

"You don't have to yell out the name, Dalton!" Sam complained, hovering down from the ceiling to inspect the spider's meager loot. "Just do it! It's a mental trigger, not a vocal command. And watch your stamina! You only have a hundred points of it. Keep yelling and swinging like that, and you'll be too tired to lift your shield."

"Of course I have to yell out the name!" Dalton protested, panting slightly but grinning from ear to ear. "It's part of me now! To strike silently is the way of the assassin, the rogue! A Knight declares his intent so that the villain may know the face of his doom!"

Before Sam could offer a cynical retort, the temperature in the tunnel seemed to drop. And then came the sound. 

*Clack-clack-clack-clack.*

It was the rhythmic, rapid-fire clicking of dozens of barbed legs against stone, accompanied by the wet snapping of massive iron mandibles. 

Sam turned pale, darting behind Dalton's helmet. "Time to go, Dalton! That's the Level 8! You're only Level 5! You're not ready! The script says run!"

Dalton did not run. He planted his feet, gripping his militia shield tightly, his eyes narrowing as the massive, armored bulk of the Iron-Jaw Centipede slithered into the torchlight. 

"I may not be ready, Master Sam," Dalton said, his voice dropping into a low, steady register completely devoid of his usual theatrical bluster. "But I am willing. Come to me, fiend! Feel my wrath!"

The centipede lunged, a terrifying blur of grey chitin and gnashing jaws.

Dalton brought the shield up just in time. The impact was horrific. The mandibles crashed against the banded iron, sending a shockwave up Dalton's arm that threatened to dislocate his shoulder. 

**[ BLOCK SUCCESSFUL. DAMAGE REDUCED. ]**
**[ DAMAGE RECEIVED: 25 ]**
**[ HEALTH: 85 / 110 ]**

"Heroic Strike!" Dalton roared, pushing back against the beast and driving his sword forward. 

The blade, empowered by the skill and his new Warrior strength, bit into the centipede's armor. It didn't bounce off this time; it carved a shallow, jagged trench through the chitin. The monster shrieked, whipping its body around and slamming its tail into Dalton's side.

What followed was a brutal, grueling dance of survival. This wasn't a quick duel; it was a desperate struggle for dominance. Dalton blocked, parried, and struck, his breath coming in ragged gasps. The rat-fur armor was quickly shredded by glancing blows, and the militia shield began to splinter under the relentless assault of the iron jaws.

**[ DAMAGE RECEIVED: 30 ]**
**[ HEALTH: 55 / 110 ]**

"Dalton, fall back!" Sam cried, frantically throwing tiny, useless sparks of magic at the beast's eyes. 

"I... hold... the line!" Dalton wheezed, his rusted helmet dented, his vision swimming with exhaustion. 

He swung again. And again. The centipede was bleeding a foul, dark ichor, but its attacks hadn't slowed. It lunged high, bypassing the shield and sinking a barbed leg deep into Dalton's shoulder.

**[ FATAL WARNING! ]**
**[ DAMAGE RECEIVED: 45 ]**
**[ HEALTH: 10 / 110 ]**

Dalton dropped to one knee, the pain blinding him. The centipede reared up, its mandibles spread wide for the killing blow. This was the moment. The exact same moment where he had died before. 

Dalton looked up at the descending jaws. He didn't see death. He saw the climax.

He poured every remaining ounce of his stamina into his right arm. He didn't try to block. He planted his back foot, using his kneeling position to launch himself upward, directly into the creature's guard. 

"HEROIC STRIKE!" Dalton screamed, his voice cracking with the sheer force of his will. 

He drove the rusted iron sword upward, bypassing the heavy armor of the creature's head and sinking the blade entirely into the soft, unarmored underside of its throat. The faint white glow of the skill flared brightly in the dim tunnel.

The centipede froze. It let out a single, gurgling click, and then its massive body dissolved into a towering cloud of grey mist, raining heavy dust down upon the kneeling warrior.

Dalton stayed on his knee, his breath rattling in his chest. His health bar hovered at a terrifying 10 points. His shield arm hung uselessly at his side. But he was alive.

"You... you crazy, glorious idiot," Sam whispered, landing softly on the dented brim of Dalton's helmet. "You actually did it."

Dalton couldn't speak. He simply smiled, letting his sword tip rest on the stone. 

Then, the blue screens began to cascade. 

**[ IRON-JAW CENTIPEDE SLAIN! ]**
**[ +150 EXP ]**
**[ LEVEL UP! ]**
**[ DALTON IS NOW LEVEL 6 ]**

But Dalton wasn't looking at the experience. He was looking at the small pile of loot the beast had left behind in the dirt. 

There, gleaming in the torchlight, was a single, perfect silver coin resting beside four copper pieces. But beneath the coins lay something even more magnificent. 

It was a chest piece of interlocking metal rings. It was heavy, covered in a fine layer of rust, and clearly designed for a man slightly wider than Dalton, but it was real, undeniable armor.

**[ LOOT ACQUIRED: 1 SILVER, 4 COPPER ]**
**[ LOOT ACQUIRED: RUSTED MAIL HAUBERK ]**
**[ EFFECT: +15 DEFENSE, +5 CONSTITUTION ]**
**[ PENALTY: -5% MOVEMENT SPEED ]**

Dalton reached out with a trembling hand and lifted the heavy chainmail. He let the shredded remains of his rat-fur vest fall to the floor, sliding the cold, heavy metal over his head. It smelled of old oil and iron.

He stood up, the chainmail clinking with a sound that belonged on a battlefield, not a stage.

"Master Sam," Dalton rasped, his eyes shining beneath his helm. "It is a bit rusty... but it's a start."
