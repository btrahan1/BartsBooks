# Chapter 24: Level Up

It took less than five minutes for the adrenaline to completely leave Dalton’s system. He didn't even make it to a tent. Right there, in the center of the muddy bandit camp, surrounded by captured villains and his weary party members, Sir Dalton began to snore. 

It was a loud, rhythmic snore, amplified slightly by the acoustics of his rusted iron helmet, which he had refused to take off even while unconscious. 

"He sleeps like the dead," Cal noted, unstringing his bow and taking a seat on an overturned crate. "I suppose he earned it. I still can't believe he actually beat Gregor one-on-one."

"I can't believe he refused my heal," Deb muttered, though there was a fond, exasperated smile on her face as she looked at the sleeping Warrior. 

With their 'meat shield' out of commission, the rest of the Vanguard set to work. Shade melted back into the shadows to keep watch, though the camp was thoroughly pacified. Dawn was only a few hours away, and the pale light of morning would make the march back to Oakhaven much easier. 

The six surviving bandits—the remnants of the ambushed scouts and the few who had surrendered after Gregor's fall—were securely tied to the wheels of the stolen merchant wagons. They looked terrified, expecting to be executed at any moment by the shadow-dwelling Rogue or incinerated by the fiery Mage.

Instead, Deb walked over to them. 

The bandits flinched as she raised her glowing blue staff, but the Healer simply laid the crystal against a nasty sword-gash on one of the captive's arms. The blue light washed over the wound, knitting it closed. 

"Quiet now," Deb said softly, moving to the next injured man. "You'll face the Reeve's justice in town, but there's no need to bleed to death in the dirt."

The bandits watched her with wide, reverent eyes. The act of mercy calmed them considerably, the fight bleeding out of them completely. 

Ferna leaned against a nearby tree, tossing a small ember from hand to hand. She looked at Deb, then over at the loudly snoring Dalton. "He'd love that, you know. Sparing the defeated. Healing the wicked so they might face proper justice. He'd probably give a twenty-minute speech about it."

"Which is exactly why we're letting him sleep," Cal chuckled. 

The sun finally crested the edge of the ravine, sending shafts of pale morning light down into the camp. The light hit Dalton’s visor, and a moment later, the familiar, celestial chime of the System rang in his ears.

Dalton awoke with a start, sitting up so fast his chainmail clattered loudly. He didn't reach for his sword; he reached for the glowing blue screen that had materialized directly in front of his face.

**[ SYSTEM ANNOUNCEMENT ]**
**[ QUEST COMPLETE: THE BANDIT THREAT ]**
**[ EXPERIENCE AWARDED ]**
**[ LEVEL UP! ]**
**[ DALTON IS NOW LEVEL 7 ]**
**[ 1 ATTRIBUTE POINT ACQUIRED. PLEASE ASSIGN. ]**

"Ah! Master Sam!" Dalton cried, his voice slightly hoarse from sleep and theatrical shouting. "The phantom brings tidings of the dawn! Level Seven! The climb continues!"

Sam fluttered out of a nearby tent, rubbing his eyes. "Yeah, yeah. Good job. Check the next screen. You did something incredibly stupid last night, and the System has decided to reward you for it."

Dalton swiped the air, revealing a second, golden notification. 

**[ SPECIAL ACHIEVEMENT UNLOCKED: REFUSING MAGICAL AID DURING A FATAL DUEL TO PRESERVE COMBAT INTEGRITY ]**
**[ NEW TITLE ACQUIRED: HONORABLE ]**
**[ EFFECT: +1 TO STRENGTH, DEXTERITY, CONSTITUTION, AND CHARISMA ]**
**[ PENALTY: -1 TO WISDOM ]**

Dalton stared at the screen. The stat boosts were solid. A plus-one to four different base attributes was a welcome reward for his unwavering principles. But his eyes immediately locked onto the single red line at the bottom. 

"Minus one to Wisdom?" Dalton frowned, an indignant rumble starting in his chest. "The phantom mocks me! It suggests that to fight with honor is to lack sense!"

Sam burst into laughter, hovering near Dalton's helmet. "Dalton, you refused a heal when you were bleeding out in the mud against a guy twice your size! The System literally thinks your sense of honor makes you dumber! It's a miracle it didn't give you a minus five!"

Dalton harumphed, crossing his arms over his rusted mail. He looked at the broad stat gains, then at the single penalty. 

"I guess I'll take it," Dalton muttered. It was hard to argue with a well-rounded boost to his combat prowess. 

He pulled up his stat sheet, looking at his newly acquired attribute point from reaching Level 7. He had planned to put it into Strength, eager to further his Warrior progression. But he could not abide a red number on his ledger. A Knight must possess wisdom as much as strength. 

With a decisive tap, he placed his free stat point directly back into Wisdom, negating the penalty. 

**[ ATTRIBUTE ASSIGNED: WISDOM ]**
**[ WISDOM: 11 ]**

Dalton stood up, dusting the mud from his armor. He looked out at his party—Cal stringing his bow, Ferna yawning, Deb finishing her rounds with the prisoners, and Shade lurking near the treeline. He felt stronger, faster, and far more resilient than he had just twenty-four hours ago. 

"A small price to pay for honor!" Dalton declared to the waking camp, his voice ringing with renewed vigor. "Come, my stalwart companions! Let us march our captives to Oakhaven and collect our well-deserved bounty! The Vanguard returns in triumph!"
