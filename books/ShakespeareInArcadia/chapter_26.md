# Chapter 26: Reputation

The celebration at the Golden Pottle that evening made the previous night's festivities look like a quiet funeral. Word had spread rapidly that the Vanguard had not only cleared the mines but had entirely dismantled the bandit threat on the South Road. The villagers of Oakhaven packed into the tavern, overflowing into the muddy street, eager to buy a round for their new heroes.

At the epicenter of the chaos stood Dalton. 

He had commandeered two large oak tables, pushing them together to serve as a makeshift stage. Still wearing his new, gleaming chainmail, he was mid-performance, seamlessly weaving fragments of Shakespearean soliloquies into the recounting of the ambush.

"And so, the giant Gregor raised his blade, seeking to cleave me in twain!" Dalton boomed, his voice carrying over the din of clinking mugs. "But I stood firm! *'Cowards die many times before their deaths; the valiant never taste of death but once!'* I stepped inside his guard! A Heroic Strike! The beast fell!"

The crowd cheered wildly. Dalton then proceeded to act out his own victory, raising his arms in triumph before stiffening like a board and perfectly re-enacting his unconscious faceplant directly onto the tavern table. 

The villagers erupted into roars of laughter and applause. It was entirely self-deprecating, utterly dramatic, and the most entertaining thing Oakhaven had seen in years. 

High above the taproom, perched on a dusty rafter, Sam rested his head in his tiny hands and let out a prolonged groan of dismay. 

At a corner table, slightly removed from the spilling ale and flying elbows, the rest of the Vanguard watched their frontline fighter with varying degrees of amusement. 

"You know," Deb said, her eyes tracking Dalton as he stood back up to take a sweeping bow, "we need to protect him."

Cal took a slow sip of his ale, raising an eyebrow. "Protect him? He took on five guys at once and then killed an Elite who was a level higher than him. I think he can handle himself."

"He survived by the skin of his teeth and because I spammed minor heals," Deb corrected softly. "He fights with his heart, not his head. But look at them." She gestured to the cheering villagers. "They believe in him. He makes them feel safe. He has a spark... he could be truly great someday."

Ferna snorted, playing with a small ember over her mug of cider. "He's going to get himself killed trying to negotiate a peace treaty with a troll, that's what he's going to do. But I'll admit, he keeps things interesting."

Shade, sitting in the darkest corner of the booth with his hood still pulled low, simply grunted into his mug. "He's loud. He's reckless. He has no concept of stealth." The Rogue paused, taking a long drink. "Maybe he could be great. He's no coward, that's for sure."

Back on the tables, Dalton was preparing to launch into the second act—a detailed description of the loot—when the air in front of him shimmered with a brilliant, golden light. 

Dalton froze. His mouth stayed open, his arm raised mid-gesture. 

The tavern immediately fell silent. The villagers held their breath, assuming this sudden, dramatic pause was part of the performance. 

Dalton didn't move. He stared at the golden pane of light that only he and Sam could see. Slowly, he lowered his arm. He leaned off the edge of the table, turning his head toward the rafters. 

"Master Sam," Dalton whispered, pitching his voice so only the imp could hear. 

Sam fluttered down, landing on Dalton's pauldron. "What? Did you pull a muscle doing the faceplant?"

"Sam," Dalton whispered again, his eyes wide. "What is 'Reputation'? And why did I just receive five hundred points of it?"

Sam blinked. He looked at the glowing screen hovering in front of Dalton. 

**[ REGIONAL MILESTONE REACHED: HERO OF OAKHAVEN ]**
**[ YOU HAVE FREED THE TOWN FROM TWO MAJOR THREATS. ]**
**[ REPUTATION INCREASED: +500 (OAKHAVEN / SOUTH REGION) ]**
**[ CURRENT STANDING: REVERED ]**

Sam let out a low whistle. A flat five-hundred point reputation drop was massive. It usually took players weeks of grinding fetch-quests for farmers to earn that kind of regional standing. 

"Reputation, Dalton," Sam said, a rare grin spreading across his impish face, "is exactly what it sounds like. It means these people know your name. It means they trust you. It means you're not just a vagrant with a sword anymore; you're a recognized figure in this region."

"Revered," Dalton read, the word rolling off his tongue with profound reverence. 

"And," Sam added, tapping the side of Dalton's helmet, "do you remember the conversation we had outside the North Mine? About the special requirements for the Knight class?"

Dalton’s breath hitched. He remembered. The Warrior base class. A noble steed. Sworn fealty to a Lord. And... 

"High honor and reputation," Dalton finished, his voice trembling slightly. 

"Exactly," Sam said. "You're a Level 7 Warrior. You have the 'Honorable' title. And now you have a 'Revered' reputation standing in the South Region."

Dalton looked out at the sea of faces watching him in the tavern. They weren't just villagers anymore. They were *his* audience. *His* people. 

He had completed three of the four requirements. All that remained was a Lord to serve, and a horse to ride. 

The curtain was finally rising on the main event.
