Dalton is a thirty-year-old stagehand at the Globe Theatre in 1600s London. While he sweeps floors and carries stools, his mind is filled with the soaring verse of Shakespeare. Adopting a persona of phantom nobility and chivalry, Dalton speaks in flowery language and lives by a code of honor forged from theater scripts. Armed with a rusted rapier and boundless imagination, he sees himself as a true knight. Little does he know that his commitment to the "performance" is about to take him to a real stage of magic and danger. Discover the origin of Sir Dalton!
