# Chapter 45: Unkie?

The ruined mahogany stroller sat in the center of the square, a silent testament to the new reality of Twin Rivers. Lord Alaric looked at the splintered wood, then back up at the infant still perched confidently on the back of the massive Alpha wolf. 

By some mental command that no human ears heard, Wolfie suddenly shifted. The giant beast hunched down, lowering his belly flat against the cobblestones until his slate-grey fur brushed the blooming clover. 

Samuel didn't hesitate. With a happy gurgle, he slid off the wolf’s back, his little armor-weave boots hitting the ground with a solid, unexpectedly heavy *thud*. 

He didn't crawl. He didn't wait for his mother. 

At four weeks old, Samuel locked his golden eyes onto the shiny silver breastplate of the Governor and took his first steps. He wobbled unsteadily, his arms out wide for balance, moving straight toward Alaric with the relentless, terrifying momentum of a miniature boulder. 

Alaric’s imperial guard tensed, their hands instinctively dropping toward their sword hilts. 

"Stand down," Alaric hissed out the side of his mouth, his own heart skipping a beat as the most dangerous infant in the Empire barreled toward his legs. 

Samuel tripped over a slightly raised cobblestone, pitching forward. 

Alaric didn't think. The hardened political veteran, a man who measured every action in ounces of gold and political leverage, simply reacted. He dropped to one knee and threw his arms out, reflexively catching the child before he hit the stone. 

"Oof!" Alaric grunted loudly, his eyes widening in genuine shock. 

The weight was astounding. It was like catching a cannonball wrapped in silk. His shoulders strained against the sudden twenty-five pounds of solid, marble-dense muscle. 

"Woah!" Alaric exclaimed, shifting his stance to keep from falling over backwards under the weight of the child. He looked down at the golden-eyed boy, who was now gripping the edges of his fur-lined cloak with an iron grip. "What are they feeding you, boy? Iron ore?"

A rare, genuine smile broke through the Governor’s usually stoic, diplomatic mask. It was a smile that hadn't been seen since before he was stationed in the harsh northern district—a smile that spoke of simple, human astonishment. 

Greg chuckled from a few paces away, stepping forward but not interfering. "Mostly venison stew and Clara's 'Verdant' barley, My Lord. He has quite the appetite."

Alaric bounced the child slightly on his knee, the sheer density of the infant requiring actual effort. Samuel stared up at him, those piercing, ancient-looking eyes analyzing the silver trim of Alaric’s armor, the lines of his face, and the remnants of the "Spark" he had learned during his extended stay on the Farm. 

Then, Samuel opened his mouth. 

The booming gurgle from earlier was gone. Instead, the child formed a clear, distinct, and highly impossible two-syllable word. 

"Unkie?" 

The square went dead silent. 

Alaric froze, the smile sliding right off his face, replaced by a look of utter, profound bewilderment. He looked at Samuel, then up at Greg, then back down at Samuel. 

"Did he just...?" Alaric whispered, his voice cracking. 

"I think he did," Clara laughed, stepping up beside the Governor and resting a hand on his shoulder. Her glowing presence immediately warmed the chilly air around them. "He’s been trying to talk for a week now. I suppose he likes your armor."

"Unkie!" Samuel repeated, louder this time. The word vibrated with that same strange, heavy Ki that had broken the stroller. He reached up and grabbed the edge of Alaric’s silver breastplate, pulling himself closer until his nose was almost touching the Governor’s. 

"He's one month old," Alaric said, his brain struggling to process the logistics of infant development against the reality of the Demigod currently attempting to unbuckle his armor. "He shouldn't be walking. He definitely shouldn't be talking. And he absolutely shouldn't be calling the District Governor his... his uncle."

"Welcome to Twin Rivers, My Lord," Silas boomed from the doorway of the forge, wiping tears of mirth from his soot-stained eyes. "Where the impossible is just a Tuesday."

Alaric looked around. The twelve timber-wolves were sitting in a perfect ring, their eyes fixed on him. The thirty-two members of the Guard were watching with barely suppressed grins. The legendary Smith was standing there like a proud father, and the Priestess was literally glowing with amusement. 

Alaric looked back down at the golden-eyed boy in his arms. 

"Well," Alaric said, letting out a long, resigned breath that sounded suspiciously like a chuckle. "I suppose there are worse titles in the Empire than 'Uncle' to the Mountain-Shaker."

Samuel giggled, a sound that sent a warm, pleasant vibration through Alaric’s chest armor, settling directly over his heart. 

"But," Alaric added, looking up at Greg with a suddenly serious expression. "If he asks for a pony, Smith... I’m buying him a heavily armored war-rhino. I won't have him breaking the livestock."

Greg threw his head back and laughed, the sound mingling with the bright, ringing chatter of the village. 

The tribute was complete. The quarantine was lifted. And Lord Alaric, Governor of Oakhaven, had just been officially adopted by the most terrifying, miraculous family in the North. 

This wasn't just about Twin Rivers anymore. The family was growing, and the Empire was about to find out exactly what happened when you let a Dragon raise a son.
