# Chapter 73: The Elves

The Ancient Glades of Alveron did not welcome outsiders. The trees themselves seemed to close ranks as King Valerius III and his Royal Escort rode deeper into the eternal, emerald twilight of the forest. 

They did not make it far. 

In a clearing ringed by massive, ancient silver-bark trees, the path simply vanished. Sitting atop a throne woven entirely from living vines and blooming midnight-orchids was the Elven Queen, Elara. She was breathtakingly beautiful, ageless, and radiated an aura of profound, detached arrogance. 

Surrounding the clearing, completely invisible until they chose not to be, were a hundred Elven archers, their bows already drawn and aimed directly at the Imperial Guard. 

"What brings you here, mortal?" Queen Elara asked, her voice like wind through crystal chimes, carrying no warmth. "We have no dealings with man."

King Valerius did not flinch at the drawn bows. He slid from his massive warhorse with a heavy clank of armor and offered a deep, formal bow before the Elven throne. 

"I do not come on behalf of man," Valerius stated, his voice ringing with the authority of the Emperor. "I come on behalf of all of mankind. And elf-kind. And dwarf-kind."

The Queen raised a delicate, perfectly arched eyebrow. 

Valerius stepped forward, refusing to be intimidated by the ancient magic of the Glades. "We have come to our final hour, Your Grace. The Lich King has risen from the abyss. He has awakened ten Drakes of Shadow, and they will soon rule the sky."

Elara adjusted her flowing, silken robes, looking thoroughly bored by the apocalyptic news. 

"Sounds like a human problem to me," the Queen sniffed dismissively. "The wars of short-lived creatures are so repetitive."

"At first, yes, perhaps," Valerius agreed, keeping his tone perfectly even. "But once they have burned our cities to ash and shattered our shield-walls, they will not stop. They will turn their green flames on your beloved forests."

"Let them come," Elara challenged, a cold, fierce pride flashing in her eyes. "The Glades have never fallen. We will be ready."

"Perhaps," replied Valerius, his voice hardening into cold steel. "But will you be ready for the Lich King himself? And an army of fifty thousand Dark Dwarves? And the entirety of the corrupted Horde hitting your borders at once?"

He watched the Queen pale slightly. The sheer logistical math of that many enemies was impossible to ignore, even for an immortal. 

"Your enemies, the Dwarves, have answered The Pact," Valerius continued, pressing his advantage. "They have sealed themselves in the deep, bleeding to hold the Draguar beneath the earth so you don't have to face them here. The Imperial Army holds the Dark Tide on the eastern plains. And soon, the lines will be joined by The Chosen from Twin Rivers."

Valerius paused, letting the name hang in the air. 

"I believe you have met their Valkyries?" the Emperor asked softly. 

Elara’s posture shifted. Her haughty demeanor cracked. The Elves of Alveron had indeed hosted the ten girls for the last year, teaching them the ancient arts of the bow. The Elves had sensed the terrifying, boundless potential within the Valkyries. They knew exactly what the Clan of Twin Rivers was capable of. 

The Queen sucked in a deep, unsteady breath. "Even with the Valkyries... we cannot fight the Horde, Valerius! We are archers, scholars, and weavers of magic. We do not have the heavy infantry to hold a line against Trolls and Orcs!"

"We don't want you to fight the Horde!" Valerius roared, his royal composure finally breaking into desperate, passionate pleading. "My Legions will fight the Horde! The Fjellbjorn will crush the Trolls! We will hold the ground!"

Valerius fell to one knee, a reigning Emperor bowing before a foreign Queen. 

"We want you to help control the skies," Valerius beseeched, his voice thick with emotion. "The Valkyries and my grandson’s dragon are riding to meet the Drakes, but they are outnumbered. They cannot hold the sky alone. None other than you and the Valkyries have the range and the magic to reach them. For The Pact, Queen Elara, I beseech you. Ride with us."

Silence fell over the clearing. The hundred invisible archers held their breath. 

Queen Elara looked at the kneeling Emperor. She thought of the ten bright, fiercely determined girls who had trained in her forests. She thought of the Draguar, and the Dwarves bleeding in the dark to honor an ancient promise. 

Slowly, the Queen stood up from her throne of vines. 

"Lower your bows," Elara commanded her hidden guard. The tension in the clearing instantly vanished as a hundred bowstrings were relaxed. 

She looked down at Valerius. 

"The Elves do not march for the Empire," Queen Elara stated, her voice ringing with ancient, terrifying power. "We march for The Pact. We march for the Valkyries. Gather your escort, Emperor. The Archers of the Ancient Glades will take the sky."
