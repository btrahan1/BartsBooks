# Chapter 75: Drake Fire

The sky turned a sickly, luminescent green as the ten Drakes of Shadow opened their jaws. 

The first wave of Drake Fire was indiscriminate. It was a torrential downpour of highly concentrated necrotic magic, melting the frozen mud into bubbling slag. The Drakes, mindless beasts driven only by an ancient, corrupt hunger, didn't care where they aimed. They gleefully sprayed their fire across the battlefield, savoring the screams of the dying. 

It didn't matter to them that the majority of those screams were coming from their own side. Hundreds of Orcs, Trolls, and Kobolds were instantly vaporized in the crossfire, their ashes mixing with the terrified cries of the Imperial frontline. 

Standing on a raised ridge behind the Horde’s main advance, Kargaroth the Invincible watched his aerial artillery slaughtering his own troops. 

"Fools!" the Lich King screamed, his raspy voice magically amplified to boom across the entire valley. "The Humans! Kill the Humans!"

High above, the ten Drakes paused their chaotic strafing runs, their sluggish, necrotic brains struggling to process the command. 

That pause was a fatal mistake. 

Down on the battlefield, the ten Valkyries did not pause. Operating with the flawless, divine synchronicity of true Avatars, they immediately locked onto the lowest-flying Drake, which had isolated itself near the western flank. 

The ten girls stood up on the saddles of their sprinting timber-wolves. They drew their iron-wood bows, the "Verdant Pulse" flaring so brightly that they looked like ten miniature suns. 

They fired as one. 

A concentrated beam of pure emerald life-energy shot upward, striking the undead Drake directly in the chest. The effect was devastating. The necrotic magic holding the beast’s skeletal frame together violently rejected the pure life-force. The Drake shrieked, its ribcage exploding outward as the green shadow-fire in its belly was snuffed out. 

The beast plummeted toward the earth like a heavy stone. 

Before it even hit the ground, the Fjellbjorn were there. The seven Crag-Bears vaulted over the burning debris, and fourteen silver-and-steel axes swung in perfect, chaotic unison. The falling Drake was shattered into a thousand pieces of unmoving bone. 

Nine Drakes to go. 

Then, cutting through the roar of the fire and the screams of the Horde, came a sound that made King Valerius, standing with his Legions, weep with relief. 

It was a trumpet. A long, clear, piercing note of silver horn that originated from the western treeline. The Elves of Alveron had arrived. 

The sudden, perfectly pitched sound distracted the remaining Drakes for exactly one second. It was all Elsie needed. 

The massive dragon folded her diamond-hard wings and went into a blindingly fast dive. She didn't breathe fire; she breathed a concentrated beam of white-hot, pure solar energy. The stream of golden light slammed into the side of the nearest Drake, instantly incinerating its right wing. 

The Shadow Drake roared in panic, spinning wildly out of control. 

As Elsie pulled up from her dive, Samuel didn't hold on. The Demigod launched himself from the dragon’s back, dropping straight down toward the flailing, burning Drake. 

He fell like an anvil from heaven, the Twin Axes of the Dragon trailing long ribbons of golden fire. Samuel hit the Drake’s long, serpentine neck with bone-shattering force. Before the beast could even register the impact, Samuel swung both axes in a tight, scissor-like arc. 

He beheaded the Drake before the rest of its body hit the ground. 

Samuel landed heavily in the mud, absorbing the impact with his massive legs. He stood up, golden fire swirling around him, and stared down Kargaroth across the sea of Orcs. 

Eight Drakes remaining. 

The Lich King realized his blunt-force tactics were failing. The Avatars were too fast, too coordinated, and possessed too much raw power to be picked off casually. 

Kargaroth raised his skeletal hands and let loose a wail. 

It wasn't a scream of anger. It was a concentrated shockwave of pure, necrotic dread. The wail hit the battlefield like a physical wall. The Imperial Legions dropped to their knees, clutching their ears. Even the Fjellbjorn and the Valkyries stumbled, their divine sparks flickering under the sheer, oppressive weight of the dark magic. 

The eight remaining Drakes responded instantly to their master's call. They ceased their chaotic flying and pulled up high into the rotting clouds, far out of range of the Valkyries' arrows. 

They began to fly in a tight, overlapping circle, their jaws dripping with fresh green fire. They were disengaging from the melee to prepare for a coordinated, organized run that would blanket the entire valley in unavoidable death. 

Samuel tightened his grip on his axes, his cosmic eyes narrowing as he watched the sky. The warmup was over. The Lich King was finally taking the battle seriously.
