# Chapter 77: The Finale

The inner chamber of the obsidian fortress was entirely empty. 

Snake stood perfectly still, his mind working through the tactical reality of his situation. The phylactery was the most important magical artifact in existence. It wasn't just a container; it was the Lich King’s anchor to the mortal plane. It required constant necrotic nourishment to maintain its integrity. 

"Now, if I were an immortal, undead warlord..." Snake murmured to himself, his dark eyes scanning the massive, empty room. "...where would I hide my immortality?"

His gaze naturally drifted toward the back of the chamber. Sitting on a raised dais was a massive throne carved entirely from human bone and dark iron. It was the focal point of the room's magical energy. 

Snake walked slowly toward the dais. "Surely he's not that arrogant, is he?"

Snake reached out with his free hand and tapped the solid iron skull that formed the center of the throne's backrest. It echoed with a hollow, thrumming vibration. The phylactery wasn't hidden in a secret vault beneath the mountain. Kargaroth had literally been sitting on it for centuries, fueled by his own absolute arrogance. 

Snake gripped the dark iron rod of the Spear of Redemption, raised it slightly, and waited. 

***

High above the eastern plains, Elsie’s jaws locked around the throat of the Drake leader. With a violent, twisting snap, the massive undead beast went limp. 

As the tenth and final Shadow Drake plummeted from the sky, the tide of the battle officially turned. 

Without their aerial artillery to suppress the Imperial Legions, and without the terrifying presence of the Drakes to organize their ranks, the Horde fell into absolute disarray. The Archers of the Ancient Glades, now completely uncontested in the sky, unleashed a relentless, devastating barrage of starlight arrows into the chaotic masses of Orcs and Trolls. 

The Ashen Tide broke. The monsters turned and began to flee back toward the crags. 

But in the center of the muddy, ash-choked battlefield, the true war was just beginning. 

Samuel and Kargaroth circled each other. The air between them warped and shimmered from the sheer, opposing density of their auras. 

Samuel began to grin. The exhaustion of the mortal shell was gone, replaced entirely by the boundless, terrifying joy of the Avatar of the Forge. It was a clash of true immortals. A battle of legends that the world had not seen since the First Age. 

Kargaroth did not wield a weapon. His skeletal hands were wreathed in a concentrated, sickly green magic that warped the very fabric of reality around his fingertips. 

Samuel charged. 

He moved faster than light, a golden blur of cosmic energy. The Twin Axes of the Dragon swung in a flawless, blinding arc aimed directly at Kargaroth’s neck. 

The Lich King didn't try to dodge or block. He knew the golden fire of the Smith would shatter any necrotic ward he could conjure. Instead, Kargaroth leaned into the strike. 

As the blazing blades of the Twin Axes sheared cleanly through Kargaroth’s cervical spine, decapitating the Lich King instantly, Kargaroth drove his right hand straight through Samuel’s chest armor. 

The battlefield seemed to freeze. 

Kargaroth’s headless corpse stood perfectly still. His skeletal hand was buried deep in Samuel’s chest, clutching the brilliant, golden spark of the Avatar's cosmic heart. With a sickening, magical *rip*, the Lich King pulled the heart free. 

The Lich King’s severed skull hit the muddy ground and rolled to a stop. The jaw unhinged, and a horrific, echoing laugh spilled from the bone. 

"We have killed each other, Mountain-Shaker!" Kargaroth’s disembodied voice boomed across the silent plains. "But you are gone forever from this plane! And I will be back in a few decades once I regain my strength!"

A thick, oily column of green smoke began to rise from the Lich King’s decapitated corpse, the immortal soul preparing to flee across the continent back to the safety of its phylactery. 

Samuel dropped his axes. The golden fire around him flickered and died. He fell heavily to his knees, his hands clutching the gaping, lightless hole in his chest. 

But the Avatar didn't look defeated. 

Samuel looked down at the laughing skull in the mud. He coughed, blood spilling over his lips, and let out a weak, triumphant laugh of his own. 

"I suggest you hurry," Samuel gasped, his golden eyes slowly fading to black. 

The Demigod fell forward, face down in the mud, and the Avatar of the Mountain-Shaker returned to the cosmos. 

Kargaroth’s soul shrieked in sudden, panicked confusion. The column of green smoke shot into the air, tearing across the sky at impossible speeds, desperate to reach the obsidian fortress before whatever the boy had planned could be executed. 

***

In the quiet, dark chamber of the throne room, the air suddenly grew freezing cold. 

A swirling vortex of thick, green smoke materialized directly in front of the bone throne. The soul of Kargaroth the Invincible rushed out of the smoke, frantic and desperate, diving straight toward the iron skull built into the backrest. 

Snake didn't blink. He didn't hesitate. 

As the green soul touched the iron, Snake stepped out of the shadows. The dark iron rod in his hand suddenly flared, the magic of The Traveller dropping away to reveal the blinding, pure white light of the Mithril Spear of Redemption. 

"I did not fail," Snake grinned. 

He thrust the spear forward with the absolute, flawless precision of a master assassin. The pure Mithril blade pierced the soul of the Lich King and plunged directly into the center of the iron skull. 

The phylactery shattered. 

The resulting explosion of compressed necrotic energy and pure, uncorrupted life-force was so massive that it didn't just level the throne room. It vaporized the entire mountain above it, sending a pillar of white light shooting into the stratosphere, signaling the definitive, absolute end of the Ashen Tide.
