Sir Gregory was the "Shield of Algoria", a legendary knight doing the Empire's bloody work. But after years of fighting wars for coin disguised as "security", a battlefield explosion gives him the perfect chance to disappear. Faking his death and leaving his noble life behind, Greg travels to the peaceful town of Twin Rivers. There, he asks the master smith for a job, stating he is tired of breaking things and wants to make them instead. Follow Greg's journey from elite warrior to humble apprentice blacksmith in this cozy story.
