Space isn't about adventure for Ford; it's just a long, expensive commute. As the pilot of *The Millennium Seagull*, Ford has spent twenty years hauling cargo across the void, chasing a dream of retiring to a reclaimed beach in Florida. At fifty-two (though looking thirty thanks to Bio-Gens), the fatigue is settling in. He needs just four million credits to buy his freedom, and he's currently at three point two. Follow Ford and his ship's AI, MOTHER, as they take on runs in this gritty, blue-collar sci-fi story!
