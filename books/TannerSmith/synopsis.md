Born to a legendary human blacksmith and a master elven mage, young Tanner Smith sees the world differently. While others see just metal and thread, Tanner sees the flows of Might and Magic. Taught by his grandfather that "borrowed strength is false strength", Tanner rejects powerful magical gifts to forge his own path. Follow the story of a young crafter who believes that if you didn't make it, you don't own it. A tale of self-reliance, unique magic, and the true power of creation!
