# Synopsis: The Alchemist

Ren is a humble apothecary in the Dock Ward, but he possesses a rare gift: the ability to see the Hues and Tones of souls and manipulate Ki to heal or harm. When a corrupt and powerful Guild Factor named Kaelen demands healing for a wound, Ren sees through his lies and refuses. 

Instead of yielding to the Factor's threats, Ren imposes a unique price: Kaelen must serve as his assistant for a week. Through a mixture of compassionate discipline and psychological dismantling, Ren forces the Factor to confront his own past trauma and find his humanity.

After exhausting his Ki reserves to heal the Factor's soul, Ren must take on his adventuring persona, **Fen**, and join a party bound for the Sunken Temple of Yar'Loth to restore his power.
