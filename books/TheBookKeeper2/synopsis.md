Nathaniel Weber is a master of probability. For years, he treated the casinos of Las Vegas as a solvable equation, winning millions as a card counter and advantage player. But when the house finally catches up and bans him for life, Nate is forced to calculate his next move. On a whim, he buys "The Printed Page," a charming but failing used bookstore in a quiet Oregon coastal town. Applying his analytical mind to the chaotic world of bookselling, Nate builds a revolutionary AI recommendation system and integrates into the small-town community. A heartwarming "competence porn" story about a man who learns that some systems are built on heart, not just numbers.
