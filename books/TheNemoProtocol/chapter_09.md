# Chapter 9: The Tiger's Path

Chapter Nine: The Tiger's Path

Four thousand miles away, the *Belgorod* cruised silently at a depth of
four hundred meters.

The mood in the Control Room was electric, but hushed. The crew had
heard the broadcast. They knew what had happened in Moscow. They had
done the impossible. They had stopped the unstoppable machine of war.

Antonov walked up to the periscope stand where Nemo was studying a
chart. The XO looked lighter, as if a physical weight had been lifted
from his shoulders.

"It is done, Captain," Antonov said, a rare smile breaking through his
scarred features. "They signed. The war is over."

"This war is over," Nemo corrected, not looking up from the chart.

Antonov's smile faded slightly. "Sir? We have achieved the objective. We
forced peace."

"We forced a pause, Antonov. Peace is a garden; it requires constant
weeding." Nemo straightened up and walked to the helm.

He looked at the digital globe displaying the world's hotspots. Europe
was cooling down, the red zones turning to blue. But in the East, the
waters were still simmering. The artificial islands. The carrier groups.
The constant buzzing of airspace over Taiwan. The posturing of dragons
and eagles over a strip of water.

The next sandbox. The next testing ground.

"We have taught the bear and the eagle to sit," Nemo said quietly. "Now
we must teach the dragon."

Volkov turned from the navigation console. "Orders, Captain? Do we
return to base? Or find a port to..." He trailed off. There were no
ports for them anymore. They were ghosts.

"Where to now, sir?" Volkov asked.

Nemo looked at the screen, his eyes fixing on the warm, turbulent waters
of the Pacific Rim.

"The Sea of China," Nemo said.

"Set a course for the Taiwan Strait. Full speed."

Antonov looked at his captain. "You intend to do the same there?"

Nemo's face was unreadable. "I intend to ensure that 'The Useless War of
Greed' doesn't get a sequel."

The deck angled downward. The *Belgorod* slipped deeper into the abyss,
turning its bow toward the rising sun. The monster was moving again.
