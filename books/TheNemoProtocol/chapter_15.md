# Chapter 15: Scorched Earth and Green Shoots

### []{#anchor}Chapter Fifteen: Scorched Earth and Green Shoots

The broadcast broke into the middle of the *Global Economic Forum*
special report. The screen didn't flicker with static this time; the
transition was seamless. One moment, a bewildered economist was
explaining why the stock market was skyrocketing despite the
geopolitical chaos, and the next, the sonar sweep was back.

But this time, there was a picture-in-picture. A live feed of Captain
Nemo, sitting in his command chair. He looked tired, unshaven, but his
eyes were bright with a terrifying energy. He held a mug of tea.

"*Well, isn't this a pickle we are in?*" Nemo said, taking a sip. He
gestured vaguely at the camera.

In the Situation Room, the President leaned forward. "He's showing his
face. He's getting bold."

"*I have been looking at the satellite data,*" Nemo continued, his voice
conversational. "*The lungs of the world are black. Burning. Illegal
mining. Cocaine processing labs hidden under the canopy. Loggers tearing
down trees older than your nations.*"

He shook his head, like a disappointed parent.

"*How can I get my point across to some of these governments? Brazil is
trying, but they are slow. Venezuela is... distracted. Colombia is
fighting a hydra.*"

Nemo set the mug down.

"*Oh, I know.*"

The screen shifted to a satellite map of the Caribbean Sea, zooming in
on the Venezuelan coastline. A red circle appeared around a landmass.

"*There we go,*" Nemo said. "*La Orchila. A naval base. A smuggler's
waypoint. A lovely strip of sand.*"

He leaned into the camera lens.

"*You might want to evacuate the few people there. The garrison, the
fishermen. Get them off. It will be gone in 12 hours.*"

"Gone?" The Brazilian President, patched into the call, sounded
horrified. "You are going to nuke the Caribbean?"

"*Kinetic,*" Nemo corrected gently. "*I don't like radiation near the
equator. It spoils the beaches. But a heavy tungsten rod at Mach 10? It
will turn La Orchila into a reef.*"

He spun his chair slightly.

"*Then... I mean, I'm no military strategist, but there's a whole lot of
bad guys in those forests to the south.*"

The map panned down to the vast, sprawling green of the Amazon basin.
Heat signatures lit up like Christmas lights---illegal camps,
clandestine runways, gold dredging barges.

"*I do so hope you guys can keep up the spirit of cooperation,*" Nemo
said, his voice hardening. "*You have the Hexagon Army. You have the
satellites. You have the drones. I want those lights turned off. I want
the chainsaws silenced. I want the mercury dumped in the rivers to
stop.*"

He checked his watch.

"*If I see thermal signatures of illegal industry in the Amazon 24 hours
from now... I will pick a new target. And it won't be an island.*"

He looked back at the map of La Orchila.

"*But, there is no reprieve for this Island. It is the sacrificial lamb.
12 hours, gentlemen. I suggest you start the helicopters.*"

The feed cut.

The President of the United States looked at the President of Venezuela
on the screen---a man he hadn't spoken to directly in years.

"Mr. President," the American said. "I have the 101st Airborne and the
4th Fleet on standby. We can help you evacuate that island. But we need
airspace clearance. Now."

The Venezuelan leader hesitated, his pride warring with his survival
instinct. "And the Amazon?"

"We hunt," the American said grimly. "We take the Hexagon Army south. We
combine with the Brazilian military. We sweep the jungle."

"It's a sovereign territory!" the Venezuelan protested weaky.

"It's a hostage situation," the American corrected. "And the hostage is
the planet. Nemo just lit the fuse."

He turned to his Chief of Staff.

"Get the DEA, the CIA, and Special Operations Command. I want every
cartel coordinate, every illegal logging road, every narco-sub drydock
we know about. We are going to clean house."

"Sir," the Admiral asked. "Do we try to intercept the missile aimed at
the island?"

The President shook his head.

"No. He said no reprieve. If we try to stop it and fail, or if we try to
stop it and he escalates... we lose a city."

He looked at the map of La Orchila.

"We save the people. We let the island burn. And then we go to war with
the jungle."
