# Chapter 23: The Last Coordinate

### []{#anchor}Chapter Twenty-Three: The Last Coordinate

Two weeks later.

The *Belgorod* was moving slow, deep in the South Atlantic. The mood on
board was quiet. The mission was done. The list of targets was empty.

Nemo sat in his quarters, the final binder on his desk. It was unmarked.

Antonov knocked and entered. He carried two glasses and a bottle of very
expensive, very old vodka.

"The world is quiet, Valery," Antonov said, pouring the drinks.
"Confused, terrified, but quiet."

"For now," Nemo said, taking the glass. "They will find new reasons to
fight. It is in their nature."

"Perhaps," Antonov shrugged. "But we gave them a chance. That is all we
could do."

He sat down. "The crew is asking... what now? We cannot dock. We cannot
go home. We are the most wanted men in human history."

Nemo swirled the vodka. "We are not men anymore, Antonov. We are a myth.
We are the monster under the bed that keeps the children behaving."

"So we patrol?" Antonov asked. "Forever?"

"No," Nemo said. "The *Belgorod* is tired. Her reactor is running hot.
She needs rest."

He opened the unmarked binder. Inside was a single chart.

"Antarctica," Antonov read the coordinates. "Point Nemo. The oceanic
pole of inaccessibility."

"Fitting, don't you think?" Nemo smiled. "The furthest point from any
land. The graveyard of satellites."

"We go there to hide?"

"We go there to wait," Nemo corrected. "We park her on the shelf. We
power down the main reactor. We leave the passive sonar running. We
listen."

He raised his glass.

"And if the world forgets..." Nemo whispered. "If the tanks start
rolling again. If the forests start burning again. If the greed
returns..."

Antonov raised his glass, clinking it against his captain's.

"Then the monster wakes up."

Nemo downed the vodka. He stood up and walked to the intercom.

"Helm," Nemo ordered. "Set course for the bottom of the world. Silent
running."

The submarine turned south, slipping into the endless dark, leaving a
world that was broken, battered, but finally, *finally* breathing.
