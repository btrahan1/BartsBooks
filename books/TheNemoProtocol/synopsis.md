Captain Valery Korolenko has seen enough of the world's proxy wars. Taking command of the advanced Russian submarine *Belgorod*, he adopts the name "Nemo" and issues an ultimatum to the world's superpowers: stop fighting or face annihilation. To prove he's not bluffing, he fires a kinetic missile at Los Angeles and a tactical nuke at an uninhabited Arctic peak. Suddenly, the leaders of the "Hexagon" are forced to cooperate to solve the world's most intractable problems—from the war in Ukraine to tensions in Taiwan, the regime in North Korea, and the conflict in Gaza. A fast-paced, high-stakes geopolitical thriller that asks what it takes to force peace on a warring planet.
