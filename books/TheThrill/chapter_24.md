# Chapter 24: The Introduction

The silence on the roof was heavy. It pressed down like a physical weight.

Beth didn't move. She stood in the center of her kill box, her knees slightly bent, her ceramic knives held in a reverse grip.

She watched the door. The tripwire was invisible in the shadow. The magnesium flares were primed.

*Any second now,* she thought. *He kicks the door. The flare blinds him. I move.*

But the door didn't open.

There was no sound. No creak of hinges. No footstep on gravel.

"You are looking the wrong way," a voice said.

Beth spun around.

He was there. Standing on top of the utility shed behind her. He had scaled the sheer concrete wall—thirty feet of vertical rise—without making a sound.

He dropped down, landing in a crouch that absorbed the impact completely.

He stood up. He was wearing the same gray tactical suit. No mask. His face was unremarkable—generic features, buzz cut hair, eyes that looked like wet stones.

He walked toward her. He didn't look at the tripwires or the flares. He looked only at her throat.

Beth’s heart hammered, but she forced her voice to be steady. She didn't step back.

"I don't think we were properly introduced the last time we met," she said clearly.

The Eraser stopped ten feet away. He tilted his head slightly, like a dog listening to a high-pitched noise.

"Introductions are for people who have a future," he said. His voice was a flat monotone. "You have minutes."

Beth smirked behind her balaclava. She twirled the knife in her right hand.

"I'm The Mocking Bird," she said.

For a second, something flickered in his dead eyes. Annoyance? Recognition?

"You are a contract," he said. "You are a number on a ledger. And you are done."

He drew his blades. Two curved karambits, blackened steel. They looked like talons.

"I don't think so," Beth said. "I think the song is just getting to the good part."

She didn't wait for him to move. She didn't wait for the music.

She attacked.
