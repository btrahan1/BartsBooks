# Chapter 26: The Submission

The Eraser was losing patience. The girl was a ghost; every time he slashed, there was only air.

He stepped forward, abandoning his defensive stance, committing to a killing thrust aimed at her chest.

It was the mistake Beth had been waiting for.

Instead of retreating, she stepped *into* the blade's arc. She caught his wrist with her left hand, twisting it away from her body.

At the same time, she jumped.

She launched herself into the air, wrapping her legs around his neck. Her right thigh pressed against his carotid artery, her left ankle locking behind his knee in a modified flying triangle.

The Eraser stumbled under the sudden weight. He flailed, trying to slam her against the ventilation unit.

But Beth squeezed.

She arched her back, using every ounce of strength in her core to tighten the noose of muscle and bone.

"Go to sleep," she hissed.

The Eraser’s knees buckled. Darkness was closing in on his vision. He tried to reach back to grab her, but his coordination was failing as the blood supply to his brain was pinched off.

He crashed to the rooftop gravel, with Beth still clinging to him like a panther. She tightened the lock.

"If he moves, he chokes himself!" Beth shouted, her voice strained.

The Eraser wasn't moving. He was gasping, his hands scrabbling feebly at her legs.

Jack Thorne holstered his gun and sprinted forward. He pulled the cuffs from his belt.

He grabbed the Eraser’s flailing left arm, wrenching it behind the assassin's back. *Click.*

"Police!" Jack shouted. "Give me the other one!"

He grabbed the right arm. *Click.*

"He's secured!" Jack yelled. "Let him go! You're going to kill him!"

Beth held the choke for one second longer—just to be sure. Then she unlaced her legs and rolled away, springing to her feet in a defensive crouch.

The Eraser lay on the gravel, coughing and retching, sucking in air. He was alive, but he was done.

Jack Thorne stood over him, panting. He looked down at the most feared hitman in Europe, now trussed up like a turkey.

Then he looked at the woman in the leather suit.

She was bleeding from her arm. Her chest was heaving. Her hair was a wild halo around her masked face.

"You're crazy," Jack said, shaking his head.

Beth straightened up. She touched the balaclava, ensuring it was still in place.

"I prefer 'eccentric'," she said, her voice shaking slightly with adrenaline.

She looked at the Eraser, then back at Jack.

"Happy Birthday, Detective."
