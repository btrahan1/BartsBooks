# Chapter 29: The Terms

Viktor Volkov gripped the phone so hard the plastic casing creaked. He looked around his destroyed office, the physical manifestation of his loss of control.

"You think this is a game?" he snarled. "You think because you got lucky with one man, you are safe?"

"Luck had nothing to do with it, Viktor," the woman's voice purred. "And I think you know that. You've been a bad boy, Viktor. A very bad boy."

Viktor’s vision blurred with red rage. He was the head of the Odessa Syndicate. He killed people for looking at him wrong.

"When I find you," he whispered, his voice dropping to a guttural rasp, "I will cut your eyes out. I will make you beg for death."

There was a pause on the line. Then, a thoughtful hum.

"Well, you could do that, I guess," she said, sounding bored. "Hmmm... but how are you going to send someone? I mean, The Eraser is gone."

She chuckled.

"Who's next, Viktor? The Pencil? The Ink Pen? Oh, I've got it! No. 2!"

Viktor tried to speak, but he was choking on his own fury.

"I took out your best," Beth continued, her voice hardening slightly. "And to be honest, I was thinking of taking a little break. Maybe."

Viktor took a breath. He forced his heart rate down. He needed to be rational. He needed to stop reacting.

"What do you want!" he shouted.

"I don't know, Viktor," Beth replied, her tone light and airy again. "I really don't. But I've had my fun, for now."

She paused, reducing the most powerful criminal in the city to a frantic listener.

"Unless you want to keep going? I have a list of ten potential targets. Warehouses. Front companies. Accounts. It's up to you."

"I..." Viktor started.

"Think about it," she interrupted. "Don't be a stranger."

*Click.*

The line went dead.

Viktor Volkov stood in the silence of his ruined empire, holding a dead phone, wondering when the bird would sing again.
