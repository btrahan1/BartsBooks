Tyler doesn't run for glory; he runs to pay the rent. By day, he's a high school senior navigating a world of boring lectures and overbearing coaches. By night, he's the "Urban Runner" for Saito's Noodle House, using his incredible agility and a custom-built AI routing system to make lightning-fast deliveries. When he quits the school's elite soccer team to work more hours, Coach Renner becomes obsessed with proving that "Total Commitment" beats Tyler's pragmatic efficiency. But when the two philosophies collide in the city tournament, Tyler's unique set of skills leads to a legend that neither of them expected. A story of family, grit, and the power of finding your own path.
