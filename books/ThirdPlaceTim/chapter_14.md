# Chapter 14: The Sawdust Pit

Oakhaven smelled of pine sap, sweat, and cheap ale. The \"arena\" was a
sawdust pit behind the largest tavern. The crowd was a loud, boisterous
collection of lumberjacks and mill workers who had come to see a fight.

Tim was nervous. Who would have thought they would fill up a full
thirty-two spots for what amounted to a brawl? The prize was five silver
crowns and a year of free drinks at the tavern, a prize worth bleeding
for. No matter. Third Place Tim would live up to his moniker.

His name was called. \"Tim! Versus\... \'Sawdust\' Silas!\"

A giant of a man with a wild red beard and hands like catcher\'s mitts
lumbered into the pit, grinning at the roar from his friends. Tim took a
deep breath. *Think. Don\'t just react.*

Little did he know that in the jostling crowd, a figure cloaked in a
simple, grey traveler\'s hood watched his every move.

\"Begin!\" the bouncer yelled.

Silas charged, bellowing, his arms open for a crushing bear hug. Tim
knew meeting that force head-on would be the end of him. Instead of
retreating, he moved forward and to the side. He placed his open palm on
the man\'s massive shoulder and pushed, not with strength, but with
timing.

Expecting to crush a small boy, Silas found only empty air. The small
push sent him completely off-balance, and he stumbled past Tim, crashing
face-first into the hemp ropes. The crowd let out a collective \"Ooooh\"
of surprise.

Silas recovered, his face red with anger, and began swinging his massive
fists. Tim was a phantom, ducking and weaving, never getting hit.
\"Stand and fight, ya little river rat!\" someone yelled.

Tim knew he couldn\'t evade forever. He saw his opening. Silas threw a
wild right hook, over-extending himself. Tim ducked under the blow,
stepping in close. He hooked his leg behind Silas\'s ankle, grabbed the
man\'s thick wrist and elbow, dropped his own center of gravity, and
heaved.

His Ki flared. Not hot and sharp, but a deep, steady, grounding warmth
from his core. It flowed through him, reinforcing his stance,
strengthening his grip. Silas\'s eyes went wide with disbelief as his
feet left the ground. He sailed over Tim\'s shoulder and landed on his
back with a *WHUMP* that shook the entire pit.

The tavern went silent.

Tim was on him in a second. He knelt on Silas\'s chest and applied a
steady, painful wrist-lock to the man\'s right arm. He didn\'t crank it;
he just held it, the pressure undeniable. Silas struggled for a moment,
then slapped the sawdust with his free hand. \"I yield! I yield!\"

Tim immediately released the hold and scrambled back. The bouncer raised
his hand. \"Winner\... Tim!\"

The silent crowd stared, processing what they had seen. Then, a single
lumberjack started to clap, a slow, appreciative rhythm. Another joined
in, then another, until the entire crowd was giving a ragged, respectful
cheer.

From the back of the crowd, Kael stared, his mouth slightly agape. \"By
the ancestors\...\" he whispered. \"It was\... awful. But it was
perfect.\"

Elara just smiled beneath her hood. The student was learning his lessons
well.
