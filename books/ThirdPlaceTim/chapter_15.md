# Chapter 15: Lumberjack\'s Folly

The air in the tavern was thick. Tim sat alone at a table in the corner,
trying to make himself inconspicuous, when a large shadow fell over him.
It was \'Sawdust\' Silas, a fresh bruise forming on his back but a wide
grin on his face. He plunked a foaming tankard of ale down in front of
Tim.

\"Well fought, little man,\" Silas boomed. \"Winner buys the loser a
drink. That\'s the rule in Oakhaven.\"

Tim, surprised by the sportsmanship, knew he couldn\'t refuse the
gesture without causing insult. He took a swig. The effect was
instantaneous and violent. The ale was thick, bitter, and impossibly
strong. He erupted into a fit of coughing, his throat on fire.

\"By the Gods, what is this!\" he wheezed.

Silas\' boomer laughter filled the tavern. He reached across the table
and slapped Tim on the back, a blow like being hit by a swinging side of
beef. The impact, however, managed to dislodge the ale from his
windpipe.

\"They call it \'Lumberjack\'s Folly\'!\" Silas roared with delight.
\"Puts hair on your chest and splinters in your stool!\"

\"Good fight!\" he said again, his laughter subsiding. \"Seriously. I
never saw it comin\'. One second I was gonna crush ya, the next I was
lookin\' at the sky. Good on ya.\" He leaned forward, his voice dropping
to a conspiratorial rumble. \"Your next match is against One-Eyed Tom.
He\'s a mean one. Likes to get in close and use his thumbs. Watch
yourself.\"

Tim nodded, grateful for the unexpected advice. He was slowly beginning
to understand the culture of this place. It was brutal, yes, but it was
also honest. A fight was a fight, and when it was over, it was over. He
looked at the grinning giant who, ten minutes ago, had been trying to
break him in half. For the first time, Tim felt a strange sense of
belonging. He raised his cup of water.

\"To a good fight,\" Tim said.

Silas roared with laughter again and clinked his massive tankard against
Tim\'s small cup. \"To a good fight!\"

From her shadowed corner of the tavern, Elara watched the scene, a
small, thoughtful smile on her lips. She had seen Tim earn respect with
his skill. Now she was watching him earn it with his character. Kael
simply shook his head in bafflement at the bizarre customs of the common
folk.
