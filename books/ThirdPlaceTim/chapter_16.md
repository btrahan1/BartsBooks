# Chapter 16: The Bully

One-Eyed Tom was a slab of gristle and malice, with a scarred face and a
single, cruel eye that sized Tim up and dismissed him. As Tim stood
across from him in the ring, he didn\'t see an opponent. He saw the slum
bullies who would shake down smaller kids for a crust of bread.

Something inside him stirred. This would be a fight for survival. He
would not maim, but he aimed to make Tom hurt. Sometimes brutes had to
be taught that not everyone would cower. His Ki seemed to agree, the
slow warmth in his core shifting into a steady, focused heat, like a
glowing coal. It was the Ki of righteous anger.

\"Begin!\" the bouncer yelled.

Tom came forward with a predator\'s shuffle. \"I\'m gonna pop ya like a
grape, little man,\" he sneered. He lunged, his hand shooting out in a
grabbing motion aimed at Tim\'s face. Tim swayed back, the grasping
fingers missing his eyes by an inch.

\"Stand still!\" Tom snarled, lunging again. Again, Tim was simply not
there, his circling movements a deliberate, almost insulting refusal to
engage. Tom\'s anger finally boiled over. He threw a wild, haymaker
punch, putting all his weight and malice behind it.

This was the moment Tim was waiting for. He didn\'t flow with the punch.
He met it. He dropped into a low stance and shot his arm up, driving his
sharp, bony elbow directly into the meaty underside of Tom\'s bicep.

*THWACK.* The sound was sickeningly solid. A jolt of pure, agonizing
pain shot up Tom\'s arm. Tim hadn\'t just used muscle; he\'d focused his
intent, that righteous heat, directly into the point of impact.

Before Tom could recover, Tim flowed in, delivering a series of sharp,
open-handed palm strikes, each one infused with that same focused Ki.
*Whap!* To the solar plexus, knocking the wind from Tom\'s lungs.
*Whap!* To the side of the thigh, making his knee buckle. *Whap!* A
final, hard slap to the ear that disoriented him. Each blow was a
lesson.

Tom stumbled back, gasping, the sneer gone, replaced by the dawning,
fearful realization that the grape had teeth. He charged again, a wild,
desperate tackle. Tim sidestepped the clumsy charge and chopped down
with the blade of his hand, connecting squarely with the nerve cluster
where Tom\'s neck met his shoulder.

Tom\'s entire right side went limp. He crashed to his knees in the
sawdust, helpless.

\"Do you yield?\" Tim asked, his voice calm and steady.

Tom glared up at him, a snarl of defiance on his lips. He tried to push
himself up, but his right side wouldn\'t cooperate. Humiliation warred
with pain on his face. Finally, through gritted teeth, he muttered the
word. \"Yield.\"

Tim nodded once, turned, and walked back to his corner without a second
glance. He was in the semi-finals, right where he needed to be. But this
victory felt different. He had stood up to a brute and walked away,
leaving the bully kneeling in the dust. And it felt surprisingly,
satisfyingly, just.
