# Chapter 19: An Unlikely Alliance

The tavern was even more raucous now. Tim, feeling it was his duty,
bought his new friend Borin one of the awful lagers. The smith accepted
it with a gracious nod, while Tim, wiser now, stuck to water.

He listened, raptly, as Borin spoke. The smith wasn\'t boasting; he was
analyzing the fight with the precision of a craftsman. \"Your balance is
your greatest weakness, and your greatest strength,\" Borin explained.
\"You\'re so light on your feet, it\'s easy to push you off-balance. But
you recover faster than anyone I\'ve ever seen. That spin you did\...
that was a trick of a dancer, not a fighter.\"

For the next hour, Tim was a sponge, soaking up every word. Borin talked
about fighting not as a series of techniques, but as a craft. He spoke
of controlling the center of the ring, of managing breathing, of how a
man\'s trade shaped his style. His words were pure gold.

In their secluded corner, Elara and Kael were having a different
conversation. \"My Lady, I must advise against this,\" Kael said, his
voice a low, urgent whisper. \"I do not think it would be wise for you
to meet with this young man.\"

\"He has earned a conversation, Kael,\" Elara countered.

\"That may be,\" Kael conceded. \"But you can see he isn\'t looking for
glory or fame. To reveal yourself, to offer the attention of House
Vance\... it would be a burden to him, not a gift. It could disrupt the
very path that makes him so\... unique. He is on a journey. To interfere
now might set him on a different road entirely, one not of his own
choosing.\"

Elara sighed, the truth of Kael\'s words settling on her. Her desire to
understand was a selfish one. Intervening would make her a patron, a
position of power that would change everything.

\"I bid that you wait,\" Kael urged gently. \"If he is truly as
remarkable as he appears, your roads will cross again. Let it happen
naturally.\"

Elara looked back at Tim. He was laughing now, a rare, genuine laugh at
something the smith had said. He looked young, relaxed, and for the
first time, truly at peace. Kael\'s wisdom was undeniable.

\"You are right, Kael,\" she said softly. \"We will continue as we are.
Ghosts in the crowd.\"

The student, it seemed, was not yet ready to meet the teacher. Or
perhaps, she mused, it was the other way around.
