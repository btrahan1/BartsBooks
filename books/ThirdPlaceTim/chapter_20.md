# Chapter 20: A Practical Demonstration

The night in Oakhaven was dark and deep. Elara had gone for a walk,
alone, insisting she needed to clear her mind. Lost in her own
intriguing thoughts, she didn\'t notice, at first, when three large,
drunk men stepped out of a side alley to block her path.

\"Well now,\" the leader slurred. \"Look what we have here. A pretty
little bird, all alone in the dark.\"

Elara\'s thoughts snapped into sharp focus. She was unarmed. She settled
into a low, ready stance, calculating her first move, when a quiet voice
came from the other end of the alley.

\"Evening.\"

It was Tim, a small sack of grain in his hand. He had seen the scene
unfolding. \"Three against one seems like poor odds,\" he observed
calmly, setting his sack down. \"Maybe I can help even them out.\"

The men laughed. But Tim wasn\'t looking at them. He was looking at the
cloaked figure, at her perfect, foundational fighting stance. A silent,
battlefield understanding passed between them.

The leader lunged at Elara. What happened next was a blur of
coordinated, yet completely unrehearsed, action. Elara moved like a
dancer, sidestepping the grab and striking a nerve cluster in the man\'s
neck. The other two thugs charged at Tim and were met by a phantom. He
ducked a wild swing, pushed one man off-balance and into his friend,
then swept the legs out from under the other.

It was a symphony of contrasting styles. Elara was the
anvil---unmovable, precise, meeting every attack with a perfect
response. Tim was the hammer---unpredictable, chaotic, creating openings
where none existed. The fight was over in less than ten seconds, the
thugs fleeing into the darkness.

\"You handle yourself well,\" Tim said.

\"You have a knack for being in the right place,\" the woman replied.
\"Your timing is\... unorthodox.\"

She took a step forward into a shaft of moonlight and pulled back her
hood. Tim's breath caught in his throat.

\"Lady\... Elara?\"

A small, genuine smile touched her lips. \"I believe,\" she said, her
voice laced with an amusement that danced in the moonlight, \"the proper
term for what we just did is called a practical demonstration.\"
