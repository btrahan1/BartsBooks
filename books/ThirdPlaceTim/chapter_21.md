# Chapter 21: A Promise and a Plea

Tim was conflicted. So very conflicted. His mind, usually so clear, was
a chaotic storm. Lady Elara. Here. Fighting alongside him. His thoughts
raced, and maybe his heart raced just a bit too. In the moonlight, she
was even more striking than he had remembered.

He wanted, NEEDED to keep a low profile. He did not want attention,
especially not the attention of a Great House. Attention got people like
him noticed, and getting noticed usually ended badly. But attention had
found him. It had found him, fought beside him, and was now looking at
him with an intensely curious, intelligent gaze.

Panic, the old, familiar instinct of the cornered rat, began to set in.
He had to end this. Now.

\"I have one more fight tomorrow, and then I will fight for third
place,\" he said, the words coming out in a rush, a shield against the
hundred questions in her eyes. \"After that, I will be leaving.
Please\... don\'t follow me.\"

The plea was genuine, a raw and honest piece of his fear. He didn\'t
wait for her to respond. He couldn\'t risk her asking a question he
couldn\'t answer or making an offer he couldn\'t refuse. With that, Tim
turned, picked up his small sack of oats, and left, walking with the
quick, purposeful stride of a man escaping a fire.

Lady Elara stood alone in the moonlit alley, watching him go, a mix of
disappointment and understanding on her face. He was afraid. Not of her,
but of what she represented: a world of power and complications he
wanted no part of.

A moment later, a massive shape detached itself from the deeper shadows.
It was not Kael. It was \'Sawdust\' Silas, a hefty wood axe in one hand.
He had been there the whole time, a silent, looming guardian he had
appointed himself after their chat in the tavern. He looked down the
empty alley where Tim had vanished.

\"That boy\... he\'s got more secrets than a squirrel\'s got nuts,\"
Silas rumbled. He looked at the Lady, his expression a mixture of awe
and confusion. \"And you, milady\... you\'re not just any traveler, are
ya?\"

Elara offered him a small, enigmatic smile, her mind replaying the
perfect, chaotic synergy of their brief fight. A soft, amused breath
escaped her lips.

\"How very interesting,\" she mimicked her own earlier sentiment, but
this time the words were filled with a new depth, a new sense of wonder.

Silas blinked, then let out a short, booming laugh that seemed to chase
the last of the shadows from the alley. He had no idea what was going
on, but he knew one thing for sure. Life in Oakhaven had suddenly gotten
a lot less boring.
