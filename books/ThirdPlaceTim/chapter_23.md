# Chapter 23: The Deal

Tim sat on an overturned barrel behind the tavern, trying to gather his
thoughts for the final match. A shadow fell over him. It was Kael. The
attendant\'s face was a mask of cold stone, and he radiated an aura of
disciplined danger.

\"The boy, Tim,\" Kael stated. Tim slowly got to his feet and waited.

\"Lady Elara sends her apologies,\" Kael began, his voice a formal
monotone. \"She did not intend to become a distraction. She sends her
thanks for your assistance in the alley. And she sends her regrets. She
understands your desire for anonymity and has agreed to stop following
you.\"

A wave of relief washed over Tim. It was over.

\"Under two conditions,\" Kael added, and the relief vanished.

\"First,\" Kael said, holding up a finger. \"You win this next fight.
And you win it convincingly. Not like the pathetic display you just
gave. She believes the fighter from yesterday is your true self. Prove
her right.\" He leaned in closer, his voice dropping. \"If you win, I
will find you after. I will double the third-place purse, personally.
She will not know.\"

Tim stared, speechless.

\"Second,\" Kael said, raising another finger. \"In one year\'s time,
you return to Devos for the annual tournament. You will enter. Not to
get third place, but to win. Whether it be staves, unarmed, or whatever
other \'skill\' you pick up. Afterward, win or lose, you will meet with
her. As an equal.\"

Kael stood to his full height. \"She wishes to see what a year of honest
effort and a clear goal will make of you. She is placing a wager on your
potential. These are her terms.\" He held out his hand. \"Deal?\"

Tim\'s mind reeled. It was an ultimatum. They were offering him a year
of freedom, at the cost of his anonymity. He looked at Kael\'s
outstretched hand. He thought of the listless, hollow feeling of his
loss. He thought of the focused, powerful feeling of his victory over
Borin. He thought of Lady Elara\'s strange, unwavering belief in him.

What was one more year on the road? He could learn. He could practice.
He could become stronger, not just for survival, but for a purpose. And
then\... a meeting. As an equal. The thought was terrifying, and more
exhilarating than any fight he had ever won.

Slowly, hesitantly, Tim reached out and shook Kael\'s hand. The grip was
like iron.

\"Deal,\" Tim said, his voice barely a whisper.
