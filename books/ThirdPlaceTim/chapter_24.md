# Chapter 24: Unleashed

As Tim stepped back into the sawdust pit for the final time, a huge,
irrepressible grin was plastered on his face. He had been given
*permission* to win. The suffocating weight of his self-imposed strategy
was gone. All he had to do was fight.

The flow of Ki through his veins was intense. The slow trickle of honey
had become a roaring river. The nascent core in his belly? An inferno.
He could feel a power coiling within him that he had never consciously
accessed before.

His opponent was the eager farmhand Borin had beaten. The man had fought
his way through the consolation bracket and was ready for his shot at
third place. He was sure the man had bet on himself to win this match.
That was a mistake.

\"Begin!\" the bouncer yelled. The farmhand charged, aiming to overwhelm
Tim with a bull-like rush.

Tim\'s grin widened. He didn\'t just evade. He danced. With a single,
explosive pivot, he was not only out of the way, but behind his
opponent. The farmhand skidded to a halt, confused. Tim flowed forward,
a blur of motion, channeling the raw, joyful energy.

*Whap!* An open-palm strike to the side of the head, infused with a
spike of Ki that was pure, focused power. The farmhand staggered. Before
the man could even defend, Tim swept a leg to disrupt his stance, then
delivered another palm-heel strike to his chest, knocking the wind out
of him.

The fight was already over, but Tim wasn\'t finished. He had been given
an order: win *convincingly*. He spun, his movements a dazzling,
improvised kata of everything he had learned---the control, the
precision, the grace---all fused together by the sheer, unbridled joy of
being unleashed. He delivered a rapid-fire series of non-injurious
strikes to nerve clusters and muscle groups. A chop to the thigh. A jab
to the ribs. A final, sharp rap to the side of the neck.

It was over in five seconds.

The farmhand crumpled to the sawdust, not unconscious, but completely
incapacitated. The tavern was dead silent. Even Silas\'s jaw was on the
floor. Kael\'s stone-faced expression had cracked, his eyes wide with
disbelief. Elara, hidden beneath her hood, felt her own heart pounding
in her chest.

Tim stood over his defeated opponent, breathing easily, the grin
softening into something more compassionate. He reached down and offered
his hand.

\"Good fight,\" he said, and this time, the words rang with the
absolute, unshakeable confidence of a man who had just discovered the
true measure of his own power.
