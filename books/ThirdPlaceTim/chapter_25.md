# Chapter 25: A Wager on Potential

The tavern was still buzzing, but in the carriage, now rumbling away
from Oakhaven, the mood was electric.

\"A year! I have to wait a *year* to see him again! After witnessing
THAT!\"

Lady Elara paced the small confines of the carriage, her usual grace
replaced by a restless, frustrated energy. \"That wasn\'t a fight! It
was a revelation! Did you see his footwork? The Ki control\... to go
from a listless trickle to a raging inferno in the space of an hour?
That\'s not just skill; that\'s genius!\"

\"It was\... an adequate performance, my Lady,\" Kael said calmly. \"He
followed my instructions.\"

\"Adequate?\" Elara spun on him. \"Kael, do not be deliberately obtuse!
He was transcendent!\" She threw herself back onto the velvet seat.
\"And I have to wait a year. A whole year! I should be there. I should
be watching. Learning!\"

\"My Lady,\" Kael said, \"It was your own condition. A year of freedom
for a promise of greatness. A wise and prudent wager.\"

\"It was a fool\'s wager!\" she retorted. \"I have locked myself out of
the most interesting classroom in the entire kingdom for a year.\"

She fell silent, staring out the window. The wait would be agonizing.
But it was also a test. A test for him, and a test for her.

\"He took your deal, Kael,\" she said softly. \"And your money?\"

\"I met him by the stables,\" Kael confirmed. \"I gave him the promised
purse. He tried to refuse it.\"

\"He did?\"

\"He said he had \'won the fight, not the purse\'. He is an
infuriatingly principled boy,\" Kael stated, a hint of grudging
admiration in his tone. \"I informed him that the money was not a prize,
but a retainer. An investment from a\... silent patron who expects a
return. He seemed to understand that language.\"

\"A year,\" Elara said again, the word no longer sounding like a curse,
but like a promise. \"He will travel. He will learn. He will fight. And
he will return to Devos, not as a desperate survivor, but as a true
competitor.\" She leaned her head back, a newfound calm settling over
her.

\"He thinks he has a year of freedom,\" she mused to herself. \"What he
really has is a year of focused, purposeful training. We did not just
set him free, Kael.\"

She looked at her attendant, a slow, brilliant smile spreading across
her face.

\"We gave him a cage of his own choosing. And I cannot wait to see the
beast that emerges from it.\"
