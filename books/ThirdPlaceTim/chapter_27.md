# Chapter 27: An Engineered Opportunity

Elara paced the length of the sun-dappled flagstones, the problem of Tim
and the nobles a knot she couldn\'t untangle. His fear was logical. It
was wise. And it was going to make him break his promise.

\"Would it be too much,\" she mused aloud, \"if we \'interfered\' just a
bit?\"

Kael frowned deeply. \"My Lady\... we agreed. His path is his own.\"

\"Not directly,\" she clarified, a clever light in her eyes. \"Not as
House Vance. But what if\... he *happened*\... to meet some trouble on
the road? And in this trouble, he managed to \'help\' someone in need? A
\'mentor\' of some sort?\"

Kael\'s disapproval began to melt into cautious curiosity.

\"What if this person is a lower noble?\" she continued. \"Someone with
a name, but not enough power to be a threat. One that might\...
\'instruct\' him on the ways of nobles, not through lectures, but
through example? He needs a friendly face on the other side of that
class divide.\"

The deep frown on Kael\'s face slowly receded, replaced by a cunning
smile. \"I have the perfect person in mind.\"

\"Oh?\"

\"Ser Roric the Bald,\" Kael announced. \"Of House Orin. A minor house,
land-poor with more honor than coin. He\'s sixty years old and
cantankerous. In his youth, he was a renowned tournament fighter. A
\'Third Place Roric\', if you will. He understands the circuit. And more
importantly, he has a reputation for being stubbornly, inflexibly
honorable. He fears no one because, as he puts it, he has nothing left
to lose.\"

\"A man of principle. And a noble,\" Elara said, liking the picture.

\"Precisely,\" Kael confirmed. \"He is currently making his way to the
Springmelt Festival in the Gray Fells. His path will intersect with the
boy\'s in the foothills of the Dragon\'s Tooth mountains in two weeks\'
time.\" Kael\'s smile sharpened. \"And that region is known for its\...
troublesome bandits. It would be a terrible shame if Ser Roric\'s two
green squires were to suddenly come down with a bout of food poisoning
the night before entering that territory.\"

Elara\'s own smile returned. It was perfect. A damsel in distress, but
the damsel was a grumpy old knight. It was a scenario Tim, with his
burgeoning hero complex, would find impossible to ignore.

\"See to it, Kael,\" she said. \"Arrange the\... unfortunate
circumstances. Let us give our ghost a reason to fight for a noble,
instead of forfeiting to one.\"

\"With pleasure, my Lady,\" Kael said, bowing. The \"awful\" and
\"infuriatingly principled\" boy had become a project they could both
truly enjoy.
