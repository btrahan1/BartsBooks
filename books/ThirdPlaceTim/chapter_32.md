# Chapter 32: A Noble\'s Character

A few months later, the first thaws of spring were softening the
iron-hard ground of Devos. Elara was in the training hall when Kael
arrived with his next report.

\"So,\" Elara said, her voice eager. \"He fights nobles now? Sometimes,
at least?\"

\"He does, my Lady,\" Kael confirmed. \"Ser Roric\'s tutelage has been
effective. He no longer forfeits automatically. He now assesses them as
he would any other opponent. His decision to fight, and *how* he fights,
appears to be based entirely on his personal assessment of the noble\'s
character.\"

\"Character?\"

\"Observation, my Lady. Before a tournament, he watches how they treat
their squires, the stable hands, the merchants. This assessment dictates
his strategy. We have observed three distinct approaches.\"

Kael explained \"The Lesson,\" a systematic, public humiliation reserved
for arrogant and cruel nobles. He described \"The Test,\" an all-out
battle of skill against honorable opponents, often ending in a
respectful draw. Finally, he detailed the strangest approach of all.

\"\'The Farce\',\" Kael said with dry amusement. \"This is reserved for
nobles he deems to be utterly incompetent. He puts on a performance,
appearing to trip over his own feet, making the fight seem like a
dramatic struggle. He learned that a public, humiliating defeat of a
powerful fool creates a dangerous enemy. A narrow, \'lucky\' victory,
however, makes the fool feel magnanimous. After one such \'loss\', the
noble\'s father, impressed by the boy\'s \'spirit\', gave him a purse of
five silver crowns.\"

Kael closed his book. \"So, that is how he chooses, my Lady. He is no
longer just a fighter. He has become a judge, a peer, and a performer.
He approaches each noble not as a threat to be feared, but as a puzzle
to be solved.\"

Elara was silent, a slow, deep sense of awe spreading through her. The
ghost had not just learned to walk in the light; he was learning to
manipulate it. He was building his own strange, unwritten code of
chivalry, forged in the slums and tempered on the road.
