# Chapter 33: The Just Tim Fund

The report was remarkable, yet Elara sensed there was more. \"Something
else?\" she prompted.

Kael shifted uncomfortably. \"It is a matter of finance, my Lady. Tim\'s
uncharacteristic knack for the markets has continued. He is actually
accumulating quite a bit of wealth. Nothing to the standards of the
nobility, but he would have a bit more\... if not for his\...
donations.\"

\"Donations?\"

\"They are always anonymous,\" Kael explained. \"A bag of coins on the
steps of a struggling orphanage. A \'forgotten\' purse in a temple\'s
collection box. Most recently, a small bar of gold for the family of a
town guard injured protecting a merchant. He earns, my Lady. And then,
he gives away nearly half of it. He seems to be\... patching the holes
he sees in the world.\"

\"And I assume we are matching his donations from our own earnings,
yes?\" she asked softly.

Kael looked surprised, then deeply respectful. \"It will be done, my
Lady.\"

\"And on that topic,\" Elara continued, \"how are we doing on our
initial investments?\"

Kael\'s uncomfortable demeanor vanished, replaced by cool confidence.
\"My Lady, our initial seed money and subsequent reinvestments have
matured. Factoring in our operational expenses and our new charitable
matching program, the \'Just Tim Fund\', as I\'ve taken the liberty of
calling it, currently holds a total value of\... seventy-three gold
crowns, four silver, and six copper.\"

Elara\'s eyebrows shot up. To have grown from a handful of silver into
such a sum in less than a year\... it was miraculous.

\"He has, in essence, unknowingly funded our entire
intelligence-gathering operation on him, his own charitable works, and
still generated a profit that would make a seasoned merchant green with
envy,\" Kael concluded, a note of pure awe in his voice.

Elara let out a low whistle. The boy was a phenomenon. A martial
prodigy, a financial wizard, and a secret philanthropist. The meeting in
Devos, now only a few short months away, would not be a meeting with a
student. It would be a meeting with a peer.
