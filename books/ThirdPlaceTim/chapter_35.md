# Chapter 35: A Copper Core

Tim, filled with a new, quiet resolve, decided to enter all five of the
competitions for which he was qualified at the Springmelt Festival. It
was no longer a time to hide. It was a time to determine what he was
made of.

He felt a deep, foundational shift within himself. His nascent core was
no longer nascent. It had coalesced, solidified, and taken on a
distinct, coppery hue in his mind\'s eye. A fully formed Copper Core.
The lowest of the formal ranks, but it was *his*. It was a true furnace
now, generating a steady, powerful Ki that hummed through his entire
body.

His routine of meditation and practice, polished by a week spent
sweeping the courtyards of a mountain temple, had paid off. He had
learned about focus, about flow, about centering himself not just in a
fight, but in every action.

Now, at the festival, it was time to put it all together.

In **Archery**, he used his Ki to focus his vision, taking second place.
In **Unarmed Brawling**, he used economy of motion to become an
immovable rock, defeating the five-time champion with a single,
effortless throw. In **Axe-Throwing**, he added a pulse of Ki at the
moment of release, sinking the axe deeper than any other competitor. In
**Gladius Sparring**, his sweeping footwork and impenetrable defense
forced his opponent to yield from sheer frustration. In the
**Steeplechase**, a brutal race up the mountainside, he drew on his core
for a relentless, inexhaustible rhythm.

Four first-place victories and one second-place.

By the end of the day, the cheerful festival crowd was silent, staring
in stunned, reverent awe. The quiet boy had not just competed; he had
dominated, and he had done it with a calm, focused grace that was both
terrifying and beautiful.

Ser Roric stood by the finish line, his mouth agape, holding a
collection of prize ribbons. The final lesson, it seemed, had been
taught.

Tim looked at the ribbons, then at the old knight\'s stunned face. A
small, confident smile touched his lips. He was no longer just a ghost,
a survivor, or a student. He knew, now, exactly what he was made of. And
he was ready for Devos.
