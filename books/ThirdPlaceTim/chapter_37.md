# Chapter 37: A Promise Renewed

The gates of Devos were just as he remembered them, but the boy who
walked through them was not the same. He was taller, broader, and moved
with a quiet confidence forged on the road. He paid the gate tax and
made his way to the now-familiar tournament grounds.

He found the registration tent, larger and more ostentatious than
before. \"Name and events?\" the clerk asked, his tone dismissive.

Tim looked up at the board. \"The Staff. And Unarmed,\" he said.

\"Name?\"

\"Tim. Just Tim.\"

\"That will be one silver crown per entry.\"

Tim calmly placed two gleaming silver crowns on the desk. He had made a
promise, but he was no fool. He would compete in the two things he was
best at. He would still avoid the sons of the most powerful, if he
could. But should one cross his path, he would not waver. He had made a
promise.

In the Vance estate, Kael delivered his report to Lady Elara. \"He has
arrived. He is, by all accounts, physically transformed. He carries
himself with a discipline that borders on monastic.\"

\"His registrations?\" Elara asked.

\"The Staff, and Unarmed Combat. A strategically sound choice. He is
playing to his strengths.\" Kael added, \"He paid his own entry fee. He
has not touched the retainer I gave him.\"

Elara nodded. Honorable. \"And his demeanor?\"

\"More than ready, my Lady. There is\... a change in his Ki. Observers
describe it as a deep, calm, steady river. His Copper Core is unusually
dense and pure.\" He paused. \"The brackets were drawn an hour ago. In
the Unarmed competition, his path is clear. But in the Staff\... if he
wins his first two matches, his opponent in the quarter-finals will be
Lord Valerius of House Corvan.\"

Elara's posture stiffened. \"Valerius? The Duke of Corvan\'s eldest son?
And one of the men Ser Barnaby---the knight Tim publicly
humiliated---swore fealty to.\"

\"Indeed, my Lady. Lord Valerius will not see Tim as just another
competitor. He will see him as an insult to be corrected.\" Kael closed
his book. \"He did not withdraw after seeing the bracket. He simply went
to the practice grounds and began his kata.\"

Elara looked out the window, toward the distant tournament grounds. He
had not wavered. The year of waiting was over. And the tournament had
just become infinitely more interesting.
