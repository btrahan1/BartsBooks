# Chapter 38: The Peacock and the Ghost

\"Wow,\" Tim murmured, looking at the huge Unarmed Combat board.
\"Sixty-four entrants. Just\... wow. Thoughts, Sir Knight?\"

Ser Roric stood beside him, squinting at the brackets. \"A lot of chaff.
Your first few matches will be against brutes and brawlers. Be
efficient. A simple throw, a clean submission.\" His gaze moved down the
bracket. \"The quarter-finals are where it gets interesting. \'Brom the
Breaker\'. A sellsword. He\'ll be dirty, but disciplined. That is a
fight where you will have to earn your victory.\"

His expression soured as he moved to the Staff board, his eyes
immediately finding the name. \"Lord Valerius,\" Roric spat. \"Arrogant
peacock. All flash and fancy twirling. He thinks a duel is a dance. But
do not mistake him for a fool, boy. He is fast, and his technique is
flawless. He represents everything you have been taught to fear.\"

\"What is your advice, Ser?\" Tim asked, his voice steady.

A slow, wolfish grin spread across the old knight\'s face. \"My advice?
Is to let the peacock dance. Let him believe he is in control.\" He
leaned in closer. \"And then, when he is at the height of his pride, you
remind him what a simple, ugly, ironwood stick can do to a man\'s
kneecaps. You show him the difference between a dance and a fight.\"

The pace of the Unarmed competition was insane, a meat grinder of
back-to-back-to-back fights. But Tim didn\'t falter. He was a model of
efficiency, winning his first three matches with simple throws and clean
submissions, barely breaking a sweat. His fourth match, to enter the
quarter-finals, was against an honorable young knight, Ser Patrek. It
was a beautiful, grueling fight, a chess match of muscle and Ki. Tim won
with a complex joint lock that forced a respectful yield.

\"Thank you for the instruction,\" Tim said sincerely after helping the
knight up.

\"The honor was mine,\" the knight replied, confused but impressed.

The next day, Tim woke groggily. The Unarmed semi-finals were tomorrow.
But today, he had the Staff. Two preliminary matches stood between him
and the looming confrontation with Lord Valerius. He dispatched both
with brutal, quiet efficiency, ending each fight in under a minute.

And now, the path was clear. A runner pointed him toward the main ring.
\"Tim! You\'re up against Lord Valerius!\"

Tim took a deep breath. He had no idea how he was going to win this
fight. But he knew, with an absolute certainty, that he was not going to
lose it on purpose. He had made a promise.
