# Chapter 43: A Champion\'s Welcome

The rest of the tournament was anti-climactic. The dramatic
confrontation with House Corvan had been the true final. Tim's remaining
matches in both the Unarmed and Staff competitions were masterclasses in
control. In the Staff final, his opponent, a skilled hedge knight,
simply yielded before the match began, laying his staff on the ground
and bowing. To fight the boy who had dismantled Lord Valerius, he
declared, would be an exercise in futility.

Tim was declared the champion of both competitions. He had fulfilled his
promise.

In her private box, the moment the final victory was announced, Lady
Elara let out a sharp, unrestrained squeal of pure delight. She wanted
to rush down to meet him, but a sharp look from Kael stopped her. *Not
here. Not now.* She composed herself, smoothing her tunic.

As she was about to move toward the private exit, she stopped. Tim was
on the arena floor, accepting the winner\'s purses, when a figure
stepped out from the shadow of the gladiators\' gate. It was a very\...
different\... Lord Valerius.

He was dressed in the formal black silks of his House. He walked
directly up to Tim, stopping a few feet away. The crowd went silent
again. Then, to the utter shock of everyone watching, Lord Valerius,
heir to the Dukedom of Corvan, bowed. It was the short, sharp,
respectful bow of one warrior to another.

\"I am in your debt,\" Valerius said, his voice quiet but clear,
stripped of its arrogance. \"You defeated my skill. You defeated my
rage. But you did not defeat my honor. You\... preserved it. When I
would have thrown it away. I do not understand you. But I respect you.
If you should ever find yourself in the lands of House Corvan, you will
be received not as an enemy, but as a guest.\"

It was an apology. It was a peace treaty. Tim, just as surprised as
everyone else, simply nodded. \"Thank you, my Lord.\"

Valerius turned and strode from the field, his back straight, his head
held high. He had lost the match, but in that moment, he had won back a
piece of himself. Elara watched, her heart swelling. Tim had not just
won a tournament. He had taught a lesson in honor to one of the proudest
houses in the kingdom. And now, finally, it was her turn.
