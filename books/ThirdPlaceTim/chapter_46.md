# Chapter 46: A Rival\'s Offer

Just as the profound, comfortable silence settled between them again, a
crisp footstep on the gravel path announced an intrusion. Kael appeared
at the edge of the garden, his expression uncharacteristically strained.
And with him, an unexpected guest.

Duke Corvan, Master Valerius, stepped into the soft twilight. He was
dressed in a simpler, though no less fine, velvet doublet. His gaze was
not on Elara, but solely on Tim. The peaceful intimacy of the moment
shattered.

The Duke stopped a respectful distance away. He ignored Kael and gave a
slight, formal bow toward Elara. Then, he turned his full attention to
Tim. There was no anger in his eyes, only a calm, powerful, and deeply
calculating curiosity.

He asked, gracefully, his voice a low, polite rumble, \"Would you mind
if I joined you?\"

It was not a request. It was a statement of intent, phrased with the
impeccable courtesy of a man who was accustomed to never being refused.
He was not asking for permission from Elara; he was asking Tim. He was
acknowledging Tim as the central figure in this new, unfolding political
landscape.

Tim\'s heart was pounding. Every instinct screamed at him to refuse, to
flee. But then he remembered Ser Roric\'s lesson: *You let them see all
of it. Because that is who you are. And it is more than enough.*

He stood up and gave the Duke a short, respectful bow, not of a servant,
but of an equal. \"Of course, Your Grace,\" Tim said, his voice steady.
\"We were just finishing. Please, take my seat.\"
