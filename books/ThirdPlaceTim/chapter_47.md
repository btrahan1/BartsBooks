# Chapter 47: The Measure of a Man

The Duke raised a hand. \"Nonsense, boy. Remain seated.\" He looked
pointedly at Kael, who, without a word, vanished and reappeared with a
heavy, carved wooden chair, placing it opposite the bench. The seating
arrangement was now a triangle of negotiation.

\"I believe we have quite a bit to discuss,\" the Duke began. He looked
first at Elara. \"I came here as a competitor, one might say. Your House
has found a remarkable asset, Lady Elara. I came to see if he could
be\... persuaded to entertain other offers.\" His gaze shifted to Tim.
\"But now\... I am not so sure I can even compete.\"

He looked directly at Tim, his eyes sharp and analytical. \"I have heard
the stories that are already spreading. Your encounter with the bandits.
Your peculiar brand of justice. Your even more peculiar talent for
investment. You are, by all accounts, a walking contradiction. You fight
like a demon, but you show the mercy of a saint. Tell me, boy\... what
is it you want?\"

It was the ultimate question. Tim felt the weight of both their gazes on
him. A year ago, the answer would have been easy. *Food. A safe place to
sleep. Ten silver crowns.* Now, the answer was far more complex. He
thought of his journey, of the lessons, of the quiet satisfaction of
seeing the bandits lay down their swords.

He met the Duke\'s gaze, his own eyes clear and honest. \"I want,\" Tim
said, his voice quiet but resonant, \"to leave the world a little better
than I found it. One fight, one choice, one pot of oats at a time.\" He
paused, a small, self-aware smile touching his lips. \"And I\'d like to
not get killed while doing it. That part is also important.\"

The answer was so simple, so devoid of the ambition the Duke was
accustomed to, that it took him completely by surprise. He had expected
a request for land, for title, for gold. He had not expected a
philosophy. The Duke let out a short, sharp bark of laughter, a laugh
not of mockery, but of genuine, surprised delight.

\"Well,\" the Duke of Corvan said, a new and dangerous respect dawning
in his eyes. \"That is the most ridiculous and noble ambition I have
ever heard.\"
