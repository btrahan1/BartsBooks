# Chapter 48: The Language of Rules

Tim\'s thoughts were spinning. He was so far out of his depth. So he did
what he always did. He spoke, honestly.

He told them everything. Of his beginnings, of his simple goals, of his
year on the road, of discovering his core. He spoke of the very long
journey he felt he had ahead of him, of feeling like he was standing at
the edge of a vast, uncharted kingdom inside himself with no idea how to
proceed. He laid his ignorance and his honesty out on the table for them
to see.

When he finished, he did something incredibly brave. He turned the
question back on the most powerful man in the room. \"So, let me ask
you, Your Grace, what would YOU do if you wanted to change the world?
Not all in one day, but over a lifetime?\"

The Duke was taken aback. \"Change the world\...\" he mused. \"When you
are a Duke, you learn that you cannot change the world. You can only
manage it. You hold the line against the chaos. You, boy, you operate on
a different level. You aren\'t changing the world. You are changing the
*people* in it. And that\... that may be the only way it can truly be
changed.\"

He stood up, the audience concluded. \"If I were you, I would not try to
change the world. I would simply continue to be the person who does. And
I would find powerful, influential friends who can make sure the world
does not crush you for your efforts.\" He gave a short, formal bow to
Elara, and a longer, more meaningful nod to Tim. \"We will speak
again.\" It was not a threat. It was a promise. And with that, he left
the garden.

Kael returned, his expression impassive but his eyes holding a new,
heavy weight. He stood before the two idealistic young fools.

\"If you mean what you say,\" Kael said, his voice cutting through the
quiet air, his gaze fixed on Tim. \"Then you are done with tournaments.
You are done with \'Just Tim\'. You are done with \'Third Place Tim\'. A
man who wishes to heal the world cannot spend his life as a ghost. That
journey has served its purpose. It has forged you. Now, it is over.\"

His gaze was like a physical force. \"You need to become \'Student
Tim\', at the one place where you can truly begin your journey\... The
Academy.\"

Elara gasped. \"The Academy? Kael, are you mad? He\'s not a noble! It\'s
impossible!\"

\"Impossible?\" Kael replied, a dangerous glint in his eye. \"My Lady,
we just listened to a former street thief broker a peace with a Duke by
offering him porridge. I believe our definition of \'impossible\' may
need to be revised.\" He turned back to Tim. \"It will be the hardest
fight of your life. But he will not be without patronage. He will have
the sponsorship of House Vance. And, I suspect, the quiet, unofficial
backing of House Corvan.\"

He looked at Tim, his plan now clear. \"The world is not changed by
tournament champions, Tim,\" Kael said, his voice ringing with absolute
conviction. \"It is changed by the people who write the rules. It is
time for you to learn the language.\"
