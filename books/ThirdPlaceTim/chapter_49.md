# Chapter 49: A Gilded Cage

**The weeks that followed were a blur of refined torture. Tim was given
a suite of rooms in the Vance estate, a gilded cage of silk sheets and
polished wood that felt more alien than any hayloft he'd ever slept in.
True to Kael's prediction, a formal, if discreet, letter arrived from
Duke Corvan, offering his House's full endorsement for Tim's
\"scholastic pursuits.\" The combined political weight of two rival
Great Houses pushing for the same goal was an irresistible force, and
the ancient bureaucracy of the Royal Ki Academy reluctantly yielded. A
writ of acceptance was issued.**

**His victory, however, had come at the price of a new kind of
servitude. He was tutored relentlessly, not in fighting, but in the
exhausting warfare of high society. A stern-faced woman named Master
Elspeth drilled him on history, reading, and writing until his head
spun. He learned which of the five forks to use for fish, how to address
a baron versus an earl, and the complex genealogies of the Great
Houses---a tangled forest of names and betrayals that made the politics
of the slum gangs seem simple by comparison.**

**He endured it. He had survived hunger and violence; he could survive
cutlery. But a quiet part of him felt like a project, a piece of raw
iron being hammered into a shape that wasn\'t his own.**

**His only respite was the training yard with Elara, and the evenings
spent with Ser Roric, who had delayed his journey home to see the boy
settled. On Tim\'s last night before the old knight's departure, they
sat on a low stone wall overlooking the gardens. Roric looked at Tim,
who now wore a simple but well-made linen shirt instead of his road-worn
tunic.**

**\"You look clean,\" Roric grunted, which was the highest form of
compliment he was capable of giving. \"They\'re trying to make a proper
little lord out of you.\"**

**\"They\'re trying,\" Tim agreed, a weary smile on his face. \"I\'m not
sure it\'s taking.\"**

**\"Good,\" the old knight said gruffly. He clapped a heavy, calloused
hand on Tim's shoulder. \"Remember the lessons of the road, boy. A book
can teach you the theory of a sword, but it can\'t teach you the feel of
it in your hand. This Academy... they\'ll try to teach the road out of
you. Don\'t let them.\" He looked Tim square in the eye. \"That shadow
of yours? The street rat? That's where your strength comes from. Never
let them make you ashamed of it.\"**

**\"I won\'t, Ser,\" Tim promised, his voice thick with gratitude.**

**\"Good. Now get out of here,\" Roric said, shoving him gently. \"Go
learn their language. And then use it to make some powerful people
profoundly uncomfortable.\"**
