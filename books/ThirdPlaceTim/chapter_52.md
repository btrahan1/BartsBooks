# Chapter 52: A Departure of Equals

**The morning of their departure was a flurry of quiet, efficient
activity. Kael oversaw the packing of a modest carriage, his expression
as impassive as ever, but Elara could see the new weight in his eyes. He
approached Tim, who was now dressed in the simple but well-made grey
wool uniform of an Academy novice.**

**\"The Academy is not a tournament,\" Kael said, his voice a low,
private instruction. \"Your enemies will not meet you in a ring. They
will test you not on your skill, but on your origins. They will use your
past as a weapon against you.\" He met Tim\'s gaze. \"Do not let them
make you forget who you are. It is your greatest strength.\"**

**\"Thank you, Kael,\" Tim said, a note of genuine warmth in his voice.
\"For everything.\"**

**A flicker of something---pride, perhaps---crossed Kael\'s face before
it was gone. \"I am merely an agent of House Vance,\" he stated. \"My
Lady requires your success. See that you do not disappoint her.\" He
bowed stiffly and stepped back.**

**Elara approached, dressed in the Academy\'s formal blue robes of an
advanced student. She looked at Tim, not as a patron looking at her
charge, but as a partner. \"Ready?\" she asked.**

**\"As I\'ll ever be,\" he replied.**

**They climbed into the carriage. It was a simple, unadorned vehicle,
bearing no house crest. They were not traveling as nobles, but as
scholars. As the carriage rumbled through the gates of the Vance estate,
Tim looked back at Kael, the silent, stoic man who had set this entire,
impossible journey in motion with a deal behind a tavern. Kael gave a
single, almost imperceptible nod. Acknowledgment. Approval.**

**The journey of the silent patron and his unwitting prodigy was over. A
new journey, as partners, was about to begin.**
