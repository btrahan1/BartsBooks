Tim was a thief in the city slums until Old Man Kai caught him and offered him a bowl of rice and a new way to use his hands. Now, Tim is on the road to Devos with a stout ironwood staff and a humble goal. He isn't fighting for the grand prize or second place glory. He wants third place—and the ten silver crowns that come with it. Ten silver crowns is the price of being honest for a month. But to get there, he'll have to navigate a tournament filled with the sons of knights, a graceful noblewoman named Lady Elara, and his own lingering instincts of a survivor. A wholesome and engaging story about choice, empathy, and finding one's foundation.
