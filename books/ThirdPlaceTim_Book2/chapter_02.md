# Chapter 2: The Baseline

The Lower Dormitory was a barracks. It was clean, stark, and
militaristic. Rows of cots lined the walls. Tim found the one assigned
to his number---Novice 94---and set his pack down.

"So, it's true."

Tim turned. Three boys stood behind him. The leader was a head taller
than Tim, with shoulders that strained his velvet doublet and a jawline
that looked like it could crack walnuts. He wore the crest of a red lion
on a ring---House Redgrave.

"I am Cassian," the boy said. He didn't offer a hand. "My father is Earl
Redgrave. He told me about the Vance 'pet' project."

Cassian stepped closer, invading Tim's personal space. "Let's get
something straight, peasant. You might have tricked some country
bumpkins and a soft-hearted Duke out in the sticks. But here? You are
filth. You are the dirt we scrape off our boots."

Tim looked at Cassian. He analyzed the boy's stance (too weight-forward,
aggressive), his breathing (shallow, angry), and his hands (clenched,
telegraphing intent).

"You're blocking the aisle," Tim said calmly. "It's a fire hazard."

Cassian blinked. He had expected fear, or anger, or a stuttered apology.
He hadn't expected a safety regulation.

"You think you're funny?" Cassian snarled. He reached out to shove Tim.

It was a slow, telegraphed move. In Tim's mind, he saw three different
ways to break Cassian's wrist, two ways to sweep his legs, and one way
to use the boy's own momentum to throw him into the neighboring cot.

*The Language of Rules,* Kael had said. *Don't fight them. Outmaneuver
them.*

Tim didn't block. He didn't dodge. He simply took a half-step back.

Cassian's hand shoved empty air. He stumbled forward, off-balance. As he
lurched, Tim loudly and deliberately tripped over his own feet, falling
backward onto his mattress with a loud *OOF*.

"Help! Help!" Tim cried out, his voice flat and unconvincing to a
trained ear, but loud enough to echo down the hall. "I am being
assaulted!"

Cassian froze, looking around wildly. "I didn't touches you! You
tripped!"

The door to the dormitory slammed open. A Prefect---an older student
with a silver armband---strode in. "What is the meaning of this noise?"

Tim sat up on his cot, adjusting his collar. He looked at the Prefect,
then at Cassian.

"My apologies, Prefect," Tim said, his voice the picture of humility. "I
am clumsy. I tripped. Novice Cassian here was just... rushing to help me
up. Weren't you, Cassian?"

Tim looked at the bully. His eyes were wide, innocent, and filled with a
trap.

If Cassian denied it, he admitted to being the aggressor in a
non-sanctioned fight on the first day---grounds for punishment. If he
agreed, he had to publicly help the "peasant."

Cassian's face turned a violent shade of purple. He looked at the
Prefect, then glared at Tim with pure hatred.

"Yes," Cassian ground out through gritted teeth. "I was... helping him."

"Excellent," the Prefect sniffed, bored. "Keep the noise down." He left.

Cassian stared at Tim. "You," he whispered, "are dead."

"Unlikely," Tim replied, opening his pack and taking out a book on basic
arithmetic. "Statistically speaking, accidents in the dormitory are
rare. Unless, of course, someone is clumsy."

He opened the book and began to read, ignoring the fuming noble. He had
won the first skirmish. And he hadn't spent a single drop of Ki.
