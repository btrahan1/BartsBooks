# Chapter 33: The Circuit

The Great Kitchens were silent at midnight, save for the low, rhythmic
breathing of the banked ovens. The staff had gone, leaving the space
warm and smelling of cooling bread.

The inner circle---The Squad---sat around the big oak table. Gareth,
Christopher, Lyssandra, Milo, Mara. And Cassian Redgrave, who looked out
of place without his usual entourage but sat with the grim determination
of a soldier who had seen too much.

They were terrified. Rumors about the Mystic Spire had been circulating
all day. They whispered of students going mad, of warriors fighting
invisible monsters until they collapsed from exhaustion.

The door swung open.

Tim walked in. He didn't limp. He didn't look like a boy who had been
bedridden hours ago. He moved with a terrifying smoothness, his steps
making no sound on the stone floor. The bandages on his hands were gone,
revealing skin that looked new---pink, unblemished, and slightly shiny,
like polished copper.

"You're... glowing," Christopher whispered, adjusting his glasses.

"I am tempered," Tim said quietly. He sat at the head of the table. "The
fire burned away the impurities. I feel... clear."

He looked at his friends. He saw the jagged yellow spikes of fear in
their auras. He saw Cassian's rigid, brittle tension. He saw
Christopher's vibrating panic.

"The Mystic Spire," Tim began, "does not fight your body. They fight
your mind. They use Ki to stimulate the amygdala, triggering
hallucinations based on your deepest insecurities."

"Great," Cassian muttered. "So I'm going to be fighting a giant version
of my father telling me I'm a disappointment."

"Essentially," Lyssandra confirmed, her face pale. "They isolate the
consciousness. You feel alone. You feel small. And then the shadows eat
you."

"They rely on isolation," Tim said. He placed his healed hands flat on
the table. "They rely on the fact that a mind is a closed room. If they
fill that room with smoke, you choke."

He looked around the circle.

"So, we are going to knock down the walls."

"How?" Gareth rumbled. "My head is thick. But it is still my head."

"We are going to build a Circuit," Tim said. "Professor Corvus calls it
a 'Resonance Link.' I call it holding the line."

Tim extended his hands, palms up.

"Gareth, take my right hand. Lyssandra, take my left. Cassian, take
Lyssandra's. Christopher, take Gareth's. Milo, Mara, close the circle."

They hesitated. Linking Ki flows was intimate. It was dangerous. If one
person panicked, the feedback loop could knock them all out.

"Trust the mechanics," Tim said softly.

Gareth reached out his massive hand and engulfed Tim's. Lyssandra took
the other. Slowly, the circle closed.

"Close your eyes," Tim commanded. "Do not push your Ki. Just... breathe.
Follow my rhythm."

Inhale.

Tim didn't light a fire this time. He became a pump. He reached into his
Copper Core, which was spinning with a smooth, silent power he had never
felt before. He sent a pulse of pure, neutral warmth out through his
right hand, into Gareth.

Gareth gasped. "It is... heavy."

The pulse traveled. Into Christopher, smoothing his vibrations. Into
Milo, steadying his heart. Into Cassian, melting his rigidity. Into
Lyssandra, warming her blood.

And then it came back to Tim through his left hand.

The Circuit was closed.

"Now," Tim whispered. "Open the doors."

He pushed harder. He didn't invade their thoughts---he wasn't a
telepath. But he opened the emotional floodgates.

Suddenly, Christopher wasn't just feeling his own fear; he felt Gareth's
unshakeable, granite stability. Lyssandra felt Cassian's burning,
iron-willed drive to win. Cassian felt Lyssandra's razor-sharp,
analytical clarity. Gareth felt Milo's survival instinct, quick and
alert.

And all of them felt Tim.

They felt the Copper. It wasn't an emotion. It was a state of being. It
was the feeling of a warm loaf of bread in a hungry belly. It was the
feeling of a sturdy pair of boots on a long road. It was the absolute,
unshakeable truth that *reality is real*.

"Do you feel it?" Tim asked, his voice echoing in their heads not as
sound, but as a shared thought.

"We are... one big room," Christopher breathed.

"Exactly," Tim said. "When the Mystics attack tomorrow, they won't be
attacking fifty individuals. They will be attacking the Circuit."

He squeezed their hands.

"If Christopher gets scared, Gareth absorbs the fear. If Gareth gets
confused, Lyssandra provides the logic. If Lyssandra gets cold, Cassian
provides the fire. And if the illusions get too strong..."

Tim's presence in the link expanded, a grounding rod driving deep into
the earth.

"...you send them to me. I will ground them out. I will show the
nightmares a simple truth: *I am real. You are dust.*"

Cassian opened his eyes. He looked at Tim with a mixture of horror and
awe. "You're sharing your Core capacity with us. You're acting as the
central processor."

"I am the wire," Tim said, opening his eyes. They glowed with that deep,
steady copper light. "You are the current."

He looked at his army. They weren't shaking anymore. They were humming
with a low, collective resonance. They were a single, multi-faceted
organism.

"Go to sleep," Tim said, breaking the physical link, though the feeling
of connection lingered in the air like ozone. "And when you wake up...
remember the Circuit. Don't fight the fog alone."

Gareth looked at his hand, flexing it. "I feel... crowded. But strong."

"That's the point, big man," Tim grinned. "Tomorrow, the Mystic Spire
isn't fighting a team. They're fighting a family."
