# Chapter 34: The Anchor and the Storm

The Staging Area for the Semi-Finals was known as the Gate of Dreams. It
was a wide stone archway leading into a dense, unnatural violet fog.
Somewhere in that fog waited the Mystic Spire.

The air was already thick with low-level psychic static---whispers that
weren't there, shadows that moved in the corner of the eye. The fifty
students of the Royal Academy stood in formation, shifting
uncomfortably. They were warriors, used to steel and fire. This... this
was fighting ghosts.

Tim walked among them. He moved down the lines, looking every student in
the eye. He didn't offer false comfort. He offered mechanics.

"The mind, alone, is fragile," Tim said, his voice cutting through the
whispers. He stopped in front of a trembling first-year from House
Miller. "It is a room with one door. If they hold that door shut, you
are trapped with your own fear."

He turned, addressing the entire group.

"But fifty minds, together?" Tim grinned, tapping his temple.
"IMPENETRABLE! You are not fifty rooms anymore. You are a fortress."

He grabbed Cassian Redgrave's shoulder and pulled him next to Gareth
Orso.

"Pair up!" Tim commanded. "Iron Head with Draft Zone. Golden Circle with
Squad. Find a partner who is *nothing* like you."

The students hesitated, then scrambled. Nobles grabbed scholarship kids.
Mages grabbed brawlers.

"This is the Anchor System," Tim explained. "The Mystics work by making
you doubt reality. They will show you dragons. They will show you your
parents disappointed in you. They will show you the floor turning to
lava."

He walked down the center aisle.

"If you see a monster, do not fight it. Look at your partner. If your
partner does not see the monster... *it is not there.*"

A ripple of understanding went through the group. It was a reality
check. A second set of eyes to verify the world.

"Trust your partner's eyes over your own," Tim said. "If they say the
floor is solid, you walk. If they say the dragon is smoke, you walk
through it."

He stopped at the front of the formation, looking into the violet fog.

"However," Tim added, his voice dropping to a serious, warning tone.
"The students of the Mystic Spire are not warriors. They pour all their
Ki into their minds. Their bodies are weak. They are standing still,
deep in a trance, projecting these nightmares."

He turned back to Gareth and the heavy hitters.

"When you break through the illusion... when you find the dreamer
standing there, defenseless..."

Tim looked them dead in the eye.

"Just try not to kill them when they falter."

Gareth cracked his knuckles. "Gentle smash?"

"A tap," Tim corrected. "A shove. Disrupt their concentration. Do not
crush them. If we win by massacre, we lose the moral high ground. And I
worked very hard to get us that high ground."

"Anchors set!" Cassian roared, his voice booming with confidence. He was
paired with Lyssandra. Fire and Ice. Passion and Logic. "We are locked
in!"

"Circuit is live," Christopher called out from the back, his hand on
Gareth's shoulder.

Tim nodded. He stepped to the front of the line. He didn't draw a
weapon. He didn't need one. He was the Grounding Rod. He was the one who
would walk into the nightmare first and tell it that it wasn't real.

"Forward," Tim commanded. "Into the fog. And remember: if it bleeds, we
heal it. If it doesn't bleed... it's just bad weather."

The fifty students of the Royal Academy stepped forward in unison. They
held onto their partners' shoulders. They focused on the warmth of their
neighbors.

And as they marched into the violet mist, Grandmaster Valdis watched
from the high tower. He saw the fog swirl and recoil, repelled not by a
spell, but by the sheer, dense weight of fifty minds deciding,
collectively, that they refused to be afraid.
