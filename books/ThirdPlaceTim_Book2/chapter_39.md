# Chapter 38: The Sun and the Mirror

The Solar Amphitheater was blinding. The white stone floor reflected the
noon sun, creating an arena where shadows went to die.

Opposite the Academy stood the High Sanctum. They were dressed in robes
of white and gold, their skin glowing with an inner luminescence that
made them painful to look at. They stood in a perfect crescent
formation, humming a single, high-pitched note that vibrated in the
teeth.

"They look... bright," Christopher whispered. He was standing in front
of Gareth, his knees shaking, but his feet planted.

"Bright is just energy," Tim's voice came over the Circuit, calm and
grounding. "Energy is just work waiting to be done."

Grandmaster Valdis gripped the railing of his box. He saw his students.
They weren't arranged in a line of duelists. They were arranged in
clustered pairs---the "Iron" standing behind the "Polish." It looked
messy. It looked weak.

"Purify the corruption!" the High Sanctum leader, a boy with eyes like
burning magnesium, screamed.

The Sanctum raised their hands. They didn't cast individual spells. They
linked their Ki into a single, massive focal point above their
formation. A miniature sun formed, crackling with judgment.

"Here it comes," Tim said. "Anchors... brace."

The Sanctum unleashed the Glare.

It wasn't a beam; it was a wall of solid, scouring light. It roared
across the amphitheater, turning the air into plasma. It was enough
power to vaporize a fortress.

"SHIELDS!" Cassian roared.

The Iron Heads---Gareth, Torian, and the heavy hitters---slammed their
feet into the stone, pushing their dense, grey Ki forward.

"COAT!" Christopher screamed.

The Polish---Lyssandra, Mara, Milo, and the Draft Zone mages---didn't
try to stop the light. They raised their hands and projected that thin,
shimmering film of perfect, frictionless blue Ki right in front of the
Iron.

The Glare hit the line.

SCREEEEEEEEECH.

The sound was like a diamond dragging across glass. The light didn't
smash through. It hit the frictionless barriers, slid sideways, and was
shunted harmlessly to the left and right, scorching the arena walls
black.

"Hold!" Tim commanded, feeling the heat rise in the Circuit.

Behind Christopher, Gareth grunted. He was absorbing the kinetic force
of the light, his Iron Core heating up like a forge. He wrapped his
massive arms around Christopher, anchoring the smaller boy.

"I have you, little man," Gareth rumbled through the grit. "You are the
glass. I am the frame."

"It's... slippery!" Christopher yelled, sweat pouring down his face as
he maintained the perfect angle.

The Sanctum poured more power in. They couldn't believe it. The "impure"
Academy wasn't burning. They were standing.

The heat in the arena spiked. The air shimmered.

"They're committing," Tim said, watching the Sanctum leader's face twist
in frustration. "They're dumping everything into the beam. They're
empty."

Tim checked the angle. The Academy line was holding, but the "Polish"
was starting to fracture. Mara was crying out in pain. Milo's nose was
bleeding.

"Now," Tim whispered.

He tapped the Marshal's band.

"Degree Thirty! REFRACT!"

The command rippled through the Circuit.

Every pair of students shifted their stance simultaneously. They didn't
push back. They simply... tilted.

The wall of "mirrors" angled inward.

The Glare, which had been splashing harmlessly to the sides, was
suddenly caught. The Academy concentrated the reflection. They took the
Sanctum's own massive beam, condensed it, and fired it back at a single
point.

Not at the leader. At the ground in front of him.

FLASH.

The reflected light hit the white stone floor of the Sanctum's side. The
glare was blinding. The Sanctum students screamed, covering their eyes.
Their formation shattered as they stumbled back, blinded by their own
righteousness.

The beam cut out. The Sanctum was in chaos, rubbing their eyes, their Ki
disrupted.

"Circuit, drop!" Tim commanded. "Iron Heads... ADVANCE!"

The Academy line split. The "Polish" dropped to their knees, exhausted.

From behind them, the Iron Heads surged. But they didn't charge blindly.
They moved with the terrifying speed of pent-up energy released.

Cassian Redgrave led the charge. He was glowing with residual heat, his
sword drawn.

The Sanctum leader, blinking tears from his eyes, tried to summon a
shield. "You... you dirty..."

Cassian didn't slash. He didn't stab.

He used the momentum. He slid under the leader's clumsy guard---a move
Tim had used on him a hundred times. He hooked the leader's ankle with
his boot. He slammed his shoulder into the boy's chest.

The Sanctum leader went horizontal, crashing hard onto the stone.

Before the boy could scramble up, Cassian was there. He planted a heavy
boot on the boy's chest. He leveled his sword at the boy's throat.

The arena went silent.

Cassian looked down at the fallen zealot. The old Cassian would have
sneered. He would have mocked him.

But Cassian was hot. He was tired. And he had just spent ten minutes
literally holding up a scholarship student to keep him from burning.

"Your light is bright," Cassian rasped, his voice rough with smoke. "But
you forgot to look in the mirror."

He pressed the tip of the blade against the boy's robe.

"Yield."

The Sanctum leader looked at the "corrupt" noble. He looked at the
Academy students, who were helping each other up, checking on their
partners. He saw Gareth carrying Christopher. He saw Lyssandra checking
Mara's burns.

"I... yield," the leader whispered.

"WINNER: THE ROYAL KI ACADEMY!"

The roar from the stands was deafening. It wasn't just the students. The
Masters were on their feet.

In the center of the arena, Tim stood amidst the steam rising from the
cooling stones. He hadn't thrown the final punch. He hadn't taken the
glory. He watched Cassian help the Sanctum leader up, sheathing his
sword with a utilitarian snap of his repaired belt.

Tim crossed his arms. A slow, genuine smile spread across his face. It
wasn't the smile of a conqueror. It was the smile of a mechanic
listening to an engine finally hum in perfect tune.

Up in the High Box, Grandmaster Valdis didn't cheer. He stood paralyzed
for a moment, staring at the scoreboard. Then, slowly, the Iron Dean's
face cracked.

He beamed. It was a terrifying expression on a man who never smiled, a
radiant, toothy grin of absolute vindication. He looked like a man who
had just watched his children learn to walk---by stomping on the enemy.

Beside him, Lady Elara didn't beam. She leaned back in her chair,
watching Tim down on the field. She saw him check on Milo. She saw him
pat Gareth's arm.

She smirked. A small, knowing, dangerous smirk.

"Well," Elara murmured to herself, watching the celebration engulf the
boy in the grey wool. "Year One is finished."

She stood up, smoothing her robes.

"Now... we have to figure out how to survive Year Two."

Tim looked up at the box. He saw Valdis beaming. He saw Elara smirking.

He adjusted his grey tunic. He was tired. He was scorched. And he was
incredibly hungry.

*Just Tim,* he thought, listening to the crowd chant his name.

For now.
