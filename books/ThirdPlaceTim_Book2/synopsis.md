Tim arrives at the Royal Ki Academy as a charity case sponsored by House Vance. Surrounded by entitled nobles and hostile instructors, the former thief must navigate a system designed for the strong. But Tim has a secret weapon: his Copper Core and his pragmatic, survivor's mind. While others focus on flashy displays of power, Tim focuses on mechanics, logistics, and building a network of misfits. A story about reverse-engineering the nobility, finding leverage in the rules, and proving that a Copper Core can outlast the sharpest steel.
