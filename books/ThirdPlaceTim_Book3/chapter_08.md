# Chapter 8: The Kill Clock

The afternoon sun was hidden behind a bank of grey clouds, casting a
flat, shadowless light over the training pits.

"Again," Tim commanded.

In the center of the pit, Cassian Redgrave faced off against Gareth
Orso. Gareth was playing the role of an Iron-Blood shock
trooper---armored heavily, moving forward with relentless, crushing
momentum.

Cassian stepped back, his eyes scanning for an opening. Gareth swung a
padded mace. Cassian ducked smoothly, the wind of the blow ruffling his
hair. He stepped inside Gareth's guard, trapped the giant's arm with his
hilt, and executed a perfect leverage throw.

Gareth went down with a heavy *thud*, kicking up dust.

Cassian stood over him, sword point at Gareth's throat. "Kill," Cassian
panted, wiping sweat from his brow.

"Good!" Tim called out from the edge of the ring, clapping his hands.
"Your center of gravity was perfect. You didn't fight his weight; you
used it."

"Good?"

The word cut through the air like a razor.

Elara stepped down from the raised dais. She walked to the edge of the
pit, looking down at Cassian with eyes that were terrifyingly
unimpressed.

"That was pathetic," she stated flatly.

Cassian bristled, his chest heaving. "I put him on the ground, my Lady.
He is neutralized."

"It took you twelve seconds," Elara said, checking a small hourglass in
her hand. "In a duel, twelve seconds is a victory. In the Shadow-Scar,
twelve seconds is a lifetime."

She pointed a gloved finger at the empty space behind Cassian.

"While you were stepping back, checking your footing, and executing that
beautiful, academy-approved throw... two Thralls flanked you. One gutted
Milo. The other dragged Mara into the dark."

She leaned over the railing, her voice dropping to a harsh whisper.

"Sure, you 'killed' the enemy. But how many of your companions died
while you were 'tactically defeating' him?"

Cassian looked at the empty space behind him. He looked at Gareth, who
was picking himself up.

"He's a giant," Cassian argued, frustration bleeding into his voice. "I
can't just walk through him. I have to create an opening!"

"The Iron-Blood Empire has a million soldiers," Elara countered. "If you
spend twelve seconds on each one, you will be dead before you cross the
first valley."

She looked at Tim. "Fix it."

Tim looked at Elara. He saw the tension in her jaw. She was terrified
for them, and her fear was manifesting as impossible standards. But she
wasn't wrong. The math was brutal.

Tim jumped into the pit. He walked up to Cassian.

"She's right," Tim said quietly. "Your technique is perfect for the
tournament. But the tournament has rules. The Hive has physics."

Tim turned to Gareth. "Attack me. Same speed."

Gareth frowned. "Commander, I am twice your weight."

"Come at me," Tim ordered.

Gareth charged. He raised the mace.

Tim didn't step back. He didn't look for footing. He didn't try to trap
the arm.

He stepped *into* the charge.

As Gareth's arm came down, Tim didn't block it. He shot his own hand
out, fingers stiffened into a spear, aiming directly for Gareth's
throat.

He stopped one inch from the skin.

At the same time, Tim stomped on the top of Gareth's foot with his heel.

Gareth flinched, his momentum arrested instantly by the threat to his
throat and the pain in his foot. He stumbled.

Tim didn't throw him. He simply shoved Gareth's face to the side and
walked past him.

"One second," Tim said, turning around.

Gareth was still standing, confused, rubbing his neck.

"He's not dead," Cassian pointed out.

"He's stopped," Tim corrected. "I crushed his windpipe (theoretically)
and broke the small bones in his foot. He is choking and lame. He is no
longer a threat. I move to the next target."

Tim looked at Cassian.

"Elara wants speed. I want survival. To get both, you have to stop
trying to *win* the fight."

Tim picked up a training sword.

"A duelist fights to prove he is better. A soldier fights to remove an
obstacle. Gareth isn't an opponent, Cassian. He's a door that's stuck.
You don't argue with a door. You kick it down, or you take it off the
hinges."

Tim turned to the class.

"We call this the 'Economy of Violence'. Do not spend ten gold coins of
energy on a ten-copper problem."

He pointed to Gareth.

"Gareth is big. Armor is heavy. But his knees?" Tim pointed to the
joint. "It takes only twenty pounds of pressure to dislocate a kneecap
if you hit it from the side. Twenty pounds. A child can do that."

He looked at Elara. "Is that fast enough?"

Elara's expression didn't soften, but she nodded once. "If they can do
it."

She stepped into the ring. She raised her hand, and five pillars of ice
materialized in a semi-circle around Cassian.

"These are Thralls," Elara announced. "You have five seconds. If you are
still fighting when the last grain of sand falls..."

She flipped the hourglass.

"...you die."

Cassian looked at the ice pillars. He looked at his sword. He took a
breath. *Inhale. Accept.*

He didn't think about form. He didn't think about his ancestors. He
thought about cost.

GO.

Cassian exploded.

First pillar: He didn't slash. He thrust the pommel of his sword into
the "face," shattering it. *One.*

Second pillar: He didn't parry. He kicked the base, snapping the "legs."
*Two.*

Third and Fourth pillars: He spun, letting the momentum of his sword do
the work, cleaving through both with a single horizontal arc. *Three.*

Fifth pillar: He didn't have time to recover his stance. He threw his
shoulder into it, channeling his Iron Ki into a battering ram, smashing
the ice to powder. *Four.*

He stood panting, surrounded by slush.

Elara looked at the hourglass. The last grain fell.

"Four seconds," she noted.

She looked at Cassian. "Sloppy. Ugly. Brutal."

A small, terrifying smile touched her lips.

"It will do."

Cassian leaned on his sword, grinning through the exhaustion. "Economy
of Violence," he wheezed. "It's a bargain."

Tim clapped him on the shoulder. "Good bargain. Now, reset. Gareth, put
some armor on your shins. We're going again."
