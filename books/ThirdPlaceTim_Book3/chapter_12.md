# Chapter 12: The Hammer and the Net

The South Fields were vast, flat, and perfect for a slaughter.

Three hundred soldiers of the Crimson Legion stood in formation. They
were a wall of red steel and disciplined silence. They didn't have the
magical flair of the Academy, but they had mass, experience, and the
"Iron Core" discipline of career soldiers.

Opposite them stood the Alloy---the one hundred students of the Task
Force.

They looked... odd.

They weren't in a line. They were in loose, scattered clusters. Gareth
and the heavy hitters were spaced out. Christopher and the mages were
hiding behind them. Aric and the Royal Guard were distributed evenly,
acting as beacons.

General Alric rode his horse to the center line. He looked at Tim, who
stood on foot at the front of his ragged formation.

"Last chance to reduce the numbers, Commander," Alric called out. "Or to
surrender."

"We are ready," Tim called back.

Alric shook his head. He raised his sword. "LEGION! ADVANCE! CRUSH
THEM!"

BOOM-BOOM-BOOM.

Three hundred soldiers began the march. It was a terrifying sound. The
ground shook. They lowered their spears, forming a phalanx three ranks
deep that spanned the entire field. It was a wave of steel designed to
engulf and destroy.

Tim tapped his comms crystal.

"Circuit is live. Aric, give me the signal."

Prince Aric, standing in the center cluster, closed his eyes. He didn't
project arrogance. He projected the Carrier Wave. *HUMMMMM.* A golden
frequency vibrated through the air. It didn't push the enemy down; it
lifted the students up. Their reaction times sharpened. Their fear
transmuted into focus.

"Hold," Tim ordered.

The Legion accelerated. They were fifty yards away. Forty. Thirty.

"They're going to hit us!" a first-year screamed over the link.

"Let them," Tim said calmly. "Elara. The Grease."

Elara flicked her wrists. She didn't summon a wall. She summoned a sheen
of ground-level frost. Not enough to slip on if you were walking, but
enough to destroy the traction of a charging phalanx.

The front rank of the Legion hit the frost patch. They didn't fall, but
they stumbled. Their perfect rhythm broke.

"NOW!" Tim shouted. "THE NET!"

The students didn't charge. They retreated.

But they retreated in a specific pattern. The center fell back rapidly,
creating a concave pocket. The flanks held firm.

The Legion, seeing the center collapse, surged forward into the gap,
driven by instinct to exploit the weakness. They poured into the pocket.

They thought they were breaking the line. They were actually walking
into the mouth.

"Gareth! Cassian! CLOSE THE JAWS!"

From the flanks, the heavy hitters slammed inward. They didn't try to
stop the 300 men. They hit the *sides* of the formation, compressing the
soldiers together.

Suddenly, the 300 men weren't a wide line. They were a confused, crowded
mob, pressed shoulder-to-shoulder, unable to swing their swords without
hitting a comrade.

"Christopher! PRESSURE!"

Christopher and the "Polish" squad unleashed a barrage of kinetic
barriers---not to block, but to push. They pressed the mob tighter and
tighter.

General Alric watched in horror. His massive army was being compressed
into a kill zone by a force a third of its size.

"Break out!" Alric roared. "Push through the center!"

The Legion surged toward the center, toward Tim.

Tim stood alone in the heart of the pocket. He saw the wall of men
coming.

He didn't use fire. He didn't use heat.

He raised both hands. *Induction Field: Max.*

He grabbed the magnetic fields of three hundred suits of plate armor.

"HEAVY," Tim whispered.

He poured his Ki into the ground, creating a high-gravity magnetic
anomaly directly under the mob.

The soldiers felt their armor suddenly weigh three times as much. Knees
buckled. Swords dropped. Men stumbled over each other, trapped by their
own protection.

"Aric! THE VOICE!"

Aric stepped forward. He flared his Gold Core, but he didn't use it to
crush. He used it to command. He projected his voice directly into the
minds of the confused, heavy, compressed soldiers.

"**HALT.**"

It wasn't a request. It was a Royal Command, amplified by Tim's magnetic
hold and the confusion of the moment.

The Crimson Legion froze. They were dogpiled, heavy, surrounded by
students who had blades at the gaps in their armor.

Tim walked up to the lead Centurion, who was on one knee, struggling to
lift his sword.

Tim placed a hand on the Centurion's pauldron. He didn't heat it. He
just held him down.

"Three to one," Tim whispered. "But only the front row can fight. If you
can't move, you're just a statue."

Tim looked across the field to where General Alric sat on his horse,
mouth agape.

"General!" Tim called out cheerfully. "I believe we have captured your
center. Would you like to negotiate terms of surrender? We have
excellent bread."

Alric looked at his elite Legion---three hundred veterans---trapped in a
magnetic, frosty, psychological net woven by a hundred children.

He slowly sheathed his sword.

"Terms accepted," Alric grumbled, shaking his head. "Gods help the
Iron-Blood Empire. You don't fight battles, Commander. You create
disasters."

Tim smiled, the copper light fading from his eyes.

"Disasters are just unplanned logistics, General. This? This was
architecture."
