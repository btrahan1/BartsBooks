The Kingdom of Aethelgard and the Iron-Blood Empire are on the brink of war over a newly discovered region rich in Sun-Stone. To settle the dispute, they invoke the "Trial of Sovereignty," sending their future generations to fight in a harsh pocket realm called the Shadow-Scar. Tim is field-promoted to Cadet Commander, leading a mixed battalion of his Academy misfits and elitist Royal Guards. Facing an army of mind-controlled thralls led by the cold and efficient Silas, Tim must prove that logistics, survival tactics, and empathy can defeat brute force and dark magic. A high-stakes military fantasy about the burden of command and the power of connection.
