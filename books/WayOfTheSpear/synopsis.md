Twelve-year-old Bran is a "mule" at the Monastery of the Sacred Fountain, a nameless orphan with no system class or magical destiny. Built strong by years of heavy labor, Bran is fascinated by human anatomy and how the body actually moves. On a supply run outside the monastery walls, he witnesses a mysterious warrior named Gaul performing impossible feats with a spear. Noticing the boy's rare instinct for movement, Gaul gifts him a powerful manual: "The Way of the Spear". Follow Bran's journey from humble pack animal to a master of the spear!
