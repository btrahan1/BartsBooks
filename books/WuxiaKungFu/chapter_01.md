# Chapter 01: The King of Pop



The sprawling metropolis of Xuanwu City, a vibrant hub for cultivators
and martial arts clans, hummed with a palpable energy. Banners
representing the formidable Lei Clan with its snarling tiger, the
graceful Shen Sect's elegant crane, and the Jiaolong Sect's coiled
serpent, fluttered from ornate rooftops, each a testament to their power
and influence. To the citizens of this Wuxia world, it was a day like
any other; for the newly arrived 'traveler', Daniel, it was an assault
on the senses.

Daniel, conspicuous in his simple, modern attire, navigated the throng
with a bemused expression. He was a traveler, a term whispered with a
mix of awe and suspicion in the cultivation world, an individual from
another reality. A crisp, blue text box, a feature unique to his
perception, offered a constant stream of information.

[System Notice: You have entered the Azure Dragon District, the
territory of the Imperial Azure Dragon Clan. It is advised to avoid
unnecessary conflict with clan members. Their tempers are as fiery as
their signature martial technique, the 'Azure Dragon's Searing
Breath'.]

"Right," Daniel muttered with a wry smile, "avoid conflict. Simple
enough."

Barely a day into his new existence, the initial wonder was already
giving way to a burgeoning annoyance. The incessant posturing of the
clan members, their chests puffed out with pride as they announced their
lineage for the most trivial of reasons, was beginning to wear on him.
The city felt like an aviary filled with peacocks, each vying to display
the most extravagant plumage.

As if to punctuate his thoughts, a commotion erupted nearby. A young
man, no older than a teenager and draped in ostentatious emerald green
silken robes, had just kicked over a fruit vendor's stall. His coterie
of similarly clad followers snickered as the elderly vendor desperately
tried to salvage his scattered produce.

"How dare you obstruct the path of this Young Master?" the green-robed
youth sneered, his chin held at a ridiculously proud angle. "Do you not
recognize the emblem of the Verdant Jade Clan? I am Lu Feng!"

Daniel let out a weary sigh. *Of course, he is,* he mused. *The Verdant
Jade Clan, renowned for their... well, their garish green robes, I
suppose.*

A crowd had quickly gathered, their whispers weaving a tapestry of awe
and reverence.

"That's Lu Feng, the third son of the Verdant Jade Clan's patriarch!"
"I've heard he reached the Qi Condensation stage at the tender age of
sixteen!" "Such prodigious talent! His pride is to be expected!"

Daniel remained unimpressed. He'd encountered bullies in his own world,
and their arrogance was just as tedious here, regardless of their
"cultivation level." He attempted to skirt the unfolding drama, but
fate, or perhaps a mischievous system, intervened as one of Lu Feng's
entourage bumped into him.

"Watch your step, commoner!" the lackey snarled, giving Daniel a rough
shove.

Daniel stumbled, his easy-going demeanor finally showing a crack. Before
he could offer a retort, Lu Feng's imperious gaze fell upon him, his
eyes narrowing as he took in Daniel's unconventional clothing.

"You are not of this city," Lu Feng observed, his voice laced with
contempt. "You have the air of a wandering cultivator. No clan, no
backing."

Daniel met his gaze with an unreadable expression.

"I am feeling magnanimous," Lu Feng announced, his chest swelling with
self-importance. "I shall grant you the honor of 'trading pointers' with
me. It will be an invaluable lesson for a rogue cultivator such as
yourself."

A ripple of anticipation went through the crowd. "Trading pointers" was
a well-known euphemism for a duel, often initiated by powerful clan
members to assert their dominance over those without a clan's
protection.

[System Alert: You have been challenged to a duel by Lu Feng of the
Verdant Jade Clan. Do you accept?] [Y/N]

Daniel glanced at the arrogant young cultivator, then at the expectant
faces in the crowd. A slow smile began to form on his lips. He could
feel a familiar power stirring within him, the unique ability to draw
upon the legends of his own world. This, he thought, could be the
perfect opportunity to inject a little chaos into the rigid hierarchy of
the martial arts clans.

"An invaluable lesson, you say?" Daniel's voice was calm, yet it carried
a peculiar weight that silenced the crowd. "Very well. I accept your
challenge."

His smile widened. Now, the only question was, who would he be today?

A wicked grin spread across Daniel's face. He tapped the glowing 'Y' in
his vision, and the world seemed to warp around him.

"I am Michael Jackson," he declared, his voice shifting, gaining a
higher, smoother tenor. "Behold!"

A wave of shimmering energy washed over him. His simple traveler's
clothes dissolved into a suit of impossible glitter, catching the
sunlight and scattering it into a thousand dazzling points of light. On
his feet, sleek black boots appeared, polished to a mirror shine. A pair
of dark, impenetrable glasses materialized over his eyes, and a sharp,
black fedora was cocked at a jaunty angle on his head.

[Persona Equipped: The King of Pop - Micah Jax Son] [Boots of Moon
Walking]: Grants the user the ability to glide effortlessly across any
surface, defying conventional physics and making movements
unpredictable. [Glittering Suit of Blinding]: A high-level defensive
artifact. Reflects and amplifies light-based or pure energy attacks,
causing disorientation and temporary blindness in opponents. [Glasses
of Dark Intent]: Obscures the user's eyes, making it impossible for
opponents to read their intentions or anticipate their next move.
[Black Hat of Flying Assault]: A versatile spiritual weapon. Can be
thrown with incredible speed and accuracy, its brim sharpened by Qi to
the consistency of razor steel.

The crowd stared, utterly bewildered. Lu Feng, who had been preening for
his expected victory, faltered. "What... what is this sorcery? What is
that garish armor?"

Daniel ignored him. He snapped his fingers, struck a pose---one hand on
his hip, the other pointing skyward---and let out a piercing, "Woo hoo!"

He then slid backward with impossible smoothness, as if the ground
beneath him were ice, even though he was clearly walking forward. The
"Boots of Moon Walking" were in full effect, and the crowd gasped.

"He's moving without moving!" someone shrieked.

Lu Feng's arrogance curdled into confusion. "Trickery! Die!" He lunged
forward, his hands glowing with a faint green light as he unleashed his
clan's signature technique. "Verdant Jade Strike!"

He thrust his palm forward, but Daniel simply spun on his heel, the
"Glittering Suit of Blinding" catching the green energy. The suit didn't
just block the attack; it absorbed the light and exploded outwards in a
blinding flash.

"Aaaargh! My eyes!" Lu Feng screamed, stumbling back, his followers
shielding their faces.

"Cha'mon!" Daniel shouted, using the opening. He whipped the "Black Hat
of Flying Assault" from his head. It flew through the air, a spinning
disc of black death, humming with Qi. It sliced through the air with a
*shiiing* sound, neatly shearing off the topknot of Lu Feng's hair
before returning perfectly to Daniel's hand. He placed it back on his
head with a smooth flick of the wrist.

Lu Feng felt the top of his head, his eyes wide with terror as his
meticulously styled hair fluttered to the ground. He had been
humiliated. Enraged, he roared and began a complex series of hand seals
for a more powerful attack.

Daniel just shook his head. He wasn't going to let him finish. He
planted his feet, bent his knees, and with a final, triumphant yell,
performed his ultimate attack.

It was a sharp, sudden, and devastatingly potent "Pelvic Thrust."

A visible wave of pure concussive force, of raw spiritual pressure,
erupted from the motion. It wasn't lewd; it was simply power. The
shockwave slammed into Lu Feng, lifting him off his feet and sending him
cartwheeling through the air. He crashed into the remnants of the fruit
stall, landing in a heap of bruised ego and squashed melons, completely
knocked out.

The crowd was dead silent. Daniel, now standing perfectly still,
adjusted his glasses and gave a slight bow. Then, he moonwalked backward
into the stunned throng, which parted before him like the Red Sea.

Later, in the main hall of the Verdant Jade Clan...

Patriarch Lu, a man with a severe face and a beard that reached his
chest, sat upon a throne of carved jade. Before him knelt the bruised
and terrified followers of his son.

"Explain," the Patriarch's voice was a low growl that promised pain.
"You tell me my son, a prodigy of our clan, was defeated in a single
exchange by a clanless rogue? What was his technique? The 'Mountain
Shattering Fist'? The 'Silent Shadow Art'?"

The first disciple trembled. "Patriarch, it... it was unlike any art we
have ever witnessed."

"He called himself... 'Micah Jaksona'," the second disciple stammered
out, butchering the name horribly.

The Patriarch's eyes narrowed. "I have never heard of this master. What
of his spiritual weapon?"

"It... was a hat, Patriarch," the first disciple whispered, bracing for
impact.

"...A hat," the Patriarch said, his voice dangerously flat. "He defeated
Lu Feng with a hat."

"It flew like a spirit wheel, sharp as a gale-wind blade!" the second
disciple squeaked. "It sheared the Young Master's topknot clean off! And
his armor, Patriarch! It was woven from captured stars! When the Young
Master struck, it unleashed a blinding sun, leaving us all disoriented!"

Patriarch Lu stroked his beard, a flicker of disbelief in his eyes. "A
defensive treasure and a throwing weapon... unorthodox, but not unheard
of. What of his movement technique? It must have been profound to evade
my son's strike."

The two disciples shared a look of pure, unadulterated confusion.

"Patriarch," the first one began, choosing his words carefully. "He...
glided. Upon the ground. He seemed to walk forward, but he moved
backward. As if the world itself was pulling him. A 'Lunar Stroll,' we
think he called it."

"And... and his eyes, Patriarch!" the second one added. "They were
hidden behind a sheet of black crystal! We could not see his gaze, could
not anticipate his killing intent!"

The Patriarch was on his feet now, his face a thundercloud. "A blinding
suit, a razor hat, a reverse-walking technique, and soul-hiding
glasses... what cowardly methods! But what was the final blow? The one
that defeated my son? It must have been a powerful palm strike to
shatter his Qi guard!"

The first disciple swallowed hard, his face paling. "No, Patriarch."

"It was... a hip movement."

Patriarch Lu froze, one hand gripping the arm of his jade throne so hard
it creaked. "...A what?"

"He thrust his pelvis forward, Patriarch," the second disciple blurted
out, shutting his eyes tight. "A wave of unimaginable spiritual force
erupted from it! It sent the Young Master flying! He... he called it the
'Pelvic Thrust'!"

The grand hall of the Verdant Jade Clan fell into a stunned, horrified
silence, broken only by the sound of the Patriarch's heavy, ragged
breathing as he tried to comprehend how his prodigious son had been
defeated by a sparkly suit, a flying hat, and a thrust of the hips.
