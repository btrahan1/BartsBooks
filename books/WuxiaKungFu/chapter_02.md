# Chapter 02: The King of Rock and Roll

Word of the eccentric wanderer who had humiliated Lu Feng spread through
Xuanwu City like wildfire. The tale grew with each telling: a mysterious
master named 'Micah Jaksona' who wore captured starlight, commanded a
flying hat of death, and defeated foes with a thrust of his groin. The
Verdant Jade Clan had become a laughingstock, their prestige taking a
significant blow.

Seeking to quell the rumors and restore their honor, they sent a
delegation to the city's central training grounds the very next day, a
vast, open-air arena where duels were officially sanctioned. They were
determined to find this 'Jaksona' and force a more "honorable" battle.

They didn't have to wait long. A figure strode confidently into the
center of the arena, drawing the eyes of the assembled crowd. It was
Daniel, back in his simple traveler's clothes. He spotted the sea of
emerald green robes and a smirk played on his lips.

An older, more severe-looking cultivator from the Verdant Jade Clan
stepped forward. "You are the rogue who ambushed our Young Master Lu
Feng?" he demanded.

"Ambushed?" Daniel chuckled. "He begged me to 'trade pointers'. I merely
obliged." He cast a theatrical gaze over the crowd. "But today is a new
day! The persona of yesterday is gone!" He struck a pose, legs wide, one
hand holding an invisible microphone. "I challenge the next stooge of
Clan Verdant. Behold, I am Elvis Presley!"

Again, the shimmering energy enveloped him. His clothes morphed into a
brilliant white jumpsuit, adorned with countless rhinestones that blazed
under the sun. The collar was so high it practically framed his head,
and a heavy, golden belt sat low on his hips. His hair poofed up into a
perfect black pompadour, and a pair of gold-rimmed sunglasses
materialized on his face.

[Persona Equipped: The King of Rock and Roll - Elvis Presley\] [Vegas
Jumpsuit of Overwhelming Charisma\]: An ostentatious defensive artifact
that mesmerizes foes with its sheer star power, lowering their will to
fight. Its gems can deflect minor physical and spiritual attacks.
[Boots of Rhythmic Assault\]: Allows the user to channel Qi into the
ground with every step, creating disorienting tremors that follow a
specific rhythm. [Sunglass-Visor of the King\]: Projects an aura of
supreme confidence, making the user's movements seem more potent and
impactful than they actually are. [Vocal Art - All Shook Up\]: A
powerful sonic technique that sends chaotic vibrations through an
opponent's meridians, disrupting their Qi flow and causing
uncontrollable muscle spasms.

The Verdant Jade Clan stared, their minds reeling. This was even more
bizarre than yesterday. The lead cultivator, Lu Jian, a senior disciple
known for his swift 'Willow Blade' sword style, grit his teeth. "Enough
of these cheap illusions! You mock my clan! I, Lu Jian, will take your
head!"

He drew a slender, sharp sword that hummed with energy. But before he
could lunge, "Elvis" grabbed an invisible microphone, tilted his head
back, and began to sing, his voice magically amplified to fill the
entire arena.

"A-well-a bless my soul, what's-a wrong with me?"

Lu Jian froze mid-stride. A strange vibration ran up his legs.

"I'm itching like a man on a fuzzy tree!"

"Elvis" swiveled his hips, and with each movement, Lu Jian's own body
twitched uncontrollably. The crowd murmured in confusion. Was this some
kind of puppet art?

"My friends say I'm actin' wild as a bug! I'm in love..." He pointed
directly at the horrified Lu Jian. "...I'm all shook up!"

As he belted out the final line, a palpable wave of sonic energy and
chaotic Qi erupted from him. Lu Jian cried out as his own spiritual
energy went haywire. His sword arm trembled violently, his knees knocked
together, and he staggered as if drunk. The "Vocal Art: All Shook Up"
had completely destabilized his Qi.

"Wha-what is this demonic sound art?" Lu Jian stammered, trying to
steady his blade.

He charged, swinging his sword in a desperate arc. "Elvis" simply
shuffled to the side, his "Boots of Rhythmic Assault" tapping out a
steady beat on the stone floor. He swiveled, ducked under a swipe, and
came up with a sneer on his face, his upper lip curling in contempt.
This simple expression seemed to carry an immense spiritual pressure,
making Lu Jian falter again, suddenly feeling deeply inadequate.

With his opponent completely disoriented and demoralized, "Elvis" saw
his opening. He spun around, launching into a move no one in this world
had ever witnessed. He swung his leg out in a wide, powerful arc while
simultaneously performing a dramatic, open-handed chop to Lu Jian's
neck. It was less a martial art and more a dance move, but it was
charged with all the flair and power of his persona.

The blow connected with a resounding *thwack*. Lu Jian's eyes rolled
back into his head, and he collapsed into an unconscious heap, his sword
clattering beside him.

"Elvis" straightened his collar, gave the stunned crowd a charming,
lopsided grin, and announced, "Thank you. Thank you very much." He then
swaggered out of the arena, leaving a second day of utter humiliation
for the Verdant Jade Clan in his wake.

That evening, in the now very tense main hall of the Verdant Jade
Clan...

Patriarch Lu's face was ashen. His teacup was trembling in his hand.
Before him knelt the disciples who had witnessed the second, even more
preposterous defeat.

"Start from the beginning," the Patriarch said, his voice a strained
whisper. "And do not leave out a single, humiliating detail."

The disciple, a young man named Wei, took a shuddering breath.
"Patriarch, it was the same rogue... but he was different. He called
himself... 'El-Vis Pres-Li'."

"Another absurd name," the Patriarch muttered. "What of his 'armor of
captured stars'?"

"It was gone, Patriarch!" Wei exclaimed. "Replaced by... a suit of pure
white, studded with thousands of shining spirit-gems! It... it shone
with a power that weakened the spirit, a 'Charismatic' aura, they called
it! And his hair... it was a sculpted monstrosity, defying the very
winds!"

Patriarch Lu's knuckles were white. "So, another defensive treasure. And
his weapon? Was it the flying hat again?"

"No, Patriarch," Wei squeaked. "His weapon... was his voice."

The Patriarch paused, his teacup halfway to his lips. "...His voice."

"Yes! He unleashed a demonic song, a 'Vocal Art'!" Wei's eyes were wide
with remembered terror. "He shouted about being... 'All Shook Up'! The
vibrations attacked Senior Brother Lu Jian's meridians directly! His Qi
was thrown into chaos! I have never seen anything like it!"

"So this 'Pres-Li' is a sound cultivator," the Patriarch mused, a
flicker of understanding in his eyes. "A rare and difficult path. But
what of his movement? The 'Lunar Stroll'?"

Wei shook his head frantically. "No, Patriarch! This was a 'Hip
Swiveling Evasion Art'! He would... gyrate! And rock! He moved with a
strange rhythm that seemed to shake the very ground he walked on!"

Patriarch Lu looked like he was about to have a stroke. "Gyrate. He
defeated a master of the 'Willow Blade' style... by gyrating."

"And... and there was one more thing, Patriarch," Wei said, his voice
barely audible. "A spiritual pressure technique. It wasn't an attack,
but... an expression."

"An expression?"

"He would... he would sneer," Wei explained weakly. "He curled his upper
lip, like this..." Wei tried his best to imitate the Elvis sneer,
resulting in a pained-looking grimace that made him look like he had a
toothache. "It... it struck at Senior Brother's spirit, crushed his
confidence!"

Patriarch Lu stared at his disciple's ridiculous face, at the utter
nonsense he was hearing, and for a moment, he felt a profound sense of
despair. His clan, one of the great clans of Xuanwu City, had now been
defeated by a pelvic thrust and a demonic song accompanied by a sneer.
He slowly placed his teacup down before he crushed it in his hand.

"Find me everything there is to know about these masters," he growled.
"'Micah Jaksona' and 'El-Vis Pres-Li'. I will not be humiliated a third
time."
