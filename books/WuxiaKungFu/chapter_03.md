# Chapter 03: The Amazonian Princess

The next day, the atmosphere in the Xuanwu City training grounds was
thick with tension. The Verdant Jade Clan had suffered two days of
unprecedented, bizarre humiliation. Today, they would not be trifled
with. They dispatched not one disciple, but five of their elite Jade
Guard, led by the stoic and powerful Lu Tian, the clan's First Disciple.
They moved as one, a phalanx of grim-faced emerald warriors, their
killing intent a palpable shroud over the arena.

The crowd watched from a safe distance, buzzing with anticipation. What
new madness would the wandering cultivator unleash today?

Daniel walked into the center of the ring, seemingly unfazed by the wall
of murderous jade facing him.

"Rogue cultivator!" Lu Tian's voice was cold as ice. "Your mockery of my
clan ends now. We are not here to 'trade pointers'. We are here to
deliver judgment."

The five Jade Guard members drew their weapons in perfect unison, their
Qi flaring.

Daniel simply placed his hands on his hips, a confident, almost serene
smile on his face. "Judgment? Let's talk about justice instead." He
raised his chin. "You face not a rogue, but a champioclan. Behold, I am Wonder Woman!"

The transformation was the most dramatic yet. Light didn't just shimmer;
it seemed to bend around him as his form shifted, becoming taller, more
statuesque, with flowing black hair. His clothes were replaced by a
striking suit of armor---a red bustier emblazoned with a golden eagle,
blue briefs spangled with white stars, and knee-high red boots. On her
wrists were thick, silver bracelets, and on her brow sat a golden tiara.
At her hip, a coiled, glowing golden rope pulsed with a gentle light.

[Persona Equipped: The Amazonian Princess - Wan Da Wu Man] [Aegis of
Themyscira]: A divine set of armor that grants superhuman durability
and resistance to all forms of damage. Its appearance is often
deceptively revealing. [Bracelets of Submission]: Indestructible
artifacts capable of deflecting any projectile, from physical arrows to
pure energy blasts. [Tiara of Ranged Assault]: A throwing weapon of
incredible speed and precision. Returns to the user's hand after being
thrown. [Lasso of Unquestionable Truth]: A mystical spirit rope. It is
unbreakable and anyone bound by it is compelled to speak only the
absolute truth. [Innate Power: Might of a Demigoddess]: Grants the
user immense physical strength, far surpassing the limits of mortal
cultivators.

The Jade Guard faltered, their eyes wide. Their opponent had not only
changed his clothes, he had become a woman. A tall, powerfully built
woman in what appeared to be the most impractical battle attire they had
ever seen.

"What is this demonic shapeshifting art?" Lu Tian snarled, trying to
regain his composure. "Do not be fooled by her scandalous armor!
Attack!"

Two guards at the rear flicked their wrists, sending a volley of
poison-tipped senbon needles whistling through the air. "Wan Da Wu Man."
didn't even try to dodge. She simply crossed her arms in front of her
face. *CLANG-CLANG-CLANG-CLANG!* The bracelets flashed with silver
light, and every single needle was deflected harmlessly into the stone
floor.

The guards stared in disbelief. Before they could react, she was moving.
She closed the distance to the front line in an instant, her speed
breathtaking. A guard swung his halberd in a deadly arc meant to cleave
her in two. She caught the polearm with one hand, stopping it dead. The
guard's eyes bulged. With her other hand, she delivered a casual
backhand slap. The resulting *CRACK* was like a thunderclap, and the
guard was sent spinning through the air, crashing into the arena wall 50
feet away, out cold.

"Preposterous strength!" another guard yelled, trying a flanking
maneuver. "Wan Da Wu Man." whipped the golden tiara from her head. It
flew like a gilded boomerang, slicing the sword from the guard's hand
with a *shiiing* before circling back perfectly to her grasp.

Only Lu Tian remained. His face was pale, but his resolve was firm. He
was the First Disciple, a master of the 'Jade Serpent Coiling Strike'.
He channeled all of his Qi, his body glowing with a malevolent green
light as he prepared his ultimate technique.

But he never got the chance to use it. With a flick of her wrist, "Won
da Wo man" sent the golden rope flying. It uncoiled with unnatural
speed, looping around the stunned Lu Tian, binding his arms to his
torso. He roared, flaring his Qi in an attempt to incinerate the rope,
but it simply glowed brighter, completely unharmed.

"You are bound by the Lasso of Truth," her voice was clear and
commanding. "Now, answer me. Do you truly believe your clan is in the
right?"

Lu Tian's face contorted. He fought it, his jaw clenched, sweat beading
on his forehead. But the Lasso's power was absolute. The words tore from
his throat against his will. "No! We are arrogant bullies who use our
name to terrorize the weak! Our pride is a fragile shell!"

The crowd gasped. Members of other clans began to laugh openly.

"Wan Da Wu Man." pressed on, her gaze unwavering. "And what do you truly
think of your Patriarch's son, Lu Feng?"

Tears of shame streamed down Lu Tian's face as the truth was ripped from
him. "He is an incompetent, spoiled brat who brings shame to the name of
cultivator! He deserved what that 'Micah Jax Son' did to him and more!"

With a final flick, she recalled the lasso. Lu Tian fell to his knees,
not wounded in body, but utterly shattered in spirit. "Wan Da Wu Man."
gave a firm, disappointed nod, then crouched and leaped, clearing the
entire arena wall in a single, graceful bound, disappearing into the
city.

That night, the Verdant Jade Clan's main hall was deathly quiet...

Patriarch Lu looked a decade older. His most elite guards had been
defeated, and his First Disciple had publicly denounced him and his son.
The kneeling disciple, Wei, who had the misfortune of witnessing all
three debacles, trembled so hard his teeth chattered.

"Tell me," the Patriarch's voice was a hollow rasp.

Wei began, his voice cracking. "Patriarch... he became a woman. A
warrior goddess. She called herself... 'Wan Da Wu Man'."

An elder choked. "A woman?"

"Her armor was... unorthodox," Wei said, blushing. "Red and blue...
very... efficient in its use of material. We thought she was a fool. We
were wrong. Her silver bracelets were impenetrable divine items, and her
strength was that of a Demon Beast!"

He recounted the swift, brutal defeat of the Jade Guard, including the
flying tiara. Patriarch Lu just stared into space, his mind struggling
to build a coherent picture of this enemy. A singing, gyrating man... a
moonwalking fop... and now a warrior goddess in her underwear.

"And Lu Tian?" the Patriarch whispered. "What of his defeat?"

Wei flinched. "She used a golden spirit rope, Patriarch. The 'Lasso of
Unquestionable Truth'."

"...The what?"

"It... it forces the bound to speak their innermost thoughts," Wei
stammered, unable to meet the Patriarch's gaze. He then repeated, word
for word, Lu Tian's devastating public confessions.

When he finished, the silence in the hall was absolute. It was no longer
a matter of pride or reputation. This was a spiritual attack, a
psychological war. Their enemy didn't just beat them; he made them beat
themselves.

Patriarch Lu finally looked up, his eyes filled not with anger, but with
a profound and chilling fear. "What manner of devil have we provoked?
What sect cultivates the arts of public humiliation?"

The Verdant Jade Clan was in a state of crisis. Their finest disciples,
representing sword arts, Qi arts, and even elite guards, had been
systematically and bizarrely dismantled. Patriarch Lu, in a desperate
gambit, summoned the clan's greatest body cultivator, Lu Bao, a mountain
of a man known as "The Jade Wall" for his nigh-impenetrable Iron Body
technique. He was their answer to the raw physical power displayed by
"Won da Wo man."
