# Chapter 04: The People's Champion

The next day, a strange sight greeted the citizens of Xuanwu City. In
the central arena, a square platform had been erected, cordoned off by
thick ropes. Daniel, back in his simple clothes, stood in the middle of
it, a hand cupped to his ear.

"I said, ARE YOU READY?!" he bellowed to the crowd, which was larger
than ever. They roared back, a mixture of confusion and wild excitement.
This wasn't the prelude to a duel; it was a festival.

Lu Bao, flanked by clan members, strode into the arena, his heavy
footsteps thudding on the stone. He was seven feet tall, with muscles
like granite boulders. He looked at the roped ring with contempt. "I
have no need for your stage, trickster. Face me on the stone of the
arena, where true warriors fight."

Daniel just grinned. He slowly turned to face Lu Bao, his eyes electric.
He raised one hand. "You want to face the warrior? First, you must face
the man." He ripped off his shirt, and the now-familiar wave of
transformative energy washed over him.

His physique swelled to impossible proportions, muscles coiling like
pythons. A magnificent, intricate tattoo of a bull's head seemed to burn
itself onto his arm. His pants became simple black briefs, and he wore
heavy black boots and elbow pads. But the most significant change was
the look in his eyes---an intensity that could burn holes in steel.

He took a deep breath, and his voice boomed across the square, a
challenge to the heavens themselves. "Can you smell what The Rock is
cooking!"

[Persona Equipped: The People's Champion - Da Wok] [Loincloth of the
Immovable Mountain]: Minimalist armor that grants the user an
unbreakable center of gravity, making him almost impossible to knock
over. [Boots of Stomping Mudholes]: Channels immense concussive force
through the user's stomps, capable of shattering stone and buckling
armor. [Spiritual Tattoo of the Brahma Bull]: A living spiritual
tattoo that provides a constant, surging source of pure physical power
and battle rage. [The People's Eyebrow of Supreme Disdain]: A
spiritual pressure technique projected through a single facial
expression. It attacks an opponent's confidence, making them question
their own strength and worth. [Finishing Art: The Rock Bottom
Earth-Shattering Slam]: A devastating grappling technique that lifts an
opponent and slams them into the ground with the force of a meteor.
[Finishing Art: The People's Elbow of Final Judgment]: A ritualistic
final blow that draws upon the adoration of the crowd to deliver an
unstoppable, Qi-infused elbow drop.

Lu Bao scoffed. "Da Wok? You name yourself after a cooking pan? You
have finally gone mad!" He charged forward, aiming to grapple this new
foe.

"Da Wok" didn't move. He simply stood there and slowly, deliberately,
raised one eyebrow.

It was an attack unlike any other. Lu Bao felt it in his soul---a wave
of pure, unadulterated disdain. It was as if this "Wok" was looking at
him not as a warrior, but as something insignificant, a bug to be
scraped off a shoe. Lu Bao's charge faltered, his Iron Body technique
flickering as his confidence wavered.

Seizing the moment, "Da Wok" exploded into motion. He met Lu Bao with a
flurry of powerful, open-handed strikes to the chest, driving the giant
back towards the roped ring. He threw Lu Bao into a corner and began
stomping his feet, each impact from the "Boots of Stomping Mudholes"
sending cracks through the arena floor and jarring Lu Bao's bones.

Lu Bao roared in fury and shoved his way out, finally managing to land a
blow---a punch that could dent steel---square on Da Wok's jaw. The
Wok's head snapped back... and then slowly turned back to Lu Bao, a
dangerous grin on his face. He hadn't even budged from his spot.

"You have sealed your fate," Da Wok growled. He ducked under a wild
swing, hoisted the massive Lu Bao onto his shoulder with shocking ease,
spun him around, and drove him into the ground with the "Rock Bottom
Earth-Shattering Slam."

CRACK-BOOM!

The entire platform splintered, and the stone beneath it spiderwebbed
with cracks. Lu Bao lay gasping, his Iron Body technique shattered, his
spirit broken.

But Da Wok wasn't finished. This was a performance. He theatrically
ripped one elbow pad off and threw it into the cheering crowd. He ran to
one side of the broken ring, bounced off the ropes, ran to the other
side, and then hit the center, leaping high into the air. For a
glorious, terrible moment, he hung there, a god of battle, before coming
down with all his weight and the focused energy of the crowd into one,
final, devastating elbow drop.

The impact was absolute. Lu Bao was knocked completely unconscious.

"Da Wok" rose, stood over his fallen foe, and raised his arms to the
heavens as the crowd chanted a name they didn't fully understand. "WOK!
WOK! WOK!"

Later, in a now heavily fortified chamber deep within the Verdant Jade
Clan's compound...

Patriarch Lu was no longer drinking tea. He was clutching a strong
bottle of sorghum wine, his face a mask of utter despair. The disciple
Wei, who now looked like a perpetually startled ghost, was giving his
report.

"...and he called himself 'Da Wok', Patriarch."

Patriarch Lu took a long, shaky swig from the bottle. "A... cooking
wok."

"Yes, Patriarch," Wei confirmed. "His armor... was mostly his own skin.
He had a demonic bull spirit tattooed on his arm that gave him monstrous
power."

"Did he use the hip-thrusting art? The demonic song? The truth rope?"
the Patriarch asked, a desperate edge to his voice.

Wei shook his head. "None of those, Patriarch. His arts were... more
primal. More insulting."

"Insulting?" an elder asked weakly.

"His opening move, Honored Elder," Wei said, his voice trembling as he
recalled it, "was... an eyebrow."

The entire room was silent save for the gurgling of the Patriarch's wine
bottle.

"An... eyebrow," the Patriarch repeated, his voice dangerously calm.

"Yes! He just... raised it!" Wei demonstrated, though it looked more
like a pained facial spasm. "The spiritual disdain was so powerful it
nearly shattered First Brother Lu Bao's battle will before the fight
even began!"

The Patriarch stared at his disciple's twitching face, then took another
long drink.

"He... he slammed Lu Bao into the ground with an art called the
'Earth-Shattering Slam'," Wei continued, "but it was the final blow...
I... I do not know how to describe it, Patriarch."

"Try," the Patriarch growled.

"He... he performed a victory ritual before the fight was even over!"
Wei blurted out. "He discarded a piece of his armor, ran around the ring
for no reason, and then... and then he struck the final blow... with his
elbow."

The Patriarch slowly placed the wine bottle on the table. He looked at
the assembled elders of his powerful clan. They had spent centuries
perfecting their sword styles, their Qi cultivation, their alchemy. They
had defenses against soul attacks, poison arts, and siege weapons.

But they had no defense against this. No ancient scroll contained a
counter for a warrior goddess's truth lasso, a pop king's demonic song,
or a brawler's eyebrow.

"We are not fighting a man," the Patriarch whispered, a horrifying
realization dawning on him. "We are fighting a pantheon of
incomprehensible, mocking gods."
