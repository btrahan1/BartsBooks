# Chapter 06: The Grand Architect

Their journey to the Crimson Peaks was, for Daniel, a three-day walk.
For Jinshi, it was the first three days of the greatest epic ever
conceived.

"Behold!" Jinshi would announce to a flock of startled birds as Daniel
crossed a stream. "The Master of Many Faces traverses the Whispering
Creek, his steps so light they do not trouble the water's spirit! What
profound movement art is this?" "It's called 'walking on rocks,'
Jinshi," Daniel would reply flatly.

Later, as Daniel bit into a steamed bun at a roadside stall: "Witness
the sublime fortitude of the Wandering Calamity!" Jinshi would proclaim
to the bewildered stall owner. "He consumes a mortal meal, not for
sustenance, but to remind himself of the fleeting nature of the world he
seeks to correct! His every bite is a lesson in humility!" "I was
hungry," Daniel would clarify, mouth full.

Despite the incessant commentary, the landscape grew more dramatic. The
gentle green hills gave way to stark, red-tinged rock formations that
clawed at the sky. The peaks looked like the upturned blades of colossal
swords, and in the center, one spire of black stone rose above them all,
an arrogant finger pointed at the heavens.

"The fortress of the Heavenly Blade Sect," Jinshi whispered, his usual
theatrics replaced by a flicker of genuine awe and fear. "They say a
disciple isn't considered proficient until they can use their Sword Qi
to peel a grape from a hundred paces."

At the base of the spire lay Blade's Edge Town, a settlement that
existed to serve the sect. The atmosphere was immediately oppressive.
Banners depicting a single, perfect sword hung from every building. The
citizens walked with their heads down, scurrying to make way for the
sect disciples who strode through the streets as if they owned them.

And in the center of the town square, the predicted trouble was brewing.

Five Heavenly Blade disciples, clad in silver and white robes
embroidered with sword motifs, were making their daily rounds of terror.
They were led by a sneering young man with a sword handle so
ostentatiously jeweled it was a wonder he could lift it.

"Look at this junk!" the leader, Jian Feng, sneered, kicking over a
blacksmith's display of farming tools. "No spirit, no edge! How can you
call yourself a smith when you forge such dull implements? You bring
shame to the name of metal!"

Two of his cronies were harassing an elderly tea merchant. "This tea is
weak! Like your spine, old man!" one of them laughed, pouring a pot onto
the ground. "Perhaps a 'sword-polishing fee' would help you afford
better leaves?"

The final two had cornered a young woman carrying a basket of herbs.
"What's this? Bitter herbs?" one of them said, grabbing a handful and
crushing them. "Your attitude is just as bitter. A pretty face shouldn't
be ruined by such a sour expression. You should smile more when the
Heavenly Blade Sect graces you with its presence!"

The townsfolk watched, their faces a mixture of fear and simmering rage,
but no one dared to intervene. The silver-and-white robes were a symbol
of absolute power in this region.

Jinshi began to tremble, but it was with excitement, not fear. He looked
at Daniel, his eyes wide, his scroll and brush already in hand.

"A new chapter in the saga!" he whispered, his voice quivering with
anticipation. "Five arrogant blades, unaware they are about to be struck
by a divine hammer! O, Great Teacher, what lesson will you impart today?
What persona will you adopt to school these fools who know only the
sword?"

Daniel looked at the five bullies, a slow, predatory grin spreading
across his face. He cracked his knuckles, the sound like a thunderclap
in the tense square.

"They believe the sword is the only path?" Daniel mused, his eyes
gleaming with a strange, new light. "That's fine. I can work with that.
Today, Jinshi, they're not getting a lesson. They're getting a
masterclass."

Jinshi's breath hitched, his eyes wide as Daniel's aura began to shift.
He recognized the signs---the air itself was warping to accommodate the
new reality. He took a deep, theatrical gasp, straightened his scholar's
cap, and unrolled his scroll, his voice booming out with the fervor of a
zealot, shaking the very stillness of Blade's Edge Town.

"Hark, ye arrogant wielders of steel and petty tyranny! You have defiled
the innocent, you have polluted the markets, and you have, most
unforgivably, interrupted the grand entrance of a True Master!"

He pointed a trembling, dramatic finger at Daniel, who was now
mid-transformation. "Prepare yourselves to face a cultivator of
unparalleled wealth and spiritual fortitude! A being whose aura is the
color of gold and the scent of sweet victory!"

The final surge of energy hit. Daniel was replaced by a figure of utter,
unapologetic bombast. He was shorter, wider, and his skin had acquired a
distinct, deep orange hue that made the silver-robed disciples look
sickly pale. His attire was a sharp, expensive-looking dark suit,
perfectly tailored, but the true spectacle was his hair---a magnificent,
impossible creation of golden-yellow spiritual energy, swept back and up
in a gravity-defying wave that seemed to possess a life of its own.

On his head sat a simple, red cap, embroidered not with a clan sigil,
but with four bold white characters: "**MA.GA.**" (Jinshi had already
translated it as **M**ake **A**nime **G**reat **A**gain, a motto he
believed summed up the master's goal to correct the world's tacky
aesthetics).

"Behold, Do Nal Da Trum Pa!" Jinshi shrieked, his voice cracking with
excitement. "The Grand Master of Commerce and King of the Golden Qi!"

[Persona Equipped: The Grand Architect - Do nal da Trum pah] [Hair of
Golden Spiritual Barrier]: An incredibly powerful, focused defensive
structure. It deflects all weak-willed attacks and forces opponents to
question its authenticity. [MAGA Hat (Make Anime Great Again)]: A
spiritual artifact that channels the boundless, unwavering confidence of
its wearer, projecting an aura of *Absolute Rightness* that makes
opponents instinctively agree with any statement made. [Skin of
Self-Regeneration]: A constantly active defense that makes minor
injuries and cuts immediately fade. It also subconsciously intimidates
those who are not as *wealthy* as the user. [Vocal Art: The Grand
Statement]: Any statement made by the user is metaphysically amplified
and treated as irrefutable truth within a small radius, demoralizing the
opponent. [Finishing Art: The Unstoppable Finger of "You're Fired!"]:
A focused beam of concentrated, pure authority Qi shot from the index
finger. It does not merely injure the opponent; it metaphysically
nullifies their employment within their current life path.

Jian Feng, the lead bully, stopped his sneering. He stared at the figure
with a mixture of confusion and disgust. "What in the name of the
Heavenly Blade is that *hair*? And that skin! Is he afflicted with the
'Great Orange Plague'?"

"Silence, little blade!" "Do Nal Da Trum Pa" bellowed, his voice surprisingly
deep and gravelly, echoing with immense spiritual power. He advanced a
single step, his suit seeming to shimmer with aggressive wealth.

"You look like... like you just rolled out of a mud hole and covered
yourself in cheap varnish!" Jian Feng retorted, trying to use his Sword
Qi to intimidate the strange man.

"Do Nal Da Trum Pa" stopped, his face contorting into an expression of theatrical
offense. He pointed at Jian Feng's jeweled sword. "Look at that sword.
It's terrible. A total loser of a sword. It's got a weak hilt, sad
metal, and the gems are fake, okay? Everyone knows it. Believe me.
Everyone is talking about it. The best swords? I know them. I own them.
I only use the best swords, the sharpest, the most tremendous swords.
And this... this sad little piece of metal is a disgrace!"

The "Vocal Art: The Grand Statement" took effect. Jian Feng's confidence
wavered. His hand subconsciously tightened on his jeweled sword, a
sudden, horrifying doubt entering his mind. *Are the gems fake? It* is\*
a bit dull...\*

"Do Nal Da Trum Pa" then turned his gaze to the two disciples who had ruined the
tea. "And *you*. Ruining good tea. Sad! The tea is tremendous. Everyone
loves this tea. You're trying to destroy a small business. That's bad,
folks. Very, very bad. We are going to make this street great again.
You're fired from your job of being pathetic bullies!"

He punctuated the statement by pointing a stiff, accusatory finger at
the disciple who had poured out the tea. A blinding, focused ray of
golden-orange Qi shot from the tip of his finger.

The beam struck the disciple's chest. It wasn't an explosion of force,
but a profound, spiritual shock. The disciple didn't fall down wounded;
he simply stared at his own hands, his face slack with existential
crisis. "F-fired? From... from my whole life...?" he whispered, his
silver robes suddenly feeling cheap and meaningless. He dropped his
sword and wandered off, completely defeated.

The final two disciples, who had been harassing the woman with herbs,
now looked terrified.

"You! And you!" "Do Nal Da Trum Pa" declared, pointing at them both with both
hands, his expression a mixture of outrage and supreme confidence.
"Terrible. Absolutely terrible. These herbs? Best herbs. Tremendous
herbs. The most beautiful herbs. You treat women with respect! We love
women! But you? You're a low-energy mess! A disaster! Get off my street,
losers!"

He raised his hands and clapped them together in a single, loud,
definitive *SNAP*.

The concussive force of the clap was enormous, sending both disciples
staggering backward, their robes covered in the crushed, bitter herbs.
They didn't even try to fight; the sheer confidence and overwhelming
negative rhetoric had reduced them to quivering wrecks.

Jian Feng, the leader, was the only one left. He roared in fury, casting
aside his self-doubt. "I don't care about your hair or your rhetoric,
demon! I am a swordsman!" He charged, drawing his sword and channeling a
wave of keen Sword Qi at "Trum pah."

"Do Nal Da Trum Pa" didn't flinch. He simply tucked his chin, letting the Sword
Qi strike his massive, immovable hairdo. The golden hair barely rippled.
The attack had been deflected by a sheer wall of narcissism.

"Pathetic," "Do Nal Da Trum Pa" said, almost sadly. He lowered his hand, his
index finger pointed straight at the defeated bully.

"You're a disgrace. A low-quality person. A total failure. You're all
done. Get out! You're fired!"

*FZZZZT.* The final, unstoppable beam of golden authority struck Jian
Feng in the heart. He slumped to the ground, his eyes clear but empty,
his dreams of becoming a Sword Immortal completely nullified.

"Do Nal Da Trum Pa" adjusted his tie, smoothed his hair, and turned to the
bewildered crowd. "A job well done. We beat them, folks. We won so big.
The biggest win. Everyone agrees. Now, let's go build a tremendous
wall."

He then marched off toward the Heavenly Blade Sect's fortress, Jinshi
running wildly after him, screaming out the miraculous deeds of Do nal
da Trum pah to the awestruck town.

Jinshi, the Teller of Tales, was practically levitating on a cloud of
manic ecstasy. His voice had gone hoarse, but he didn't care. He ran
alongside "Do Nal Da Trum Pa," who was now striding with immense,
purposeful energy toward the impossibly tall black spire of the Heavenly
Blade Sect.

"Did you witness it, O, people of Blade's Edge Town?!" Jinshi screeched,
waving his scroll like a lunatic. "The Grand Master of Commerce and
Authority has spoken! The Unstoppable Finger of 'You're Fired!' has
rendered five arrogant swordsmen metaphysically unemployed! Their very
destiny has been *nullified*! This is not mere combat; this is the
restructuring of fate itself!"

The townsfolk, freed from their daily torment, looked on with a mixture
of terror and dawning hope. They had been ruled by the sword, but now
they had witnessed the supreme power of the 'Make Anime Great Again'
principle.

"Do Nal Da Trum Pa" marched past the last of the town structures and onto the
winding, steep path that led up to the fortress gates. The air
immediately grew colder, and the spiritual energy denser and sharper---a
testament to the decades of focused Sword Qi cultivation within the
walls above.

"This place," "Do Nal Da Trum Pa" announced, wrinkling his orange nose with
profound distaste. "It's a total mess. The security is weak. Very, very
weak. They need a wall, a tremendous wall, and I know how to build it.
But first... we need to clean house. These people? They're losers. Sad!"

At the first defensive checkpoint, two imposing figures in heavier, dark
grey robes stood guard. Their swords were drawn, their expressions grim,
their Qi pulsating with controlled menace. They were not mere bullies;
they were professional guards, disciples who took their duty with the
utmost seriousness.

"Halt!" one of the guards commanded, his voice sharp as steel. "This
path is restricted! State your name and business, or face the Blade of
Execution!"

"Do Nal Da Trum Pa" stopped, placed his hands on his hips, and his expression
shifted from casual disdain to the surgical focus of a CEO about to cut
a massive line of expense.

"Listen, folks," he declared, his voice cutting through the mountain
air. "I'm the best thing that ever happened to this mountain. I'm going
to make this Sect so great. So great. But you two? You're blocking the
road. Bad optics. Terrible security. And those robes? Cheap fabric. A
disgrace."

He gestured dismissively toward the immense fortress spire looming
above. "This whole place is a disaster. And you? You're fired. Both of
you. Go home."

The guard who had spoken felt the "Vocal Art" hit him. His mind reeled,
momentarily agreeing that his robes *were* a cheap fabric and his
security posture was indeed 'bad optics.' Before he could recover, "Trum
pah" had already swept past him, marching straight for the massive,
black gates of the Heavenly Blade Sect.

Jinshi scrambled past the two bewildered guards, throwing them a final,
triumphant shout: "Witness the power of the Market Correction! Your
services are terminated! The Grand Master proceeds to the highest floor!
Who awaits him there?!"

Daniel, now past the first line of defense, felt the massive gates of
the fortress before him. He knew the Heavenly Blade Sect would not send
out more low-level thugs. They would be sending someone serious---a
master, an elder, a Sword Saint---to stop this unprecedented invasion.
