# Chapter 07: The Greatest

The stage is set. The Heavenly Blade Sect's elite are waiting behind
those gates. Who is the next unstoppable persona Daniel will adopt to
prove that the sword is not the only way?

"This sect, folks, it's a total mess. A disgrace. The best people, the
strongest people, they're not in charge here. But we're going to fix it.
We're going to make this sect great again!" "Do Nal Da Trum Pa"
announced, his voice reverberating through the heavy, black gates of the
Heavenly Blade Sect, causing them to vibrate subtly with his
overwhelming confidence.

Before the sect could even send their counter-attack, Daniel's form
began to shimmer. Jinshi, who had just finished explaining the economic
ramifications of being 'fired' to the two stunned guards at the gate,
gasped. "A new challenger approaches the stage! Prepare for a
demonstration of speed, grace, and unparalleled poetic fury!"

The expensive suit dissolved. The gravity-defying hair condensed.
Daniel's form grew leaner, his muscles toned and coiled. His skin took
on a rich, natural tone, and his expression shifted from bombast to a
fierce, intelligent defiance. He donned simple white boxing shorts with
a red stripe, red boxing gloves, and high-laced boots. His physique was
a marvel of athletic perfection.

He began to bob and weave, his movements fluid and impossible to track,
even to a martial artist's eye. He shadowboxed the air, each punch a
whistle of displaced wind, a whisper of unseen power.

"Behold, Mu Ha Med Ah Li!" Jinshi shrieked, his voice reaching a new,
ear-splitting crescendo of zeal. "The Greatest! He floats like a
butterfly and stings like a bee!"

[Persona Equipped: The Champion - Mu hah med Ah Li] [Gloves of the
Untouchable Hand]: Grants the user perfect defensive deflection. Any
incoming physical strike, regardless of force, is subtly redirected and
absorbed, often without the user appearing to move. [Boots of the
Shuffle Step]: Provides unparalleled agility and footwork, allowing the
user to move with blur-like speed and unpredictable rhythm, making them
almost impossible to pin down. [Mouth of Rhyming Retort]: A vocal
spiritual art. The user's words, when delivered in rhythmic verse,
inflict a powerful mental debuff on opponents, eroding their will to
fight and filling them with self-doubt. [Aura of Unshakeable Belief]:
A passive spiritual shield. The user's absolute self-confidence projects
an aura that intimidates and mentally weakens any opponent who does not
share the same level of self-belief. [Finishing Art: The Rumble in the
Jungle Combination\]: A flurry of impossibly fast, precise strikes that
targets pressure points and meridians, overwhelming the opponent's
defenses and leading to a definitive knockout.

Just as "Mu Ha Med Ah Li's" transformation completed, the massive gates of the
Heavenly Blade Sect swung open with a resounding *CREAK*, revealing a
phalanx of fifty elite Blade Disciples, their swords drawn and glowing
with Sword Qi. At their head stood a figure draped in shimmering black
robes, his white hair pulled back severely, his eyes like chips of
flint. This was Elder Jian, the Head of the Sect's Enforcement Division,
a master of the 'Severing Wind Blade' style.

"Enough of this mockery!" Elder Jian's voice boomed, sharp as a honed
blade. "You have trespassed, insulted our sect, and publicly humiliated
our disciples! Prepare to face true justice from the Heavenly Blade!"

"Mu Ha Med Ah Li" simply began to shuffle, his feet dancing a rhythmic, impossible
pattern. "I seen you, Elder. You ain't so tough. You ain't so quick. You
ain't got the heart. Just a little old man with a rusty blade, trying to
play big."

Elder Jian scoffed, his patience at an end. "Attack! Cut this clown
down!"

The fifty disciples moved as one, a storm of whistling blades and
surging Sword Qi. It was a perfectly coordinated assault designed to
overwhelm any single opponent.

"Mu Ha Med Ah Li" just shook his head, a playful smirk on his lips. "You squares
don't know nothin' 'bout floatin'!"

He began to move. He didn't seem to dodge; the blades simply *missed*. A
sword aimed at his head would slice through empty air. A thrust to his
gut would pass inches from his body. His "Boots of the Shuffle Step"
created a blur of movement, making him appear in three places at once.
His "Gloves of the Untouchable Hand" would subtly nudge an incoming
sword, sending it harmlessly wide. The storm of steel parted around him
like water around a stone.

"You can't hit what you can't see, old man!" "Mu Ha Med Ah Li" taunted, his words
flowing with a rhythmic, rhyming power. "Your sword's too slow, your
spirit's too low! You think you're fast? You're a blast from the past!
You're gonna fall, and you're gonna crawl!"

The disciples attacking him felt a strange confusion. Their perfectly
aimed strikes were going awry. Their Qi felt sluggish. The rhymes of "Ah
Li" were not just words; they were a mental assault, subtly undermining
their focus and resolve.

Elder Jian, enraged, decided to intervene directly. He leaped forward,
his black robes fluttering, his sword now glowing with a dangerous, dark
Sword Qi. "Severing Wind Blade: One Thousand Cuts!"

He unleashed a barrage of invisible blade-energies, each capable of
slicing flesh and bone from a distance.

"Mu Ha Med Ah Li" simply raised his "Gloves of the Untouchable Hand." *WHAP! WHAP!
WHAP!* Each invisible blade-energy struck the red leather gloves and was
absorbed or deflected, sending faint ripples of Qi harmlessly into the
sky. He didn't even stop his rhythmic taunting.

"I'm the prettiest! I'm the wittiest! I'm gonna hit ya 'til you quit it,
see! I'm so mean, I make medicine sick! I'm so fast, I could turn off
the light and be in bed before the room gets dark!"

Elder Jian found himself panting, his ultimate technique completely
nullified. He could not land a single blow, physical or spiritual. The
constant rhyming taunts were chipping away at his formidable mental
defenses, sowing seeds of doubt he had never known.

"Mu Ha Med Ah Li" saw his opening. He stopped his defensive shuffle, his eyes
narrowing with intent. "You're too small, you're too old, you're too
cold! Get ready to fall, 'cause I'm gonna give you the all!"

He moved with an explosive burst of speed, unleashing the "Rumble in the
Jungle Combination." It wasn't a sword style; it was pure, refined
pugilism. A blur of lightning-fast jabs, hooks, and uppercuts struck
Elder Jian's chest, his ribs, his chin, his solar plexus, each blow
precisely aimed at an unseen meridian or pressure point. It wasn't brute
force; it was surgical, overwhelming precision.

Elder Jian tried to block, tried to defend, but the blows were too
numerous, too fast, too perfectly placed. His Qi defenses shattered. His
vision tunneled.

With a final, devastating uppercut that sent the Elder's head snapping
back, "Mu Ha Med Ah Li" stepped back, his chest heaving slightly, but otherwise
completely unharmed. Elder Jian's eyes rolled back in his head, and he
collapsed in a heap, utterly knocked out.

"Mu Ha Med Ah Li" stood over him, hands on his hips, his chest puffed out. He
pointed at the fallen Elder, then to himself.

"I am the greatest!" he roared, his voice echoing from the mountain
peaks. "I'm so bad, I make innocent people jump out of windows! I'm
gonna show the world how great I am!"

He then turned, gave a dramatic pose to the dumbfounded disciples, and,
with Jinshi trailing breathlessly behind, began his triumphant march up
the winding path deeper into the Heavenly Blade Sect's fortress. The
remaining fifty disciples stood frozen, their swords lowered, their
minds reeling from a martial art they could never have conceived.

Jinshi, wiping sweat from his brow, was still yelling explanations of
"Mu Ha Med Ah Li's" genius to the stunned Heavenly Blade disciples as Daniel
ascended the winding path deeper into the sect's fortress. His voice was
hoarse, but his zeal was unflagging. "Did you not see the 'Float like a
butterfly, sting like a bee' technique?! A profound insight into kinetic
energy and spiritual misdirection!"
