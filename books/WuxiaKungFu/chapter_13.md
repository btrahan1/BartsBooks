# Chapter 13: The Sultan of Smooth

Jinshi, having finally recovered from the profound emotional experience
of "Bay toe vin," was back in his element, eagerly scanning the
tournament pairings posted on a large jade tablet in the city square.

"Ah, Grand Master! Your next opponent has been chosen!" he announced,
rushing back to Daniel. "She is a formidable one! Lady Xia, the
'Whispering Petal Dancer'! They say she can turn a simple flower into a
weapon of lethal grace, her movements as swift and silent as a falling
leaf!"

Daniel's expression, which had been one of combat readiness, suddenly
softened into one of mild panic.

"Our next opponent is a woman!?" he exclaimed, a look of genuine alarm
on his face. "Oh my! Jinshi, this is a crisis! We can't hit a woman!
That is simply not done. It's an unwritten rule, a fundamental law!"

Jinshi blinked, his chronicle-ready hand hovering in the air. "But...
Grand Master... it is a tournament... a duel to incapacitation... How
will you..."

"We will not fight," Daniel declared, his face hardening with a new,
suave resolve. "We will woo. We will charm. We will conquer her heart so
completely that the thought of battle will be but a distant, unpleasant
memory. Jinshi, prepare the masses for a performance of a lifetime. It's
time to bring out Barry Manilow!"

The arena was abuzz. Lady Xia stood in the center, a vision of deadly
elegance. Her robes were the color of cherry blossoms, and her movements
were so light she barely seemed to touch the ground. Her expression was
cold and focused, her eyes holding the sharp glint of a duelist ready
for the kill.

The crowd waited for her opponent. What bizarre warrior would 'The
Boundary Breaker' become to face this graceful swordswoman?

Jinshi cleared his throat, a hand pressed dramatically to his heart. "O,
citizens of Blazing Sun City! Prepare not for a battle of fists, but for
a duel of hearts! Witness a power that disarms not with force, but with
feeling! A legend whose voice can melt the coldest resolve! Behold...
Beari Man i lo!"

A wave of impossibly smooth, gentle energy washed over Daniel. His
simple clothes transformed into a magnificent, deep blue tuxedo jacket
worn over a white shirt unbuttoned just a little too far, revealing a
tasteful gold medallion. His hair feathered perfectly into a luscious,
well-coiffed mane. And in his hand, a glowing, ethereal microphone
materialized.

He didn't walk; he glided onto the stage, a smile of pure, unadulterated
charm on his face.

[Persona Equipped: The Sultan of Smooth - Beari Man I Lo] [Tuxedo of
Suave Confidence\]: A defensive garment that doesn't block physical
attacks, but instead projects an aura of such overwhelming charm that
opponents are subconsciously reluctant to strike. \[Microphone of
Melodious Intent\]: A spiritual artifact that amplifies the user's
voice, transforming it into waves of pure, romantic Qi that can soothe
aggression and inspire feelings of love. \[Vocal Art: The Velvet
Heart\]: The user's singing voice bypasses all physical and spiritual
defenses, directly targeting the listener's heart and filling it with an
irresistible, warm, and fuzzy feeling. \[The Key Change of Emotional
Elevation\]: A special technique where a sudden, dramatic shift in
musical key elevates the romantic Qi to a higher, more potent level,
often causing spontaneous swooning. \[Finishing Art: The Mandy Gambit\]:
A focused, high-intensity vocal attack where the user sings a single,
powerful name ("Mandy"), sending a concentrated beam of pure, distilled
longing that shatters emotional defenses and forces immediate surrender.

Lady Xia looked at this... this preening crooner... with utter disdain.
"Do you mock me? Is this some kind of joke?" she asked, her voice like
ice. "Draw your weapon."

"My only weapon," "Beari Man I Lo" said, his voice a velvety baritone
that seemed to caress the very air, "is this song... for you."

He brought the microphone to his lips as Lady Xia, disgusted, attacked.
She flicked her wrist, and a dozen razor-sharp cherry blossom petals
shot towards him, each a miniature blade of focused Qi.

"Beari Man I Lo" simply began to sing, his eyes locked on hers. "I can't
smiiiiile... without you..."

The "Vocal Art of the Velvet Heart" washed over the arena. The deadly
petals slowed in mid-air, their lethal edges softening. They lost all
momentum and fluttered harmlessly to the ground around him, like a
gentle spring shower.

Lady Xia's eyes widened. She felt a strange, unwelcome warmth spreading
through her chest. Her focus, her killing intent, was being... diluted
by this cheesy, heartfelt melody.

"Enough!" she cried, leaping into the air, spinning like a top as she
unleashed her 'Thousand Petal Whirlwind' technique, a vortex of lethal
blossoms designed to shred any defense.

"Beari Man I Lo" met this overwhelming assault with his ultimate
technique. He raised his free hand dramatically, his voice swelling with
power and emotion as he hit the "Key Change of Emotional Elevation."

"Looks like we maaaaade it!" he belted out, the sudden shift in key
sending a tidal wave of triumphant, romantic Qi crashing against her
attack. The whirlwind of petals instantly dissipated, its violent energy
transformed into a shower of harmless, decorative confetti.

Lady Xia landed, stumbling, her cold composure completely shattered. Her
heart was pounding, her cheeks were flushed, and she was looking at
"Beari Man I Lo" not as an opponent, but with a strange, new,
confusing... admiration.

He saw his moment. He glided forward, dropped to one knee, and looked up
at her with eyes full of devastating longing. The music softened, the
arena seemed to fade away, and he unleashed the "Mandy Gambit."

"Oh, Maaaaandy!" he sang, his voice a focused beam of pure,
heart-wrenching devotion. "Well, you came and you gave without taking...
But I sent you away... Oh, Mandy!"

The final, powerful note struck Lady Xia's soul. It wasn't an attack; it
was a revelation. Her sword, her training, her cold discipline... it all
felt so empty compared to the profound, overwhelming emotion she was
feeling. A single, perfect tear rolled down her cheek.

She dropped her sword.

"I... I forfeit," she whispered, her voice choked with emotion.

Jinshi, who had been clutching his chest and swaying for the entire
performance, snapped back to reality. He leaped to his feet, a
triumphant tear in his own eye.

"VICTORY!" he roared. "The Whispering Petal Dancer has been disarmed!
Her Thousand Petal Whirlwind unraveled by the 'Key Change of Emotional
Elevation'! Her very will to fight has been serenaded into submission by
the 'Velvet Heart'! The Sultan of Smooth, Beari Man i lo, has won the
duel through sheer, unadulterated ROMANCE!"

The final, devastatingly romantic note of the "Mandy Gambit" still
seemed to hang in the air, shimmering with pure, unadulterated feeling.
The entire arena was held in a state of stunned, emotional reverence.

"Beari Man i lo," still on one knee, did not release Lady Xia's gaze.
Instead, he reached out, his movements impossibly gentle, and took her
hand in his. Her hand, which could guide a sword with lethal precision,
was trembling slightly. He brought it to his lips and bestowed a chaste,
yet profoundly charming, kiss upon her knuckles.

"May I buy you dinner?" he asked, his voice a low, velvety murmur that
was for her alone, yet seemed to carry to every corner of the silent
arena.

Lady Xia, the formidable 'Whispering Petal Dancer', looked down at the
man kneeling before her. Her formidable Qi, which had been as sharp and
controlled as a razor's edge, was now a warm, chaotic, and utterly
confusing swirl in her chest. A deep blush spread from her neck to her
cheeks. She had trained her entire life for combat, for the cold
calculus of the duel. She had absolutely no defense for this.

All she could manage was a small, almost imperceptible nod. A whispered,
"Yes," escaped her lips before she could stop it.

A wave of profound confusion washed over the arena. The cultivators in
the stands stared, their mouths agape. They had seen victories by
knockout, by submission, by spiritual pressure. They had never, ever
seen a victory by... date proposal.

"Is... is this a post-duel ritual?" one spectator muttered to another.
"Perhaps he is absorbing her life force through a romantic pact?" his
companion theorized, his face grim. "A 'dinner date'... is it some kind
of advanced soul-binding technique?" a third whispered in awe.

But Jinshi, who had been clutching his heart and sobbing with pure,
romantic joy, understood. Of course, he understood! He sprang to his
feet, his scroll held aloft like a holy scripture.

"BEHOLD!" he roared, his voice filled with newfound insight. "The
ultimate technique! The **Consolidation of Emotional Victory**! He does
not merely defeat his opponent; he assimilates her into his sphere of
influence through a ritual feast! A 'dinner date'! It is a display of
such profound dominance that the battle continues on the spiritual and
social planes! He has not just won the match; he has won the
*narrative*! Truly, the boundaries of martial arts have been shattered
today!"

"Beari Man I Lo" rose gracefully, never releasing Lady Xia's hand. He
gave the bewildered crowd a dazzling, megawatt smile, a slight bow, and
then gently led the still-dazed, blushing swordswoman out of the arena,
leaving behind a sea of confused cultivators and one extremely excited
chronicler.

"Well," Daniel thought to himself as the "Beari Man I Lo" persona faded,
leaving him holding the hand of his very surprised former opponent, "I
guess this is one way to get to the next round."
