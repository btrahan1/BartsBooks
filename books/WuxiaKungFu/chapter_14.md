# Chapter 14: The Untouchable Showman

The news of Daniel's victory-by-dinner-date had sent shockwaves through
the tournament. It was the most talked-about duel in the city's history,
and the stands were overflowing the next day. In a specially reserved
section, two unlikely figures sat together: Lady Xia, her usual cold
demeanor replaced by a flustered, curious warmth, and Master Ling Tao,
the silent swordsman, who now seemed to be listening to the world with
an entirely new kind of attention.

Their gazes were fixed on the arena floor, where Daniel's next opponent
was making his entrance. He was Bao "The Crusher" Xiong, a mountain of a
man known for his brutal 'Earthbreaker' style. He didn't just defeat
opponents; he pulverized them, often leaving them with broken bones and
shattered meridians for no other reason than the sheer joy of it. He
stomped into the arena, cracking his knuckles with a sound like grinding
boulders, a cruel sneer on his face.

"So this is the trickster who sings and asks for dates," Bao bellowed,
his voice a gravelly roar. "Enough games! Today, you face real power!"

Daniel walked out calmly. He saw Lady Xia in the stands and gave her a
charming, subtle wink, causing her to blush and look away. He then
turned his attention to the bully. A look of profound disapproval
crossed his face.

"Jinshi," Daniel said, his voice ringing with a new, funky rhythm. "This
man's style is illegitimate. His force is excessive. His attitude? Not
legit. It is time to bring the Hammer."

Jinshi, sensing a high-energy transformation, puffed out his chest. "The
crowd is hyped! The bully is posturing! Prepare for a lesson in
untouchable power! Behold... Em Cee Hammah!"

A wave of vibrant, kinetic energy exploded from Daniel. His simple
clothes were replaced by the most voluminous, ostentatious pair of
shimmering golden pants anyone had ever seen. On his torso, a tight,
black, sleeveless top revealed impressively toned arms, and he wore a
pair of gleaming white shoes. A pair of dark glasses materialized on his
face. He struck a pose, a whirlwind of energy ready to be unleashed.

[Persona Equipped: The Untouchable Showman - MC Hammer] [Hammer
Pants of Unpredictable Evasion]: A defensive artifact of immense size.
The billowing fabric creates a field of spatial distortion, making it
nearly impossible for an opponent to target the wearer's actual
location. [Boots of the Perpetual Shuffle]: Grants the user the
ability to perform a constant, high-speed, side-to-side shuffling dance,
generating a field of kinetic energy that repels slow-moving attacks.
[Vocal Art: U Can't Touch This]: A powerful, taunting spiritual
command. When uttered, it creates a short-range aura of kinetic
repulsion, subtly deflecting incoming physical blows and Qi strikes.
[Finishing Art: The Hammer Dance of Dizzying Defeat]: A high-energy,
multi-step dance combination that overwhelms an opponent's senses,
shatters their stance, and disrupts their Qi flow through a series of
rhythmic spiritual shockwaves, leading to a non-lethal knockout. [Sonic
Command: Stop!]: A focused sonic burst that causes momentary
motor-function paralysis in an opponent, creating an opening for a
follow-up technique.

Bao the Crusher stared at the ridiculous golden pants and roared with
laughter. "You look like a fool! I will crush you and your silly
trousers!"

He charged, unleashing his signature 'Crushing Boulder Fist,' a strike
meant to shatter stone. "Em Cee Hammah" didn't retreat. He simply began
to dance, shuffling side-to-side with impossible speed.

Bao's fist plunged into the vast, shimmering fabric of the Hammer
Pants... and hit nothing but air. The spatial distortion effect caused
his punch to pass harmlessly through the space where Daniel's leg should
have been.

"Wha---?" Bao stammered, his momentum carrying him off balance.

"Em Cee Hammah" slid past him, his feet a blur. He spun, struck a pose,
and pointed at the bully. "U Can't Touch This!" he declared, the vocal
art washing over Bao in a wave of pure, funky confidence.

Enraged, Bao attacked again, a flurry of heavy, brutal punches. But it
was useless. Every blow was either lost in the dimensional folds of the
Hammer Pants or subtly repelled by the kinetic aura of the Perpetual
Shuffle and the "U Can't Touch This" command. "Em Cee Hammah" flowed
around him like a river of gold, a whirlwind of untouchable energy.

Bao, growing desperate, gathered all his Qi for one final, devastating
blow. He roared, leaping into the air for a 'Mountain Collapsing Stomp'
meant to shatter the entire arena floor.

As he descended, "Em Cee Hammah" struck a dramatic pose, holding one
hand up.

"Stop!"

The sonic command hit Bao mid-air. His muscles locked. He froze,
suspended in his attack posture for a single, impossible second.

"Hammer Time!" "Em Cee Hammah" shouted.

He exploded into the "Hammer Dance of Dizzying Defeat." He shuffled
side-to-side, he did the running man, he spun, he dropped and rose, each
movement a percussive beat that sent rhythmic shockwaves of spiritual
energy into the paralyzed Bao. The bully's Qi was violently disrupted,
his senses were overwhelmed by the dazzling display, and his body was
rattled by the invisible impacts.

With a final, triumphant spin, "Em Cee Hammah" struck a pose, pointing
at Bao. The accumulated rhythmic energy released in a final, concussive,
yet non-damaging pulse. Bao the Crusher, his motor functions restored,
immediately crumpled to the ground, his eyes rolling back, not from
injury, but from pure, overwhelming sensory overload and Qi sickness.

In the stands, Lady Xia watched with wide, disbelieving eyes, a strange
smile on her lips. Master Ling Tao simply nodded slowly, as if
recognizing the profound, unbeatable rhythm of the universe in the
dance.

Jinshi, nearly falling from his perch with excitement, bellowed his
chronicle to the heavens. "THE BULLY IS BEATEN! THE CRUSHER IS CRUSHED!
His excessive force was rendered impotent by the 'Spatial Distortion
Trousers'! His every blow denied by the 'U Can't Touch This' spiritual
edict! And he was finally undone by the 'Rhythmic Art of Unpredictable
Pummeling'! Truly, it was... Hammer Time!"
