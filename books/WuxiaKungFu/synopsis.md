Daniel is a traveler in the cultivation world of Xuanwu City, but he's not your typical hero. Possessing a unique system that allows him to channel the legends of his own world, Daniel decides to shake up the rigid martial arts hierarchy. When an arrogant young master from the Verdant Jade Clan challenges him, Daniel equips the persona of "The King of Pop"—complete with Moonwalking Boots, a Glittering Suit, and a razor-sharp fedora. Discover the hilarious and chaotic story of a fighter who defeats immortals with dance moves!
