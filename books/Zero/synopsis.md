What if all your debts suddenly vanished? For Marcus, Ben, and Sarah, it seemed like a miracle when a mysterious entity named "Zero" cleared their student loans, medical bills, and mortgages. But the celebration is short-lived. As the financial system collapses and the supply chain grinds to a halt, they quickly learn that a world without obligation is a world without support. A thought-provoking short story about the thin veneer of civilization, the true cost of safety, and what happens when the game of economics is reset to zero.
